# DVM Finance – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy applies to the DVM Finance app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"). Divya van Mahajan makes the app and is responsible for it.

## Summary

The app keeps all your data on your device. The app does not collect, send, sell, or share your personal data. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

## Data that the app stores

The app stores the data that you put into it, for example:

- transactions from the bank statements that you import
- categories, tags, rules, and budgets
- snapshot files that you import from the DVM Finance desktop app

The app stores this data in a local database in the private storage area of the app on your device. The developer cannot see or get this data.

When you delete the app, iOS deletes this data. iOS can include this data in your device backups (iCloud or computer), according to your backup settings.

## Network connections

The app makes network connections only in these cases:

- **Sync from computer.** When you scan a QR code that DVM Finance shows on your computer, the app downloads one snapshot file directly from your computer over your local network. The data does not go through a server of the developer.
- **Exchange rates.** When you import an SEB statement, the app can download public exchange rates from the European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu). This request does not contain your financial data.
- **Links.** When you tap a link in the Help screen, iOS opens the web page in your browser.

## Camera

The app uses the camera only to scan the QR code for "Sync from computer". The app does not keep or send camera images.

## Sharing and export

When you export a snapshot, the app gives the file to the iOS share sheet. You choose where the file goes. After that, the privacy policy of the app or service that you choose applies.

## TestFlight

If you test a beta version through Apple TestFlight, Apple can collect data such as crash reports, usage data, and the feedback that you send. Apple shares some of this data with the developer. The Apple privacy policy applies to this data: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## Children

The app is not for children. The app does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your rights

The developer does not hold your personal data from the app, so there is no data to access, correct, or delete on the side of the developer. You control all your data on your device. If you have a question about your rights under the GDPR, contact the developer.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new effective date.
