## Thesis

Michael Novati, co-founder and CTO of Formation, spent 2009 to 2017 at Facebook/Meta, rising from intern to E7 in six years. He tells Gergely Orosz that Meta's "coding machine" archetype was created for him to reward impact, not raw output. He argues that Meta's engineering-first culture empowered ICs, but that even there an individual contributor's influence has limits.

## Key points

- **Archetypes.** Novati says archetypes serve fairness, letting Meta pattern-match senior engineers against existing ones, and compares them to positions on a pro sports team. The coding machine moves prototypes forward and refactors code fast; his skip-level sponsor, Tom Allison, championed it.
- **Fast promotion.** As an intern he rewrote the internal org chart tool on his second or third day. He credits trust and transparency with his managers and a running notepad of accomplishments; Orosz links this to Julia Evans' brag document and his own work log.
- **IC limits and tenure.** Novati pushed for more E8s than the roughly 10 at the time versus 20 to 30 directors, but now accepts that one engineer cannot replace thousands. He says external senior hires often struggled and left, while those promoted internally embodied the values.
- **Internal tools as products.** He says Meta built internal tools on the same codebase as the public site, which made the tools team exciting. Former Meta tools helped seed companies such as Statsig and Sentry; Orosz adds Graphite.
- **Outages and Zuck Reviews.** A profile photo template prototype used a two-way graph edge; after the Paris attacks about 100 million people added the French flag within a day or two, overloading one database node. The database infrastructure team fixed it by making the edge one-way.
- **Hiring.** Novati describes a 45-minute technical screen, whiteboard-style onsite interviews with little small talk, and hiring committees of at least three directors or VPs that focused mostly on level. He says interview styles predate LeetCode and that online assessments and team matching now add more filters.
