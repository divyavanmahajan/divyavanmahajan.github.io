## Thesis

Five days after stepping down as CEO of Stability AI, Emad Mostaque tells Peter Diamandis that he sees himself as a founder and strategist, not an operator. He says the more urgent job is decentralizing AI. He argues that governments will set their AI strategies within a year or two, and that if no open, democratically governed alternative exists by then, a few centralized labs will control the infrastructure.

## Key points

- **Why he left.** Mostaque quotes Elon Musk's line that being a CEO is "staring into the abyss and chewing glass". He says Stability went from its first researcher to models with over 300 million downloads in two years. He says he could not work on governance while running the company.
- **What decentralization means.** He describes three parts: everyone can access the technology, the data that shapes models is governed transparently, and the infrastructure is modular rather than a single monolith. He proposes a national model for every country, owned by its people and coordinated through a Web3-style protocol for identity, attribution and payments, not speculative tokens.
- **Data over compute.** Mostaque predicts that a Llama 70B-class model could cost under $10,000, perhaps $1,000, to train by the end of next year. He advises national leaders to treat curated data sets as infrastructure. He cites a Render Network deal for $10 million to $250 million of distributed compute to build open 3D data sets.
- **Democracy at a fork.** He sees two paths: a "1984 on steroids" driven by hyper-personalized persuasive speech, or citizen assemblies and AI tools that break down legislation. He calls Google and Meta amoral advertising businesses.
- **Health and education.** He plans an open GPT-4-level model for cancer so that no patient is alone, drawing on his work analyzing autism research for his son. He also wants AI tutors on every child's device, in the spirit of Neal Stephenson's illustrated primer.
- **Critique of big labs.** He accuses centralized AGI labs of building systems that they admit could remove freedom or kill us all.
