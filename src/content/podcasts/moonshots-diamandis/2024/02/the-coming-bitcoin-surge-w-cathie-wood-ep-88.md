## Thesis

Cathie Wood, founder of ARK Invest, argues that the shift to benchmark-hugging passive investing has left markets blind to converging technologies. She expects that convergence to produce super-exponential rather than merely exponential growth. Peter Diamandis agrees throughout and adds his own framing: the "six Ds" of exponentials, and AI as the ultimate user interface.

## Key points

- **Passive investing.** Wood says passive strategies now hold well over half of assets under management. She blames fear left over from the 2000 tech bust and the 2008 crisis. She also says 2021-22 rate hikes hit all long-duration assets, and she now sees deflation, not inflation, as the real issue.
- **Robotaxis.** Wood puts autonomous taxi platforms at an 8-10 trillion dollar revenue opportunity by 2030. She cites accident data: surface-street miles between accidents rise from about 190,000 for the average car to roughly 3.2 million for a Tesla using FSD. She also expects air taxis to reach taxi-like prices once regulators allow scale.
- **AI costs and AGI.** Wood says ARK estimates AI training costs fall 75% a year and inference costs 85-90% a year. She notes that futurists' AGI forecasts dropped from 80 years away in 2019 to about eight. She says ARK trimmed Nvidia in favour of firms with domain expertise and proprietary data, such as Recursion.
- **Bitcoin.** Wood calls Bitcoin a new asset class and the first rules-based global monetary system. She says major wirehouses have not yet approved Bitcoin on their platforms, and she repeats ARK's bull case of $1.5 million by 2027.
- **Robots.** Using Wright's Law, Wood expects generalisable robotics to be the fastest-growing segment, at more than 80% a year in market value. She also sees disruptive innovation overall growing from about 20 trillion to 220 trillion dollars by 2030. Diamandis says his fund and ARK's venture fund both took positions in Figure.
- **Open source and venture access.** Diamandis expects open-source and nation-state language models to win. Wood describes ARK's venture interval fund: a $500 minimum, a 2.75% fee and no carry.
