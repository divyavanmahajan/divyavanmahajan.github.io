## Thesis

In an on-stage conversation at the FII8 Summit, Elon Musk tells Peter Diamandis that AI is advancing very fast and is mostly likely to go well. He calls it the biggest near-term existential risk, with falling birth rates as the longer-term one. His case for a good outcome rests on truth-seeking AI, plentiful solar energy and humanoid robots, which he says lead to an age of abundance.

## Key points

- **Pace of AI.** Musk says AI is getting about 10x better per year. He predicts it could do anything a single human can do within a year or two, and match all humans combined around 2028-2029. He puts the chance of a bad outcome at 10-20%.
- **Safety through truth-seeking.** Musk argues that AI should be "maximally truth seeking" and should love humanity. He says models trained in the San Francisco Bay Area absorb a politically correct philosophy, and gives this as his reason for founding xAI. He says xAI's Colossus is the most powerful training cluster and is about to double.
- **Energy and sovereign compute.** He says clusters are now limited by voltage transformers and will next be limited by energy. Long term, he expects almost all energy to be solar. He predicts every country will run its own AI clusters, but notes that training frontier models takes skills only a few companies have.
- **Robots and autonomy.** Musk predicts at least 10 billion humanoid robots by 2040, at roughly $20,000-25,000 each. He expects unsupervised Full Self-Driving in the US in 2025, Cybercab volume production in 2026, and Optimus limited production in 2025.
- **Mars and regulation.** He plans uncrewed Starships to Mars in about two years, with crewed flights two years after that. He names overregulation as the biggest obstacle and says he is more optimistic under a Trump administration.
- **Post-scarcity.** Musk agrees with Diamandis that society may become post-capitalist, with near-zero marginal costs for goods and services.
