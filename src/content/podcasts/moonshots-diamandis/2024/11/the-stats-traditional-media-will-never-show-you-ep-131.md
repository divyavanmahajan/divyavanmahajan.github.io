## Thesis

In a talk recorded at Abundance Platinum 2024, Peter Diamandis argues that technology repeatedly turns scarcity into abundance, and that the data shows this even though the media rarely reports it. He then argues that a leader's mindset is their most important asset, and sets out five mindsets he thinks entrepreneurs need for an exponential decade.

## Key points

- **Proof of abundance.** Diamandis shows charts he says show extreme poverty falling from 90% to under 10%, global literacy rising from 15% to over 80%, and US reported crime down 50% according to FBI data. He also cites falling solar costs and about 7 billion smartphones. An audience member challenges his democracy slide, and Diamandis agrees the data ends in 2022 and needs updating.
- **Purpose.** He calls a "massive transformative purpose" (MTP) the source of persistence. He says two of his 27 companies were started only to make money, and both failed. He uses Martine Rothblatt and United Therapeutics as an example of purpose-driven building.
- **Exponential thinking.** Diamandis argues that human minds are wired for linear thinking. He uses Kodak's dismissal of the digital camera and Ray Kurzweil's 1999 prediction of human-level AI by 2029 to show how exponential progress is underestimated.
- **Moonshot organisations.** Citing Astro Teller of Google X, he advises CEOs to aim for 10x rather than 10%. Moonshot teams should be physically separated from the core business and report directly to the CEO, to avoid an organisational "immune reaction". He gives the Macintosh team and Lockheed's Skunk Works as examples.
- **Longevity and abundance mindsets.** He calls longevity possibly the largest business opportunity on the planet, driven by converging technologies, and urges listeners to protect their mindset by choosing what information they consume.
