## Thesis

MicroStrategy executive chairman Michael Saylor tells Peter Diamandis that traditional stores of wealth lose value over time through inflation, taxation or seizure, and that Bitcoin is the first asset that can hold economic energy across generations. He sees Bitcoin as a move of money, property and energy into cyberspace. In his view, the main obstacle to technology-driven abundance is politics, not engineering.

## Key points

- **Price math.** Saylor says the world holds about $500 trillion of stores of value. If Bitcoin replaced gold ($10 trillion), he estimates $500,000 a coin. At $100 trillion as digital property, he estimates $5 million. He frames these as long-term scenarios, not guarantees.
- **Why stores of value leak.** Saylor claims the dollar has expanded about 7% a year for a century, and 20% a year in the two years before the interview. He says gold has a "half-life" of about 35 years because of new mining, and that land is exposed to taxes and seizure.
- **MicroStrategy's pivot.** In Q2 2020, Saylor says, the company had $500 million of idle cash. Buybacks, holding cash and acquisitions all looked like slow or fast deaths, so it bought Bitcoin. Diamandis says the stock has since outperformed 97% of S&P 500 stocks.
- **Digital energy.** Saylor argues that because Bitcoin solves double-spending, it behaves like conserved energy rather than copyable information. He suggests this could let sites charge real costs for spam, bots and denial-of-service attacks.
- **Politics as the brake.** Saylor points to Germany's nuclear shutdown, banned prediction markets and Zillow's limited reach outside the US as cases where regulation and market structure blocked useful technology. He would prioritise fixing money and free online education over Mars colonies.
- **Ten observations.** Saylor's personal advice includes focus your energy, guard your time, think for yourself, keep your promises and upgrade the world. The "X Prize" he would fund is a mobile app holding dollars and Bitcoin for anyone on Earth.
