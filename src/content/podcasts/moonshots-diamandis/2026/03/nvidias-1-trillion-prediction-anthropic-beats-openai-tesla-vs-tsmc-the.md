## Thesis

Recapping Nvidia's GTC 2026, Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin and Alexander Wissner-Gross argue that AI demand has moved ahead of the world's ability to make chips. Cheaper inference is reshaping the frontier-lab race in Anthropic's favour, and the Mates see the same forces wiping out entry-level knowledge work.

## Key points

- **Nvidia's trillion.** Jensen Huang's keynote claimed at least $1 trillion through 2027. Blundin says these are bookings recognised over two years, and that TSMC is the bottleneck: Nvidia has locked up 70% of its 3-nanometer volume. Diamandis raises antitrust concerns. Wissner-Gross sees GTC as the West's answer to China's AI plus plan and says the Groq acquisition deserves more scrutiny.
- **OpenClaw goes enterprise.** Nvidia announced NemoClaw to support OpenClaw. Ismail calls this the start of an "organizational singularity", where agents take over human-to-human workflows. Blundin says he had OpenClaw running on Amazon Bedrock in under 10 minutes.
- **Inference deflation.** Sam Altman says the cost of the same hard answer fell about 1,000x from o1 to GPT-5.4. Wissner-Gross puts this down to an unused inference-time compute overhang and expects the next architecture after transformers not to be recurrent. Ismail asks whether this much efficiency might make the giant energy buildout unnecessary.
- **Anthropic vs. OpenAI.** A slide shows Anthropic's share of first-time enterprise customers rising from 40% to 73% while OpenAI's fell from 60% to 26%. Wissner-Gross says OpenAI lost a bet on consumer demand for reasoning. Blundin says Dario Amodei is easy to partner with because Anthropic builds neither chips nor data centres.
- **TerraFab.** Elon Musk's planned fab targets 100,000 wafer starts a month, rising to a million. Blundin names ASML's output of about 700 machines a year as the hard limit. Wissner-Gross sees US production as lowering the geopolitical risk around Taiwan.
- **CS placement collapse.** A professor's figures, posted by Tech Layoff Tracker, show placement falling from 89% in fall 2023 to 19% this spring. The hosts tell graduates to found or join startups. Blundin predicts government jobs will be the last to be automated.
