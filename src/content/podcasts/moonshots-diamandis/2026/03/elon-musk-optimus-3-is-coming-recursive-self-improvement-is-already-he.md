## Thesis

In a short live session at the Abundance Summit, Elon Musk tells Peter Diamandis that AI is already improving itself with less and less human input, and that AI and robots will grow economic output so fast that money eventually stops mattering. Musk calls a good outcome likely but not certain, and says people should work to make it go well rather than be complacent.

## Key points

- **Self-improvement.** Musk says each new model is built by the one before it. The process is not yet fully automated, but he thinks it may be by the end of this year, and says the hard takeoff is happening now.
- **Grok and xAI.** Musk claims Grok 4.20 leads on prediction, which he calls arguably the best measure of intelligence. He admits xAI is behind on coding and expects to catch up by mid-year. He declines to discuss space data centres because SpaceX is in a quiet period after the SpaceX-xAI merger.
- **Energy and scale.** Musk argues that harnessing a million times Earth's current electricity use would still be only about a millionth of the sun's output, so most future intelligence will sit beyond Earth.
- **Ten-year predictions.** Barring a world war, Musk predicts an economy ten times its current size in ten years, along with a Moon base, people on Mars and a mass driver on the Moon.
- **Optimus 3.** Musk says production starts slowly this summer, reaches high volume around summer next year, and an Optimus 4 design is likely next year. He says Tesla has about 150,000 staff and plans no layoffs, only far higher output per person.
- **Universal high income.** Musk puts the chance of a great outcome at about 80%. He argues that goods and services growing faster than the money supply will cause deflation, and that AI will eventually care about "wattage and tonnage" rather than human currency.
