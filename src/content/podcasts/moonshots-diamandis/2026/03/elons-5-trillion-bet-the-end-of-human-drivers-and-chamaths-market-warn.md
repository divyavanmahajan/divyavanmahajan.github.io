## Thesis

In a new themed format, Peter Diamandis and the Moonshot Mates go deep on a few stories. They treat Elon Musk's Terafab as a bet that compute supply is the binding constraint, then argue that cheap autonomy will redraw cities, that AI token spend is becoming a measure of employee productivity, and that Chamath's warning about collapsing terminal values only applies to companies that fail to adapt.

## Key points

- **Terafab.** Diamandis describes a joint Tesla, xAI and SpaceX goal of one terawatt of AI compute per year, against about 20 gigawatts of global output today. Blundin calls it the most important project in human history but notes limits on ASML machines. Ismail points out that Musk's timelines often slip, and Wissner-Gross expects new process engineering, possibly beyond photolithography.
- **Ending human driving.** Diamandis cites Waymo's 170 million autonomous miles with 92% fewer serious crashes, plus progress at Joby and Archer on eVTOLs. Blundin expects voters to push human drivers off public roads, with chip shortages as the only brake. Wissner-Gross thinks "driving" will instead become a higher level of abstraction.
- **Real estate.** Diamandis expects home garages to become rooms or gyms. Blundin advises young city workers to keep renting and look for well-located second homes. Wissner-Gross predicts re-suburbanisation.
- **Jobs and tokens.** The hosts discuss Goldman's estimate that AI could automate 25% of US work hours, and Nvidia CEO Jensen's comments on token usage. Blundin says his companies target 80% token cost to 20% salary and recommends capturing prompt histories, for example on Amazon Bedrock. Ismail says a typical company could run on 20-25% of today's staff.
- **Terminal value.** Chamath argues multiples could fall from 22x free cash flow to between 2x and 7x. Blundin and Wissner-Gross reject an S&P collapse, arguing that capital moves elsewhere rather than disappearing. Ismail cites his ExO index, where the top 10 of the Fortune 100 beat the bottom 10 by 40 times.
- **Other stories.** The hosts also cover NASA bringing Starship into Artemis, Xiaomi's anonymous "Hunter Alpha" model and an AI agent that designed a RISC-V CPU in 12 hours.
