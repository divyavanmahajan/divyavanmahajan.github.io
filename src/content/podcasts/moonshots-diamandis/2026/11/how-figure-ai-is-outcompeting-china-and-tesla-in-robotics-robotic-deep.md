## Thesis

Recording at Figure's campus, Peter Diamandis and Dave Blundin hear Brett Adcock describe a four-chapter plan for humanoids: functional hardware, an end-to-end AI recipe, then scaling intelligence and scaling robots. Adcock says Figure is now in chapter three, where the main constraints are data and compute rather than mechanics. He argues that progress in China and at Tesla is aimed at a different and less practical problem.

## Key points

- **Robots outnumber staff.** Adcock says Figure has well over a thousand robots on campus, more than its roughly 700-800 employees. All Helix inference runs on board; only speech and fleet orchestration use the cloud.
- **Zero-shot homes.** Adcock says Helix 2.5 did tasks in 30 rented homes it had never seen and showed what he calls the first humanoid data scaling laws. He calls the unreleased Helix 3 a "baby AGI" moment. He hopes for long-horizon work in any home within about six months and would be "very shocked" not to see it by the end of 2027.
- **Home launch criteria.** Figure will not ship to homes until the product works without teleoperation. Adcock expects a lease of $300 to $500 a month and says Figure will handle repair itself at first.
- **Figure 4 and manufacturing.** Adcock says Figure 4 will be the biggest step between generations, with a new hand after seven iterations, and is designed for lights-out manufacturing. The BotQ facility can make over 40,000 units a year across four lines.
- **Compute scarcity.** Adcock says Figure spends nine figures a year on compute, expects billion-dollar training runs next summer, and describes a 2027 deal that Blundin puts at 100,000 GPUs for over three billion dollars. Memory suppliers told him they had no capacity for humanoids.
- **China, Tesla, Hark.** Adcock calls Chinese demos open-loop motion-capture replays and names "the collective China" as Figure's closest competitor. He says he has seen little from Tesla. He rules out defence work, describes ending the OpenAI collaboration, and predicts his AI company Hark could overtake Figure within six months.
