## Thesis

Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin, Alexander Wissner-Gross and guest Emad Mostaque argue that AI capability is now advancing faster than the institutions around it. The allegations around Jensen Huang show how thin export controls can be, while Anthropic's new models and Elon Musk's plans for fabs and robots point, in the hosts' view, to a very disruptive 2027.

## Key points

- **NVIDIA allegations.** Wissner-Gross cites reports that about $8 billion of GPUs may have gone to China through a Singapore syndicate. He says this makes the "Pax Silica" look porous. Blundin sees no succession plan and expects a long investigation. Mostaque asks why the story is coming out now, and expects it to lead to a push against Chinese open-source models in regulated industries.
- **Anthropic IPO and Fable 5.2.** Diamandis reports the IPO roadshow at $1.3-1.4 trillion and says about 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents formalised Fermat's last theorem in 30 hours. Wissner-Gross was more impressed by the robotics and product-design results than by the physics claims. He notes log-log extrapolations of autonomy time horizons that reach a vertical asymptote around summer 2027.
- **Output per kilowatt hour.** Diamandis's own modelling puts Anthropic at $4.39 of output per kWh against $6.30 for New York City workers, with the lines crossing in 2027.
- **Musk's robot economy.** In clips from the Baron conference, Musk says a terawatt-scale fab would need 30 times next year's global chip output, and that 20-30 million robots could match US output, probably rented. Wissner-Gross reads this as a move from selling products to selling subscriptions. Ismail doubts the five-times productivity figure.
- **Corporate form and robotics startups.** Delaware's draft "artificial intelligence company" would let an AI agent run a firm. Ismail urges caution, while Wissner-Gross expects the matter to become federal. On the shelf-stocking robot startup Theseus, Blundin and Wissner-Gross advise founders to go after high-value work, while Mostaque sees a niche for simpler robots.
