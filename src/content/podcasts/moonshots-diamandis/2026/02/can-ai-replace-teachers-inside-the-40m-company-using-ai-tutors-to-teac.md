## Thesis

Alpha School co-founder MacKenzie Price and principal Joe Liemandt tell Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail and Dave Blundin that learning science has long shown kids can learn much faster, but the teacher-in-front-of-a-classroom model blocks it. Their answer is AI-generated personalised lessons in a two-hour morning block, with adults recast as motivational "guides". Liemandt argues the biggest obstacle is not technology or students but what parents believe school should look like.

## Key points

- **Five pillars.** Liemandt and Price describe the model as: kids must love school, learn faster with AI tutors, spend afternoons on life-skills workshops, have guides focused on motivation, and grow character among good classmates. Liemandt says 40-60% of students would rather go to school than on vacation.
- **Results claimed.** Price says the average SAT score is 1410 across the high school and 1535 for seniors. Diamandis opens with US proficiency data (35% of 12th graders proficient in reading, 22% in math).
- **The Timeback platform.** Liemandt says Alpha has put over $100 million into its Timeback platform, plus third-party apps such as Math Academy. Lessons target 80-85% accuracy, and vision models watch the screen to flag guessing or distraction, at roughly $10,000 per student per year in AI tokens, a cost he wants to cut.
- **Guides, not teachers.** Guides start at six-figure salaries; Liemandt says 80,000 people applied. Each student gets a weekly 30-minute one-on-one. Chat functionality is off in the morning because, he says, unmanaged chatbots get used for cheating.
- **Mastery over time.** In a mastery-based system, Liemandt argues, success depends on effort rather than IQ. He says incoming A students ranged from one year ahead to three years behind.
- **Scaling.** Alpha owns all its schools, opens micro-schools of about 25 students, and plans cheaper models such as Texas Sports Academy. Liemandt says 10 charter applications produced one virtual approval, in Arizona. Liemandt wants a million-student randomised trial.
