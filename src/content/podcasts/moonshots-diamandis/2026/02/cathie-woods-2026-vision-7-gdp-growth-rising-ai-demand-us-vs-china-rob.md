## Thesis

Cathie Wood walks the Moonshot Mates through ARK Invest's Big Ideas 2026 report. Her central claim is that robotics, energy storage, AI, blockchain and multiomic sequencing are converging. In ARK's view this makes the step from 3% to 7% or more in global real GDP growth a conservative call, because price declines (Wright's law) set off much larger growth in units sold.

## Key points

- **The GDP step change.** Wood cites ARK research putting real growth at about 0.6% from 1500 to 1900 and about 3% over the following 125 years. Blundin relays the sceptical view he heard at Davos. Salim Ismail asks whether deflation shrinks GDP, and Wood answers that unit growth more than makes up for it. Wissner-Gross adds that work handed to agents could show up as new commerce.
- **Inflation and measurement.** Wood says Truflation already shows inflation at about 1.2% and expects it to fall below 2% within a year. She prefers gross national income to GDP because it is easier to measure.
- **AI economics.** Wood notes OpenAI's planned $60 ad pricing against about $20 at Facebook. Her analysts think Gemini can undercut it. Blundin argues that demand for inference is close to unlimited however cheap it gets. The hosts discuss the open-source agent Clawdbot, since renamed Moltbot.
- **US vs China.** Wood says the US pushed China into open source and that China now leads there, investing about 40% of GDP against just over 20% in the US. Ismail expects the US to win at the application layer.
- **Bitcoin.** ARK keeps its bull case of $1.5 million by 2030. Wood says stablecoins take $200,000-300,000 off the target, while gold's rise supports Bitcoin's role as "digital gold".
- **Robotaxis.** ARK estimates 24 million robotaxis could cover all US urban miles. It expects Tesla to beat Waymo on cost by 50% and eventually charge about 20 cents per mile, against Uber's $2.80.
