## Thesis

Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin and Alexander Wissner-Gross treat the coming Musk v. OpenAI trial as a contest over who governs frontier AI, at a moment when capital is concentrating in a few labs and Anthropic appears to be gaining on OpenAI. On jobs they disagree on timing more than direction: Blundin sees near-term white-collar displacement, while Wissner-Gross and Ismail expect net job creation through many more, smaller companies.

## Key points

- **xAI rebuild.** Diamandis notes eight founding engineers have left xAI ahead of a planned IPO. Wissner-Gross speculates that earlier Grok models were "benchmaxed", and reads xAI's plan to train models up to 10 trillion parameters as a sign that the parameter-scaling race has plateaued.
- **Musk v. OpenAI.** Musk is suing for $100 billion, with jury selection set for 27 April. Diamandis predicts a settlement in which Sam Altman steps down as CEO. Wissner-Gross is sympathetic to OpenAI's iterative governance changes; Blundin counters that converting a nonprofit this way is untested in case law.
- **Anthropic vs. OpenAI.** OpenAI raised $122 billion at an $852 billion valuation, but Diamandis cites secondary-market demand of $2 billion for Anthropic shares versus $600 million for OpenAI. Blundin calls OpenAI's cap table the most "screwed up" he has seen. Wissner-Gross stresses the value of US competition and OpenAI's 900 million users.
- **Capital concentration.** Diamandis says Q1 2026 AI venture investment hit $242 billion, 64% of it in four companies. Blundin argues startups are fine; large public companies will be sold to fund the AI IPOs.
- **Jobs and the social contract.** Blundin reports a panel estimate of about 99% that a random white-collar job could be done by AI within two years. Ismail sides with Marc Andreessen's optimism and expects four to five times more companies. Wissner-Gross rejects purely redistributive models in favour of AI-powered micro-entrepreneurs.
- **Science, robots, quantum.** The hosts also cover the OpenAI Foundation, Anthropic's $400 million purchase of Coefficient Bio, Chinese humanoid makers AgiBot and Unitree, and Google's 2029 quantum timeline.
