## Thesis

Recorded on stage at the 2026 Abundance360 Summit, this conversation has David Sinclair arguing that age reversal is close to its first human test. He compares the moment to the Wright brothers. Sinclair and Peter Diamandis say the next steps are cheaper delivery, pills rather than gene therapy, and faster private funding, so that the technology can reach far more people than the very wealthy.

## Key points

- **First human trial.** Sinclair says Life Biosciences, which the FDA recently cleared for the clinic, is recruiting patients for ER100. It delivers three Yamanaka genes (OSK) into the eye to treat blindness, and the liver is the likely next target. He says labs see benefits in every tissue they test in mice.
- **Tissue by tissue.** Sinclair explains that starting with one organ keeps regulators comfortable. Treating the whole body would need lipid nanoparticles or a small molecule rather than viral particles.
- **From gene therapy to a pill.** Diamandis puts AAV gene therapies at roughly half a million to two million dollars per treatment. Sinclair says his lab has used AI to screen billions of molecules in silico, has a three-molecule cocktail as proof of concept, and hopes to enter a human trial within months as part of the Healthspan XPRIZE. He would ultimately take a single molecule to market, ideally priced like metformin.
- **No fixed lifespan limit.** Sinclair says he sees no biological or physical reason for an upper limit, though he does not know yet how to get there. He also says he is not claiming humans can live forever.
- **Philanthropic funding model.** Diamandis says Friends of Sinclair Lab (about 70 members) raised around six million dollars a year after federal funding was cut. Sinclair says this freed him from spending half his time on grants and lets projects start within weeks.
- **Personal protocols.** Sinclair describes his own routine, including resveratrol, NMN, metformin or berberine, nattokinase, and cutting out alcohol. He presents these as his personal choices, not as recommendations.
