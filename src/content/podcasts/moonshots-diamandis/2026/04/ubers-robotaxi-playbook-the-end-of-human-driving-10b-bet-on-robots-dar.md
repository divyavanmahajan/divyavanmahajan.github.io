## Thesis

In an audience Q&A at the 2026 Abundance360 Summit, hosted by Peter Diamandis, Dara Khosrowshahi argues that autonomous vehicles will arrive gradually, augment more jobs than they remove, and need new insurance models. He also argues that large companies should grow bolder, not more conservative, as long as new bets "rhyme" with what they already do.

## Key points

- **Riders say yes.** Khosrowshahi says that in Atlanta and Austin about 80% of riders matched with an autonomous vehicle accept it, and regulators are the slower side. He tells a 13-year-old he should still get a licence in three years, but expects that in about 10 years people will not need to drive.
- **Machines are easier to dispatch.** Uber has roughly 9-10 million drivers and 40 million trips a day. Its dispatch predicts the next few seconds of demand, and Khosrowshahi expects robots to be more predictable than humans, with higher acceptance rates.
- **Insurance needs volume.** He expects layered cover: the autonomy provider insures its driver, Uber insures the rest. He says autonomous trips last year were under 1% of Uber's volume growth, too little for actuaries to price.
- **China and partners.** Khosrowshahi supports the earlier China exit, notes Uber's stake in Didi, and lists Chinese partners BYD, Pony, WeRide and possibly Baidu.
- **Jobs and labour.** He says automation usually automates 20-30% of tasks rather than whole jobs, but admits AI is moving faster than he expected. He targets 20 million people on Uber's platform by 2035, up from 10 million, and expects few combustion engines in 20 years.
- **Culture and bets.** He describes the value "do the right thing, period" and says Uber, now expecting $10 billion in cash flow, should take big bets in adjacencies such as Eats, Freight and Uber AI Solutions.
