## Thesis

Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin, Alexander Wissner-Gross and Emad Mostaque argue that the October sell-off in neoclouds reflects bad debt deals rather than weak AI demand. Their view is that the AI economy is splitting into winners with cash flow and losers that borrowed to rent out chips. They also debate whether Anthropic's planned IPO and its governance structure fit a company of that scale.

## Key points

- **Neocloud sell-off.** Diamandis cites CoreWeave and Nebius down 30% and Iren down 40%, and Elon Musk's prediction of "guppy level death" among about 200 neoclouds. Blundin compares it to the dark-fibre bust and says compute is sold out for years. In his view only operators borrowing at around 15% are at risk, while Musk borrows at 8%.
- **Demand-shock risk.** Wissner-Gross says the likelier bubble trigger is a demand shortfall, or a jump in efficiency through recursive self-improvement that leaves hardware idle. Mostaque puts direct data-centre debt at about $500 billion and says he would avoid RAM stocks (not financial advice).
- **Anthropic IPO.** Diamandis reports a filing under ticker ANT, a planned $100 billion raise at a $1.5-2 trillion valuation, H1 2026 revenue of $16.3 billion and over $500 billion in compute commitments. Blundin calls it correctly priced. Mostaque argues Anthropic should have stayed private.
- **Long-term benefit trust.** Ismail praises the trust's power to appoint most of the board as a way of building the mission into governance. Wissner-Gross worries it could turn into ideological control by trustees who are not economically aligned with shareholders.
- **Kimi K3.1 Pro.** Diamandis says the Moonshot AI model ranks fourth on the Artificial Analysis index, and that Ramez Naam estimates open models are about five weeks behind the US frontier. Mostaque attributes much of this to distillation. Blundin says the Chinese labs have also innovated on their own.
- **Chain theory.** Wissner-Gross presents his framework for rebuilding physics from entangled finite quantum systems and says it derives the fine structure constant with no tuned inputs. He calls it early but falsifiable through future measurements.
