## Thesis

Recorded after XPRIZE Visioneering, Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Emad Mostaque and XPRIZE CEO Anousheh Ansari argue that you get what you incentivise. The group says this applies to prize-driven innovation, to how AI labs train and align models, and to how people will find purpose once AI and robots do most of the work.

## Key points

- **Flying Roadster.** Diamandis describes Tesla's unveiling in Waco of a Roadster that hops on compressed-air thrusters, with Elon Musk claiming production in 2028. Ismail says regulators are left behind, calling Musk "two generations ahead", while others in the group ask who would insure it.
- **Robot brains getting cheaper.** Diamandis cites a RoboCurve benchmark in which the cost for a frontier model to complete a robot subtask fell from $2.36 to $0.38 between July and September. Mostaque reads this as generalist models beating specialist ones, so hardware and the supply chain become the constraint.
- **AI physics.** The hosts discuss a test of 50 unpublished physics problems that GPT-6 Astra reportedly solved for about $3 each. Mostaque says AI now beats humans in verifiable domains. Ismail gives a bank KYC pilot as an example, where 22-step workflows dropped to four steps.
- **Prizes as leverage.** Diamandis traces incentive prizes from the 1714 longitude prize to the Ansari XPRIZE. Ansari says XPRIZE has run 36 prizes, that teams have drawn about $12 billion in investment, and that each prize dollar creates about 62 times its value. New prizes include Habitable Worlds and organ biostasis.
- **Alignment by incentive and curriculum.** Ismail argues humans are not aligned with each other in the first place. Mostaque argues for deliberate pre-training data and curriculum learning rather than scaling on everything. One of the guests warns that win-at-all-costs rewards and military uses undermine alignment. Diamandis suggests insurance-backed certification on the model of Underwriters Laboratories.
- **Purpose after work.** Ismail cites a figure that 79% of corporate workers are dissatisfied and says people will shift from doing to being. Ansari pushes back that AI may outdo people even at the work they love. Mostaque answers that meaning comes from community.
