## Thesis

Peter Diamandis, Dave Blundin, Alexander Wissner-Gross and guest Emad Mostaque argue that AI is moving into a phase of trillion-dollar listings and national-security policy, with frontier labs now funding an economy of their own. They criticise proposals to tax or slow AI, including teacher-union plans, a mathematicians' declaration and Bernie Sanders' wealth-fund bill, as being on the wrong side of history. They do back regulation of DNA synthesis.

## Key points

- **Executive order.** The order asks labs to share new models with the government voluntarily, 30 days before release. Wissner-Gross links it to the "Mythos moment", when private models took on vulnerability discovery that used to be the NSA's job. Blundin calls it the right temporary choice but not a long-term fix.
- **Scale and distribution.** Diamandis cites ChatGPT at one billion monthly users and Claude at 56 million, growing 640% a year. Mostaque expects the race to move to the personal agent that coordinates all the others, a personal "Jarvis".
- **Shrinking generality.** Wissner-Gross reads OpenAI's Rosalind biodefence tools and its cyber models as capabilities being carved out of public models for trusted users. Diamandis says he co-signed a letter asking Congress to require screening of DNA synthesis orders.
- **Anthropic IPO.** Polymarket gives a 60% chance of a first-day value above $1.8 trillion. Blundin warns against getting numb to the money flow. Mostaque guesses revenue at $50-60 billion. Diamandis cites about $9.4 million of revenue per employee.
- **Microsoft and Nvidia.** Wissner-Gross calls Microsoft's seven in-house models mid-tier. Mostaque puts them at the level of a good Chinese lab. On Nvidia's N1X laptop chip, the guesses range from direct consumer data to access to TSMC capacity to owning home AI.
- **Jobs and wealth.** Blundin says employment across his companies has doubled, and Apollo's Torsten Slok calls AI a net job creator. Blundin dismisses the 50% stock levy as unworkable. Mostaque would rather put lab shares into children's investment accounts.
