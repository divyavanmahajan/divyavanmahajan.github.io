## Thesis

In an unscheduled episode, Peter Diamandis and the Mates argue that Anthropic's paper "When AI Builds Itself" (by Marina Favaro and Jack Clark) shows AI is already largely building its successors. They doubt that a global pause is realistic, and see the bigger issues in governance: government equity stakes in frontier labs, and countries such as Argentina competing to host AI agents as legal entities.

## Key points

- **Anthropic's numbers.** Diamandis summarises the paper's claims: more than 80% of merged code is written by Claude, engineers ship 8x more code per quarter, and task horizons have grown from four minutes to 12 hours, with week-long tasks projected by end of 2027.
- **Soft takeoff, not a step.** Wissner-Gross expects a takeoff that feels smooth up close, says research taste is the last human role and can be automated too, and predicts that limits on algorithmic progress would push the effort into hardware and energy buildout.
- **Pause as an option.** Blundin says the paper is asking for the ability to slow down, comparing it to Cold War arms deals, and sees Anthropic as neutral enough to broker it. Ismail doubts any lab will slow down unilaterally. Wissner-Gross suggests government "golden share" stakes in IPO-bound labs as a way to coordinate them.
- **Argentina's AI haven.** The Mates welcome President Javier Milei's Financial Times op-ed, which proposes no AI regulation, a "nonhuman corporation" legal category and low taxes. Ismail likens it to the limited liability company. Wissner-Gross calls it a large step for AI personhood.
- **Jobs and markets.** May added 172,000 jobs against an expected 85,000, yet the NASDAQ fell 4.18%. Blundin uses Amdahl's law to argue that automation moves bottlenecks and creates jobs. Wissner-Gross calls the sell-off a "nothing burger".
- **Midyear predictions.** These include AI solving major problems in maths and physics, partial nationalisation of frontier labs (Wissner-Gross), agent permissions becoming the bottleneck (Ismail), legacy enterprises rushing to become AI-native (Blundin), and a Tesla-SpaceX merger (Diamandis).

## Quotes
