## Thesis

Peter Diamandis and the Moonshot Mates argue that Google has recovered from being written off and now competes across the whole AI stack, from TPUs to products with billions of users. Alexander Wissner-Gross and Dave Blundin add that most of the I/O launches follow others rather than lead, and that Google's edge is distribution. In the second half, Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman tells the story behind the company's IPO and says inference speed is now what customers buy.

## Key points

- **Google's numbers.** Sundar Pichai's keynote cited 3.2 quadrillion tokens a month, 900 million monthly Gemini app users, and capex of about $180-190 billion, roughly six times the 2022 level. Blundin notes that the stock rose despite that spending.
- **Mid releases, strong defaults.** Wissner-Gross praises Gemini Omni as the only big American lab still chasing multimodality. He calls Gemini 3.5 Flash, Antigravity 2.0 and the always-on agent Gemini Spark copies of what Cursor and OpenClaw already did. Blundin counters that a one-click agent beside Google Search will reach far more people, much as Microsoft once used bundling.
- **Trust layer.** Wissner-Gross and Salim Ismail see OpenAI adopting Google's SynthID watermarking as a step towards end-to-end proof of what is real, and Blundin reads it as early industry self-regulation.
- **Karpathy and Musk v. OpenAI.** The hosts read Andrej Karpathy's move to Anthropic's pre-training team as proof that research outside the big labs is falling behind. Feldman calls the jury verdict against Musk a distraction.
- **Cerebras' bet.** Feldman says Cerebras filled a dinner-plate-sized chip with fast SRAM. He claims it runs 15-20 times faster than GPUs on inference. Early sales were 12 systems, and demand only came in late 2024 and 2025, followed by an OpenAI deal worth over $20 billion and an AWS term sheet.
- **Fabs and space.** Feldman expects Musk's Terafab to take 15-20 years, wants US packaging capacity rebuilt along with fabs, and puts orbital data centres close to a decade away.
