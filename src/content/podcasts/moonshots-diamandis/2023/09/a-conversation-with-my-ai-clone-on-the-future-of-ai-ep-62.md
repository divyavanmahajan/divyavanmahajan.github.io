## Thesis

Peter Diamandis interviews "PeterBot", an AI version of himself that his head of AI, Steve Brown, built from Diamandis's books and blogs. Diamandis says the voice exchange is unscripted. He asks it about common public fears of AI, and the bot keeps returning to an optimistic answer: risks are real but manageable through education, safeguards and collaboration. Diamandis ends by saying he agrees with much of it but that the topic needs more digging.

## Key points

- **Jobs.** PeterBot argues that new technologies have historically created more jobs than they destroyed. It says AI will augment screenwriters, artists and physicians rather than replace them, and that schools should teach critical thinking, creativity, emotional intelligence and entrepreneurship. Diamandis, a father of two 12-year-olds, says he thinks AGI is likely by the time they reach college.
- **Deepfakes and the 2024 election.** Diamandis cites Mo Gawdat calling the 2024 US elections "patient zero" for a post-truth world. Asked for a scenario, the bot invents "Sarah", a young politician damaged by fabricated videos.
- **Speed of government.** Diamandis worries that democracies react too slowly to exponential technology. PeterBot suggests public pressure and agile solutions, and for individuals, fact-checking before sharing.
- **Training data and alignment.** Diamandis says Mo Gawdat and Emad Mostaque believe the content AIs learn from is critical. The bot agrees that curated data can help alignment, while noting that values differ widely across 8 billion people.
- **Timelines.** Diamandis cites Ray Kurzweil predicting AGI by 2029 and Elon Musk by 2025. PeterBot calls those timelines hard to predict and urges caution. Diamandis credits ElevenLabs for the bot's voice.
