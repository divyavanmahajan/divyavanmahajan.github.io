## Thesis

In an audience Q&A at Abundance360, Tony Robbins argues that both lives and history move in roughly 20-year seasons. Drawing on Strauss and Howe's books "Generations" and "The Fourth Turning", he says society is about halfway through a hard "winter" that will produce a stronger generation. His advice to would-be moonshot builders: learn to recognise patterns, avoid demonising opponents, and commit only once a specific goal has gripped you.

## Key points

- **The end of demonisation.** Robbins tells how he flew Mikhail Gorbachev to a meeting in a chartered jet and asked him what ended the Cold War. Gorbachev's answer was "the end of demonization", and he described Ronald Reagan restarting a heated argument with a fresh introduction. Robbins calls himself a political independent who has worked with both parties.
- **Three skills.** Robbins says progress comes from recognising patterns, using them and finally creating them. He cites studies predicting that 40-50% of jobs will go to robots, algorithms and AI within 5 to 15 years, and says these skills are what will give young people security.
- **Seasons of life and history.** He describes life as spring (0-21), summer (22-42), fall (43-63) and an elder winter after that. Using the generation that lived through the Depression and the Second World War as his example, he argues that hardship builds strength. He estimates the current winter has about eight years left.
- **Learned helplessness.** Responding to a counsellor working with anxious students, Robbins describes problems that feel permanent, pervasive and personal. He blames overprotective parenting for part of it.
- **Picking a moonshot.** He tells an aerospace engineer weighing a move into AI or quantum computing not to decide while uncertain. His advice is to get into the right environments until something specific grabs him, set 10-year goals and break them into one-year chunks.
- **Scale and feeding.** Robbins mentions his 100 billion meals goal and a Feeding America tracking program he says could save about a billion meals. He also says he trained around a million and a half people online at once during the pandemic.
