## Thesis

Recorded at Abundance360 in March 2023, with Peter Diamandis and Keith Ferrazzi interviewing, will.i.am presents AI as a creative partner that will free hyper-creative people while making many routine jobs obsolete. He argues that people must own their likeness and voice before AI can safely imitate them. He also says that funding children's education, especially in inner cities, matters more now than ever.

## Key points

- **FYI, a creative workspace.** will.i.am describes FYI as a single interface for creative teams. It combines messaging, file storage, digital asset management, calendar and conferencing with encryption keys, and puts several AIs into group conversations. Diamandis says it runs on GPT-4, which will.i.am got early access to.
- **Ownership of identity.** He says nobody owns their facial data or vocal timbre today, citing Beyoncé and Leonardo DiCaprio, and argues that "clone" is the wrong word until regulation protects identity.
- **Jobs and creativity.** He predicts bus, truck and delivery drivers, legal assistants and finance managers will lose their jobs within about ten years. He argues AI will give tireless idea-generators a sparring partner and push humans to be more human.
- **Intellectual dysmorphia.** Comparing AI to the effect of photo filters on body image, he worries people will feel inadequate next to AI and let AI versions of themselves speak for them. Diamandis says they may have coined the term.
- **Bias and inclusion.** He argues algorithmic bias can only be fixed by more diverse participation, which is why his i.am Angel Foundation backs FIRST robotics. He says it grew from three teams to 400 LA schools. His College Track commitment grew from 65 to 12,000 students.
- **No "fail fast" for dreamers.** He rejects fail-fast thinking as an investor's mindset and says 12-year commitments are realistic. He also says he would never get a brain-computer interface.
