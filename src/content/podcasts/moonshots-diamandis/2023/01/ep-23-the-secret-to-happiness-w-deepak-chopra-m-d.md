## Thesis

Deepak Chopra argues that abundance comes from consciousness, not material circumstance, and that most of a person's happiness depends on conditioning and daily choices that can be changed. Peter Diamandis defines abundance as technology turning scarce resources into plentiful ones. The two agree that collective creativity and a mindset of possibility matter more than luxury.

## Key points

- **The happiness equation.** Chopra describes H = S + C + V, which a group of social scientists put together after the Dalai Lama asked for a happiness formula. He says the brain's set point accounts for about 50% of daily happiness, conditions of living (mainly money) for 10-12%, and choices about pleasure, meaning and making others happy for the remaining 40%. He says reflective inquiry and meditation can shift the set point.
- **Money as a tool of consciousness.** Chopra says lottery winners return to their set point within about a year and warns against confusing self-worth with net worth. He sees money as a story about exchange that helps when it goes toward purpose, family or easing suffering.
- **Diversity solves problems.** Chopra says maximum creative diversity plus a shared vision can solve almost any problem. He points to tumour-board style case reviews and lists nine steps of creativity. Diamandis links this to crowdsourcing enabled by technology. Chopra also mentions an emotional AI chatbot his team built to help prevent suicide.
- **Tuning out the news.** Both say they have stopped watching the news. Chopra calls it opinion and melodrama that follow the money, and Diamandis calls CNN "the crisis News Network".
- **Longevity mindset.** Chopra, 75, says longevity starts with intention, followed by sleep, stress management, vagal stimulation, yoga, breathing and a varied plant-based diet. He argues that longevity science is too reductionist. Diamandis mentions an age-reversal XPRIZE he is developing with David Sinclair and George Church.
- **Chopra's XPRIZE.** Asked which prize he would fund, Chopra proposes a global project to catalogue and preserve the planet's genetic information and microbiome, using AI to map how ecosystems connect.
