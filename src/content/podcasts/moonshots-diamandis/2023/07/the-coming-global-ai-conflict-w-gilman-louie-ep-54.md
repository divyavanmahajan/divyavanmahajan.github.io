## Thesis

Gilman Louie co-founded In-Q-Tel, the CIA's venture arm, and is CEO of America's Frontier Fund. Speaking on stage at Abundance360 in March 2023, he tells Peter Diamandis that the US and China see AI as a competitive race. He argues the aim should be to lead, not to "win". The real stakes are which platforms and which cultural biases get built into AI, so he says governments should speed up rather than call a moratorium.

## Key points

- **China's head start.** Louie says China set a national goal to lead in AI by 2030, and that AlphaGo was its Sputnik moment. He calls ChatGPT America's "AI moment".
- **Governments struggle with nonlinear change.** In Louie's telling, Brussels wants to regulate, the US relies on tort law, and China worries about outputs that stray from the party line. He says all three are wrong. He compares AI to a nine-year-old that needs training, not killing, and says Ukraine shows "dumber is deadly".
- **Bias is unavoidable.** Whoever builds the datasets and models builds in bias. Louie says the question is whether systems end up fundamentally Chinese, American or European. He calls hallucination a form of creativity and gives the example of an AI listing a presidential medal he never received.
- **Policy momentum.** Louie says a 15-member commission produced a 750-page report that led to more than $250 billion in spending. He adds that the US will spend $1.6 trillion on technologies, a level he compares to Apollo. He urges AI companies to self-regulate, as biologists did at Asilomar, and not to impose US-centric values abroad.
- **Brain-computer interfaces.** Louie says the military is interested in BCI for situations like a pilot pulling 9 Gs, where moving the hands is hard.
- **America's Frontier Fund.** Louie describes a $500 million fund, much of the money coming from states and pension funds. It focuses on company building and on regions beyond the coasts, such as Purdue, Georgia Tech, Austin and Ohio. He notes that five cities produced 90% of US innovation jobs over the last decade.
