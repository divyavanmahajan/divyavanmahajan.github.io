## Thesis

Andrew Yang, founder of the Forward Party, describes two moonshots: fixing American democracy first, then using universal basic income to reduce poverty and the fear that comes with automation. He argues that politicians are poorly prepared for AI-generated disinformation. In his view, the root problem is an incentive structure that rewards pleasing primary voters. Peter Diamandis adds his view that AI will make the basics of life much cheaper.

## Key points

- **AI and the 2024 election.** Yang warns that when video, text and images cost nothing to fake, elections get flooded. He cites a fake image of the Pentagon on fire that moved markets, and expects the US to react only after "one disaster". Diamandis repeats Mo Gawdat's description of elections as AI's "patient zero".
- **Broken incentives.** Yang says members of Congress spend 30-60% of their time fundraising, incumbents are re-elected 94% of the time, and most only need to satisfy the 10-14% of voters who take part in primaries.
- **Open primaries.** Yang points to Alaska's all-candidate primary plus ranked-choice voting (a $6 million ballot initiative) and a similar measure in Nevada ($17 million, 53% support). He estimates about $200 million across 10 states could change national politics, crediting Michael Porter and Katherine Gehl's "The Politics Industry".
- **A 2028 independent primary.** Yang sketches a smartphone primary verified by a mailed QR code. He puts nationwide ballot access at about $50 million and says Forward is recognised in about eight states.
- **The evidence on cash transfers.** Yang cites the enhanced child tax credit and the Eastern Band of Cherokee casino dividend ($4,000-6,000 a year). He proposes a data dividend and AI taxation, noting that 2 million Americans work in call centres.
- **Regulation and purpose.** Yang expects professions with lobbies to resist automation and cites Dean Kamen's portable dialysis machine as an example. He argues that idle people, especially men, need structure and community as well as money.
