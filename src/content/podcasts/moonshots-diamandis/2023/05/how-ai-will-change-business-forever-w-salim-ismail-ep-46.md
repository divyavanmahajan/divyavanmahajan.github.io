## Thesis

Peter Diamandis and Salim Ismail, co-authors of Exponential Organizations 2.0, treat "AI and algorithms" as one of the external attributes of an exponential organization (ExO) and argue it has become non-negotiable. Diamandis says there will be only two kinds of companies by the end of the decade: those fully using AI and those out of business. Ismail adds that AI plus a human will beat either alone, and that automation tends to expand work rather than eliminate it.

## Key points

- **Why now.** Diamandis lists four drivers: cheap cloud computation and Nvidia chips, data doubling roughly every two years (heading to 175 zettabytes), a claimed 99.5% drop in the cost of training AI over five years, and large capital flows ($160 billion of corporate AI investment in 2021, by his figure).
- **Medicine and law.** Diamandis notes ChatGPT passed the US medical licensing exam two months after launch and predicts it will become malpractice to diagnose without an AI co-pilot. Ismail cites 2,500 cancer papers published a day.
- **Jobs.** Ismail argues the "robots will take all jobs in five years" headline dates back to 1964. He points to ATMs: branch costs fell 10x, banks opened 10 times more branches, and teller numbers held steady.
- **Tiny companies.** Ismail sketches a three-person billion-dollar startup (CEO, product lead, operations lead plus AI bots) and estimates 10,000 startups are being built on that premise. Diamandis says the marginal cost of starting a business is approaching zero.
- **Practical advice.** Diamandis urges every company to appoint a chief AI officer, someone who understands the platforms rather than one who codes models. Ismail suggests feeding your MTP into a generative AI to find white spaces, and keeping a chatbot open in board meetings.
- **Data as the moat.** Citing a leaked Google memo that says generative AI has "no moat", Diamandis argues a company's unique data is its edge. Ismail says some firms have raised market cap four to five times by spinning data and AI into a separate entity.
