## Thesis

Rick Smith, founder and CEO of Axon, tells Peter Diamandis that Axon's moonshot is to cut gun-related deaths between police and the public by 50% by 2032. Smith's starting point is that police use lethal force because it is reliable, not because it kills. In his view, a non-lethal tool that is more effective than a gun would be adopted on performance alone, the way people choose electric cars.

## Key points

- **The founding story.** Smith started the company in 1993 after two friends were shot dead. He partnered with Jack Cover, the original TASER inventor, in Cover's garage. He says the first two products failed, the company nearly bankrupted his father, and the first outside money came only at the IPO, after pitches to roughly 300 to 500 investors.
- **Science before product.** A $2,500 pig experiment in 1996 showed that early devices were built on assumptions rather than measurements. Smith says that work led to more than 100 patents and the pivot from consumers to police.
- **The moonshot's numbers.** Smith says the toll is a little over 1,100 deaths a year and rising about 3% annually. After reviewing 500 use-of-force incidents, Axon estimates that 40-70% could be avoided, with the upper end requiring drones and robotics. The goal counts officers killed as well as civilians, which Smith says won support from police unions and civil rights advocates.
- **TASER 10 and VR training.** The device fires up to 10 darts to 45 feet and automatically picks the most widely spaced pair. Smith says VR makes training cheap compared with roughly $30 per live shot.
- **Drones and ethics.** Smith describes school-safety and home-defence drones, and cites a survey in which 70% of Americans supported drones or robots to stop mass shooters. He insists that a human, not an algorithm, must make any decision to use force.
- **Leadership lessons.** Smith recommends hiring people smarter than yourself and firing fast. He credits Abundance360 with helping Axon grow from a $200 million TASER company to a $16 billion tech company.
