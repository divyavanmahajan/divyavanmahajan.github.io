## Thesis

This bonus episode is the closing segment of Peter Diamandis's Abundance360 summit in 2023. He takes the audience 100 years back, to 1923, and then 10 years forward, to 2033. His argument is that the pace of change is speeding up, that the breakthroughs his faculty predict will create trillion-dollar companies, and that entrepreneurs and leaders have a responsibility to steer that change on purpose rather than let it happen to them.

## Key points

- **The world of 1923.** Diamandis lists the context: 13 million cars on the road (about 300 million today), a $290 Ford Model T, 40% of homes with electricity, a world population of 2 billion and male life expectancy of 56.
- **Innovation is accelerating.** His team counted six notable inventions in 1922 and twelve in 1923. The 1923 list includes the electric shaver, Clarence Birdseye's frozen food, the three-position traffic signal and the mass production of insulin. He compares electrifying everything manual then with adding AI to everything now.
- **Ten headlines for 2033.** Diamandis reads predictions credited to his faculty. They include free AI tutoring for every child in their native language (credited to Sal Khan), universal basic income funded by taxing AI and robots (Andrew Yang), human-level AI as a cloud service (Ray Kurzweil), more than 50 million humanoid robots in the US (Elon Musk), clinical age reversal (David Sinclair) and three companies reaching commercial-scale fusion.
- **Business framing.** He presents these as the basis for trillion-dollar industries and asks the audience to use them to shape their massive transformative purpose (MTP) and their moonshots.
- **Steering the future.** Diamandis says Singularity University and Abundance360 exist to build a community that guides technology on purpose. He argues that individuals now have tools greater than those of the wealthiest people of 10 to 30 years ago.
