## Thesis

Speaking at Abundance360, Khan Academy founder Sal Khan argues that personalised tutoring has always been the gold standard in education but was never affordable. In his view, GPT-4 finally makes it practical. He describes Khanmigo, built with early GPT-4 access, as a tutor for every student and an assistant for every teacher. He says most of what is left to do is engineering, not research.

## Key points

- **Early GPT-4 access.** Khan says OpenAI approached Khan Academy in late July or early August 2022. It wanted Khan Academy's exam items to benchmark the model and wanted launch partners with positive social uses. Seeing GPT-4 explain an AP Biology answer convinced him.
- **Private reasoning.** Khan says the model often praised wrong student work. Letting it work out the problem first in hidden private thoughts and then compare that with the student's answer raised math accuracy three- to four-fold, by his account.
- **Roadmap.** He calls voice, persistent memory and immersive settings (such as touring ancient Greece with an AI Aristotle, which Diamandis proposes) engineering work. He also plans AI-facilitated debates between students.
- **Scale and impact.** Khan cites about 150 million registered users, 50+ languages, and learning minutes that roughly tripled during the pandemic, from about 30 million to 90 million a day. He says formal district partnerships grew from nine to about 500, and that pilots show 30-60 minutes a week accelerating students 30-50%.
- **Credentials.** He tells the story of Sultana, who taught herself on Khan Academy under the Taliban and was admitted to Arizona State University on an SAT score alone. He says Khan Academy is working on turning learning into credit and jobs.
- **Teachers.** Khan sees AI as an army of teaching assistants that handles grading, pairing and custom lessons, leaving teachers free for human connection and experiential learning.
