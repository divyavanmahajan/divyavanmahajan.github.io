## Thesis

Recorded on stage at Peter Diamandis's Abundance360 summit, this conversation has Emad Mostaque, founder and CEO of Stability AI, arguing that generative AI has learned "how to make human scale". In his view the winning approach is open, specialised models that people, companies and countries own and run themselves, not one closed general model. He expects the result to be the most disruptive change society has seen, and he frames it as a reason for hope rather than fear.

## Key points

- **Open, owned models.** Mostaque says Stability builds models across audio, video, protein folding, DNA, chemistry and language, plus sector models (a "banker GPT") and national models for India, Indonesia and Japan. He argues firms will never send internal data to ChatGPT, and he cites 25 million downloads of GPT-J and NeoX.
- **Compression and speed.** He says Stable Diffusion compressed 100 terabytes of images into a 2 GB file, and that four of the top 10 App Store apps in December were built on it. He claims a 100x speed-up since release, to 60 images per second, and expects ChatGPT-class models to run on phones without internet by the end of the next year.
- **Work disruption.** Mostaque claims 41% of code on GitHub is AI-generated and predicts "no programmers in five years". He describes a Big Four accounting firm planning to spend $300 million over two quarters on the question of whether it needs as many auditors.
- **Advice to leaders.** Diamandis suggests a chief AI officer. Mostaque says companies should look for passion over credentials, pointing to a former Amazon warehouse worker who now develops for Stability.
- **Education moonshot.** Mostaque says his tablet programme teaches 76% of children in refugee camps literacy and numeracy in 13 months at one hour a day. He describes plans to give every child in Malawi their own AI, funded through a World Bank-backed outcome bond.
- **Open as a human right.** He calls AI "the great equalizer or it's the great controller", and says this is why he argues it must not be controlled by any single entity.
