## Thesis

Peter Diamandis and Gary Brecka, co-founder of 10X Health System and host of The Ultimate Human podcast, walk through the home health-tech devices they use daily. Both argue that a wave of affordable devices, sensors and data is already available to consumers, and that AI will soon pull it together into a personal health assistant. Diamandis calls this the "golden age of healthspan". The health claims in the episode are the speakers' own views, not established findings.

## Key points

- **Cheap versus premium devices.** Brecka ranks hydrogen water tablets (which he says cost under a dollar a day) and a hydrogen bath generator (about $7,000) alongside red light beds that he says can cost over $100,000. He stresses that cheaper alternatives such as sunlight, clinic memberships or panels exist for most devices.
- **Methylation as the data problem.** Brecka argues that common genetic variants such as MTHFR affect how people process nutrients, and that symptoms often blamed on ageing come from "missing raw material". He says AI is needed to make sense of the volume of metabolic activity involved.
- **AI as the integrator.** Diamandis imagines a "Jarvis" that reads body sensors and recommends interventions. He argues that people who say lifespans can't be extended echo those who said humans would never fly.
- **Wearables and real-world data.** Diamandis, an early Oura investor, says he uses an Oura ring, a CGM and an Eight Sleep mattress mainly to gamify sleep and diet. Brecka argues that opt-in studies of thousands of Oura or Whoop users in their own homes can give better data than small lab sleep studies. Diamandis expects subdermal implantables next and says his venture fund has invested in several.
- **The device catalogue.** The pair cover saunas, cold plunges, hyperbaric chambers (Brecka is building a podcast studio inside one), ozone therapy, lymphatic compression, PEMF mats and an oral irrigation device called Proclaim.
- **Blood filtration.** Both back ExThera's filter, originally from DARPA, which Diamandis says will appear at his Abundance Summit as part of a shift from diagnostics to therapeutics.
