## Thesis

In a Thanksgiving-week episode (Dave Blundin was absent), Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Alexander Wissner-Gross and guest Emad Mostaque argue that AI is turning science and software into compute problems. Wissner-Gross treats the US Genesis Mission as a "1939 moment" and Claude Opus 4.5 as evidence that labs are close to recursive self-improvement. The panel then debates how fast the economic effects will arrive and how society should absorb job losses.

## Key points

- **Genesis Mission.** Diamandis describes the executive order directing the Department of Energy to connect US supercomputers and federal lab data into one platform. Wissner-Gross likens it to the Manhattan Project. Mostaque says its impact depends on whether the science stays open or sits behind IP-protected public-private partnerships.
- **Claude Opus 4.5.** Wissner-Gross points to Anthropic's statement that the model outperforms incoming performance-team candidates on take-home tests. Mostaque says it scored 52% on SWE-bench Pro without reasoning tokens, against 45% for his Intelligent Internet framework, and that it scores higher when orchestrating Haiku or Sonnet sub-agents.
- **Specialist agents.** Wissner-Gross reads ChatGPT's shopping research as OpenAI's third vertical agent and expects more for finance, medicine and consulting. Mostaque cites Andy Jassy's claim that Amazon's Rufus could add $10 billion in incremental sales.
- **Compute buildout.** The hosts cover Google's Ironwood TPU, Amazon's planned $50 billion of government AI infrastructure (1.3 gigawatts) and the 2.2-gigawatt Indiana campus running Trainium 2 chips for Anthropic. Mostaque calls Trainium about a generation behind and better suited to inference than training.
- **Bold timelines.** Mostaque repeats his prediction that most keyboard-and-mouse work will have negative economic value within about 900 days. Wissner-Gross predicts a full high-resolution human connectome within five years and notes Paradromics' claimed 200 bits per second for its brain implant.
- **Transition policy.** Ismail argues for UBI or universal basic services. Mostaque proposes AI "social scientists" for coordination plus a personal AI for every citizen. Wissner-Gross says the priority is growing the economy faster than AI erodes the value of labour.
