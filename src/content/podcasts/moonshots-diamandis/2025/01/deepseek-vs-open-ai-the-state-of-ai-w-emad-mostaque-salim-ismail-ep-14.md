## Thesis

Emad Mostaque, founder of Intelligent Internet and former CEO of Stability AI, tells Peter Diamandis and Salim Ismail that DeepSeek's shock was expected: an engineering-driven team, forced by chip limits to be efficient, matched frontier models at a fraction of the cost. He argues that cheaper intelligence should raise demand rather than hurt OpenAI or Nvidia. In his view the real issues are mass displacement of screen-based work, a crisis of meaning, and the need for open, publicly available AI.

## Key points

- **Why R1 landed.** Mostaque says DeepSeek V3, which he links to the roughly $6 million training-cost figure, matched GPT-4o in December. He thinks R1 caught on because it was open, ran on laptops in smaller versions, and showed its chain of thought, which OpenAI's o1 hides. He puts R1 at about 96% cheaper than o1.
- **Constraints as a driver.** Mostaque describes low-level PTX code, H800 chips with reduced interconnect, and a mixture-of-experts design that scales on memory. He guesses DeepSeek has about 10,000 chips in total and doubts that it simply distilled OpenAI's models, as David Sacks claimed.
- **Market impact.** Ismail and Mostaque expect overall demand for AI to absorb the shock to Nvidia. Mostaque argues that falling costs help OpenAI, which he says lost $5 billion last year, $3 billion of it on training.
- **Labour disruption.** Mostaque names business process outsourcing as the worst-case casualty for 2025 and advises remote workers to return to the office. Ismail expects a splintering, with countries such as India building their own models.
- **Safety.** Ismail sees no way to regulate AI line by line. Mostaque argues that the only defence is well-aligned open models offered as public infrastructure, and he worries that the race between labs leads to cutting corners.
- **Intelligent Internet.** Mostaque describes "universal basic AI": open datasets and models for areas such as cancer, autism and education, funded by a compute-backed digital currency whose details are still to come.
