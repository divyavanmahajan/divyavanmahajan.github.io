## Thesis

Peter Diamandis and Salim Ismail treat the start of 2025 as the moment a tech-friendly administration meets fast-moving technology. Both argue that AI should be used to simplify regulation and policy. They see launch costs, AI models, new materials and robots all compounding at once, though they disagree on energy and on robot design and timelines.

## Key points

- **Predictions.** Ismail predicts negotiated deals on major geopolitical conflicts, a crypto-based basic-income scheme, and a temporary housing-code change to speed rebuilding in LA. Diamandis predicts a push to the Moon and Mars, biotech deregulation alongside tighter food regulation, energy expansion, AI-assisted regulatory review and a Bitcoin reserve.
- **Space race.** Diamandis celebrates Starship's seventh flight, with the booster caught by the tower and the upper stage lost, and Blue Origin's New Glenn launch. He frames SpaceX and Blue Origin as squeezing out older launch providers.
- **AI platforms.** The hosts discuss Nvidia's Cosmos platform for robots and vehicles, and Project Digits, a $3,000 desktop AI supercomputer. They also cover Sam Altman's claims of 300 million weekly ChatGPT users and confidence in how to build AGI. Ismail asks that people define AGI before debating it. The hosts also pitch AI for drafting a "minimum viable policy".
- **Energy.** Ismail argues that solar plus storage may make fusion unnecessary. Diamandis backs fourth-generation small modular reactors and AI-accelerated fusion.
- **Materials and Bitcoin.** Ismail calls Microsoft's MatterGen the AlphaFold of materials science. Diamandis says MicroStrategy returned 693% against Bitcoin's 144% over a year. Ismail says a proposed $20 billion US reserve should be half a trillion.
- **Robots.** Diamandis highlights Unitree's $6,000 G1 and Elon Musk's target of 50,000 Optimus robots in 2026. Ismail questions humanoid form factors and doubts the timelines. The episode closes with Ismail describing his 97-year-old father's assisted death in Canada as a peaceful experience.
