## Thesis

Speaking with Peter Diamandis at the 2025 Abundance Summit, Anduril founder Palmer Luckey argues that the US defence industry is slowed by cost-plus contracting. In his view, the fix is a "defense product company" that spends its own money on working systems and then sells them. He describes how that model let Anduril build autonomous aircraft and submarines, and take over the Army's IVAS headset programme from Microsoft.

## Key points

- **Product company, not contractor.** Luckey says cost-plus contracts reward slow work and expensive parts. He claims about 80% of major defence acquisition programmes go to five companies and 30% have a single bidder, and compares the sector to home renovation.
- **IVAS takeover.** Luckey describes how Microsoft handed the soldier heads-up display contract to Anduril. He says Anduril bought Microsoft's whole mixed reality business and will do in six months what would take others eight years.
- **Four-part filter.** Anduril takes on a problem only if the Pentagon cares, Congress cares, Anduril can do it well and others are doing a poor job. Luckey says Congress is now focused on great-power conflict rather than counterterrorism.
- **Products shown.** He mentions the YFQ-44 unmanned fighter (rendered "F q44" in the captions), Ghost drones, Anvil counter-drone interceptors, Sentry towers covering about 35% of the US southern border, Dive submarines and Roadrunner. He expects early humanoid robots in defence to operate existing crewed systems rather than act as soldiers.
- **Oculus and motivation.** Luckey says he sold Oculus for the R&D commitment rather than the price: Mark Zuckerberg promised $1 billion a year for ten years, and Luckey says actual spending reached $60 billion. He is open that proving his critics wrong drives him.
- **Role of weapons makers.** Luckey says weapons companies should not lobby on which conflicts to fight or how long they last; those decisions belong to accountable civilian leaders. He also argues against fully autonomous warfare because humans must stay responsible for the use of force.
