## Thesis

In an end-of-year episode, Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin, Alexander Wissner-Gross and guest Emad Mostaque each present two predictions for 2026, picked by the production team. Their shared view is that 2026 is the year the acceleration becomes impossible to ignore: AI takes over large parts of knowledge work as it is done today, physical autonomy arrives, and institutions such as companies and universities have to be rebuilt rather than patched.

## Key points

- **Benchmarks and maths.** Wissner-Gross predicts AI will solve one of the six remaining Clay Millennium Prize problems, most likely Navier-Stokes, and that GDPval passes 90%, FrontierMath Tier 4 40% and Humanity's Last Exam 75%. He reads 90% on GDPval as most current knowledge work being automatable.
- **Model scale.** Blundin expects a 100x jump in effective model size, driven by quantisation research (FP4 and ternary weights) largely from chip-constrained Chinese labs. Mostaque puts the realistic gain nearer 10-20x.
- **AI-native rewrites.** Ismail says digital transformation is "officially dead": firms will build AI-first versions of themselves at the edge with 10-20x fewer employees. He thinks consultancies will survive by changing their model.
- **Remote Turing test and autonomy.** Mostaque predicts people will not be able to tell whether a co-worker on a 1080p Zoom call is human or AI, and that level-five autonomy arrives in cloud-backed cars and robots. Ismail is sceptical about the robot side.
- **New wealth.** Blundin expects one or more very young billionaires from a new acronym-sized niche, as RLHF data labelling created Mercor's founders. Wissner-Gross and Mostaque expect the first AI billionaire, probably via trading.
- **Space, education, longevity.** Diamandis predicts Blue Origin lands cargo at the Moon's Shackleton Crater before SpaceX, and that Life Biosciences' partial epigenetic reprogramming enters human trials. Ismail expects education to split into credential factories and portfolio-based "agency accelerators".
