## Thesis

Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin, Alex Wissner-Gross and guest Emad Mostaque agree that GPT-5 was no leap in raw capability and was weakly presented. Wissner-Gross and Ismail argue the lasting effect is what he calls "hyperdeflation" in the cost of intelligence, plus lifting hundreds of millions of users to a frontier model. Mostaque suspects OpenAI is deliberately holding its best models back.

## Key points

- **Launch reception.** Mostaque describes GPT-5 as a router from mini up to pro, roughly in line with expectations. Blundin calls the event "folksy" and notes that Polymarket odds of OpenAI having the best model flipped from about 80% towards Google during the coding demos.
- **Benchmarks.** Wissner-Gross highlights GPT-5 taking first place on LMArena and its result on Frontier Math Tier 4. Extrapolating, he projects AI solving 15-20% of hard math problems by end of 2025, 35-40% by end of 2026 and 70% by end of 2027. Mostaque notes Grok 4 still leads on ARC-AGI.
- **Price war.** The hosts compare GPT-4.5's $75/$150 per million input/output tokens with GPT-5's $1.25/$10. Mostaque says GPT-5 is priced about 40% below Sonnet to go after Anthropic's API revenue, half of which he attributes to Cursor and Microsoft Copilot. Blundin claims OpenAI is running at roughly break-even rather than losing money.
- **Health and assistants.** Sam Altman's HealthBench segment (built with 250 physicians) is read by Blundin as protection against regulators, while Ismail calls it mostly PR. Mostaque mentions his open-source II Medical model running on a Raspberry Pi.
- **Open weights.** Mostaque estimates OpenAI's open model cost about $4 million to train. Wissner-Gross cautions that the figure may hide distillation from a larger teacher model, and sees American open-weight models as useful for regulated, disconnected systems.
- **Rest of the race.** The group discusses Google's Genie 3 world model and AlphaEarth, Meta's talent poaching (100 OpenAI staff approached, 90% declined, as presented), OpenAI's $1.5 million retention bonuses, Stargate Norway and the concentration of chip manufacturing at TSMC.
