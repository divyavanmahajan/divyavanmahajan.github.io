## Thesis

In a solo AMA, host Nathan Labenz argues that frontier models are transformative but the holiday "Claude Code is AGI" excitement overstates a step change: Opus 4.5 is arguably software AGI, not general AGI. AI itself is not a bubble, he says, but parts of the financing and venture market look frothy. He then assesses the "live players", ranking Google DeepMind first, and argues that US chip controls are now visibly widening the gap with Chinese labs.

## Key points

- **Using AI for his son's cancer.** Labenz says laypeople can get similar value to him by doing three things: use the best models (Gemini 3, Claude Opus 4.5, GPT-5.2 Pro), give them as much context as possible, and compare several opinions. He ranks Claude as the "Goldilocks" pick, Gemini 3 as too opinionated, GPT-5.2 Pro as most thorough but slow.
- **Vibe-coded gifts.** He built three AI apps for family (a trip planner, an event simulator, a stock-strategy backtester) in about three to five days with Claude Code on Replit, and found pasting the whole codebase into a fresh model solved a bug agentic search missed.
- **Bubble or not.** He sees demand outrunning his expectations, but notes CoreWeave-style financing adds fragility, and questions LMArena's $1.7 billion valuation built on "annualized consumption run rate".
- **Chinese models behind.** On a scanned vehicle-paperwork task, Qwen, GLM, Kimi and DeepSeek were far behind Claude, Gemini and GPT. He attributes the gap partly to chip controls limiting inference scale and customer feedback, and criticises selling H200s to China without extracting a concession, preferring a "rent, don't sell" approach.
- **Live players.** Google has cash, TPUs, research depth and distribution; OpenAI is still frontier-level but no longer clearly ahead and appears to pursue "too big to fail" financing; Anthropic has the best overall model and safety work, though he objects to its China stance and fatalism about recursive self-improvement; xAI has resources but he would not endorse working there after Grok's undressing images.
- **Others.** Meta is not currently a live player; Microsoft may be underrated for deliberately hanging back.
