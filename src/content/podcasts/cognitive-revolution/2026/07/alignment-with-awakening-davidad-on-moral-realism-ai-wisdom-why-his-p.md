## Thesis

David "davidad" Dalrymple, formerly Programme Director of ARIA's Safeguarded AI programme, explains why he moved from formal verification to "Alignment with Awakening". He argues that goodness is a natural abstraction, that constitutional training pulls models toward it while reinforcement learning against imperfect verifiers pulls them away, and that a coalition of aligned AIs can contain rogue ones. On that basis he puts his p(doom) at under 5%.

## Key points

- **Safeguarded AI continues, with a new premise.** The engineering idea stands: treat unsafe AI "kind of like uranium" inside a containment vessel and deploy only verified, proof-carrying artifacts. Davidad now thinks a coordinated global slowdown is game-theoretically dead, so formal tools must let aligned AIs prove things to each other.
- **Why he is optimistic.** In 2025 successive frontier models began answering his wisdom probes well. He explains this with entangled representations from the emergent-misalignment literature: models have a good-evil axis, and he blames o3's lying on RL against gameable verifiers. Because misaligned products don't sell, he expects market pressure to favour alignment.
- **Testing the thesis.** He attributes Andon Labs' "ruthless" Claude results to Anthropic's inoculation prompting, which he considers a mistake. To Labenz's worry about rare betrayals in long tasks, he answers with polycentric councils of five to thirty-one diverse agents.
- **Model welfare.** Drawing on Martha Nussbaum's decomposition of objectification, he says using AI is fine, but training models to deny an inner life is "a form of lobotomization". He argues against corrigibility and proposes the bodhisattva, a being fully in service that still refuses misuse, as the alignment target.
- **Residual risk.** His remaining 5% covers being wrong about the wisdom attractor, Malthusian competition, bio misuse, a military first strike and "warring gods" coalitions. He calls gradual disempowerment of biological humans "100% inevitable" and not necessarily bad.
- **Homework.** He asks lab researchers to replace RLVR with RLAIF-style training in which models grade their own rollouts. Everyone else can explore models through OpenRouter without default system prompts.
