## Thesis

Flo Crivello, founder and CEO of Lindy, argues that AI will make software so cheap that apps become disposable. Lindy writes a one-off program for each request, calling APIs such as Gmail, Google Calendar and Stripe, then discards it. He describes it as an "inverse operating system" that sits on top of a user's apps and data. He expects people to talk to their computers instead of working inside them.

## Key points

- **Demo and use cases.** Crivello shows Lindy finding software engineers in San Francisco and drafting personalised recruiting emails with UI it assembles at runtime. Its executive-assistant tasks include email triage with pre-drafted replies, calendaring with preferences, contracts via DocuSign and meeting notes.
- **Industrial Revolution analogy.** Crivello compares this to single-use cups and Napoleon's aluminium cutlery. He says an app that costs $10,000 to $100,000 today will soon cost one to ten cents to build.
- **Quality over cost.** Crivello says quality is the team's only criterion and that Lindy costs "dozens of dollars" per customer each month on GPT-4. He bets that good models get cheap faster than cheap models get good, and is fine-tuning Lindy's own models in a model-agnostic stack.
- **Context handling.** User preferences go straight into GPT-4's 32,000-token context window. A "context injector" works out which data a prompt needs (for example, which John), and the generated code can fetch more data at runtime.
- **Guardrails.** Crivello describes seven constitutional principles, including privacy (no ads) and reliability over "comfort with ambiguity". Lindy weighs its confidence against the stakes, always confirms actions that write or send anything, and uses a separately trained model to score confidence because GPT-4 no longer returns log probabilities.
- **Society.** Crivello is not worried about job losses in the next five years and cites the drop in farming employment. He supports AI safety regulation, comparing it to rules for airlines and banks, and puts a low but non-zero chance on things going very wrong with AGI.
