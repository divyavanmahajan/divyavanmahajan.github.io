## Thesis

In this a16z Show crosspost hosted by Erik Torenberg, Emmett Shear (Twitch founder, briefly interim OpenAI CEO, now founder of Softmax) argues that alignment is an ongoing process rather than a fixed state, and that today's focus on steering and control is a dead end: a controllable superhuman tool is dangerous in human hands, and if AIs are beings, control amounts to slavery. Séb Krier of Google DeepMind pushes back, holding that even AGI or ASI can remain a tool and an extension of human agency.

## Key points

- **Alignment needs an object.** Shear says "aligned AI" usually means aligned to its builders' goals, and that real moral alignment is continual learning, as with humanity's moral discovery that slavery was wrong.
- **Goals are inferred, not transferred.** Shear separates a goal from a description of a goal; technical alignment is the competence to infer goals (theory of mind) and act on them. Krier frames it as instruction-following and principal-agent problems, citing Stuart Russell's room-cleaning robot.
- **Tool or being.** Shear, a self-described functionalist, presses Krier on what observation would change his mind about AI moral status; Krier says substrate matters, that a model saying "I'm hungry" differs from a human saying it, and that false positives also carry costs.
- **Softmax's approach.** Shear says Softmax trains agents in multi-agent simulations where they must cooperate and compete, a surrogate "pre-training" for theory of mind, starting with animal-level care such as a digital guard dog.
- **Chatbots as mirrors.** Shear calls one-on-one chatbots a pool of Narcissus and wants multiplayer AIs living in group chats; he describes ChatGPT as still sycophantic, Claude as the most neurotic and Gemini as repressed.
- **Yudkowsky and OpenAI.** Shear agrees a superhuman tool controlled by steering would kill everyone, but disagrees that caring AI is impossible; he says he told OpenAI he would stay at most 90 days.

## Quotes

> "A tool that you can control, bad. A being that isn't aligned, bad. The only good outcome is a being that is, that cares, that actually cares about us." — Emmett Shear

> "I think there's no contradiction in thinking that an AGI can remain a tool, an ASI can remain a tool" — Séb Krier
