## Thesis

In this solo episode, Ksenia Se argues that Google's moves on August 5th end "the old Google" but not Google itself. Centralising AI under Koray Kavukcuoglu makes Google a faster, Gemini-focused product company. Meanwhile, Jeff Dean's departure to found Discovery Loop and Demis Hassabis's step back may be better for science and, she suggests, for humanity.

## Key points

- **The three moves.** Hassabis gave up day-to-day control of Google DeepMind to become its chair and Alphabet's chief scientist. Kavukcuoglu took over Gemini model development, frontier research, the Gemini app and developer teams, reporting to Sundar Pichai. Dean left after 27 years. Se notes there is no new DeepMind CEO, only a senior vice president under Pichai.
- **Research vs money.** Se repeats Turing Post's earlier view: Google had a split between the people who ran its research and those who controlled money and compute (Pichai, Sergey Brin, Larry Page). Brin pushed a coding-model strike team and the race to AGI first, and coding became Gemini's training target and operating model.
- **Dean's long arc.** Se traces Dean from his 1990 Minnesota thesis on parallel backpropagation and his HIV/AIDS modelling work to his 1996 Vortex compiler thesis on whole-program optimisation. She then follows MapReduce, Bigtable, Spanner, TensorFlow, TPUs and Gemini. Across this work she sees one method: observe the whole system and optimise where measurement shows it matters.
- **Discovery Loop.** Dean leaves with Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals and Quoc Le to automate the research cycle, running thousands of experiment branches in parallel. They start with machine learning research because its feedback is fast and scorable, and will later move to medicine, energy and water. Le has suggested the system might find an architecture beyond the transformer. Vinyals names generating genuinely new ideas as a hard part.
- **Why outside Google.** Wired reports Pichai tried repeatedly to keep them. Se argues Google had grown too big and bureaucratic, and she speculates that Andrej Karpathy might join them.
- **What remains.** Se says it is "lazy" to call Google done, citing AlphaFold, AlphaEvolve, TPUs and its research base. Hassabis now has more time for Isomorphic Labs.
