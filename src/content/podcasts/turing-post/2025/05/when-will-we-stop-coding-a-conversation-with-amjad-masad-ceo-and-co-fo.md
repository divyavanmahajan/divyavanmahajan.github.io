## Thesis

Amjad Masad, CEO and co-founder of Replit, explains why he went from championing coding literacy to telling most people they don't need to learn to code. He sees agents as the next layer of abstraction after punch cards, assembly, C, Python and JavaScript. He now expects that shift in about two years rather than five or ten. He is more cautious about AGI, which he thinks is harder than Silicon Valley's consensus suggests.

## Key points

- **Agents that run for longer.** Masad says Agent V1 worked unsupervised for two to three minutes and Agent V2 for 15. Replit hopes to get Agent V3 to an hour. If that improvement repeats a few times a year, agents will eventually work for a day or two on a problem. Besides more compute and data, he says this needs a platform that supports long-running agents, testing and parallel forking.
- **Agents in production.** Masad cites a large real estate firm whose Replit-built lead-routing algorithm improved conversion by 10%, worth "millions of dollars". He also mentions an Airbnb property manager who runs his whole back office on Replit. He prefers "software developer agent" to "vibe coding" and argues that generated code beats no-code tools on flexibility and lock-in.
- **Billion-dollar small teams.** He points to Cursor reaching a $10 billion valuation with about 50 people. He predicts that every startup will be an AI startup, just as every startup is now a cloud startup.
- **AGI as efficient generalisation.** For Masad, true AGI means dropping an agent into any environment and having it learn skills on the fly. A "remote worker" trained on domain data is not that. He sees no Skynet-style risk.
- **Open source and MCP.** Replit gives agents open-source languages and tools rather than closed internal systems. Masad says MCP has "won" because a slight early edge compounds for open protocols.
- **Books and hope.** He recommends Douglas Hofstadter's I Am a Strange Loop, though it left him less convinced about consciousness. He hopes AI will lower the cost of building software and create opportunities for entrepreneurs in emerging markets.
