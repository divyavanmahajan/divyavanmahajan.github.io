## Thesis

Alexandr Wang presents Scale AI as the "data foundry" for the data pillar of AI, next to compute and algorithms. He argues the industry has used up the easy data on the internet and must now choose data abundance through expert-produced frontier data, enterprise proprietary data and hybrid human-AI synthetic data. He expects slow, problem-by-problem progress toward AGI.

## Key points

- **Riding the waves.** Wang dropped out of MIT in 2016, went through YC and started with sensor-fusion data (lidar plus cameras) for autonomous vehicle makers such as General Motors and Toyota. Scale then moved into geospatial data for government, and worked with OpenAI on early RLHF experiments on GPT-2 and on InstructGPT.
- **Frontier data.** Wang says models now need reasoning chains from experts such as mathematicians, doctors and lawyers, agent workflow data, multilingual data and multimodal data. He notes JP Morgan's proprietary dataset is 150 petabytes, while GPT-4 was trained on less than one petabyte.
- **Humans stay in the loop.** Asked whether human expertise stops adding value, Wang says "never": models are trained on short "nuggets" of intelligence and lack long-horizon reasoning, so human critique and guidance remain complementary.
- **Evaluations.** Scale published GSM 1K, a held-out math eval that showed some models perform much worse than reported. Wang plans private held-out leaderboards rerun every few months, starting with math, coding, instruction following and adversarial capabilities.
- **Funding and products.** The hosts note a raise of a billion dollars at almost 14 billion valuation, with AMD, Cisco and Meta among the investors. Wang describes agentic features for government customers in the Donovan product and a platform to help enterprises build self-improving data flywheels.
- **AGI as many small problems.** Wang sees very limited generality, including no positive transfer between modalities, so each capability needs its own data flywheel. He sees this as a bullish case for society adapting.
