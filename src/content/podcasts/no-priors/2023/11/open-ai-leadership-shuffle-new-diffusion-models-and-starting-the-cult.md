## Thesis

In a hosts-only episode, Sarah Guo and Elad Gil argue that the OpenAI leadership crisis shows governance, capital and incentives matter more than many founders assumed, and that OpenAI likely emerges more stable. They then argue that diffusion models for video, image and audio are back in focus, and that AI's opportunity lies in converting low-margin services spend into higher-margin software revenue.

## Key points

- **Lessons from the board fight.** Guo says nonprofit governance is often poor because performance is hard to measure, and that compute (held by Microsoft) and loyal staff decided the outcome. She notes employees also feared losing value from the $86 billion tender offer. Gil cites Charlie Munger, who died that day, on underestimating incentives.
- **Second-sourcing models.** Gil points to Braintrust's AI proxy, which routes the OpenAI SDK to models such as Mistral and Llama, and expects enterprises to keep a second vendor, as Juniper is to Cisco or AMD to Intel. Guo says OpenAI still leads on capability, such as code generation and GPT-4V.
- **Diffusion's comeback.** Gil describes founders swinging from image generation to LLMs after ChatGPT, while a few, such as Pika's two Stanford PhD founders, stayed on diffusion. He says the original Stable Diffusion reportedly cost about $600k of GPU time, so small teams can train their own models.
- **Video is still early.** Guo says video generation is data-intensive, hard to caption and struggles with temporal coherence, which leaves more room for technical disagreement and for startups.
- **Creative markets are large.** Guo says skeptics underestimated Midjourney. Gil cites Adobe's market cap of roughly $280-300 billion as a proxy, and Guo argues the tools eat into production spend, not editing software.
- **Margin over price.** Gil estimates software spend at about $500 billion a year against $3-5 trillion in services, and uses Anduril's drones as an example of earning similar margin at a tenth of the price. Guo reframes it as "your ASP is my opportunity."
