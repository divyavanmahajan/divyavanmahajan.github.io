## Thesis

Rey Banatao, who leads Materra, X's moonshot for circularity, argues that recycling has not failed. Instead, consumer materials have changed faster than recycling infrastructure, and nobody has good data on what waste contains at the molecular level. Materra uses infrared spectroscopy and AI to identify materials in milliseconds and route them to the right process. The aim is to make recycling profitable, which Banatao and Astro Teller see as the way to get it adopted worldwide.

## Key points

- **The economics.** Banatao says less than 10% of the world's plastics have ever been recycled, while plastics are a $600 billion industry. He sees value of a similar scale in critical minerals from electronics and batteries.
- **Fragmented infrastructure.** According to Banatao, the US has more than 9,000 recycling systems. Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs) differ widely: Hawaii accepts far fewer materials than California, and many facilities are "choking on waste".
- **Why molecules matter.** Banatao says a few percent of additives can wreck both mechanical and chemical recycling. In pyrolysis, the difference is between usable oil and "gobbledygook". The team could not buy composition data, so it began with handheld spectrometers and Banatao's own blue bin during COVID.
- **The tanker-load goal.** Working with Recology and a partner in Oregon, Materra sorted plastic rejected by an MRF and bound for landfill. A pyrolysis partner turned it into a tanker load of oil. This year the team wants to increase volume tenfold with continuous, commercial-grade deployments.
- **Policy as a tailwind.** Banatao expects molecular data to support extended producer responsibility laws, which he says five US states have approved and under which packaging brands pay fees when their packaging is not recycled.
- **What comes next.** He names textiles, where synthetic fibres make up more than half of clothing, and e-waste as the next targets. He also describes a team trip to Alaska where 10 people collected 20 tons of ocean plastic in about a week.
