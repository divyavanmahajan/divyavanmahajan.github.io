## Thesis

In his fourth conversation with Lex Fridman, Elon Musk ranges from war to AI. On geopolitics he argues that "conspicuous acts of kindness" are the only way to stop cycles of reciprocal violence. On AI he argues that a useful model has to be grounded in physics and checked against reality, and that the bottleneck for AI is moving from silicon to electrical power.

## Key points

- **Wars and kindness.** Musk believes Hamas wanted to provoke an overreaction, and recommends Israel pursue Hamas members while also making highly visible humanitarian efforts such as a mobile hospital. On Ukraine he expects a truce close to the current lines, because attackers lose several times as many people as defenders.
- **Grok and truthfulness.** Musk says xAI's Grok is modelled on The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy and that xAI's mission is to understand the universe. He says other LLMs hallucinate most on hard questions and that token-by-token prediction drifts unless the model revises its output for coherence.
- **Compute per watt.** Musk predicts a voltage step-down transformer shortage in about a year and wider electricity shortages in about two. He told utilities to plan for tripling electricity output and to buy batteries. He contrasts Tesla's 100-watt self-driving computer with a 10 megawatt GPU cluster.
- **Regulation and open source.** Musk wants an objective third-party referee for leading AI labs, but mocks permitting delays at SpaceX, such as playing sonic booms to a seal wearing headphones. He says Grok was trained on 8,000 A100s, that he gave OpenAI over $40 million at the start, and that he leans towards open sourcing models, possibly with a delay of about six months.
- **X and Community Notes.** Musk wants recommendations on X to be fully AI-driven through vector matching, and he says notes only appear when raters who have disagreed in the past agree.
- **Optimus.** Musk says Tesla had to design the robot's motors and actuators itself, and that the car's video-in, controls-out approach will carry over to the robot.
