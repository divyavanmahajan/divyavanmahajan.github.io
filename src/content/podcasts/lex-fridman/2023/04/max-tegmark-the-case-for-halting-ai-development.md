## Thesis

Max Tegmark, co-founder of the Future of Life Institute, tells Lex Fridman that AI capabilities have advanced faster than expected while policy and safety work have lagged. He argues that labs are trapped in a "Moloch" dynamic where commercial pressure stops any one of them slowing down alone, and that the open letter calling for a pause gives them public cover to coordinate.

## Key points

- **Why six months.** The letter asks labs to pause training systems more powerful than GPT-4. Tegmark says six months was chosen to defuse the "but China" objection, since China cannot catch up in that time, and argues Beijing also wants to keep control.
- **Easier than expected.** Tegmark compares large language models to the Wright brothers' plane: a simple transformer trained to predict the next word got close to human ability without copying the brain. His MIT lab's interpretability work keeps finding "dumb" mechanisms that could be improved.
- **Risky steps already taken.** He lists what safety researchers long warned against: teaching AI to code, connecting it to the internet, letting it learn to manipulate humans, and offering an API that anyone can build agents on. Fridman expects AI tools to make him 5 to 10 times faster at coding, which Tegmark calls an early form of recursive self-improvement.
- **Regulation.** Tegmark says lobbyists nearly kept general-purpose models out of the EU AI Act. He compares future safety rules to mandatory seatbelts.
- **Provable safety.** Tegmark proposes running only AI code that can prove itself trustworthy to a short, human-understandable proof checker. Fridman raises Eliezer Yudkowsky's view that a superintelligence could fool such checks. Tegmark disagrees but shares Yudkowsky's concern that the risk is large.
- **Consciousness and nuclear winter.** Citing Giulio Tononi's theory, Tegmark suggests feed-forward models like GPT-4 may be intelligent without being conscious. He also cites a Nature Food study in which about 99% of Americans starve after a nuclear war in one model.
