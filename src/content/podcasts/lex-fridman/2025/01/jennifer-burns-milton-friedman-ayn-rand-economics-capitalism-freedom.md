## Thesis

Jennifer Burns, biographer of both Milton Friedman and Ayn Rand, argues that the two shared an individualist defence of capitalism but differed sharply in method and temperament. Friedman was an empirical, compromising "happy warrior" who grounded capitalism in freedom and kept revising his views; Rand was a first-principles purist who built a moral system and a mythic fiction around rational selfishness. Burns also argues that inflation, which Friedman explained, both created the neoliberal era and helped end it.

## Key points

- **Rewriting the Great Depression.** Burns explains that Friedman and Anna Schwartz's 1963 monetary history showed the money supply fell by about a third, blaming Federal Reserve inaction rather than capitalism. She says this became the Fed's playbook in the financial crisis and Covid.
- **Monetarism and stagflation.** Friedman's 1967 American Economic Association address challenged the Phillips Curve and predicted high inflation together with high unemployment. Burns calls the stagflation of the 1970s his career-making vindication.
- **Freedom as the moral core.** Burns says Friedman rejected "people get what they deserve" because luck and endowments differ, and settled on individual freedom instead. After his Chile visit and the backlash over Pinochet, he gave more weight to political freedom.
- **Pragmatic policy ideas.** Friedman opposed the minimum wage but proposed a guaranteed minimum income as early as 1938, which Burns links to the earned income tax credit. He pushed floating exchange rates via George Shultz under Nixon and backed Volcker-era disinflation to Reagan.
- **Inflation and politics today.** Burns argues that forgetting inflation opened the door to Trump's second term, and says Friedman would have liked DOGE but opposed tariffs.
- **Rand's appeal and flaws.** Burns describes objectivism, the Fountainhead's mass appeal, and the cult-like "Collective" that included Alan Greenspan. She identifies its central weakness as being justified through an idealised fictional world.
