## Thesis

Dave Smith, a libertarian comedian and host of Part of the Problem, argues that killing innocent people is never justified, that the burden is on any government to prove a war was unavoidable, and that military campaigns from Yemen to Gaza produce more of the enemy they target. Lex Fridman shares his opposition to war but pushes back on several points, notably on Russia's responsibility for the Ukraine war and on the ethical weight of stated intent to spare civilians.

## Key points

- **Blowback.** Smith credits Ron Paul with explaining 9/11 as a reaction to US policy and cites General McChrystal's "insurgent math" (10 minus two equals 20). He claims AQAP grew from 2009 to 2015 under drone strikes in which, he says, 96% of those killed were innocent.
- **Israel and Gaza.** Smith calls October 7th an indefensible act of terrorism, but says Netanyahu propped up Hamas through Qatari cash, and argues that millions of Palestinians living without votes under Israeli control since 1967 make Israel an apartheid state, not a democracy. Fridman argues that a stated aim of protecting civilians is an ethical difference; Smith compares it to a first-degree murder defence.
- **Antisemitism.** Smith, who is Jewish, says he sees more Jew hatred on X than ever, links it partly to woke identity politics and the Gaza war, and says calling legitimate criticism antisemitic empties the word.
- **Ukraine.** Smith says NATO expansion provoked the invasion, citing Stoltenberg on Putin's 2021 draft treaty, William Burns's "Nyet Means Nyet" cable and George Kennan in 1998, while agreeing that Putin bears responsibility for launching it. He backs Trump's push for a deal.
- **Epstein and conspiracies.** Smith believes Epstein's career only makes sense with intelligence ties and asks where the blackmail tapes are, but warns that conspiracy thinking gets sloppy, citing PNAC's "Pearl Harbor" line as weak evidence.
- **Hope.** Smith cites economist Gene Epstein's case for optimism and argues the regime has lost its monopoly on propaganda.
