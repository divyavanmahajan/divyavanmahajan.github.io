## Thesis

Modi frames his life, from poverty in Vadnagar and wandering in the Himalayas to the RSS and the prime ministership, as one continuous commitment to service, which he says draws on Swami Vivekananda and the RSS. He argues that India's strength comes from its 1.4 billion people and its civilisational identity, and that this lets India put its own interests first while presenting itself as a peacemaker. Fridman, who fasted for 45 hours before the conversation, mostly lets Modi set out his own account.

## Key points

- **Fasting as discipline.** Modi says he has fasted for 50 to 55 years. He eats one meal a day during Chaturmas and drinks only hot water during the nine days of Navratri, and says fasting sharpens his senses and thinking.
- **Foreign policy and war.** Modi says India will "see eye-to-eye" with other powers. On Ukraine he says he can tell Putin "this is not the time for war" and tell Zelensky that the war will not be settled on the battlefield. He blames Pakistan for state-sponsored terrorism and says his peace gestures, such as inviting SAARC leaders to his swearing-in, were met with hostility.
- **China and Trump.** Modi says the border has returned to normal since he last met Xi, after the 2020 clashes. He praises Trump's courage at the Howdy Modi rally in Houston and links Elon Musk's DOGE to his own reforms: he claims 100 million fake or duplicate welfare names were removed and nearly 3 trillion rupees saved through direct benefit transfer.
- **Gujarat 2002.** Fridman notes that the Supreme Court found no involvement by Modi. Modi describes the terror attacks that came before, and the Godhra incident, and says Gujarat has had no major riot in the 22 years since. He calls much of the media coverage allegation rather than criticism.
- **Elections and decisions.** Modi gives figures for 2024: 980 million registered voters, 646 million votes cast, more than a million polling booths. He says his COVID policy rejected advice to print money, and he credits that choice with sparing India the inflation seen elsewhere.
- **AI and talent.** Modi argues that AI "will remain incomplete without India", pointing to its pool of engineers, the 5G rollout and 200 space startups.
