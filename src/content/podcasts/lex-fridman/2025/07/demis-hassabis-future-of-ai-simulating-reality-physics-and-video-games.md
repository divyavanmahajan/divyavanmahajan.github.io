## Thesis

Demis Hassabis, CEO of Google DeepMind, argues that any pattern produced by evolution or other selection processes has structure that a classical learning algorithm can discover efficiently. AlphaFold, AlphaGo and Veo 3 are his evidence, and he sees AGI as the tool to answer deeper questions such as P equals NP and the nature of reality. He puts the odds of AGI by 2030 at about 50% and calls for cautious optimism about the risks.

## Key points

- **Learnable natural systems.** Hassabis explains his Nobel lecture conjecture: there are about 10 to 300 possible protein structures, yet proteins fold in milliseconds, so nature must have a learnable "survival of the stablest" structure. He is working on a possible new complexity class for such problems.
- **Veo 3 and intuitive physics.** He says Veo 3's handling of liquids, lighting and materials shows that intuitive physics can be learned from passive observation. That contradicts his earlier view that embodiment was needed. Interactive video would be the next step toward a world model.
- **Testing for AGI.** Hassabis would run tens of thousands of cognitive tasks and give a few hundred top experts a month or two to find flaws. He also looks for "Move 37" moments, such as rediscovering relativity with a 1900 knowledge cutoff or inventing a game as deep as Go.
- **Scaling vs. new ideas.** Pre-training, post-training and inference-time scaling all still have room, he says. DeepMind puts roughly half its resources into blue-sky research, and he claims 80-90% of the breakthroughs behind modern AI came from Google Brain, Google Research and DeepMind.
- **Virtual Cell.** He describes a 25-year ambition, discussed with Paul Nurse, to simulate a yeast cell at the protein level. The goal is to run most experiments in silico, potentially a hundred-fold speed-up.
- **Disruption and risk.** Hassabis expects roughly 10 times the impact of the Industrial Revolution, arriving 10 times faster. He won't give a p(doom) number but calls the risk non-zero and non-negligible, and he wants 10 times more safety research. He also says Meta is not at the frontier.
