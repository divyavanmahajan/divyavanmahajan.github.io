## Thesis

Ivanka Trump describes a career across real estate, fashion and four years as a White House adviser, and argues that the same habits carried through all of it: walking the site instead of trusting the plan, asking questions of the people doing the work, and building coalitions quietly. She also explains why she chose to stay out of the 2024 campaign to prioritise her three children.

## Key points

- **Renovating the Old Post Office.** Trump says the Washington building was losing $6 million a year when her team won a roughly two-year GSA bidding process. Its almost 300 rooms needed almost 300 different layouts, and she found columns on site that blueprints had missed.
- **Asking the duct worker.** She recounts her father questioning workers on the site; one called the atrium heating and cooling design "obscenely over-designed", and the system was overhauled mid-construction. Fridman links this to flat organisations at Elon Musk's companies.
- **Fashion for working women.** Starting with fine jewellery aimed at women buying for themselves, she grew the brand to about 11 product categories and hundreds of millions of dollars in sales, with a Nine West founder as her shoe partner.
- **Policy work.** She credits the doubled child tax credit with giving 40 million families an average of $2,200 a year, and cites paid family leave for nine million more Americans, over 16 million private-sector retraining commitments, the Great American Outdoors Act and nine anti-trafficking laws.
- **Pardons and Alice Johnson.** After Johnson's sentence was commuted, Trump says the two worked on 23 further pardons or commutations; she spent her final days in office on such cases.
- **Not hitting back.** She cites the Jewish concept of Lashon hara (evil speech) as her reason for never answering attacks, and says politics is too dark to "dabble in", so she is staying out of the 2024 race.
