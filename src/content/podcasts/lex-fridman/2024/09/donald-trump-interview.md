## Thesis

Trump argues that he wins through drive and by reaching audiences on new platforms, and that his negotiating style would end the wars in Ukraine and the Middle East. Fridman, describing himself as an independent, repeatedly asks about the 2020 fraud claims, division and the corrupting effect of power; Trump mostly answers by criticising Biden and Harris. In a closing solo segment Fridman explains his "historian" approach to interviewing world leaders and answers listener questions.

## Key points

- **Winning and media.** Trump says champions he has known, such as Tiger Woods and Jack Nicklaus, share being driven, and that platforms like his Spaces with Elon now reach numbers radio and television cannot.
- **Ukraine and China.** Trump claims he would have a Ukraine deal "guaranteed" as president-elect but declines to give his plans for Russia or China, saying surprise is part of them. He says the stick generally works better than the carrot in war.
- **2020 and immigration.** Asked about fraud claims, Trump calls the election "a fraud", proposes paper ballots, voter ID and proof of citizenship, and says the border is 25 times worse than in 2016.
- **Policy positions.** He says he has not read Project 2025 and it has nothing to do with him, and that medical marijuana has been "amazing", pointing to Florida's approach. He says he would push the Pentagon to release UFO footage and would be inclined to release the Epstein material.
- **Power and division.** Trump says he chose not to prosecute Hillary Clinton to bring the country together; Fridman tells him he is at his best describing a positive vision rather than criticising opponents.
- **Fridman's AMA.** Fridman calls Pavel Durov's arrest in France and Alexandre de Moraes's ban of X in Brazil government power grabs, and advises programmers worried about AI to master tools like Cursor and become big-picture designers.
