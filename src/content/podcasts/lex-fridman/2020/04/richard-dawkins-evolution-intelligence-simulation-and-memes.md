## Thesis

Evolutionary biologist Richard Dawkins tells Lex Fridman that there are no shortcuts: complex intelligence, whether alien, artificial or the programmer of a simulated universe, has to come from a gradual, step-by-step process such as Darwinian evolution or engineering design. He applies the same Darwinian lens to culture, arguing that memes spread because they are attractive, not because they are true, and that morality comes from cultural change rather than religious texts.

## Key points

- **Life and intelligence elsewhere.** With an estimated 10^22 stars, Dawkins thinks intelligent life beyond Earth is highly likely, and that any life found will be Darwinian, though probably not DNA-based. Contact would most likely come by radio, perhaps signalled with prime numbers.
- **Evolution as wasteful engineering.** Dawkins calls evolution deplorably wasteful yet capable of superb designs such as soaring birds. His example of bad design is the recurrent laryngeal nerve, which in a giraffe makes a detour of many feet around an artery in the chest.
- **AI and the brain.** He believes nothing non-physical happens in brains, so machine intelligence is possible in principle, and that any superhuman intelligence will be artificial because natural selection no longer favours bigger brains in humans.
- **Memes and the internet.** Dawkins coined "meme" in 1976 as the cultural equivalent of a gene. He says the internet is a step change in how fast ideas spread and has created distributed "villages" such as Flat Earth communities.
- **Religion, conspiracies and morality.** Dawkins attributes the persistence of religion mainly to childhood indoctrination, says supernatural explanations are a non-explanation, and argues that moral values shift steadily over the centuries, independent of Bronze Age scripture.
- **Simulation and perception.** He finds Nick Bostrom's simulation argument interesting but does not believe it, noting the programmer would still need to have evolved. Fridman adds that the simulation itself would likely have to be grown gradually.
