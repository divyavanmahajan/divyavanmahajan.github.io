## Thesis

Karl Friston tells Lex Fridman that the free energy principle is a deliberately deflationary statement: if something exists as a thing separate from its environment, it must look as if it is minimizing variational free energy, the same quantity machine learning calls the negative evidence lower bound (ELBO). He argues that what makes something alive is autonomous, non-random movement, and that self-awareness becomes necessary when a planning system lives among others like itself.

## Key points

- **Brain structure.** Friston describes the brain as a sparse, recurrent, hierarchical onion, not a magic soup. Neuroimaging, from PET to functional MRI in the early 1990s, confirmed functional segregation, but the harder problem is how regions integrate.
- **Imaging trade-offs.** Blood-flow signals locate activity to within a few millimeters but lag by seconds. EEG and MEG are accurate to milliseconds but spatially diffuse. He jokingly calls statistical parametric mapping a refined form of blobology.
- **Brain-computer interfaces.** Friston is ambivalent. He credits deep brain stimulation for Parkinson's and sensory substitution such as a felt magnetic-field sense. He notes current BCIs run at bits per second, though, and compares meaningful control to steering the weather. Fridman, after visiting Neuralink, is more hopeful that engineering and machine learning can help.
- **Markov blankets.** Friston splits any system into internal, external and blanket states, with blanket states divided into sensory and active states. An oil drop has this structure; a tadpole that moves itself to test hypotheses, such as following chemical gradients, is alive.
- **Machine learning's gap.** Friston argues that movement and active data sampling have reached robotics but not deep learning, which relies on big data instead.
- **Consciousness and meaning.** Planning needs a generative model of the consequences of your own actions. Friston argues that self-awareness follows from living among similar creatures, which forces you to work out which of you is speaking. For him, meaning is fulfilling the narratives you were given; his own were Einstein and Sherlock Holmes.
