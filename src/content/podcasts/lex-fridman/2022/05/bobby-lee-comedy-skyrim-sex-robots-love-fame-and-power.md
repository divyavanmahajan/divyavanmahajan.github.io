## Thesis

Behind a lot of absurd banter, comedian and podcaster Bobby Lee tells Lex Fridman that his stand-up, his self-critical inner voice and his drinking and drug use all come from the same place: a childhood with a physically abusive immigrant father. He says a recent relapse brought him close to death, and that sobriety, intensive therapy and the support of his partner Khalyla have left him feeling better than ever.

## Key points

- **The inner critic.** Lee says 99% of the negative thoughts in his head are not true and he wants to stop living by them. Fridman admits he is deeply self-critical too, and worries he would become lazy without it.
- **Khalyla and TigerBelly.** Lee says his career was stuck, with half-empty rooms on the road, until Khalyla pushed him to audition again and started TigerBelly on her own before he joined. Bad Friends with Andrew Santino followed. He credits producer George Kimmel and engineer Gilbert for building the show.
- **Father and family.** Lee's father died in August 2019. He describes how his parents doubted him until he got The Tonight Show and MADtv in 2000, after which his father appeared in several sketches. He also recounts his mother's childhood trauma.
- **Relapse and recovery.** About four months before recording, an edible before a flight to film Magnum P.I. began a three-month relapse. Coughing up blood, and shaken by the deaths of Bob Saget and Louie Anderson, he entered a programme in Arizona with 12 hours of therapy a day. He says his health checks came back clean and he has quit smoking and stayed sober.
- **Carlos Mencia.** Lee calls Mencia a mentor who bought him a car, but says the joke-stealing evidence was hard to deny. He cut ties, still feels some guilt, and wishes Mencia would simply apologise in broad terms.
- **Power.** Asked if he would want absolute power, Lee says no, because no ruler can please everyone. Fridman would refuse too, saying he does not trust his own mind with power.
