## Thesis

Ben Shapiro, co-founder of The Daily Wire, tells Lex Fridman that the best defence against evil is to accept that anyone can be drawn into it, and that polarised "stop them by any means" politics is how democracies fail. He argues for wide free speech on platforms, against blaming commentators for violence by their audiences, and for a society built around local roles such as parent and spouse. Fridman repeatedly pushes him to be less snarky and to extend love, not just respect, to strangers.

## Key points

- **Ye and antisemitism.** Shapiro calls Ye's comments antisemitic, suggests a manic episode may play a part, and says jumping from one person to "all Jews" is textbook bigotry. He separates ethnic, religious and political antisemitism.
- **Weimar lessons.** Shapiro says power had already been centralised before Hitler was elected, and cites Franz von Papen backing Hitler to stop the communists as a warning about choosing a "lesser" radical.
- **Speech and platforms.** Shapiro welcomes Elon Musk's purchase of Twitter, says Trump and Alex Jones should be restored, and argues the suppression of the Hunter Biden laptop story was itself the bigger story. Fridman notes worries about viral misinformation.
- **Trump, Biden, Ukraine.** Shapiro credits Trump with Middle East peace deals but faults his lack of filter; he calls Biden a good father but says he governed as a divider. On Ukraine, he argues Biden should push an off-ramp that likely leaves Russia with Crimea and parts of Luhansk and Donetsk; Fridman says Ukrainians are "all in" on full victory.
- **Abortion and climate.** Shapiro holds that life begins at conception, with only a life-of-the-mother exception. He accepts human-caused climate change but argues for adaptation and nuclear energy over current mitigation plans.
- **Role theory.** Shapiro describes a book in progress arguing meaning comes from roles (father, spouse, citizen) and that liberty has instrumental, not inherent, value. Fridman says people left alone tend towards good; Shapiro disagrees.
