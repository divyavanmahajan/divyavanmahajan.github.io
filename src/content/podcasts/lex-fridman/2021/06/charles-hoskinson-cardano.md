## Thesis

Charles Hoskinson, founder of Cardano and co-founder of Ethereum, tells Lex Fridman that Cardano is meant to be a "financial operating system" that gives everyone equal access to identity, value transfer and governance. He argues that rigorous, peer-reviewed science and functional programming are the way to get there, and that the real goal is a system that can evolve itself without its founders. He is openly critical of Bitcoin's culture.

## Key points

- **Research first.** Hoskinson says Cardano's team has written 105 papers and picked Haskell because functional code sits closer to the maths, so it is easier to turn the papers into code. He gives the example of Mantis, an Ethereum node written in Scala in about 14-15,000 lines, compared with 120-150,000 for C++ Bitcoin.
- **Proof of stake versus proof of work.** He describes both as ways to pick a block producer using different resources. He says proof of work tends to concentrate into mining oligarchies, while the Ouroboros protocols and the K parameter push stake towards more pools, now over 3,000.
- **Extended UTXO and Plutus.** Hoskinson says adding data and scripts to Bitcoin-style "cash register" accounting allows smart contracts with costs that are predictable locally and no global state. At the time of recording he expected Alonzo to bring smart contracts within 90 days.
- **Governance and real-world use.** He says Cardano's treasury holds over a billion dollars of ADA, which the community spends through Catalyst voting. He also describes a project with Ethiopia's Ministry of Education to give 5 million students decentralised identities using Atala PRISM.
- **Critiques of other projects.** Hoskinson says Bitcoin has no incentive to evolve and its community has become a religion. He calls Dogecoin's ownership highly concentrated and a risk for retail buyers, and Polkadot "Ethereum 1.5". Fridman pushes back, defending fun and risk-taking as sources of innovation.
- **Power and grounding.** Hoskinson says ranching, animals and giving things away keep him grounded. He recounts giving his 293,000 ether to his secretary after leaving Ethereum.
