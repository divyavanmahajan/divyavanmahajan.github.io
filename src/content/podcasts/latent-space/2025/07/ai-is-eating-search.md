## Thesis

Robert, co-founder of Scrunch AI and former CTO of Hearsay, argues that the bigger shift is not AI replacing search but AI replacing web browsing. People prefer to stay inside ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude, so businesses need to optimise the whole "agent experience" of their content, not only where they rank.

## Key points

- **Monitoring simulated prompts.** Scrunch simulates consumer prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Meta AI. Robert calls Meta AI a "silent monster" because of its distribution, and names Perplexity the second-biggest AI-native search platform after ChatGPT.
- **AI search is still search.** Robert says most AI search runs traditional retrieval (BM25, TF-IDF, PageRank) and then re-ranks results. Pages with clickbait metadata often get re-ranked out even when they rank well, so descriptive titles matter.
- **Technical basics.** ChatGPT and most AI retrievers don't execute JavaScript, so content must be server-rendered. In his view llms.txt is not used for discovery by default and embedding-based optimisation makes little difference.
- **Disclose more.** For shopping and B2B software, Robert advises describing who a product is for and what it is good for. He says users run B2B software "bake-offs" in ChatGPT and deep research, and that outdated pricing pages can lead to wrong answers.
- **Results.** Clerk saw about six times more ChatGPT traffic and a nine times lift in conversions. Robert says active customers typically see double-digit traffic gains within a month or two. The hosts mention that Lenny's newsletter got more traffic from ChatGPT than from Twitter.
- **Grey-hat tactics.** Robert says prompt injection on product pages often works for now, but he warns that it will be penalised. He describes the current window as positive sum: clearer content helps the user, the model and the business.
