## Thesis

Hosts of Redpoint's Unsupervised Learning (Jacob and Jordan) and Latent Space's swyx and Alessio Fanelli interview each other about the past year in AI. Their shared view is that the interesting value sits at the application layer, that a handful of agent form factors (coding, support, deep research) have clear product-market fit, and that defensibility comes from velocity, brand and network effects rather than unique data or custom models.

## Key points

- **Biggest surprises.** Reasoning models arrived right after Ilya's NeurIPS "scaling is dead" talk, which swyx calls suspiciously neat timing. Citing Ankur from Braintrust, the hosts note open-model usage in enterprises at about 5% and falling; swyx argues it was DeepSeek, not "open source", that caught up.
- **Missed opportunity.** swyx finds it bizarre that low-code players like Zapier, Airtable, Retool and Notion did not capture the AI builder market won by Bolt and Lovable, both reporting 0 to 20 million in three months.
- **Hype check.** Alessio calls agent frameworks overhyped, comparing the moment to the jQuery era; swyx says the protocol (MCP) matters more than any framework. Jordan is bullish on Apple's private cloud compute; swyx says memory is underhyped, noting OpenAI's agents SDK shipped without it.
- **Product-market fit.** swyx estimates OpenAI's deep research upgrade from $20 to $200 is worth billions in ARR. The group points to Brett Taylor choosing customer support for Sierra, and to home-services businesses that miss 50% of calls, where an AI that works 75% of the time still adds revenue.
- **Defensibility.** swyx cites Chai Research as a marketplace with network effects; the Redpoint side says brand leaders have seen ACVs double despite expected price compression.
- **Open questions.** Whether RL works in non-verifiable domains such as law and sales, the "rule of nines" for reliability that Bob McGrew described, and swyx's pick: agent authentication.

## Quotes

> "I wish that more AI builders or AI founders emphasize network effects, 'cause that, that's the only thing that you're gonna have at the end of the day." — swyx
