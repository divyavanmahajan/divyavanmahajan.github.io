## Thesis

Josephine Teo, Singapore's Minister for Digital Development and Information, tells swyx and Alessio Fanelli that Singapore's AI strategy is judged by what it does for citizens, businesses and workers, not by the technology itself. She describes a small, single-tier government that works across ministries, makes compute, data and talent available, and keeps binding rules for specific cases such as elections.

## Key points

- **Why "development".** Teo says the ministry was renamed from Communications and Information to Digital Development because it cares about AI's effect on society. She compares the role to a property developer that plans, builds, monitors and fixes. Its four pillars are the digital economy, digital society, digital government and digital security.
- **Strategy pieces.** The refreshed national AI strategy, informed by Singaporeans in the Bay Area through RAISE, covers compute for researchers and startups, data made available under long-standing data protection laws, and three tiers of talent: AI creators, practitioners and users. swyx points to the goal of tripling AI practitioners from 5,000 to 15,000 and argues for using the job title "AI engineer".
- **Regulation by use case.** Teo compares AI to electricity: rules govern particular uses, not the technology as a whole. Singapore has voluntary transparency guidelines, testing tools such as AI Verify and an AI safety institute. Its only AI-specific law so far is a proposed one on synthetic content during elections.
- **Reskilling.** Teo says employers send the strongest signal to workers; she cites Grab pausing work for a week of gen AI training. For people whose employers do not offer training, jobs transformation maps show how roles will change over three to five years, for example auditors moving into digital forensics.
- **Infrastructure.** Government uses a cloud-first, hybrid approach. Teo argues that not all AI workloads can or should be hosted in Singapore, and she sees data center growth elsewhere in Southeast Asia as positive.
- **Internal use.** Her favourite example is the government's "AI Bots", RAG assistants built on agencies' internal memos, used for work such as tax appeals.
