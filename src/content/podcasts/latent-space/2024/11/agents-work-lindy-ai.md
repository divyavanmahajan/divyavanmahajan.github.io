## Thesis

Florent Crivello, founder of the no-code agent platform Lindy, says the first Lindy, a large prompt plus tools, overestimated what LLMs could do. Lindy 2.0 puts agents "on rails": fixed triggers and steps, with AI filling individual fields. He argues that a few horizontal agent platforms will dominate, and that since Claude 3.5 Sonnet the model is no longer the bottleneck.

## Key points

- **Rails over prompts.** Crivello's example: a Zendesk agent should always consult the knowledge base, so that step is fixed rather than left to the prompt. He says 60 to 70 percent of prompts users type into a text box "don't mean anything", so a GUI works better.
- **Live examples.** He shows Lindys that log holiday-rental bookings from emails into a spreadsheet, record meetings and send coaching notes he can question afterwards, and review pull requests against roughly 40 pages of guidelines. He prunes agent memories because agents get confused by too many.
- **Horizontal thesis.** Comparing agents to Google search, he argues agents in each vertical have more in common with each other than with the vertical, and one platform lets agents work together.
- **Models and APIs.** Lindy began with 4,000-token context windows, and that pruning work was thrown away. He calls GPT-4o overhyped for agentic work. An append-only ledger makes prompt caching effective. He prefers APIs to computer use, citing years of Android lag caused by betting on hardware catching up.
- **Failures and evals.** Lindy once sent a customer a Rickroll instead of a tutorial link; logs showed three or four earlier cases, fixed with one system-prompt line. Its in-house eval tool turns conversations into tests, but he expects to switch to Braintrust. His bar is "better than a human".
- **Company views.** After running Teamflow, a remote-office startup, he moved Lindy back in person: remote suits cost-optimised work, in-person suits creative work. He also argues tech workers outside San Francisco lack judgment or ambition and puts his p(doom) at about 10%.
