## Thesis

Adam Brown, a Stanford theoretical physicist who co-founded the Blueshift team at Google DeepMind, argues that many limits on the far future are set by physics we only partly understand. The cosmological constant may not be fixed, black holes cannot be mined quickly, and gravity caps how much information a region can hold. He also argues that LLMs are already strong physics tutors, and that deriving general relativity may be the last step before they match human intelligence.

## Key points

- **Vacuum decay as engineering.** Brown explains that a truly constant dark energy dooms us to heat death. Descendants might trigger a controlled vacuum decay to lower it, but the new vacuum would expand at light speed through the future light cone, a negative externality he says rules out libertarian governance.
- **Bubble universes.** He puts about 50% on the Big Bang being a local vacuum decay inside a larger universe, noting that energy is not conserved globally in an expanding universe.
- **LLMs on physics.** Brown's private eval, a graduate general relativity exam, went from zero three years ago to near-perfect scores recently. He uses LLMs for literature search and tutoring, such as squeezed light at LIGO, but says models must be taught "beauty", because fitting data alone produces epicycles.
- **Physics stagnation.** He says the Standard Model "won": it predicts everything colliders can reach, and the LHC found the Higgs but nothing unexpected. CERN costs more than big training runs, so he favours cheap experiments like BICEP.
- **Mining black holes.** His paper shows that "claw" schemes for speeding up Hawking radiation fail. Even a rope at the physical limit of strength, a fundamental string, can only hold its own weight, so evaporation time still scales with mass cubed. Small black holes could still convert matter to energy at nearly 100% of mc squared.
- **Holography.** The maximum information in a region scales with its surface area (area over 4Għ), which led to Maldacena's AdS/CFT duality. Brown regards the two descriptions as equally real, though AdS/CFT assumes a negative cosmological constant, unlike our universe.
