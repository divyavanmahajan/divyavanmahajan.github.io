## Thesis

Dominic Cummings, former chief adviser to Boris Johnson and director of Vote Leave, tells Dwarkesh Patel that Western governments cannot act because nobody holds both responsibility and authority, a permanent civil service promotes from within, and ministers spend their time on media theatre. In his view COVID exposed this, yet the system reverted to normal, and real change needs a new group of able people willing to rebuild institutions rather than reform them from inside.

## Key points

- **Bottlenecks at the top.** Cummings says a Defence Secretary can fire only about three people, so only the Prime Minister can force decisions, such as sending grounded airliners to China for PPE in March 2020.
- **Process over outcome.** He describes a data project that took eight to twelve weeks of work and two and a half years of emails about legal permissions, and a four-year fight over Google Docs versus Teams.
- **COVID lessons discarded.** According to Cummings, the vaccine task force, sewage monitoring and rapid testing were wound down, while removing about half a dozen permanent secretaries in summer 2020 was called a "rolling coup".
- **Security and defence.** He claims China's penetration of UK infrastructure, nuclear safety and budgets are worse than MPs know, calls the Watchkeeper drone a failure the MoD admitted privately, and calls aircraft carriers "a joke".
- **How to rebuild.** Cummings would build a parallel procurement system and close the old one, citing his cut of the Department for Education communications team from over 250 to under 50; he calls a war over Taiwan "a completely insane idea".
- **Bismarck as misaligned AI.** He reads Bismarck's career through AI alignment: an intelligence that defeats every safety mechanism and cannot be switched off until the Kaiser dismisses him.
