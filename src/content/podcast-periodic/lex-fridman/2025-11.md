## Overview

November had two long conversations about hard science. Helion CEO David Kirtley made the case that fusion is now mainly an engineering and manufacturing problem, with AI data centres as an early customer. Michael Levin of Tufts argued that intelligence is a continuum to be measured by experiment rather than settled by definition.

## Main themes

### Fusion as an engineering and manufacturing problem

- Kirtley describes Helion's method as magneto-inertial fusion, which he says “does a little bit of both” inertial and magnetic fusion. Machines pulse one to ten times a second, so output can be turned up and down ([Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- The field-reversed configuration traps plasma with its own current. Kirtley says the field must reverse within a millionth of a second, which only semiconductor switching allows ([Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- Helion recovers electricity directly as the plasma pushes back on the magnetic field. Kirtley cites over 95% recovery of input energy and theoretical conversion of 80-85%, against 30-35% for steam turbines ([Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- He stresses speed: seven machines built, a team that grew from 50 to over 500 people, and a workforce that is now half technicians, with in-house power-supply production lines ([Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).

### Fusion safety and power for AI

- Kirtley says a fusion system holds only about one second of fuel, so it cannot run away. An analysis for the NRC of a meteor strike concluded that no evacuation would be needed ([Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- A 2023 deal with Microsoft commits Helion to first electrons for one of its data centres by 2028. The plant will feed the grid the data centre is plugged into ([Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- Kirtley says AI experts tell him the cost of computation tends towards the cost of electricity. Helion is exploring direct DC power to GPUs and sites with 100 generators each ([Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).

### Intelligence as a continuum

- Levin rejects sharp lines between living and non-living, or mind and non-mind: “I don’t believe in any such line.” He says rigid categories now mostly hold science back ([Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- His cognitive light cone measures a system by the biggest goal it can pursue. He ranks systems from clocks and thermostats to dogs and humans by how much they can be persuaded rather than mechanically controlled ([Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- With students, Levin found that a simple sorting algorithm routes around a broken digit, which he calls delayed gratification. He also describes his lab's work on self-organising cell constructs, which he treats as more evidence for the continuum ([Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- He proposes that bodies and machines are interfaces to a structured Platonic space of patterns, and predicts that space will be mapped or shown to be random within about 20 years ([Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
