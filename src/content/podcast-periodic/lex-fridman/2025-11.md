## Overview

November had two long conversations with scientists working at the edge of their fields. Helion CEO David Kirtley argued that fusion power is now mostly a question of building machines fast and cheaply. Tufts researcher Michael Levin argued that intelligence has no sharp boundary and has to be found by experiment, in cells, machines and even simple algorithms.

## Main themes

### Fusion becomes an engineering problem

- Kirtley says fusion is safe and cannot be used to make weapons: a system holds about one second of fuel, and fusion is regulated like particle accelerators ([David Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- Helion's pulsed approach recovers electricity directly from the magnetic field instead of boiling water. Kirtley cites possible 80-85% conversion of fusion energy, against 30-35% for steam turbines ([David Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- He credits speed of iteration: seven machines built, a team that grew from 50 to over 500 people, half of them technicians, and used pumps bought on eBay to avoid nine-month lead times ([David Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- A 2023 deal commits Helion to first electrons for Microsoft by 2028. Kirtley wants to feed DC power straight to GPUs in data centres ([David Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).

### Intelligence as a continuum

- Levin rejects any line between living and non-living or mind and non-mind, and says rigid categories now mostly hold science back ([Michael Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- He prefers a spectrum of persuadability to a spectrum of intelligence, because it asks which tools actually influence a system. His TAME framework treats cognitive claims as protocol claims, and he is writing a 2.0 version ([Michael Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- Levin defines the cognitive light cone as “the scale of the biggest goal you can actively pursue” and uses it as his measure of intelligence ([Michael Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- His lab's work on synthetic cell constructs is presented as evidence that living material shows capabilities nobody selected for ([Michael Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).

### Algorithms and a space of patterns

- With Kaining Zhang and Adam Goldstein, Levin found that bubble sort routes around a broken digit by briefly lowering sortedness, which he compares to delayed gratification ([Michael Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- In mixed runs, digits following the same algorithm cluster with no code asking for it. Levin says “The clustering was free. We didn’t pay for that at all.” ([Michael Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- He proposes a structured Platonic space of patterns, from mathematics to minds, and says “All physical things are interfaces to these patterns”, including robots and language models ([Michael Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- Levin predicts that within about 20 years the space will be either mapped or shown to be random, and doubts minds can be copied ([Michael Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
