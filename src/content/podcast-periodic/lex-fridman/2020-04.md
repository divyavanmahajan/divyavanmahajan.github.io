## Overview

April 2020, recorded as the pandemic began, combined AI, physics and institutions. David Silver told the AlphaGo story from the inside, while Eric Weinstein, Stephen Wolfram and Harry Cliff offered three very different routes to fundamental physics. Jack Dorsey closed the month on payments, Bitcoin and the risks of AI.

## Main themes

### Learning by self-play, and where intelligence comes from

- David Silver frames intelligence as the reinforcement learning problem. AlphaGo Zero, a 50/50 bet, beat the earlier AlphaGo 100 games to zero, and AlphaZero then worked unmodified on chess and shogi ([Silver](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/david-silver-alphago-alphazero-and-deep-reinforcement-learning/)).
- Of the Lee Sedol match, watched by about 100 million people online, Silver says “I had been off in my estimation of how significant this moment was by several orders of magnitude” ([Silver](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/david-silver-alphago-alphazero-and-deep-reinforcement-learning/)).
- Silver points to MuZero, which learns without being given the rules, and to other teams applying AlphaZero to chemical synthesis and quantum computation ([Silver](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/david-silver-alphago-alphazero-and-deep-reinforcement-learning/)).
- Richard Dawkins argues any complex intelligence must come from a gradual process. He sees nothing non-physical in brains and expects any superhuman intelligence to be artificial ([Dawkins](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/richard-dawkins-evolution-intelligence-simulation-and-memes/)).

### New routes to fundamental physics

- Eric Weinstein presents Geometric Unity, a 14-dimensional attempt at a single origin story for physics, but admits he cannot say at what energy its predicted particles would appear ([Weinstein](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/eric-weinstein-geometric-unity-and-the-call-for-new-ideas-leaders-inst/)).
- Stephen Wolfram argues simple rules produce computation as sophisticated as brains, so many outcomes are irreducible. He bets the universe runs on a simple hypergraph rewriting rule ([Wolfram](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/stephen-wolfram-cellular-automata-computation-and-physics/)).
- Harry Cliff describes the LHC as a microscope for the vacuum. He calls the Higgs troublingly fine-tuned, and says LHCb hunts for subtle effects of unseen fields on beauty quarks ([Cliff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/harry-cliff-particle-physics-and-the-large-hadron-collider/)).

### Institutions, outsiders and the pandemic

- Weinstein says the pandemic ends a 75-year great nap of weak institutional leadership. He cites Aaron Swartz's prosecution and argues most PhD programmes should be shut down ([Weinstein](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/eric-weinstein-geometric-unity-and-the-call-for-new-ideas-leaders-inst/)).
- On Elon Musk, Weinstein says “the rest of us have forgotten our capabilities”, and that Mars is a story to remind us we need to leave the planet ([Weinstein](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/eric-weinstein-geometric-unity-and-the-call-for-new-ideas-leaders-inst/)).
- Dmitry Korkin describes how open data and preprints let computational groups share early coronavirus research within weeks, and argues knowledge, not the journal, is becoming the core value ([Korkin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/dmitry-korkin-computational-biology-of-coronavirus/)).

### Payments, Bitcoin and AI risk

- Jack Dorsey says Square's mission is access: data-driven risk models raised merchant approval from roughly 30-40% of applicants to 99% ([Dorsey](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/jack-dorsey-square-cryptocurrency-and-artificial-intelligence/)).
- Dorsey believes the internet deserves a native currency and calls the Bitcoin white paper one of the most important computer science works of recent decades ([Dorsey](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/jack-dorsey-square-cryptocurrency-and-artificial-intelligence/)).
- He sees unexplainable AI decisions and centralised data as the main risks, expects cashiers to follow truck drivers in losing jobs, and backs a $1,000-a-month floor ([Dorsey](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/jack-dorsey-square-cryptocurrency-and-artificial-intelligence/)).
