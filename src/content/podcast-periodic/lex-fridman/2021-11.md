## Overview

November was a wide-ranging month for Lex Fridman. Two episodes looked at autonomous trucking from opposite ends, the worker and the builder, while historians and a novelist discussed money, war and how technology follows historical accident. Two wrestling conversations and a product story from Instagram's co-founder filled out the month.

## Main themes

### Autonomous trucks and the people who drive them

- Sociologist Steve Viscelli says long-haul trucking went from a well-paid Teamsters job to one where many drivers barely earn minimum wage, and that the problem is job quality rather than a driver shortage ([Steve Viscelli](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/steve-viscelli-trucking-and-the-decline-of-the-american-dream/)).
- Viscelli estimates about 300,000 jobs directly at risk from self-driving trucks, but expects bigger shifts in warehouse location, rail freight and electrification. He argues the outcome depends on worker power, not the technology alone ([Steve Viscelli](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/steve-viscelli-trucking-and-the-decline-of-the-american-dream/)).
- Boris Sofman, head of trucking at Waymo, describes narrowly scoped L4 hub-to-hub freeway routes, roughly twice a car's sensors, and about 1,000 simulated miles per real mile. He says evaluation is the hard part ([Boris Sofman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/boris-sofman-waymo-cozmo-self-driving-cars-and-the-future-of-robotics/)).

### History, war and accountability

- Niall Ferguson treats history as a set of forking paths, naming Britain's August 1914 decision to enter the war as the great turning point, and traces money as trust from clay tablets to crypto ([Niall Ferguson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/niall-ferguson-history-of-money-power-war-and-truth/)).
- Robert Crews argues the 2001 invasion of Afghanistan was a panicked mistake and that successive presidents, ending with Biden, were never held accountable for their choices ([Robert Crews](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/robert-crews-afghanistan-taliban-bin-laden-and-war-in-the-middle-east/)).
- Outgoing NIH director Francis Collins defends the agency's oversight and pandemic communication, while Fridman argues leaders should have shown more humility ([Francis Collins](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/francis-collins-national-institutes-of-health-nih/)).

### Technology shaped by accident and design

- Neal Stephenson argues chemical rockets dominate because of the V2 programme and nuclear delivery, and expects solar geoengineering to be pushed by unilateral actors, as in his novel Termination Shock ([Neal Stephenson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/neal-stephenson-sci-fi-space-aliens-ai-vr-the-future-of-humanity/)).
- Kevin Systrom says Instagram won by reading usage data fast, cutting everything but photo sharing, and hiding latency with tricks such as background uploads ([Kevin Systrom](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/kevin-systrom-instagram/)).
- Systrom expects future social networks to be less social, drawing on all content rather than friends' posts. Sofman says Anki's Cozmo won people over through character, not hardware ([Kevin Systrom](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/kevin-systrom-instagram/), [Boris Sofman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/boris-sofman-waymo-cozmo-self-driving-cars-and-the-future-of-robotics/)).

### The psychology of champions

- Ben Askren credits his rise to obsessive, creative problem-solving rather than athleticism, and disagrees with Fridman on drilling, favouring graded live sparring ([Ben Askren](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/ben-askren-wrestling-and-mma/)).
- Iowa coach Tom Brands says hatred of losing and self-chosen repetition separate champions from athletes who only do what the coach asks ([Tom Brands](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/tom-brands-iowa-wrestling/)).
- Askren argues wrestling is far harder than chess for AI because forces and timing are continuous and decided in an instant ([Ben Askren](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/ben-askren-wrestling-and-mma/)).

## Notable episodes

- [Steve Viscelli: Trucking and the Decline of the American Dream](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/steve-viscelli-trucking-and-the-decline-of-the-american-dream/): a grounded look at how automation could change freight work, with six concrete deployment scenarios.
- [Boris Sofman: Waymo, Cozmo, Self-Driving Cars, and the Future of Robotics](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/boris-sofman-waymo-cozmo-self-driving-cars-and-the-future-of-robotics/): candid on why Anki failed and how Waymo validates driverless trucks.
- [Kevin Systrom: Instagram](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/kevin-systrom-instagram/): early product and engineering decisions, plus a view on recommender-driven social networks.
- [Niall Ferguson: History of Money, Power, War, and Truth](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/niall-ferguson-history-of-money-power-war-and-truth/): counterfactual history and a case for new institutions such as the University of Austin.
