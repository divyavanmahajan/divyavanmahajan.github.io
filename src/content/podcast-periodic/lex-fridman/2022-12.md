## Overview

December paired two views of socialism: Michael Malice on the Soviet record and Bhaskar Sunkara on a democratic, market-based version. Coffeezilla dissected the FTX collapse, Noam Brown explained how AI learned to win at poker and Diplomacy, and two astrobiologists looked for life beyond Earth. Cartels and jiu jitsu rounded out the month.

## Main themes

### Communism, past and proposed

- Malice steelmans socialism as a promise that “no one is left behind”, but argues central planning without prices and a state news monopoly bring authoritarianism ([Malice](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/michael-malice-christmas-special/)).
- He describes Stalin as the party politician who pushed socialism in one country, and calls the Holodomor deliberate. Fridman argues the press failure came from journalists' peers at home ([Malice](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/michael-malice-christmas-special/)).
- Sunkara wants worker cooperatives competing in markets, with public banks and multi-party elections. He calls forced collectivisation a disaster and says today's Chinese Communist Party has nothing to do with socialism ([Sunkara](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/bhaskar-sunkara-the-case-for-socialism/)).
- Sunkara praises Bernie Sanders as the clearest communicator on the left; Fridman presses him on incentives and tax burden ([Sunkara](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/bhaskar-sunkara-the-case-for-socialism/)).

### FTX, fraud and trust

- Coffeezilla argues FTX's collapse was designed deception, pointing to about 50 entities and a one-way information window from Alameda Research into FTX ([Coffeezilla](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/coffeezilla-sbf-ftx-fraud-scams-fake-gurus-money-fame-and-power/)).
- He says insiders told him Alameda bought coins ahead of FTX listings, and that customer funds were never properly segregated ([Coffeezilla](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/coffeezilla-sbf-ftx-fraud-scams-fake-gurus-money-fame-and-power/)).
- He calls Andrew Tate's Hustler's University a get-rich-quick scheme and says MLMs are pyramid schemes in structure ([Coffeezilla](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/coffeezilla-sbf-ftx-fraud-scams-fake-gurus-money-fame-and-power/)).
- Coffeezilla and Malice both welcomed the Twitter Files as a check on power ([Coffeezilla](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/coffeezilla-sbf-ftx-fraud-scams-fake-gurus-money-fame-and-power/), [Malice](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/michael-malice-christmas-special/)).

### AI in games of strategy and negotiation

- Noam Brown says Libratus beat top poker pros by approximating a Nash equilibrium, and that adding search was worth a thousandfold bigger precomputed strategy ([Brown](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/noam-brown-ai-vs-humans-in-poker-and-games-of-strategic-negotiation/)).
- Meta's Cicero pairs a language model with intents chosen by a planning model; it ranked second among the 19 players with five or more games ([Brown](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/noam-brown-ai-vs-humans-in-poker-and-games-of-strategic-negotiation/)).
- Brown says lying made the bot score worse because people stopped trusting it, so the team filtered lies out ([Brown](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/noam-brown-ai-vs-humans-in-poker-and-games-of-strategic-negotiation/)).

### Life beyond Earth and its origins

- Nathalie Cabrol argues the search should target the nature of life as information, and uses high-altitude Andean lakes as stand-ins for early Mars ([Cabrol](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/nathalie-cabrol-search-for-alien-life/)).
- Cabrol calls AI a very smart tool built by humans, while Fridman points to AlphaZero and AlphaFold ([Cabrol](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/nathalie-cabrol-search-for-alien-life/)).
- Betül Kaçar treats the cell's translation machinery as biology's oldest computer and says “I'm very optimistic that our community will get a handle of this problem in this decade” ([Kaçar](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/betul-kacar-bet-l-ka-ar-origin-of-life-ancient-dna-panspermia-and-alie/)).
- Both guests discussed panspermia; Kaçar doubts it because it moves the question off Earth, where it cannot be tested ([Kaçar](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/betul-kacar-bet-l-ka-ar-origin-of-life-ancient-dna-panspermia-and-alie/), [Cabrol](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/nathalie-cabrol-search-for-alien-life/)).

### Adversaries and mastery

- Ed Calderon says Mexico's cartel conflict persists through systemic corruption, and that to train a counter-ambush team you make its members good ambushers ([Calderon](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/ed-calderon-mexican-drug-cartels/)).
- Roger Gracie says his simple game is not basic: he only finished reliably with the cross-collar choke from mount years after his black belt ([Gracie](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/roger-gracie-greatest-jiu-jitsu-competitor-of-all-time/)).
