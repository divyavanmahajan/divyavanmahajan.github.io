## Overview

In 2021 the Lex Fridman Podcast ran 101 episodes. Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies were the biggest new thread, central in ten conversations. Consciousness, free will and meaning recurred all year, alongside AI, physics and wrestlers and fighters on mental toughness. Pandemic trust, free speech and power shaped the political conversations.

## Main themes

### Bitcoin, Ethereum and the crypto wave

- Silvio Micali calls Bitcoin a great store of value but too slow for transacting, and argues cryptographic lotteries can fix that ([Silvio Micali](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/03/silvio-micali-cryptocurrency-blockchain-algorand-bitcoin-and-ethereum/)).
- Nic Carter contrasts Ethereum's frequent hard forks with Bitcoin's fixed rules and says “i tend to think the bitcoin is the most disruptive one because it's the most equipped to challenge sovereign currencies” ([Nic Carter](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/04/nic-carter-bitcoin-core-values-layered-scaling-and-blocksize-debates/)).
- Vitalik Buterin and Charles Hoskinson set out proof of stake for Ethereum 2.0 and Cardano ([Vitalik Buterin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/06/vitalik-buterin-ethereum-2-0/), [Charles Hoskinson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/06/charles-hoskinson-cardano/)).
- Alex Gladstein presents Bitcoin as a tool for people under authoritarian regimes, while Lex Fridman questions the community's certainty ([Alex Gladstein](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/10/alex-gladstein-bitcoin-authoritarianism-and-human-rights/)).

### Consciousness, free will and meaning

- Max Tegmark treats consciousness as information processing and hopes science can build a consciousness detector ([Max Tegmark](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/max-tegmark-ai-and-physics/)).
- Sam Harris argues consciousness cannot be an illusion but free will and the self can ([Sam Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/sam-harris-consciousness-free-will-psychedelics-ai-ufos-and-meaning/)); Joscha Bach sees all three as models inside a brain-generated simulation ([Joscha Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/08/joscha-bach-nature-of-reality-dreams-and-consciousness/)).
- John Danaher says “the minute mankind's existence became more or less guaranteed the problems shift from survival to meaning” ([John Danaher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/john-danaher-the-path-to-mastery-in-jiu-jitsu-grappling-judo-and-mma/)). Brian Greene argues a universe without built-in purpose leaves each person free to make their own ([Brian Greene](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/10/brian-greene-quantum-gravity-big-bang-aliens-life-death-and-meaning/)).

### AI: risks, scale and the older routes

- Tegmark says “i think algorithms should never make life or death decisions they should be left to humans” ([Max Tegmark](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/max-tegmark-ai-and-physics/)).
- Wojciech Zaremba backs scaled next-word prediction and Codex ([Wojciech Zaremba](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/08/wojciech-zaremba-openai-codex-gpt-3-robotics-and-the-future-of-ai/)), while Douglas Lenat argues AI needs Cyc's hand-built common-sense rules ([Douglas Lenat](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/09/douglas-lenat-cyc-and-the-quest-to-solve-common-sense-reasoning-in-ai/)).
- Rodney Brooks doubts camera-only driving can go all the way ([Rodney Brooks](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/09/rodney-brooks-robotics/)); Elon Musk argues hard manufacturing work makes camera-only self-driving possible ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/12/elon-musk-spacex-mars-tesla-autopilot-self-driving-robotics-and-ai/)).

### Mental toughness on the mat

- Dan Gable says champions learn relentlessly from losses and create their own luck through preparation ([Dan Gable](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/dan-gable-olympic-wrestling-mental-toughness-the-making-of-champions/)).
- Travis Stevens describes winning nationals with nine broken ribs ([Travis Stevens](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/09/travis-stevens-judo-olympics-and-mental-toughness/)); Jimmy Pedro and Tom Brands point to hating losing more than loving winning ([Jimmy Pedro](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/10/jimmy-pedro-judo-and-the-forging-of-champions/), [Tom Brands](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/11/tom-brands-iowa-wrestling/)).

### String theory and theories of everything

- Cumrun Vafa argues string theory earns credibility through theoretical connections despite no experimental evidence ([Cumrun Vafa](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/07/cumrun-vafa-string-theory/)).
- Peter Woit says it failed because it can accommodate almost anything ([Peter Woit](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/12/peter-woit-theories-of-everything-and-why-string-theory-is-not-even-wr/)); Eric Weinstein previews his Geometric Unity paper ([Eric Weinstein](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/02/eric-weinstein-difficult-conversations-freedom-of-speech-and-physics/)).
