## Overview

February split between space and physics on one side and human behaviour on the other. Tim Dodd and Andrew Strominger covered rockets and black holes, while Paul Conti, Aaron Smith-Levin, Aella and Tim Urban looked at envy, control, sexuality and tribal thinking.

## Main themes

### Rockets and reusability

- Tim Dodd argues SpaceX's lead comes from questioning constraints, iterating on real hardware and treating reusability as essential ([Tim Dodd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-dodd-spacex-starship-rocket-engines-and-future-of-space-travel/)).
- He explains full-flow staged combustion in the Raptor engine and compares Starship's roughly 75 meganewtons of thrust with 45 for the Soviet N1 ([Tim Dodd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-dodd-spacex-starship-rocket-engines-and-future-of-space-travel/)).
- Dodd expects two or three major launch providers in the long run and says firms not building reusable rockets will not survive. He also discusses his place on the dearMoon crew ([Tim Dodd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-dodd-spacex-starship-rocket-engines-and-future-of-space-travel/)).

### Black holes and the limits of theory

- Andrew Strominger sees the clash between quantum mechanics and general relativity, sharpened by black holes, as the central puzzle in physics today ([Andrew Strominger](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/andrew-strominger-black-holes-quantum-gravity-and-theoretical-physics/)).
- He describes his work with Cumrun Vafa counting black hole information through string theory, and with Stephen Hawking and Malcolm Perry on soft hair ([Andrew Strominger](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/andrew-strominger-black-holes-quantum-gravity-and-theoretical-physics/)).
- He calls string theory a stepping stone rather than a final theory, and tells Fridman that AI researchers have a real chance to shape how their work affects society ([Andrew Strominger](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/andrew-strominger-black-holes-quantum-gravity-and-theoretical-physics/)).

### Envy, narcissism and control

- Paul Conti argues that orchestrated evil is driven by envy rooted in inadequacy, often learned through childhood trauma. Fridman questions whether jealousy and envy really differ in kind ([Paul Conti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/paul-conti-narcissism-sociopathy-envy-and-the-nature-of-good-and-evil/)).
- Conti says society underinvests in mental health and urges people to interview therapists as empowered consumers ([Paul Conti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/paul-conti-narcissism-sociopathy-envy-and-the-nature-of-good-and-evil/)).
- Aaron Smith-Levin grants that many Scientologists find auditing helpful, but argues that information control and forced family disconnection make the organisation harmful ([Scientology](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/aaron-smith-levin-scientology/)).
- He estimates no more than 35,000 active Scientologists against a peak of about 100,000, and describes his own refusal to disconnect from his mother ([Scientology](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/aaron-smith-levin-scientology/)).

### Sexuality, measured

- Aella argues that large online surveys with open methods and data can study sexuality more honestly than small academic studies ([Aella](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/aella-sex-work-onlyfans-porn-escorting-dating-and-human-sexuality/)).
- Her fetish survey grew from 40,000 to 500,000 respondents after a result went viral on TikTok. Fridman pushes back with a more romantic view of love ([Aella](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/aella-sex-work-onlyfans-porn-escorting-dating-and-human-sexuality/)).

### Tribalism and liberal culture

- Tim Urban says how a belief is formed matters more than where it sits on the left-right spectrum, and that low-rung tribal thinking weakens liberal democracy ([Tim Urban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-urban-tribalism-marxism-liberalism-social-justice-and-politics/)).
- He argues that firing and ostracism inside institutions push views underground, and that the First Amendment alone cannot protect free speech without a liberal culture ([Tim Urban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-urban-tribalism-marxism-liberalism-social-justice-and-politics/)).
