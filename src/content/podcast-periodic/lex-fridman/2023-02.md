## Overview

February leaned towards psychology and belief. A psychiatrist on envy and trauma, Tim Urban on tribal thinking and a former Scientologist on control set the tone, with Hitler as a recurring case study and trauma also surfacing in Aella's episode. Two science conversations, on SpaceX's Starship and on black holes, balanced the month.

## Main themes

### Envy, trauma and the roots of evil

- Paul Conti argues that orchestrated evil is driven by envy, the wish to pull others down to soothe one's own inadequacy. He uses Hitler as his main example ([Paul Conti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/paul-conti-narcissism-sociopathy-envy-and-the-nature-of-good-and-evil/)).
- Conti says narcissism, though rarely diagnosed, causes more than half of the destructive things he sees. Fridman pushes back that envy and jealousy are the same flame at different strengths ([Paul Conti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/paul-conti-narcissism-sociopathy-envy-and-the-nature-of-good-and-evil/)).
- Drawing on his brother's suicide, Conti says trauma teaches false lessons of shame, and the first step is putting it into words. He calls child abuse “potentially the greatest cause of suffering directly and indirectly on the face of the planet” ([Paul Conti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/paul-conti-narcissism-sociopathy-envy-and-the-nature-of-good-and-evil/)).
- Aella describes a controlling evangelical childhood and credits one psychedelic experience with reframing her own trauma ([Aella](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/aella-sex-work-onlyfans-porn-escorting-dating-and-human-sexuality/)).

### Tribes, orthodoxy and control

- Tim Urban says how a belief is formed matters more than where it sits on the left-right axis. Low-rung tribal thinking on both sides, he argues, weakens the culture liberal democracy needs ([Tim Urban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-urban-tribalism-marxism-liberalism-social-justice-and-politics/)).
- Urban contrasts criticism culture, which attacks ideas, with cancel culture, which attacks people. He says “First Amendment plus cancel culture equals you might as well have government censorship” ([Tim Urban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-urban-tribalism-marxism-liberalism-social-justice-and-politics/)).
- Aaron Smith-Levin grants that many members find Scientology's auditing helpful, but argues information control and forced disconnection from family make it destructive ([Scientology](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/aaron-smith-levin-scientology/)).
- He estimates no more than 35,000 active Scientologists worldwide, down from a peak of about 100,000, and calls the claim that the tech always works unfalsifiable ([Scientology](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/aaron-smith-levin-scientology/)).

### Starship and rocket engineering

- Tim Dodd says SpaceX's lead comes from questioning constraints and iterating on real hardware. He doubts the company could keep its Mars drive without Elon Musk ([Tim Dodd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-dodd-spacex-starship-rocket-engines-and-future-of-space-travel/)).
- He explains full-flow staged combustion in Raptor and Starship's 33-engine booster, and expects only reusable launch providers to survive ([Tim Dodd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-dodd-spacex-starship-rocket-engines-and-future-of-space-travel/)).
- Dodd, a dearMoon crew member, notes Starship has no launch abort system and wants to see many successful flights first ([Tim Dodd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-dodd-spacex-starship-rocket-engines-and-future-of-space-travel/)).

### Black holes and the limits of theory

- Andrew Strominger says the places where theories break down, above all the singularity, drive discovery. He recalls that Einstein doubted both black holes and gravitational waves ([Strominger](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/andrew-strominger-black-holes-quantum-gravity-and-theoretical-physics/)).
- He describes reproducing the Bekenstein-Hawking information count with string theory as a moment of truth, and his soft hair work with Hawking and Perry on the information paradox ([Strominger](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/andrew-strominger-black-holes-quantum-gravity-and-theoretical-physics/)).
- Strominger sees string theory as a stepping stone, not a final theory, and tells Fridman AI researchers can shape how their work affects society ([Strominger](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/andrew-strominger-black-holes-quantum-gravity-and-theoretical-physics/)).

### Studying sexuality with open surveys

- Aella argues large online surveys with open data reveal more than small academic studies. Her fetish survey grew to 500,000 respondents after going viral on TikTok ([Aella](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/aella-sex-work-onlyfans-porn-escorting-dating-and-human-sexuality/)).
- She describes camming and escorting as marketing businesses, and Fridman offers a more romantic view of love than her data-driven one ([Aella](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/aella-sex-work-onlyfans-porn-escorting-dating-and-human-sexuality/)).
