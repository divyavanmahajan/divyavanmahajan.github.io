## Overview

July had only two episodes, both about practical competence. Ivanka Trump traced a career through real estate, fashion and the White House, and Alone winner Jordan Jonas explained how he survived 77 days in the wild. Fridman closed the month with comments on the attempted assassination of Donald Trump.

## Main themes

### Details, people on the ground and policy

- Ivanka Trump argues that walking the site and asking the people doing the work matters more than trusting the plan. On the Old Post Office in Washington she found columns that blueprints had missed ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/)).
- She recounts a duct worker calling the atrium heating and cooling over-designed, which led to an overhaul mid-construction. Fridman links this to flat organisations at Elon Musk's companies ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/)).
- She credits the doubled child tax credit, paid family leave, retraining commitments and anti-trafficking laws as her policy work, and describes 23 further pardons or commutations after Alice Johnson's ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/)).
- She says she never answers attacks and is staying out of the 2024 race to put her three children first ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/)).

### Survival, hardship and purpose

- Jordan Jonas says survival comes down to adaptability, learning fast from mistakes and staying active. After missing his first moose he built a fence to funnel game and shot one with a bow on day 20 ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).
- He found not knowing when the show would end the hardest part, and learned that rabbit alone cannot sustain you because it lacks fat ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).
- His skills came from years with a native trapper and Evenki reindeer herders in Siberia. He credits The Gulag Archipelago with teaching him to pursue duty rather than happiness ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).
- In his closing AMA, Fridman calls extreme division the real enemy and asks for transparency on the Secret Service failure ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).
