## Overview

January 2021 mixed science and AI with politics and sport. Max Tegmark made the case for intelligible, aligned AI, Avi Loeb argued that 'Oumuamua may be alien technology, and Tim Dillon took on deplatforming in the weeks after Trump's bans. Lex Fridman also recorded his first solo AMA, and Dan Gable talked about what makes champions.

## Main themes

### AI risk, alignment and intelligible AI

- Max Tegmark splits alignment into three open problems: machines must understand, adopt and retain our goals. He argues the same incentive problem applies to corporations and governments, citing Teddy Roosevelt's trust-busting ([Max Tegmark](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/max-tegmark-ai-and-physics/)).
- Tegmark calls scaling ever bigger black boxes reckless and puts the chance of extinction at about 50 percent on that route. He says the risk is competence, not malice, and that “the robots are coming right now but they're mainly not coming to kill us they're coming to hack us” ([Max Tegmark](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/max-tegmark-ai-and-physics/)).
- He defends Elon Musk as a positive visionary whose “summoning the demon” remark was taken out of context, and calls 2020 a turning point for autonomous weapons ([Max Tegmark](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/max-tegmark-ai-and-physics/)).
- In his AMA, Fridman argues that AI systems that live among humans will need the possibility of suffering, while task-specific systems will not ([AMA](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/ama1-lex-fridman-ask-me-anything-ama-january-2021/)).

### AI as a tool for science

- Tegmark's AI Feynman trains a neural network, then breaks the learned formula into simpler pieces. It recovered all 100 test equations from the Feynman Lectures and can be pip installed ([Max Tegmark](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/max-tegmark-ai-and-physics/)).
- Tegmark also expects physics to help AI through hardware, such as Marin Soljačić's optical chip ([Max Tegmark](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/max-tegmark-ai-and-physics/)).
- Dmitry Korkin ranks DeepMind's AlphaFold 2 among the top AI achievements for scientific impact, while saying the problem is not solved for larger, more complex cases ([Dmitry Korkin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/dmitry-korkin-evolution-of-proteins-viruses-life-and-ai/)).

### 'Oumuamua and the search for alien technology

- Avi Loeb lists the anomalies of the first interstellar object seen near Earth: a flat shape, no cometary tail and a push beyond gravity. He argues sunlight on a very thin light sail fits best ([Avi Loeb](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/avi-loeb-aliens-black-holes-and-the-mystery-of-the-oumuamua/)).
- Loeb says science should treat anomalies as gifts and stop treating the search for technological signatures as taboo. He proposes looking for industrial pollutants in exoplanet atmospheres ([Avi Loeb](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/avi-loeb-aliens-black-holes-and-the-mystery-of-the-oumuamua/)).

### Platforms, power and dissent

- Tim Dillon calls Trump's permanent bans an overreaction that made Twitter more powerful than a president. He and Fridman both find Amazon removing Parler from AWS troubling ([Tim Dillon](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/tim-dillon-comedy-power-conspiracy-theories-and-freedom/)).
- On Alex Jones, Dillon says “the greater danger for society comes again from stifling all dissent”. Fridman suggests a transparent, jury-like review ([Tim Dillon](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/tim-dillon-comedy-power-conspiracy-theories-and-freedom/)).

### Humans and machines at work

- Dan Kokotov says Rev's human transcribers correct an ASR draft, and their paid work produces the labelled data its models learn from. He reports about a 14% word error rate against a 2-3% human target ([Dan Kokotov](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/dan-kokotov-speech-recognition-with-ai-and-humans/)).
- Dan Gable credits his 1972 gold, won without giving up a point, to years of winning in practice and to combining toughness with Russian technical science ([Dan Gable](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/01/dan-gable-olympic-wrestling-mental-toughness-the-making-of-champions/)).
