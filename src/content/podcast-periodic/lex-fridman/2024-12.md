## Overview

December had two episodes with little in common. Saagar Enjeti read the 2024 election through history and argued that Washington's institutions will limit the new administration. Astrophysicist Adam Frank explained why the search for alien life is now a question for data.

## Main themes

### The election and the limits of presidential power

- Saagar Enjeti attributes Trump's win to inflation, a global anti-incumbent mood, immigration and above all a cultural backlash against elites, with education now dividing voters more than race ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).
- He describes DOGE as an unfunded advisory commission that depends on Republican appropriators. With entitlements off the table, he argues real savings would need a Pentagon procurement overhaul ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).
- Drawing on Graham Allison's Essence of Decision, Enjeti argues that whoever drafts the president's options holds the power ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).
- On immigration he supports a merit-based points system and mandatory E-Verify, while doubting the public is ready for mass deportation ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).

### Searching for life beyond Earth

- Adam Frank says exoplanet surveys have pinned down the astronomical terms of the Drake equation. His 2016 paper with Woody Sullivan concludes we are unlikely to be the first civilization ([Adam Frank](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/adam-frank-alien-civilizations-and-the-search-for-extraterrestrial-lif/)).
- He leads the first NASA grant for exoplanet technosignatures, building a library that includes chlorofluorocarbons, city lights and solar panel glints ([Adam Frank](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/adam-frank-alien-civilizations-and-the-search-for-extraterrestrial-lif/)).
- Frank says SETI has searched only a tiny fraction of the sky so far. He backs open study of UAPs but says no claim meets scientific standards; Fridman is more open to pilot testimony ([Adam Frank](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/adam-frank-alien-civilizations-and-the-search-for-extraterrestrial-lif/)).
- In his Blind Spot argument, Frank says science has forgotten its roots in lived experience, citing quantum mechanics and time ([Adam Frank](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/adam-frank-alien-civilizations-and-the-search-for-extraterrestrial-lif/)).
