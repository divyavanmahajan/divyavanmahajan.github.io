## Overview

December paired a post-election political conversation with an astrophysics one. Saagar Enjeti read Trump's victory as a cultural realignment and argued Washington's institutions will limit what he can change. Adam Frank argued that exoplanet data has turned the question of alien life into one that can be tested.

## Main themes

### Reading the 2024 election

- Saagar Enjeti says Trump's win makes him “the most important and transformative figure in American politics since FDR”, and calls him one of the most racially depolarizing figures in US history ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).
- He defines MAGA as a rejection of cultural elitism, with education rather than race or income now dividing voters ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).
- Enjeti calls Biden the second-worst modern president, blaming his arrogance, and says Harris was punished for her 2019 positions despite moving to the centre ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).

### Immigration, DOGE and the limits of the presidency

- Enjeti argues immigration may be second only to inflation in explaining Trump's win, and backs a merit-based points system like Australia's or Canada's ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).
- He describes DOGE as an unfunded, non-statutory body that cannot compel anyone and depends on Republican appropriators. Real savings, he says, would need Pentagon procurement reform ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).
- Drawing on Graham Allison, he argues whoever drafts the president's options holds the power, and that the deep state is “not even individuals. It’s actually an ideology” ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).

### Are we alone?

- Adam Frank says exoplanet surveys have shown that every star has planets and about one in five has one in the habitable zone. With 10 billion trillion such planets, his 2016 paper with Woody Sullivan concludes we are not the first civilization unless the odds per planet are below 10 to the minus 22 ([Adam Frank](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/adam-frank-alien-civilizations-and-the-search-for-extraterrestrial-lif/)).
- He argues there is no indirect Fermi paradox because nobody has funded a real search, and says the Hard Steps model fails since life and planets co-evolve ([Adam Frank](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/adam-frank-alien-civilizations-and-the-search-for-extraterrestrial-lif/)).
- Frank leads the first NASA grant for exoplanet technosignatures. He supports open study of UAPs but says no claimed evidence meets scientific standards ([Adam Frank](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/adam-frank-alien-civilizations-and-the-search-for-extraterrestrial-lif/)).
