## Overview

November was dominated by war and geopolitics a month after October 7. Elon Musk and John Mearsheimer both discussed Gaza, Ukraine and China, and Michael Malice closed the month with antisemitism, human nature and a pushback on Mearsheimer's realism. Musk's episode also carried the month's AI content: Grok, oversight of AI labs and electricity as the next bottleneck.

## Main themes

### China as a rising power

- Musk expects China's economy to become two or three times larger than that of the US, and says China has been clear it will incorporate Taiwan peacefully or militarily ([Musk](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/elon-musk-war-ai-aliens-politics-physics-video-games-and-humanity/)).
- Mearsheimer thinks the Taiwan Strait makes invasion hard and says the US should defend Taiwan to protect its alliances. Deterrence, he argues, only needs to make Chinese victory costly ([Mearsheimer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/john-mearsheimer-israel-palestine-russia-ukraine-china-nato-and-ww3/)).
- He jokes that he is “basically a rock and roll star in China” because the Chinese are realists ([Mearsheimer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/john-mearsheimer-israel-palestine-russia-ukraine-china-nato-and-ww3/)).

### Offensive realism, Ukraine and Gaza

- Mearsheimer argues states under anarchy maximise relative power, built on population size and wealth. He separates this structural view from Morgenthau's human nature realism ([Mearsheimer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/john-mearsheimer-israel-palestine-russia-ukraine-china-nato-and-ww3/)).
- He blames NATO expansion, especially the 2008 Bucharest pledge, for the Ukraine war and expects at best a frozen conflict. Musk expects a truce close to current lines ([Mearsheimer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/john-mearsheimer-israel-palestine-russia-ukraine-china-nato-and-ww3/), [Musk](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/elon-musk-war-ai-aliens-politics-physics-video-games-and-humanity/)).
- Mearsheimer traces October 7 to the occupation, calls Israel's Gaza campaign a massacre but not a genocide, and doubts Israel can finish off Hamas ([Mearsheimer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/john-mearsheimer-israel-palestine-russia-ukraine-china-nato-and-ww3/)).
- Musk says Hamas wanted to provoke an overreaction and recommends Israel “engage in the most conspicuous acts of kindness possible”, citing the Berlin Airlift ([Musk](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/elon-musk-war-ai-aliens-politics-physics-video-games-and-humanity/)).

### Grok, oversight and the power bottleneck

- Musk says Grok is modelled on The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy and was trained on 8,000 A100s. Fridman notes a small team built a close to state-of-the-art system quickly ([Musk](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/elon-musk-war-ai-aliens-politics-physics-video-games-and-humanity/)).
- Musk wants an objective third-party referee for leading AI labs, and leans towards open-sourcing models, possibly with a delay of about six months ([Musk](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/elon-musk-war-ai-aliens-politics-physics-video-games-and-humanity/)).
- He predicts a transformer shortage in about a year and wider electricity shortages in about two, and says useful compute per watt is what counts ([Musk](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/elon-musk-war-ai-aliens-politics-physics-video-games-and-humanity/)).

### Antisemitism and human nature

- Malice argues Jew hatred differs from other bigotry because it openly calls for killing. His remedy is an ambassador programme of being a good counterexample to stereotypes ([Malice](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/michael-malice-thanksgiving-pirate-special/)).
- Malice says most people are “almost fundamentally deranged” beneath a veneer of civilisation; Fridman counters that the communal desire for good outweighs tribal hate ([Malice](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/michael-malice-thanksgiving-pirate-special/)).
- Against Mearsheimer, Malice argues American influence comes more from exported culture than from military bases ([Malice](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/michael-malice-thanksgiving-pirate-special/)).
