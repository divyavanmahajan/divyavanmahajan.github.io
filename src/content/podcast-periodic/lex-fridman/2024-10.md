## Overview

October opened with the one AI episode of the month, a long technical conversation with the four Cursor founders. The other four episodes turned to history, belief and politics: Jordan Peterson on Nietzsche and meaning, Graham Hancock on a possible lost Ice Age civilization, Bernie Sanders on money in US politics, and historian Rick Spence on intelligence services and secret societies.

## Main themes

### Building an AI code editor

- Michael Truell explains why Cursor forked VS Code: as a plugin, you are “very limited in the control you have over a code editor” ([Cursor Team](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/cursor-team-future-of-programming-with-ai/)).
- Aman Sanger says Cursor Tab aims to remove low-entropy edits by predicting the next change and where to jump. It runs on small sparse (MoE) models with speculative edits ([Cursor Team](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/cursor-team-future-of-programming-with-ai/)).
- The team says frontier models are poor at exact diffs and at finding bugs, so Cursor trains its own Apply model. Truell calls SWE-Bench heavily contaminated and relies on human checks ([Cursor Team](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/cursor-team-future-of-programming-with-ai/)).
- Arvid Lunnemark notes that over 80% of users are on Windows machines, which limits local models, and hopes for homomorphic encryption to protect prompts ([Cursor Team](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/cursor-team-future-of-programming-with-ai/)).

### Meaning, ideology and the death of God

- Jordan Peterson reads Nietzsche's death of God as a warning: without a unifying ethos, societies fragment or something else rises to unify them ([Jordan Peterson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/jordan-peterson-nietzsche-hitler-god-psychopathy-suffering-meaning/)).
- He argues communism fails at scale because a large society needs distributed cognition, though its axioms may work in families and small tribes ([Jordan Peterson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/jordan-peterson-nietzsche-hitler-god-psychopathy-suffering-meaning/)).
- Peterson describes the divine as calling and conscience, and says meaning comes from “the hardest possible available adventure voluntarily undertaken” ([Jordan Peterson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/jordan-peterson-nietzsche-hitler-god-psychopathy-suffering-meaning/)).

### Hidden history: lost civilizations and secret networks

- Graham Hancock presents, as a hypothesis, a seafaring Ice Age civilization erased by the Younger Dryas cataclysm, citing Göbekli Tepe and Giza. He says he was perhaps too harsh on archaeology in season 1 of Ancient Apocalypse ([Graham Hancock](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/graham-hancock-lost-civilization-of-the-ice-age-ancient-human-history/)).
- Hancock also calls shamanism the earliest science and talks about his many ayahuasca experiences ([Graham Hancock](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/graham-hancock-lost-civilization-of-the-ice-age-ancient-human-history/)).
- Rick Spence argues spy agencies, secret societies and cults share the same levers: secrecy, recruitment, ritual and ego. He says the CIA learned in the 1970s to fund sensitive research through private intermediaries ([Rick Spence](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/rick-spence-cia-kgb-illuminati-secret-societies-cults-conspiracies/)).
- Spence calls Bohemian Grove a place rather than an organization, but says it is “a perfect private venue to vet people for political office” ([Rick Spence](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/rick-spence-cia-kgb-illuminati-secret-societies-cults-conspiracies/)).

### Money, power and the Democratic Party

- Bernie Sanders argues the US is drifting toward oligarchy, with wealthy donors and lobbyists setting the agenda. He wants Citizens United overturned and public funding of elections ([Bernie Sanders](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/bernie-sanders-interview/)).
- He sees the Democratic Party split between corporate and progressive wings, and says Democrats have not answered working-class anger ([Bernie Sanders](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/bernie-sanders-interview/)).
- Hitler came up twice: Peterson on power ideologies, and Spence on the Thule Society and the army's role in the early Nazi party ([Jordan Peterson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/jordan-peterson-nietzsche-hitler-god-psychopathy-suffering-meaning/), [Rick Spence](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/rick-spence-cia-kgb-illuminati-secret-societies-cults-conspiracies/)).
