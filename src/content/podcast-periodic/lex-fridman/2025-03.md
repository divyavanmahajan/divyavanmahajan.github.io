## Overview

March was mostly a politics month for Lex Fridman. He interviewed India's prime minister, two liberal writers on the state of the Democrats and DOGE, and Douglas Murray on the wars in Ukraine and Gaza. The one technical episode, with ThePrimeagen, asked what AI coding tools do to a programmer's skill.

## Main themes

### Leaders and how they govern

- Narendra Modi presents his career, from poverty in Vadnagar to the prime ministership, as one continuous commitment to service drawn from Swami Vivekananda and the RSS. Fridman mostly lets him set out his own account ([Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)).
- Modi links Elon Musk's DOGE to his own reforms, claiming 100 million fake or duplicate welfare names were removed through direct benefit transfer. He also credits his refusal to print money during COVID with sparing India high inflation ([Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)).
- Modi argues India's engineers, 5G rollout and 200 space startups make it essential to AI ([Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)).

### Ukraine, Gaza and the search for peace

- Modi says he can tell both Putin and Zelensky that the war will not be settled on the battlefield, and presents India as nation-first but peace-seeking ([Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)).
- Douglas Murray, who watched the Trump-Zelenskyy Oval Office meeting from a Ukrainian dugout, calls it premature. He expects Ukraine to cede eastern territory and doubts any ceasefire would hold ([Douglas Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/douglas-murray-putin-zelenskyy-trump-israel-netanyahu-hamas-gaza/)).
- On Gaza, Murray argues Hamas spent 18 years building tunnels rather than a state and rejects the idea that proportionality means equal casualties. Fridman keeps pushing for negotiation and empathy for civilians on every side ([Douglas Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/douglas-murray-putin-zelenskyy-trump-israel-netanyahu-hamas-gaza/)).

### Abundance, Democrats and DOGE

- Ezra Klein and Derek Thompson argue the American left has become good at blocking rather than building, and that blue states fail on cost of living because their own rules choke housing and clean energy ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).
- Thompson cites Chicago spending $11 billion on 10,000 affordable units and a rural broadband programme that only 3 of 56 states and jurisdictions had completed by November 2024 ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).
- Both see DOGE as a drive to centralise power rather than an efficiency effort. Fridman suggests the destruction may be a needed first step and defends Musk's engineering culture at Twitter ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).

### Skill, AI and attention

- ThePrimeagen (Michael Paulson) stopped using GitHub Copilot when he noticed he was autopiloting and adding bugs. He worries juniors who hand everything to AI will cap their skill at the models' level ([ThePrimeagen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/theprimeagen-programming-ai-adhd-productivity-addiction-and-god/)).
- Fridman broadly agrees but argues that working with AI is itself a skill worth practising ([ThePrimeagen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/theprimeagen-programming-ai-adhd-productivity-addiction-and-god/)).
- Klein makes a similar point from outside programming: AI may make people less capable by automating the hardest parts of thinking, such as reading and writing a first draft ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).
