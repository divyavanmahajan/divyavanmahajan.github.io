## Overview

August was a short month with two long conversations about the mind. Joscha Bach looked at minds from the AI side, judging today's language models and arguing for building conscious AI. Andrew Huberman looked at them from the human side, through the subconscious, loyalty and relationships.

## Main themes

### Language models and conscious AI

- Bach says “ChatGPT is like an intern that I have to micromanage”, though he found it faster than Stack Overflow when writing a Swift tool he could not otherwise have written ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/)).
- He calls transformer models ugly and brutalist, a brute-force strategy like Claude Shannon's for chess, and thinks their gaps in real-time learning are solvable without new algorithms ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/)).
- Bach describes language models as golems with nobody home. He would build small conscious systems from self-organisation principles and says “it would be very important to build conscious AI and do this as the primary goal” ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/)).

### Doom, alignment and openness

- Bach finds most rebuttals of Eliezer Yudkowsky unconvincing, but says Yudkowsky is tied to a narrow human aesthetic ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/)).
- He maps AI fears onto his seven stages of lucidity and argues that RLHF cannot hardwire the values of a planetary AI ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/)).
- Bach warns against licensing model training and is not worried by Meta open-sourcing Llama. He also criticises Elon Musk's decisions at Twitter, which Fridman raised again with Huberman ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/), [Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/andrew-huberman-relationships-drama-betrayal-sex-and-love/)).

### The subconscious and honest relationships

- Citing psychiatrist Paul Conti, Huberman says the subconscious drives most decisions, and calls his series with Conti the most important work of his career ([Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/andrew-huberman-relationships-drama-betrayal-sex-and-love/)).
- He separates explicit agreements from hidden, resentful ones: “Covert contracts are the signature of everything bad” ([Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/andrew-huberman-relationships-drama-betrayal-sex-and-love/)).
- Huberman reports that friends in long relationships built friendship first, and argues that peace at home and focus at work feed each other ([Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/andrew-huberman-relationships-drama-betrayal-sex-and-love/)).
