## Overview

November so far has two episodes. Michael Kofman and Preston Stewart assess the Russia-Ukraine war in its fifth year, arguing that Ukraine is having its best year on the battlefield. Former jewel thief Larry Lawton explains how heists really work and argues that the US prison system needs reform.

## Main themes

### Drones and the kill zone

- Kofman says statistics show drones inflict about 80% of losses, but Ukraine's defence still relies on minefields and artillery as well ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- He estimates one side alone flies 8,000-10,000 FPV drones a day, and describes a drone engagement zone of roughly 15 km either side of the front that troops call the kill zone ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- It is not an autonomous war: almost every drone is flown by a small team, and destroying a tank can take dozens of small drones. Tactics change on a three-to-four-month cycle ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).

### Russian tactics and false maps

- Stewart relays an American volunteer's view that Russian infiltration gains ground at the cost of 90-95% of the soldiers sent. Kofman says Ukraine cannot fight that way because it lacks the manpower ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- Kofman says Russian maps put their lines 10-15 km ahead of reality, and that autumn offensives intensify because commanders are “pressed to actually take the territory that they’ve been claiming to control” ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- He calls Bakhmut a classical siege in which Wagner wore down defenders with convict assault groups, but argues its loss was not critical: Russia then needed a year to reach the next town ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).

### US policy and the path to peace

- Both guests criticise the Trump administration for giving up leverage. Kofman says its engagement has been fitful and distracted by the Iran war, though he credits it with prioritising an end to the war ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- Kofman expects Putin to wait on the winter strike campaign and European elections, and warns that a weak ceasefire could lead to a third war ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).

### Heists, the mob and GTA 6

- Lawton cased stores for two to six weeks before robbing them and says “the first thing you do in a robbery is you work backwards” from the exit ([Larry Lawton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/larry-lawton-gta-6-crime-jewel-heists-prison-corrupt-cops-mafia-miami/)).
- He worked with the Gambino family, whose bosses banned robberies in New York because stores there paid for protection. He says Miami police at the time were widely corrupt ([Larry Lawton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/larry-lawton-gta-6-crime-jewel-heists-prison-corrupt-cops-mafia-miami/)).
- He critiques the GTA 6 trailer as unrealistic and hopes the game shows proper casing ([Larry Lawton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/larry-lawton-gta-6-crime-jewel-heists-prison-corrupt-cops-mafia-miami/)).

### Prisons and mass incarceration

- Lawton cites 1.9 million US prisoners and contrasts this with countries such as Switzerland, Sweden and Germany, which he says have less crime with fewer prisoners ([Larry Lawton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/larry-lawton-gta-6-crime-jewel-heists-prison-corrupt-cops-mafia-miami/)).
- He describes 11 months in solitary during 12 years in federal prison, notes that guards get three weeks of training, and proposes unannounced inspection teams that include a former inmate ([Larry Lawton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/larry-lawton-gta-6-crime-jewel-heists-prison-corrupt-cops-mafia-miami/)).
