## Overview

November so far has two episodes. Michael Kofman and Preston Stewart assessed the Russia-Ukraine war in its fifth year, arguing that 2026 is Ukraine's best year on the battlefield and Russia's worst. Former jewel thief Larry Lawton then explained how heists really work and why he thinks the US prison system needs reform.

## Main themes

### Drones and the kill zone

- Kofman says drones inflict about 80% of losses, “However, it is really a combination of things”, with minefields and artillery still essential ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- He estimates 8,000 to 10,000 FPV drones a day for one side alone, and a drone engagement zone of roughly 15 km either side of the front that troops call the kill zone ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- It is not an autonomous war: almost every drone is flown by a small team, and unjammable fibre-optic drones are maybe 15% or less of the total ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).

### How Russia fights and reports

- Stewart relays a volunteer's view that infiltration tactics take ground while losing most of the soldiers. Kofman says Ukraine cannot fight that way because it lacks the manpower ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- Kofman says Russian maps put their lines 10-15 km ahead of reality, so offensives intensify in autumn as commanders try to take ground they already claimed ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- He calls Bakhmut a classical siege and argues its loss was not critical, since Russia needed a year to reach Chasiv Yar ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).

### Endurance and the politics of peace

- Russia spends 44% of its budget on the war, while Ukraine depends on Western money and intelligence, with Europeans now paying for US weapons ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
- Both guests say the Trump administration gave up leverage. Kofman credits it with prioritising an end to the war but says its engagement is fitful, and warns that a weak ceasefire could lead to a third war ([Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).

### Heists, the Mafia and prison

- Lawton says heists depend on studying people, not technology. He cased stores for weeks and planned from the escape backwards: “the first thing you do in a robbery is you work backwards” ([Larry Lawton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/larry-lawton-gta-6-crime-jewel-heists-prison-corrupt-cops-mafia-miami/)).
- He worked with the Gambino family, whose bosses forbade robbing in New York, and says Miami police were widely corrupt at the time ([Larry Lawton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/larry-lawton-gta-6-crime-jewel-heists-prison-corrupt-cops-mafia-miami/)).
- He critiques the GTA 6 trailer as unrealistic, cites 1.9 million US prisoners, and proposes unannounced inspections by a team including a former inmate and a guard ([Larry Lawton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/larry-lawton-gta-6-crime-jewel-heists-prison-corrupt-cops-mafia-miami/)).
