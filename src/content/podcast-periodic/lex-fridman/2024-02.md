## Overview

February was mostly politics, with three of four episodes touching Joe Biden, Donald Trump or the Gaza war. Omar Suleiman, Bill Ackman and Tucker Carlson each pushed a strong view on war, institutions or the press, while Marc Raibert's robotics conversation was the month's one technical episode.

## Main themes

### Gaza, October 7 and the US election

- Omar Suleiman argues the war did not begin on October 7 but grew out of occupation, blockade and settlement expansion. He says US media cover the hostages and leave out what followed: “October 8th is missing. October 9th is missing.” ([Omar Suleiman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/omar-suleiman-palestine-gaza-oct-7-israel-resistance-faith-islam/)).
- Suleiman says the United States is not an honest broker because it funds Israel. He argues Benjamin Netanyahu is prolonging the war for his political career, and that the deeper problem is the occupation itself ([Omar Suleiman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/omar-suleiman-palestine-gaza-oct-7-israel-resistance-faith-islam/)).
- He expects Muslim voters to punish Biden in November without seeing Trump as a saviour, noting Trump moved the embassy to Jerusalem ([Omar Suleiman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/omar-suleiman-palestine-gaza-oct-7-israel-resistance-faith-islam/)).
- Bill Ackman comes at October 7 from the campus side. He says he wanted Harvard's president out for failing to stop rising antisemitism, and contrasts Harvard's response with Dartmouth's ([Bill Ackman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/bill-ackman-investing-financial-battles-harvard-dei-x-free-speech/)).

### Robots: athletic first, cognitive next

- Marc Raibert splits intelligence into an athletic part and a cognitive part. He says Boston Dynamics “has set the standard for what athletic intelligence can be”, but most robots remain cognitively dumb ([Marc Raibert](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/marc-raibert-boston-dynamics-and-the-future-of-robotics/)).
- He traces the company from pogo-stick robots and a bootstrapped simulation business, with no investors until Google bought it after 20 years, to BigDog and about 1,500 deployed Spots ([Marc Raibert](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/marc-raibert-boston-dynamics-and-the-future-of-robotics/)).
- The Boston Dynamics AI Institute aims to join the two kinds of intelligence through "stepping stones to moonshots", such as robots learning bicycle repair by watching people ([Marc Raibert](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/marc-raibert-boston-dynamics-and-the-future-of-robotics/)).

### Investing, governance and incentives

- Ackman describes activist investing as restoring the balance of power between owners and managers, with examples from Wendy's, General Growth Properties and the Canadian Pacific proxy fight ([Bill Ackman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/bill-ackman-investing-financial-battles-harvard-dei-x-free-speech/)).
- He recounts his worst stretch, Valeant plus the Carl Icahn-backed squeeze on his Herbalife short, and says Icahn's motive was to hurt him ([Bill Ackman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/bill-ackman-investing-financial-battles-harvard-dei-x-free-speech/)).
- He saw Alphabet as mispriced after the poor Bard demo, while calling AI the biggest disruption risk for any investor ([Bill Ackman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/bill-ackman-investing-financial-battles-harvard-dei-x-free-speech/)).

### Media, plagiarism and free speech

- Ackman says Business Insider gave him and Neri Oxman 92 minutes to respond before calling her clerical errors plagiarism. He argues plagiarism requires intent and plans to pursue a correction or a lawsuit ([Bill Ackman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/bill-ackman-investing-financial-battles-harvard-dei-x-free-speech/)).
- Tucker Carlson says he interviewed Putin to inform Americans, and that sanctions and surveillance damage US freedom more than they hurt Russia. He says the NSA read his Signal messages ([Tucker Carlson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/tucker-carlson-putin-navalny-trump-cia-nsa-war-politics-freedom/)).
- On Navalny, Carlson says nobody knows how he died, yet a country that jails opposition leaders is not politically free. Fridman pushes back on Ukraine, Zelensky and press freedom ([Tucker Carlson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/tucker-carlson-putin-navalny-trump-cia-nsa-war-politics-freedom/)).
