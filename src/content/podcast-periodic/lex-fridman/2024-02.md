## Overview

February was mostly a politics month for Lex Fridman. Two episodes dealt with wars, Gaza with Omar Suleiman and Russia and Ukraine with Tucker Carlson, and Bill Ackman linked investing to governance fights at Harvard and in the media. Marc Raibert's conversation on Boston Dynamics was the one technology episode.

## Main themes

### Wars and how they are reported

- Omar Suleiman argues the Gaza war grows out of occupation, blockade and settlement expansion that Western media largely ignore. He calls a ceasefire the bare minimum and says the United States is not an honest broker ([Omar Suleiman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/omar-suleiman-palestine-gaza-oct-7-israel-resistance-faith-islam/)).
- Suleiman holds that occupied people have a right to resist, but that killing women, children and non-combatants is terrorism whoever does it ([Omar Suleiman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/omar-suleiman-palestine-gaza-oct-7-israel-resistance-faith-islam/)).
- Tucker Carlson says he interviewed Vladimir Putin to inform Americans, and that sanctions push countries towards BRICS and away from the dollar. Fridman pushes back, saying Ukraine could still win and that Carlson could have been tougher on Putin ([Tucker Carlson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/tucker-carlson-putin-navalny-trump-cia-nsa-war-politics-freedom/)).

### Speech, surveillance and institutions

- Carlson says the NSA read his Signal messages. Fridman cites the World Press Freedom Index, which ranks the US 71st, and Carlson replies that questioning the war costs people their jobs ([Tucker Carlson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/tucker-carlson-putin-navalny-trump-cia-nsa-war-politics-freedom/)).
- Bill Ackman blames Harvard's self-perpetuating board for its failures after October 7th. He cites its last place of 250 on FIRE's free-speech list and wants boards with permanently diverse viewpoints ([Bill Ackman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/bill-ackman-investing-financial-battles-harvard-dei-x-free-speech/)).
- Ackman says Business Insider gave him 92 minutes to respond before publishing about Neri Oxman. He credits X with letting him answer critics directly ([Bill Ackman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/bill-ackman-investing-financial-battles-harvard-dei-x-free-speech/)).

### Investing through governance

- Ackman looks for simple, predictable businesses that are hard to disrupt, bought at a discount, with governance he can fix. Canadian Pacific, where he recruited Hunter Harrison and won 99% of the vote, is his model case ([Bill Ackman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/bill-ackman-investing-financial-battles-harvard-dei-x-free-speech/)).
- He saw Alphabet's poor Bard demo as a mispricing at about 15 times earnings, while calling AI the biggest disruption risk any investor faces ([Bill Ackman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/bill-ackman-investing-financial-battles-harvard-dei-x-free-speech/)).

### Robots that move, and robots that learn

- Marc Raibert splits intelligence into athletic and cognitive parts. Boston Dynamics showed what athletic intelligence can do, but he says most robots are still cognitively dumb ([Marc Raibert](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/marc-raibert-boston-dynamics-and-the-future-of-robotics/)).
- He traces the path from pogo stick robots through BigDog to Spot, of which about 1,500 are deployed. He says Atlas still relies mostly on model predictive control and expects learning and traditional control to merge ([Marc Raibert](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/marc-raibert-boston-dynamics-and-the-future-of-robotics/)).
- At the Boston Dynamics AI Institute, projects such as Watch Understand Do aim for robots that learn tasks by watching people. Raibert is not worried about AGI ([Marc Raibert](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/marc-raibert-boston-dynamics-and-the-future-of-robotics/)).
