## Overview

In 2024 the Lex Fridman Podcast leaned hard into politics and history, with the US election drawing Donald Trump, Bernie Sanders and voices from left and right. Wars in Gaza and Ukraine ran through the first half, often as debates. AI came from its leaders, Sam Altman, Yann LeCun and Dario Amodei, and from builders at Perplexity and Cursor. History, from Rome to lost civilizations of the Americas, filled the autumn.

## Main themes

### The 2024 US election

- Donald Trump presented himself as a dealmaker who can end wars, while Fridman pressed him on 2020 and division ([Donald Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/09/donald-trump-interview/)).
- Vivek Ramaswamy urged conservatives to dismantle the federal bureaucracy ([Vivek Ramaswamy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/09/vivek-ramaswamy-trump-conservatism-nationalism-immigration-and-war/)); Bernie Sanders and Cenk Uygur put big money at the centre of US politics ([Bernie Sanders](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/bernie-sanders-interview/), [Cenk Uygur](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/cenk-uygur-trump-vs-harris-progressive-politics-communism-capitalism/)).
- Saagar Enjeti read the result as a cultural realignment against elites ([Saagar Enjeti](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/12/saagar-enjeti-trump-maga-doge-obama-fdr-jfk-history-politics/)).
- Javier Milei argued fiscal cuts and deregulation have curbed inflation in Argentina ([Javier Milei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/javier-milei-president-of-argentina-freedom-economics-and-corruption/)).

### Gaza, Ukraine and the risk of war

- A four-way debate with Benny Morris and Norman Finkelstein clashed over 1948, October 7 and Gaza ([Israel-Palestine Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/israel-palestine-debate-finkelstein-destiny-m-rabbani-benny-morris/)); Omar Suleiman and Bassem Youssef stressed occupation and an imbalance of power ([Omar Suleiman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/omar-suleiman-palestine-gaza-oct-7-israel-resistance-faith-islam/), [Bassem Youssef](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/04/bassem-youssef-israel-palestine-gaza-hamas-middle-east-satire-fame/)).
- Serhii Plokhy called the Russo-Ukrainian war a war of Soviet succession, not a NATO story ([Serhii Plokhy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/serhii-plokhy-history-of-ukraine-russia-soviet-union-kgb-nazis-war/)), against Tucker Carlson's view of US policy ([Tucker Carlson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/02/tucker-carlson-putin-navalny-trump-cia-nsa-war-politics-freedom/)).
- Annie Jacobsen warned that launch on warning leaves deterrence one miscalculation from nuclear war ([Annie Jacobsen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/annie-jacobsen-nuclear-war-cia-kgb-aliens-area-51-roswell-secrecy/)).

### AI leaders on AGI, openness and safety

- Sam Altman said the board crisis showed OpenAI needs sturdier governance ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/sam-altman-openai-gpt-5-sora-board-saga-elon-musk-ilya-power-agi/)).
- Yann LeCun argued LLMs need world models learned from video and that open source guards against concentrated power ([Yann LeCun](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/yann-lecun-meta-ai-open-source-limits-of-llms-agi-the-future-of-ai/)).
- Dario Amodei expects powerful AI around 2026-2027 and backs capability-triggered safeguards ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)); Roman Yampolskiy says superintelligence cannot be controlled ([Roman Yampolskiy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/06/roman-yampolskiy-dangers-of-superintelligent-ai/)).

### Building with AI

- Aravind Srinivas bets Perplexity can beat Google with cited answers built on retrieval and ranking ([Aravind Srinivas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/06/aravind-srinivas-perplexity-ceo-on-future-of-ai-search-the-internet/)).
- Cursor's founders want AI coding tools that keep the programmer in control ([Cursor Team](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/cursor-team-future-of-programming-with-ai/)); Pieter Levels ships solo AI products fast on a simple stack ([Pieter Levels](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/pieter-levels-programming-viral-ai-startups-and-digital-nomad-life/)).
- Neuralink presented its brain interface as a way to restore independence first, then raise human-computer bandwidth ([Neuralink](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/elon-musk-and-neuralink-team-elon-musk-neuralink-and-the-future-of-hum/)).

### History and lost civilizations

- Gregory Aldrete explained Rome's rise and fall ([Roman Empire](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/09/gregory-aldrete-the-roman-empire-rise-and-fall-of-ancient-rome/)); Vejas Liulevicius traced the terror of Soviet and Maoist regimes ([Vejas Liulevicius](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/09/vejas-liulevicius-communism-marxism-nazism-stalin-mao-and-hitler/)).
- Ed Barnhart and Graham Hancock argued ancient civilizations were older and more advanced than taught ([Ed Barnhart](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/09/ed-barnhart-maya-aztec-inca-and-lost-civilizations-of-south-america/), [Graham Hancock](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/10/graham-hancock-lost-civilization-of-the-ice-age-ancient-human-history/)).
