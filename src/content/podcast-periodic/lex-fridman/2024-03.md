## Overview

March was a busy eight-episode month that brought AI back to the centre, with Yann LeCun and Sam Altman giving contrasting views of the road to AGI. Elon Musk came up in half the episodes. The rest split between Russia, Ukraine and nuclear risk, a four-way Israel-Palestine debate, and founders talking about how they built their businesses.

## Main themes

### AGI: how soon, how open, who controls it

- Yann LeCun argues autoregressive LLMs lack world understanding, memory, reasoning and planning, and that AGI is at least a decade away. He says “It’s not going to be an event. It’s going to be gradual progress.” ([Yann LeCun](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/yann-lecun-meta-ai-open-source-limits-of-llms-agi-the-future-of-ai/)).
- LeCun says open-source base models do not hurt Meta's ad and services business, and predicts most AI systems will be built on open platforms. He dismisses doomers, arguing a desire to dominate would have to be built in ([Yann LeCun](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/yann-lecun-meta-ai-open-source-limits-of-llms-agi-the-future-of-ai/)).
- Sam Altman calls the November 2023 board crisis a governance failure, since the board could fire him on paper but not in practice. He says the road to AGI will be “a giant power struggle” and wants governments to set rules ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/sam-altman-openai-gpt-5-sora-board-saga-elon-musk-ilya-power-agi/)).
- Altman expects the GPT-4 to GPT-5 gap to match GPT-3 to GPT-4 and sees energy as the hardest constraint on compute ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/sam-altman-openai-gpt-5-sora-board-saga-elon-musk-ilya-power-agi/)).

### Elon Musk from four angles

- Altman says Musk thought OpenAI would fail, wanted total control or a merger with Tesla, and calls the lawsuit unbecoming of a builder he respects ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/sam-altman-openai-gpt-5-sora-board-saga-elon-musk-ilya-power-agi/)).
- Kimbal Musk recalls Zip2, a top-100 website that made no money, and Tesla nearly going bankrupt two or three times around the Model 3 ([Kimbal Musk](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/kimbal-musk-the-art-of-cooking-tesla-spacex-zip2-and-family/)).
- Mark Cuban defends DEI against Musk's criticism and notes Grok has had errors like Google's Gemini. Dana White says he was arranging venues for a Musk-Zuckerberg fight ([Mark Cuban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/mark-cuban-shark-tank-dei-wokeism-debate-elon-musk-politics-drugs/), [Dana White](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/dana-white-ufc-fighting-khabib-conor-tyson-ali-rogan-elon-zuck/)).

### Russia, the KGB and nuclear risk

- Serhii Plokhy calls the war in Ukraine a war of Soviet succession, with NATO a pretext. He argues KGB culture entered the Kremlin with Putin's circle ([Serhii Plokhy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/serhii-plokhy-history-of-ukraine-russia-soviet-union-kgb-nazis-war/)).
- Annie Jacobsen argues launch on warning and sole presidential authority leave one person about six minutes to decide, and that only communication between adversaries offers a way out ([Annie Jacobsen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/annie-jacobsen-nuclear-war-cia-kgb-aliens-area-51-roswell-secrecy/)).

### The Israel-Palestine debate

- Benny Morris and Steven Bonnell say the 1948 expulsions came from a war the Arabs started. Norman Finkelstein and Mouin Rabbani say displacement was built into Zionism and call the Gaza war genocidal ([Israel-Palestine Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/israel-palestine-debate-finkelstein-destiny-m-rabbani-benny-morris/)).
- They also clash over October 7 and failed peace talks; none of the four expects peace soon ([Israel-Palestine Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/israel-palestine-debate-finkelstein-destiny-m-rabbani-benny-morris/)).

### Building businesses

- Cuban says selling is just helping, explains how he values Shark Tank pitches, and argues Cost Plus Drugs disrupts drug pricing through transparency ([Mark Cuban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/mark-cuban-shark-tank-dei-wokeism-debate-elon-musk-politics-drugs/)).
- White credits passion, risky matchups and honest storytelling for the UFC's growth after the Fertittas bought it for $2 million ([Dana White](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/dana-white-ufc-fighting-khabib-conor-tyson-ali-rogan-elon-zuck/)).
