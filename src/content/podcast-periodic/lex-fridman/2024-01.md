## Overview

January had no AI episodes. Its centre was a long debate between Ben Shapiro and Destiny on US politics and foreign policy, with a former con man's account of mortgage fraud and a musician's view of performing without fear on either side. No item came up in more than one episode.

## Main themes

### US politics: Trump, January 6 and institutions

- Shapiro argues Trump's foreign policy and economic record beat Biden's and that voters have priced in his rhetoric. Destiny calls Trump “the most divisive president I think we’ve ever had” ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).
- Destiny says the goal of the fake-elector plan and pressure on Pence was to circumvent the peaceful transfer of power. Shapiro calls Trump's conduct wrong but says the legal tests for incitement and insurrection were not met ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).
- On universities, Shapiro says conservatives lost them in the late 1960s, while Destiny blames conservatives for disengaging and warns against populist disregard for institutions ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).
- On schools, Shapiro cites Roland Fryer on two-parent households; Destiny cites research on school meals and air conditioning ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).

### Iran, Israel and Ukraine

- Shapiro argues Iran activated Hamas to derail Saudi-Israeli normalisation and wants containment. Destiny wants diplomatic communication with Iran ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).
- Destiny warns a civilian toll of 40,000-60,000 in Gaza could reverse Arab normalisation. Shapiro says Israel's care for civilians has cost its highest military death toll since 1973 ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).
- On Ukraine, Shapiro says Biden outsourced war aims to Zelenskyy; Destiny defends the NATO coalition ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).

### Inside a mortgage fraud

- Matthew Cox describes a slide from whiting out one late rent payment to running a brokerage where he estimates 60-70% of loans involved fraud ([Cox](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/matthew-cox-fbi-most-wanted-con-man-55-million-in-bank-fraud/)).
- In Ybor City he bought houses for about $50,000 and recorded sales at $200,000 to create fake comparables. The FBI counted 109 houses, which he disputes ([Cox](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/matthew-cox-fbi-most-wanted-con-man-55-million-in-bank-fraud/)).
- On the run he held over 50 identities. He was sentenced to 26 years; cooperation and filings by fellow inmate Frank Amodeo cut 12 years off ([Cox](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/matthew-cox-fbi-most-wanted-con-man-55-million-in-bank-fraud/)).

### Music without fear

- Tal Wilkenfeld argues fear and confidence both block a player on stage, while trust means knowing you can handle what comes ([Wilkenfeld](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/tal-wilkenfeld-music-guitar-bass-jeff-beck-prince-and-leonard-cohen/)).
- She recalls lessons from Jeff Beck, Prince and Leonard Cohen, including Prince's tape recording with no punch-ins and Cohen rereading lyrics in many states of mind ([Wilkenfeld](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/tal-wilkenfeld-music-guitar-bass-jeff-beck-prince-and-leonard-cohen/)).
