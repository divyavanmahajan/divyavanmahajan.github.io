## Overview

January had no AI episode. Its three conversations covered US politics as a debate between Ben Shapiro and Destiny, the story of a convicted mortgage fraudster, and a musician on trust and craft.

## Main themes

### Politics as a structured debate

- Ben Shapiro argues for local, limited government and family structure as the root of social problems. Destiny argues government can fix problems at the margin ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).
- On January 6, Destiny describes a plot against the transfer of power. Shapiro calls Trump's conduct wrong but says the legal tests for incitement and insurrection were not met ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).
- On Iran, Israel and Ukraine, Shapiro wants containment and criticises Biden's war aims, while Destiny backs diplomacy with Iran and defends the NATO coalition ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).
- Both agree DEI ideas went too far, but Destiny blames conservatives for abandoning academia ([Shapiro vs Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/ben-shapiro-destiny-debate-ben-shapiro-vs-destiny-debate-politics-jan/)).

### How fraud grows

- Matthew Cox says his slide began when a manager told him to white out a late rent payment. At his own brokerage he estimates 60-70% of loans involved fraud ([Cox](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/matthew-cox-fbi-most-wanted-con-man-55-million-in-bank-fraud/)).
- He built synthetic borrowers and recorded inflated house sales to create comparables. The FBI counted 109 houses and about $11.5 million ([Cox](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/matthew-cox-fbi-most-wanted-con-man-55-million-in-bank-fraud/)).
- Cox argues nearly every criminal cooperates with prosecutors in the end. Lex Fridman keeps pushing back on that view of loyalty ([Cox](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/matthew-cox-fbi-most-wanted-con-man-55-million-in-bank-fraud/)).

### Trust and craft in music

- Tal Wilkenfeld argues that fear and confidence both block a performer, while trust means knowing you can handle whatever comes ([Wilkenfeld](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/tal-wilkenfeld-music-guitar-bass-jeff-beck-prince-and-leonard-cohen/)).
- She recalls Leonard Cohen's advice to reread a lyric in many states of mind, and Prince recording to tape with no punch-ins ([Wilkenfeld](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/tal-wilkenfeld-music-guitar-bass-jeff-beck-prince-and-leonard-cohen/)).
- She has never practised more than an hour a day and recommends playing very slowly to hear each note ([Wilkenfeld](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/01/tal-wilkenfeld-music-guitar-bass-jeff-beck-prince-and-leonard-cohen/)).
