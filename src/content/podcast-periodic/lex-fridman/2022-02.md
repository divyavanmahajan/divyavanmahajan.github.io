## Overview

February closed with Mark Zuckerberg on the metaverse and content moderation, and free speech was the one thread across episodes. Nicole Perlroth covered cyberwar and John Abramson questioned who controls medical evidence. The rest of the month went to big questions about minds and aliens, and to arm wrestling.

## Main themes

### Free speech, moderation and the metaverse

- Mark Zuckerberg says the metaverse is about presence: hands, eye contact and spatial audio, with AI as the enabling technology ([Zuckerberg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/mark-zuckerberg-meta-facebook-instagram-and-the-metaverse/)).
- He says Meta tracks about 20 kinds of harm with over a thousand engineers on AI detection, and that “I don't think pressure from advertisers or politicians directly in any way affects how we think about this” ([Zuckerberg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/mark-zuckerberg-meta-facebook-instagram-and-the-metaverse/)).
- Zuckerberg says his free-speech stance has not changed but that a pandemic justifies tolerating less harmful speech. Fridman argues for leaning toward free speech ([Zuckerberg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/mark-zuckerberg-meta-facebook-instagram-and-the-metaverse/)).
- Tim Urban argues collective intelligence gets dumber when topics turn tribal, and that progress needs a culture of open disagreement ([Urban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/tim-urban-elon-musk-neuralink-ai-aliens-and-the-future-of-humanity/)).

### Cyberwar and the zero-day market

- Nicole Perlroth says governments built an underground market for zero-day exploits that also leaves their own citizens exposed ([Perlroth](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/nicole-perlroth-cybersecurity-and-the-weapons-of-cyberwar/)).
- She believes “any geopolitical conflict from now on is guaranteed to have some cyber element to it”, and notes over 80% of US critical infrastructure is privately owned with no duty to report breaches ([Perlroth](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/nicole-perlroth-cybersecurity-and-the-weapons-of-cyberwar/)).
- Her one fix is multi-factor authentication, noting Colonial Pipeline was breached through an old account without it ([Perlroth](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/nicole-perlroth-cybersecurity-and-the-weapons-of-cyberwar/)).

### Who controls the evidence

- John Abramson argues drug companies control the medical knowledge doctors rely on, and that peer reviewers and guideline authors rarely see the underlying trial data ([Abramson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/john-abramson-big-pharma/)).
- He criticises US direct-to-consumer drug advertising, allowed elsewhere only in New Zealand, and backs a ban on staff moving between pharma and regulators ([Abramson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/john-abramson-big-pharma/)).
- Fridman frames the episode as a counterpoint to his Pfizer CEO interview and pushes back on censorship as a remedy ([Abramson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/john-abramson-big-pharma/)).

### Minds, aliens and brain-computer interfaces

- Philip Goff defends panpsychism: physics describes what matter does, consciousness is what matter is. He warns mind uploading might amount to suicide ([Goff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/philip-goff-consciousness-panpsychism-and-the-philosophy-of-mind/)).
- Garry Nolan treats UFO reports and materials as data, saying “it's not about debunking it's about getting the more spectacular and hyped cases off the table” ([Nolan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/garry-nolan-ufos-and-aliens/)).
- Urban calls Neuralink Musk's most ambitious project and says working BCIs could mark a new dividing line in history ([Urban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/tim-urban-elon-musk-neuralink-ai-aliens-and-the-future-of-humanity/)).

### Commitment in arm wrestling

- Devon Larratt trains with high volume and never to failure, a lesson he credits to Russian arm wrestlers ([Larratt](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/devon-larratt-arm-wrestling/)).
- He says the internet and new investment made the sport grow fast, and that Denis Cyplenkov's 2018 win showed him a higher level of professionalism ([Larratt](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/devon-larratt-arm-wrestling/)).
