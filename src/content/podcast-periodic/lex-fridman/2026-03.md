## Overview

March mixed one big AI conversation with two about creative craft. Jensen Huang explained how NVIDIA sees computing, agents and jobs, while Jeff Kaplan and Rick Beato talked about games and music and how AI touches their fields. No topic recurred across episodes.

## Main themes

### NVIDIA and the AI factory

- Huang argues computing moved from retrieval to generation, so data centres are now factories that produce tokens ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).
- He says problems no longer fit in one GPU, so chips, networking, power and cooling must be designed together. He runs NVIDIA the same way: “I don’t do one-on-ones. We present a problem and all of us attack it” ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).
- Putting CUDA on GeForce raised costs by about 50% at a 35% gross-margin company, and the market cap fell to around $1.5 billion. Huang now calls the CUDA install base NVIDIA's main moat ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).

### Agents, scaling and power

- Huang says agents that spawn sub-agents create large teams, which drives what he calls the agentic scaling law. He calls OpenClaw the iPhone of tokens ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).
- He argues capable agents will use existing software tools rather than replace them, and describes NVIDIA's OpenShell, which limits the rights an agent can hold at once ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).
- The grid runs at about 60% of peak most of the time, Huang says, so data centres should degrade gracefully instead of demanding six-nines contracts ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).

### AI and jobs

- Huang separates a job's purpose from its tasks, noting that “Every radiology platform and package today is driven by AI, and yet the number of radiologists grew” ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).
- He would hire the candidate who is expert in AI for every role, and expects coders to grow from 30 million to about 1 billion ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).

### Games, teams and crunch

- Kaplan credits Blizzard's early success to leadership by three gamer-programmers who shielded developers. He says the hits faded once finance and revenue targets took over ([Jeff Kaplan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jeff-kaplan-world-of-warcraft-overwatch-blizzard-and-future-of-gaming/)).
- He blames Titan's failure on leadership, including himself, after the team hired about 70 environment artists in a year without a vision. Overwatch was pitched in six weeks from what remained ([Jeff Kaplan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jeff-kaplan-world-of-warcraft-overwatch-blizzard-and-future-of-gaming/)).
- Kaplan argues forced crunch is a problem and that executives on 40-hour weeks should not ask others to stay late. He also says social media rewards maximum hyperbole ([Jeff Kaplan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jeff-kaplan-world-of-warcraft-overwatch-blizzard-and-future-of-gaming/)).

### AI in creative work

- Beato generated about 130 song ideas with Suno and found three good ones. He sees AI as an idea generator that helps skilled songwriters most ([Rick Beato](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/rick-beato-greatest-guitarists-of-all-time-history-future-of-music/)).
- Fridman argues listeners want authenticity, and Kaplan calls AI in game development mostly a hot mess: useful for chores but often wrong ([Rick Beato](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/rick-beato-greatest-guitarists-of-all-time-history-future-of-music/), [Jeff Kaplan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jeff-kaplan-world-of-warcraft-overwatch-blizzard-and-future-of-gaming/)).
