## Overview

May had two long technical conversations with no overlap. The first looked at FFmpeg and VLC, the open source software behind most online video, and the small group of volunteers who maintain it. The second was a tour of open problems in particle physics and cosmology with Fermilab's Don Lincoln.

## Main themes

### Video infrastructure run by volunteers

- Jean-Baptiste Kempf describes FFmpeg as the low-level libraries for codecs, muxers, demuxers and filters, used in everything from VLC to Chrome to smart TVs. Lex Fridman cites an estimate that over 90% of video processing workflows involve FFmpeg ([FFmpeg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/ffmpeg-the-incredible-technology-behind-video-on-the-internet/)).
- Kieran Kunhya says “FFmpeg is probably one of the biggest CPU users in the world”, so every instruction matters ([FFmpeg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/ffmpeg-the-incredible-technology-behind-video-on-the-internet/)).
- Both guests say the work rests on perhaps 5 VLC and 10-15 FFmpeg maintainers. Kunhya criticises Google for sending volunteers AI-generated security reports under a 90-day deadline, and Microsoft Teams for offering a few thousand dollars for a "high priority" bug ([FFmpeg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/ffmpeg-the-incredible-technology-behind-video-on-the-internet/)).
- Kempf says he refused offers worth tens of millions to bundle toolbars or ads into VLC, and refused two intelligence agencies that asked for a backdoor ([FFmpeg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/ffmpeg-the-incredible-technology-behind-video-on-the-internet/)).

### Handwritten assembly and the limits of compilers

- Kempf says the dav1d AV1 decoder has about 30,000 lines of C and 240,000 lines of handwritten assembly, more than the roughly 100,000 lines across all of FFmpeg ([FFmpeg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/ffmpeg-the-incredible-technology-behind-video-on-the-internet/)).
- Kunhya reports 10x to 50x speedups over C. Kempf says compiler auto-vectorisation is not close: “It’s multiple times slower.” ([FFmpeg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/ffmpeg-the-incredible-technology-behind-video-on-the-internet/)).
- Kempf argues that low-level optimisation matters more now that Moore's law has ended, and that hardware tuning is the part that cannot be vibe coded ([FFmpeg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/ffmpeg-the-incredible-technology-behind-video-on-the-internet/)).

### Physics as a series of unifications

- Lincoln tells the history of physics from Newton through Maxwell and Einstein to electroweak unification, and calls Higgs theory a Band-Aid on electroweak theory ([Don Lincoln](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/don-lincoln-biggest-mysteries-in-physics-antimatter-dark-energy-toe-do/)).
- He stresses that the 2012 LHC result was a particle consistent with the Higgs; later measurements fixed its spin at zero. Fermilab's Tevatron had narrowed the mass range before CERN's announcement ([Don Lincoln](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/don-lincoln-biggest-mysteries-in-physics-antimatter-dark-energy-toe-do/)).
- As an experimentalist, he puts a theory of everything centuries away, because the energy needed is far beyond today's accelerators. He likes string theory but calls it untested ([Don Lincoln](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/don-lincoln-biggest-mysteries-in-physics-antimatter-dark-energy-toe-do/)).

### Antimatter, dark matter and dark energy

- Lincoln says the mechanism behind the slight surplus of matter over antimatter “is not understood”. Fermilab is testing leptogenesis by comparing neutrino and antineutrino oscillations ([Don Lincoln](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/don-lincoln-biggest-mysteries-in-physics-antimatter-dark-energy-toe-do/)).
- He once favoured modified gravity, but the Bullet Cluster and the galaxies DF2 and DF4 convinced him dark matter is real ([Don Lincoln](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/don-lincoln-biggest-mysteries-in-physics-antimatter-dark-energy-toe-do/)).
- On dark energy, he notes a recent, unconfirmed measurement that hints it is getting smaller, and offers a guess he warns nobody should believe ([Don Lincoln](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/don-lincoln-biggest-mysteries-in-physics-antimatter-dark-energy-toe-do/)).
