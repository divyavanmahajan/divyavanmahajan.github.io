## Overview

September kept returning to one question: what AI can and cannot do that humans do. Ray Kurzweil, Rana el Kaliouby, Nick Lane and Will Sasso each took a position on it. The rest of the month covered the meaning crisis, World War I, and two games where players now study with computers.

## Main themes

### The singularity and machines that feel

- Ray Kurzweil holds to his forecasts: a rigorous Turing test passed in 2029, the neocortex connected to the cloud in the 2030s, and a singularity by 2045. He frames this as humans merging with AI rather than being replaced ([Ray Kurzweil](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/ray-kurzweil-singularity-superintelligence-and-immortality/)).
- Kurzweil argues price-performance of computing has grown exponentially since 1939, and points to falling extreme poverty and rising life expectancy as evidence that wellbeing is improving ([Ray Kurzweil](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/ray-kurzweil-singularity-superintelligence-and-immortality/)).
- Nick Lane says AI can match human information processing but doubts it will have feelings, which he ties to life itself. Fridman argues emotional intelligence may emerge as systems scale ([Nick Lane](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/nick-lane-origin-of-life-evolution-aliens-biology-and-consciousness/)).
- Will Sasso thinks AI can feed comedians absurd premises but can never duplicate a real person. Fridman expects digital replicas to become possible soon ([Will Sasso](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/will-sasso-comedy-madtv-ai-friendship-madness-and-pro-wrestling/)).

### Emotion AI and machines in daily life

- Rana el Kaliouby argues emotion AI must avoid simplistic face-to-emotion mappings, combine several signals and rest on opt-in consent. Her main worry is biased systems deployed at scale ([Rana el Kaliouby](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/rana-el-kaliouby-emotion-ai-social-robots-and-self-driving-cars/)).
- She says Smart Eye has been selected by 14 carmakers for 94 models, and that semi-autonomous cars need driver monitoring for safe handovers. Fridman calls Tesla's over-the-air updates the real revolution ([Rana el Kaliouby](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/rana-el-kaliouby-emotion-ai-social-robots-and-self-driving-cars/)).
- Fridman thinks social robots fail on cost and manufacturing, and praises the Tesla Bot team's focus on cheap mass production ([Rana el Kaliouby](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/rana-el-kaliouby-emotion-ai-social-robots-and-self-driving-cars/)).

### Meaning, wisdom and origins

- John Vervaeke describes meaning as felt connectedness to reality, and argues the meaning crisis comes from losing the practices that once cultivated wisdom ([John Vervaeke](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/john-vervaeke-meaning-crisis-atheism-religion-the-search-for-wisdom/)).
- Lane argues simple life is close to inevitable on a wet, rocky planet, but complex cells arose only once, so he expects many planets with bacteria and few intelligent civilisations ([Nick Lane](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/nick-lane-origin-of-life-evolution-aliens-biology-and-consciousness/)).
- Christopher Capozzola argues World War I was a war of choice for the US that redefined citizenship and built a surveillance core that outlasted it ([Christopher Capozzola](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/christopher-capozzola-world-war-i-ideology-propaganda-and-politics/)).

### Games, solvers and fame

- Daniel Negreanu says AI solvers made poker something that can be studied, but the best players still win by exploiting human deviations from optimal play ([Daniel Negreanu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/daniel-negreanu-poker/)).
- Alexandra and Andrea Botez argue that playing for enjoyment improves both their chess and their streams, and describe burnout as the loneliest part of creating ([Botez Sisters](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/botez-sisters-chess-streaming-and-fame/)).
- Asked whether Fridman should interview Andrew Tate, the sisters weigh the value of a challenging conversation against the acceptance airtime signals ([Botez Sisters](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/botez-sisters-chess-streaming-and-fame/)).

## Notable episodes

- [Ray Kurzweil](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/ray-kurzweil-singularity-superintelligence-and-immortality/): the 2029 and 2045 forecasts restated, with the evidence he uses for them.
- [Rana el Kaliouby](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/rana-el-kaliouby-emotion-ai-social-robots-and-self-driving-cars/): a grounded look at in-cabin sensing and the limits of reading faces.
