## Overview

November had two long conversations. Dario Amodei, Amanda Askell and Chris Olah of Anthropic covered scaling, safety policy, Claude's character and interpretability. Argentine President Javier Milei then described his programme of spending cuts and deregulation.

## Main themes

### Scaling, safety and regulation at Anthropic

- Dario Amodei expects scaling to keep working and says powerful AI could arrive around 2026 or 2027. He calls scaling laws empirical regularities rather than laws of the universe ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- He cites real-world coding tasks on SWE-bench rising from about 3% to 50% in ten months, and expects frontier clusters to pass $10 billion in 2026 ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- Amodei describes Anthropic's responsible scaling policy as if-then safeguards that tighten only when tests show danger. He supported the amended SB 1047 and warns that badly designed regulation would turn opinion against regulation for good ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- In his Machines of Loving Grace picture, transformation comes within five to ten years, and his larger worry is concentration and abuse of power ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).

### Claude's character and what networks learn

- Amanda Askell treats Claude's character as an alignment problem: honest, not sycophantic and careful about sharing opinions. She calls system prompts cheap patches next to character training ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- Chris Olah explains superposition and how sparse autoencoders unfold it. On Claude 3 Sonnet this surfaced features for security vulnerabilities, backdoors and deception that change behaviour when forced on ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).

### Milei's economic shock therapy

- Javier Milei says he inherited inflation of 1% a day, a deficit of 15% of GDP and negative Central Bank reserves ([Javier Milei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/javier-milei-president-of-argentina-freedom-economics-and-corruption/)).
- He reports cutting ministries to eight, dismissing about 50,000 public employees and reaching fiscal balance in his first month. He claims wholesale inflation fell from 54% to 2% a month ([Javier Milei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/javier-milei-president-of-argentina-freedom-economics-and-corruption/)).
- Deregulation minister Federico Sturzenegger removes one to five regulations a day. Milei's advice to Elon Musk and Vivek Ramaswamy on DOGE is to go all the way ([Javier Milei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/javier-milei-president-of-argentina-freedom-economics-and-corruption/)).
- He argues libertarians must also win the cultural battle or see gains reversed, and credits X with breaking the media's monopoly ([Javier Milei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/javier-milei-president-of-argentina-freedom-economics-and-corruption/)).
