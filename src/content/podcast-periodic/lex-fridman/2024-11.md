## Overview

November had two long conversations. Anthropic's Dario Amodei, with Amanda Askell and Chris Olah, laid out the case for fast AI progress and for managing its risks now. Argentina's president Javier Milei described his first year of cuts and deregulation.

## Main themes

### Scaling, timelines and coding

- Dario Amodei says that if you extrapolate current curves, “it does make you think that we’ll get there by 2026 or 2027”, though data, cluster scaling or a Taiwan disruption could delay it ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- He says models went from about 3% to 50% on SWE-bench in ten months, and that the updated Sonnet 3.5 is smarter than the original Opus 3 at Sonnet's cost ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- Amodei expects programming to change fastest, with humans moving towards system design and UX. Anthropic powers editors such as Cursor through its API rather than building its own ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).

### Safety, scaling policy and regulation

- Amodei names catastrophic misuse and model autonomy as the two biggest risks, handled through if-then safeguards that tighten as tests show danger ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- He argues companies cannot be trusted to follow voluntary plans alone, and that “the worst enemy of those who want real accountability is badly designed regulation” ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- He wants action in 2025, and sees interpretability as an independent check once models might sandbag tests ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).

### Claude's character and inside the network

- Amanda Askell wants Claude to behave as you would want anyone to behave in its position, talking with millions of people. Character training is a variant of constitutional AI without human data ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- Amodei says Anthropic has never changed a deployed model's weights without telling anyone; reports of Claude getting dumber come from prompt sensitivity and system prompt changes ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).
- Chris Olah explains superposition and how sparse autoencoders found features for vulnerabilities, backdoors and deception in Claude 3 Sonnet ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-on-claude-agi-the-future-of-ai-humanity/)).

### Milei's Argentina

- Javier Milei says he inherited inflation of 1% a day, a deficit of 15% of GDP and negative reserves, and balanced the budget in his first month ([Javier Milei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/javier-milei-president-of-argentina-freedom-economics-and-corruption/)).
- He calls himself an anarcho-capitalist in theory and a minarchist in practice, and reports daily deregulation under Federico Sturzenegger. His advice to DOGE is to go all the way ([Javier Milei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/javier-milei-president-of-argentina-freedom-economics-and-corruption/)).
- Milei claims wholesale inflation fell from 54% to 2% a month and poverty from 57% to 46%, and that Argentina jumped 90 places in economic freedom ([Javier Milei](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/11/javier-milei-president-of-argentina-freedom-economics-and-corruption/)).
