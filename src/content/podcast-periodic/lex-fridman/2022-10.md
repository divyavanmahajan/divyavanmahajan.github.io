## Overview

October mixed AI and robotics with chess, combat sports and politics. Andrej Karpathy and Kate Darling covered how neural networks and robots will work alongside people, while two chess episodes picked apart the Carlsen-Niemann cheating affair. Annaka Harris, Balaji Srinivasan and Ye brought conversations on consciousness, institutions and a contested interview about antisemitism.

## Main themes

### Software 2.0, robots and AGI

- Andrej Karpathy says software is moving from hand-written code to neural network weights compiled from data. At Tesla, C++ fusion and tracking code gave way to nets that read all eight cameras and predict in 3D ([Karpathy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving-optimus-aliens-and-agi/)).
- Karpathy treats radar, ultrasonics and LiDAR as a cost and calibration burden, says the fleet matters more than the sensors, and sees Optimus as a hedge in case internet data is not enough for AGI ([Karpathy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving-optimus-aliens-and-agi/)).
- Kate Darling argues robots should be designed like domesticated animals, as supplements to human skills, and calls the humanoid form a distraction. Fridman is more open to Optimus ([Darling](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/kate-darling-social-robots-ethics-privacy-and-the-future-of-mit/)).
- Darling warns that social robots combine persuasion, data collection and embodiment, and questions Sony's aibo subscription model ([Darling](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/kate-darling-social-robots-ethics-privacy-and-the-future-of-mit/)).

### Chess, engines and trust

- Levy Rozman (GothamChess) walks through the Niemann timeline and evidence. He says the case looks stronger but is unproven, and that over-the-board security is weak ([GothamChess](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/gothamchess-hans-niemann-magnus-carlsen-cheating-scandal-chess-bots/)).
- Hikaru Nakamura is undecided on Niemann, puts the main blame on chess.com rather than Carlsen, and names Carlsen the greatest player because he has no clear weakness ([Nakamura](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/hikaru-nakamura-chess-magnus-kasparov-and-the-psychology-of-greatness/)).
- Both guests describe how engines changed play: Rozman says engine moves look counter-intuitive until you see the lines, and Nakamura says small advantages rarely win, so players press with white ([GothamChess](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/gothamchess-hans-niemann-magnus-carlsen-cheating-scandal-chess-bots/), [Nakamura](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/hikaru-nakamura-chess-magnus-kasparov-and-the-psychology-of-greatness/)).

### Mind, self and living systems

- Annaka Harris argues that the feeling of conscious will is an illusion and that consciousness may be fundamental to reality. She puts her own odds at roughly 51/49 ([Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/annaka-harris-free-will-consciousness-and-the-nature-of-reality/)).
- Harris says an AI could be conscious but with a very non-human experience, and that memory and a sense of self raise the capacity for suffering ([Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/annaka-harris-free-will-consciousness-and-the-nature-of-reality/)).
- Michael Levin describes cells as goal-seeking materials that engineers should steer with high-level signals. He also discloses an early-stage spin-off, Morphoceuticals, and Fridman draws parallels to AI and robotics ([Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/michael-levin-biology-life-aliens-evolution-embryogenesis-xenobots/)).

### Institutions, platforms and public argument

- Balaji Srinivasan argues that exit beats reform: network states, science published on chain and alternatives to the FDA, much as Bitcoin was easier to build than reforming the Fed ([Srinivasan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/balaji-srinivasan-how-to-fix-government-twitter-science-and-the-fda/)).
- Srinivasan predicts a tripolar world of the US establishment, the CCP and crypto, and says decentralized AI such as Stable Diffusion is more representative than centralized models ([Srinivasan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/balaji-srinivasan-how-to-fix-government-twitter-science-and-the-fda/)).
- Ye defends his statements about Jewish people in media; Fridman pushes back throughout, calls the group language a dog whistle, and Ye apologises near the end ([Ye](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/ye-aka-kanye-west-kanye-ye-west-interview/)).
- John Danaher says confidence comes from accumulated physical skill, not motivational talk, after his rebuilt team led ADCC 2022 ([Danaher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/john-danaher-submission-grappling-adcc-animal-combat-and-knives/)).
