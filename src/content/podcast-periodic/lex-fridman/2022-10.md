## Overview

October was the chess month: two episodes dealt with the Hans Niemann cheating scandal and what engines have done to the game. The month closed with Andrej Karpathy on Software 2.0 and Tesla's robot. In between came free will, social robots, network states, grappling and a contentious interview with Ye.

## Main themes

### The Carlsen-Niemann cheating scandal

- Levy Rozman (GothamChess) walks through the timeline, from Niemann's win with black at the Sinquefield Cup to Carlsen resigning after one move. Fridman reads Carlsen's statement calling cheating “an existential threat to the game” ([GothamChess](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/gothamchess-hans-niemann-magnus-carlsen-cheating-scandal-chess-bots/)).
- Rozman lists the circumstantial evidence, including engine-correlation statistics and odd post-game interviews, but stresses there is no physical proof and each point can be argued away ([GothamChess](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/gothamchess-hans-niemann-magnus-carlsen-cheating-scandal-chess-bots/)).
- Hikaru Nakamura is undecided. He says hard proof would need someone caught in the act, expects 6 to 12 months of results to settle it, and puts the main blame on chess.com rather than Carlsen ([Nakamura](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/hikaru-nakamura-chess-magnus-kasparov-and-the-psychology-of-greatness/)).

### Engines, greatness and the psychology of chess

- Rozman says engine cheating is worse than doping because a cheater can fake mistakes, and that Stockfish at 0.1 milliseconds per move still beats humans ([GothamChess](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/gothamchess-hans-niemann-magnus-carlsen-cheating-scandal-chess-bots/)).
- Nakamura says engines have changed style: players press with white and play solid with black, and a solved game would likely be a draw ([Nakamura](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/hikaru-nakamura-chess-magnus-kasparov-and-the-psychology-of-greatness/)).
- He picks Carlsen as the greatest ever for having no clear weakness, credits Kasparov's 20-plus years at number one, and says streaming income freed him from money pressure ([Nakamura](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/hikaru-nakamura-chess-magnus-kasparov-and-the-psychology-of-greatness/)).

### Software 2.0, self-driving and Optimus

- Karpathy describes Tesla's autopilot replacing C++ fusion code with neural nets over all eight cameras, and suggests Hugging Face is the GitHub of Software 2.0 ([Karpathy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving-optimus-aliens-and-agi/)).
- He calls extra sensors a liability in cost and calibration, and says a fleet matters more than sensor choice ([Karpathy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving-optimus-aliens-and-agi/)).
- Karpathy says “this robot currently thinks it's a car” because much of the autopilot stack was copied into Optimus, which he sees as a hedge on AGI ([Karpathy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving-optimus-aliens-and-agi/)).
- Kate Darling takes the other side on humanoids, calling the form a distraction and arguing robots should be designed like domesticated animals ([Darling](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/kate-darling-social-robots-ethics-privacy-and-the-future-of-mit/)).

### Minds, will and agency

- Annaka Harris accepts free will as the brain's decision process but calls the feeling that consciousness drives behaviour an illusion. She puts roughly even odds on consciousness being fundamental ([Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/annaka-harris-free-will-consciousness-and-the-nature-of-reality/)).
- Michael Levin argues cells are goal-seeking agents, so engineering should work through high-level signals; Fridman links this to AI and to Karpathy's Software 2.0 ([Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/michael-levin-biology-life-aliens-evolution-embryogenesis-xenobots/)).
- Google's LaMDA sentience claim came up three times. Darling argues believing a system is sentient is not naive, since even experts anthropomorphise robots ([Darling](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/kate-darling-social-robots-ethics-privacy-and-the-future-of-mit/), [Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/annaka-harris-free-will-consciousness-and-the-nature-of-reality/)).

### Exit, institutions and platforms

- Balaji Srinivasan argues building new opt-in institutions beats reform, from network states to science on chain and alternatives to FDA review ([Srinivasan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/balaji-srinivasan-how-to-fix-government-twitter-science-and-the-fda/)).
- Ye defends his statements about Jewish people in media while Fridman pushes back throughout; Ye also plans to build Parler alongside Elon Musk's Twitter ([Ye](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/ye-aka-kanye-west-kanye-ye-west-interview/)).
