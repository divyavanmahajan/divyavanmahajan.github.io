## Overview

April 2022 was a busy month of nine episodes. Rick Rubin was the most connected figure, as a guest and as an influence on Andrew Huberman and Grimes. With the war in Ukraine in the background, two episodes looked at war and its civilian cost, while others covered the mind, money and chips, and a debate on alien life.

## Main themes

### Rick Rubin and the creative process

- Rubin says he comes to every project blank and tests every suggestion by playing it, because people imagine described ideas differently ([Rubin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/rick-rubin-legendary-music-producer/)).
- He prefers the essential: “The very first album I ever produced, the credit I took was reduced by me instead of produced by me for that reason.” ([Rubin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/rick-rubin-legendary-music-producer/)).
- Huberman credits Rubin with convincing him to build a fixed routine before recording ([Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/andrew-huberman-focus-stress-relationships-and-friendship/)).
- Grimes places herself in a post-Rubin school where one person is engineer, producer and artist, and calls producers underrated ([Grimes](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/grimes-music-ai-and-the-future-of-humanity/)).

### War, power and hunger

- Chris Blattman argues war is so costly that most rivals find a deal. Fighting starts when forces such as unaccountable leaders, uncertainty and commitment problems remove that room ([Blattman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/chris-blattman-war-and-violence/)).
- He reads Ukraine as a refusal to give up sovereignty, met by a Russian elite threatened by Ukrainian democracy. Fridman, half Ukrainian and half Russian, gives a more emotional defence of resistance ([Blattman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/chris-blattman-war-and-violence/)).
- Skye Fitzgerald argues much of today's hunger is caused deliberately by war, in Yemen, Ethiopia and Ukraine. He wants concrete, personal sanctions on the leaders responsible ([Fitzgerald](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/skye-fitzgerald-hunger-war-and-human-suffering/)).

### The mind, at a high level

- Karl Deisseroth argues psychiatry still has no objective tests and diagnoses by conversation. He sees causal tools that link symptoms to brain circuits as the way forward ([Deisseroth](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/karl-deisseroth-depression-schizophrenia-and-psychiatry/)).
- Rubin talks openly about his own depression and how he eventually found a treatment that worked ([Rubin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/rick-rubin-legendary-music-producer/)).
- Huberman argues focus and performance depend on deliberately managing brain states, starting with enough good sleep. Michael Saylor also gives focus as his main advice to young people ([Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/andrew-huberman-focus-stress-relationships-and-friendship/), [Saylor](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/michael-saylor-bitcoin-inflation-and-the-future-of-money/)).

### Money and connectivity

- Saylor argues inflation is mismeasured as one number and presents Bitcoin as the first digital property. Fridman pushes back that securities law is not the same as ethics ([Saylor](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/michael-saylor-bitcoin-inflation-and-the-future-of-money/)).
- In Saylor's layered model, layer one is slow settlement: “The big trick is to move a billion dollars from here to the year 2140.” ([Saylor](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/michael-saylor-bitcoin-inflation-and-the-future-of-money/)).
- Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon says 5G was built for a society fully connected to the cloud. He wants Qualcomm known for the connected intelligent edge: PCs, cars, robots and industry ([Amon](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/cristiano-amon-qualcomm-ceo/)).

### The alien debate

- Sara Walker and Lee Cronin explain assembly theory: the shortest path to build an object, measured in the lab, with a high index pointing to life ([Alien Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/alien-debate-sara-walker-and-lee-cronin/)).
- Cronin thinks alien life must be everywhere; Walker says nobody can yet claim life is common. Cronin adds “we might have to make aliens in the lab before we find aliens in the universe” ([Alien Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/alien-debate-sara-walker-and-lee-cronin/)).

## Notable episodes

- [#275 – Rick Rubin: Legendary Music Producer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/rick-rubin-legendary-music-producer/): a practical account of editing, protecting artists and working without rules.
- [#273 – Chris Blattman: War and Violence](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/chris-blattman-war-and-violence/): a clear framework for why wars are rare and why they still happen, applied to Ukraine.
- [#279 – Alien Debate: Sara Walker and Lee Cronin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/alien-debate-sara-walker-and-lee-cronin/): two collaborators agree on a theory of life and disagree on how common it is.
- [#280 – Cristiano Amon: Qualcomm CEO](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/cristiano-amon-qualcomm-ceo/): a CEO's view of 5G, the chip shortage and edge AI.
- [#278 – Skye Fitzgerald: Hunger, War, and Human Suffering](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/skye-fitzgerald-hunger-war-and-human-suffering/): a filmmaker on starvation as a weapon and the ethics of filming suffering.
