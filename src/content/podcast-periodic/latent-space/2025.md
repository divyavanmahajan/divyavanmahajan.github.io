## Overview

Latent Space spent 2025 tracking agents from a loosely defined idea to a product category, led by coding agents. MCP went from a side project to a standard under a neutral foundation. Model gains came from reasoning, post-training and reinforcement learning rather than bigger pre-training runs. The year closed with world models and a run of state-of-the-field episodes.

## Main themes

### Coding agents leave the IDE

- Factory.ai's founders describe enterprise work moving from pairing with AI in the IDE to delegating whole tasks to agents ([The AI Coding Factory](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/the-ai-coding-factory/)).
- Cline spends freely on context instead of cost-cutting shortcuts ([Cline](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/07/cline-the-open-source-coding-agent-that-doesn-t-cut-costs/)), while Amp stays minimal so it can be rebuilt when models change ([Amp](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/09/amp-the-emperor-has-no-clothes/)).
- Coding agents became platforms with Jules and GitHub's Agent HQ, where Jared Palmer names reliability as the next step ([Jules](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/11/aie-code-preview-inside-google-labs-building-the-gemini-coding-agent-j/), [Agent HQ](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/11/inside-github-s-ai-revolution-jared-palmer-reveals-agent-hq-the-future/)).
- Steve Yegge argues coding in an IDE is ending and orchestrators come next ([Vibe Coding Manifesto](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/steve-yegge-s-vibe-coding-manifesto-why-claude-code-isn-t-it-what-come/)); OpenAI explains how Codex models are trained for trust ([GPT5-Codex-Max](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/gpt5-codex-max-training-agents-with-personality-tools-trust-brian-fioc/)).

### MCP becomes the standard

- Dharmesh Shah says “MCP as a standard is one of the better things that's happened in the world of AI, because a standard needed to exist” ([The Agent Network](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/03/the-agent-network-dharmesh-shah/)).
- David Soria Parra and Justin Spahr-Summers trace MCP from a two-person side project to a multi-company standard ([Creators of MCP](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/04/the-creators-of-model-context-protocol/)).
- By December the protocol had moved to the neutral Agentic AI Foundation ([One Year of MCP](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/one-year-of-mcp-with-david-soria-parra-and-aaif-leads-from-openai-goos/)).

### Reasoning, post-training and RL

- OpenAI researchers say GPT-4.1's gains came mostly from post-training rather than scale ([GPT 4.1](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/04/gpt-4-1-the-new-openai-workhorse/)); Noam Brown expects reasoning models to keep scaling ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/scaling-test-time-compute-to-multi-agent-civilizations-noam-brown-open/)).
- Nathan Lambert traces how RLVR grew out of Tulu 3 ([RLVR Revolution](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/07/the-rlvr-revolution-with-nathan-lambert-ai2-interconnects-ai/)), and Kyle Corbitt says on the frontier model side “investments in RL are paying off” ([Why RL Won](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/10/why-rl-won-kyle-corbitt-openpipe-acq-coreweave/)).
- Sarah Catanzaro calls RL environments a fad ([State of AI Startups](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/state-of-ai-startups-memory-learning-rl-envs-dbt-fivetran-sarah-catanz/)), and Ashvin Nair says RL generalises poorly without real work in the training data ([State of RL](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/state-of-rl-reasoning-imo-ioi-gold-openai-o3-gpt-5-and-cursor-composer/)).

### Agent platforms and context engineering

- Simon Willison found agents limited by gullibility, and noted DeepSeek V3 was “trained for $5.5 million, which is a 10th of the price that people thought” ([Simon Willison](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/01/ride-home-simon-willison-things-we-learned-about-llms-in-2024/)).
- OpenAI shipped the Responses API and Agents SDK in March, then AgentKit and the Apps SDK at DevDay ([Agents Platform](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/03/the-new-openai-agents-platform/), [DevDay 2025](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/10/devday-2025-apps-sdk-agent-kit-mcp-codex-and-why-prompting-is-more-imp/)).
- Lance Martin maps context engineering and says builders must strip out structure as models improve ([Context Engineering](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/09/context-engineering-for-agents-lance-martin-langchain/)).

### World models

- Fei-Fei Li and Justin Johnson present Marble as a step toward spatial intelligence ([World Labs](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/11/after-llms-spatial-intelligence-and-world-models-fei-fei-li-justin-joh/)).
- General Intuition bets that game clips can train world models as web text trained LLMs ([General Intuition](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/world-models-general-intuition-khosla-s-largest-bet-since-llms-openai/)).
