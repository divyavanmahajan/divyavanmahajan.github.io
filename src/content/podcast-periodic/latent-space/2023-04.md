## Overview

April 2023 on Latent Space kept returning to how models are judged and who gets to build them. The hosts opened with a guest-free history of benchmarks, Mike Conover made the case for open instruction data with Dolly, and two guests showed applied work: Joseph Nelson on computer vision after Segment Anything and Deedy Das on enterprise search.

## Main themes

### Benchmarks and model evaluation

- swyx and Alessio Fanelli argue benchmarks are as influential as data and compute, yet each one is beaten within a few years. GPT-4 scored 95 on HellaSwag, essentially solving it ([Benchmarks 101](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/ai-fundamentals-benchmarks-101/)).
- They flag contamination: GPT-4 did better on the harder AMC 12 than on AMC 10 and solved old Codeforces problems but none from 2022. They add that RLHF made GPT-4 overconfident ([Benchmarks 101](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/ai-fundamentals-benchmarks-101/)).
- The hosts note that how labs present results “is a form of marketing”, and call for benchmarks on latency, cost and throughput ([Benchmarks 101](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/ai-fundamentals-benchmarks-101/)).
- Conover saw GPT-J and Dolly score almost the same on benchmarks while behaving very differently, so his team uses a reference battery of prompts and wants better evaluation tooling ([Dolly](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/mapping-the-future-of-truly-open-models-and-training-dolly-for-30-with/)).

### Open models and open data

- Conover says Dolly went from 1.0 on GPT-J to 2.0 on EleutherAI's 12B Pythia in 19 days. The first version trained on one eight-GPU A100 node in under an hour, about $30 at cloud prices ([Dolly](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/mapping-the-future-of-truly-open-models-and-training-dolly-for-30-with/)).
- Dolly 2.0 replaced ChatGPT-generated Alpaca data with a dataset written by thousands of Databricks employees, which the community translated into Spanish and Japanese within 24 hours ([Dolly](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/mapping-the-future-of-truly-open-models-and-training-dolly-for-30-with/)).
- Conover argues open release lets safety and bias researchers inspect the real system, while swyx notes licensing is built around code even though value has moved to weights ([Dolly](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/mapping-the-future-of-truly-open-models-and-training-dolly-for-30-with/)).
- Das finds running a local LLM “really, really cool, but also supremely dangerous”, but concludes open models are good for innovation ([Glean](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/ai-powered-search-for-the-enterprise-with-deedy-das-of-glean/)).

### Computer vision after Segment Anything

- Nelson's heuristic is 30 to 50 images for a first working model in a controlled scene and about 200 per class for one you can rely on ([Segment Anything](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/segment-anything-model-and-the-hard-problems-of-computer-vision-with-j/)).
- He sees Meta's SAM as a turning point: use large zero-shot models to label data, then distil them into small owned models for the edge. He says annotation will become less of a blocker ([Segment Anything](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/segment-anything-model-and-the-hard-problems-of-computer-vision-with-j/)).
- SAM produces masks without text labels, so Nelson pairs it with Grounding DINO, and he expects GPT-4 to make OCR largely solved ([Segment Anything](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/segment-anything-model-and-the-hard-problems-of-computer-vision-with-j/)).

### Enterprise search and chat

- Das says Glean recreates Moma, Google's internal search tool, and became feasible thanks to rich SaaS APIs and distributed Elastic ([Glean](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/ai-powered-search-for-the-enterprise-with-deedy-das-of-glean/)).
- He argues search alone does not retain users, so Glean became an employee portal, and that such tools are hard to sell because they do not clearly raise revenue or cut cost ([Glean](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/ai-powered-search-for-the-enterprise-with-deedy-das-of-glean/)).
- Das thinks ChatGPT wins on long-tail and coding questions and Google on fresh queries, and warns that chat threatens the ad model behind the free web ([Glean](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/04/ai-powered-search-for-the-enterprise-with-deedy-das-of-glean/)).
