## Overview

September on Latent Space covered the practical layers between a model and a working application. Episodes looked at why inference keeps getting cheaper, how OpenAI built structured outputs and launched o1, which prompting techniques hold up, and how to design agents and their tools. Agents, prompting and token pricing each recurred across two episodes.

## Main themes

### Cheaper inference, cheaper tokens

- The intro frames the episode with GPT-3-level intelligence falling from $60 per million tokens in 2020 to 27 cents by the Mixtral price war, and asks “what are the driving forces” behind prices falling over 90% a year ([Efficiency is Coming](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/efficiency-is-coming-3000x-faster-cheaper-better-ai-inference-from-har/)).
- Nyla Worker draws on computer vision, where hardware, batching, lower precision, quantization and pruning stacked up to almost 3000x throughput over six years. She says the target is human-perceived latency, not raw speed ([Efficiency is Coming](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/efficiency-is-coming-3000x-faster-cheaper-better-ai-inference-from-har/)).
- She expects LLMs to follow through distillation and data quality, and describes Convai's embodied 3D characters, used in games and for staff training ([Efficiency is Coming](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/efficiency-is-coming-3000x-faster-cheaper-better-ai-inference-from-har/)).
- In the OpenAI AMA, Shengjia Zhao said prices historically fall 10x every one to two years ([From API to AGI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/from-api-to-agi-structured-outputs-openai-api-platform-and-o1-q-a-with/)).

### Structured outputs and o1

- Michelle Pokrass argues agentic apps fail when chained calls are only 95% reliable, so guaranteed schema adherence matters. Structured outputs combine constrained decoding with a model trained to follow formats ([From API to AGI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/from-api-to-agi-structured-outputs-openai-api-platform-and-o1-q-a-with/)).
- swyx says replacing Instructor with structured outputs cut AI News API costs by 55% by removing retries. Pokrass recommends starting with 4o mini, then 4o, then fine-tuning ([From API to AGI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/from-api-to-agi-structured-outputs-openai-api-platform-and-o1-q-a-with/)).
- swyx notes the o1 API launched without a system role, temperature, streaming or tool calling. Noam Brown said in the AMA that o1 is a single model trained to produce a long chain of thought ([From API to AGI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/from-api-to-agi-structured-outputs-openai-api-platform-and-o1-q-a-with/)).

### Prompting without the folklore

- Sander Schulhoff's Prompt Report catalogued 58 text-based techniques. His working toolbox is few-shot examples, chain of thought and good information about the problem ([Prompting](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/the-ultimate-guide-to-prompting/)).
- He says role prompts do not improve accuracy: in a small MMLU study an "idiot" role beat a "genius" one. Exemplar ordering alone has swung accuracy from 0% to 90%, and DSPy beat his own manual tuning ([Prompting](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/the-ultimate-guide-to-prompting/)).
- HackAPrompt collected 600,000 malicious prompts and won an EMNLP best paper award ([Prompting](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/the-ultimate-guide-to-prompting/)).
- swyx argues “you should not be a prompt engineer because it is the goal of the big labs to put you out of a job” ([Language Agents](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/language-agents-from-reasoning-to-acting/)).

### Designing agents and their interfaces

- Shunyu Yao says ReAct's lasting contribution is one general method that pairs reasoning with tool use. Harrison Chase says ReAct-style loops dominate production, and developers encode planning in code because models plan poorly ([Language Agents](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/language-agents-from-reasoning-to-acting/)).
- Yao argues for treating agents as customers. SWE-agent showed an off-the-shelf terminal confuses models, so interfaces should be A/B tested on agents ([Language Agents](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/language-agents-from-reasoning-to-acting/)).
- Yao's TauBench tests customer-service reliability with simulated users; the best model averaged about 48%. Chase says memory is still unsolved ([Language Agents](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/language-agents-from-reasoning-to-acting/)).
