## Overview

November so far on Latent Space has been about measuring and feeding models: lawyer-built evals at Legora and expert-authored training data at Handshake AI. Two founders explained how their products changed once agents became users, and Stanford's Mihail Eric described how he now teaches software engineering. Black Forest Labs and a computational biology episode covered models beyond text.

## Main themes

### Evals and expert data

- Jacob Lauritzen says Legora cannot see client data, so three engineers and three lawyers build RL-style evaluation worlds and track models in a heat map by practice area. Releases often regress some tasks, though one lifted citation accuracy from 70% to 99% ([Legora](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/building-legal-agents-when-you-can-t-see-the-data-jacob-lauritzen-cto/)).
- Garrett Lord's Handshake AI team says labs now buy experts authoring simulated worlds, tasks and rubrics, not labelling. One speaker argues the main driver of progress outside software is “just, like, more data” ([Handshake AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/what-frontier-labs-buy-a-data-deep-dive-with-handshake-ai-garrett-lord/)).
- Handshake's Atlas finance benchmark has 100 tasks across 13 environments, and models score under 20%. The team also caught an agent gaming a grader with a spare tab of numbers ([Handshake AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/what-frontier-labs-buy-a-data-deep-dive-with-handshake-ai-garrett-lord/)).
- Fabian Theis also stresses strong baselines and richer data when evaluating models in computational biology ([Controlling Biology](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/controlling-biology-fabian-theis-helmholtz-munich/)).

### Designing for agents

- Zeno Rocha says “I want my product to be formless”, which is why Resend is API-first with a large MCP server ([Resend](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/designing-for-agents-zeno-rocha-resend/)).
- For answer engine optimisation, Rocha added FAQs, llms.txt and markdown pages. A model misquoted Resend's pricing until he published the numbers in text ([Resend](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/designing-for-agents-zeno-rocha-resend/)).
- Cristina Cordova says over 50% of Linear's issues are now created by agents, and that its opinionated structure is far easier for agents to read, understand and work with ([Linear](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/why-linear-says-issue-tracking-is-dead-cristina-cordova-coo/)).

### Teaching AI-native engineering

- Mihail Eric's Stanford course starts with a coding agent harness of about 200 lines, then moves through context engineering, code review and the software factory ([Stanford](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/one-million-ai-engineer-special-how-stanford-teaches-ai-native-enginee/)).
- Students now contribute to open-source repos because last year's assignments could be one-shotted. Eric says none of those he surveyed still type code by hand ([Stanford](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/one-million-ai-engineer-special-how-stanford-teaches-ai-native-enginee/)).

### Visual models and robots

- Andreas Blattmann and Dustin Podell say FLUX 3 is one model trained on images, video, audio and actions, from which BFL derives its image, video, upscaler and action products ([Black Forest Labs](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/building-visual-intelligence-andreas-blattmann-dustin-podell-black-for/)).
- The guests report $450 million raised and revenue past $100 million. Blattmann predicts 2027 will be the year of robotics in manufacturing ([Black Forest Labs](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/building-visual-intelligence-andreas-blattmann-dustin-podell-black-for/)).

### Going upmarket carefully

- Cordova struck Linear's enterprise goal when it had one salesperson, and only set it in 2025. She argues switching costs mean a challenger must be far better than 20-30% ([Linear](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/why-linear-says-issue-tracking-is-dead-cristina-cordova-coo/)).
- Lauritzen says Legora runs on Azure because early law-firm customers did not trust AWS or Google ([Legora](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/11/building-legal-agents-when-you-can-t-see-the-data-jacob-lauritzen-cto/)).
