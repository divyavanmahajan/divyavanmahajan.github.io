## Overview

Latent Space spent 2024 on agents, which moved from a diluted buzzword to minimal loops, computer use and coding IDEs. Evaluation became a bottleneck of its own. Synthetic data and open models narrowed the gap with closed labs. By the year-end review, pre-training scaling had slowed, inference-time compute had taken over and OpenAI's share was shrinking.

## Main themes

### Agents, from buzzword to working loops

- David Luan sees reliable multimodal agents as the path to AGI, while a host advised startups to drop the word agents as too diluted ([Adept](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/why-google-failed-to-make-gpt-3-why-multimodal-agents-are-the-path-to/)).
- Shunyu Yao says “we should treat agents as our customers” and design interfaces for them ([Language Agents](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/language-agents-from-reasoning-to-acting/)); Erik Schluntz credits a minimal loop and carefully engineered tools for Claude 3.5 Sonnet's SWE-bench result ([Claude 3.5 Sonnet](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/11/the-new-claude-3-5-sonnet-computer-use-and-building-sota-agents-with-e/)).
- At work, Dust bets on simple agents built by non-developers ([Dust](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/11/agents-work-dust-tt/)) and Lindy on agents on rails ([Lindy](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/11/agents-work-lindy-ai/)).
- Graham Neubig expects agent-focused LLMs and saturated benchmarks in 2025 ([2024 in Agents](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/12/2024-in-agents-ls-live-neurips-2024/)).

### Coding agents and AI IDEs

- Cosine fine-tuned GPT-4o on data showing how human engineers work to beat prompted models on SWE-bench ([Genie](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/08/is-finetuning-gpt4o-worth-it-with-alistair-pullen-cosine-genie/)).
- Bolt grew past $8m ARR in two months on frontier models with full app context ([Bolt.new](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/12/bolt-new-flow-engineering-for-code-agents-and-8m-arr-in-2-months-as-a/)); Codeium forked VS Code into Windsurf for enterprises ([Windsurf](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/12/windsurf-the-enterprise-ai-ide-with-varun-and-anshul-of-codeium-ai/)).

### Benchmarks and evals under strain

- Moritz Hardt finds “diversity comes at the cost of sensitivity. There's no free lunch in multitask benchmarks” ([ICLR Benchmarks](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/06/iclr-2024-best-papers-talks-benchmarks-reasoning-agents-ft-graham-neub/)).
- Clémentine Fourrier prefers reproducible leaderboards to arenas and LLM judges ([Benchmarks 201](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/07/benchmarks-201-why-leaderboards-arenas-llm-as-judge/)); Chatbot Arena's leads defend pairwise votes ([LMSys](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/11/in-the-arena-how-lmsys-changed-llm-benchmarking-forever/)).
- Nicholas Carlini urges personal benchmarks ([Carlini](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/08/why-you-should-write-your-own-llm-benchmarks-with-nicholas-carlini-goo/)); Ankur Goyal calls evals the core workflow of AI engineering ([Braintrust](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/10/production-ai-engineering-starts-with-evals-with-ankur-goyal-of-braint/)).

### Synthetic data and open models

- Soumith Chintala warns synthetic data is no magic wand ([Soumith Chintala](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/open-source-ai-is-ai-we-can-trust-with-soumith-chintala-of-meta-ai/)); Thomas Scialom describes Llama 3's RLHF on synthetic data and over-training past Chinchilla ([Llama 2, 3 & 4](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/07/llama-2-3-4-synthetic-data-rlhf-agents-on-the-path-to-open-source-agi/)).
- Ben Firshman argues open licences must allow fine-tuning ([Replicate](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/a-brief-history-of-the-open-source-ai-hacker-with-ben-firshman-of-repl/)); Lin Qiao expects compound open systems to beat closed models ([Fireworks](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/11/why-compound-ai-open-source-will-beat-closed-ai/)).
- NeurIPS guests say open models closed much of the gap but face rising costs ([2024 in Open Models](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/12/2024-in-open-models-ls-live-neurips/)), while synthetic data now runs through every training stage ([Synthetic Data and Smol Models](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/12/2024-in-synthetic-data-and-smol-models-ls-live-neurips/)).

### OpenAI, o1 and the price of intelligence

- Luan says OpenAI beat Google because “we took big swings and we focused” ([Adept](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/why-google-failed-to-make-gpt-3-why-multimodal-agents-are-the-path-to/)).
- The Q2 recap marked the end of OpenAI's default status as Claude 3.5 Sonnet took the lead ([Winds of AI Winter](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/08/the-winds-of-ai-winter-q2-four-wars-recap-chatgpt-voice-mode-preview/)).
- OpenAI shipped structured outputs and o1 ([From API to AGI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/from-api-to-agi-structured-outputs-openai-api-platform-and-o1-q-a-with/)) and pitched itself as a developer platform at Dev Day ([Dev Day 2024](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/10/building-agi-in-real-time-openai-dev-day-2024/)).
- Inference costs keep falling ([Efficiency is Coming](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/09/efficiency-is-coming-3000x-faster-cheaper-better-ai-inference-from-har/), [Year in Review](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/12/latent-space-2024-year-in-review/)).
