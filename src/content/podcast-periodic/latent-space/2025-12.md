## Overview

December was a busy month for Latent Space, with twelve episodes and a year-end run of State of... recaps. OpenAI was the common thread: its researchers covered Codex, post-training and RL, while other guests debated how far coding agents can be trusted and how to measure them. MCP's first year, red teaming, world models and Meta's SAM 3 rounded out the month.

## Main themes

### Trusting coding agents

- Brian Fioca and Bill Chen say trust in Codex is trained in through behaviours such as communication, planning and checking work. Partners found a ripgrep tool named rg works better than one named grep ([Codex Max](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/gpt5-codex-max-training-agents-with-personality-tools-trust-brian-fioc/)).
- One guest says “I haven't written a single line of code by hand in months because I know what I can trust it to do” ([Codex Max](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/gpt5-codex-max-training-agents-with-personality-tools-trust-brian-fioc/)).
- Steve Yegge estimates 80-90% of programmers do not use Claude Code or anything like it because it is too hard. He expects an agent orchestration dashboard to replace the IDE, saying we are moving “into the John Deere era of coding” ([Yegge](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/steve-yegge-s-vibe-coding-manifesto-why-claude-code-isn-t-it-what-come/)).
- Loïc Houssier says onboarding to unfamiliar code fell from a day to thirty minutes with Claude Code, and Superhuman's PR throughput rose from about four to six per engineer per week ([Superhuman](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/the-future-of-email-superhuman-cto-on-your-inbox-as-the-real-ai-agent/)).

### Post-training and RL from inside the labs

- Josh McGrath says real progress comes from trustworthy reward signals, not new optimisers. From GPT-5 to 5.1, evals rose a little while the tokens needed fell sharply ([Post-Training](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/state-of-post-training-from-gpt-4-1-to-5-1-rlvr-agent-token-efficiency/)).
- Ashvin Nair, who worked on o1 and o3, argues RL on LLMs masters its training distribution but generalises little. Context, not intelligence, is the bottleneck for most jobs ([RL/Reasoning](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/state-of-rl-reasoning-imo-ioi-gold-openai-o3-gpt-5-and-cursor-composer/)).
- Nair joined Cursor to co-design product and model; its online Tab model gets a policy update about every two hours. Sarah Catanzaro cites Cursor's use of real user activity to argue RL environments are a fad ([RL/Reasoning](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/state-of-rl-reasoning-imo-ioi-gold-openai-o3-gpt-5-and-cursor-composer/), [AI Startups](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/state-of-ai-startups-memory-learning-rl-envs-dbt-fivetran-sarah-catanz/)).

### Measuring models after SWE-bench

- John Yang presents CodeClash, where models maintain their own codebases through a tournament, as the step beyond isolated unit-test tasks ([Code Evals](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/state-of-code-evals-after-swe-bench-code-clash-sota-coding-benchmarks/)).
- A host suggests benchmarks include impossible tasks on purpose to catch cheating; Yang cites ImpossibleBench ([Code Evals](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/state-of-code-evals-after-swe-bench-code-clash-sota-coding-benchmarks/)).
- The Codex guests see a lack of real-world evals and describe LLM-as-judge over full trajectories. Superhuman moved to canonical queries per dimension ([Codex Max](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/gpt5-codex-max-training-agents-with-personality-tools-trust-brian-fioc/), [Superhuman](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/the-future-of-email-superhuman-cto-on-your-inbox-as-the-real-ai-agent/)).

### Open protocols and system-level security

- David Soria Parra traces MCP's first year and its move to the Agentic AI Foundation, which he calls an umbrella to keep projects neutral. Jim Zemlin says 50 companies joined on day one ([One Year of MCP](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/one-year-of-mcp-with-david-soria-parra-and-aaif-leads-from-openai-goos/)).
- Pliny the Liberator and John V call model guardrails security theatre and argue real security work belongs at the system layer, in the tools and agents a model connects to ([Jailbreaking AGI](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/jailbreaking-agi-pliny-the-liberator-john-v-on-red-teaming-bt6-and-the/)).

### World models and vision

- Pim of General Intuition bets that Medal's 3.8 billion action-labelled game clips can train world models. He declined a reported $500 million OpenAI offer and raised a $134 million seed led by Khosla Ventures ([World Models](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/world-models-general-intuition-khosla-s-largest-bet-since-llms-openai/)).
- Meta's SAM 3 segments any short noun phrase, backed by a benchmark of more than 200,000 concepts and AI verifiers in the data engine ([SAM 3](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/12/sam-3-the-eyes-for-ai-nikhila-pengchuan-meta-superintelligence-ft-jose/)).
