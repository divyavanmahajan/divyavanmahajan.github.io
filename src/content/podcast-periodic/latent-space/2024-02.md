## Overview

February on Latent Space was an infrastructure month. Three founders who sell compute for open models (Together AI, Modal and Replicate) explained how they get GPUs, price them and make them easy to use. Retool's David Hsu supplied the enterprise view: the value of AI lies in automating internal workflows, not in chat.

## Main themes

### Buying and reselling GPUs

- Together AI's Vipul Ved Prakash discloses about 7,000-8,000 GPUs and says new supply is “minimally like two to three months out”, with inventory clearing fast ([Together AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/cloud-intelligence-at-the-speed-of-5000-tok-s-with-ce-zhang-and-vipul/)).
- Ben Firshman says clouds will not rent a single A100 but will rent 100 for two years, so Replicate aggregates demand into long commitments on GCP and CoreWeave. swyx likened this to a bank's maturity transformation ([Replicate](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/a-brief-history-of-the-open-source-ai-hacker-with-ben-firshman-of-repl/)).
- Firshman takes a different view of scarcity: “From our point of view, demand is not outpacing supply of GPUs” ([Replicate](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/a-brief-history-of-the-open-source-ai-hacker-with-ben-firshman-of-repl/)).
- Erik Bernhardsson compares the VC-subsidised GPU glut to the 1998-99 fiber boom, and wants Modal's costs to track revenue closely ([Modal](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/truly-serverless-infra-for-ai-engineers-with-erik-bernhardsson-of-moda/)).

### Serverless infrastructure and standards for open models

- Bernhardsson argues generative AI fits serverless well. Modal starts containers within a second or two and charges only while GPUs run, so many inference users save money despite a higher hourly price ([Modal](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/truly-serverless-infra-for-ai-engineers-with-erik-bernhardsson-of-moda/)).
- Firshman describes Cog as a Docker container with an OpenAPI specification, and says the magic of Docker is the agreed standard rather than the software ([Replicate](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/a-brief-history-of-the-open-source-ai-hacker-with-ben-firshman-of-repl/)).
- Both Modal and Replicate stay out of the LLM price war. Bernhardsson focuses on custom image, audio and video models; Firshman says Replicate prices sensibly ([Modal](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/truly-serverless-infra-for-ai-engineers-with-erik-bernhardsson-of-moda/), [Replicate](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/a-brief-history-of-the-open-source-ai-hacker-with-ben-firshman-of-repl/)).
- Firshman says licences must keep fine-tuning possible, or a release loses what makes it open ([Replicate](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/a-brief-history-of-the-open-source-ai-hacker-with-ben-firshman-of-repl/)).

### Faster inference through joint optimisation

- Ce Zhang argues the next 10x in efficiency needs data, algorithms, architecture and systems optimised together, since pushing one direction hits diminishing returns ([Together AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/cloud-intelligence-at-the-speed-of-5000-tok-s-with-ce-zhang-and-vipul/)).
- Together's inference stack combines about 50 techniques. The founders target 5,000 tokens per second for machine-consumed output, which they say transformers alone will not reach ([Together AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/cloud-intelligence-at-the-speed-of-5000-tok-s-with-ce-zhang-and-vipul/)).
- RedPajama V2 offers 30 trillion tokens with about 40 precomputed quality signals so users can build their own filters ([Together AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/cloud-intelligence-at-the-speed-of-5000-tok-s-with-ce-zhang-and-vipul/)).

### Enterprise AI as workflow automation

- David Hsu sees the next breakthrough in automating whole processes, not chatbots. He thinks agents fail because errors compound, so Retool Workflows has humans lay out steps that can each call AI ([Retool](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/why-stackoverflow-usage-is-down-50-with-david-hsu-of-retool/)).
- He expects “every internal app especially, is gonna be AI infused over the next, like, three years”, and estimates Copilot makes coding 10-20% faster ([Retool](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/why-stackoverflow-usage-is-down-50-with-david-hsu-of-retool/)).
- Retool's survey of about 1,600 people found 52% call AI overrated and 27% have it in production, mostly for internal use ([Retool](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/02/why-stackoverflow-usage-is-down-50-with-david-hsu-of-retool/)).
