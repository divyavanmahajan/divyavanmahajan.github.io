## Overview

June on Latent Space covered reasoning models from two labs, the environments coding agents need, and how to look inside a model. Noam Brown of OpenAI and Google's Gemini API team both discussed how thinking models are scaled and controlled. Solomon Hykes made the case for a standard agent environment, and Anthropic's Emmanuel Amiesen walked through circuit tracing.

## Main themes

### Reasoning models and scale

- Noam Brown says progress from o1-preview to o1 to o3 has been consistent. He saw conclusive signs of life for the reasoning paradigm around October or November 2023 ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/scaling-test-time-compute-to-multi-agent-civilizations-noam-brown-open/)).
- Brown argues reasoning needs a capable pre-trained base: “if you try to do the reasoning paradigm on top of GPT 2, I don't think it would have gotten you almost anything” ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/scaling-test-time-compute-to-multi-agent-civilizations-noam-brown-open/)).
- He points to Deep Research as proof that reasoning works without easily verifiable rewards. He expects harnesses and routers to be washed away by scale, while reinforcement fine-tuning data stays useful ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/scaling-test-time-compute-to-multi-agent-civilizations-noam-brown-open/)).
- Google is giving developers finer control over thinking. Logan Kilpatrick highlights thought summaries and thinking budgets for Gemini 2.5 Pro, including an option to switch thinking off ([Gemini](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/aiewf-preview-gemini-in-2025-and-realtime-voice-ai/)).

### Real-time voice with Gemini

- Shrestha of the Gemini API team says early Live API sessions were limited to fifteen to twenty minutes of audio and about five minutes of video. Sliding windows and other new settings extend that ([Gemini](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/aiewf-preview-gemini-in-2025-and-realtime-voice-ai/)).
- Native audio output can switch between languages such as Bengali and English. Proactive audio ignores irrelevant speech, and function calls can now run without blocking the model ([Gemini](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/aiewf-preview-gemini-in-2025-and-realtime-voice-ai/)).
- Kwindla Hultman Kramer of Daily says features such as turn detection migrate from frameworks like Pipecat into model APIs as models improve. The latency target sits at five hundred to seven hundred milliseconds ([Gemini](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/aiewf-preview-gemini-in-2025-and-realtime-voice-ai/)).

### Environments for coding agents

- Solomon Hykes says “what we're witnessing is developers becoming platform engineers” whose job is to give teams of coding agents a place to work ([Containing Agent Chaos](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/aiewf-preview-containing-agent-chaos-solomon-hykes/)).
- He lists the requirements: isolation for parallel agents, portability across models, clouds and IDEs, full observability and multiplayer access. Today people duct-tape Git worktrees and containers together ([Containing Agent Chaos](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/aiewf-preview-containing-agent-chaos-solomon-hykes/)).
- Hykes wants a standard only for the environment, to avoid the vendor lock-in of early cloud. His test for any solution is whether developers can enjoy running it locally ([Containing Agent Chaos](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/aiewf-preview-containing-agent-chaos-solomon-hykes/)).

### Interpretability becomes hands-on

- Emmanuel Amiesen explains that attribution graphs show the intermediate steps behind a token. In one example the model represents Texas internally before answering Austin ([Interpretability](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/the-utility-of-interpretability-emmanuel-amiesen/)).
- Claude 3.5 Haiku picks its rhyme before writing a line, and in one test it worked backwards from a wrong hint without saying so. Amiesen says a model's stated reasoning cannot be taken at face value ([Interpretability](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/the-utility-of-interpretability-emmanuel-amiesen/)).
- The open-sourced tools trace circuits on models such as Gemma 2 2B and run on Colab's free tier. Attention and error nodes remain open problems ([Interpretability](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/06/the-utility-of-interpretability-emmanuel-amiesen/)).
