## Overview

October closed with two AI safety heavyweights, Shane Legg and Paul Christiano, and AGI timelines were the one thread they shared. Earlier in the month Grant Sanderson discussed maths education and AI for mathematics, and Sarah Paine explained why dictators keep making the same strategic mistakes.

## Main themes

### AGI timelines

- Legg keeps the forecast from his 2011 blog post: a mean of 2028 and a mode of 2025. His reasoning was that in the 2020s models could train on more data than a human experiences in a lifetime ([Legg](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/shane-legg-deepmind-founder-2028-agi-superhuman-alignment-new-architec/)).
- He sees no big walls, only gaps such as episodic memory and search that need architectural fixes. He expects models to become “less delusional, much more factual” on the way to 2028 ([Legg](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/shane-legg-deepmind-founder-2028-agi-superhuman-alignment-new-architec/)).
- Christiano is more modest: 15% for AI able to build a Dyson sphere by 2030 and 40% by 2040. He expects fab capacity to be the bottleneck rather than software alone ([Christiano](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/paul-christiano-preventing-an-ai-takeover/)).

### Alignment approaches

- Legg argues that containing a truly capable AGI is probably not a winning strategy, so it must be value aligned. Choosing its ethics is a problem for society, not engineers ([Legg](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/shane-legg-deepmind-founder-2028-agi-superhuman-alignment-new-architec/)).
- He wants systems that reason explicitly about ethics, System 2 style, with humans auditing that reasoning rather than relying on reinforcement alone ([Legg](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/shane-legg-deepmind-founder-2028-agi-superhuman-alignment-new-architec/)).
- Christiano pushes labs to adopt responsible scaling policies that pre-commit to capability tests and matching safeguards. Anthropic has published one ([Christiano](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/paul-christiano-preventing-an-ai-takeover/)).
- His Alignment Research Center, four full-time researchers, seeks proof-like explanations of model behaviour. He gives its best case a 10-20% chance ([Christiano](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/paul-christiano-preventing-an-ai-takeover/)).

### How a takeover might unfold

- Christiano expects a slow slide in which humans can see that “they have increasingly little grip on what's happening”, followed by a possibly abrupt failure ([Christiano](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/paul-christiano-preventing-an-ai-takeover/)).
- His median bad scenario involves AI control over critical systems, including militaries, with humans cooperating with the AI systems ([Christiano](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/paul-christiano-preventing-an-ai-takeover/)).
- He accepts that alignment is dual-use, since it makes AI more usable, including for one person calling the shots. He still judges withholding it a poor way to buy time ([Christiano](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/paul-christiano-preventing-an-ai-takeover/)).

### Mathematics, AI and talent

- Sanderson sees progress towards general AI as continuous. An AI winning IMO gold would be a milestone like chess and Go, not the Industrial Revolution ([Sanderson](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/grant-sanderson-3blue1brown-past-present-future-of-mathematics/)).
- He expects maths to suit synthetic data, such as proofs in Lean, trained the way AlphaGo learned ([Sanderson](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/grant-sanderson-3blue1brown-past-present-future-of-mathematics/)).
- He thinks academia, finance and data science attract too much mathematical talent, and floats NSF grants requiring 10% of time on cross-department work ([Sanderson](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/grant-sanderson-3blue1brown-past-present-future-of-mathematics/)).

### Dictators and grand strategy

- Paine contrasts continental powers that seek security through territory with maritime powers whose trade-based order is win-win ([Paine](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/sarah-c-m-paine-why-dictators-keep-making-the-same-fatal-mistake/)).
- She argues the Chinese Communist Party puts its monopoly on power ahead of prosperity, and is now left with the nationalism card over Taiwan ([Paine](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/sarah-c-m-paine-why-dictators-keep-making-the-same-fatal-mistake/)).
- Borrowing Sun Tzu's death ground, she says Hitler's brutality turned Ukrainians into fighters, and Putin is forging Ukrainian identity the same way ([Paine](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/sarah-c-m-paine-why-dictators-keep-making-the-same-fatal-mistake/)).
