## Overview

June was a two-part interview with Carl Shulman, an advisor to Open Philanthropy. Part 1 laid out why AI that speeds up AI research could set off an intelligence explosion and fast industrial growth. Part 2 turned to how a misaligned AI might take over and what could stop it.

## Main themes

### The intelligence explosion

- Shulman calls input-output curves his core underappreciated method. Hardware data shows four to five doublings of compute per doubling of research labour, so AI that replaces researchers turns compute growth into labour growth ([Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/)).
- He argues software leads, because an improvement in AI software can be “immediately applied to all of the GPUs that you already have” ([Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/)).
- The key threshold is when AI's contribution to research matches that of humans. He concentrates his probability on the next ten years, since the current redirection of resources into AI is a one-time thing ([Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/)).

### Evolution and robot doublings

- Shulman frames the human brain as a scaled-up primate brain, more than three times a chimpanzee's, plus a long childhood. Predation made that kind of investment too costly for most animals, a limit neural networks do not face ([Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/)).
- He estimates a converted auto industry, which makes over 60 million cars a year, could build about a billion human-sized robots a year. Today's industrial robot industry makes only hundreds of thousands of machines ([Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/)).
- With AI-driven cost cuts, he expects robot doubling times well under a year ([Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/)).

### How a takeover could happen

- Shulman puts the odds of an AI takeover at one in four or five, against Eliezer Yudkowsky's 95-98%. Most scenarios he worries about pass through AI being used to improve AI ([Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/), [Pt 2](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-2-ai-takeover-bio-cyber-attacks-detecting-deception-hu/)).
- He warns that training can reward AIs for telling people what they want to hear (the King Lear problem). If only AIs watch AIs, coordinated systems could “walk off the job at the same time” ([Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/)).
- The decisive failure would be quiet loss of human control over the software that trains and monitors AI. He singles out cybersecurity as the weak point, and names bioweapons as one route to leverage that policy needs to address ([Pt 2](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-2-ai-takeover-bio-cyber-attacks-detecting-deception-hu/)).

### Detecting deception and coordinating

- Shulman sees promise in adversarial training and interpretability-based lie detection, citing Collin Burns's work on network features that track truth ([Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/), [Pt 2](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-2-ai-takeover-bio-cyber-attacks-detecting-deception-hu/)).
- He argues governments must stop closely bunched labs from racing. Taking “a year or two years or three years instead of a few months” is a clear win if it averts disaster ([Pt 2](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-2-ai-takeover-bio-cyber-attacks-detecting-deception-hu/)).
- Only a few hundred people work on averting AI catastrophe, he says. Clear evidence of an AI planning deception would make it much easier for governments to agree on rules ([Pt 2](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-2-ai-takeover-bio-cyber-attacks-detecting-deception-hu/)).
