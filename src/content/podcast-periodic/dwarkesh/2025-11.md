## Overview

November had two heavyweight AI interviews. Satya Nadella explained how Microsoft is building infrastructure for a world without a single winning model, and Ilya Sutskever argued the field is leaving the age of scaling for an age of research. Continual learning and OpenAI came up in both.

## Main themes

### Microsoft builds for many models

- Nadella says Microsoft did not want to be a hoster with a massive book of business from one customer. He calls its hyperscale business a long tail of AI workloads, not five bare-metal contracts ([Satya Nadella](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/satya-nadella-how-microsoft-is-preparing-for-agi/)).
- Scott Guthrie says Microsoft aims to 10x training capacity every 18 to 24 months, and that the Fairwater 2 network optics nearly match all of Azure two and a half years ago ([Satya Nadella](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/satya-nadella-how-microsoft-is-preparing-for-agi/)).
- Dylan Patel points to $500 billion of hyperscaler capex next year and free cash flow heading to zero. Nadella counters that software gains of 5x to 40x in throughput raise return on capital ([Satya Nadella](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/satya-nadella-how-microsoft-is-preparing-for-agi/)).
- Nadella expects Microsoft's end-user tools business to become “an infrastructure business in support of agents doing work”, priced per user and per agent ([Satya Nadella](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/satya-nadella-how-microsoft-is-preparing-for-agi/)).

### OpenAI terms and the coding market

- Microsoft keeps access to OpenAI models for seven more years, plus its chip and system IP except consumer hardware. OpenAI's API is Azure-exclusive while ChatGPT can run anywhere ([Satya Nadella](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/satya-nadella-how-microsoft-is-preparing-for-agi/)).
- Dwarkesh Patel cites Claude Code, Cursor and Copilot at around $1 billion each, and Dylan Patel says Microsoft fell below 25% share in a year. Nadella notes Copilot grew from 20 to 26 million subscribers ([Satya Nadella](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/satya-nadella-how-microsoft-is-preparing-for-agi/)).
- Nadella argues a model company can be one copy away from commoditisation, while whoever holds data and scaffolding captures value ([Satya Nadella](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/satya-nadella-how-microsoft-is-preparing-for-agi/)).

### Back to the age of research

- Sutskever dates an age of research from 2012 to 2020 and an age of scaling from 2020 to 2025. He says it is now “back to the age of research again, just with big computers” ([Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/ilya-sutskever-we-re-moving-from-the-age-of-scaling-to-the-age-of-rese/)).
- He calls weak generalisation the crux: models resemble a student who practised 10,000 hours of competitive programming and does not transfer to other things ([Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/ilya-sutskever-we-re-moving-from-the-age-of-scaling-to-the-age-of-rese/)).
- Scaling left more companies than ideas, he says. SSI has raised $3 billion and he argues its research compute is comparable to rivals' ([Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/ilya-sutskever-we-re-moving-from-the-age-of-scaling-to-the-age-of-rese/)).

### Continual learning, alignment and safety

- Sutskever reframes superintelligence as an eager learner that picks up jobs after deployment, and thinks broad deployment could bring very rapid economic growth. The Nadella interview's hosts asked whether continual learning could shift margins away from infrastructure ([Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/ilya-sutskever-we-re-moving-from-the-age-of-scaling-to-the-age-of-rese/), [Satya Nadella](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/satya-nadella-how-microsoft-is-preparing-for-agi/)).
- He predicts AI companies will become “much more paranoid” about safety once AI feels powerful. He favours AI that cares about sentient life and capping the power of the strongest systems ([Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/ilya-sutskever-we-re-moving-from-the-age-of-scaling-to-the-age-of-rese/)).
- He gives 5 to 20 years for human-like learners ([Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/11/ilya-sutskever-we-re-moving-from-the-age-of-scaling-to-the-age-of-rese/)).
