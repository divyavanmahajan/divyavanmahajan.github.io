## Overview

February brought two long interviews with leaders of frontier AI companies. Elon Musk argued that electricity is the near-term limit on AI and that space will soon be the cheapest place to run it. Dario Amodei argued that the scaling exponential is nearly done, and explained how that shapes Anthropic's compute purchases, revenue and policy views.

## Main themes

### Power, chips and data centres in space

- Musk says “The output of chips is growing pretty much exponentially, but the output of electricity is flat.” He adds that gas turbines are sold out through 2030 ([Musk](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/elon-musk-in-36-months-the-cheapest-place-to-put-ai-will-be-space/)).
- He predicts that within 30-36 months space will be the cheapest place for AI, since solar panels produce about five times more power in orbit. He targets a few hundred gigawatts launched per year within five years ([Musk](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/elon-musk-in-36-months-the-cheapest-place-to-put-ai-will-be-space/)).
- Once power is solved, Musk expects chips and especially memory to be the constraint, and plans a TeraFab for logic, memory and packaging ([Musk](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/elon-musk-in-36-months-the-cheapest-place-to-put-ai-will-be-space/)).

### Anthropic's growth and compute bets

- Amodei says Anthropic's revenue grew roughly 10x a year, to $9-10 billion in 2025, and added another few billion in January ([Amodei](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/dario-amodei-we-are-near-the-end-of-the-exponential/)).
- He explains why Anthropic does not buy $1 trillion of compute for 2027: if growth came in at 5x instead of 10x, the company would go bankrupt. He puts industry build-out at 10-15 gigawatts this year, rising about 3x a year ([Amodei](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/dario-amodei-we-are-near-the-end-of-the-exponential/)).
- In his toy model, “Each model makes money, but the company loses money” because training the next model costs far more ([Amodei](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/dario-amodei-we-are-near-the-end-of-the-exponential/)).

### Coding automation and economic diffusion

- Amodei says models writing 90% of code happened at Anthropic but is a weak test. He expects end-to-end software engineering in one to two years and estimates a 15-20% total factor speed-up today. Dwarkesh Patel cites the METR study showing a slowdown ([Amodei](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/dario-amodei-we-are-near-the-end-of-the-exponential/)).
- He says big enterprises are adopting Claude Code faster than they usually adopt new technology. Patel calls Claude Code a category leader in a crowded market ([Amodei](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/dario-amodei-we-are-near-the-end-of-the-exponential/)).
- Amodei describes diffusion as a second fast exponential that is not instant. Patel calls diffusion cope. Amodei still finds it hard to see no trillions in AI revenue before 2030 ([Amodei](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/dario-amodei-we-are-near-the-end-of-the-exponential/)).

### Alignment, robots and government

- Musk doubts humans can control far smarter AI and wants Grok to be rigorously truth-seeking, warning that forcing AI to lie can make it go insane. He praises Anthropic's interpretability work ([Musk](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/elon-musk-in-36-months-the-cheapest-place-to-put-ai-will-be-space/)).
- He argues the US cannot out-labour China but might win with humanoid robots, describing an Optimus Academy of thousands of robots doing self-play ([Musk](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/elon-musk-in-36-months-the-cheapest-place-to-put-ai-will-be-space/)).
- Musk defends DOGE's Treasury payment codes, while Patel questions his fraud figure ([Musk](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/elon-musk-in-36-months-the-cheapest-place-to-put-ai-will-be-space/)).
- Amodei opposed the 10-year moratorium on state AI laws, favours a federal transparency standard and supports chip export controls ([Amodei](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/02/dario-amodei-we-are-near-the-end-of-the-exponential/)).
