## Overview

June put two opposite views of AGI timelines side by side. Leopold Aschenbrenner argued that straight-line trends point to AGI around 2027 and a national security race with China, while François Chollet argued that scaling LLMs adds skill but not intelligence. Tony Blair closed the month on why governments struggle with major change, and why AI is now their central task.

## Main themes

### How close is AGI

- Aschenbrenner extrapolates training compute from GPT-4's roughly 25,000 A100s to a gigawatt cluster by 2026 and 10 GW by 2028. He says: “The 10 GW range is my best guess for when you get true AGI” ([Aschenbrenner](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/leopold-aschenbrenner-2027-agi-china-us-super-intelligence-race-the-re/)).
- He expects AI research to be among the first jobs automated, so that 100 million automated researchers could compress a decade of ML progress into a year. Dwarkesh Patel counters that 100x more researchers than a century ago has not produced 100x faster progress ([Aschenbrenner](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/leopold-aschenbrenner-2027-agi-china-us-super-intelligence-race-the-re/)).
- Chollet defines intelligence as adapting efficiently to novelty and sees LLMs as big interpolative memories. Patel pushes back that models sit on a smooth spectrum of generality that scale keeps climbing ([Chollet](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/francois-chollet-why-the-biggest-ai-models-can-t-solve-simple-puzzles/)).

### ARC and program synthesis

- Chollet says “ARC is intended as a kind of IQ test for machine intelligence”. Smart humans should score 90-95%, while GPT-3 scored zero in the 2020 Kaggle contest ([Chollet](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/francois-chollet-why-the-biggest-ai-models-can-t-solve-simple-puzzles/)).
- He and Mike Knoop launch the ARC Prize: a little over $1 million in total, with $500,000 for the first team to reach 85% on a private test set and solutions released into the public domain ([Chollet](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/francois-chollet-why-the-biggest-ai-models-can-t-solve-simple-puzzles/)).
- Chollet expects winners to merge deep learning with discrete program search, which is data efficient but compute hungry. He also says OpenAI set back AGI research by ending open publication ([Chollet](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/francois-chollet-why-the-biggest-ai-models-can-t-solve-simple-puzzles/)).

### Security, China and a government project

- Aschenbrenner says stealing lab secrets is currently extremely easy and that algorithmic secrets are underrated next to compute. He expects the CCP to mount an all-out effort to infiltrate American labs ([Aschenbrenner](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/leopold-aschenbrenner-2027-agi-china-us-super-intelligence-race-the-re/)).
- He argues people underrate the chance that AGI becomes a government project, run like Operation Warp Speed. Patel replies that a government-held ASI is no safer than a private one ([Aschenbrenner](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/leopold-aschenbrenner-2027-agi-china-us-super-intelligence-race-the-re/)).
- Aschenbrenner says OpenAI dropped its 20% superalignment compute commitment, and that he was fired after sharing a security memo with board members ([Aschenbrenner](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/leopold-aschenbrenner-2027-agi-china-us-super-intelligence-race-the-re/)).

### Governing through change

- Blair calls AI the biggest technological change since the Industrial Revolution and says that if an AI crisis hit now, political leaders “wouldn't know where to begin in solving that problem” ([Blair](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/tony-blair-why-political-leaders-keep-failing-at-major-change/)).
- He says leaders confuse ambitions with policies and put politics before policy. His four Ps are prioritise, policy, personnel and performance management ([Blair](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/tony-blair-why-political-leaders-keep-failing-at-major-change/)).
- Blair points to Lee Kuan Yew's three contested early decisions for Singapore and proposes a reimagined state using AI tutors and AI doctors ([Blair](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2024/06/tony-blair-why-political-leaders-keep-failing-at-major-change/)).
