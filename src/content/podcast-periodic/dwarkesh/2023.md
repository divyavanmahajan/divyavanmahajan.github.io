## Overview

2023 is the year the Dwarkesh Podcast turned to AI. AI alignment was central in seven of 19 episodes, with guests from OpenAI, Anthropic and DeepMind alongside Eliezer Yudkowsky, Carl Shulman and Paul Christiano. The rest of the year ran through nuclear history, war and dictators, and the aftermath of FTX. Longtermism and the 2022 run of economists faded.

## Main themes

### Alignment and the risk of takeover

- Holden Karnofsky argues trial-and-error training can produce a system pursuing an unintended goal ([Holden Karnofsky](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/01/holden-karnofsky-history-s-most-important-century/)).
- Yudkowsky says alignment cannot be verified before a smarter system can deceive us, adding “I am no longer willing to say that GPT-6 does not end the world” ([Eliezer Yudkowsky](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/04/eliezer-yudkowsky-why-ai-will-kill-us-aligning-llms-nature-of-intellig/)).
- Carl Shulman puts the odds of an AI takeover at roughly one in four or five, but argues oversight, deception detection and international coordination can still prevent it ([Carl Shulman Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/), [Pt 2](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-2-ai-takeover-bio-cyber-attacks-detecting-deception-hu/)).
- Paul Christiano sees takeover coming more likely from gradual loss of human understanding plus competition, and wants pre-committed scaling policies at labs ([Paul Christiano](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/paul-christiano-preventing-an-ai-takeover/)).

### AGI timelines and the intelligence explosion

- Ilya Sutskever expects a multi-year window of growing economic value before AGI and names reliability as the most likely reason LLMs could disappoint ([Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/03/ilya-sutskever-openai-chief-scientist-why-next-token-prediction-could/)).
- Shulman argues an intelligence explosion starts once AI contributions to research match human ones ([Carl Shulman Pt 1](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/06/carl-shulman-pt-1-intelligence-explosion-primate-evolution-robot-doubl/)).
- Shane Legg keeps his 50% odds of AGI by 2028 ([Shane Legg](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/shane-legg-deepmind-founder-2028-agi-superhuman-alignment-new-architec/)); Christiano gives roughly 15% for Dyson-sphere-capable AI by 2030 and 40% by 2040 ([Paul Christiano](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/paul-christiano-preventing-an-ai-takeover/)).

### Scaling, interpretability and lab security

- Dario Amodei says of why scaling works: “we still don't know. It's almost entirely an empirical fact” ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/08/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-the-hidden-pattern-behind-every-ai-breakthr/)).
- Amodei treats interpretability as an independent check that labs should never train against, and admits Anthropic could not stop a determined state actor stealing its weights ([Dario Amodei](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/08/dario-amodei-anthropic-ceo-the-hidden-pattern-behind-every-ai-breakthr/)).

### Nuclear weapons, war and dictators

- Richard Rhodes argues the bomb was close to inevitable once fission was found, and warns that Putin uses nuclear weapons to backstop conventional war ([Richard Rhodes](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/05/richard-rhodes-the-making-of-the-atomic-bomb/)).
- Sarah Paine argues continental powers that seek security through conquest make pivotal errors, and that a Chinese attack on Taiwan would be one ([Sarah C. M. Paine](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/10/sarah-c-m-paine-why-dictators-keep-making-the-same-fatal-mistake/)).
- Andrew Roberts says Hitler “put his Nazi ideology before the strategic best interests of the German Reich” ([Andrew Roberts](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/andrew-roberts-why-hitler-lost-wwii-churchill-as-applied-historian-nap/)). Jung Chang traces Mao's famine to his choice to export food for Soviet arms ([Jung Chang](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/jung-chang-wild-swans-author-living-through-history-s-largest-man-made/)).

### Founders, FTX and land

- Brett Harrison describes SBF's dysfunctional leadership at FTX and the lessons on governance ([Brett Harrison](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/03/brett-harrison-ftx-us-former-president-speaks-out/)).
- Marc Andreessen says great entrepreneurs, not capital, are the real constraint ([Marc Andreessen](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/02/marc-andreessen-ai-crypto-1000-elon-musks-regrets-vulnerabilities-mana/)); Lars Doucet defends the land value tax ([Lars Doucet](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/01/lars-doucet-progress-poverty-georgism-why-rent-is-too-damn-high/)).
