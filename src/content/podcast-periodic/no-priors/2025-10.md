## Overview

October on No Priors mixed operator interviews with a look back at the year. Palo Alto Networks, Warp and Base Power covered how agents change software businesses, security and coding, and how batteries could meet rising power demand. Eric Zelikman and the best-of compilation added a research angle on reasoning models, jobs and the geopolitics of superintelligence.

## Main themes

### Agents, business models and security

- Nikesh Arora argues agents are a bigger disruption than generative AI, because they bypass the interfaces many apps and ad models depend on. He says “the most vulnerable people are where there is poor loyalty to the UI” ([Palo Alto Networks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/the-impact-of-ai-from-business-models-to-cybersecurity-with-palo-alto/)).
- He sees travel booking and similar thin front ends as exposed, and expects search ads to shift from paying for leads to paying for completed transactions ([Palo Alto Networks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/the-impact-of-ai-from-business-models-to-cybersecurity-with-palo-alto/)).
- Arora says the time from intrusion to data theft has fallen from three to four days to as little as 23 minutes, while response still takes days. He puts 89% of attacks down to credential theft and argues for consolidated platforms over point tools ([Palo Alto Networks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/the-impact-of-ai-from-business-models-to-cybersecurity-with-palo-alto/)).
- No enterprise yet gives LLMs autonomy for agentic tasks, in his view. He expects 80-90% of customer support work to disappear within two to five years ([Palo Alto Networks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/the-impact-of-ai-from-business-models-to-cybersecurity-with-palo-alto/)).

### Coding moves from prompting to automation

- Zach Lloyd describes three phases: develop by hand, develop by prompt and automated development, which he sees as the bigger market ([Warp](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/reinventing-the-developer-terminal-with-warp-co-founder-and-ceo-zach-l/)).
- He argues automation is a better business than productivity tools, since coding tools struggle to prove ROI. He treats agents as junior engineers that still need senior review ([Warp](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/reinventing-the-developer-terminal-with-warp-co-founder-and-ceo-zach-l/)).
- Lloyd notes smaller gains from Sonnet 4 to 4.5 than from 3.7 to 4, and expects code review, security checks and CI to fold into agent platforms ([Warp](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/reinventing-the-developer-terminal-with-warp-co-founder-and-ceo-zach-l/)).

### Reasoning models and the people around them

- OpenAI researchers say tool use, such as cropping images or writing code, gives o3 a much steeper test-time scaling slope, letting models allocate compute more efficiently ([Best of 2025](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/the-best-of-2025-so-far-with-sarah-guo-and-elad-gil/)).
- Eric Zelikman, creator of STaR, argues the field measures progress by how long models run without people. His company humans& wants models that track people's goals over time and help groups collaborate ([humans&](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/humans-bridging-iq-and-eq-in-machine-learning-with-eric-zelikman/)).
- Zelikman links single-turn training to models that never ask clarifying questions, and calls memory underinvested ([humans&](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/humans-bridging-iq-and-eq-in-machine-learning-with-eric-zelikman/)).
- On jobs, Brendan Foody expects fast, painful displacement in digital roles and a populist backlash. Dan Hendrycks suggests states may deter rival superintelligence bids with cyber attacks on data centers ([Best of 2025](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/the-best-of-2025-so-far-with-sarah-guo-and-elad-gil/)).

### Power demand and home batteries

- Zach Dell says Base Power designs, makes, installs, owns and operates home batteries and sells power directly to Texas homeowners, saving them 10-20% a month ([Base Power](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/unlocking-the-road-to-energy-abundance-with-base-power-ceo-and-co-foun/)).
- Dell puts US peak demand near 700 GW against an average near 300 GW, and argues batteries and software can use the gap. He says “Batteries move energy through time right poles and wires move energy through space” ([Base Power](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/unlocking-the-road-to-energy-abundance-with-base-power-ceo-and-co-foun/)).
- Base has raised about $1.3 billion in 18 months, and Dell thinks demand growth projections may even be understated ([Base Power](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/unlocking-the-road-to-energy-abundance-with-base-power-ceo-and-co-foun/)).
