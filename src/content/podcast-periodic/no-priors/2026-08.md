## Overview

August on No Priors ranged widely across four episodes. Sarah Guo and Elad Gil spent a hosts-only episode on founder ambition, exits and regulation, and Eon's founders argued that enterprise data is now the moat and agents the new insider risk. The other two episodes looked at human skill after machines become superhuman, through Chess.com, and at brain-computer interfaces as a business.

## Main themes

### Enterprise data and agent security

- Ofir Ehrlich argues that models and tools are now available to everyone, so a company's real advantage is its people and its data. He says labs are approaching hedge funds to buy data ([Eon](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/rethinking-legacy-data-infrastructure-with-eon-co-founders-ofir-ehrlic/)).
- That data is locked in business-unit silos. Eon maps and classifies it, builds a semantic layer and ingests it without touching production ([Eon](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/rethinking-legacy-data-infrastructure-with-eon-co-founders-ofir-ehrlic/)).
- The founders started Eon after seeing a large AWS customer with 60% of its environment exposed to ransomware. They now see the same threat from agents that “essentially have legitimate access to the environment”, such as agents dropping tables ([Eon](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/rethinking-legacy-data-infrastructure-with-eon-co-founders-ofir-ehrlic/)).
- Ehrlich says companies must assume breach, and expects non-human identity and endpoint security to become top problems as agents call agents ([Eon](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/rethinking-legacy-data-infrastructure-with-eon-co-founders-ofir-ehrlic/)).

### Founder ambition, exits and the RSI clock

- Gil argues that few companies can reach the revenue a trillion-dollar valuation needs within five years. Guo counters that investors underestimate how far AI companies can grow by charging for outcomes ([Sarah & Elad](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/chasing-trillion-dollar-companies-founder-ambition-token-budgets-and-r/)).
- Gil suggests a scheduled board discussion on selling every six months, because “every year of AI time is like 3 to 4 years of normal cycle time” ([Sarah & Elad](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/chasing-trillion-dollar-companies-founder-ambition-token-budgets-and-r/)).
- Gil reports lab expectations of light recursive self-improvement by the end of next year. Guo notes this has been forecast 18 months out for five years and sees physical compute as the bigger limit ([Sarah & Elad](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/chasing-trillion-dollar-companies-founder-ambition-token-budgets-and-r/)).
- Gil expects companies to ration token budgets by return, which is why he thinks the death of SaaS is overstated ([Sarah & Elad](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/chasing-trillion-dollar-companies-founder-ambition-token-budgets-and-r/)).

### Regulation and capture

- Gil contrasts France's 70% nuclear share with 18% in the US, blaming a 1970s safety lobby, and warns that labs could use heavy safety rules to hold others back ([Sarah & Elad](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/chasing-trillion-dollar-companies-founder-ambition-token-budgets-and-r/)).
- Guo calls California's billionaire tax the fastest way to drive the ecosystem out. Both point to Texas as an energy and hardware hub ([Sarah & Elad](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/chasing-trillion-dollar-companies-founder-ambition-token-budgets-and-r/)).

### Human skill alongside superhuman AI

- Erik Allebest says chess grew after computers surpassed humans, because people want to compete with other humans. Guo calls the idea that human skill no longer matters nonsense ([Chess.com](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/what-chess-com-teaches-us-about-superhuman-capabilities-with-ceo-erik/)).
- Chess.com has about 10 million daily users and expects a little over $200 million in revenue this year, still bootstrapped after secondary sales to General Atlantic and CVC ([Chess.com](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/what-chess-com-teaches-us-about-superhuman-capabilities-with-ceo-erik/)).
- The company uses AI for support, data analysis and agentic development, and is prototyping an AI coach ([Chess.com](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/what-chess-com-teaches-us-about-superhuman-capabilities-with-ceo-erik/)).

### Brain-computer interfaces as a business

- Max Hodak says “BCI is a category kind of like how pharma is a category”, so a single BCI bet makes little sense for investors ([Max Hodak](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/from-restoring-sight-to-reimagining-the-brain-with-max-hodak/)).
- Science Corporation received European marketing approval for its PRIMA retinal implant in July and raised mostly from tech rather than biotech investors ([Max Hodak](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/from-restoring-sight-to-reimagining-the-brain-with-max-hodak/)).
- Hodak backs the platonic representation hypothesis and says it convinced him AI is not hitting a wall ([Max Hodak](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/08/from-restoring-sight-to-reimagining-the-brain-with-max-hodak/)).
