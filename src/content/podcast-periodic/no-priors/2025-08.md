## Overview

August on No Priors looked at the physical and economic base under AI. Dylan Patel and Jacob Helberg both covered data centres, power and the contest with China, while Cloudflare's Matthew Prince asked who will pay for the content that models consume. Andrew Ng brought it back to how startups build with agents.

## Main themes

### Who pays for content

- Prince says it is now 10 times harder for content to get a click from Google than a decade ago, and much harder still with OpenAI and Anthropic. He warns this will starve the AI companies that depend on that content ([Cloudflare](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/the-shifting-value-of-content-in-the-ai-age-with-cloudflare-ceo-matthe/)).
- On July 1 Cloudflare began blocking AI training crawlers by default for all customers. Prince's reasoning: “In order to have a market you have to have scarcity.” ([Cloudflare](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/the-shifting-value-of-content-in-the-ai-age-with-cloudflare-ceo-matthe/)).
- He says AI companies accept paying for content if Google plays by the same rules. He calls flat deals naive and wants revenue shares that grow with AI companies ([Cloudflare](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/the-shifting-value-of-content-in-the-ai-age-with-cloudflare-ceo-matthe/)).

### Chips, neoclouds and bottlenecks

- Patel calls Nvidia a three-headed dragon in hardware, networking and software, and says “Nvidia charges a lot of money because they're the best” ([SemiAnalysis](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/chips-neoclouds-and-the-quest-for-ai-dominance-with-semianalysis-found/)).
- He argues a challenger's chip must be far more than two to four times faster to overcome stacked penalties on process, memory and networking. AMD, Trainium or TPUs are likelier alternatives than startups ([SemiAnalysis](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/chips-neoclouds-and-the-quest-for-ai-dominance-with-semianalysis-found/)).
- Patel expects most of about 200 neoclouds to consolidate or fail. Bottlenecks have moved from packaging and HBM to transformers, power and electricians, and Meta is putting GPUs in tents ([SemiAnalysis](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/chips-neoclouds-and-the-quest-for-ai-dominance-with-semianalysis-found/)).
- On exports, Patel backs selling services and tokens first, but says China cannot be given zero GPUs because it can retaliate with rare earths ([SemiAnalysis](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/chips-neoclouds-and-the-quest-for-ai-dominance-with-semianalysis-found/)).

### Agentic AI and startups

- Andrew Ng says the biggest barrier to agentic workflows is talent, especially teams that can run systematic error analysis with evals ([Andrew Ng](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/how-agentic-ai-is-transforming-the-startup-landscape-with-andrew-ng/)).
- Ng rejects the term vibe coding as too easy-sounding. With work that took six engineers three months now done over a weekend, the bottleneck has moved to deciding what to build ([Andrew Ng](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/how-agentic-ai-is-transforming-the-startup-landscape-with-andrew-ng/)).
- He thinks “software engineering is a harbinger of what will happen in other disciplines”, and favours technical founders and small AI-enabled teams. The hosts push back that staying lean can be a trap ([Andrew Ng](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/how-agentic-ai-is-transforming-the-startup-landscape-with-andrew-ng/)).

### AI capex and reindustrialisation

- Jacob Helberg says capex is over 2% of GDP and likely to double, with AI infrastructure adding about a point of GDP growth ([Jacob Helberg](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/reshaping-america-s-economy-for-the-superintelligence-century-with-jac/)).
- He argues AI productivity will erode developing countries' labour-cost advantage and let the US reindustrialise, backed by nuclear power and reshored supply chains for critical minerals and chips ([Jacob Helberg](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/reshaping-america-s-economy-for-the-superintelligence-century-with-jac/)).
- Helberg points to the DoD's deal with MP Materials as a template for rare earths and calls Meta's open-source work important for exporting the American stack ([Jacob Helberg](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/reshaping-america-s-economy-for-the-superintelligence-century-with-jac/)).
