## Overview

June on No Priors was mostly about how established companies put generative AI to work. The CEOs of Anduril, Datadog, Instacart and Snowflake each described where AI fits their business. A listener Q&A between Sarah Guo and Elad Gil added a sceptical view of early AI regulation and some thoughts on context windows.

## Main themes

### Autonomy and AI in defence

- Brian Schimpf says Anduril does no AI research of its own. It applies techniques that already work to defence problems, built on one software core (Lattice) that runs everything from border towers to a school-bus-sized autonomous submarine ([Anduril](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/ai-defense-technology-with-anduril-ceo-brian-schimpf/)).
- He says the US will not field robots that make lethal decisions on their own: “we assign accountability to the person that employed a weapon system”. He argues for a crawl-walk-run approach that keeps systems predictable ([Anduril](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/ai-defense-technology-with-anduril-ceo-brian-schimpf/)).
- Schimpf sees LLMs as useful for summarising intelligence, where hallucination is unacceptable, and for drafting mission plans that a human then edits ([Anduril](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/ai-defense-technology-with-anduril-ceo-brian-schimpf/)).
- On procurement, Gil says Anduril landed a program of record faster than any company since the Korean War. Schimpf describes demand that jumps from 10 units a year to 5,000 ([Anduril](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/ai-defense-technology-with-anduril-ceo-brian-schimpf/)).

### Regulation and the nuclear analogy

- Gil, who calls himself a long-term doomer, argues that industry calls for AI regulation are naive. He notes that no new nuclear reactor designs were approved for 50 years after the regulator was set up ([Listener Q&A](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/listener-q-a-ai-misconceptions-the-reality-of-regulation-infinite-cont/)).
- Guo adds that licensing costs mean the US builds none of the newer, safer reactor designs. Gil says the current debate lumps hate speech and bias together with existential risk ([Listener Q&A](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/listener-q-a-ai-misconceptions-the-reality-of-regulation-infinite-cont/)).
- Gil repeats that calls for regulation are premature in the Instacart conversation ([Instacart](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/what-s-for-dinner-ai-driven-commerce-with-instacart-ceo-fidji-simo/)).

### AI-written code and operations

- Olivier Pomel argues that engineers who become ten times more productive will understand less of what they ship. Value then moves to observing, securing and running software ([Datadog](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/what-happens-to-observability-if-code-is-ai-generated-the-potential-fo/)).
- Pomel says operations has little tolerance for error, because customers switch off alerts that wake them for nothing. A stack trace alone gets poor answers from GPT, but adding the program state gives precise ones ([Datadog](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/what-happens-to-observability-if-code-is-ai-generated-the-potential-fo/)).
- Guo expects context to grow to fill any window and says structuring what goes into context is the real product problem. Gil thinks window size will be reasonably solved within two years ([Listener Q&A](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/listener-q-a-ai-misconceptions-the-reality-of-regulation-infinite-cont/)).

### Commerce and enterprise data

- Fidji Simo says it is “very bizarre that we have forced human to train themselves to express their Commerce needs in the form of keywords”. Ask Instacart sits in the main search box ([Instacart](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/what-s-for-dinner-ai-driven-commerce-with-instacart-ceo-fidji-simo/)).
- Simo wants Instacart's 80,000-store network to turn intent into a delivery within two hours, as the layer between software and physical goods ([Instacart](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/what-s-for-dinner-ai-driven-commerce-with-instacart-ceo-fidji-simo/)).
- Frank Slootman says the premise of the data cloud is that “the work comes to the data”. He argues that enterprise AI value lies in governed, proprietary structured data, and that natural-language querying will hit BI dashboards hard ([Snowflake](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/06/bringing-ai-to-the-data-cloud-with-snowflake-s-ceo-frank-slootman/)).
