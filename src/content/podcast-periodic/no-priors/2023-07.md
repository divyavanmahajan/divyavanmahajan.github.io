## Overview

July had two episodes with little overlap. Meta's Devi Parikh explained why generative video is harder than images. Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar described how his company wraps LLMs in an ontology, evals and narrow agent permissions to make them usable in large organisations.

## Main themes

### Generative video and control

- Parikh explains that Make-A-Video learns appearance and language from image-text pairs and motion from unlabelled video, so it needs no paired video-text data ([Devi Parikh](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/the-timeline-for-realistic-4-d-devi-parikh-from-meta-on-research-hurdl/)).
- She says the output is still a four-second clip of one scene, “essentially an animated image”. She has seen no breakthrough since, unlike images, and lists costly infrastructure, weak representations, hierarchical architectures and data recipes as bottlenecks ([Devi Parikh](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/the-timeline-for-realistic-4-d-devi-parikh-from-meta-on-research-hurdl/)).
- Parikh calls text prompting an indirect form of control. She wants multimodal prompts and iterative editing, but expects control to lag behind core capability ([Devi Parikh](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/the-timeline-for-realistic-4-d-devi-parikh-from-meta-on-research-hurdl/)).
- She names multimodality as under-explored and calls audio under-invested. For robots, she says video understanding matters more than generation ([Devi Parikh](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/the-timeline-for-realistic-4-d-devi-parikh-from-meta-on-research-hurdl/)).

### LLMs in the enterprise, Palantir's way

- Sankar says the ontology “massively grounds your llm in your reality in your business context” and compresses what goes into the context window. Palantir invested heavily in LLMs from late 2022 for that reason ([Palantir](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/how-palantir-s-ai-bet-is-revolutionizing-defense-and-beyond-with-cto-s/)).
- He treats LLMs as a stochastic genie that needs a full toolchain. He wants to invest most in an eval environment and favours use cases where a wrong answer does no harm ([Palantir](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/how-palantir-s-ai-bet-is-revolutionizing-defense-and-beyond-with-cto-s/)).
- Sankar is sceptical of agents that make their own plans. He would give an agent authority over one state transition and expand it gradually, which he says turns individual contributors into managers of agents ([Palantir](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/how-palantir-s-ai-bet-is-revolutionizing-defense-and-beyond-with-cto-s/)).
- His examples go beyond chat: a Hawaii evacuation plan delivered as maps and resource matrices, and copilots that turn alert inboxes into staged solutions for humans to approve ([Palantir](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/how-palantir-s-ai-bet-is-revolutionizing-defense-and-beyond-with-cto-s/)).

### Building the company

- Sankar describes forward deployed engineering as one role that combines product management, customer success and engineering. He traces the idea to Alex Karp's French-restaurant analogy ([Palantir](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/how-palantir-s-ai-bet-is-revolutionizing-defense-and-beyond-with-cto-s/)).
- He calls data integration a boring and highly unsolved problem, and says healthcare is roughly a third of Palantir's business ([Palantir](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/how-palantir-s-ai-bet-is-revolutionizing-defense-and-beyond-with-cto-s/)).
