## Overview

November on No Priors opened with OpenAI's chief scientist and closed with the hosts reading OpenAI's board crisis. In between, Mistral's CEO argued for small open models and Imbue's founders framed agents as a reliability problem. Open versus closed research and what makes agents dependable ran through most of the month.

## Main themes

### OpenAI: mission, reliability and governance

- Ilya Sutskever says “The goal did not evolve over time. The tactic evolved over time.” A non-profit could not fund the compute, so OpenAI became capped-profit ([Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/what-is-digital-life-with-openai-co-founder-chief-scientist-ilya-sutsk/)).
- He argues reliability, not size, is the main bottleneck, and that the superalignment work must make future superintelligent systems prosocial. He defines digital life by autonomy rather than reproduction ([Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/what-is-digital-life-with-openai-co-founder-chief-scientist-ilya-sutsk/)).
- After the board crisis, Sarah Guo calls nonprofit governance as a class poor and says compute held by Microsoft and loyal staff decided the outcome. Elad Gil cites Charlie Munger on underestimating incentives ([Board crisis](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/open-ai-leadership-shuffle-new-diffusion-models-and-starting-the-cult/)).
- Gil expects enterprises to keep a second model vendor, while Guo says OpenAI still leads on capability such as code generation and GPT-4V ([Board crisis](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/open-ai-leadership-shuffle-new-diffusion-models-and-starting-the-cult/)).

### The case for open models

- Arthur Mensch says research moved fast while labs published openly until about 2020, and that the closed regime since then is bad for science ([Mistral](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/mistral-7b-and-the-open-source-revolution-with-arthur-mensch-ceo-mistr/)).
- He argues that banning open source “is really a way well to enforce regulatory capture”. He calls the 10^26 compute threshold arbitrary and wants capabilities measured instead ([Mistral](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/mistral-7b-and-the-open-source-revolution-with-arthur-mensch-ceo-mistr/)).
- Mensch disputes current misuse fears as unproven and traced through policy papers citing each other. Sutskever supports open models at today's capability but says highly autonomous models are a different case ([Mistral](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/mistral-7b-and-the-open-source-revolution-with-arthur-mensch-ceo-mistr/), [Ilya Sutskever](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/what-is-digital-life-with-openai-co-founder-chief-scientist-ilya-sutsk/)).
- On efficiency, Mensch says Chinchilla gave better models that were four times cheaper to serve, and Mistral 7B runs on a MacBook Pro ([Mistral](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/mistral-7b-and-the-open-source-revolution-with-arthur-mensch-ceo-mistr/)).

### Agents as a reliability problem

- Kanjun Qiu says founders building agents find “it's really hard to get these systems to work reliably”. Imbue treats Chain of Thought and similar methods as error correction ([Imbue](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/ai-agents-that-reason-and-code-with-imbue-co-founders-kanjun-qiu-and-j/)).
- Qiu places agents on two axes, specific to general and copilot to autonomous. The founders describe progress as moving from 60% to 70% to 80% reliable ([Imbue](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/ai-agents-that-reason-and-code-with-imbue-co-founders-kanjun-qiu-and-j/)).
- Josh Albrecht focuses on code because evaluations are objective, and argues that turning compute into good code removes engineering hiring as the bottleneck. Mensch adds that cheap inference matters for agents ([Imbue](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/ai-agents-that-reason-and-code-with-imbue-co-founders-kanjun-qiu-and-j/), [Mistral](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/mistral-7b-and-the-open-source-revolution-with-arthur-mensch-ceo-mistr/)).

### Diffusion models and creative markets

- Gil says founders swung from image generation to LLMs after ChatGPT, but those who stayed on diffusion, such as Pika, are now launching strong products. He expects more teams to train their own diffusion models ([Board crisis](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/open-ai-leadership-shuffle-new-diffusion-models-and-starting-the-cult/)).
- Guo says video generation is still early and data-intensive, leaving room for startups. Gil frames AI as turning low-margin services spend into software margin ([Board crisis](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/open-ai-leadership-shuffle-new-diffusion-models-and-starting-the-cult/)).
