## Overview

May mixed one research conversation with two company stories and a hosts' episode. OpenAI's Eric Mitchell and Brandon McKinzie explained what makes o3 work, Luis von Ahn and Arvind Jain described how AI changed Duolingo and Glean, and Sarah Guo and Elad Gil argued that several AI markets are settling on clear leaders.

## Main themes

### Reasoning models, RL and tools

- Mitchell and McKinzie say the main difference between o3 and the GPT series is reinforcement learning on hard tasks. Tools such as browsing, code execution and image cropping keep extra thinking time productive ([o3](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/o3-and-the-next-leap-in-reasoning-with-openai-s-eric-mitchell-and-bran/)).
- They want one steerable model; one of them hopes models will gain “a more precise understanding of their own level of uncertainty” so they answer instantly when they can ([o3](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/o3-and-the-next-leap-in-reasoning-with-openai-s-eric-mitchell-and-bran/)).
- Mitchell says tasks are hard when they need uncertain interaction with the world or are bound by real-world time, which is why coding agents move faster than robotics. Uncontaminated evals top their data wish list ([o3](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/o3-and-the-next-leap-in-reasoning-with-openai-s-eric-mitchell-and-bran/)).
- Guo says real-world RL needs environments rich enough to teach yet cheap to run, and that behaviour cloning breaks once agents go off-path. She doubts near-term commercial uses of world models ([AI Consolidation](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/ai-consolidation-biotech-opportunities-and-world-models-with-sarah-and/)).

### AI in consumer learning

- Luis von Ahn says motivation is the hardest problem in learning. Cutting lessons from 30 to 2 minutes was Duolingo's first big win, and exercises are pitched at about an 83% chance of success ([Duolingo](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/gaming-as-the-future-of-education-with-duolingo-ceo-luis-von-ahn/)).
- Duolingo rebuilt its content pipeline around LLMs, so it now teaches its 40 languages from every base language. For illustration, “What one artist could do before in like a month now they can do in like a day” ([Duolingo](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/gaming-as-the-future-of-education-with-duolingo-ceo-luis-von-ahn/)).
- He expects schools to use AI for individual attention within 20 years, but slowly, with private schools and some countries first ([Duolingo](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/gaming-as-the-future-of-education-with-duolingo-ceo-luis-von-ahn/)).

### Enterprise search becomes an AI foundation

- Arvind Jain calls the old enterprise search market “a graveyard like you know of all these companies that tried to solve the problem”. SaaS APIs, cloud scale and transformers made it solvable ([Glean](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/ai-is-making-enterprise-search-relevant-with-arvind-jain-of-glean/)).
- He doubts large context windows remove the need for retrieval, since freshness and authority still matter. Glean moved from search to an assistant to curated apps that customers call agents ([Glean](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/ai-is-making-enterprise-search-relevant-with-arvind-jain-of-glean/)).
- Good search exposed governance gaps, so many companies now buy Glean partly to fix permissions and become AI ready ([Glean](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/ai-is-making-enterprise-search-relevant-with-arvind-jain-of-glean/)).

### Markets consolidate

- Gil says LLMs, coding, customer support and medical scribing are settling on a few likely winners for the next two years, while sales, financial analysis and accounting stay open. He suggests leading startups consider merging ([AI Consolidation](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/ai-consolidation-biotech-opportunities-and-world-models-with-sarah-and/)).
- Guo notes OpenAI betting on both async cloud agents with Codex and the IDE through Windsurf, with o3 as the reasoning model behind its products ([AI Consolidation](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/ai-consolidation-biotech-opportunities-and-world-models-with-sarah-and/), [o3](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/o3-and-the-next-leap-in-reasoning-with-openai-s-eric-mitchell-and-bran/)).
- Gil argues biotech's funding model, built to sell companies into pharma pipelines, leaves large consumer-facing markets unaddressed ([AI Consolidation](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/ai-consolidation-biotech-opportunities-and-world-models-with-sarah-and/)).
