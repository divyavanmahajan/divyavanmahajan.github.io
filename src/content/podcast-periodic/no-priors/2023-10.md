## Overview

October on No Priors was about getting more out of the models that already exist. The hosts made the case for customisation and open source, a Google Cloud leader described how enterprises actually adopt generative AI, and two founders looked at AI through the lens of CRM and cyber security.

## Main themes

### Better AI from today's models

- Elad Gil lists six levers for more useful systems: multimodality, long context, model customisation, memory, recursion and small specialised models behind a router. He says these can make systems 10x to 100x better for some tasks ([Model Quality](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/model-quality-fine-tuning-meta-sponsoring-open-source-ecosystem/)).
- Sarah Guo sees trust as the core driver of retrieval-augmented generation, “citation control of information source”, with cost and freshness close behind ([Model Quality](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/model-quality-fine-tuning-meta-sponsoring-open-source-ecosystem/)).
- Gil points to ChatGPT, GPT-3.5 fine-tuned with RLHF, as proof that fine-tuning changes usefulness. Kawal Gandhi warns that jumping straight to fine-tuning is common, and that deployment and monitoring are where the serious work starts ([Model Quality](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/model-quality-fine-tuning-meta-sponsoring-open-source-ecosystem/), [Google Cloud](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/what-google-cloud-can-teach-enterprises-developing-rolling-out-ai-tool/)).

### Meta, open source and a new opening for social

- Guo compares Meta's sponsorship of Llama to Facebook hardening MySQL to avoid vendor dependence. Gil counters with IBM's spending on Linux as a counterweight to Microsoft ([Model Quality](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/model-quality-fine-tuning-meta-sponsoring-open-source-ecosystem/)).
- Guo expects Meta to use the models in its own data centres, ad serving and consumer products such as Instagram chatbots, not to sell them B2B ([Model Quality](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/model-quality-fine-tuning-meta-sponsoring-open-source-ecosystem/)).
- Gil notes TikTok was the last large new social network and sees generative AI as the first real opening since. Guo says generation could solve the cold start for content, as TikTok solved it for relevance ([Model Quality](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/model-quality-fine-tuning-meta-sponsoring-open-source-ecosystem/)).

### How enterprises adopt generative AI

- Gandhi describes adoption in stages, efficiency then productivity then creativity, with trust growing at each step. Early demand came from sales and marketing, then customer care and internal HR experiences ([Google Cloud](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/what-google-cloud-can-teach-enterprises-developing-rolling-out-ai-tool/)).
- He says success is “less about the technology it's more about like where they see themsel in the industry”, meaning ambition and investment ([Google Cloud](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/what-google-cloud-can-teach-enterprises-developing-rolling-out-ai-tool/)).
- Google dogfooded generative features in Workspace before Duet AI, keeps customer data and adapter weights in the customer's tenant, and reports more than 10,000 people certified on GCP generative AI ([Google Cloud](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/what-google-cloud-can-teach-enterprises-developing-rolling-out-ai-tool/)).

### AI in CRM and marketing

- Brian Halligan argues this is an unusual disruption where incumbents with years of customer data hold the advantage. HubSpot's ChatSpot uses OpenAI and Anthropic models ([HubSpot](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/from-app-to-suite-to-platform-with-hubspot-s-co-founder-brian-halligan/)).
- He expects buyers to ask chatbots instead of visiting websites, gated content to return, and support and SDR teams to become far more efficient. Mass-produced AI content, he says, will not work ([HubSpot](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/from-app-to-suite-to-platform-with-hubspot-s-co-founder-brian-halligan/)).

### Cyber security attack and defence

- Ryan Noon sees impersonation at scale as the main threat: one attacker with a for loop can replace a room of scammers. He says “intelligence is now a commodity that we can arms race” ([Material Security](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/ai-threats-opportunities-in-cyber-security-with-material-security-co-f/)).
- For defenders he recommends off-the-shelf models over fine-tuning to fake a moat. Material uses LLMs to filter noisy signals before analysts look at them ([Material Security](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/ai-threats-opportunities-in-cyber-security-with-material-security-co-f/)).
