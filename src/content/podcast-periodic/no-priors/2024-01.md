## Overview

January on No Priors began with a clip show from 2023 and then moved to applied AI in three settings: software development, warehouse robotics and ecommerce. The common thread was reliability. Each guest described how data, context or a human reviewer closes the gap between a good demo and dependable use.

## Main themes

### Context makes AI coding work

- Beyang Liu says Cody's edge is feeding the model context from Sourcegraph's code search and code graph. When that works, “the quality of code generated by Cody uh it's like night and day” ([Sourcegraph](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/coding-in-collaboration-with-ai-with-sourcegraph-cto-beyang-liu/)).
- Liu's long-term goal is issue-to-pull-request automation without human intervention, starting with toil such as dependency updates. Sarah Guo thinks the AI engineer will come sooner rather than later ([Sourcegraph](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/coding-in-collaboration-with-ai-with-sourcegraph-cto-beyang-liu/)).
- He warns that chained steps below 90% success decay towards zero, and that small models such as StarCoder 7B match larger ones for completions when given good context ([Sourcegraph](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/coding-in-collaboration-with-ai-with-sourcegraph-cto-beyang-liu/)).
- Liu sees coordination as the core problem of software. He expects AI to compress the boilerplate middle layer and CS fundamentals to matter more ([Sourcegraph](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/coding-in-collaboration-with-ai-with-sourcegraph-cto-beyang-liu/)).

### Robotics foundation models need real data

- Peter Chen says Covariant's one bet is that the future of robotics will be built by whoever has the most robotics data. Its robots in more than 10 countries all feed one shared model ([Covariant](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/building-the-factories-of-the-future-with-covariant-ceo-peter-chen/)).
- Internet data lacks sub-millimetre precision and action-outcome pairs, he says, so the only route is deploying robots customers already pay for. Warehouse customers often see more than 100% annual staff turnover ([Covariant](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/building-the-factories-of-the-future-with-covariant-ceo-peter-chen/)).
- Chen says robotics needs ChatGPT-like generality plus high reliability, since 70% success is not acceptable. He expects it first in industry, with one person supervising 10 to 30 robots ([Covariant](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/building-the-factories-of-the-future-with-covariant-ceo-peter-chen/)).

### Copilots that propose rather than commit

- Glen Coates says Shopify's AI suggests changes and the merchant commits them. That gives a three-part signal: accepted clean, accepted with edits, or rejected ([Shopify](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/the-copilot-for-ecommerce-with-shopify-vp-of-core-product-glen-coates/)).
- He imagines offering a full auto mode to merchants who accept suggestions unedited about 99% of the time ([Shopify](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/the-copilot-for-ecommerce-with-shopify-vp-of-core-product-glen-coates/)).
- Coates argues fixing core data models is what makes AI features useful. A standard taxonomy, with AI guessing category and attributes, makes products more discoverable on Google, Facebook and Amazon ([Shopify](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/the-copilot-for-ecommerce-with-shopify-vp-of-core-product-glen-coates/)).
- He says an agent like Sidekick reaches 75% quality quickly but is hard to push to 95% ([Shopify](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/the-copilot-for-ecommerce-with-shopify-vp-of-core-product-glen-coates/)).

### Looking back at 2023

- Ilya Sutskever explains OpenAI's capped-profit structure as a way to fund a large compute cluster while limiting investor returns ([Best of 2023](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/a-no-priors-clip-show-the-best-of-2023/)).
- Mustafa Suleyman says defining intelligence as generality has “over rotated the entire field on one aspect of of intelligence” and stresses directing attention to salient features ([Best of 2023](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/a-no-priors-clip-show-the-best-of-2023/)).
- Other clips cover Reid Hoffman on people plus AI, Arthur Mensch on modular guardrails, and Jensen Huang on Nvidia as two organisations side by side ([Best of 2023](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/a-no-priors-clip-show-the-best-of-2023/)).
