## Overview

February on No Priors leaned towards open source. Stability AI's Emad Mostaque and Hugging Face's Clem Delangue both argued that open, smaller and specialised models would win over a single giant model. The other three episodes covered game-playing AI and reasoning at Meta, AI video tools at Runway and autonomous medical delivery at Zipline.

## Main themes

### Open models as public infrastructure

- Mostaque argues that base models should be shared infrastructure while companies compete on instruction tuning and human-in-the-loop deployment. He says “Foundation models will all be open source for the Deep learning phase” ([Stability AI](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/how-can-we-make-sure-that-everyone-has-access-to-ai-can-small-models-o/)).
- He compares AI to 5G-level infrastructure that deserves national investment, and supports registering models above a compute threshold, dataset opt-outs and provenance metadata ([Stability AI](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/how-can-we-make-sure-that-everyone-has-access-to-ai-can-small-models-o/)).
- Delangue says open source is why machine learning moves from paper to production in weeks. He sees open and proprietary models trading the lead task by task, and backs opt-out datasets such as BigCode's ([Hugging Face](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/why-the-future-of-machine-learning-is-open-source-with-huggingface-s-c/)).
- Hugging Face runs a freemium model: about 15,000 companies use the platform and 3,000 pay, mostly for security, compliance and compute ([Hugging Face](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/why-the-future-of-machine-learning-is-open-source-with-huggingface-s-c/)).

### Small, specialised models

- Mostaque cites InstructGPT's 1.3B model and Flan-T5 beating the much larger GPT-3, and says distilled Stable Diffusion will run in a couple of seconds on an iPhone ([Stability AI](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/how-can-we-make-sure-that-everyone-has-access-to-ai-can-small-models-o/)).
- Delangue points to 250,000 models from almost 15,000 companies on the hub. He says specialised models are cheaper, faster and often more accurate for a given use case ([Hugging Face](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/why-the-future-of-machine-learning-is-open-source-with-huggingface-s-c/)).

### Reasoning and inference-time compute

- Noam Brown says adding search at play time was worth about 100,000x more scale for his poker bot. He calls inference-time compute a dimension people are not yet scaling ([Cicero](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/the-bot-cicero-can-collaborate-scheme-and-build-trust-with-humans-what/)).
- Brown's Cicero conditions its dialogue on a plan and played 40 games of Diplomacy without being detected as a bot. He calls it the first major game AI breakthrough involving cooperation ([Cicero](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/the-bot-cicero-can-collaborate-scheme-and-build-trust-with-humans-what/)).
- He sees reasoning as the main weakness of current bots and thinks a general solution could prove the Riemann hypothesis within five years ([Cicero](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/the-bot-cicero-can-collaborate-scheme-and-build-trust-with-humans-what/)).

### AI tools for video and creative work

- Cristobal Valenzuela describes Runway as an applied research company. It started as an app store of about 400 models and now offers around 35 "magic tools" ([Runway](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/what-does-ai-powered-content-creation-look-like-with-runway-ml-s-crist/)).
- He argues that 80% is enough in creative work: a director can try three ideas instead of one, and the artist stays at the centre ([Runway](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/what-does-ai-powered-content-creation-look-like-with-runway-ml-s-crist/)).

### Full-stack autonomy in the real world

- Keller Rinaudo Cliffton calls Zipline the largest commercial autonomous system on earth. He says the vehicle is “only like 15% of the complexity of the solution” ([Zipline](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/founder-stories-what-s-behind-the-largest-commercial-autonomous-system/)).
- Zipline's acoustic detect-and-avoid system uses cheap, light microphones and neural nets to identify nearby aircraft across 360 degrees ([Zipline](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/founder-stories-what-s-behind-the-largest-commercial-autonomous-system/)).
