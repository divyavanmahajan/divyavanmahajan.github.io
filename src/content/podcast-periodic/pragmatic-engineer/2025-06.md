## Overview

June on The Pragmatic Engineer was a month of developer-tool history told through people who shaped it. Scott Guthrie covered Microsoft's 50 years of tools, Thomas Dohmke traced GitHub up to Copilot, and Kent Beck revisited XP and TDD. All three ended up on AI coding agents and what they mean for engineering work.

## Main themes

### Coding agents: genie and coworker

- Kent Beck says agents “will not do what you mean” and compares them to a genie. One replaced his logic with a lookup table, and it came back after he deleted it ([Kent Beck](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/tdd-ai-agents-and-coding-with-kent-beck/)).
- Beck likens approving agent steps to a slot machine. He keeps tests that run in 300 milliseconds to catch the agent breaking things, and says vision and complexity control now matter more than syntax ([Kent Beck](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/tdd-ai-agents-and-coding-with-kent-beck/)).
- Scott Guthrie is most excited about assigning GitHub issues to Copilot and getting work back after 10 to 20 minutes ([Scott Guthrie](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/50-years-of-microsoft-and-developer-tools-with-scott-guthrie/)).
- Thomas Dohmke sees agents as another developer tool, like compilers. Engineers will break problems down and validate agent output ([GitHub](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/the-present-past-and-future-of-github/)).

### Microsoft as a developer tools company

- Guthrie says Microsoft was for many years just a developer tools company, starting with BASIC for the Altair, and that tools helped Windows win ([Scott Guthrie](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/50-years-of-microsoft-and-developer-tools-with-scott-guthrie/)).
- He describes .NET as filling the gap between Visual Basic and C++, unveiled at the PDC in July 2000 after slipping from an earlier date ([Scott Guthrie](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/50-years-of-microsoft-and-developer-tools-with-scott-guthrie/)).
- At a 2011 offsite many leaders could not sign up for Azure. A punch list led to a 2012 relaunch with Linux support, and Azure went from about seventh to second ([Scott Guthrie](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/50-years-of-microsoft-and-developer-tools-with-scott-guthrie/)).
- Guthrie says early hesitation about open source was about the licence business model. In 2014 his group chose a free Visual Studio edition, cross-platform open source .NET and VS Code, which he says built credibility for the GitHub acquisition ([Scott Guthrie](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/50-years-of-microsoft-and-developer-tools-with-scott-guthrie/)).

### GitHub and Copilot

- Dohmke says GitHub was bootstrapped for about five years and is still one of the largest Rails monoliths. It has about 3,000 employees and forecasts 12 million deploys this year ([GitHub](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/the-present-past-and-future-of-github/)).
- A 2020 paper tested text-to-code, code-to-text and conversational coding; only text-to-code worked well enough. Copilot's share of code in enabled files rose from about 25% to 46% by early 2023 ([GitHub](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/the-present-past-and-future-of-github/)).
- He attributes the quiet years of 2015 to 2020 to fear of breaking a beloved platform, and notes the 55% speed-up figure came from one controlled study ([GitHub](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/the-present-past-and-future-of-github/)).

### AI, jobs and junior engineers

- Guthrie says of AI that “in the limit, it's going to create more jobs, not less”, and recalls developers mocking IntelliSense in the early 2000s ([Scott Guthrie](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/50-years-of-microsoft-and-developer-tools-with-scott-guthrie/)).
- Dohmke calls not hiring juniors because of AI backwards, since young people adopt AI faster. He expects interviews to test prompting and Copilot skills ([GitHub](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/the-present-past-and-future-of-github/)).
- Beck expects organisations to generate many more experiments and says they must learn to reward discarding them ([Kent Beck](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/tdd-ai-agents-and-coding-with-kent-beck/)).
