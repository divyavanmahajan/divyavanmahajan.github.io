## Overview

In its first full year the show mixed company deep dives with craft and career episodes. AI coding tools became the main thread, moving from autocomplete to agents and vibe coding. Guests kept returning to code review, measurement and junior engineers. Engineering culture at Big Tech ran alongside all year.

## Main themes

### From autocomplete to coding agents

- Gautam Korlam says autocomplete lets you type less and think more, but tools focus too narrowly on completion or review ([Developer Experience at Uber](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/03/developer-experience-at-uber-with-gautam-korlam/)).
- Kent Beck calls agents an unpredictable genie that needs fast tests and human design judgement ([Kent Beck](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/tdd-ai-agents-and-coding-with-kent-beck/)); Thomas Dohmke sees them as tools directed by engineers ([GitHub](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/the-present-past-and-future-of-github/)).
- Varun Mohan says an AI IDE is won through evals, own models and fast inference ([Building Windsurf](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/05/building-windsurf-with-varun-mohan/)); Armin Ronacher says language choice still matters ([Armin Ronacher](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/10/python-go-rust-typescript-and-ai-with-armin-ronacher/)).

### Vibe coding, review and verification

- Korlam calls vibe coding fine for prototypes but unworkable at enterprise scale without strong constraints ([Developer Experience at Uber](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/03/developer-experience-at-uber-with-gautam-korlam/)).
- Steve Yegge says agents feel easy but are hard to steer ([Steve Yegge](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/07/amazon-google-and-vibe-coding-with-steve-yegge/)); Addy Osmani wants production work where the human plans and reviews every change ([Beyond Vibe Coding](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/10/beyond-vibe-coding-with-addy-osmani/)).
- Tomas Reimers says AI code makes human review matter more ([Stacked diffs](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/stacked-diffs-and-tooling-at-meta-with-tomas-reimers/)); Martin Fowler and Johannes Dahse stress testing and verification ([Martin Fowler](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/11/how-ai-will-change-software-engineering-with-martin-fowler/), [Code security](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/11/code-security-for-software-engineers/)).

### Measuring productivity and experience

- Nicole Forsgren rejects single metrics such as PR counts and warns: “It's real hard to build the AI things when your pipeline's trash” ([Nicole Forsgren](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/developer-productivity-with-dr-nicole-forsgren-creator-of-dora-co-crea/)).
- Laura Tacho measures AI through utilization, impact and cost against a developer-experience baseline ([Laura Tacho](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/07/measuring-the-impact-of-ai-on-software-engineering-with-laura-tacho/)).
- Uber's move to monorepos and Reddit's Core Stack rebuild were both run for developer experience ([Developer Experience at Uber](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/03/developer-experience-at-uber-with-gautam-korlam/), [Reddit](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/building-reddits-ios-and-android-app/)).

### Jobs and junior engineers

- Mohan says employers cutting engineers for AI efficiency are “being really, really short-sighted” ([Building Windsurf](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/05/building-windsurf-with-varun-mohan/)); Scott Guthrie says each productivity leap created more developer work ([Scott Guthrie](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/50-years-of-microsoft-and-developer-tools-with-scott-guthrie/)).
- Dohmke calls not hiring juniors because of AI backwards, and Shopify leans on AI-reflexive interns ([GitHub](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/06/the-present-past-and-future-of-github/), [Shopify](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/07/how-ai-is-changing-software-engineering-at-shopify-with-farhan-thawar/)).

### Big Tech engineering culture

- Michael Novati says “internal tools were products” at Facebook ([The Coding Machine](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/01/the-coding-machine-at-meta-with-michael-novati/)).
- Dave Anderson says Amazon teams own their stack and oncall; Steve Huynh calls senior to principal a two-and-a-half level jump ([Dave Anderson](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/working-at-amazon-as-a-software-engineer-with-dave-anderson/), [Steve Huynh](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/07/what-is-a-principal-engineer-at-amazon-with-steve-huynh/)).
- Google's culture came from early scale and changed with layoffs ([Google](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/10/googles-engineering-culture/)); Elizabeth Stone credits talent density at Netflix ([Netflix](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/11/netflixs-engineering-culture/)).
