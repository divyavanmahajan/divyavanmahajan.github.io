## Overview

November had two episodes, both with former Uber engineers comparing Uber with a company that works very differently. Irina Stanescu set Google's design docs, readability reviews and promo committees against Uber's move-fast culture. Sabin Roman explained how Linear ships with principles instead of process.

## Main themes

### Promotions and influence

- Irina Stanescu explains that Google promo committees, drawn from across the company, judged packets of self, manager and peer assessments. Google designed it this way to decouple promotion from the manager relationship ([Google](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/promotions-and-tooling-at-google-with-irina-stanescu-ex-google/)).
- Her first promotion attempt at Google Fiber was rejected for lack of impact. She concluded that getting promoted meant “to show you're operating at the next level” ([Google](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/promotions-and-tooling-at-google-with-irina-stanescu-ex-google/)).
- Stanescu and Gergely Orosz recall Uber copying the committee model in 2018, then moving to a lighter process when peer reviews broke down as people left. At Linear, Roman says there are no titles or levels ([Google](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/promotions-and-tooling-at-google-with-irina-stanescu-ex-google/), [Linear](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/linear-move-fast-with-little-process-with-first-engineering-manager-sa/)).
- Stanescu separates influencing from having influence. She says a common mistake is trying to influence before building credibility, visibility or a track record ([Google](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/promotions-and-tooling-at-google-with-irina-stanescu-ex-google/)).

### Google's engineering culture, seen from Uber

- Stanescu says design docs were mandatory at Google around 2012-2013, covering goals, timelines, risks and a launch plan. At early Uber, colleagues were surprised she wrote one at all ([Google](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/promotions-and-tooling-at-google-with-irina-stanescu-ex-google/)).
- Every Google change needed a reviewer with readability certification in that language, and every launch went through SRE and production readiness review ([Google](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/promotions-and-tooling-at-google-with-irina-stanescu-ex-google/)).
- She says Uber's internal tools were not at Google's level, and ex-Googler colleagues wrote a document translating one set of tools into the other ([Google](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/promotions-and-tooling-at-google-with-irina-stanescu-ex-google/)).

### Linear: little process, high trust

- Roman describes Linear as preferring creativity over process and principles over guidebooks, while keeping clear steps for incident response. He credits driven, product-minded hires whose judgment can be relied on ([Linear](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/linear-move-fast-with-little-process-with-first-engineering-manager-sa/)).
- Two engineers a week act as goalies, triaging customer reports under a zero-bug policy with an official SLA of a week and an unofficial one of a day ([Linear](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/linear-move-fast-with-little-process-with-first-engineering-manager-sa/)).
- Hiring uses simplified pieces of real Linear code and a one-week paid work trial. Some candidates shipped their trial project as their first project after joining ([Linear](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/linear-move-fast-with-little-process-with-first-engineering-manager-sa/)).
- Projects run as “talk, do, show, talk again” cycles behind feature flags, then get de-scoped to the core value proposition. The project page rework took about four months ([Linear](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/linear-move-fast-with-little-process-with-first-engineering-manager-sa/)).

### Remote work

- Roman sees focus and autonomy as the gains of Linear's fully remote setup, with no internal email. Weekly demos and two or three offsites a year counter weaker team belonging. He admits remote is harder for managers ([Linear](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/linear-move-fast-with-little-process-with-first-engineering-manager-sa/)).
