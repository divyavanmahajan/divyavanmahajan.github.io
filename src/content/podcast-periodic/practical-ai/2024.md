## Overview

Practical AI spent 2024 on the engineering that turns generative AI into products. RAG became the default first build, alongside prompting, fine-tuning, evaluation and data quality. Policy ran alongside, from Congress to the EU AI Act. Multimodal models and Kyutai's real-time voice assistant marked the model side.

## Main themes

### RAG becomes the default build

- Daniel Whitenack says RAG systems are “the first thing that everybody is building” among Prediction Guard's clients ([Advent of GenAI Hackathon recap](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/01/advent-of-genai-hackathon-recap/)).
- Demetrios Brinkmann's MLOps Community survey shows RAG overtaking fine-tuning, with evaluation still manual and slow ([RAG continues to rise](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/rag-continues-to-rise/)).
- Chris Benson warns many organisations stop at naive RAG ([Apple Intelligence & Advanced RAG](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/06/apple-intelligence-advanced-rag/)); later guests cover vector databases and GraphRAG ([Pinecone](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/07/vectoring-in-on-pinecone/), [GraphRAG](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/09/graphrag-beyond-the-hype/)).

### Prompting, fine-tuning and evaluation

- Raza Habib says “people should first push the limits of prompt engineering” before fine-tuning, which now mainly serves cost, latency and tone ([Collaboration & evaluation for LLM apps](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/01/collaboration-evaluation-for-llm-apps/)).
- Jared Zoneraich treats prompt engineering as a discipline needing registries, eval datasets and logging ([Prompting the future](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/03/prompting-the-future/)).
- Shahul Es argues LLM apps need their own metrics, separate from model benchmarks ([Metrics Driven Development](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/08/metrics-driven-development/)).

### Data quality, synthetic data and open models

- Karan Malhotra explains how Nous Research used synthetic data from larger models to make small open models competitive ([Data synthesis for SOTA LLMs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/02/data-synthesis-for-sota-llms/)).
- Hugging Face's Argilla team says data quality comes from domain experts and engineers working together, with humans checking AI-generated data ([Towards high-quality datasets](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/10/towards-high-quality-maybe-synthetic-datasets/)).
- The hosts note the narrowing gap between open and closed models ([Clones, commerce & campaigns](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/11/clones-commerce-campaigns/)).

### AI policy and regulation

- Rep. Don Beyer explains how Congress sorts hundreds of AI bills and says it “moves glacially” ([AI in the U.S. Congress](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/05/ai-in-the-u-s-congress/)).
- Stanford's AI Index shows a jump in US state AI laws ([Stanford's AI Index Report 2024](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/07/stanford-s-ai-index-report-2024/)); the hosts review the EU AI Act's risk tiers ([Only as good as the data](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/08/only-as-good-as-the-data/)).
- NIST's Elham Tabassi explains the voluntary AI Risk Management Framework ([The path towards trustworthy AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/10/the-path-towards-trustworthy-ai/)).

### Multimodal and real-time voice

- The hosts see 2024 as the year multimodal input and output took off, prompted by Udio ([Udio & the age of multi-modal AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/udio-the-age-of-multi-modal-ai/)) and GPT-4o's voice interface ([First impressions of GPT-4o](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/05/first-impressions-of-gpt-4o/)).
- Kyutai, a small non-profit lab, released a real-time voice assistant before OpenAI ([The first real-time voice assistant](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/07/the-first-real-time-voice-assistant/)); Alexandre Défossez later explained Moshi ([Kyutai](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/12/full-duplex-real-time-dialogue-with-kyutai/)).
