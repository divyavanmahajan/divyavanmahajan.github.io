## Overview

April on Practical AI was a month of applied AI inside four companies. Graphcore explained how it builds a machine learning chip, CARMERA described maps for autonomous driving, Intuit talked about forecasting for small businesses and PolyAI about voice assistants for customer service. A common thread was building for real-world constraints rather than benchmarks.

## Main themes

### AI chips and their software stack

- Dave Lacey describes CPUs as scalar processors and GPUs as processors for large vectors. He calls Graphcore's IPU a graph processor, highly parallel but able to handle data that does not come in contiguous blocks ([Graphcore](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/going-full-bore-with-graphcore/)).
- Lacey says co-design means designing “your software stack and your hardware architecture at the same time”. He warns against hard-wiring a model such as ResNet-50 into silicon, because a chip must last years ([Graphcore](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/going-full-bore-with-graphcore/)).
- Graphcore now has more software than hardware engineers. Lacey estimates about 90% of users work at the PyTorch or TensorFlow level and recommends some sympathy with the hardware, such as batch sizes and matrix shapes ([Graphcore](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/going-full-bore-with-graphcore/)).

### Maps that keep up with the road

- Ro Gupta explains that consumer SD maps can be off by tens of meters, while HD maps aim for tens of centimeters and hundreds of features per intersection ([Mapping the world](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/mapping-the-world/)).
- He calls change management “the biggest unsolved problem really in mapping for all levels of fidelity”. CARMERA keeps change data at medium definition and upgrades to HD only where needed ([Mapping the world](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/mapping-the-world/)).
- CARMERA sources data from dash cams on delivery fleets, which it calls pro-sourcing. Gupta's rule of thumb is a 100x gain in both ubiquity and freshness ([Mapping the world](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/mapping-the-world/)).
- Gupta frames maps as priors and draws on the Yann LeCun and Gary Marcus debate about pure learning versus built-in structure ([Mapping the world](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/mapping-the-world/)).

### Forecasting and trust in financial products

- Nhung Ho describes Intuit's Design for Delight process of customer interviews and quick MVPs. Showing customers predictions on their own data produced far richer feedback ([Intuit](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/data-science-for-intuitive-user-experiences/)).
- Customers asked for invoice reminders. Ho's team instead offered a predicted date range for when each invoice would be paid ([Intuit](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/data-science-for-intuitive-user-experiences/)).
- When lockdowns began, some small businesses saw revenue drop 99%, which made historical cash flow data wrong. Intuit reworked its models and built an aid assist tool for PPP eligibility ([Intuit](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/data-science-for-intuitive-user-experiences/)).
- Deep learning only recently beat traditional time series methods for her team, but customer-facing money decisions stay with explainable models to build trust ([Intuit](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/data-science-for-intuitive-user-experiences/)).

### Voice assistants for customer service

- Nikola Mrkšić says 60 to 70% of customer service interactions happen by phone, and call volumes rose during COVID ([Voice assistants](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/next-gen-voice-assistants/)).
- He describes PolyAI's ConveRT as “an encoding model for dialogues” rather than a language model, pre-trained on conversations from Reddit, Quora and Twitter ([Voice assistants](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/next-gen-voice-assistants/)).
- One unified multilingual model sits at the core of the system and is retrained to add each new language ([Voice assistants](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/next-gen-voice-assistants/)).

- [Data science for intuitive user experiences](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/data-science-for-intuitive-user-experiences/): candid on how the pandemic broke forecasting models and how Intuit responded.
- [Next-gen voice assistants](/podcasts/practical-ai/2021/04/next-gen-voice-assistants/): explains why narrow, task-oriented dialogue can be measured and improved.
