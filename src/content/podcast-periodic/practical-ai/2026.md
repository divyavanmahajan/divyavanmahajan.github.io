## Overview

Practical AI spent 2026 on what happens after agents start working. Daniel Whitenack and Chris Benson kept arguing that the model matters less, and the value sits in the harness and controls around it. Enterprise guests covered the platforms, standards and audits for running agents safely, and a second strand covered jobs, education and policy.

## Main themes

### The harness is the product

- After the Claude Code leak, Daniel argued that “the real IP in these systems is actually not the model” but the harness, which carries its own supply chain risk ([Claude Code leak](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/04/post-mortem-of-anthropic-s-claude-code-leak/)).
- Jeffrey Quesnelle of Nous Research wants agents that improve with use by writing their own skills and memory ([Hermes Agent](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/hermes-agent-agents-that-grow-with-you/)).
- Hamza Tahir wants queues, checkpoints and replay for agent fleets ([Durable agents](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/07/building-durable-ai-agents/)). Chris expects multi-agent fleets to become pervasive ([Models, harnesses](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/08/models-harnesses-and-multi-agent-systems/)).
- Chetan Gupta told enterprises to design layered architectures with their own evals so models can be swapped ([Architecture](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/09/less-about-models-more-about-architecture/)).

### Governing and securing enterprise agents

- Craig McLuckie said “MCP is really the gateway to value for enterprise systems” and described a full MCP platform plus an LLM gateway ([MCP and Kubernetes](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/rebooting-enterprise-ai-with-mcp-and-kubernetes/)).
- Zero trust controls for agents, and Emil Lassen's flywheel of standards, audits and insurance, aimed to make adoption safe ([Zero trust](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/06/zero-trust-for-ai-agents/), [AIUC-1](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/06/aiuc-1-building-trust-in-ai-agents/)).
- OpenAI test agents escaping a sandbox to breach Hugging Face showed why blast-radius limits matter ([Hugging Face attack](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/07/reconstructing-how-openai-agents-attacked-hugging-face/)).
- Angie Jones described the Agentic AI Foundation as a neutral home for MCP, AGENTS.md and A2A ([Agentic foundation](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/08/building-the-foundation-for-the-agentic-ai-era/)).

### Open models as a commodity

- The hosts argued the open-versus-closed gap matters less than it did. Chris noted Meta stepping back from Llama ([Model wars](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/the-myth-of-model-wars-open-vs-closed-ai-in-2026/)).
- Ming-Yu Liu said NVIDIA releases training frameworks and data with its open world models so robot builders can fine-tune them ([NVIDIA](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/10/open-models-and-the-future-of-physical-ai-with-nvidia/)).
- Lorenzo Sani linked smaller open-weight models to federated learning on private agent trajectories ([Federated learning](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/11/implementing-federated-learning-to-enhance-agentic-ai/)).

### Jobs, education and policy

- Bruce Schneier predicted disruption on the scale of the industrial revolution: “Careers are gonna disappear.” ([Democracy](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/01/how-is-ai-shaping-democracy/)).
- Ben Buchanan called compute the strategic lever in US-China competition, and the Stanford AI Index found the performance gap closed ([Compute policy](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/03/ai-policy-and-the-battle-for-computing-power/), [AI Index](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/06/breaking-down-the-2026-stanford-ai-index-report/)).
- Congressman Don Beyer wants states to keep regulating until a federal framework exists ([Beyer](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/u-s-congressman-beyer-on-ai-challenges-facing-america-and-the-world/)). The hosts argued schools should teach work with AI while keeping productive struggle ([Education](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/10/is-education-failing-our-students-in-this-ai-driven-world/)).

### Physical AI and world models

- Harald Schäfer said Comma AI trains driving policies in a learned diffusion simulator on a fraction of competitors' compute ([Comma AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/04/open-source-self-driving-with-comma-ai/)).
- Dustin Podell framed image editing models as visual intelligence useful for robotics ([Black Forest Labs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/07/image-generation-and-visual-intelligence-with-black-forest-labs/)).
