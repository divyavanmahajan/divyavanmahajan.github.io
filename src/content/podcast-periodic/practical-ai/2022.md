## Overview

Practical AI spent 2022 on the plumbing of applied machine learning: open datasets, open model hubs and getting models into production. An AI in Africa series put low-resource languages and local data at the centre. In the second half DALL-E 2 and Stable Diffusion raised questions about licensing, ownership and responsible release.

## Main themes

### Open data for low-resource and African languages

- David Kanter argues large, permissively licensed speech datasets help everyone, and that “more open data means more ML, means more people doing cool things with computers, means more sales” ([Democratizing ML for speech](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/01/democratizing-ml-for-speech/)).
- Kathleen Siminyu and Joyce Nabende describe how Masakhane and Common Voice let communities build datasets for languages such as Kiswahili and Luganda ([AI in Africa - Voice & language tools](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/02/ai-in-africa-voice-language-tools/)).
- Farm AI in Africa works only on locally collected open data and outputs designed for farmers ([AI in Africa - Agriculture](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/04/ai-in-africa-agriculture/)), while Sarah Moeller uses active learning to speed up linguists documenting endangered languages ([Active learning & endangered languages](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/05/active-learning-endangered-languages/)).

### Hugging Face and the open model ecosystem

- Thomas Simonini describes Hugging Face as “the GitHub for the AI models” ([Exploring deep reinforcement learning](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/02/exploring-deep-reinforcement-learning/)).
- Merve Noyan presents the Hub as the core of open-source ML ([The AI community building the future](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/05/the-ai-community-building-the-future/)); Gradio turns models into shareable demos ([Quick, beautiful web UIs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/04/quick-beautiful-web-uis-for-ml-apps/)).
- Coqui open-sourced voice cloning ([Cloning voices with Coqui](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/07/cloning-voices-with-coqui/)).

### MLOps and shipping models like software

- Greeneye and ClearML took model deployments to tractors from about ten a year to thousands through automation, with a human decision kept in the loop ([Deploying models to tractors](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/03/deploying-models-to-tractors/)).
- OctoML's Luis Ceze argues a trained model is ordinary software, so post-training work belongs to DevOps ([MLOps is NOT Real](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/04/mlops-is-not-real/)); the hosts make a similar case ([CMU's AI pilot](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/08/cmu-s-ai-pilot-lands-in-the-news/)).

### Generative models go open

- The hosts read DALL-E 2's coherence as clever recombination, not sentience ([DALL-E is one giant leap for raccoons](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/07/dall-e-is-one-giant-leap-for-raccoons/)).
- Stable Diffusion spread because it was openly released and ran on consumer GPUs ([Stable Diffusion](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/09/stable-diffusion/)); Daniel Whitenack credits openness and community for its adoption ([Licensing & automating creativity](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/09/licensing-automating-creativity/)).
- Hour One argues consented virtual humans turn deepfakes into a business video tool ([Positive uses for deep fakes](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/08/practical-positive-uses-for-deep-fakes/)).

### Data ownership, privacy and responsible release

- Mozilla's guests tie AI harms to concentrated power ([AI IRL](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/08/ai-irl-mozilla-s-internet-health-report/)), and John K. Thompson says “we own the data. And number two, they should pay for it” ([Data for All](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/10/data-for-all/)).
- The hosts argue practitioners must minimise and de-identify data themselves ([Privacy in the age of AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/08/privacy-in-the-age-of-ai/)).
- Whitenack wrote a responsible AI licence for his team's models ([Practicalities of releasing models](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/11/the-practicalities-of-releasing-models/)); the Copilot class action then exposed licensing gaps ([Copilot lawsuits](/podcasts/practical-ai/2022/11/copilot-lawsuits-galactica-science/)).
