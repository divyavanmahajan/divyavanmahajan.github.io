## Overview

November on Practical AI ranged from production infrastructure to culture. Interviews from O'Reilly AI covered Uber's Michelangelo platform and Intel's work on edge models, while a news episode unpacked Google's BERT and Facebook's Horizon. The other two episodes looked at how people enter data science and how artists can explain AI's effects on society.

## Main themes

### ML platforms and production at scale

- Mike Del Balso says all of Uber's main ML, from fraud to arrival-time prediction to Uber Eats dish ranking, runs through Michelangelo, with well over 100 use cases ([Uber and Intel](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/uber-and-intel-s-machine-learning-platforms/)).
- The feature store was the most adopted part. It applies the same transforms offline for training and in real time for serving, and lets teams reuse each other's features ([Uber and Intel](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/uber-and-intel-s-machine-learning-platforms/)).
- Del Balso argues practices such as reproducibility and versioning are less established in data science than CI/CD is in software, and Michelangelo monitors accuracy and model drift ([Uber and Intel](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/uber-and-intel-s-machine-learning-platforms/)).

### Making models fit the edge

- Cormac Brick of Intel's Movidius group says the Myriad 2 chip runs in smart cameras, robots, Google Clips and DJI drones ([Uber and Intel](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/uber-and-intel-s-machine-learning-platforms/)).
- He names four portable techniques: channel pruning, sparsification, 8-bit quantization and lower-bit weights. A ResNet 50 retrained for 100 household objects can be much smaller ([Uber and Intel](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/uber-and-intel-s-machine-learning-platforms/)).
- Brick admits reproducing the results is still “pretty non-trivial” and points to Intel's open source Distiller project ([Uber and Intel](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/uber-and-intel-s-machine-learning-platforms/)).

### BERT and applied research from the big labs

- Daniel Whitenack explains BERT as a pre-trained transformer encoder trained on masked word prediction and next sentence prediction ([BERT](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/bert-one-nlp-model-to-rule-them-all/)).
- Pre-training takes four days on Cloud TPUs, about $7,000 by Whitenack's estimate, but Google released the models so teams only fine-tune ([BERT](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/bert-one-nlp-model-to-rule-them-all/)).
- Chris Benson describes Facebook's Horizon, an open-source RL platform built on PyTorch and Caffe2 and used in Messenger and for 360-degree video ([BERT](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/bert-one-nlp-model-to-rule-them-all/)).

### AI, bias and art

- Brett Gaylor of Mozilla says automated decision systems often “just reinforce previous inequalities”, citing ProPublica's work on reoffending risk scores ([Art and film](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/analyzing-ai-s-impact-on-society-through-art-and-film/)).
- He argues the public must understand AI issues before fixes for bias gain traction, and describes Mozilla's Creative Media Award winners on hiring, dating apps and censorship ([Art and film](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/analyzing-ai-s-impact-on-society-through-art-and-film/)).
- Benson reports a GAN-generated portrait sold at Christie's for $432,500, about 45 times its estimate ([BERT](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/bert-one-nlp-model-to-rule-them-all/)).

### Getting into data science

- Himani Agrawal of AT&T says scientists and engineers already have many of the skills and mostly lack industry tools and jargon ([Getting into data science](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/getting-into-data-science-and-ai/)).
- She says “There is a huge scarcity of machine learning and data science expertise” and recommends bootcamps and communities such as Women in Machine Learning ([Getting into data science](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/getting-into-data-science-and-ai/)).
- At AT&T she uses streaming alarm data to find where outages start, and weather, flood and power data to time repairs after hurricanes ([Getting into data science](/podcasts/practical-ai/2018/11/getting-into-data-science-and-ai/)).
