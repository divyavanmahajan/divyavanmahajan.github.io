## Overview

March on Practical AI covered four separate subjects with no shared thread. One researcher argued that AI safety starts with recommender systems, the hosts reviewed the new US executive order on AI, Hugging Face made the case for social chatbots, and Anima Anandkumar explained why deep learning should not start from scratch.

## Main themes

### AI safety means robust learning today

- El Mahdi El Mhamdi of EPFL says AI safety is not mainly about future killer robots. He argues the real ones are already here, “and they are called recommender systems” ([AI safety research](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/staving-off-disaster-through-ai-safety-research/)).
- He explains that poisoning a recommender needs no hacking: a small, hyperactive group only has to like, comment and post. Because gradient descent averages gradients, that minority can skew the model ([AI safety research](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/staving-off-disaster-through-ai-safety-research/)).
- His group proposes median-like, Byzantine-resilient aggregation, with a NeurIPS 2017 paper, ICML 2018 follow-ups and a modified TensorFlow called AggregaThor. He also notes impossibility results when too many inputs are malicious, and is exploring a PageRank-style approach that follows expert sources ([AI safety research](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/staving-off-disaster-through-ai-safety-research/)).

### A national AI strategy without new money

- Daniel Whitenack lists the order's five areas: R&D, access to federal data and compute, standards for safe AI, workforce training and international engagement. He notes the order “doesn't actually allocate any federal funding towards executing this vision” ([Executive Order](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/the-white-house-executive-order-on-ai/)).
- Agencies are asked to reallocate existing budgets. Whitenack contrasts this with China's stated $150 billion AI spend through 2030, and Chris Benson wanted a Kennedy-style moonshot ([Executive Order](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/the-white-house-executive-order-on-ai/)).
- Both hosts say visa problems push foreign AI PhD graduates out of the US. Benson adds that the Department of Defense, through DARPA and the Joint AI Center, had already moved further than the White House ([Executive Order](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/the-white-house-executive-order-on-ai/)).

### Conversational AI that is social, not transactional

- Clément Delangue argues people want AI that is entertaining and emotional, unlike Siri or Alexa. Hugging Face had passed half a billion messages and more than half a million love declarations from users ([Hugging Face](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/social-ai-with-hugging-face/)).
- Each message runs through several models in parallel, from simple intent models to end-to-end generation, with a one-second response threshold and fallback to faster models ([Hugging Face](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/social-ai-with-hugging-face/)).
- Delangue calls the company's PyTorch repository of BERT and GPT-2 the largest open source collection of pre-trained transfer learning models in NLP. He predicts language understanding will be largely solved within three years ([Hugging Face](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/social-ai-with-hugging-face/)).

### Prior knowledge plus learning from data

- Anima Anandkumar, of NVIDIA and Caltech, describes using tensors to extract topics, work that later shipped in AWS Comprehend, and argues networks should keep multidimensional structure ([Anima Anandkumar](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/growing-up-to-become-a-world-class-ai-expert/)).
- Her team learned the ground effect for drone landing as a residual on an existing controller with Lyapunov stability guarantees, giving fast but smooth landings where a plain network crashed. She says the lesson is not to apply deep learning blindly ([Anima Anandkumar](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/growing-up-to-become-a-world-class-ai-expert/)).
- She also explains why she spoke out about the old NIPS name as the conference grew ([Anima Anandkumar](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/growing-up-to-become-a-world-class-ai-expert/)).
