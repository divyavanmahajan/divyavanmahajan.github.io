## Overview

November on Practical AI was dominated by governance. Daniel Whitenack and Chris Benson read through the White House executive order on AI, then closed the month with a retrospective on Sam Altman's firing and return at OpenAI. In between, Mozilla's Solana Larsen made the case for people-first AI, and Shopify's Russ Maschmeyer showed how to build product imagery from off-the-shelf open models.

## Main themes

### Regulation arrives

- Benson explains that the October 30 executive order acts as law and was more detailed than he expected, but it assigns tasks and deadlines without saying how they will be done or paid for ([Government regulation](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/government-regulation-of-ai-has-arrived/)).
- The order sets reporting thresholds on training compute, with a lower line for biological sequence models. Whitenack asks where fine-tuning ends and building a new model begins, and predicts teams will train just under the line ([Government regulation](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/government-regulation-of-ai-has-arrived/)).
- Benson notes the irony that an order aimed at equity adds a burden that favours large players, and expects litigation and new compliance businesses. He says “There are huge business opportunities in helping people get through regulatory.” ([Government regulation](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/government-regulation-of-ai-has-arrived/)).
- On labelling AI content, Whitenack says detection is still an open research problem, especially when humans and models edit text together. Benson calls the Bletchley Declaration short, non-binding and worth supporting ([Government regulation](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/government-regulation-of-ai-has-arrived/)).

### OpenAI's structure and single-supplier risk

- Benson describes OpenAI Inc., a nonprofit, controlling a for-profit LLC with returns capped at 100 times investment. Board members could not hold a financial stake, so Microsoft, with more than $10 billion invested, had no seat ([OpenAI debacle](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/the-openai-debacle-a-retrospective/)).
- The hosts walk through the week: Altman fired a day after Dev Day, Microsoft told a minute before the world, the Microsoft job offer, the staff revolt and a new board chaired by Bret Taylor ([OpenAI debacle](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/the-openai-debacle-a-retrospective/)).
- Whitenack spent the week answering Prediction Guard customers reconsidering their dependence on OpenAI. He says “the resilience of relying on this kind of single family of models, I think, really was showing in that moment” ([OpenAI debacle](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/the-openai-debacle-a-retrospective/)).
- Whitenack also suggests the crisis, together with Meta disbanding its responsible AI team, tells regulators that self-regulation may not hold up ([OpenAI debacle](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/the-openai-debacle-a-retrospective/)).

### People over profit

- Larsen says openness is only good when it serves a purpose such as transparency or accountability, and warns it can also be used to consolidate a market ([AI trailblazers](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/ai-trailblazers-putting-people-first/)).
- She contrasts badly paid content moderators in Kenya with Karya in India, which pays voice donors again each time it resells a dataset ([AI trailblazers](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/ai-trailblazers-putting-people-first/)).
- Other examples include an indigenous data sovereignty licence for Māori voice data and Credo AI's governance dashboards ([AI trailblazers](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/ai-trailblazers-putting-people-first/)).

### Grounded product imagery

- Maschmeyer says the goal was to keep every product pixel unchanged while regenerating the scene around it. Earlier tools lacked shadows and matching camera angles ([Shopify](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/generating-product-imagery-at-shopify/)).
- Shopify's fix feeds Stable Diffusion XL a masked depth map plus a small bloom of depth at the product's base, then composites the original pixels back, with no model training ([Shopify](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/generating-product-imagery-at-shopify/)).
