## Overview

March on Practical AI split between the people side of data science and applied machine learning in specific domains. Two episodes asked what data science actually is and how to get a job in it. The others covered enterprise ML tooling at Google Cloud, the components of commercial speech recognition and deep learning on geospatial data at Esri.

## Main themes

### Defining data science and getting into it

- Matt Brems of General Assembly defines data science broadly: “using data to make a more informed decision than if you were to not use that data.” He counts analysis in Excel as data science ([What is data science](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/what-exactly-is-data-science-these-days/)).
- Brems says most project time goes on gathering and cleaning data, that regression often solves the problem, and that SQL is table stakes. He names deepfakes as the biggest coming challenge ([What is data science](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/what-exactly-is-data-science-these-days/)).
- Emily Robinson, co-author of Build a Career in Data Science, says the field has no well-trodden path, so interviews range from no coding to take-home projects ([Career in Data Science](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/building-a-career-in-data-science/)).
- Robinson says the job market is good for people with a year of experience but harder for bootcamp graduates. She recommends an original portfolio, a scannable one-page resume and learning before trying to fix things in a new job ([Career in Data Science](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/building-a-career-in-data-science/)).

### Enterprise ML and build vs buy

- Craig Wiley of Google Cloud AI Platform argues the goal of ML tooling is shorter cycle time from data to production, and that models only count once their inferences create value ([TensorFlow in the cloud](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/tensorflow-in-the-cloud/)).
- Wiley says buying AI makes sense “if it's something that you're very comfortable with your competitor buying as well.” Contact centres are a sensible buy; retail supply chains are worth building ([TensorFlow in the cloud](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/tensorflow-in-the-cloud/)).
- TensorFlow Enterprise adds two more years of patches on top of the open source support window. Wiley sees data silos as a policy problem as much as a technical one ([TensorFlow in the cloud](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/tensorflow-in-the-cloud/)).

### How speech recognition works

- Catherine Breslin of Cobalt explains the hybrid pipeline of lexicon, acoustic model and language model, and credits the move from Gaussian mixture models to neural networks for large accuracy gains ([Speech recognition](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/speech-recognition-to-say-it-just-right/)).
- She says end-to-end models are a major research topic but, to her knowledge, on par with or slightly behind hybrid systems ([Speech recognition](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/speech-recognition-to-say-it-just-right/)).
- Transfer learning to a new language needs roughly 100 to 1,000 hours of audio. Breslin wants voice interfaces to reach people who do not read and write and elderly users ([Speech recognition](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/speech-recognition-to-say-it-just-right/)).

### Deep learning meets GIS

- Daniel Wilson and Rob Fletcher of Esri describe automating extraction of building footprints and roads from imagery with convolutional networks, then feeding the output into ArcGIS for further analysis ([AI and GIS](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/mapping-the-intersection-of-ai-and-gis/)).
- Fletcher describes work for the DoD Joint AI Center detecting roads and debris after disasters, and says polygons with arbitrary vertex counts are hard to feed into neural networks ([AI and GIS](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/mapping-the-intersection-of-ai-and-gis/)).
- Fletcher wants unsupervised pre-training for satellite imagery. Wilson is most interested in multimodal problems such as car accident prediction from terrain, road geometry and weather ([AI and GIS](/podcasts/practical-ai/2020/03/mapping-the-intersection-of-ai-and-gis/)).
