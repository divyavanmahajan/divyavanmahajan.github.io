## Overview

May on The Cognitive Revolution turned to governance. Two episodes dissected California's SB 1047, one with a co-sponsor and a critic and one with its author, Senator Scott Wiener. Zvi Mowshowitz joined twice to weigh the GPT-4o and Google I/O launches and the departure of OpenAI's safety leaders, while other episodes covered evals, careers and building on Claude 3.

## Main themes

### SB 1047 and where to regulate

- Nathan Calvin, a co-sponsor, says the bill is a testing and liability regime for models trained with 10^26 FLOPs: “It doesn't have rule making power. It doesn't issue licenses.” ([SB 1047 debate](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/emergency-pod-examining-sb-1047-california-s-proposed-ai-legislation/)).
- Dean Ball argues the model layer is the wrong place, “like regulating metallurgy rather than missiles”, and that a fee-funded division would slowly squeeze out open source ([SB 1047 debate](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/emergency-pod-examining-sb-1047-california-s-proposed-ai-legislation/)).
- Scott Wiener calls it a light-touch law that reduces rather than eliminates catastrophic risk. He says no big tech company supports it and that he is open to changes protecting open source ([Scott Wiener](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/governing-frontier-ai-with-ca-senator-scott-wiener-author-of-sb-1047/)).
- In a talk recorded in late 2023, Labenz warns that hostile, tribal discourse makes good regulation harder, and that control is only feasible at the frontier ([State of AI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/the-state-of-ai-from-the-80-000-hours-podcast/)).

### Open weights and control

- Labenz notes that light fine-tuning can reverse safety training, and proposes a sharper line between derivative and new models so responsibility shifts with substantial extra training ([SB 1047 debate](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/emergency-pod-examining-sb-1047-california-s-proposed-ai-legislation/)).
- With Robert Wright, Labenz argues that models are trained rather than engineered and only partly controlled. Interpretability is advancing but dual use, since flipping a steering vector reverses the behaviour ([Open Source AI Question](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/the-open-source-ai-question-part-2-robert-wright-nathan-labenz/)).

### OpenAI's month: launches and departures

- Mowshowitz says GPT-4o is fast, multimodal and half price in the API, but the demos were thin. He warns that treating an AI as an emotional partner is harmful; Labenz worries about a big tech singularity ([Race to Her](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/openai-and-google-race-to-her-is-the-big-tech-singularity-near-part-1/)).
- Mowshowitz argues the giants' limited bandwidth leaves room for startups, especially those built to improve with each new model ([Race to Her](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/openai-and-google-race-to-her-is-the-big-tech-singularity-near-part-1/)).
- After Jan Leike said superalignment never got its promised compute, Mowshowitz argues OpenAI should be treated as a product-first startup. Without trust, he says, third-party testing is needed ([Safety Team Exodus](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/openai-s-safety-team-exodus-ilya-departs-leike-speaks-out-altman-respo/)).

### Evals, careers and building on Claude

- Labenz tells students that AI gives firms infinite interns, so junior workers win by mastering tools, task automation and evals ([Infinite AI Interns](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/infinite-ai-interns-how-young-professionals-can-win-in-an-ai-powered-w/)).
- Alex Albert of Anthropic says model APIs are converging while evals and products are only starting. He recommends pairing Haiku for retrieval with Opus for synthesis, and a separate Haiku guardrail classifier ([Claude Interpreter](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/claude-interpreter-taking-safe-ai-to-market-with-alex-albert-of-anthro/)).
- Adam Majmudar built a teaching GPU from scratch with open chip tooling and AI tutors, and argues memory, not compute, is the real GPU bottleneck ([TinyGPU](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/tinygpu-massive-learning-with-adam-majmudar/)).
