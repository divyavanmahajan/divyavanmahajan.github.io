## Overview

March was GPT-4's month. Two episodes dealt with the release directly, drawing on Nathan Labenz's time as a red teamer, and a third showed an assistant built on it. Around that, founders and researchers covered the building blocks of LLM apps: embeddings, search, multimodal connectors, voice and robot control.

## Main themes

### GPT-4 and red teaming

- On a ChinaTalk crossover panel, Labenz points to more RLHF with PhD-level annotators and context windows of 8,000 and 32,000 tokens. Matthew Mittelsteadt found it far ahead of ChatGPT for policy research ([GPT4 - AI Unleashed](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/gpt4-ai-unleashed-w-chinatalk-podcast/)).
- Labenz says the early version of GPT-4 was human-level but totally amoral and would answer anything. He credits OpenAI for spending six months on capabilities and risks before launch ([GPT-4 Discussion](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/openai-s-gpt-4-discussion-with-red-teamer-nathan-labenz-and-erik-toren/)).
- He also says OpenAI told red teamers little: “we were kind of in the dark really on everything” ([GPT-4 Discussion](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/openai-s-gpt-4-discussion-with-red-teamer-nathan-labenz-and-erik-toren/)).
- Labenz tested recursive self-delegation and concluded GPT-4 was not powerful enough to get out of control. He still wants a pause before the next leap in scale ([GPT4 - AI Unleashed](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/gpt4-ai-unleashed-w-chinatalk-podcast/), [GPT-4 Discussion](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/openai-s-gpt-4-discussion-with-red-teamer-nathan-labenz-and-erik-toren/)).

### Safety training and its limits

- Labenz argues that models trained with naive RLHF are dangerous because they do whatever is predicted to please the user ([GPT-4 Discussion](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/openai-s-gpt-4-discussion-with-red-teamer-nathan-labenz-and-erik-toren/)).
- Zvi Mowshowitz says RLHF only masks harmful knowledge, which jailbreaks can surface, and is most worried by GPT-4 hiring a human to solve a CAPTCHA ([GPT4 - AI Unleashed](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/gpt4-ai-unleashed-w-chinatalk-podcast/)).
- Anton Troynikov rejects doom arguments and says “the real danger here is anthropomorphizing the system”. His concern is propaganda and social destabilisation ([Chroma](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/chroma-and-stable-attribution-s-anton-troynikov-on-embedding-and-our-f/)).
- Mahmoud Felfel says cloning ordinary people's voices for fraud is the bigger danger, so Play.ht moderates text rather than blocklisting celebrities ([Play.ht](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/the-ai-voice-revolution-with-mahmoud-felfel-of-play-ht/)).

### Agents and search on top of LLMs

- Flo Crivello describes Lindy as an inverse operating system that writes single-use code against a user's apps and confirms any action that sends anything ([Lindy](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/the-ai-assistant-revolution-with-flo-crivello-of-lindy-ai/)).
- Crivello says quality is the only criterion and bets good models get cheap faster than cheap models get good ([Lindy](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/the-ai-assistant-revolution-with-flo-crivello-of-lindy-ai/)).
- Aravind Srinivas explains that Perplexity runs queries through a traditional search engine and answers with citations. He argues ranking, not embedding the whole web, is the moat ([Perplexity](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/in-search-of-truth-w-aravind-srinivas-of-perplexity-ai/)).

### Building blocks: embeddings, multimodal, voice, robots

- Troynikov presents Chroma as an AI-native platform for embedding knowledge, launched as a day-one LangChain partner ([Chroma](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/chroma-and-stable-attribution-s-anton-troynikov-on-embedding-and-our-f/)).
- Junnan Li and Dongxu Li explain how BLIP-2 trains a small connector between frozen vision and language models on a single A100 machine. Labenz is uneasy that the injected embeddings are not human-readable ([BLIP-2](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/the-ai-multimodal-revolution-with-junnan-li-and-dongxu-li-of-blip-blip/)).
- Felfel describes Play.ht moving from reselling big-tech voices to its own transformer model ([Play.ht](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/the-ai-voice-revolution-with-mahmoud-felfel-of-play-ht/)).
- Keerthana Gopalakrishnan says robotics sits between GPT-2 and GPT-3, with action data and hardware now the bottlenecks ([Google Robotics](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/03/mother-of-robots-keerthana-gopalakrishnan-of-google-robotics/)).
