## Overview

After April's pause debate, May turned to where GPT-class models were landing. Three episodes looked at medicine, one at the Hollywood writers' strike, and several at how investors and businesses should approach AI. The month closed out with reliability and safety work, from output validation to bounded system design, and a hardware founder's view of AI compute.

## Main themes

### GPT-4 in healthcare, with humans in the loop

- Isaac Kohane says GPT-4 performs better than many doctors he has seen but cannot be used without direct human supervision. He favours a partner model in which the AI checks the doctor's work ([Isaac Kohane](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/the-ai-revolution-in-medicine-with-dr-isaac-kohane-of-harvard-medical/)).
- Kohane expects GPT-4 to transform healthcare billing and approvals, and pushback from those with “a chokehold on how information flows in the medical care system” ([Isaac Kohane](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/the-ai-revolution-in-medicine-with-dr-isaac-kohane-of-harvard-medical/)).
- Neal Khosla wants to rebuild primary care around data and computing, with doctors making decisions, regression testing of models, and autonomy only after FDA-style evidence ([Curai Health](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/revolutionizing-patient-care-with-neal-khosla-of-curai-health/)).
- Vivek Natarajan argues exam benchmarks are a weak proxy for usefulness and says models like Med-PaLM 2 are not ready for autonomous use ([Med-PaLM](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/google-s-med-palm-and-med-palm2-with-vivek-natarajan/)).

### Writers, jobs and the studios

- Trey Kollmer lists the WGA's four AI demands, which centre on credit, pay and training data rather than banning ChatGPT ([WGA writers](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/the-ai-revolution-in-hollywood-with-wga-writers-trey-kollmer-sophia-le/)).
- Garrett Schabb says “the studios see a future in Hollywood that involves as little human contribution as possible.” Sophia Lear doubts the ATM-and-bank-teller pattern will hold ([WGA writers](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/the-ai-revolution-in-hollywood-with-wga-writers-trey-kollmer-sophia-le/)).
- Nathan Labenz argues that if progress continues for another year or two, broader social contract reforms will be needed ([WGA writers](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/the-ai-revolution-in-hollywood-with-wga-writers-trey-kollmer-sophia-le/)).

### Where the value lands

- Sarah Guo and Elad Gil say generative AI is the first ML wave where startups can build very large companies. Gil names voice, social, big B2B applications and infrastructure as overlooked ([No Priors](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/vc-insights-on-investing-in-artificial-intelligence-with-sarah-guo-and/)).
- Rachel Woods says most companies had no AI plan for 2023 and should start with low-risk internal use cases. Labenz sees delegating discrete tasks as the bigger unlock ([Rachel Woods](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/helping-businesses-use-ai-with-rachel-woods-of-the-ai-exchange/)).
- Ben Tossell expects AI built into the big platforms to win most users because of switching costs. Robert Scoble bets on AR-glasses assistants and home robots ([Scouting](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/scouting-the-ai-revolution-with-robert-scoble-and-ben-s-bites-creator/)).

### Reliability, safety and compute

- Shreya Rajpal argues stochastic LLMs need a validation layer with developer-defined specs, re-asking and filtering ([Guardrails AI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/keeping-the-ai-revolution-on-the-rails-with-shreya-rajpal-of-guardrail/)).
- Connor Leahy says black-box models cannot be made safe as they are. He proposes bounded cognitive emulation that makes a user work like 1,000 human-level AGIs ([Connor Leahy](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/bonus-episode-connor-leahy-on-agi-gpt-4-and-cognitive-emulation-w-fli/)).
- Andrew Feldman explains why Cerebras builds a wafer-sized chip for sparse AI work and sells whole systems, not chips ([Cerebras](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/05/the-ai-chip-revolution-with-andrew-feldman-of-cerebras/)).
