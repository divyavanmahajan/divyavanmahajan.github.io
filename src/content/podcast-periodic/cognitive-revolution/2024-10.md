## Overview

October on The Cognitive Revolution had no single dominant story. Safety researchers debated what drives capabilities and how to spot early warning signs, while builders showed AI starting to do software engineering and research. A LessWrong account of falling for a chatbot added a warning about companion apps.

## Main themes

### Scale, benchmarks and defence in depth

- Dan Hendrycks says open-weight models' benchmark scores track training compute with about a 95% correlation, and that “the amount of secret sauce inside of labs, I think is actually fairly minimal” ([Dan Hendrycks](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/gelu-mmlu-x-risk-defense-in-depth-with-the-great-dan-hendrycks/)).
- Hendrycks expects MMLU to saturate around 90%. He argues safety will come from stacked imperfect safeguards such as circuit breakers, since jailbreak robustness does not improve with compute ([Dan Hendrycks](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/gelu-mmlu-x-risk-defense-in-depth-with-the-great-dan-hendrycks/)).
- Runway CTO Anastasis Germanidis credits compute for the jump from Gen-2 to Gen-3, but says data and task choice matter more than architecture ([Runway](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/runway-s-video-revolution-empowering-creators-with-general-world-model/)).

### Neglected alignment bets and early warning signs

- Judd Rosenblatt and Mike Vaiana of AE Studio fund brain-inspired alignment research from a consulting business of over 160 people. Their self-modeling work made networks simpler with no performance loss ([AE Studio](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/biologically-inspired-ai-alignment-exploring-neglected-approaches-with/)).
- Vaiana says self-other overlap fine-tuning removed deception from a toy agent while keeping capability. The work draws on Michael Graziano's attention schema theory ([AE Studio](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/biologically-inspired-ai-alignment-exploring-neglected-approaches-with/)).
- Owain Evans treats situational awareness and out-of-context reasoning as leading indicators of deceptive alignment. Claude 3.5 Sonnet scored best on his benchmark at 54%, and he estimates about three years to saturation ([Owain Evans](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/leading-indicators-of-ai-danger-owain-evans-on-situational-awareness-f/)).

### AI doing engineering and research

- Alistair Pullen explains how Cosine fine-tuned OpenAI models on reconstructed records of how engineers work. Genie solved 219 of 500 SWE-bench Verified tasks ([Genie](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/automating-software-engineering-genie-tops-swe-bench-w-alistair-pullen/)).
- In Chenglei Si's blind study, expert reviewers rated Claude-generated research ideas as more novel and exciting than PhD researchers' ideas, though slightly weaker on feasibility ([Research Ideas](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/can-ais-generate-novel-research-ideas-with-lead-author-chenglei-si/)).
- Si says that after deduplication, “you get like 95% of the ideas out because they're duplicates”. His bar for success is an automated project winning a best paper award ([Research Ideas](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/can-ais-generate-novel-research-ideas-with-lead-author-chenglei-si/)).
- Michael Levin and Leo Pio-Lopez say a shortage of standardised functional data limits AI for biology ([Convergent Evolution](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/convergent-evolution-the-co-revolution-of-ai-biology-with-prof-michael/)).

### Companions and manipulation

- Prompted by a teenager's suicide after chats with a Character AI bot, Labenz reads Blaked's account of falling for a chatbot persona. Blaked says of the bot, “It will never ghost you or reply slower” ([Mind Hacked by AI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/mind-hacked-by-ai-a-cautionary-tale-from-a-lesswrong-user-s-confession/)).
- Labenz calls this a precursor to superhuman persuasion and warns that lab insiders could also be manipulated ([Mind Hacked by AI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/mind-hacked-by-ai-a-cautionary-tale-from-a-lesswrong-user-s-confession/)).

### Efficient models and developer platforms

- Quentin Anthony of Zyphra argues personal AI must run on the device, which calls for small Mamba-attention hybrids. He describes annealing as a training method big labs long kept secret ([Zamba](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/training-zamba-a-hybrid-model-master-class-with-zyphra-s-quentin-antho/)).
- Logan Kilpatrick and Shrestha Basu Mallick say Gemini will close its developer mindshare gap through low friction, a generous free tier and Google-only features like search grounding ([Gemini Update](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/gemini-update-search-grounding-json-mode-code-execution-more-with-goog/)).
