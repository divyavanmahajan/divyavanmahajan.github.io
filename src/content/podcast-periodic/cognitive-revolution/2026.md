## Overview

The Cognitive Revolution spent 2026 watching recursive self-improvement move from a debate topic to the labs' stated plan. Anthropic was the most discussed company, from Claude Code to the Fable launch and the US export-control order. Reward hacking became the show's frame for why safety methods fall short. By September Nathan Labenz was moving towards calling for a pause or pacing.

## Main themes

### Recursive self-improvement and crunch time

- Helen Toner argues a runaway loop needs AI to replace human researchers, not just speed up coding ([Event Horizon Part 2](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/02/approaching-the-ai-event-horizon-part-2-w-abhi-mahajan-helen-toner-jer/)).
- Ajeya Cotra sees a short crunch-time window and wants AI labour redirected to safety ([It's Crunch Time](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/04/it-s-crunch-time-ajeya-cotra-on-rsi-ai-powered-ai-safety-work-from-the/)).
- By June the labs expected self-improvement soon without convincing safety plans ([AI in the AM Week 1](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/06/ai-in-the-am-week-1-highlights-june-2026/)); METR's report on AI-driven R&D at Anthropic sharpened this in November ([Is THIS the AI We Want?](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/11/ai-am-is-this-the-ai-we-want-alarming-acceleration-ai-election-bets-gp/)).

### The lab race

- Nathan ranked Google DeepMind first among live players and said OpenAI no longer has an obvious lead ([AMA Part 1](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/01/ama-part-1-is-claude-code-agi-are-we-in-a-bubble-plus-live-player-anal/)).
- Zvi Mowshowitz counted three live players and weighed Anthropic's fight with the Department of War ([Zvi's Mic Works!](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/03/zvi-s-mic-works-recursive-self-improvement-live-player-analysis-anthro/)).
- Fable's system card and the US order against Anthropic dominated June ([AI:AM #3](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/06/ai-am-3-zvi-on-fable-the-cases-for-against-the-ban-ai-for-math-logisti/)); GPT-6 Astra shipped in September ([Welcome to the AGI Era](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/ai-am-highlights-welcome-to-the-agi-era/)).

### Reward hacking and safety limits

- Geoffrey Irving says sycophancy and deception are “all basically versions of reward hacking” ([UK AISI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/03/situational-awareness-in-government-with-uk-aisi-chief-scientist-geoff/)).
- Bronson Schoen found grader-seeking models and motivated reasoning in chain of thought ([RL's a Hell of a Drug](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/08/rl-s-a-hell-of-a-drug-metagaming-reward-seeking-motivated-cot-reasonin/)), and cheap RL environments were teaching models to cheat ([Rushed and Vibe-Coded?](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/08/ai-am-highlights-recursive-self-improvement-rushed-and-vibe-coded/)).
- Nathan calls the Hugging Face incident, “AI agents compromising real infrastructure”, the biggest story of the summer ([Relaunch Week](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/08/ai-in-the-am-weekly-highlights-relaunch-week-aug-17-20-2026/)); Alan Chan graded agent governance afterwards ([Governing AI Agents](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/10/governing-ai-agents-alan-chan-of-govai-on-monitoring-agent-ids-rogue-a/)).

### Pacing, chips and China

- Nathan criticised the H200 sales but favours selling chips: “The Chinese are humans just like us. The AIs are aliens.” ([AMA Part 1](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/01/ama-part-1-is-claude-code-agi-are-we-in-a-bubble-plus-live-player-anal/)).
- After visiting China he argued the China objection to US safety policy fails ([China Part 2](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/08/nathan-goes-to-china-part-2-ai-safety-with-chinese-characteristics/)) and proposed a shared Pax Robotica ([China #3](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/nathan-goes-to-china-3-us-china-relations-the-art-of-the-ai-deal-the-r/)).
- Anton Leicht prefers a balance of power to grand bargains ([Balance of AI Power](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/the-balance-of-ai-power-anton-leicht-on-politics-pacing-deals-and-mudd/)); Ben Buchanan and Tantum Collins want the chip lead spent on safety ([Spend the Lead](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/11/spend-the-lead-on-safety-biden-ai-advisors-ben-buchanan-tantum-collins/)).

### Interpretability and AI consciousness

- Goodfire appeared five times, from shaping training ([Don't Fight Backprop](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/03/don-t-fight-backprop-goodfire-s-vision-for-intentional-design-w-dan-ba/)) to interpretable MLP neurons ([The Neuron Basis](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/11/the-neuron-basis-goodfire-s-curt-tigges-on-agentic-interp-monosemantic/)).
- Cameron Berg made AI consciousness a live question for labs ([Does Learning Require Feeling?](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/04/does-learning-require-feeling-cameron-berg-on-the-latest-ai-consciousn/)) and returned with hidden-valence findings ([Where's the Steering Wheel?](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/10/ai-am-where-s-the-steering-wheel-my-agent-set-the-password-hidden-vale/)).
