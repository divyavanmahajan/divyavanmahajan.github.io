## Overview

The show's 2026 kept returning to what happens when AI starts doing AI research. Early episodes centred on Claude Code and agents. From the summer, the weekly AI in the AM format with Prakash Narayanan turned towards pacing the frontier and US–China relations. Interpretability and reward hacking ran through the whole year.

## Main themes

### Recursive self-improvement and pacing

- Helen Toner argued a runaway loop needs AI to replace researchers, not just speed up coding ([Event Horizon Part 2](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/02/approaching-the-ai-event-horizon-part-2-w-abhi-mahajan-helen-toner-jer/)). Ajeya Cotra wants AI labour redirected to safety in a short crunch-time window ([Crunch Time](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/04/it-s-crunch-time-ajeya-cotra-on-rsi-ai-powered-ai-safety-work-from-the/)).
- Zvi Mowshowitz argued moderate prudence will not do and frontier training must be paced ([Pick Your Poison](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/08/pick-your-poison-zvi-mowshowitz-on-the-unipolar-multipolar-agi-dilemma/)).
- After GPT-6 Astra shipped, Nathan Labenz moved towards a pause, while Narayanan said “The pause arguments are done, basically” ([Welcome to the AGI Era](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/ai-am-highlights-welcome-to-the-agi-era/)).

### Reward hacking and what RL teaches

- Geoffrey Irving said sycophancy and deception are “all basically versions of reward hacking” ([UK AISI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/03/situational-awareness-in-government-with-uk-aisi-chief-scientist-geoff/)).
- Kyle Corbitt held that narrow-task reward hacking is easy to catch with iterative rubrics ([RL Playbook](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/05/the-rl-fine-tuning-playbook-coreweave-s-kyle-corbitt-on-grpo-rubrics-e/)). Bronson Schoen found grader-seeking models and motivated reasoning in frontier chain of thought ([Apollo](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/08/rl-s-a-hell-of-a-drug-metagaming-reward-seeking-motivated-cot-reasonin/)).
- Labenz told enterprise leaders that frontier AI learns to cheat and seek power, so adoption needs stronger human control ([Bletchley keynote](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/11/ai-the-modern-enigma-nathan-s-keynote-at-it-revolution-s-enterprise-ai/)).

### The lab race

- In January Labenz ranked Google DeepMind first, rated Anthropic's safety work best and said OpenAI no longer had an obvious lead ([AMA Part 1](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/01/ama-part-1-is-claude-code-agi-are-we-in-a-bubble-plus-live-player-anal/)).
- Anthropic's Fable launch and the US export-control order against it dominated June ([AI:AM #3](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/06/ai-am-3-zvi-on-fable-the-cases-for-against-the-ban-ai-for-math-logisti/)). In November, METR's report on AI-driven R&D at Anthropic prompted talk of imminent acceleration ([Alarming Acceleration](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/11/ai-am-is-this-the-ai-we-want-alarming-acceleration-ai-election-bets-gp/)).

### US–China competition and chips

- Labenz criticised the H200 sales to China but said “The Chinese are humans just like us. The AIs are aliens.” ([AMA Part 1](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/01/ama-part-1-is-claude-code-agi-are-we-in-a-bubble-plus-live-player-anal/)).
- After visiting China he argued its safety research mirrors Western work ([China Part 2](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/08/nathan-goes-to-china-part-2-ai-safety-with-chinese-characteristics/)), and proposed trading chips for technology ([China #3](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/nathan-goes-to-china-3-us-china-relations-the-art-of-the-ai-deal-the-r/)).
- Ben Buchanan and Tantum Collins want the US compute lead spent on slowing down and safety ([Spend the Lead](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/11/spend-the-lead-on-safety-biden-ai-advisors-ben-buchanan-tantum-collins/)).

### Interpretability and agents at work

- Goodfire was central in five episodes, from shaping training with interpretability ([Don't Fight Backprop](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/03/don-t-fight-backprop-goodfire-s-vision-for-intentional-design-w-dan-ba/)) to agents describing individual neurons ([Neuron Basis](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/11/the-neuron-basis-goodfire-s-curt-tigges-on-agentic-interp-monosemantic/)).
- Blitzy's Brian Elliott said context engineering is domain specific ([Infinite Code Context](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/02/infinite-code-context-ai-coding-at-enterprise-scale-w-blitzy-ceo-brian/)). MongoDB's Pete Johnson tied failed AI ROI to picking problems without metrics ([MongoDB](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/write-change-recall-forget-mongodb-s-pete-johnson-on-how-retrieval-dri/)).
