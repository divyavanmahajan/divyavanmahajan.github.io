## Overview

The Moonshot Podcast began in March 2025, the year X, Alphabet's moonshot factory, turned 15. The spring season ran one X project family per episode, from Waymo and Wing to fusion, waste and the grid. From June it switched to long deep-dive interviews with single founders and engineers. The common thread was how X works: small teams, scrappy prototypes, and honest decisions to stop.

## Main themes

### How X does moonshots

- Astro Teller argues “you can't change an industry for the better without breaking some of its most closely held beliefs” ([Driving The Future](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/03/driving-the-future/)).
- Sebastian Thrun says early X had no finance staff, no PowerPoints, little reporting and teams of about ten people ([Thrun on Waymo's Early Days](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/06/deep-dive-sebastian-thrun-on-waymos-early-days/)).
- Live at SXSW, Teller, Adam Savage and Catie Cuan tie invention to tight learning loops and destigmatised failure ([Prototyping The Future](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/05/prototyping-the-future-live-from-sxsw-2025/)).
- Stopping is part of the method: Loon failed on economics but its laser links became Taara ([Essential Connections](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/03/essential-connections/)), and X wound down Z-pinch fusion honestly ([Powering Up](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/powering-up/)).

### Waymo, Wing and autonomy

- Andrew Chatham recalls a driver-assist trial cancelled after testers fell asleep, which pushed Waymo towards full autonomy ([Driving The Future](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/03/driving-the-future/)).
- André Prager says “the flying part isn't so much the moonshot. The interesting part is everything else” ([Driving The Future](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/03/driving-the-future/)).
- In his deep dive, Prager frames drone delivery as a systems problem solved with cheap, passive hardware that software improves over time ([Prototyping at Wing](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/07/deep-dive-andre-prager-on-prototyping-at-wing/)).

### Google Brain and the bet on scale

- Brain began at X on the bet that much bigger neural networks would learn better ([Machines That Think](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/03/machines-that-think-the-ai-moonshot/)).
- Andrew Ng recalls how controversial scaling was, and says the next wave of value lies in applications and fast execution ([Andrew Ng](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/08/the-moonshot-podcast-deep-dive-andrew-ng-on-deep-learning-and-google-b/)).
- Jeff Dean traces the line from a large neural network experiment to TPUs and transformers ([Jeff Dean](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/08/deep-dive-jeff-dean-on-google-brains-early-days/)).

### Robots people can live with

- Everyday Robots and Intrinsic argue robots should learn rather than be programmed, and behave in ways people find legible ([Living With Robots](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/living-with-robots/)).
- Catie Cuan says “We're going to see billions of robots in our lifetimes”, and sees choreography and sound as what will make them welcome ([Catie Cuan](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/07/deep-dive-catie-cuan-on-dancing-and-living-with-robots/)).

### Wearables and data for the physical world

- Glass is recast as a learning platform that came too early for consumers ([Supersenses](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/05/supersenses/), [Glass's Early Days](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/10/deep-dive-ivy-ross-isabelle-olsson-on-the-early-days-of-google-glass/)).
- Jason Rugolo argues computing moves into the ear only once a device improves hearing ([Jason Rugolo](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/11/the-moonshot-podcast-deep-dive-jason-rugolo-on-iyo-and-the-future-of-a/)).
- Mineral and Tidal bet on field data from cheap hardware for food production ([Feeding The World](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/03/feeding-the-world/)), and David Guerena applies phone-based computer vision to bean breeding ([David Guerena](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/09/deep-dive-david-guerena-on-the-future-of-agriculture/)).

## Notable episodes

- [Driving The Future](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/03/driving-the-future/): X's first two moonshots and how safety, cost and regulation were solved one at a time.
- [Machines That Think](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/03/machines-that-think-the-ai-moonshot/): the origin of Google Brain and the scaling bet.
- [Powering Up](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/powering-up/): a rare account of a 1% bet on fusion and why it was stopped.
- [Prototyping The Future](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/05/prototyping-the-future-live-from-sxsw-2025/): X's culture of failure and learning, discussed live.
- [Jeff Dean](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/08/deep-dive-jeff-dean-on-google-brains-early-days/): Brain's path to TPUs and transformers from the inside.
