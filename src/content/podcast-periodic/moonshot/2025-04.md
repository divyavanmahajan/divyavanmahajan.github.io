## Overview

In April, Astro Teller paired X projects two at a time around robotics, waste, climate resilience and energy. Several of the stories were about what happened after X: a merger into DeepMind, a move to Google's philanthropy arm, a full spin-out and an honest shutdown. Data, often geospatial or visual, was the common raw material.

## Main themes

### Robots that learn and earn trust

- Everyday Robots set out to build helper robots that learn rather than being programmed. Hans Peter Brondmo points to ageing populations as the driver, and about 180 robots ended up cleaning tables and sorting trash in Google buildings before the work merged into DeepMind in 2023 ([Living With Robots](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/living-with-robots/)).
- Catie Cuan argues that in places like nursing homes “safety is not just important, it's the perception of safety”. Her music mode made people rate robots as more likable, intelligent and safe ([Living With Robots](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/living-with-robots/)).
- Wendy Tan White presents Intrinsic's Flowstate, a drag-and-drop builder for robot cells with a skills library that developers can eventually be paid for ([Living With Robots](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/living-with-robots/)).

### Waste as a data problem

- The show says food wasted by consumers and retailers costs the global economy an estimated $1 trillion a year, and Emily Ma says food waste is the largest source of landfill methane ([Trash to Treasure](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/trash-to-treasure/)).
- Ma's Delta project standardised how surplus food is described so it could reach food banks. It lacked a profitable business model and moved to Google's philanthropy arm in 2020, becoming open-source software used by parts of Feeding America ([Trash to Treasure](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/trash-to-treasure/)).
- Rey Banatao's team combines infrared spectroscopy, cameras and AI to sort plastics at the molecular level, and delivered a tanker of pyrolysis oil made from landfill-bound plastic ([Trash to Treasure](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/trash-to-treasure/)).

### Climate resilience and carbon removal

- Sarah Russell says Bellwether's hard part was organising about 8,000 geospatial data formats. It gives insurers absolute per-home fire probabilities instead of relative risk bands ([Fires, Floods and Future-Proofing](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/fires-floods-and-future-proofing/)).
- Bellwether is piloted in the US to plan burn lines and fuel work, can rerun scenarios such as a controlled burn, and processed 70,000 images after Hurricane Helene ([Fires, Floods and Future-Proofing](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/fires-floods-and-future-proofing/)).
- Jacques Gagne's 280 Earth moves a cheap silica sorbent between air and heating stations, running on data-centre waste heat and capturing water too. Helen Riley explains the full spin-out with Alphabet as a minority investor ([Fires, Floods and Future-Proofing](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/fires-floods-and-future-proofing/)).

### Energy: a fusion bet and the grid

- Matt Forkin's Blixt team spent two years trying to disprove its Z-pinch fusion hypothesis in simulation before building hardware. After a few hundred shots they saw an early instability, and the project was wound down with results to be published ([Powering Up](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/powering-up/)).
- Teller frames the bet as expected value, a small chance at a very large payoff, and calls the shutdown a smart outcome ([Powering Up](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/powering-up/)).
- Page Crahan describes 80 million km of grid still run on an 1880s design. Tapestry built a map of grid assets and planning tools, and Juan Carlos Olmedo says Chile's planning analysis dropped from months to a day ([Powering Up](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/powering-up/)).

## Notable episodes

- [Living With Robots](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/living-with-robots/): robot behaviour and sound treated as design problems, alongside a platform play for factory robotics.
- [Trash to Treasure](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/trash-to-treasure/): shows a project finding its impact as open-source software rather than a business.
- [Fires, Floods and Future-Proofing](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/fires-floods-and-future-proofing/): disaster forecasting for insurers and planners, and a carbon-capture spin-out.
- [Powering Up](/podcasts/moonshot/2025/04/powering-up/): a frank account of ending a fusion moonshot, contrasted with grid software in use in Chile.
