## Overview

August on Moonshots centred on GPT-5: a panel reaction to the launch, an interview with OpenAI's Kevin Weil and a debate on whether AI is a bubble. The other half of the month covered home humanoids with 1X's Bernt Bornich, crypto as the financial layer for AI agents, and Balaji Srinivasan's case for many AGIs rather than one.

## Main themes

### GPT-5: cheaper, not a leap

- The panel found the launch weak as a show. Diamandis quoted Sam Altman: “GPT5 is a significant step on our way to AGI which also means it isn't AGI yet.” ([GPT-5 Update](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/ai-insiders-breakdown-the-gpt-5-update-what-it-means-for-the-ai-race-w/)).
- Emad Mostaque described GPT-5 as a router from mini up to pro and suspects OpenAI holds back better internal models. Dave Blundin said it cut the cost of AI at least in half and caught up in coding ([GPT-5 Update](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/ai-insiders-breakdown-the-gpt-5-update-what-it-means-for-the-ai-race-w/)).
- Alexander Wissner-Gross noted GPT-5 taking first place on LMArena and argued that “whoever is defining the benchmarks wins” ([GPT-5 Update](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/ai-insiders-breakdown-the-gpt-5-update-what-it-means-for-the-ai-race-w/)).
- Kevin Weil said feedback from about 700 million users showed a wooden personality, which OpenAI fixed quickly. He said most benchmarks are saturated, so OpenAI now tests economically valuable tasks ([Kevin Weil](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/open-ais-head-of-product-on-the-ai-race-google-the-reality-of-agi-w-ke/)).

### OpenAI, Google and the bubble question

- Blundin said Polymarket odds of OpenAI having the best model flipped towards Google during the coding demos. He noted Demis Hassabis has 6,000 people in AI R&D against OpenAI's just under 2,000 ([GPT-5 Update](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/ai-insiders-breakdown-the-gpt-5-update-what-it-means-for-the-ai-race-w/)).
- Weil said OpenAI's compute is completely maxed out and told startups to build where models barely work, not patches the next model will make unnecessary ([Kevin Weil](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/open-ais-head-of-product-on-the-ai-race-google-the-reality-of-agi-w-ke/)).
- Blundin rejected the bubble narrative and read Altman's remarks as deliberate downplay. Salim Ismail saw market-cap management, and Wissner-Gross said data-centre capex can continue while revenue grows spectacularly ([Bubble](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/is-ai-a-bubble-experts-debate-the-future-of-ai-w-david-blundin-salim-i/)).

### Humanoids start in the home

- Bornich argues the home gives 1X the fastest path to scale and the diverse data general robot intelligence needs. He sees humanoids as a phase before more specialised machines ([1X](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/robotics-ceo-the-humanoid-robot-revolution-is-real-it-starts-now-w-ber/)).
- He targets a factory run rate above 20,000 robots a year by end of 2026 and described owner-approved teleoperation with a 24-hour window to delete clips ([1X](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/robotics-ceo-the-humanoid-robot-revolution-is-real-it-starts-now-w-ber/)).

### Chips, crypto and money for agents

- Blundin called chip fabs the real bottleneck in the US-China race, noted Intel's paused Ohio fabs and predicted Intel will be turned around one way or another ([Eric Pulier](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/the-ai-crypto-collision-that-will-redefine-global-power-w-eric-pulier/)).
- Eric Pulier argued stablecoins and tokenised assets will become the internet's financial layer. Blundin warned weak SEC guidance could repeat 2008 ([Eric Pulier](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/the-ai-crypto-collision-that-will-redefine-global-power-w-eric-pulier/)).
- Balaji Srinivasan called Bitcoin digital gold and argued crypto is the natural currency for agents, naming quantum cryptography as a risk to watch ([Balaji](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/balaji-opens-up-on-ai-agi-bitcoin-americas-incoming-collapse-w-dave-bl/)).

### Many AGIs or one winner

- Balaji argued there will be many AGIs, pointing to leapfrogging US labs and Chinese open models, and to limits set by cryptography, chaos and turbulence ([Balaji](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/balaji-opens-up-on-ai-agi-bitcoin-americas-incoming-collapse-w-dave-bl/)).
- He said AI “does a middle to middle right now because the bottlenecks are prompting and verifying”. Blundin countered that self-improvement has begun and a single winner may show within months ([Balaji](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/balaji-opens-up-on-ai-agi-bitcoin-americas-incoming-collapse-w-dave-bl/)).
