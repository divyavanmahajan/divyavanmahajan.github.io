## Overview

March brought several live sessions from the Abundance Summit, with Elon Musk, Eric Schmidt, Emad Mostaque and Uber's Dara Khosrowshahi. AI and jobs ran through eight of the ten episodes, alongside Anthropic's enterprise rise and an argument over whether recursive self-improvement has started. Chips, electricity and robotaxis filled out the month.

## Main themes

### Job loss and the income question

- Andrew Yang argues that UBI must come before Musk's universal high income, because white-collar losses are already under way. He says one tech CEO told him privately “we're going to fire 15% of our workers and then two years from now we're going to fire another 20%.” ([Andrew Yang](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/andrew-yang-ubi-before-uhi-solving-job-loss-and-the-future-of-work-236/)).
- Yang describes the career pyramid turning into columns, leaving young people no entry point. A professor's figures show CS placement falling from 89% in fall 2023 to 19% this spring ([Andrew Yang](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/andrew-yang-ubi-before-uhi-solving-job-loss-and-the-future-of-work-236/), [NVIDIA's $1 Trillion](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/nvidias-1-trillion-prediction-anthropic-beats-openai-tesla-vs-tsmc-the/)).
- Musk puts the chance of a great outcome at about 80% and expects universal high income. Mostaque argues for universal basic AI ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/elon-musk-optimus-3-is-coming-recursive-self-improvement-is-already-he/), [Abundance Summit live](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/meta-buys-moltbook-gpt-5-4-and-fruitfly-brain-upload-moonshots-live-at/)).

### Anthropic, OpenAI and the enterprise

- Dario Amodei refused the Pentagon's demand to remove safeguards on surveillance and autonomous weapons, putting $200 million in contracts at risk ([Anthropic vs. The Pentagon](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/anthropic-vs-the-pentagon-claude-outpaces-chatgpt-and-consulting-gets/)).
- A chart shows Anthropic revenue growing 10x a year against OpenAI's 3.4x. Later in the month, Anthropic's share of first-time enterprise customers rises to 73%, and Wissner-Gross says OpenAI lost a bet on consumer demand for reasoning ([Anthropic vs. The Pentagon](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/anthropic-vs-the-pentagon-claude-outpaces-chatgpt-and-consulting-gets/), [NVIDIA's $1 Trillion](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/nvidias-1-trillion-prediction-anthropic-beats-openai-tesla-vs-tsmc-the/)).
- Anthropic loosened its safety pledge and Amazon tied up to $50 billion for OpenAI to an IPO and AGI. Wissner-Gross argues safety will come from competition ([Financializing Super Intelligence](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/financializing-super-intelligence-amazons-50b-late-fee-235/)).

### Has recursive self-improvement started?

- Musk says each new model is built by the one before it, not yet fully automated, and that the hard takeoff is happening now ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/elon-musk-optimus-3-is-coming-recursive-self-improvement-is-already-he/)).
- Wissner-Gross says “we're we're deep in the middle of recursive self-improvement right now”, pointing to GPT-5.4 and Karpathy's autoresearch ([Abundance Summit live](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/meta-buys-moltbook-gpt-5-4-and-fruitfly-brain-upload-moonshots-live-at/)).
- Eric Schmidt disagrees: his reviews find no agreed approach and only limited demos, though he says coding has flipped from 80/20 human to 20/80 AI ([Eric Schmidt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/eric-schmidt-singularitys-arrival-the-92-gigawatt-problem-and-recursiv/)).

### Chips, power and agents

- Jensen Huang claimed at least $1 trillion through 2027. Blundin says TSMC is the bottleneck, with Nvidia holding 70% of its 3-nanometer volume ([NVIDIA's $1 Trillion](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/nvidias-1-trillion-prediction-anthropic-beats-openai-tesla-vs-tsmc-the/)).
- Schmidt estimates a 92 GW US power shortfall by 2030, about 60 nuclear plants, and asks “what's the real resource constraint in in America? And it's electricity.” ([Eric Schmidt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/eric-schmidt-singularitys-arrival-the-92-gigawatt-problem-and-recursiv/)).
- Alex Finn runs OpenClaw agents locally as an org chart of AI employees, while Wissner-Gross raises security concerns. Nvidia's NemoClaw took the framework towards the enterprise ([OpenClaw Explained](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/openclaw-explained-baby-agi-security-threats-and-how-a-mac-mini-became/), [NVIDIA's $1 Trillion](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/nvidias-1-trillion-prediction-anthropic-beats-openai-tesla-vs-tsmc-the/)).

### Robotaxis and the end of human driving

- Diamandis cites Waymo's 170 million autonomous miles with 92% fewer serious crashes. Blundin expects voters to push human drivers off public roads ([Elon's $5 Trillion Bet](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/elons-5-trillion-bet-the-end-of-human-drivers-and-chamaths-market-warn/)).
- Khosrowshahi says Uber will win by partnering with over 20 autonomy companies, aims for 15 cities by year end, and expects financial players such as Blackstone to own fleets ([Uber CEO](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/uber-ceo-on-winning-the-robotaxi-race-the-end-of-car-ownership-and-ube/)).
