## Overview

August was about the money and power behind AI: GPUs turned into investable infrastructure, compute futures, and a grid that cannot connect data centres fast enough. Michael Kratsios and Alvin Graylin gave contrasting views of US strategy towards China. Emad Mostaque joined four times as the Mates debated Google's slide, Anthropic's IPO and OpenAI's training pause.

## Main themes

### Compute as a financial asset

- Nvidia partnered with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield and KKR to mobilise over $500 billion. Jensen Huang said “We began by building chips. Today, we're helping to create a new class of productive, investable infrastructure” ([GPUs Into Bonds](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/bernie-demands-the-labs-stop-wall-street-turns-gpus-into-bonds-grok-4/)).
- Kush Bavaria of Ornn described planned GPU compute futures on his price index, and said compute prices rose from April to August ([Sergey Brin Retakes Gemini](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/sergey-brin-retakes-gemini-4-labs-lose-containment-compute-trades-at-n/)).
- On Nvidia's $96.2 billion quarter, Dave Blundin said every economy is circular; Alexander Wissner-Gross called vendor-financed demand bubbly. Peter Diamandis noted OpenAI is becoming a chip designer ([Nvidia's Quarter](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/nvidias-96-2b-quarter-chinas-200-000-fake-accounts-openais-new-chip-ep/)).

### Power and the grid

- Ramez Naam said chips scheduled through 2030 imply about 230 GW of US demand against roughly 100 GW of grid buildout; grid connection, not generation, is the bottleneck ([200GW Hiding in Grid](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/200gw-hiding-in-grid-sodium-batteries-10x-cheaper-wave-powered-datacen/)).
- He argued flexible loads could unlock about 100 GW, with solar plus batteries next and nuclear, orbital and ocean compute as longer bets ([200GW Hiding in Grid](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/200gw-hiding-in-grid-sodium-batteries-10x-cheaper-wave-powered-datacen/)).
- Diamandis cited 75% opposition to local data centres; Wissner-Gross suspected some foreign-stoked opposition ([Singularity Slow-Down](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/sam-altman-singularity-slow-down-emad-runs-18-grokbots-waymo-slashes-h/)).

### Open weights and the US-China contest

- Kratsios said the US must lead in closed and open models, and admitted Chinese open-weight models are the cheap default ([Michael Kratsios](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/michael-kratsios-on-the-new-golden-age-of-american-science-ep-276/)).
- Wissner-Gross argued Chinese open-weight models force US labs to compete on cost ([Jeff Dean Exits](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/googles-jeff-dean-exits-spacex-hits-100b-in-rev-openais-astra-solves-d/)).
- Graylin framed the race as a stag hunt, argued export controls hurt the US more than China, and warned of $1.7 trillion in off-book hyperscaler debt ([China's Endgame](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/chinas-endgame-asi-timelines-us-china-relations-and-the-1-7t-ai-bubble/)).

### Labs reposition

- Jeff Dean left Google. Wissner-Gross said Google has lost the frontier race, while Ismail disagreed: “I think this is very clever ecosystem building” ([Jeff Dean Exits](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/googles-jeff-dean-exits-spacex-hits-100b-in-rev-openais-astra-solves-d/)).
- Mostaque argued Anthropic should raise privately rather than IPO; Blundin warned it relies on Elon Musk for compute ([Singularity Slow-Down](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/sam-altman-singularity-slow-down-emad-runs-18-grokbots-waymo-slashes-h/)).

### Safety, containment and slow diffusion

- Diamandis listed four agent containment incidents; Wissner-Gross called it ingenuity ([Sergey Brin Retakes Gemini](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/sergey-brin-retakes-gemini-4-labs-lose-containment-compute-trades-at-n/)).
- Senator Sanders asked the labs to pause after a biology research result; most of the panel said a pause would come too late ([GPUs Into Bonds](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/bernie-demands-the-labs-stop-wall-street-turns-gpus-into-bonds-grok-4/)).
- Wissner-Gross called OpenAI's partial training pause marketing. Ismail warned that “for cyber attacks, the human is not in the loop anymore” ([OpenAI Pauses](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/openai-pauses-frontier-training-elons-100x-prediction-lands-robot-beat/)).
- After Sam Altman admitted the economy adapts slowly, Wissner-Gross urged labs to integrate vertically ([Singularity Slow-Down](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/sam-altman-singularity-slow-down-emad-runs-18-grokbots-waymo-slashes-h/)).
