## Overview

January's centre of gravity was Astro Teller, whose talk and Q&A from the 2022 Abundance360 summit explain how Alphabet's X picks, funds and kills moonshots. Around that, Anthony Pompliano made the long-term case for Bitcoin, Deepak Chopra set out his view of happiness and abundance, and Lou Reese pitched a cheaper alternative to costly antibody drugs.

## Main themes

### How X runs a moonshot factory

- Teller frames innovation as exploration, not exploitation. He says people are taught from about age six to exploit, and that nobody picks winning moonshots better than random ([How to Build a Moonshot](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-25-how-to-build-a-moonshot-w-astro-teller/)).
- What X can do is gather evidence fast. Teller says “experimentation is your job”, with duct-taped version-zero experiments and the hardest part tackled first ([How to Build a Moonshot](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-25-how-to-build-a-moonshot-w-astro-teller/)).
- X needs a huge problem, a science-fiction-sounding solution and a hypothesis testable in about six months. Waymo and Wing came out of it, and lasers from the closed Loon project were reused by Taara ([How to Build a Moonshot](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-25-how-to-build-a-moonshot-w-astro-teller/), [Teller AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-24-astro-tellers-ama-allocating-resources-for-audacious-ideas/)).

### Killing projects and allocating capital

- Teller says X has killed roughly 2,000 projects and keeps a graveyard because ideas come back. Foghorn's seawater-to-fuel work may return after a green-hydrogen breakthrough ([Teller AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-24-astro-tellers-ama-allocating-resources-for-audacious-ideas/)).
- Teams that kill projects are celebrated at All Hands, and X marks the Day of the Dead for cancelled work. Teller warns that tying pride to being right means “a 99% chance of being miserable at X” ([Teller AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-24-astro-tellers-ama-allocating-resources-for-audacious-ideas/), [How to Build a Moonshot](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-25-how-to-build-a-moonshot-w-astro-teller/)).
- X uses no OKRs and gives no equity until a project graduates, because stock in a darling makes it hard to kill. Teams are deliberately under-resourced, after X once put almost a third of its resources on one project ([Teller AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-24-astro-tellers-ama-allocating-resources-for-audacious-ideas/)).
- For large companies, Teller advises a meaningful barrier between a moonshot group and the core business ([Teller AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-24-astro-tellers-ama-allocating-resources-for-audacious-ideas/)).

### Bitcoin as hard money

- Anthony Pompliano argues Bitcoin must become a store of value before a medium of exchange. He says 67% of bitcoin did not move during a 75% price drop, which he reads as conviction ([Bitcoin in 2023](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-22-why-bitcoin-in-2023-w-anthony-pomp-pompliano/)).
- He says adoption ran from individuals to companies such as MicroStrategy, Block and Tesla, with countries like El Salvador next. He warns that central bank digital currencies could enable surveillance ([Bitcoin in 2023](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-22-why-bitcoin-in-2023-w-anthony-pomp-pompliano/)).
- For ordinary savers he suggests a 1-5% position, dollar-cost averaged, held for years and kept in self-custody ([Bitcoin in 2023](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-22-why-bitcoin-in-2023-w-anthony-pomp-pompliano/)).

### Abundance, happiness and the cost of medicine

- Deepak Chopra describes the formula H = S + C + V. He says the brain's set point drives about half of daily happiness and money only 10-12%, leaving daily choices the rest ([Deepak Chopra](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-23-the-secret-to-happiness-w-deepak-chopra-m-d/)).
- Diamandis counters with his own definition: “technology as a force that converts what used to be scarce into abundance”. Both have stopped watching the news ([Deepak Chopra](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-23-the-secret-to-happiness-w-deepak-chopra-m-d/)).
- Lou Reese of Vaxxinity, where Diamandis is vice chairman, frames antibody drugs as a market problem: over $220 billion in sales, over $100,000 per patient per year, and reach of under 1% of the world. He pitches a much cheaper vaccine platform as the answer ([Lou Reese](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-21-the-vaccine-that-will-cure-chronic-diseases-w-lou-reese/)).
