## Overview

November split between AI and health. Emad Mostaque discussed AI governance in the middle of the OpenAI board crisis, and Sal Khan and Jacqueline Novogratz looked at AI tutors and the future of work. The rest of the month made Peter Diamandis's case for a healthspan revolution, ending with the launch of the $101 million XPRIZE Healthspan.

## Main themes

### Governing AI after the OpenAI board crisis

- Recorded during the turmoil on OpenAI's board, Emad Mostaque says “the biggest lesson is it's very hard to align humans”. He finds it anti-democratic that a few unelected people could steer a technology this powerful ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/who-will-govern-the-future-of-agi-w-emad-mostaque-stability-ai-founder/)).
- Mostaque argues the governance debate has been taken over by AGI doom talk. He wants transparent training data, data standards and nationally owned models, since regulated industries cannot run on black boxes ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/who-will-govern-the-future-of-agi-w-emad-mostaque-stability-ai-founder/)).
- He predicts GPT-4-level performance on a smartphone within 12 to 18 months. Viome's Guru Banavar gives a 50% chance of human-level AI by 2050, while Naveen Jain says around 2035 ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/who-will-govern-the-future-of-agi-w-emad-mostaque-stability-ai-founder/), [Viome](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/the-tech-that-will-prevent-and-reverse-chronic-disease-w-naveen-jain-g/)).

### AI tutors and the jobs that remain

- Sal Khan predicts every student will have a capable AI tutor within ten years. He says “the floor can be raised pretty significantly”, while also raising the ceiling for strong teachers ([Khan & Novogratz](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/how-ai-will-democratize-education-for-the-world-w-sal-khan-jacqueline/)).
- Khan says GPT-4 costs about $5-15 a month per Khanmigo user and expects that to fall sharply. He contrasts Khan Academy's $60 million budget with $5 trillion in global education spending ([Khan & Novogratz](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/how-ai-will-democratize-education-for-the-world-w-sal-khan-jacqueline/)).
- Khan now points to critical thinking and human connection as lasting skills, and Novogratz describes a caring economy. Mostaque predicts fewer graduate hires, says there will be no programmers in five years, and prefers universal basic jobs to UBI ([Khan & Novogratz](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/how-ai-will-democratize-education-for-the-world-w-sal-khan-jacqueline/), [Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/who-will-govern-the-future-of-agi-w-emad-mostaque-stability-ai-founder/)).

### The business case for healthspan

- In a solo talk, Diamandis names four forces behind a healthspan revolution, starting with capital: over $5 billion invested in longevity in 2022, which he expects to rise tenfold ([American Healthcare](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/the-truth-about-american-healthcare-ep-74/)).
- Diamandis cites a study valuing one extra year of healthspan at $38 trillion for the global economy, and calls longevity “the biggest business opportunity in the world” ([American Healthcare](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/the-truth-about-american-healthcare-ep-74/), [Jamie Justice](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/the-truth-about-aging-longevity-expert-debunks-common-misconceptions-w/)).
- Jamie Justice presents the $101 million XPRIZE Healthspan, which judges teams on restored function in older adults. She says regulation is the bottleneck: the FDA will engage but needs a consensus from the field ([Jamie Justice](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/the-truth-about-aging-longevity-expert-debunks-common-misconceptions-w/)).

### Consumer health data and wearables

- Naveen Jain says most healthcare spending goes on chronic disease. Viome built a direct-to-consumer business that doubles as a data engine, with Diamandis's fund as an investor ([Viome](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/the-tech-that-will-prevent-and-reverse-chronic-disease-w-naveen-jain-g/)).
- Oura CEO Tom Hale and researcher Rebecca Robbins argue sleep improves most through behaviour change, with wearables measuring deviations from each person's own baseline ([Oura](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/a-data-backed-approach-to-perfect-sleep-w-tom-hale-oura-ceo-dr-rebecca/)).
