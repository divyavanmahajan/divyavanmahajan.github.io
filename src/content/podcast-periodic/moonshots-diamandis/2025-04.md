## Overview

April drew heavily on more stage sessions from the 2025 Abundance Summit, led by two Vinod Khosla appearances on robots, fusion and nearly free expertise. Two news roundups, one introducing Dave Blundin, asked where value will settle as model labs leapfrog each other. The rest of the month profiled founders building in the physical world, from Travis Kalanick to Magic Johnson and Colossal's Ben Lamm.

## Main themes

### Robots and energy abundance

- Khosla repeats his forecast of a billion bipedal robots by 2040 and says the robot business will be “larger than the auto industry within 20 years and the auto industry doesn't know it” ([Khosla](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/ai-venture-capitalist-these-tech-predictions-will-change-everything-by/)).
- In a joint Q&A, Brett Adcock says Figure will not customise its chassis, because one platform protects reliability and gets costs to about $20,000. He says Figure already has demand for 100,000 robots and rules out military work ([Q&A](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/ai-entrepreneurs-q-a-how-every-industry-is-about-to-be-transformed-by/)).
- Khosla sees a path to 5,000 fusion plants by 2050 and expects fusion boilers to repower coal and gas plants. He argues permitting, not technology, slows fission ([Khosla](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/ai-venture-capitalist-these-tech-predictions-will-change-everything-by/), [Q&A](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/ai-entrepreneurs-q-a-how-every-industry-is-about-to-be-transformed-by/)).
- Khosla tells professionals to plan on five AI interns each, expects job displacement and wider income gaps, and says each country will need policies to share the gains ([Q&A](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/ai-entrepreneurs-q-a-how-every-industry-is-about-to-be-transformed-by/)).

### Where AI value lands

- Blundin says the AI revolution justifies OpenAI's $300 billion price but nobody knows if foundation models will win. Ismail says he would stay out, citing competition and a talent drain ([Apple's AI Crisis](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/apples-ai-crisis-chatgpts-world-domination-w-dave-blundin-salim-ismail/)).
- Ismail calls Apple's Siri problem “the standard big company innovator's dilemma”. Blundin sees lasting value in becoming the consumer's first stop and in vertical applications ([Apple's AI Crisis](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/apples-ai-crisis-chatgpts-world-domination-w-dave-blundin-salim-ismail/)).
- Diamandis and Ismail describe two leaderboards: Gemini 2.5 leads benchmarks while OpenAI leads revenue. They question Thinking Machines' $10 billion seed valuation and discuss the AI 2027 scenarios ([Bitcoin and AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/bitcoins-bull-run-the-ai-arms-race-what-you-need-to-know-w-salim-ismai/)).
- With Bitcoin back above $90,000, both hosts say they hold it long term ([Bitcoin and AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/bitcoins-bull-run-the-ai-arms-race-what-you-need-to-know-w-salim-ismai/)).

### Avatars as the face of agents

- HeyGen CEO Joshua Xu says “the most natural way to interact with agent will be a video format”. He expects businesses to make about 100x more video ([Avatars](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/the-future-of-ai-avatars-and-deepfake-technology-w-joshua-xu-steve-bro/)).
- Xu says every avatar needs first-party consent and that fraud and political campaigns are blocked. Diamandis advises families to agree a code word against deepfake scam calls ([Avatars](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/the-future-of-ai-avatars-and-deepfake-technology-w-joshua-xu-steve-bro/)).
- Steve Brown argues an avatar becomes useful only once trained on its owner's knowledge, and shows a personal health agent as an example ([Avatars](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/the-future-of-ai-avatars-and-deepfake-technology-w-joshua-xu-steve-bro/)).

### Building in the physical world

- Kalanick describes his career as digitising the physical world and defines innovation as progress divided by risk. He separates chatbots from AI that acts on atoms ([Kalanick](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/uber-founder-on-ai-risk-and-building-the-future-w-travis-kalanick-ep-1/)).
- Ben Lamm presents Colossal as a 50-year platform business valued at $10.2 billion, funded by spinouts, biodiversity credits and government work ([Colossal](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/why-this-billionaire-is-bringing-back-the-dire-wolf-woolly-mammoth-mor/)).
- Magic Johnson explains how proving that Starbucks could profit in underserved neighbourhoods built his business empire ([Magic Johnson](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/inside-magic-johnsons-billion-dollar-empire-ep-163/)).
- Matt Walker pitches Nightfall IQ, which applies AI analysis to wearable sleep data ([Sleep](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/sleep-scientist-this-is-what-poor-sleep-really-does-to-your-body-and-b/)).
