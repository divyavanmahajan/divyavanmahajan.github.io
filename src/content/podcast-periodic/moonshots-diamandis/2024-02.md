## Overview

February was the month Peter Diamandis and Salim Ismail started their regular news episodes, and three of the six episodes were theirs. AGI timelines, deepfakes, humanoid robots and Bitcoin ran through those conversations. Ray Kurzweil and Cathie Wood added long-range forecasts, and one episode turned to everyday health habits.

## Main themes

### AGI arrives sooner, and gets normalised

- Diamandis shows an ARK Invest chart in which AGI forecasts fell from 50 years away in 2020 to 8 years in 2023. He says that if the trend holds, “we're going to have it by 2027 and at the outmost it's 20 31” ([Deep Fakes and AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/taylor-swift-ai-deep-fakes-agi-sam-altman-the-latest-in-tech-w-salim-i/)).
- The hosts play a Sam Altman clip predicting AGI will come sooner than expected but change the world less at first. Ismail agrees, saying people normalise new technology very fast, and calls the question of whether we hit AGI trivial next to what we do with it ([Deep Fakes and AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/taylor-swift-ai-deep-fakes-agi-sam-altman-the-latest-in-tech-w-salim-i/)).
- Cathie Wood notes futurists' AGI forecasts dropped from 80 years away in 2019 to about eight, and says ARK estimates AI training costs fall 75% a year ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/the-coming-bitcoin-surge-w-cathie-wood-ep-88/)).
- Kurzweil repeats his timeline of the Turing test by 2029 and frames AI as an extension of the human mind, not a rival ([Kurzweil Q&A](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/ray-kurzweil-q-a-the-singularity-human-machine-integration-ai-ep-83/)).

### Deepfakes and AI for truth

- After the Taylor Swift deepfakes, Diamandis proposes four responses: harsh criminal penalties, white-hat AI entrepreneurs, blockchain metadata for authentication and a social default of disbelief ([Deep Fakes and AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/taylor-swift-ai-deep-fakes-agi-sam-altman-the-latest-in-tech-w-salim-i/)).
- Diamandis frames it as one technology with two names: “when something is uh is dystopian we call it a deep fake when it's useful we call it an avatar”. Ismail calls it an arms race with a dangerous gap in an election year ([Deep Fakes and AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/taylor-swift-ai-deep-fakes-agi-sam-altman-the-latest-in-tech-w-salim-i/)).
- Two weeks later the hosts discuss the FCC ban on AI robocalls and propose an XPRIZE for AI that classifies claims as fact, opinion or disinformation ([Vision Pro and Bitcoin](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/latest-ai-news-apple-vision-pro-bitcoin-surge-w-salim-ismail-ep-87/)).

### Humanoid robots and brain interfaces

- Diamandis, whose fund invested in Figure AI, describes its robot learning to make coffee by watching humans, and reports Microsoft and OpenAI were in talks to put $500 million into the company ([Neuralink](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/first-neuralink-implanted-where-other-tech-giants-are-headed-w-salim-i/)).
- He cites Figure's forecast of single-digit millions of humanoids by 2030. Ismail is sceptical of the human form and sees construction and dangerous or drudge work as the stronger cases ([Neuralink](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/first-neuralink-implanted-where-other-tech-giants-are-headed-w-salim-i/)).
- Both hosts are enthusiastic about brain-computer interfaces after Neuralink's first human implant, and both say they would get one ([Neuralink](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/first-neuralink-implanted-where-other-tech-giants-are-headed-w-salim-i/)).
- Wood expects generalisable robotics to be the fastest-growing segment, at more than 80% a year in market value ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/the-coming-bitcoin-surge-w-cathie-wood-ep-88/)).

### Bitcoin and converging technologies

- With Bitcoin near $50,000, Ismail recalls watching it rise from 5 cents and argues fiat systems grow on debt while technology is deflationary ([Vision Pro and Bitcoin](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/latest-ai-news-apple-vision-pro-bitcoin-surge-w-salim-ismail-ep-87/)).
- Wood calls Bitcoin a new asset class and repeats ARK's bull case of $1.5 million by 2027. She argues passive investing has left markets blind to converging technologies such as robotaxis and AI ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/the-coming-bitcoin-surge-w-cathie-wood-ep-88/)).
- The same news episode covers Apple Vision Pro, with about 700 native apps, and Sam Altman's reported $7 trillion chip-and-AI raise. Diamandis advises every company to appoint a chief AI officer ([Vision Pro and Bitcoin](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/latest-ai-news-apple-vision-pro-bitcoin-surge-w-salim-ismail-ep-87/)).
