## Overview

Moonshots spent 2024 moving between AI forecasts and Bitcoin, with Salim Ismail as Peter Diamandis's regular co-host. Elon Musk appeared three times and was discussed in a third of the episodes. Humanoid robots and AI's effect on jobs grew from a side topic into a steady thread. Later in the year the show turned to quantum technology, the US election and the open-source AI debate.

## Main themes

### Bitcoin and converging technologies

- Ismail recalls watching Bitcoin rise from 5 cents to $50,000 as it crossed that mark in February ([EP #87](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/latest-ai-news-apple-vision-pro-bitcoin-surge-w-salim-ismail-ep-87/)).
- Cathie Wood argues AI, robotaxis, robots, multi-omics and Bitcoin are converging and that passive investing has misallocated capital away from them ([The Coming Bitcoin Surge](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/the-coming-bitcoin-surge-w-cathie-wood-ep-88/)).
- Michael Saylor wants companies to move treasuries from cash into Bitcoin ([Michael Saylor](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/the-future-of-bitcoin-w-michael-saylor-2024-ep-92/)), and Bill Barhydt sees US politics turning friendlier to crypto ([Bill Barhydt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/08/bitcoin-the-us-election-and-ai-w-bill-barhydt-ep-113/)).

### Musk, AGI timelines and safety

- Musk says AI developers “should uh aim for maximum truth seeking and and curiosity” rather than program morality ([EP #79](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/elon-musk-on-abundance-agi-and-the-media-in-2024-ep-79-x-spaces/)).
- In March he predicts AI smarter than any human by the end of 2025 ([EP #91](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/elon-musk-on-agi-safety-superintelligence-and-neuralink-2024-ep-91/)); in November he says AI is improving about 10x a year ([EP #129](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/elons-predictions-for-the-future-ai-free-energy-and-more-ep-129/)).
- Ray Kurzweil holds to AGI by 2029 ([EP #125](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/the-man-who-predicted-agi-decades-ago-w-ray-kurzweil-ep-125/)), while he and Geoffrey Hinton split on merging and open-sourcing ([Kurzweil and Hinton](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/ray-kurzweil-geoff-hinton-debate-the-future-of-ai-ep-95/)).

### Humanoid robots and jobs

- Diamandis bets on billions of humanoid robots; Ismail expects construction and specific use cases to matter more ([EP #85](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/02/first-neuralink-implanted-where-other-tech-giants-are-headed-w-salim-i/)).
- On Klarna's support chats, Ismail says “I think AIS are ripe to take over a lot of that” ([EP #89](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/bitcoin-surge-ai-job-loss-2024-agi-predictions-w-salim-ismail-ep-89/)).
- Brett Adcock says reliability, not volume, is the hardest problem for Figure ([Brett Adcock](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/08/the-man-taking-on-tesla-in-the-race-for-humanoid-robots-w-brett-adcock/)); Emad Mostaque compares cheap agents and robots to a new continent of workers ([EP #114](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/08/humanoid-robots-the-job-market-mass-automation-the-current-state-of-ai/)).

### Open versus centralised AI

- Mostaque leaves Stability AI to push decentralised, nationally owned AI before centralised labs set the norms ([EP #93](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/why-im-leaving-my-company-immediately-stability-ai-w-emad-mostaque-ep/)).
- Eric Schmidt expects a few frontier models to be heavily regulated ([Eric Schmidt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/05/how-governments-should-handle-ai-policy-deepfakes-w-eric-schmidt-ep-99/)); Mark Surman wants open, public AI as shared infrastructure ([Mark Surman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/should-ai-be-open-sourced-the-debate-that-will-shape-everything-w-mark/)).
- Kai-Fu Lee argues cheap inference, not bigger models, will unlock applications ([Kai-Fu Lee](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/ex-google-china-president-on-how-china-is-shaping-the-future-of-ai-w-k/)).

### Quantum technology and large quantitative models

- Jack Hidary says “LQMs are about starting with equations to generate data”, aiming at parts of the economy language models cannot reach ([EP #123](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/how-quantum-ai-will-shape-the-worlds-future-w-jack-hidary-ep-123/)).
- He presents SandboxAQ's quantum sensors and post-quantum security as businesses already selling today ([EP #124](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/the-world-changing-technology-everyone-is-missing-w-jack-hidary-ep-124/)).
