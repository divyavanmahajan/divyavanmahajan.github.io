## Overview

July on Moonshots turned from AI's effect on jobs towards safety, regulation and geopolitics. Emad Mostaque and Mo Gawdat returned with views on how AI could go wrong and what data it should learn from, while Gilman Louie framed AI as a long contest between the US and China. Ben Lamm's Colossal episode was the month's business case study.

## Main themes

### AI safety starts with the data

- Emad Mostaque calls himself a pessimist at the core. He warns that the number of companies able to train a GPT-4 class model will go from two to 20 next year “and there's no guard rails” ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/outcomes-of-an-ai-future-w-emad-mostaque-ep-55/)).
- Mostaque argues models amplify the junk of the internet. He wants transparent pre-training data and a national dataset for every country, and puts the window to act at a couple of years ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/outcomes-of-an-ai-future-w-emad-mostaque-ep-55/)).
- He finds the pivotal action idea, where a good AI stops any other AGI, terrifying ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/outcomes-of-an-ai-future-w-emad-mostaque-ep-55/)).
- Mo Gawdat expects excitement, then panic after a patient zero event, then a contested long term. He believes smarter beings become more pro-life and urges billionaires to fund AI safety ([Mo Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/solving-the-sadness-epidemic-w-mo-gawdat-ep-56/)).

### The US-China contest and policy speed

- Gilman Louie says China set a goal to lead AI by 2030 and credits it with applying AI faster than the US. He argues the aim is to lead, not to win ([Gilman Louie](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/the-coming-global-ai-conflict-w-gilman-louie-ep-54/)).
- Louie says Brussels, Washington and Beijing all regulate AI the wrong way. He wants governments to speed up, not pause, and AI companies to self-regulate ([Gilman Louie](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/the-coming-global-ai-conflict-w-gilman-louie-ep-54/)).
- His $500 million America's Frontier Fund backs company building in frontier technologies, aiming beyond the coastal hubs ([Gilman Louie](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/the-coming-global-ai-conflict-w-gilman-louie-ep-54/)).
- Both Gawdat and Diamandis say political leaders are not keeping up with AI ([Mo Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/solving-the-sadness-epidemic-w-mo-gawdat-ep-56/)).

### Generative AI and Hollywood

- Recorded during the Screen Actors Guild strike, Mostaque says the whole media industry “is about to be disrupted because the cost of production reduces” ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/outcomes-of-an-ai-future-w-emad-mostaque-ep-55/)).
- He predicts personalised movies in three to five years and warns that Chinese studios will move faster if Hollywood refuses AI ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/outcomes-of-an-ai-future-w-emad-mostaque-ep-55/)).
- He expects no coder to work without AI and forecasts a trillion dollars flowing into the sector ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/outcomes-of-an-ai-future-w-emad-mostaque-ep-55/)).

### Happiness and human skills

- Gawdat defines happiness as events minus expectations and sees it as a default state that consumer culture teaches people to chase ([Mo Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/solving-the-sadness-epidemic-w-mo-gawdat-ep-56/)).
- His 1 Billion Happy mission spreads person to person and he counts about 50 million people reached who acted on it ([Mo Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/solving-the-sadness-epidemic-w-mo-gawdat-ep-56/)).
- For children he names hands-on AI, human connection, resilience and living in the present; Diamandis adds curiosity and exponential thinking ([Mo Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/solving-the-sadness-epidemic-w-mo-gawdat-ep-56/)).

### A moonshot as a business

- Ben Lamm presents Colossal as a systems problem a software entrepreneur can lead. He reports $225 million raised at about a $1.5 billion valuation ([Ben Lamm](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/how-ai-is-helping-solve-extinction-w-ben-lamm-ep-53/)).
- Revenue comes from technology spin-outs, education and large government conservation contracts. The team uses LLMs for literature review ([Ben Lamm](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/how-ai-is-helping-solve-extinction-w-ben-lamm-ep-53/)).
