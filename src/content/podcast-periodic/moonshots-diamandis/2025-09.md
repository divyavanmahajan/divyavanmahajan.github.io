## Overview

September on Moonshots treated frontier AI as a hardware race, with Nvidia, xAI and OpenAI competing for chips, fabs and power. The hosts paired that with two coding-platform founders and a long look at what AI does to entry-level jobs. A recurring line was that everything is getting abundant except compute.

## Main themes

### The compute race: chips, fabs and power

- Dave Blundin read Nvidia's investment in Intel, after a 10% US government stake, as a rescue of the only truly domestic leading-edge fab. He said Nvidia “needs chip manufacturing” because fabs constrain all chip creation ([Nvidia and Intel](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/nvidia-the-us-government-just-bailed-out-intel-sending-the-stock-soari/)).
- The panel discussed Nvidia's planned $100 billion investment in OpenAI for 10 gigawatts of compute; Blundin noted it equals half of annual US venture funding ([Latest in AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/the-latest-in-ai-job-loss-elon-sam-altman-chip-race-the-ai-bubble-w-br/)).
- OpenAI news included a planned 1-gigawatt India data center and a 3-nanometer chip run with Broadcom ([Reid Hoffman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/linkedin-co-founder-opens-up-on-ai-job-loss-w-reid-hoffman-dave-salim/)).
- Peter Diamandis cited data center capacity rising from 44 gigawatts to 156 by 2030, and Emad Mostaque predicted large solar and battery buildouts ([Latest in AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/the-latest-in-ai-job-loss-elon-sam-altman-chip-race-the-ai-bubble-w-br/)).

### Elon Musk and xAI's winner-take-all bet

- Diamandis described xAI's Colossus 2 as a 1-gigawatt Memphis site built for 500,000 Blackwell GPUs. Alex Wissner-Gross called the race Sutton's bitter lesson applied to hardware ([Musk's Master Plan](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/ai-insiders-reveal-elon-musks-master-plan-to-win-ai-w-dave-blundin-ale/)).
- Blundin argued the race is all or nothing: “You don't build the second biggest data center” ([Musk's Master Plan](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/ai-insiders-reveal-elon-musks-master-plan-to-win-ai-w-dave-blundin-ale/)).
- The hosts also covered cheap Grok Code Fast 1 pricing as a threat to SaaS incumbents, and Musk's claim that xAI will be first to 10 gigawatts ([Musk's Master Plan](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/ai-insiders-reveal-elon-musks-master-plan-to-win-ai-w-dave-blundin-ale/), [Latest in AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/the-latest-in-ai-job-loss-elon-sam-altman-chip-race-the-ai-bubble-w-br/)).

### AI and entry-level jobs

- Reid Hoffman expects script-following work to be automated but still sees strong demand for software engineers. Salim Ismail reported entry-level software jobs in India down about 20 to 25% ([Reid Hoffman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/linkedin-co-founder-opens-up-on-ai-job-loss-w-reid-hoffman-dave-salim/)).
- Blundin agreed that job creation wins in the long run but warned the timeline is short, so new graduates are hit hardest as firms like Salesforce cut hiring early ([Reid Hoffman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/linkedin-co-founder-opens-up-on-ai-job-loss-w-reid-hoffman-dave-salim/)).
- Later in the month Brian Elliott said firms will choose between full-time humans and no humans, and the hosts doubted CEO talk of a three-day week ([Latest in AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/the-latest-in-ai-job-loss-elon-sam-altman-chip-race-the-ai-bubble-w-br/)).

### Autonomous coding for enterprises and everyone

- Blitzy's Elliott and Sid Pardeshi described a platform that ingests 100-million-line codebases, does about 80% of the work and hands the rest to developers. Pardeshi claimed 86.8% on SWE-bench Verified ([Blitzy founders](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/the-state-of-ai-elons-1t-package-apples-600b-for-trump-how-startups-wi/)).
- Their advice to startups was to build on the frontier labs and be a large paying customer of them ([Blitzy founders](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/the-state-of-ai-elons-1t-package-apples-600b-for-trump-how-startups-wi/)).
- Amjad Masad previewed Replit's Agent 3, which builds other agents and ran for 4.5 hours in a test. He cited 500 licences at Zillow after one product manager's vibe-coded success ([Replit](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/replit-ceo-on-vibe-coding-and-the-future-of-software-development-w-amj/)).

### Bubble or not, and the robot stage

- Blundin compared Cisco in 2000 with Nvidia, whose price tracks forward earnings. Mostaque argued every GPU OpenAI uses will be booked out ([Latest in AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/the-latest-in-ai-job-loss-elon-sam-altman-chip-race-the-ai-bubble-w-br/)).
- Humanoid robots came up in four episodes. Wissner-Gross argued real economic growth likely passes through a humanoid stage, and Blundin would first build “the robot that's manufacturing the robot” ([Nvidia and Intel](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/nvidia-the-us-government-just-bailed-out-intel-sending-the-stock-soari/)).
