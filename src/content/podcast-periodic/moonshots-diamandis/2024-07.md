## Overview

July on Moonshots opened with Cathie Wood's market views on AI, robots and Bitcoin, then turned to personal health. Dave Asprey and Tonal CEO Krystal Zell each described businesses that use data and AI to personalise health and training. Jeff Karp closed the month on how to turn academic research into companies, shaped by his own experience of ADHD.

## Main themes

### Cathie Wood on AI incumbents and robots

- Wood says Apple's management turnover and its retreat from autonomous vehicles were ARK's “first clue that, you know, AI was a tough nut for Apple to crack” ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/cathie-wood-on-the-bitcoin-halving-apple-open-ai-nvdias-dominance-w-ca/)).
- She questions whether Nvidia can defend gross margins in the high 70s when its biggest customers are hyperscalers designing their own chips. Diamandis sees the Apple and OpenAI partnership as a direct challenge to Google search ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/cathie-wood-on-the-bitcoin-halving-apple-open-ai-nvdias-dominance-w-ca/)).
- Wood sizes humanoid robots as a $25 trillion market, split between manufacturing and non-manufacturing. Diamandis cites forecasts of one to ten billion humanoids by 2040 from Vinod Khosla and Brett Adcock ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/cathie-wood-on-the-bitcoin-halving-apple-open-ai-nvdias-dominance-w-ca/)).

### Bitcoin and ARK's bets

- ARK's on-chain analytics suggest the Bitcoin bull market is about halfway through. Its 2030 base case is around $650,000 per coin and its bull case is over $1.5 million ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/cathie-wood-on-the-bitcoin-halving-apple-open-ai-nvdias-dominance-w-ca/)).
- The case assumes Bitcoin takes 50% of gold's role, helped by wealth platforms listing spot ETFs ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/cathie-wood-on-the-bitcoin-halving-apple-open-ai-nvdias-dominance-w-ca/)).
- Wood says ARK organises analysts across industries rather than by sector, runs a small venture fund with a position in xAI, and is bringing its education initiative into Pinellas County middle schools ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/cathie-wood-on-the-bitcoin-halving-apple-open-ai-nvdias-dominance-w-ca/)).

### Personalised health as a business

- Dave Asprey frames biohacking as measuring what works for one person rather than following population averages. His advice is to drop anything that does not measurably work ([Asprey](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/the-5-biohacks-anyone-can-implement-to-live-longer-w-dave-asprey-ep-10/)).
- Asprey runs Upgrade Labs and 40 Years of Zen, a five-day executive programme in Seattle that he says “1500 elite executives have come through” ([Asprey](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/the-5-biohacks-anyone-can-implement-to-live-longer-w-dave-asprey-ep-10/)).
- Diamandis pitches an AI at Fountain Life that turns a person's full health data into a personal regimen. He and Asprey both criticise FDA limits, and Diamandis proposes an accredited patient model ([Asprey](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/the-5-biohacks-anyone-can-implement-to-live-longer-w-dave-asprey-ep-10/)).
- Krystal Zell says Tonal has more than 150,000 members and millions of workouts. It sells hardware plus a $59.95 monthly subscription and is testing a conversational AI coach called Tony ([Tonal](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/how-building-muscle-can-add-years-to-your-life-ozempics-long-term-effe/)).

### Longevity as the month's thread

- Longevity came up in three of four episodes, from Wood's biotech picks to Asprey's training claims and Zell's pitch for strength training ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/cathie-wood-on-the-bitcoin-halving-apple-open-ai-nvdias-dominance-w-ca/), [Asprey](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/the-5-biohacks-anyone-can-implement-to-live-longer-w-dave-asprey-ep-10/), [Tonal](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/how-building-muscle-can-add-years-to-your-life-ozempics-long-term-effe/)).
- Diamandis cites Ray Kurzweil's 2030 forecast for longevity escape velocity and his own Healthspan XPRIZE ([Tonal](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/how-building-muscle-can-add-years-to-your-life-ozempics-long-term-effe/)).

### From lab to company

- Jeff Karp, a Harvard and MIT bioengineer who has co-founded 13 companies, says a product-ready problem definition must also cover manufacturing, patents, regulation, reimbursement and financing ([Karp](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/how-to-be-insanely-productive-even-with-adhd-w-dr-jeff-karp-ep-111/)).
- For 10 years he met patent lawyers, regulatory experts and manufacturers every two to three weeks as an informal advisory board. He argues for radical simplicity to cut regulatory risk ([Karp](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/how-to-be-insanely-productive-even-with-adhd-w-dr-jeff-karp-ep-111/)).
- Karp ties his methods to growing up with undiagnosed ADHD and building his own tools. His book LIT draws on 40 interviews ([Karp](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/07/how-to-be-insanely-productive-even-with-adhd-w-dr-jeff-karp-ep-111/)).
