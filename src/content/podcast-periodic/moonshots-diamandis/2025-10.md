## Overview

October on Moonshots followed the money behind AI: data center capex, chip deals and the debt that funds them, with OpenAI at the centre of most episodes. The hosts also argued that math is close to being solved and that the singularity is already under way. Two guest episodes, with Circle's Jeremy Allaire and Solana's Anatoly Yakovenko, made the case for stablecoins as the money layer for an agent economy.

## Main themes

### Capex, chips and how compute is financed

- Dave Blundin said OpenAI's reported $100 billion Nvidia deal is a lease because Sam Altman lacks the cash. Alex Wissner-Gross saw an “impedance mismatch between selling high margin GPUs and low to negative margin neocloud and cloud businesses” rather than a credit bubble ([The AI War](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/the-ai-war-openai-ads-sora-2-grok-partners-with-us-government-googles/)).
- The AMD deal for 6 gigawatts of GPUs could give OpenAI about 10% of AMD. Emad Mostaque put buildout at about $50 billion per gigawatt, and Blundin named chip fab capacity as the hard ceiling ([Everything App](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/openai-vs-grok-the-race-to-build-the-everything-app-w-emad-mostaque-da/)).
- Meta's SPV is borrowing $27 billion for a Louisiana data center. Wissner-Gross described credit funding the lower half of the AI stack and equity the upper half ([ChatGPT Atlas](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/the-openai-internet-browser-has-arrived-chatgpt-atlas-w-dave-blundin-a/)).

### OpenAI's race to own attention and commerce

- Blundin expects Google's roughly $300 billion ad business to move into AI conversations, and warned that data center costs push labs toward aggressive advertising. Diamandis welcomed instant checkout in ChatGPT with Stripe ([The AI War](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/the-ai-war-openai-ads-sora-2-grok-partners-with-us-government-googles/)).
- After Dev Day, Mostaque framed apps inside ChatGPT as a WeChat-style super-app battle. Blundin called node-and-line agent builders a transitional design ([Everything App](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/openai-vs-grok-the-race-to-build-the-everything-app-w-emad-mostaque-da/)).
- Wissner-Gross read Sora 2 and Meta Vibes as a shift from algorithmic content selection to generation; Diamandis stressed that both are free ([The AI War](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/the-ai-war-openai-ads-sora-2-grok-partners-with-us-government-googles/)).
- Blundin compared Altman with Bill Gates after the Atlas browser launch, a larger installed base defended by devices, data centers and now a browser ([ChatGPT Atlas](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/the-openai-internet-browser-has-arrived-chatgpt-atlas-w-dave-blundin-a/)).

### Math, the singularity and AGI timelines

- Wissner-Gross said he had seen no sign that “math is not going to be solved in the next few months” ([The AI War](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/the-ai-war-openai-ads-sora-2-grok-partners-with-us-government-googles/)).
- He noted GPT-5 Pro's 13% on Frontier Math Tier 4, past his 10% threshold, and argued the singularity feels smooth from the inside. Blundin highlighted OpenAI models helping with chip layouts ([Singularity](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/the-singularity-is-here-ai-is-solving-math-sora-outpaces-chat-gpt-ai-i/)).
- At XPRIZE Visioneering, Diamandis set Aleksander Madry's 2026 AGI view against Andrej Karpathy's 10 years, while Salim Ismail said the term has no agreed test ([This Week in AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/this-week-in-ai-nvidias-most-powerful-chip-robotics-reach-a-new-milest/)).

### Stablecoins as money for agents

- Allaire described USDC as fully reserved and said a regulated stablecoin inherits central bank monetary policy rather than replacing it. He expects most stablecoin transactions to be AI-mediated within five years ([Stablecoins](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/money-after-ai-meet-the-new-digital-dollar-built-for-the-internet-stab/)).
- Yakovenko pitched Solana as one execution layer for global markets and expects stablecoin legislation to speed adoption ([Solana](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/solana-founder-crypto-is-about-to-change-finance-like-the-internet-cha/)).

### Robots and jobs

- The panel covered Figure 03, Unitree's prices and Amazon's 66-fold robot fleet growth; Ismail doubted home robots can handle the edge cases soon ([This Week in AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/this-week-in-ai-nvidias-most-powerful-chip-robotics-reach-a-new-milest/)).
- Ismail noted that Sweden, South Korea and Germany combine high robot density with low unemployment, while Mostaque expects cognitive labour to lose economic value within about three years ([This Week in AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/this-week-in-ai-nvidias-most-powerful-chip-robotics-reach-a-new-milest/)).
- OpenAI reportedly pays bankers to train models on deal work, which Blundin expects in every field ([ChatGPT Atlas](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/the-openai-internet-browser-has-arrived-chatgpt-atlas-w-dave-blundin-a/)).
