## Overview

In 2023 Moonshots turned into a show about AI. After GPT-4 arrived in March, the conversation moved to jobs, open models and who should govern AI. Peter Diamandis and Salim Ismail spent much of the year on the Exponential Organizations 2.0 playbook. Longevity and healthspan ran through the whole year and took over the final months.

## Main themes

### AI, jobs and the case for optimism

- Salim Ismail notes that “the first article that came out saying that robots are going to take all the jobs in 5 years appeared in 1964” ([How AI Will Change Business Forever](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/how-ai-will-change-business-forever-w-salim-ismail-ep-46/)).
- Mo Gawdat says the near-term danger is human greed, not superintelligence, and that AI learns its values from how people behave ([Mo Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/ai-experts-urgent-wake-up-call-unveiling-the-silent-threat-w-mo-gawdat/)).
- Diamandis debated a pessimistic AI persona and his own AI clone on job loss ([Doomsday AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/09/1-on-1-with-a-doomsday-ai-ais-worst-case-scenario-ep-63/), [AI Clone](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/09/a-conversation-with-my-ai-clone-on-the-future-of-ai-ep-62/)).
- Andrew Yang backs cash transfers to cushion AI job losses ([Andrew Yang](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/how-ai-is-impacting-politics-accelerating-the-need-for-ubi-w-andrew-ya/)).

### Open models and who governs AI

- Emad Mostaque signed the pause letter and called the following six months the last window for public debate on frontier AI ([Stability AI Founder](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/04/ep-36-stability-ai-founder-halting-ai-training-is-hyper-critical-for-o/)).
- During the OpenAI board crisis he called for open, nationally owned models instead of unelected boards ([Who Will Govern AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/who-will-govern-the-future-of-agi-w-emad-mostaque-stability-ai-founder/)).
- Gilman Louie sees the US-China contest as a long game over whose values go into AI ([Global AI Conflict](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/07/the-coming-global-ai-conflict-w-gilman-louie-ep-54/)); Palmer Luckey built Anduril around broken defense procurement ([Palmer Luckey](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/04/ep-37-how-the-founder-of-oculus-plans-to-save-america-w-palmer-luckey/)).

### Exponential organizations and purpose

- Diamandis says “the only kind of organizations that going to survive this decade are exponential organizations” ([10x Your Business](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/10x-your-business-why-transitioning-from-linear-to-exponential-is-cruc/)).
- He and Ismail call a massive transformative purpose the trait all fast-scaling companies share ([Success Guaranteed](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/success-guaranteed-what-all-ultra-successful-founders-or-companies-hav/)), the start of their 12-step playbook ([Don't Fall Behind](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/09/dont-fall-behind-in-the-ai-and-tech-revolution-exponential-organizatio/)).
- They say they had to rewrite large parts of the book after ChatGPT launched ([How AI Will Change Business Forever](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/how-ai-will-change-business-forever-w-salim-ismail-ep-46/)).

### Longevity and healthspan

- Steven Kotler and Diamandis argue that mindset and lifestyle shape healthspan more than genes ([Steven Kotler](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/03/ep-34-the-mindset-that-extends-your-life-w-steven-kotler/)).
- David Sinclair predicts age reversal will become the century's dominant pharmaceutical industry ([David Sinclair](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/aging-is-now-optional-w-david-sinclair-ep-60/)). Jamie Justice and Diamandis launch the $101 million XPRIZE Healthspan ([Jamie Justice](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/11/the-truth-about-aging-longevity-expert-debunks-common-misconceptions-w/)).
- In December, Fountain Life's Helen Messier presents diet and exercise as the cheapest levers ([Helen Messier](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/the-simple-exercise-routine-that-will-extend-your-life-w-helen-messier/)).

### Moonshot culture and abundance

- Astro Teller says X wins through cheap, honest experiments and rewards people for killing bad ideas: “if your pride is going to be connected to being right you have a 99% chance of being miserable at X” ([Astro Teller's AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/01/ep-24-astro-tellers-ama-allocating-resources-for-audacious-ideas/)).
- Marian Tupy and Gale Pooley argue resources grow more abundant faster than population ([Overpopulation](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/03/ep-31-the-truth-about-overpopulation-w-dr-marian-l-tupy-dr-gale-l-pool/)).
