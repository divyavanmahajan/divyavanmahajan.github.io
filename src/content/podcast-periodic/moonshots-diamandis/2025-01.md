## Overview

January on Moonshots was dominated by the change of US administration and what it could mean for AI, crypto, energy and space. Marc Benioff made the case for AI agents as digital labour, and Emad Mostaque explained the DeepSeek shock at the end of the month. Neil deGrasse Tyson, Mary Lou Jepsen and Steven Kotler added episodes on exponential thinking, cheaper medical devices and wildfires.

## Main themes

### AI agents and jobs

- Benioff says agents now resolve about 31,000 of the 36,000 weekly inquiries on help.salesforce.com, and he plans to move a couple of thousand of his 9,000 support staff into other jobs ([Benioff](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/salesforce-founder-gives-the-truth-on-ai-agents-w-marc-benioff-ep-141/)).
- He frames today as “humans with agents working together” and treats agents and robots as digital labour, a response to declining birth rates and labour shortages. He still wants 2,000 more salespeople ([Benioff](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/salesforce-founder-gives-the-truth-on-ai-agents-w-marc-benioff-ep-141/)).
- Mostaque names business process outsourcing as the worst-case casualty for 2025 and advises remote workers to return to the office ([DeepSeek vs. OpenAI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/deepseek-vs-open-ai-the-state-of-ai-w-emad-mostaque-salim-ismail-ep-14/)).

### The new administration: policy, crypto and energy

- Salim Ismail predicts negotiated deals on major conflicts and a temporary housing-code change to speed rebuilding in LA. Diamandis expects a push to the Moon and Mars, biotech deregulation and a Bitcoin reserve ([New Administration](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/the-new-administration-means-major-change-for-crypto-ai-tech-w-salim-i/)).
- Ismail argues a proposed $20 billion US Bitcoin reserve should be half a trillion. The hosts disagree on energy: Ismail backs solar plus storage, Diamandis small modular reactors and fusion ([New Administration](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/the-new-administration-means-major-change-for-crypto-ai-tech-w-salim-i/)).
- Ben Horowitz calls it the most dramatic shift in tech policy he has seen, reopening M&A, crypto and AI startups. He wants small modular reactors next to data centres ([Horowitz](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/how-the-new-administration-will-impact-crypto-ai-tech-globally-w-ben-h/)).

### DeepSeek and the US-China AI race

- Mostaque says DeepSeek matched frontier models because chip limits forced an efficient engineering team. He argues cheaper intelligence raises demand and helps OpenAI and Nvidia rather than hurting them ([DeepSeek vs. OpenAI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/deepseek-vs-open-ai-the-state-of-ai-w-emad-mostaque-salim-ismail-ep-14/)).
- Mostaque says OpenAI made $3 billion in revenue last year and lost $5 billion, $3 billion of it on training. He argues for open, aligned models as public infrastructure ([DeepSeek vs. OpenAI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/deepseek-vs-open-ai-the-state-of-ai-w-emad-mostaque-salim-ismail-ep-14/)).
- Horowitz sees China's edge in merging state and private sector, and America's in bottom-up startups. Ismail counters that “when you have a government that won't allow you to break any rules, you can't do disruptive innovation” ([Horowitz](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/how-the-new-administration-will-impact-crypto-ai-tech-globally-w-ben-h/)).

### Humanoid robots and AGI timelines

- Diamandis says he tracks about 30 of roughly 100 humanoid robot companies and highlights Nvidia's Cosmos and GR00T announcements at CES. Ismail questions why robots must look human ([New Administration](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/the-new-administration-means-major-change-for-crypto-ai-tech-w-salim-i/)).
- Ismail asks people to define AGI before debating it, yet predicts it will be passed within six months. Diamandis says the goalposts keep moving ([New Administration](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/the-new-administration-means-major-change-for-crypto-ai-tech-w-salim-i/)).

### Space, wildfires and exponential thinking

- Tyson argues brains evolved for linear thinking and that big projects are funded by fear, money or rulers. He predicts the solar system opened up like an interstate system, with asteroid mining ending wars over resources ([Tyson](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/neil-degrasse-tyson-on-understanding-our-current-reality-xprize-vision/)).
- After the Palisades fire, Kotler and Diamandis argue Western wildfires are solvable with capital, scale and regulatory room. Diamandis describes the $11 million Wildfire XPRIZE ([Wildfires](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/the-wildfire-crisis-the-causes-consequences-and-solutions-w-steven-kot/)).
- Jepsen argues medical devices can follow the consumer-electronics cost curve, and that open-sourcing Openwater's platform spreads costs and builds trust ([Jepsen](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/she-left-google-to-build-tech-that-could-save-millions-w-mary-lou-jeps/)).
