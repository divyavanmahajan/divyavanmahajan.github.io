## Overview

December on Moonshots centred on jobs: what share of work AI can already do, and why most large companies are not yet using it. GPT-5.2's benchmark results anchored that debate, and the year closed with a predictions episode and a guest who argued enterprise adoption is harder than the benchmarks suggest. Robots, energy, US-China competition and a Mustafa Suleyman interview filled out the month.

## Main themes

### AI and jobs

- Diamandis cites a McKinsey claim that AI can already automate 57% of US work and an MIT estimate of 11.7% of the workforce. Ismail expects measured GDP to shrink; Naveen Jain expects people to do more ([AGI Timeline](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/our-updated-agi-timeline-57-job-automation-risk-and-solving-the-us-deb/)).
- Diamandis notes 1.1 million layoffs in 2025. Dave Blundin expects AI to be able to replace 80-90% of jobs, with panic by late 2026 ([GPT 5.2 Release](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/gpt-5-2-release-corporate-collapse-in-2026-and-1-1m-job-loss-w-alexand/)).
- Wissner-Gross advises aligning careers with the intelligence explosion ([AGI Timeline](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/our-updated-agi-timeline-57-job-automation-risk-and-solving-the-us-deb/)).

### GPT-5.2 and the benchmark debate

- Wissner-Gross reads GPT-5.2's GDPval score (70.9%, up from 38.8%) as evidence that knowledge work can be automated. He suspects the gains came from more compute and post-training on chosen benchmarks ([GPT 5.2 Release](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/gpt-5-2-release-corporate-collapse-in-2026-and-1-1m-job-loss-w-alexand/)).
- On FrontierMath Tier 4 he counts Gemini 3 Pro beating GPT-5.2 as a win for Google. Blundin thinks OpenAI is starved of compute ([GPT 5.2 Release](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/gpt-5-2-release-corporate-collapse-in-2026-and-1-1m-job-loss-w-alexand/)).
- Matt Fitzpatrick argues enterprises need custom evals comparing AI with expert humans. Blundin sees owning a niche benchmark as an opening ([New AI Benchmarks](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/why-we-need-new-ai-benchmarks-which-industries-survive-ai-and-recursiv/)).

### Incumbents stall; AI-native rewrites

- Ismail says maybe three of the roughly 20 large companies OpenExO works with are doing half of the right thing. Blundin advises rebuilding from scratch ([GPT 5.2 Release](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/gpt-5-2-release-corporate-collapse-in-2026-and-1-1m-job-loss-w-alexand/)).
- Ismail predicts digital transformation gives way to AI-native rewrites at the edge, warning: “AI won't destroy your company, but your org chart will if you don't do this” ([2026 Predictions](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/2026-predictions-ai-automates-knowledge-work-autonomous-robots-ai-ceo/)).
- Fitzpatrick's playbook: pick two or three use cases, fix the data, build a pilot. He uses the Klarna reversal to argue for a mix of humans and agents ([New AI Benchmarks](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/why-we-need-new-ai-benchmarks-which-industries-survive-ai-and-recursiv/)).

### Robots, energy and the US-China contest

- Jack Hidary says five robots-building-robots factories are under construction, two in the US and three in China. He expects energy scarcity to flip to abundance in seven to nine years ([Unlocking AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/unlocking-agi-how-life-changes-for-everyone-w-jack-hidary-salim-ismail/)).
- Diamandis cites China installing nine times as many industrial robots as the US ([AGI Timeline](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/our-updated-agi-timeline-57-job-automation-risk-and-solving-the-us-deb/)).
- Emad Mostaque says people do not trust China and are losing trust in the US week by week, and expects two competing tech stacks. Ismail calls a $300 billion Anthropic valuation cheap at 10x revenue ([US vs China](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/us-vs-china-why-trust-will-win-the-ai-race-gpt-5-2-anthropic-ipo-w-ema/)).

### Agents, containment and 2026 bets

- Mustafa Suleyman says “all of these user interfaces are going to get subsumed into a conversational agentic form.” He rejects a winner-takes-all AGI race and says superintelligence must be contained before alignment ([Suleyman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/mustafa-suleyman-the-agi-race-is-fake-building-safe-superintelligence/)).
- For 2026, Wissner-Gross predicts AI solves a Millennium Prize problem and GDPval passes 90%. Mostaque predicts a remote Turing test on Zoom calls ([2026 Predictions](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/2026-predictions-ai-automates-knowledge-work-autonomous-robots-ai-ceo/)).
- Diamandis bets Blue Origin lands cargo on the Moon before SpaceX, and expects longevity research to enter human trials ([2026 Predictions](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/2026-predictions-ai-automates-knowledge-work-autonomous-robots-ai-ceo/)).
