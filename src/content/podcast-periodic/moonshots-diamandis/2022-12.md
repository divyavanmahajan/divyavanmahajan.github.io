## Overview

December paired three two-part guest visits with a two-part listener AMA. Emad Mostaque made the case for open generative AI as public infrastructure, David Sinclair argued that aging should be treated as a disease, and Steve Aoki talked about purpose, cyborgs and sleep. Peter Diamandis closed the year answering questions on AI co-pilots, space and how to build a company.

## Main themes

### Generative AI as open infrastructure

- Emad Mostaque calls AI “infrastructure for the next generation of human thought” and argues that access to it should be a commons, not something a few corporations control ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-16-ai-is-creating-massive-entrepreneurial-opportunity-w-emad-mostaq/)).
- He says Stable Diffusion fits 2 billion images of training data into a 1.6 GB file that runs offline, and that generation sped up 30x after launch. Stability AI runs a 4,000-A100 cluster on Amazon and has about 120,000 people in its open communities ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-16-ai-is-creating-massive-entrepreneurial-opportunity-w-emad-mostaq/)).
- His advice to 20-year-olds is to drop everything for generative AI. He predicts $100 billion and then $1 trillion flowing into the sector, and wants companies to set up small, free teams ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-16-ai-is-creating-massive-entrepreneurial-opportunity-w-emad-mostaq/)).
- Both episodes point to education as a target: Mostaque proposes national AI tutors, and Diamandis argues schools should teach how to learn ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-16-ai-is-creating-massive-entrepreneurial-opportunity-w-emad-mostaq/), [Ask Peter Part 1](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-19-ask-peter-anything-part-1-our-future-in-space-successful-mindset/)).

### Longevity as a policy and investment question

- Sinclair says no drug can be approved for aging because aging is not a medical condition, and that this holds back investment. He wants public and political pressure to change it ([Sinclair AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-17-david-sinclairs-ama-age-reversal-breakthroughs-fda-approval-and/), [Aging is a Disease](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-18-why-aging-is-a-disease-w-david-sinclair/)).
- He points to drug-development cost, with companies able to spend $400-500 million and still fail. Diamandis argues regulators weigh the risk of approving a harmful drug far above the cost of delay ([Sinclair AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-17-david-sinclairs-ama-age-reversal-breakthroughs-fda-approval-and/)).
- Diamandis describes a planned, majority-funded $101 million Age Reversal XPRIZE with Sinclair and George Church as scientific co-chairs. Sinclair puts the long-run US value of one extra healthy year at $86 trillion ([Sinclair AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-17-david-sinclairs-ama-age-reversal-breakthroughs-fda-approval-and/)).
- Sinclair sees 150 years as reachable but not immortality. He expects accessible treatments in about 10 years, and says 18-to-30-year-olds are now the field's most passionate supporters ([Sinclair AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-17-david-sinclairs-ama-age-reversal-breakthroughs-fda-approval-and/), [Aging is a Disease](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-18-why-aging-is-a-disease-w-david-sinclair/)).

### Merging with machines

- Steve Aoki predicts “eventually we'll become Androids”, replacing failing parts with technology. Diamandis relays Ray Kurzweil's forecast of high-bandwidth brain-cloud interfaces in the early to mid 2030s ([Steve Aoki AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-14-steve-aokis-ama-a-djs-crazy-schedule-cryonics-to-live-forever-an/)).
- Aoki has signed up for cryopreservation with Alcor, and the two discuss the identity questions raised by brain uploading ([Steve Aoki AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-14-steve-aokis-ama-a-djs-crazy-schedule-cryonics-to-live-forever-an/)).
- Aoki says touring left him with very little REM sleep, and that wearable data pushed him to change. Diamandis calls sleep the single most important habit ([Sleep for Creatives](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-15-the-importance-of-sleep-for-creatives-w-steve-aoki/)).

### AI co-pilots, space and founder advice

- Diamandis says “all of us are going to have these AI co-pilots”, borrowing Reid Hoffman's phrase. He suggests diagnosing without AI in the loop will become malpractice ([Ask Peter Part 1](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-19-ask-peter-anything-part-1-our-future-in-space-successful-mindset/)).
- He calls spaceflight a barnstorming era like aviation before 1927, and calls Starship the first vehicle designed to land on the Moon and Mars ([Ask Peter Part 1](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-19-ask-peter-anything-part-1-our-future-in-space-successful-mindset/)).
- Mentoring five listeners, he pairs a bold purpose with early revenue, a narrow first niche and modest valuations, since down rounds are hard ([Ask Peter Part 2](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-20-ask-peter-anything-part-2-how-to-be-a-great-entrepreneur-leader/)).
