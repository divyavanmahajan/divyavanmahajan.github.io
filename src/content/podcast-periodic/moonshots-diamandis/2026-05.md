## Overview

May on Moonshots was dominated by GPT-5.5, which was central in four episodes, and by the money and compute flowing into Anthropic from Google, Amazon and SpaceX. The Moonshot Mates kept returning to one idea: demand for AI outruns compute supply, and that scarcity shapes lab strategy, earnings and government oversight. Salim Ismail used a solo-style episode to set out how companies should reorganise around AI.

## Main themes

### GPT-5.5 and the coding race

- Alexander Wissner-Gross reads GPT-5.5's jump on Terminal Bench 2.0 as a push for Codex, saying “it very much feels like a release that's intended to strengthen OpenAI's codecs”. The hosts note API pricing doubled versus 5.4 ([EP #252](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/google-invests-40b-into-anthropic-gpt-5-5-drops-and-google-cloud-domin/)).
- Brian Elliott claims GPT-5.5 matches Anthropic's Mythos ([EP 254](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/googles-record-quarter-the-white-house-intervenes-and-gpt-5-5-silently/)). It later scored 70% on Data Curve's DeepSWE against 54% for Claude Opus 4.7, and Dave Blundin says Opus uses about twice the tokens ([EP #259](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/pope-leo-vs-ai-gpt-5-5-beats-claude-and-sam-altman-walks-back-job-apoc/)).
- GPT-5.5 led the FutureSim forecasting benchmark and beat Polymarket on the Super Bowl, and an unreleased OpenAI model disproved an 80-year-old Erdős conjecture ([EP #257](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/spacex-75b-historic-ipo-gpt-5-5-outperforms-polymarket-and-ai-solves-8/)).

### Cash for compute, and compute that never catches up

- Google commits up to $40 billion to Anthropic at a $350 billion valuation plus 5 GW of TPUs, and Amazon adds $25 billion against Anthropic spending $100 billion or more on AWS. Blundin says TSMC is the constraint; Wissner-Gross points to permitting and powered land ([EP #252](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/google-invests-40b-into-anthropic-gpt-5-5-drops-and-google-cloud-domin/)).
- Blundin calls scarcity permanent: “This is not, you know, a temporary shortage.” Elliott says Google makes customers apply and queue for large compute ([EP 254](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/googles-record-quarter-the-white-house-intervenes-and-gpt-5-5-silently/)).
- Anthropic's lease of SpaceX's Colossus 1 let it double Claude Code rate limits, and Wissner-Gross infers xAI is turning SpaceX AI into a hyperscaler ([EP #255](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/anthropic-partners-with-spacex-ai-leopolds-5-5b-bet-and-the-singularit/)). The SpaceX IPO filing reads to him as an AI infrastructure company ([EP #257](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/spacex-75b-historic-ipo-gpt-5-5-outperforms-polymarket-and-ai-solves-8/)).

### Google and OpenAI change position

- Alphabet revenue rose 22% and Google Cloud 63%. Wissner-Gross calls OpenAI's consumer-first bet an error after reports of missed targets, and OpenAI ended Azure exclusivity ([EP 254](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/googles-record-quarter-the-white-house-intervenes-and-gpt-5-5-silently/)).
- At Google I/O the hosts saw strong distribution but fast-follower launches. Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman explained how a wafer-sized chip won on inference speed ([EP #256](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/google-i-o-2026-karpathy-joins-anthropic-and-cerebras-95b-ipo-ep-256/)).
- In Musk v. OpenAI, Blundin argues Musk only needs to slow OpenAI down ([EP #253](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/demis-hassabis-on-agi-robots-scale-production-and-elons-1t-mars-shot-c/)); Feldman later calls the verdict against Musk a distraction ([EP #256](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/google-i-o-2026-karpathy-joins-anthropic-and-cerebras-95b-ipo-ep-256/)).

### Pre-release vetting

- On a proposed White House review of frontier models, Wissner-Gross links the shift to Mythos. Elliott says government must preview models but not gatekeep, and Wissner-Gross worries more about labs self-policing ([EP 254](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/googles-record-quarter-the-white-house-intervenes-and-gpt-5-5-silently/)).
- By month end the planned 90-day review order had been pulled after pushback from David Sacks and others, and Pope Leo XIV's encyclical asked for regulation and a slower pace ([EP #259](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/pope-leo-vs-ai-gpt-5-5-beats-claude-and-sam-altman-walks-back-job-apoc/)).

### Reorganising work around AI

- Ismail defines the organizational singularity as organising a company around intelligence instead of hierarchy, built as an AI-native twin at the edge of the business. He expects middle-management coordination work to fall about 90% over a 5-7 year transition ([EP #258](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/the-organizational-singularity-ai-proof-your-company-ep-258/)).
- Wissner-Gross pressed Ismail for falsifiable benchmarks on his 100x claim ([EP #257](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/spacex-75b-historic-ipo-gpt-5-5-outperforms-polymarket-and-ai-solves-8/)).
- Sam Altman walked back his job-apocalypse warnings. The hosts argue the real effect so far is a freeze on hiring young people plus a startup boom ([EP #259](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/pope-leo-vs-ai-gpt-5-5-beats-claude-and-sam-altman-walks-back-job-apoc/)).
