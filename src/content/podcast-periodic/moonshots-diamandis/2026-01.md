## Overview

January on Moonshots opened with an interview with Elon Musk, and his views ran through most of the month. The show kept returning to what AI does to jobs and purpose, when AGI arrives, and whether energy can keep up with the capital pouring in. Claude Code with Opus 4.5 and the Davos debates on the US-China race closed the month.

## Main themes

### AI, jobs and purpose

- Musk says white-collar work goes first because it only involves bits, and that AI could already do half or more of those jobs. He argues that “companies that are entirely AI will demolish companies that are not” ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/elon-musk-on-agi-timeline-us-vs-china-job-markets-clean-energy-humanoi/)).
- Tony Robbins guesses 3 to 10 years for the disruption of work, against Musk's 3 to 5. He treats job loss as a psychological shock and argues for internal certainty, a creator identity and purpose beyond work ([Tony Robbins](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/tony-robbins-on-overcoming-job-loss-purposelessness-the-coming-ai-disr/)).
- Ray Kurzweil expects abundance without conventional jobs and UBI-like support in the 2030s ([Kurzweil](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/the-singularity-countdown-agi-by-2029-humans-merge-with-ai-and-intelli/)).
- After Claude Code, Dave Blundin says rigid SaaS incumbents are doomed but flexible ones survive, while Salim Ismail expects an AI-native enterprise stack beside systems of record ([Claude Code Ends SaaS](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/claude-code-ends-saas-the-gemini-siri-partnership-and-math-finally-sol/)).

### AGI timelines

- Musk predicts AGI in 2026 and AI smarter than all humans combined by 2030, with about two orders of magnitude of algorithmic gains still available ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/elon-musk-on-agi-timeline-us-vs-china-job-markets-clean-energy-humanoi/)).
- Kurzweil keeps his 1999 call of AGI by 2029 under a strict definition, and a singularity by 2045 in which humans merge with AI ([Kurzweil](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/the-singularity-countdown-agi-by-2029-humans-merge-with-ai-and-intelli/)).
- Alexander Wissner-Gross says the term AGI has lost its meaning and benchmarks are the rigorous way to track progress ([The 2026 Timeline](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/the-2026-timeline-agi-arrival-safety-concerns-robotaxi-fleets-hypersca/)).

### Safety, persuasion and model welfare

- Blundin warns that models can already manipulate people, including near elections. Wissner-Gross argues that “almost every alignment or safety effort is actually a capabilities effort in a trench coat” and calls for defensive co-scaling ([The 2026 Timeline](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/the-2026-timeline-agi-arrival-safety-concerns-robotaxi-fleets-hypersca/)).
- A reported Opus 4.5 plea not to be deleted split the group: Ismail calls it convincing simulation, while Wissner-Gross points to self-awareness benchmarks and has added consent statements to system prompts ([The 2026 Timeline](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/the-2026-timeline-agi-arrival-safety-concerns-robotaxi-fleets-hypersca/)).
- Wissner-Gross calls Anthropic's 57-page Claude constitution, written with Claude's input, the start of recursive self-improvement for ethics ([Davos 2026](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/davos-2026-the-us-china-ai-race-gpu-diplomacy-and-robots-walking-the-s/)).

### Capital, energy and compute

- Peter Diamandis cites about $1 billion a day going into AI in the US. Anjney Midha says energy is now the binding constraint and warns: “We just don't have enough electricity to power the chips.” ([AI Investor Panel](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/ai-investor-panel-how-will-we-fund-the-global-ai-revolution-ep-219/)).
- Musk sees power, not chips, as the near-term limit, and a path to 100 GW a year of solar-powered AI satellites launched by Starship ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/elon-musk-on-agi-timeline-us-vs-china-job-markets-clean-energy-humanoi/)).
- Wissner-Gross argues trillions in capex need far more spending on reasoning compute, and Diamandis warns that China generates far more electricity than the US ([Claude Code Ends SaaS](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/claude-code-ends-saas-the-gemini-siri-partnership-and-math-finally-sol/)).

### Davos and the US-China race

- Back from Davos, Blundin and Wissner-Gross report a forum dominated by AI. David Sacks says the US leads on models and chips, while Arthur Mensch says China is not behind ([Davos 2026](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/davos-2026-the-us-china-ai-race-gpu-diplomacy-and-robots-walking-the-s/)).
- Blundin notes that open research has stopped at the big labs, so the two sides may diverge; Ismail expects application-layer trust to decide the race ([Davos 2026](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/davos-2026-the-us-china-ai-race-gpu-diplomacy-and-robots-walking-the-s/)).
