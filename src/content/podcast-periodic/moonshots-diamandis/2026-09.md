## Overview

September brought fifteen episodes, including two listener AMAs and recordings from the first Moonshots Live. OpenAI and Anthropic dominated the news, first with GPT-6 Astra and then with calls from inside the labs to slow down. The Mates rejected a slowdown and argued for steering and defensive co-scaling.

## Main themes

### GPT-6 Astra and whether AGI has arrived

- Peter Diamandis reads OpenAI's claims for Astra: 98% on FrontierMath Tier 4 and 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3. Emad Mostaque notes it trails on the Artificial Analysis ranking ([GPT-6 Astra](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/gpt-6-astra-saturates-arc-agi-3-teslas-30k-cybercab-floods-austin-anth/)).
- Jensen Huang posted that AGI has arrived. Salim Ismail calls the label semantics, and Alexander Wissner-Gross treats OpenAI's reported Navier-Stokes result as the bigger news ([Jensen and AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/why-jensen-huang-believes-weve-reached-agi-and-inside-openais-german-w/)).
- Mostaque reports Anthropic formalising Fermat's Last Theorem in 13 million lines of code, and Diamandis counts 12 major model releases in 22 days ([GPT-6 Astra](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/gpt-6-astra-saturates-arc-agi-3-teslas-30k-cybercab-floods-austin-anth/), [Doomers](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/why-jensen-and-zuck-think-the-doomers-are-wrong-plus-ai-gets-a-rebrand/)).

### The slowdown debate

- Dario Amodei's pacing essay drew agreement from Sam Altman and others, and Altman said OpenAI will not go public in 2026. On Amodei, Ismail says “I think he's authentic, but I called BS and I'm very cynical of the whole thing” ([Frontier Labs](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/frontier-labs-want-to-slow-down-openai-delays-its-2026-ipo-anthropic-f/)).
- Wissner-Gross calls the push a safety cartel, and Dave Blundin argues the real risk is bad actors, not escape ([Frontier Labs](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/frontier-labs-want-to-slow-down-openai-delays-its-2026-ipo-anthropic-f/)).
- After warnings from lab insiders, Mostaque says his p(doom) has fallen to 20% and Ismail puts his at about 0.1% ([Slow Down](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/should-we-slow-down-ai-progress-moonshots-288/)).
- In a clip, Huang says “Nobody's building more computer today than the people asking to be slowed down” ([Doomers](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/why-jensen-and-zuck-think-the-doomers-are-wrong-plus-ai-gets-a-rebrand/)).

### Regulation, bans and liability

- The Mates criticise the Sanders/Casar bill to ban superintelligence and contrast it with the G20 Carolina principles. Elon Musk told the G20 that “new things must be default legal as opposed to default illegal” ([GPT-6 Astra](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/gpt-6-astra-saturates-arc-agi-3-teslas-30k-cybercab-floods-austin-anth/)).
- Blundin warns that states without data centres will never get them back if the Sanders plan passes ([Doomers](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/why-jensen-and-zuck-think-the-doomers-are-wrong-plus-ai-gets-a-rebrand/)).
- Vlad Tenev says lab liability depends on the blast radius and expects AI code to ship with formal correctness certificates ([Vlad Tenev](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/robinhoods-vlad-tenev-on-tokenizing-everything-openais-6-misalignment/)).
- In the AMAs, Wissner-Gross names overregulation as the main risk and puts the odds of a good outcome beyond ten years above 90% ([AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/ask-the-mates-anything-moonshots-ama-289/), [AMA Round 2](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/ask-the-mates-anything-round-2-moonshots-ama-293/)).

### Lab alliances, pricing and energy

- OpenAI cut Cursor off after the SpaceX acquisition and Anthropic stepped in. The hosts expect outcome-based pricing to spread after Salesforce and OpenAI ([Star Probe](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/humanitys-first-star-probe-architect-labs-beats-nvidia-3-4x-musk-wants/)).
- Musk says SpaceX and Tesla will each build 100 GW a year of solar capacity and cast turbine blades in-house ([Star Probe](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/humanitys-first-star-probe-architect-labs-beats-nvidia-3-4x-musk-wants/)).
- Diamandis notes H100 rental prices rose 22% in a month, and a cited study found data gave larger efficiency gains than architecture ([Slow Down](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/should-we-slow-down-ai-progress-moonshots-288/)).

### Moonshots Live guests

- Palmer Luckey argues autonomous weapons are ancient and that defense will stay specialised, so Anduril skips humanoids ([Palmer Luckey](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/palmer-luckey-autonomous-weapons-are-ancient-and-why-anduril-wont-buil/)).
- Circle's Nikhil Chandhok expects more AIs than humans online next year, needing stablecoin rails ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/cathie-wood-on-tesla-spacex-merger-1m-bitcoin-more-ais-than-humans-ep/)).
- Two XPRIZE finales rewarded AI companies built in 90 days and hopeful science-fiction films ([Gemini XPRIZE](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/we-gave-away-2-000-000-to-5-ai-builders-moonshots-live/), [FutureVision](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/why-we-gave-away-3-500-000-00-for-someone-to-build-the-next-star-trek/)).
- Martine Rothblatt and Ben Lamm made business cases for biotech moonshots, with Lamm naming ethics and regulation as the real limits ([Rothblatt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/martine-rothblatt-growing-unlimited-organs-ai-consciousness-digital-pe/), [Ben Lamm](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/09/why-were-living-in-a-biological-singularity-with-ben-lamm-moonshots-li/)).
