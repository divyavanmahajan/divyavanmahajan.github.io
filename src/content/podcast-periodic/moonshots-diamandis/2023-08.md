## Overview

August on Moonshots was a run of single-guest episodes, each on a different moonshot. Brett Adcock made the case for humanoid robots, Andrew Yang warned about AI in the 2024 election, and two founders and a scientist pitched cultivated meat and age reversal as future industries. Keith Ferrazzi added a practical episode on writing a book, with ChatGPT as a new tool.

## Main themes

### Humanoid robots enter the labour market

- Brett Adcock argues the world is built for humans, so a general-purpose humanoid can work without changing the environment. He says Figure's goal is “to put as many humanoids as humans on the planet to make a physical labor a choice” ([Humanoid Robotics](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/the-rise-of-humanoid-robotics-w-brett-adcock-ep-57/)).
- Figure 01 is about 61 kg with roughly a thousand parts and vision-only perception. Adcock suggests a cost of about $30K, or around $500 a month to lease ([Humanoid Robotics](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/the-rise-of-humanoid-robotics-w-brett-adcock-ep-57/)).
- He expects structured warehouse and factory work first, paid commercial work as early as 2024, and homes around the end of the decade. He says Figure builds most of its hardware in-house because off-the-shelf parts were not good enough ([Humanoid Robotics](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/the-rise-of-humanoid-robotics-w-brett-adcock-ep-57/)).

### AI, elections and cash transfers

- Andrew Yang expects generative AI to do real harm in the 2024 cycle as the cost of faking video, text and images falls to zero. He cites a fake image of the Pentagon on fire that moved markets ([Andrew Yang](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/how-ai-is-impacting-politics-accelerating-the-need-for-ubi-w-andrew-ya/)).
- He says the US political system is reactive and will act only after damage is done. His fix is open primaries and ranked-choice voting, and he notes the Forward Party “already has legal recognition in about eight states” ([Andrew Yang](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/how-ai-is-impacting-politics-accelerating-the-need-for-ubi-w-andrew-ya/)).
- Yang sketches a 2028 independent primary held on smartphones and verified by mailed QR codes. On jobs, he proposes a data dividend and AI taxation, and argues displaced people need structure and community as well as money ([Andrew Yang](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/how-ai-is-impacting-politics-accelerating-the-need-for-ubi-w-andrew-ya/)).

### Biology as a business moonshot

- Josh Tetrick of GOOD Meat argues the remaining barrier for cultivated meat is the economics of scale, not the science. He sees a path to the roughly $4 per pound cost of conventional meat within 10 years through bigger vessels and cheaper inputs ([Slaughter-Free Meat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/slaughter-free-meat-coming-soon-to-your-dinner-tables-w-josh-tetrick-e/)).
- Tetrick describes regulatory approval in Singapore first, then in the US, but says the company is still tiny and sells at a loss. He expects incumbents such as Tyson to adopt the technology once it is proven ([Slaughter-Free Meat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/slaughter-free-meat-coming-soon-to-your-dinner-tables-w-josh-tetrick-e/)).
- David Sinclair predicts age-reversal therapy will become a major pharmaceutical market, with a first human trial expected in about two years. He says AI and rising investment are speeding the field up; these are his forecasts, largely based on animal work ([David Sinclair](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/aging-is-now-optional-w-david-sinclair-ep-60/)).

### Writing a book, with and without AI

- Keith Ferrazzi says “everybody write their book I don't care if you publish it”, because it forces you to define your brand. Both he and Peter Diamandis say the money comes from speaking, not book sales ([Write That Book](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/how-to-finally-write-that-book-make-it-a-bestseller-w-keith-ferrazzi-e/)).
- Ferrazzi paused his book on high-performing teams to rework it after prompting ChatGPT for how Freud or Jung would see executive teams. Diamandis says authors using it should at least disclose that ([Write That Book](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/how-to-finally-write-that-book-make-it-a-bestseller-w-keith-ferrazzi-e/)).
- The two compare advances, self-publishing and the sales needed to reach the New York Times list ([Write That Book](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/08/how-to-finally-write-that-book-make-it-a-bestseller-w-keith-ferrazzi-e/)).
