## Overview

October on Moonshots was dominated by two conversations with Jack Hidary of SandboxAQ, who argued that AI trained on physics equations and quantum sensors are already a business, years before fault-tolerant quantum computers. Ray Kurzweil restated his AGI and singularity dates, Arianna Huffington pitched an AI health coach built with OpenAI, and the month opened with the 30th anniversary story of XPRIZE.

## Main themes

### Large quantitative models and SandboxAQ

- Hidary argues that most of the economy is described by numbers rather than words. He says the “majority of our world is governed by numbers”, which language models trained on text cannot reach ([Quantum & AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/how-quantum-ai-will-shape-the-worlds-future-w-jack-hidary-ep-123/)).
- SandboxAQ runs quantum equations on GPUs to generate training data, which Hidary calls large quantitative models. He frames the company as a horizontal software platform for pharma, chemicals firms such as Dow Chemical, batteries and materials ([Quantum & AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/how-quantum-ai-will-shape-the-worlds-future-w-jack-hidary-ep-123/)).
- Diamandis notes that Hidary founded Alphabet's quantum group in 2016 and spun out SandboxAQ with Eric Schmidt as chairman and a $500 million round ([Quantum & AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/how-quantum-ai-will-shape-the-worlds-future-w-jack-hidary-ep-123/), [Technology Everyone Is Missing](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/the-world-changing-technology-everyone-is-missing-w-jack-hidary-ep-124/)).
- Hidary pitches drug discovery as the clearest commercial case, aiming to cut the long timelines and high failure rates of drug development ([Technology Everyone Is Missing](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/the-world-changing-technology-everyone-is-missing-w-jack-hidary-ep-124/)).

### Quantum timelines, sensors and post-quantum security

- Hidary estimates that breaking RSA needs about 5,000 logical qubits, or 5 million physical qubits at a 1,000:1 ratio. He expects modular blocks of 1,000-5,000 physical qubits by 2029 and treats 2031-2032 as the critical period ([Quantum & AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/how-quantum-ai-will-shape-the-worlds-future-w-jack-hidary-ep-123/)).
- He says quantum sensors are usable now without error correction. AQNav reads the Earth's magnetic field to navigate when GPS is jammed, and has been tested by the US Air Force ([Technology Everyone Is Missing](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/the-world-changing-technology-everyone-is-missing-w-jack-hidary-ep-124/)).
- Pointing to the post-quantum standards of August 13, 2024, Hidary says banks need seven to ten years to migrate and urges the Bitcoin and Ethereum communities to start now ([Technology Everyone Is Missing](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/the-world-changing-technology-everyone-is-missing-w-jack-hidary-ep-124/)).

### Kurzweil on AGI, merger and jobs

- Kurzweil says he predicted AGI by 2029 back in 1999 and now calls that date conservative ([Kurzweil](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/the-man-who-predicted-agi-decades-ago-w-ray-kurzweil-ep-125/)).
- He rejects a hard takeoff and expects a smooth exponential path driven by computation per dollar, with humans merging with AI around the 2045 singularity ([Kurzweil](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/the-man-who-predicted-agi-decades-ago-w-ray-kurzweil-ep-125/)).
- On jobs, he notes that food production fell from 80% of workers to 2% while more people work today, and expects universal basic income in the 2030s ([Kurzweil](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/the-man-who-predicted-agi-decades-ago-w-ray-kurzweil-ep-125/)).

### Prizes and personal AI coaches

- Gregg Maryniak says “our prizes Target those wrong assumptions” behind things people think impossible, here that only governments could fly people to space ([Ansari XPRIZE](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/how-a-10m-bet-made-civilian-spaceflight-a-reality-w-anousheh-ansari-gr/)).
- Anousheh Ansari describes the prize as a risk-mitigated investment, since teams are paid only on success ([Ansari XPRIZE](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/how-a-10m-bet-made-civilian-spaceflight-a-reality-w-anousheh-ansari-gr/)).
- Huffington presents Thrive AI Health, a joint venture with OpenAI, as a hyper-personalized coach sold to employers. She disagrees with Kurzweil's merger view, saying humans have a soul and AI does not ([Huffington](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/10/im-working-with-openai-to-make-work-better-for-humans-w-arianna-huffin/)).
