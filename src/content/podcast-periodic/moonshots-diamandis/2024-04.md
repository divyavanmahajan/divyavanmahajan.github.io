## Overview

April was an Abundance360 month. All five episodes came from or recapped the 2024 summit, whose theme asked whether digital superintelligence is humanity's greatest hope or gravest threat. Emad Mostaque, fresh from leaving Stability AI, appeared in four of them, and Ray Kurzweil in three.

## Main themes

### Superintelligence: hope or threat

- Peter Diamandis and Salim Ismail argue AI cannot be stopped, only guided. Elon Musk, in a clip, puts the risk of AI ending humanity at roughly 10-20% but calls abundance the most likely outcome; Ismail would put the risk nearer one in a thousand ([Summit recap](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/leading-experts-predictions-on-the-future-of-ai-agi-w-salim-ismail-ep/)).
- Ismail argues that more conscious AI will do fewer harmful things, so the goal is to make it as conscious as possible, fast ([Summit recap](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/leading-experts-predictions-on-the-future-of-ai-agi-w-salim-ismail-ep/)).
- On the panel, Tristan Harris says AI's benefits cannot be separated from its risks and wants tools that let society's defences keep pace. Mo Gawdat says competitive pressure rules out any slowdown ([Panel](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/ai-panel-discussion-w-emad-mostaque-ray-kurzweil-mo-gawdat-tristan-har/)).

### Kurzweil and Hinton on timelines and merging

- The hosts recall Kurzweil's 1999 prediction of human-level AI by 2029 and cite an 86% track record for his forecasts ([Summit recap](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/leading-experts-predictions-on-the-future-of-ai-agi-w-salim-ismail-ep/)).
- Geoffrey Hinton gives a 50% chance of superintelligence in 5 to 20 years and says “for everybody except Ray it's moving faster than we expected” ([Kurzweil & Hinton](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/ray-kurzweil-geoff-hinton-debate-the-future-of-ai-ep-95/)).
- Kurzweil expects humans to merge with AI. Hinton fears AI may have little reason to merge and could leave people behind, and argues chatbots can already have subjective experience ([Kurzweil & Hinton](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/ray-kurzweil-geoff-hinton-debate-the-future-of-ai-ep-95/)).

### The open-source argument

- Hinton compares open-sourcing large models to letting any graduate student build atomic bombs, notes fine-tuning for harm is cheap, and concludes “open sourcing these big models is not caution”. Kurzweil replies that the same models can defend against those dangers ([Kurzweil & Hinton](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/ray-kurzweil-geoff-hinton-debate-the-future-of-ai-ep-95/)).
- Mostaque argues open models save dozens of groups from training their own, and edge optimisation cuts energy use ([Panel](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/ai-panel-discussion-w-emad-mostaque-ray-kurzweil-mo-gawdat-tristan-har/)).
- Mostaque cites 330 million downloads of Stability models on Hugging Face and plans for about 100 national models with citizen-owned data ([Panel Pt. 2](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/ai-panel-discussion-pt-2-w-emad-mostaque-salim-ismail-benjamin-plummer/), [Ex-CEOs](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/2-ex-ai-ceos-debate-the-future-of-ai-w-emad-mostaque-nat-friedman-ep-9/)).

### Adoption, agents and the shape of companies

- Nat Friedman says the past year fired the starting gun on real LLM adoption. He compares open models to graduates you hire and proprietary ones to consultants, and says both will exist ([Ex-CEOs](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/2-ex-ai-ceos-debate-the-future-of-ai-w-emad-mostaque-nat-friedman-ep-9/)).
- Friedman says “today these things are not agents they're tools”, but expects departments to become models or swarms of models as trusted units of work grow ([Ex-CEOs](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/2-ex-ai-ceos-debate-the-future-of-ai-w-emad-mostaque-nat-friedman-ep-9/)).
- The Pt. 2 panel argues automation brings more firms, not mass unemployment. Guillaume Verdon pictures companies that are 80% AI and 20% human, and Ismail points to ATMs, after which banks opened more branches ([Panel Pt. 2](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/04/ai-panel-discussion-pt-2-w-emad-mostaque-salim-ismail-benjamin-plummer/)).
