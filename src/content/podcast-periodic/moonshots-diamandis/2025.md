## Overview

Moonshots spent 2025 tracking how fast AI was moving into work, money and machines. From April, the news roundtable with Dave Blundin and Salim Ismail became the backbone of the show. AI and jobs and the lab race ran all year; in the second half the talk shifted from Bitcoin and longevity to data centres, power and benchmarks.

## Main themes

### AI agents and the fight over jobs

- Marc Benioff says Salesforce will move support staff into other roles, framing the moment as “it's humans with agents working together that's where we are right now” ([Marc Benioff](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/salesforce-founder-gives-the-truth-on-ai-agents-w-marc-benioff-ep-141/)).
- Ray Dalio expects AI to widen gaps during a late-stage debt cycle ([Ray Dalio](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/ray-dalio-on-ai-job-loss-the-future-of-the-economy-ep-148/)); Mo Gawdat warns of a decade of disruption before abundance ([AGI Is Here](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/agi-is-here-you-just-dont-realize-it-yet-w-mo-gawdat-salim-ismail-ep-1/)).
- Reid Hoffman calls entry-level job losses painful but temporary ([Reid Hoffman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/linkedin-co-founder-opens-up-on-ai-job-loss-w-reid-hoffman-dave-salim/)). By December the hosts read GPT-5.2 as a sign that knowledge work can now be automated ([GPT 5.2 Release](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/12/gpt-5-2-release-corporate-collapse-in-2026-and-1-1m-job-loss-w-alexand/)).

### The frontier lab race as benchmarks saturate

- Emad Mostaque argues DeepSeek's cheap open model came from engineering under chip limits ([DeepSeek vs. OpenAI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/deepseek-vs-open-ai-the-state-of-ai-w-emad-mostaque-salim-ismail-ep-14/)).
- OpenAI's Jony Ive deal and Google I/O became a race for the consumer AI interface ([OpenAI's $6.5 Billion](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/why-openai-paid-6-5-billion-to-make-the-new-iphone-how-google-just-end/)).
- After Grok 4, Peter Diamandis worries that “at some point we're unable to measure how rapidly it's advancing” ([Grok 4](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ai-experts-react-elons-grok-4-is-now-1-in-ai-this-changes-everything-w/)). GPT-5 landed as an underwhelming show but a price cut ([GPT-5 Update](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/ai-insiders-breakdown-the-gpt-5-update-what-it-means-for-the-ai-race-w/)).
- The hosts call Gemini 3 a step change that put Google in the lead ([Gemini 3](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/ai-roundtable-what-everyone-missed-about-gemini-3-w-salim-ismail-dave/)).

### Compute, chips and power

- Eric Schmidt says of AI: “It accelerates to its natural limit. The natural limit is electricity” ([Eric Schmidt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ex-google-ceo-what-artificial-superintelligence-will-actually-look-lik/)).
- The hosts read Nvidia's Intel investment as a rescue of US chip fabs ([Nvidia and Intel](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/09/nvidia-the-us-government-just-bailed-out-intel-sending-the-stock-soari/)) and call AI a compute-scarce transition, not a bubble ([Is AI a Bubble?](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/is-ai-a-bubble-experts-debate-the-future-of-ai-w-david-blundin-salim-i/)).
- Schmidt says the US leads China on frontier AI but trails on electricity, robotics and open models ([US vs. China](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/ex-google-ceo-breaks-down-the-us-vs-china-ai-race-how-we-avoid-a-globa/)).

### Humanoid robots

- Brett Adcock argues humanoids are the body AGI needs ([Figure](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/03/a-humanoid-robot-in-every-home-its-closer-than-you-think-w-brett-adcoc/)); Vinod Khosla predicts bipedal robots will outgrow the car industry ([Vinod Khosla](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/04/ai-venture-capitalist-these-tech-predictions-will-change-everything-by/)).
- Bernt Bornich of 1X says home robots are the fastest path to scale and real-world data ([1X](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/robotics-ceo-the-humanoid-robot-revolution-is-real-it-starts-now-w-ber/)).

### Bitcoin, stablecoins and policy

- Early in the year Salim Ismail called a proposed $20 billion US Bitcoin reserve too small ([New Administration](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/01/the-new-administration-means-major-change-for-crypto-ai-tech-w-salim-i/)).
- Eric Pulier and Jeremy Allaire argue stablecoins will be the internet's money layer, increasingly used by AI agents ([AI-Crypto Collision](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/08/the-ai-crypto-collision-that-will-redefine-global-power-w-eric-pulier/), [Circle](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/10/money-after-ai-meet-the-new-digital-dollar-built-for-the-internet-stab/)).
