# How AI Starts Doing the Work in 2026 — With Anthropic CPO Mike Krieger

## Overview

This talk features **Mike Krieger**, Chief Product Officer of **Anthropic**, in a conversation on *The AI Daily Brief* podcast. The discussion centres on the near-term trajectory of AI in 2026, with a particular focus on agentic AI systems — models that move beyond answering questions to actively completing tasks — as well as the phenomenon known as "vibe coding" and what it signals about how humans and AI will collaborate on work. The conversation matters because it offers an insider perspective from a senior product leader at one of the most influential AI safety and research companies on how AI tooling is expected to evolve in the coming year.

## Prerequisites

Readers will benefit from familiarity with the following before engaging with this material:

- **Basic AI/LLM concepts** — what large language models (LLMs) are and how they are used as assistants or chatbots
- **Anthropic and Claude** — Anthropic's mission (AI safety) and its flagship model family, Claude
- **Agentic AI / AI agents** — the idea of AI systems that can plan, take actions, and complete multi-step tasks autonomously
- **"Vibe coding"** — a colloquial term for directing AI to write code through high-level, informal natural-language prompts rather than writing code directly
- **Product management in AI** — familiarity with how AI capabilities are translated into consumer and enterprise products

## Main Points

### 1. The Shift from AI as Assistant to AI as Worker
- The central theme is that AI in 2026 will move from *answering questions* to *doing work* — completing tasks end-to-end rather than merely informing human decisions.
- This represents a qualitative change in the human–AI relationship: the user becomes more of a delegator or manager than an operator.
- Krieger frames this as the defining product and societal challenge for the year ahead.

### 2. Vibe Coding as a Leading Indicator
- "Vibe coding" — using natural language to guide AI in writing software without deep technical expertise — is highlighted as an early, visible example of AI doing substantive work.
- It signals a broader pattern: entire professional workflows (not just coding) being restructured around AI-generated output that humans review and direct rather than produce.
- The implication is that vibe coding is not a niche curiosity but a preview of how many knowledge-work disciplines will operate.

### 3. Anthropic's Product Philosophy and 2026 Roadmap Directions
- Krieger discusses how Anthropic thinks about translating research capabilities into usable products, balancing capability with safety.
- The focus for 2026 is on making agentic interactions reliable, trustworthy, and controllable — so users can confidently delegate longer-horizon tasks to Claude.
- A key product challenge is building the *scaffolding* (memory, tool use, error recovery) that lets models operate autonomously without frequent human intervention.

### 4. Trust and Safety in Agentic Systems
- As AI does more work autonomously, questions of trust become critical: How does a user know the AI made the right decisions? How are mistakes caught and corrected?
- Anthropic's safety-first culture directly informs how they approach agentic products — erring toward transparency and user control rather than maximising autonomy at the expense of oversight.
- Krieger signals that interpretability and reliability are not academic concerns but immediate product requirements.

### 5. The Human Role in an AI-Augmented Workflow
- Even as AI takes on more tasks, Krieger emphasises that human judgment, taste, and direction remain central.
- The skill set that becomes valuable shifts: from *execution* to *evaluation* — knowing whether AI output is good, and knowing how to course-correct.
- This has implications for hiring, education, and how organisations structure teams.

## Key Concepts

- **Agentic AI**: AI systems capable of planning and executing multi-step tasks autonomously, using tools, memory, and environmental feedback — going beyond single-turn question-answering.
- **Vibe coding**: The practice of describing desired software behaviour in natural language and having an AI model generate the corresponding code, without the user writing code directly.
- **Scaffolding (in AI products)**: The surrounding infrastructure — memory management, tool integrations, error-handling loops — that enables a model to operate reliably over longer, more complex tasks.
- **Delegation model**: A framing of human–AI collaboration in which the human acts as a manager assigning tasks, and the AI acts as an executor completing them.
- **AI safety (Anthropic context)**: Anthropic's guiding principle that AI systems should be safe, interpretable, and aligned with human values — applied here not just in research but in product decisions.
- **Interpretability**: The ability to understand *why* an AI model produced a given output or took a given action, a prerequisite for trust in agentic settings.
- **Knowledge work automation**: The application of AI to tasks previously requiring human expertise and judgment — writing, coding, analysis, decision-making.

---

## Summary

Mike Krieger argues that 2026 will mark a meaningful inflection point in AI's role: systems like Claude will increasingly *do* work rather than merely *assist* with it, transforming how individuals and organisations relate to AI tools. Vibe coding is presented as an early, tangible demonstration of this shift — a glimpse of workflows where AI handles execution and humans provide direction and judgment. Anthropic's product priorities for the coming year reflect this transition, centred on building agentic systems that are reliable enough to be trusted with longer-horizon tasks while remaining transparent and controllable. Krieger's broader message is that the most important skill in an AI-augmented world is not technical proficiency in the traditional sense, but the capacity to evaluate, direct, and take responsibility for AI-generated work — a reorientation that carries significant implications for how people work, learn, and collaborate.
