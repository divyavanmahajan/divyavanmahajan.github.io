## Overview

January on Practical AI opened with ChatGPT crossing into everyday conversation, which the hosts used to explain RLHF and the new split of work between people and models. The guest episodes stayed practical: language technology for under-served languages, data science in small companies, GPU development environments and industrial simulation at NVIDIA.

## Main themes

### ChatGPT goes mainstream

- Daniel Whitenack says family, church members and his barber asked him about ChatGPT. Chris Benson calls it the first GPT model to cross “into mainstream awareness and broad use.” ([ChatGPT](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/chatgpt-goes-prime-time/)).
- Whitenack walks through RLHF: pre-train a language model, train a reward model on human ratings, then fine-tune with PPO. He notes OpenAI kept weights closed but made access far easier than GPT-3's waitlist ([ChatGPT](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/chatgpt-goes-prime-time/)).
- The answers are coherent but not always correct: asked for guest ideas, ChatGPT still placed Timnit Gebru at Google ([ChatGPT](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/chatgpt-goes-prime-time/)).
- Benson now edits rather than authors when drafting with it. Whitenack argues “the AI bits are really driving the creativity and the humans are enforcing the logic, the facts, the accuracy and the precision.” ([ChatGPT](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/chatgpt-goes-prime-time/)).

### Language technology with local communities

- Recorded at EMNLP 2022, four researchers argue that models designed for English-like languages work poorly on scarce data and different grammar. Rooweither Mabuya says builders wrongly assume one design should work for every language ([Local communities](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/nlp-research-by-for-local-communities/)).
- Andiswa Bukula says linguists and NLP practitioners in South Africa work apart, and asks how long African languages will keep being called under-resourced ([Local communities](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/nlp-research-by-for-local-communities/)).
- Bonaventure Dossou describes Masakhane as a grassroots movement building NLP for African languages by Africans. Whitenack notes that ever-larger models move further from small-data settings ([Local communities](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/nlp-research-by-for-local-communities/)).

### Practical ML operations for small teams

- Kirsten Lum of Storytellers says small firms hold back because they don't know how to hire, judge data readiness or integrate model output. A small-company data scientist covers the whole cycle at shallow depth, including ETL ([Small organizations](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/machine-learning-at-small-organizations/)).
- Lum says small companies mostly face tabular data and batch inference, and earn trust through measurable results and A/B testing ([Small organizations](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/machine-learning-at-small-organizations/)).
- Nader Khalil of Brev.dev describes a CLI that spins up remote GPU environments and connects local tools to them. Shared environments are copied, not shared machines ([GPU dev environments](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/gpu-dev-environments-that-just-work/)).
- Khalil says GPUs often sit idle while developers code, so Brev auto-stops instances, a lesson from Workday's wasted spend. He cites one user's Google Cloud bill falling from about $280 to roughly $25-27 ([GPU dev environments](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/gpu-dev-environments-that-just-work/)).

### Simulation and the industrial metaverse

- Beau Perschall of NVIDIA calls the metaverse the next evolution of the internet, with connected 3D worlds that no single company builds ([NVIDIA](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/3d-assets-simulation-at-nvidia/)).
- He describes digital twins where simulation and real systems report to each other, so robot arms can be retrained without taking a work cell offline. BMW and Ericsson are among Omniverse users ([NVIDIA](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/3d-assets-simulation-at-nvidia/)).
- Perschall says 3D assets rarely carry the labels, physics and materials data scientists need, which his SimReady standard on top of USD aims to fix ([NVIDIA](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/3d-assets-simulation-at-nvidia/)).
