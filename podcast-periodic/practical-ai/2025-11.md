## Overview

November on Practical AI split between money and machines. The hosts asked whether AI spending is a bubble, and a Fireflies founder explained how an AI product becomes a profitable business. The other half of the month was physical AI: Waymo's robotaxis and Chris Benson's case for swarms, home automation and Rust on small hardware.

## Main themes

### Is AI a bubble?

- Daniel Whitenack cites a source saying over 50% of VC funding in Q2 2025 went to AI companies, at revenue multiples of 15x to 30x or more ([AI bubble](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/are-we-in-an-ai-bubble/)).
- Chris Benson points to concentration risk: AI-related stocks account for about 75% of S&P 500 returns, 80% of earnings growth and 90% of capital spending growth ([AI bubble](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/are-we-in-an-ai-bubble/)).
- Against that, Fed Chair Jerome Powell told Fortune that AI companies have earnings, and Whitenack notes investment relative to GDP is still lower than for railroads or electrification ([AI bubble](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/are-we-in-an-ai-bubble/)).
- Their verdict is no single dot-com-style bubble, but possibly many small ones, with AI-washing making the market hard to read ([AI bubble](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/are-we-in-an-ai-bubble/)).

### Physical AI and autonomous vehicles

- Drago Anguelov says Waymo serves hundreds of thousands of paid rides a week in five cities and has passed 100 million autonomous miles, with London and Tokyo next ([Waymo](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/autonomous-vehicle-research-at-waymo/)).
- He describes two equally hard problems: building the onboard driver and validating it through large-scale simulation of rare events. The onboard stack is moving to fewer, larger models ([Waymo](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/autonomous-vehicle-research-at-waymo/)).
- Anguelov sees LLM ideas such as next-token prediction transferring to driving, and is interested in fast/slow system-one and system-two robot designs ([Waymo](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/autonomous-vehicle-research-at-waymo/)).
- Benson argues AI, hardware and robotics are converging, saying “we live in the coolest moment in human history right now” ([Chris on AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/chris-on-ai-autonomous-swarming-home-automation-and-rust/)).

### Swarms, open models and makers

- Benson sets a high bar for swarming: independent autonomous platforms acting as one decentralised decision-making entity. Drone light shows are pre-programmed fleets, not swarms ([Chris on AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/chris-on-ai-autonomous-swarming-home-automation-and-rust/)).
- He expects many products calling themselves swarms within two to three years, but few meeting his definition ([Chris on AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/chris-on-ai-autonomous-swarming-home-automation-and-rust/)).
- Benson says the gap between frontier and open models “has narrowed dramatically”, pushing big providers towards vertical AI services ([Chris on AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/chris-on-ai-autonomous-swarming-home-automation-and-rust/)).
- At home he is leaving cloud services such as Alexa for local, private systems with small Hugging Face models, and recommends ROS 2 and Rust for edge projects ([Chris on AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/chris-on-ai-autonomous-swarming-home-automation-and-rust/)).

### The moat in AI note-taking

- Krish Ramineni says off-the-shelf parts get a notetaker to about 80%; integrations, security, compliance and search make up the hard remainder that builds a business ([Fireflies](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/beyond-note-taking-with-fireflies/)).
- Fireflies has been profitable since 2023 without touching its Series A, and runs more traffic on its own bare-metal servers than on Google or AWS ([Fireflies](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/beyond-note-taking-with-fireflies/)).
- Ramineni says “Product market fit is not about finding it once”. The new Live Assist feature helps during meetings, and manual queries rose rather than fell ([Fireflies](/podcasts/practical-ai/2025/11/beyond-note-taking-with-fireflies/)).
