## Overview

Practical AI's first full year opened with OpenAI's partial release of GPT-2 and a debate on fake content and responsible disclosure. The hosts explained GANs, reinforcement learning and transfer learning, while guests covered infrastructure, AutoML, serving and NLP tooling. Policy came in through the US executive order on AI and blacklisted Chinese surveillance firms.

## Main themes

### GPT-2, deepfakes and safety

- Daniel says of GPT-2 that “the transformer idea isn't really new”; its advance is scale, and OpenAI's partial release opened a debate on disclosure ([GPT-2](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/02/openai-s-new-dangerous-gpt-2-language-model/)).
- El Mahdi El Mhamdi calls poisoned recommender systems today's real AI safety problem ([AI safety research](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/staving-off-disaster-through-ai-safety-research/)).
- The hosts see deepfake defence resting on staged releases, detection, legislation and platform labelling ([DeepFakes](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/06/exposing-the-deception-of-deepfakes/)).

### GANs, reinforcement learning and transfer learning

- The hosts frame all three as training approaches wrapped around ordinary networks, and Chris says nearly every practitioner has used transfer learning ([GANs, RL, and transfer learning](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/06/gans-rl-and-transfer-learning-oh-my/)).
- Adam Stooke describes scaling trial-and-error learning across GPUs, from Atari to StarCraft II and Dota 2 ([Deep Reinforcement Learning](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/04/deep-reinforcement-learning/)); the hosts say OpenAI's robot hand mattered for dexterity learned in simulation, not the cube ([Robot hands](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/11/robot-hands-solving-rubik-s-cubes/)).
- Jakub Langr and Vladimir Bok say GANs lead image generation but are deployed mostly by specialists ([Making GANs practical](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/12/making-gans-practical/)).

### Infrastructure, AutoML and production

- Daniel frames AI infrastructure as compute and storage, with storage usually set by what a company already runs ([AI infrastructure](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/04/the-landscape-of-ai-infrastructure/)).
- Google's Sherol Chen says AutoML aims to “deliver the best prediction model”, though data preparation still needs experts ([AutoML at Google](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/09/automl-and-ai-at-google/)).
- Evan Sparks says most teams outside big tech still run deep learning on manual scripts ([Dark ages of AI infrastructure](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/12/escaping-the-dark-ages-of-ai-infrastructure/)).

### Enterprise adoption and AI policy

- Ben Lorica finds mature adopters pulling ahead, with early winners building on existing analytics infrastructure ([AI adoption in the enterprise](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/05/ai-adoption-in-the-enterprise/)).
- The hosts find the US executive order light on funding, and Chris argues US AI leadership is not a foregone conclusion against China ([Executive Order](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/the-white-house-executive-order-on-ai/), [Blacklisted companies](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/10/blacklisted-facial-recognition-and-surveillance-companies/)).
- Chris, chief AI strategist at Lockheed Martin, explains how AI is reshaping its HPC strategy ([AI and HPC](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/09/worlds-are-colliding-ai-and-hpc/)).

### Open source and the NLP stack

- Clément Delangue wants conversational AI to be fun and emotional, built on transformers and open source ([Hugging Face](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/03/social-ai-with-hugging-face/)); distillation later shrinks BERT for production ([Model distillation](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/09/ai-in-the-majority-world-and-model-distillation/)).
- Samuel Taylor calls open source the foundation of ML work ([Open source](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/09/the-influence-of-open-source-on-ai-development/)); Joel Grus urges software engineering discipline for research code ([AI code for good science](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/07/ai-code-that-facilitates-good-science/)).
- spaCy's creators put annotation tooling ahead of ever-larger models ([Modern NLP with spaCy](/podcasts/practical-ai/2019/12/modern-nlp-with-spacy/)).
