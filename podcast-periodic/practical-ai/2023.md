## Overview

Practical AI's 2023 began with ChatGPT reaching the mainstream and spent the year on how practitioners build on large language models. Prompting, retrieval and validated output replaced training as daily work. Open models such as Llama 2 weakened the case for one closed provider. The year closed on the US executive order and OpenAI's boardroom crisis.

## Main themes

### ChatGPT and the shift to prompting

- The hosts explain ChatGPT as a GPT-3.5 model refined with RLHF, and Chris Benson calls it the one that has “crossed over into mainstream awareness and broad use” ([ChatGPT goes prime time!](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/01/chatgpt-goes-prime-time/)).
- Daniel Whitenack argues the prompt is the main lever practitioners control over output ([Success (and failure) in prompting](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/02/success-and-failure-in-prompting/)), and Rajiv Shah says in-context learning opens many ML tasks to non-specialists ([Capabilities of LLMs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/04/capabilities-of-llms/)).

### Open models erode the moat

- Shah expects enterprises to move to open models they can control, since “most people would like to have a model that they can fit inside their computer or a couple of GPUs” ([Capabilities of LLMs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/04/capabilities-of-llms/)).
- The hosts read Databricks' $1.3 billion MosaicML deal as a sign open models are taking share ([Cambrian explosion of generative models](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/07/cambrian-explosion-of-generative-models/)), and see Llama 2's licence making task-specific fine-tunes practical ([There's a new Llama in town](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/07/there-s-a-new-llama-in-town/)).
- Casper da Costa-Luis urges checking licences and openness carefully and fine-tuning for real value ([The state of open source AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/12/the-state-of-open-source-ai/)).

### Building LLM applications

- Travis Fischer advises starting with hosted models and simple prompts ([The last mile](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/05/the-last-mile-of-ai-app-development/)); Whitenack's Prediction Guard adds validated output ([Controlled and compliant AI applications](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/05/controlled-and-compliant-ai-applications/)).
- Jerry Liu connects LLMs to private data ([LlamaIndex](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/05/data-augmentation-with-llamaindex/)), and Demetrios Brinkmann says most teams wanting fine-tuning really need RAG ([Fine-tuning vs RAG](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/09/fine-tuning-vs-rag/)).
- Prashanth Rao weighs vector database trade-offs ([Vector databases](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/08/vector-databases-beyond-the-hype/)); Chang She argues for embedded, on-disk search ([LanceDB](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/12/open-source-on-disk-vector-search-with-lancedb/)).

### MLOps and inference infrastructure

- Brinkmann argues MLOps is DevOps with data sprinkled on it, and that generative models make it more important ([MLOps is alive and well](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/02/mlops-is-alive-and-well/)).
- Banana and Modal pitched serverless GPU inference ([Serverless GPUs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/02/serverless-gpus/), [Modal](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/03/end-to-end-cloud-compute-for-ai-ml/)); Neural Magic runs large models on CPUs ([Large models on CPUs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/05/large-models-on-cpus/)).
- Baseten sees teams preferring managed hosting ([Self-hosting & scaling models](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/10/self-hosting-scaling-models/)).

### Regulation, accountability and OpenAI's crisis

- Benson argues policymakers are far behind practitioners ([Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/08/blueprint-for-an-ai-bill-of-rights/)); the US executive order arrived binding but unfunded ([Government regulation](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/government-regulation-of-ai-has-arrived/)).
- Damien Riehl says generative AI strains copyright and patent law ([Legal consequences](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/07/legal-consequences-of-generated-content/)).
- After Sam Altman's firing and return, the hosts tell enterprises to stop depending on one model family ([The OpenAI debacle](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/11/the-openai-debacle-a-retrospective/)).
