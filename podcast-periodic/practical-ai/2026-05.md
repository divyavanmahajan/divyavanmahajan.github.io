## Overview

May on Practical AI argued that the model matters less than what surrounds it. The hosts declared models a commodity, Nous Research described a harness that writes its own skills, and Stacklok's CEO laid out the platform enterprises need to connect agents to real systems. A congressman covered the policy side.

## Main themes

### Open versus closed matters less

- Daniel Whitenack says recent open models such as Kimi and Qwen 3.5 still trail closed models by a few points, but argues “the model is now a complete commodity.” ([Model wars](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/the-myth-of-model-wars-open-vs-closed-ai-in-2026/)).
- Open models still win in air-gapped, data-sovereign and high-volume settings. Chris Benson notes Meta has moved from Llama to a closed family, leaving China with the leading open models ([Model wars](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/the-myth-of-model-wars-open-vs-closed-ai-in-2026/)).
- Jeffrey Quesnelle traces the same shift from Llama to a near-frontier Chinese open model, and says Chinese firms use open releases to get attention ([Hermes Agent](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/hermes-agent-agents-that-grow-with-you/)).

### The harness does the work

- Quesnelle says “A better harness with a worse model can beat a better model with a worse harness.” He likens the model to a brain and the harness to a body ([Hermes Agent](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/hermes-agent-agents-that-grow-with-you/)).
- Hermes Agent started as an internal research tool at Nous. It ships few hard-coded features and lets the model write its own skills and memory, so it improves with use ([Hermes Agent](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/hermes-agent-agents-that-grow-with-you/)).
- An internal read-only agent connected via MCP built up enough skills in a month that support staff now use it for reports and root-cause analysis ([Hermes Agent](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/hermes-agent-agents-that-grow-with-you/)).
- Whitenack expects the value to lie in managing agent sprawl: governance, MCP servers and agent-to-agent communication ([Model wars](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/the-myth-of-model-wars-open-vs-closed-ai-in-2026/)).

### An enterprise platform for MCP

- Craig McLuckie describes MCP as “the selectively permeable membrane that an organization can wrap around its existing systems” ([MCP and Kubernetes](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/rebooting-enterprise-ai-with-mcp-and-kubernetes/)).
- His four pieces are a secure runtime, a registry, a gateway and a control plane, with an LLM gateway as the second bookend ([MCP and Kubernetes](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/rebooting-enterprise-ai-with-mcp-and-kubernetes/)).
- He calls identity the hard part and recommends token exchange that scopes claims down. A find-tool, call-tool proxy pattern can cut input tokens by 80-90% ([MCP and Kubernetes](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/rebooting-enterprise-ai-with-mcp-and-kubernetes/)).

### Policy struggles to keep up

- Don Beyer notes Congress has passed one AI bill from its task force's 80 recommendations while over 700 state bills exist. He wants states free to regulate until a national framework exists ([Congressman Beyer](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/u-s-congressman-beyer-on-ai-challenges-facing-america-and-the-world/)).
- He sees bipartisan ground on surveillance, sexual imagery and white-collar job displacement, and says the US adapted badly to past manufacturing job losses ([Congressman Beyer](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/u-s-congressman-beyer-on-ai-challenges-facing-america-and-the-world/)).
- Beyer calls Anthropic's Mythos a cybersecurity wake-up call and backs a transparency act requiring the biggest model makers to safety-test ([Congressman Beyer](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/u-s-congressman-beyer-on-ai-challenges-facing-america-and-the-world/)).
