## Overview

April on Practical AI was about putting LLMs to work inside organisations. Retrieval-augmented generation and enterprise adoption were the threads across episodes, joined by AI21's case for reliable, cheap models and a self-hosted chat interface. One episode stepped aside to look at AI-generated music and how multimodal models work.

## Main themes

### RAG takes over and evaluation lags

- Demetrios Brinkmann shares MLOps Community survey results with 322 respondents. RAG was the clear standout, and fine-tuning has lost ground ([RAG continues to rise](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/rag-continues-to-rise/)).
- Brinkmann says fine-tuning is mainly for output form, such as function calling. Daniel Whitenack adds that enterprises often drop the urge to fine-tune once they solve use cases without it ([RAG continues to rise](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/rag-continues-to-rise/)).
- Evaluation is largely manual: 42% test against their own data sets and 72% of ground-truth labels were made by hand. Whitenack notes many vector databases lack update logic and role-based access control ([RAG continues to rise](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/rag-continues-to-rise/)).
- Danny Avila's LibreChat runs file uploads through a local vector database. He finds OpenAI's assistants RAG naive and a black box ([Private chat UIs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/private-open-source-chat-uis/)).

### Getting people to use generative AI

- Chris Benson says organisations are making tools available, “but they're really struggling with adoption”. He sees fear of job loss and of being watched as a barrier ([Should kids still learn to code?](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/should-kids-still-learn-to-code/)).
- Whitenack has seen adoption spread when senior champions use chat tools live in meetings, rather than through top-down directives. He suggests private chat tools or LM Studio as safe starting points ([Should kids still learn to code?](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/should-kids-still-learn-to-code/)).
- Both hosts disagree with Jensen Huang that kids no longer need to code. Whitenack expects routine front-end work to go to AI, but says AI systems still need engineers to build, scale and maintain them ([Should kids still learn to code?](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/should-kids-still-learn-to-code/)).

### Reliable, affordable models for the enterprise

- Yoav Shoham of AI21 argues that “if you're brilliant 95% of the time and garbage 5% of the time, you're dead in the water” in enterprise use ([Mamba & Jamba](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/mamba-jamba/)).
- He sees task-specific models as small systems with pre-processing and validators. Cascading LLM calls compounds cost and errors, so he expects orchestration layers he calls an AI OS ([Mamba & Jamba](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/mamba-jamba/)).
- Jamba mixes Mamba state-space layers with an attention layer every 8 or 16 layers to cut the cost of long context, and fits on a single 80 GB GPU. It is AI21's first open model, released as a base model ([Mamba & Jamba](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/mamba-jamba/)).

### Owning your chat data

- Avila argues data is the new commodity, and conversations with capable models are valuable for training the next one, so they should stay under your ownership ([Private chat UIs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/private-open-source-chat-uis/)).
- His demo moves one conversation between a local Ollama model, Llama 3 70B on Groq and Cohere. Whitenack shows Prediction Guard's fork running on Intel Gaudi 2 hardware ([Private chat UIs](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/private-open-source-chat-uis/)).

### AI music and multimodal models

- Whitenack plays songs made with Udio and understands machine-generated music is not copyrightable in the US today. He expects prompting to be recognised as creative eventually, as photography was ([Udio](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/udio-the-age-of-multi-modal-ai/)).
- He explains how LLaVA pairs a CLIP vision encoder with a language model through a trained projection, as part of the shift from chained single-mode models to joint ones ([Udio](/podcasts/practical-ai/2024/04/udio-the-age-of-multi-modal-ai/)).
