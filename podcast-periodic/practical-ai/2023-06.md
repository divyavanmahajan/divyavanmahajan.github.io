## Overview

June on Practical AI took stock of how generative AI changes the practitioner's job. The hosts explained the move from training models to prompting them, and a crossover with Latent Space traced MLOps turning into LLM ops. Two applied episodes covered marketing content and automated maps.

## Main themes

### From training to prompting

- Daniel Whitenack dates a first shift to fine-tuning pretrained models such as BERT around 2019-2020. Chris Benson notes this moves the expensive training cost onto large providers ([From ML to generative AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/from-ml-to-ai-to-generative-ai/)).
- Whitenack says the second shift is that “people now view these foundation models that are being produced these days as useful in and of themselves without any further fine tuning.” ([From ML to generative AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/from-ml-to-ai-to-generative-ai/)).
- Benson still guesses that at least 95% of AI in industry is supervised learning. He says 2023 is the year public perception of AI changed ([From ML to generative AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/from-ml-to-ai-to-generative-ai/)).
- Prompting came up in three episodes, from Whitenack's ad-image prompts to Kate Bradley Chernis's view that people need prompting skills ([From ML to generative AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/from-ml-to-ai-to-generative-ai/), [Lately](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/accidentally-building-sota-ai/), [Latent Space crossover](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/ai-trends-a-latent-space-crossover/)).

### MLOps becomes LLM ops

- Swyx says “I feel this evolution moving from essentially the MLOps era into, for lack of a better word, LLM ops.” He frames the rise of the AI engineer ([Latent Space crossover](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/ai-trends-a-latent-space-crossover/)).
- Alessio Fanelli notes that benchmarks are mostly multiple choice while production work is open-ended, so evaluation is unsettled ([Latent Space crossover](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/ai-trends-a-latent-space-crossover/)).
- Whitenack finds most enterprises have not yet integrated LLMs. He urges them to move from prompting to data augmentation, fine-tuning and only then training their own models ([Latent Space crossover](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/ai-trends-a-latent-space-crossover/)).

### Risk without AGI

- Benson argues the main risk needs no AGI, only humans combining capable models with harmful intent ([From ML to generative AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/from-ml-to-ai-to-generative-ai/)).
- Whitenack's example is a chat-with-your-docs tool over aircraft manuals that wrongly says a critical part can be removed. He notes the EU's step towards regulating high-risk uses ([From ML to generative AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/from-ml-to-ai-to-generative-ai/)).

### Applied AI in marketing and mapping

- Bradley Chernis says Lately.ai built AI before realising it. It learns a brand's voice from its own social analytics and turns long content into many short posts, using only private customer data ([Lately](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/accidentally-building-sota-ai/)).
- She argues human judgement stays central to marketing and tells listeners not to panic about AI ([Lately](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/accidentally-building-sota-ai/)).
- Gabriel Ortiz of the Government of Cantabria counted crowds on more than 100 beaches from aerial surveys. His team also produced a land-cover map generated entirely by AI, with mistakes left in on purpose ([Cartography](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/automated-cartography-using-ai/)).
- Ortiz says careful labelling and older models such as U-Net and Mask R-CNN matter more than new architectures. He and the crossover hosts both mention Segment Anything ([Cartography](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/automated-cartography-using-ai/), [Latent Space crossover](/podcasts/practical-ai/2023/06/ai-trends-a-latent-space-crossover/)).
