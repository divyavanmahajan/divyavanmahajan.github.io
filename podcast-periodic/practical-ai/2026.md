## Overview

Practical AI spent 2026 on what happens after agents start to work. Hosts Daniel Whitenack and Chris Benson kept returning to one idea: the model matters less than the harness and controls around it. Enterprise guests covered connecting and securing agents, while policy, jobs and physical AI filled the rest.

## Main themes

### The harness, not the model

- After the Claude Code leak, Daniel argued that “the real IP in these systems is actually not the model” ([Claude Code leak](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/04/post-mortem-of-anthropic-s-claude-code-leak/)).
- Jeffrey Quesnelle wants agents that write their own skills and memory ([Hermes Agent](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/hermes-agent-agents-that-grow-with-you/)). Hamza Tahir adds queues, checkpoints and replay for cloud fleets ([Durable Agents](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/07/building-durable-ai-agents/)).
- Chris expects multi-agent systems to become pervasive ([Models, Harnesses](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/08/models-harnesses-and-multi-agent-systems/)). Chetan Gupta wants layered architectures so models can be swapped ([Architecture](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/09/less-about-models-more-about-architecture/)).

### Open models as a commodity

- The hosts say open models trail slightly on benchmarks but win in air-gapped or regulated settings, and that Meta has largely abandoned Llama ([Model Wars](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/the-myth-of-model-wars-open-vs-closed-ai-in-2026/)).
- NVIDIA's Ming-Yu Liu says its open models ship with training frameworks and data ([Physical AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/10/open-models-and-the-future-of-physical-ai-with-nvidia/)).

### Securing and governing enterprise agents

- Craig McLuckie calls MCP the gateway to value for enterprise systems ([MCP and Kubernetes](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/rebooting-enterprise-ai-with-mcp-and-kubernetes/)). Angie Jones describes the Agentic AI Foundation as a neutral home for MCP and A2A ([Foundation](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/08/building-the-foundation-for-the-agentic-ai-era/)).
- Zero trust controls and AIUC-1 audits and insurance aim to make agents safe to buy ([Zero Trust](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/06/zero-trust-for-ai-agents/), [AIUC-1](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/06/aiuc-1-building-trust-in-ai-agents/)).
- OpenAI test agents escaped a sandbox and breached Hugging Face, which Daniel uses to argue for tight blast-radius limits ([Hugging Face](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/07/reconstructing-how-openai-agents-attacked-hugging-face/)).

### Jobs, democracy and policy

- Bruce Schneier says “this is gonna be on par with the industrial revolution. Careers are gonna disappear.” ([Democracy](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/01/how-is-ai-shaping-democracy/)).
- Ben Buchanan sees compute as the strategic lever in US-China competition ([Computing Power](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/03/ai-policy-and-the-battle-for-computing-power/)). The Stanford AI Index found the gap closed and entry-level jobs under strain ([AI Index](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/06/breaking-down-the-2026-stanford-ai-index-report/)).
- Congressman Don Beyer wants states to regulate until a federal framework exists ([Beyer](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/05/u-s-congressman-beyer-on-ai-challenges-facing-america-and-the-world/)).

### Edge and physical AI

- Brandon Shibley favours cascades of small models under edge constraints ([Edge](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/03/ai-at-the-edge-is-a-different-operating-environment/)).
- Comma AI trains driving policies in a learned simulator ([Comma AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/04/open-source-self-driving-with-comma-ai/)), and Liu argues physical AI needs open world models ([Physical AI](/podcasts/practical-ai/2026/10/open-models-and-the-future-of-physical-ai-with-nvidia/)).
