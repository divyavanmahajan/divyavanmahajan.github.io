## Overview

May 2022 was anchored by two conversations on the war in Ukraine that reached opposite conclusions: Oliver Stone and Stephen Kotkin. Around them were episodes on contested social questions, three personal stories about fame, addiction and manipulation, and a Bitcoin economist. Elon Musk came up in four episodes, mostly in passing.

## Main themes

### Putin and the war in Ukraine

- Oliver Stone, who interviewed Putin in 2015-2017, frames the war against decades of US policy, NATO expansion and Western propaganda. He still wrote that “russia was wrong to invade” ([Stone](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/oliver-stone-vladimir-putin-and-war-in-ukraine/)).
- Fridman, half Ukrainian and half Russian, challenged Stone on Russian propaganda and on fear in Russian society. Stone declined to steelman Zelensky ([Stone](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/oliver-stone-vladimir-putin-and-war-in-ukraine/)).
- Stephen Kotkin rejects the NATO-expansion explanation. He says Ukraine's membership was never going to happen in our lifetimes, and treats the invasion as Putin's choice after weak Western responses ([Kotkin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/stephen-kotkin-putin-zelenskyy-and-war-in-ukraine/)).
- Both discussed nuclear risk. Stone warns of escalation and wants Biden to talk to Putin in Moscow; Kotkin argues tactical fallout would also hit Russia ([Stone](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/oliver-stone-vladimir-putin-and-war-in-ukraine/), [Kotkin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/stephen-kotkin-putin-zelenskyy-and-war-in-ukraine/)).

### Race, sex differences and cancel culture

- Glenn Loury argues fair treatment does not imply equal group outcomes, and that the racism charge shuts down debate: “by calling people a racist you may shut them up but you won't change their minds” ([Loury](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/glenn-loury-race-racism-identity-politics-and-cancel-culture/)).
- Loury says he hates affirmative action as a band-aid that substitutes for building black Americans' capacity to compete ([Loury](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/glenn-loury-race-racism-identity-politics-and-cancel-culture/)).
- David Buss presents human mating psychology as a set of evolved, partly sex-differentiated strategies. He says he would rather be scientifically than politically correct, and stresses that describing is not endorsing ([Buss](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/david-buss-sex-dating-relationships-and-sex-differences/)).

### Fame, addiction and manipulation

- Dan Reynolds says fame made him feel he had to play the public persona, and that delaying therapy almost cost him his life ([Reynolds](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/dan-reynolds-imagine-dragons/)).
- Bobby Lee describes a recent relapse that brought him close to death, and credits sobriety, intensive therapy and his partner Khalyla with turning things around ([Lee](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/bobby-lee-comedy-skyrim-sex-robots-love-fame-and-power/)).
- Sarma Melngailis argues that her fall into fraud came from slow manipulation by Anthony Strangis, and that Netflix's Bad Vegan misrepresented her intent ([Melngailis](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/sarma-melngailis-bad-vegan/)).

### Hard money

- Saifedean Ammous argues money is whatever is hardest to produce, that fiat credit money fuels inflation and war, and that Bitcoin is the hardest money yet ([Ammous](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/saifedean-ammous-bitcoin-anarchy-and-austrian-economics/)).
- He defends the Austrian school, saying “without guiding theory, data is mute”. Fridman repeatedly calls him too certain, citing his Soviet upbringing as a warning ([Ammous](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/saifedean-ammous-bitcoin-anarchy-and-austrian-economics/)).

### The long view in space

- Chris Mason argues that humans, as the only species aware of extinction, have a duty to keep life going beyond Earth over the next 500 years ([Mason](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/chris-mason-space-travel-colonization-and-long-term-survival-in-space/)).
- He says commercial spaceflight has made flying mostly a question of money, and sees a multi-country space race 2.0 ([Mason](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/chris-mason-space-travel-colonization-and-long-term-survival-in-space/)).
