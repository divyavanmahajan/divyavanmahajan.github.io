## Overview

May leaned heavily towards politics and personal stories, with almost no AI. Fridman set Oliver Stone and Stephen Kotkin against each other on the war in Ukraine, followed April's Bitcoin conversation with Saifedean Ammous, and spoke with Glenn Loury on race. Several guests told stories of self-criticism, addiction, manipulation and recovery.

## Main themes

### Two readings of the war in Ukraine

- Oliver Stone, who interviewed Putin, argues the invasion must be read against US hostility, NATO expansion and Western propaganda, though he says Russia was wrong to invade. Fridman challenges him on Russian propaganda and asks him to steelman Zelensky; Stone declines ([Oliver Stone](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/oliver-stone-vladimir-putin-and-war-in-ukraine/)).
- Historian Stephen Kotkin rejects the NATO explanation, citing agreements Moscow signed on countries choosing their own alliances, and treats the invasion as a choice born of Russian ambition outrunning capability ([Stephen Kotkin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/stephen-kotkin-putin-zelenskyy-and-war-in-ukraine/)).
- Kotkin says weak Western responses to earlier Russian actions encouraged Putin, and that Xi Jinping's support for Putin has undermined China's strategy of splitting the US from Europe ([Stephen Kotkin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/stephen-kotkin-putin-zelenskyy-and-war-in-ukraine/)).

### Money, markets and Bitcoin

- Saifedean Ammous argues money is whatever is hardest to produce, that fiat credit money fuels inflation and short-termism, and that Bitcoin is the hardest money yet ([Saifedean Ammous](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/saifedean-ammous-bitcoin-anarchy-and-austrian-economics/)).
- Fridman pushes back on Ammous's certainty about Keynesian economics, citing his Soviet upbringing, and argues for an open mind on NFTs and dapps. Ammous warns that central bank digital currencies could bring state control of money ([Saifedean Ammous](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/saifedean-ammous-bitcoin-anarchy-and-austrian-economics/)).

### Race, sex differences and contested science

- Economist Glenn Loury argues that fair treatment does not imply equal group outcomes and that calling people racist instead of arguing drives views underground ([Glenn Loury](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/glenn-loury-race-racism-identity-politics-and-cancel-culture/)).
- Loury criticises affirmative action as a band-aid and rejects research funding bans based on predicted political effects. Fridman blames growing university administrations for the climate ([Glenn Loury](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/glenn-loury-race-racism-identity-politics-and-cancel-culture/)).
- Evolutionary psychologist David Buss describes mating as a menu of evolved strategies with overlapping sex differences, and says he would rather be scientifically than politically correct ([David Buss](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/david-buss-sex-dating-relationships-and-sex-differences/)).

### Self-criticism, manipulation and recovery

- Comedian Bobby Lee traces his inner critic and addiction to childhood, and credits sobriety, therapy and his partner Khalyla for turning his life around. Fridman admits he is deeply self-critical too ([Bobby Lee](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/bobby-lee-comedy-skyrim-sex-robots-love-fame-and-power/)).
- Restaurateur Sarma Melngailis says slow manipulation by Anthony Strangis led to her fraud, and that the Netflix film Bad Vegan misrepresented her intent ([Sarma Melngailis](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/sarma-melngailis-bad-vegan/)).
- Imagine Dragons' Dan Reynolds says audiences detect inauthenticity instantly, and links his songs to depression, a lost Mormon faith and therapy ([Dan Reynolds](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/dan-reynolds-imagine-dragons/)).

### Survival beyond Earth

- Cornell's Chris Mason argues that humans, aware of extinction, have a duty to keep life going in space over the next 500 years, and sees commercial flights turning access into a question of money ([Chris Mason](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/chris-mason-space-travel-colonization-and-long-term-survival-in-space/)).
- Mason hopes AI inherits the same duty to protect life, and suggests an uncertain system should consult other AIs ([Chris Mason](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/chris-mason-space-travel-colonization-and-long-term-survival-in-space/)).
