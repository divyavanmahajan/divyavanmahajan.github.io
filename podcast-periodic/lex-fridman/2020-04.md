## Overview

April was dominated by physics, with three guests offering very different routes to fundamental theory. David Silver gave the month's main AI conversation, on reinforcement learning and self-play. The early pandemic shaped two episodes, and Jack Dorsey brought payments and Bitcoin into the mix.

## Main themes

### Learning by self-play

- David Silver argues intelligence can be framed as reinforcement learning, and that a system able to correct its own errors can climb from random play to superhuman level ([Silver](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/david-silver-alphago-alphazero-and-deep-reinforcement-learning/)).
- He rated self-play alone a 50/50 bet. AlphaGo Zero then beat the earlier AlphaGo 100 games to zero, and AlphaZero worked unmodified on chess and shogi ([Silver](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/david-silver-alphago-alphazero-and-deep-reinforcement-learning/)).
- Silver says MuZero learns a model without being given the rules, and cites uses of AlphaZero in chemical synthesis and quantum computation ([Silver](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/david-silver-alphago-alphazero-and-deep-reinforcement-learning/)).

### New theories of everything

- Stephen Wolfram argues simple rules can compute as richly as brains, so many outcomes are irreducible. He bets the universe runs on a simple hypergraph rewriting rule ([Wolfram](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/stephen-wolfram-cellular-automata-computation-and-physics/)).
- Eric Weinstein presents Geometric Unity, a 14-dimensional framework he has worked on for over 30 years, but admits he cannot say at what energy its predicted particles would appear ([Weinstein](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/eric-weinstein-geometric-unity-and-the-call-for-new-ideas-leaders-inst/)).
- Harry Cliff describes the Large Hadron Collider as a microscope for the vacuum. He says the Higgs looks fine-tuned, and a decade of data has not shown the new particles many expected ([Cliff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/harry-cliff-particle-physics-and-the-large-hadron-collider/)).
- Cliff hopes machine learning on raw detector hits can improve which collisions get kept ([Cliff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/harry-cliff-particle-physics-and-the-large-hadron-collider/)).

### Evolution, intelligence and simulation

- Richard Dawkins argues that any intelligence, alien, artificial or a simulator, must come from a gradual process such as evolution or engineering ([Dawkins](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/richard-dawkins-evolution-intelligence-simulation-and-memes/)).
- He says machine intelligence is possible because nothing non-physical happens in brains, and that the internet sped up how memes spread ([Dawkins](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/richard-dawkins-evolution-intelligence-simulation-and-memes/)).
- Wolfram sees no bright line between intelligence and mere computation, and proposes competing AI ethics modules rather than one global standard ([Wolfram](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/stephen-wolfram-cellular-automata-computation-and-physics/)).

### Payments, Bitcoin and AI risk

- Dorsey says Square raised merchant approval from roughly 30-40% to 99% by replacing credit checks with risk models built on transaction data ([Dorsey](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/jack-dorsey-square-cryptocurrency-and-artificial-intelligence/)).
- He sees Bitcoin as the internet's native currency and calls unexplainable AI and concentrated data the real risks. He also backs a $1,000-a-month income floor ([Dorsey](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/jack-dorsey-square-cryptocurrency-and-artificial-intelligence/)).

### Science and institutions in the pandemic

- Dmitry Korkin describes how his group used computation and open data to publish an early model of the new coronavirus's proteins within weeks. He argues knowledge, not the journal, is becoming the core value ([Korkin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/dmitry-korkin-computational-biology-of-coronavirus/)).
- Weinstein calls the crisis an institutional failure and accuses public health bodies of misleading people; Fridman partly disagrees, defending some caution ([Weinstein](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2020/04/eric-weinstein-geometric-unity-and-the-call-for-new-ideas-leaders-inst/)).
