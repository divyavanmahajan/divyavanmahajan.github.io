## Overview

July was a geopolitics month. Three of the four episodes dealt with Israel and Palestine from sharply different positions: Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, his critic Yuval Noah Harari and Palestinian writer Mohammed el-Kurd. Robert F. Kennedy Jr. covered Ukraine and US institutions, and AI came in through Harari's warnings and Netanyahu's view on jobs.

## Main themes

### Israel and Palestine, three ways

- Netanyahu says the core obstacle is Palestinian refusal to recognise a Jewish state in any boundary. He proposes Palestinian self-government with Israel keeping overall security ([Netanyahu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/benjamin-netanyahu-israel-palestine-power-corruption-hate-and-peace/)).
- He argues for an outside-in peace, tracing the Abraham Accords with the UAE, Bahrain, Morocco and Sudan to shared concern about Iran and hoping to add Saudi Arabia ([Netanyahu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/benjamin-netanyahu-israel-palestine-power-corruption-hate-and-peace/)).
- Harari says neither side has enough motivation for peace, and argues the government's plan for the Supreme Court would remove Israel's only check on power ([Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).
- El-Kurd frames the conflict as one over land and dispossession, starting from his family's home in Sheikh Jarrah. He says: “I don’t think there’s a geography in which a two state solution is possible” ([el-Kurd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/mohammed-el-kurd-palestine/)).

### Antisemitism, anti-Zionism and violence

- Netanyahu does not distinguish anti-Zionism from antisemitism; el-Kurd rejects that claim and criticises the ADL ([Netanyahu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/benjamin-netanyahu-israel-palestine-power-corruption-hate-and-peace/), [el-Kurd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/mohammed-el-kurd-palestine/)).
- Fridman presses el-Kurd on the glorification of violence and on Israeli security concerns. El-Kurd says violence alone will not bring change ([el-Kurd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/mohammed-el-kurd-palestine/)).

### Ukraine, Putin and American power

- Kennedy calls Russia's invasion illegal and brutal but provoked by NATO expansion since 1997, describes a proxy war Ukraine cannot win, and would negotiate with Putin and Zelenskyy ([Kennedy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/robert-f-kennedy-jr-cia-power-corruption-war-freedom-and-meaning/)).
- Harari says peace in Ukraine depends on Putin choosing to withdraw ([Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).
- Kennedy argues US institutions, from the CIA to regulators he sees as captured by drug companies, have drifted from democratic values. Fridman urges listeners to also hear his scientific critics ([Kennedy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/robert-f-kennedy-jr-cia-power-corruption-war-freedom-and-meaning/)).

### AI that decides, persuades and replaces

- Harari calls AI the first tool that can make decisions and create ideas by itself, and warns that machines built to grab intimacy are more dangerous than those built for attention ([Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).
- He treats consciousness as a social convention and says “This is the most important thing to ask about AI: Can it suffer?” ([Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).
- Netanyahu bets “it will annihilate many more jobs than it will create”; Fridman bets the opposite. Both Netanyahu and Kennedy see a need for AI regulation or treaties ([Netanyahu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/benjamin-netanyahu-israel-palestine-power-corruption-hate-and-peace/), [Kennedy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/robert-f-kennedy-jr-cia-power-corruption-war-freedom-and-meaning/)).
