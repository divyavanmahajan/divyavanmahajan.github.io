## Overview

July was a month of politics and geopolitics. Fridman spoke with Benjamin Netanyahu, Yuval Noah Harari and Mohammed el-Kurd in turn, giving three views of Israel and Palestine, and with presidential candidate Robert F. Kennedy Jr. on US power. AI came up mainly as a question of jobs and of who controls decisions.

## Main themes

### Israel and Palestine from three sides

- Netanyahu argues for an outside-in peace: normalise relations with Arab states first, as with the Abraham Accords, then offer Palestinian self-rule under overall Israeli security control ([Netanyahu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/benjamin-netanyahu-israel-palestine-power-corruption-hate-and-peace/)).
- Harari, a critic of the Netanyahu government, describes a de facto three-class arrangement but rejects the apartheid analogy ([Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).
- El-Kurd, from Sheikh Jarrah, argues the conflict is about land and dispossession, not religion. He says settlements make two states geographically impossible, while Fridman presses him on violence and Israeli security ([El-Kurd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/mohammed-el-kurd-palestine/)).

### Courts, power and corruption

- Netanyahu says Israel's Supreme Court became the most activist on the planet and that he dropped the sweeping override clause months earlier. He denies being corrupted by power ([Netanyahu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/benjamin-netanyahu-israel-palestine-power-corruption-hate-and-peace/)).
- Harari argues the reform would remove Israel's only check on government power ([Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).
- El-Kurd compares the legality of evictions to Jim Crow and says Israeli courts would not consider his family's older documents ([El-Kurd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/mohammed-el-kurd-palestine/)).

### US power, the CIA and Ukraine

- Kennedy calls Russia's invasion illegal but says the US provoked it through NATO expansion, and would negotiate with Putin and Zelenskyy. Harari says peace depends on Putin choosing to withdraw ([Kennedy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/robert-f-kennedy-jr-cia-power-corruption-war-freedom-and-meaning/), [Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).
- Kennedy argues the CIA and regulators captured by drug companies have drifted from democratic values. Fridman opened by urging listeners to hear his scientific critics and disputed his vaccine-trial claims ([Kennedy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/robert-f-kennedy-jr-cia-power-corruption-war-freedom-and-meaning/)).
- Harari offers a counterpoint on hidden power: he says global cabal theories are always wrong because the world is too complex for a small group to control ([Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).

### AI, jobs and human stories

- Harari separates intelligence from consciousness and warns that AI is the first tool that can make decisions and create ideas by itself. He wants AI barred from pretending to be human ([Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).
- After talks with Sam Altman and Elon Musk, Netanyahu says Israel must enter AI with full force and expects AI to destroy more jobs than it creates. Fridman bets on the opposite ([Netanyahu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/benjamin-netanyahu-israel-palestine-power-corruption-hate-and-peace/)).
- Kennedy uses JFK's Cold War diplomacy to argue for treaties on AI ([Kennedy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/robert-f-kennedy-jr-cia-power-corruption-war-freedom-and-meaning/)).
