## Overview

The Lex Fridman Podcast ran 96 episodes in 2022, roughly two a week, across a very wide range of guests. Russia's war in Ukraine shaped the political conversations from spring onward. AI stayed a steady thread, from Yann LeCun in January to Noam Brown in December. Alongside it ran recurring debates on money and economic systems, alien life and consciousness, and what makes people great at chess, poker and grappling.

## Main themes

### War in Ukraine and the wider geopolitics

- Oliver Stone reads the war against decades of US policy while Fridman pushes back ([Oliver Stone](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/oliver-stone-vladimir-putin-and-war-in-ukraine/)); a week later Stephen Kotkin calls the invasion a strategic choice, not a reaction to NATO expansion ([Stephen Kotkin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/stephen-kotkin-putin-zelenskyy-and-war-in-ukraine/)).
- Noam Chomsky calls the invasion a supreme crime and warns about a US-China war ([Noam Chomsky](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/08/noam-chomsky-putin-ukraine-china-and-nuclear-war/)). Fiona Hill asks the West for strategic empathy about how Putin thinks ([Fiona Hill](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/fiona-hill-vladimir-putin-and-donald-trump/)).
- Chris Blattman explains why wars are rare and what squeezes out a peaceful bargain ([Chris Blattman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/chris-blattman-war-and-violence/)). Abbas Amanat reads Iran's protests as a youth-led break with the Islamic Republic ([Abbas Amanat](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/abbas-amanat-iran-protests-mahsa-amini-history-cia-nuclear-weapons/)).

### AI from world models to general agents

- Yann LeCun argues self-supervised learning of predictive world models is the missing piece for common sense ([Yann LeCun](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/01/yann-lecun-dark-matter-of-intelligence-and-self-supervised-learning/)).
- DeepMind's Demis Hassabis sees general learning systems as tools for science, starting with AlphaFold ([Demis Hassabis](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/07/demis-hassabis-deepmind/)). Oriol Vinyals presents Gato as the start of general agents ([Oriol Vinyals](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/07/oriol-vinyals-deep-learning-and-artificial-general-intelligence/)).
- Andrej Karpathy describes Tesla's camera-only data engine as Software 2.0 at production scale ([Andrej Karpathy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving-optimus-aliens-and-agi/)). John Carmack bets AGI will rest on a few simple insights ([John Carmack](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/08/john-carmack-doom-quake-vr-agi-programming-video-games-and-rockets/)).
- Ray Kurzweil keeps his 2029 Turing test and 2045 singularity forecasts ([Ray Kurzweil](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/ray-kurzweil-singularity-superintelligence-and-immortality/)). Noam Brown explains how winning Diplomacy required modelling humans and trust ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/noam-brown-ai-vs-humans-in-poker-and-games-of-strategic-negotiation/)).

### Money, crypto and economic systems

- Michael Saylor and Saifedean Ammous make the case for Bitcoin as sound money ([Michael Saylor](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/michael-saylor-bitcoin-inflation-and-the-future-of-money/), [Saifedean Ammous](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/saifedean-ammous-bitcoin-anarchy-and-austrian-economics/)). Brian Armstrong wants clear regulation to take crypto to a billion users ([Brian Armstrong](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/07/brian-armstrong-coinbase-cryptocurrency-and-government-regulation/)).
- By December, Coffeezilla argues the FTX collapse was designed deception ([Coffeezilla](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/coffeezilla-sbf-ftx-fraud-scams-fake-gurus-money-fame-and-power/)).
- Marxism got a hearing from both sides: Richard Wolff and Bhaskar Sunkara for, Douglas Murray against ([Richard Wolff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/richard-wolff-marxism-and-communism/), [Bhaskar Sunkara](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/bhaskar-sunkara-the-case-for-socialism/), [Douglas Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/douglas-murray-racism-marxism-and-the-war-on-the-west/)). Magatte Wade blames over-regulation for Africa's poverty ([Magatte Wade](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/08/magatte-wade-africa-capitalism-communism-and-the-future-of-humanity/)).

### Aliens, the origin of life and consciousness

- Sara Walker and Lee Cronin propose assembly theory as a way to detect life ([Alien Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/alien-debate-sara-walker-and-lee-cronin/)). Nick Lane argues simple life may be common and intelligence rare ([Nick Lane](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/nick-lane-origin-of-life-evolution-aliens-biology-and-consciousness/)).
- Garry Nolan and pilot Ryan Graves want UFO reports studied openly as data ([Garry Nolan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/garry-nolan-ufos-and-aliens/), [Ryan Graves](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/08/ryan-graves-ufos-fighter-jets-and-aliens/)).
- Philip Goff, Donald Hoffman and Annaka Harris each argue consciousness may be fundamental rather than a product of brains ([Philip Goff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/philip-goff-consciousness-panpsychism-and-the-philosophy-of-mind/), [Donald Hoffman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/donald-hoffman-reality-is-an-illusion-how-evolution-hid-the-truth/), [Annaka Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/annaka-harris-free-will-consciousness-and-the-nature-of-reality/)).

### Mastery in games and sport

- Magnus Carlsen credits evaluation and intuition over calculation ([Magnus Carlsen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/08/magnus-carlsen-greatest-chess-player-of-all-time/)); Hikaru Nakamura calls top chess mostly psychological ([Hikaru Nakamura](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/hikaru-nakamura-chess-magnus-kasparov-and-the-psychology-of-greatness/)).
- Daniel Negreanu blends solver-based game theory with reads of real opponents ([Daniel Negreanu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/daniel-negreanu-poker/)). John Danaher says confidence comes from accumulated physical skill ([John Danaher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/john-danaher-submission-grappling-adcc-animal-combat-and-knives/)).
