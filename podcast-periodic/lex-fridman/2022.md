## Overview

In 2022 the Lex Fridman Podcast was pulled toward Russia's invasion of Ukraine, returning to Putin and the war with historians, spies, a filmmaker and Noam Chomsky. A second strand set Marxism and communism against their critics. AI stayed central through DeepMind, Yann LeCun and Andrej Karpathy, often tied to questions about consciousness. Guests on the origin of life and aliens, and masters of chess, music and grappling, filled out the year.

## Main themes

### Putin and the war in Ukraine

- Chris Blattman explains war as rare and costly, driven mainly by unaccountable power, and defends Ukraine's refusal to cede sovereignty ([Chris Blattman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/chris-blattman-war-and-violence/)).
- Oliver Stone reads the war against decades of US policy while Lex pushes back ([Oliver Stone](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/oliver-stone-vladimir-putin-and-war-in-ukraine/)); Stephen Kotkin calls it a strategic choice, not a result of NATO expansion, and says “Stalin presided over the World War II victory, and then he lost the peace” ([Stephen Kotkin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/05/stephen-kotkin-putin-zelenskyy-and-war-in-ukraine/)).
- Former spies Jack Barsky and Andrew Bustamante add intelligence views on Putin and Ukraine ([Jack Barsky](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/07/jack-barsky-kgb-spy/), [Andrew Bustamante](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/08/andrew-bustamante-cia-spy/)).
- Chomsky pairs military support with diplomacy ([Noam Chomsky](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/08/noam-chomsky-putin-ukraine-china-and-nuclear-war/)); Fiona Hill links US dysfunction to Putin's war and to Trump ([Fiona Hill](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/fiona-hill-vladimir-putin-and-donald-trump/)).

### Marxism, communism and their critics

- Richard Wolff says Marxism is about who controls the workplace surplus, not state control ([Richard Wolff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/richard-wolff-marxism-and-communism/)); Douglas Murray says it has failed wherever tried ([Douglas Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/douglas-murray-racism-marxism-and-the-war-on-the-west/)).
- December paired Michael Malice on the Soviet record with Bhaskar Sunkara's case for market socialism ([Michael Malice](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/michael-malice-christmas-special/), [Bhaskar Sunkara](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/bhaskar-sunkara-the-case-for-socialism/)).

### AI, from world models to Software 2.0

- Yann LeCun argues self-supervised world models are the missing piece for common sense ([Yann LeCun](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/01/yann-lecun-dark-matter-of-intelligence-and-self-supervised-learning/)).
- Demis Hassabis frames DeepMind's systems as tools for science and guesses “consciousness and intelligence are double dissociable” ([Demis Hassabis](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/07/demis-hassabis-deepmind/)); Oriol Vinyals presents Gato as a first general agent ([Oriol Vinyals](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/07/oriol-vinyals-deep-learning-and-artificial-general-intelligence/)).
- John Carmack bets AGI rests on a few simple insights ([John Carmack](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/08/john-carmack-doom-quake-vr-agi-programming-video-games-and-rockets/)); Andrej Karpathy describes Tesla's data engine as Software 2.0 at scale ([Andrej Karpathy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/10/andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving-optimus-aliens-and-agi/)).

### Origins of life and the search for aliens

- Lee Cronin and Sara Walker propose assembly theory as a way to tell evolved objects apart ([Lee Cronin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/03/lee-cronin-origin-of-life-aliens-complexity-and-consciousness/), [Alien Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/04/alien-debate-sara-walker-and-lee-cronin/)).
- Nick Lane argues simple life may be common but intelligence rare ([Nick Lane](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/nick-lane-origin-of-life-evolution-aliens-biology-and-consciousness/)); Robin Hanson argues for fast-expanding grabby aliens ([Robin Hanson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/robin-hanson-alien-civilizations-ufos-and-the-future-of-humanity/)).
- The year closed with Nathalie Cabrol and Betül Kaçar on how to search for life ([Nathalie Cabrol](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/nathalie-cabrol-search-for-alien-life/), [Betül Kaçar](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/12/betul-kacar-bet-l-ka-ar-origin-of-life-ancient-dna-panspermia-and-alie/)).

### Social media, polarisation and free speech

- Jonathan Haidt says “twitter in particular is making our public square much worse” ([Jonathan Haidt](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/jonathan-haidt-the-case-against-social-media/)); Tim Urban traces polarisation to environment rather than human nature ([Tim Urban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/02/tim-urban-elon-musk-neuralink-ai-aliens-and-the-future-of-humanity/)).
- Ben Shapiro and Destiny both defend broad free speech from opposite sides ([Ben Shapiro](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/ben-shapiro-politics-kanye-trump-biden-hitler-extremism-and-war/), [Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/destiny-politics-free-speech-controversy-sex-war-and-relationships/)).
