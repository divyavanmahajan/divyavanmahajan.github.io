## Overview

August moved away from AI towards geopolitics, history and old-school software. Economist Keyu Jin and antiwar writer Scott Horton both discussed Donald Trump, from trade policy and from foreign policy. Jack Weatherford covered Genghis Khan, and former Microsoft engineer Dave Plummer told stories about Windows NT and life with autism.

## Main themes

### China's economy and the trade war

- Keyu Jin says she has “rarely seen a more capitalist society than China, from the pure economic side”. She credits the mayor economy, where local officials compete on growth for promotion ([Keyu Jin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/keyu-jin-china-s-economy-tariffs-trade-trump-communism-capitalism/)).
- She argues US export controls ended 20 years of stalled Chinese chip development and sped up domestic capacity. She cites DeepSeek, a private company, as crisis innovation and warns that leverage such as rare earths weakens each time it is used ([Keyu Jin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/keyu-jin-china-s-economy-tariffs-trade-trump-communism-capitalism/)).
- Jin says China prepared five years for Trump's return, and that US trade deficits widened because the US saves less than it invests ([Keyu Jin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/keyu-jin-china-s-economy-tariffs-trade-trump-communism-capitalism/)).
- China's real problems, in her view, are weak consumption, eroding meritocracy and the property downturn, which she says takes three to five years to work through in a good case and ten in a bad one ([Keyu Jin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/keyu-jin-china-s-economy-tariffs-trade-trump-communism-capitalism/)).

### US wars, blowback and Gaza

- Scott Horton argues that US interventions since the Cold War were sold on false pretexts and served bureaucrats, lobbies and arms makers. Fridman opens with Brown University estimates of 900,000 to 940,000 direct deaths and $8 trillion spent on the post-9/11 wars ([Scott Horton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/scott-horton-the-case-against-war-and-the-military-industrial-complex/)).
- Citing Chalmers Johnson, Horton defines blowback as “the long-term consequences of secret foreign policies” and says 1990s planners treated terrorism as a price of superpower status ([Scott Horton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/scott-horton-the-case-against-war-and-the-military-industrial-complex/)).
- Horton calls Israel's campaign in Gaza a genocide and blames Benjamin Netanyahu for propping up Hamas. Gaza also came up in Fridman's closing remarks in the Weatherford episode ([Scott Horton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/scott-horton-the-case-against-war-and-the-military-industrial-complex/), [Jack Weatherford](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/jack-weatherford-genghis-khan-and-the-mongol-empire/)).
- On Ukraine, Horton says the US provoked the war through NATO expansion; Fridman puts chief responsibility on Putin. Horton thinks Trump could make peace with Iran, Russia, China and North Korea ([Scott Horton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/scott-horton-the-case-against-war-and-the-military-industrial-complex/)).

### Genghis Khan beyond conquest

- Jack Weatherford traces Temüjin's early life of betrayal and calls the kidnapping of his wife Börte the defining moment of his life ([Jack Weatherford](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/jack-weatherford-genghis-khan-and-the-mongol-empire/)).
- He argues the lasting legacy lies in institutions: individual religious freedom, protected envoys and trade, merit-based promotion and women ruling in their own name ([Jack Weatherford](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/jack-weatherford-genghis-khan-and-the-mongol-empire/)).

### Old-school Microsoft and autism

- Dave Plummer built Task Manager to be small and robust, shipping it at about 87k. He says it is now used about two billion times a month ([Dave Plummer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/dave-plummer-programming-autism-and-old-school-microsoft-stories/)).
- Debugging NT meant working in raw assembly across four instruction sets because there was no source-level debugging ([Dave Plummer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/dave-plummer-programming-autism-and-old-school-microsoft-stories/)).
- Plummer describes autism through monotropism and rejects the idea that autistic people lack empathy. He advises them to sell their work rather than their personality ([Dave Plummer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/dave-plummer-programming-autism-and-old-school-microsoft-stories/)).
