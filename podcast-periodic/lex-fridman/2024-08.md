## Overview

August mixed technology and incentives. Elon Musk and the Neuralink team made the case for brain interfaces, and Pieter Levels explained how one developer builds profitable AI products. Craig Jones and Cenk Uygur, in very different arenas, both argued that money decides who wins and set out to change the rules.

## Main themes

### Neuralink and the bandwidth argument

- Elon Musk argues human output bandwidth is the bottleneck between people and AI, and that faster output could help keep collective human will aligned with AI ([Neuralink](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/elon-musk-and-neuralink-team-elon-musk-neuralink-and-the-future-of-hum/)).
- The Neuralink team describes a nearer goal: restoring digital independence to people with paralysis. They frame the user experience, not the hardware, as the main open problem ([Neuralink](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/elon-musk-and-neuralink-team-elon-musk-neuralink-and-the-future-of-hum/)).
- Noland Arbaugh, the first recipient, describes using a computer at night without a caregiver and wants to control Optimus next ([Neuralink](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/elon-musk-and-neuralink-team-elon-musk-neuralink-and-the-future-of-hum/)).

### Solo builders and viral AI products

- Pieter Levels launches ideas within about two weeks and counts them as validated only when people pay. Nomad List began as a public Google spreadsheet ([Pieter Levels](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/pieter-levels-programming-viral-ai-startups-and-digital-nomad-life/)).
- He says Interior AI made 10-20K a month within a week, and his AI avatar launch ran on a Stripe link and a Typeform before Lensa built a similar feature ([Pieter Levels](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/pieter-levels-programming-viral-ai-startups-and-digital-nomad-life/)).
- Levels credits a plain PHP, jQuery and SQLite stack, deploying straight to production and automating instead of hiring, with GPT-4o moderating reviews and a large chat ([Pieter Levels](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/pieter-levels-programming-viral-ai-startups-and-digital-nomad-life/)).
- He argues regulation and a lack of entrepreneurial culture hold Europe back ([Pieter Levels](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/pieter-levels-programming-viral-ai-startups-and-digital-nomad-life/)).

### Changing who gets paid

- Craig Jones says a second-place ADCC finish earned him only $6,000. His CJI tournament, funded by an anonymous friend, pays every entrant $10,001 and $1 million to each division winner ([Craig Jones](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/craig-jones-jiu-jitsu-2-million-prize-cji-adcc-ukraine-trolling/)).
- CJI streams free, runs as a nonprofit and wants public view counts so athletes can use them with sponsors. Jones says ADCC then equalised women's prize money ([Craig Jones](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/craig-jones-jiu-jitsu-2-million-prize-cji-adcc-ukraine-trolling/)).
- On trips to Ukraine, soldiers told Jones a cheap FPV drone can destroy a multi-million-dollar tank, and Fridman worries about autonomous drone swarms ([Craig Jones](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/craig-jones-jiu-jitsu-2-million-prize-cji-adcc-ukraine-trolling/)).

### Money in politics and the 2024 race

- Cenk Uygur says the real divide is populism versus corporatism, and that corporate money has captured both parties, the courts and the media ([Cenk Uygur](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/cenk-uygur-trump-vs-harris-progressive-politics-communism-capitalism/)).
- His fix is a constitutional amendment ending private campaign finance and corporate personhood. Fridman suggests term limits instead and defends companies that build things ([Cenk Uygur](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/cenk-uygur-trump-vs-harris-progressive-politics-communism-capitalism/)).
- Uygur gives Kamala Harris a 55% chance of winning, calls Trump's fake electors scheme disqualifying, and predicts cable will collapse ([Cenk Uygur](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/08/cenk-uygur-trump-vs-harris-progressive-politics-communism-capitalism/)).
