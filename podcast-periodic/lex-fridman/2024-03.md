## Overview

March brought two of the year's main AI conversations, with Yann LeCun and Sam Altman, who disagree on how AGI will be reached but both worry about concentrated power. Three episodes covered war: Ukraine's history, a five-hour Israel-Palestine debate and a nuclear war scenario. The rest were founder stories from Kimbal Musk, Dana White and Mark Cuban.

## Main themes

### Two views of the road to AGI

- Yann LeCun argues autoregressive LLMs lack understanding of the physical world, persistent memory, reasoning and planning. He wants joint-embedding architectures such as V-JEPA that learn world models from video ([Yann LeCun](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/yann-lecun-meta-ai-open-source-limits-of-llms-agi-the-future-of-ai/)).
- Sam Altman says GPT-4 falls well short of where models are heading and expects the step to GPT-5 to match the one from GPT-3 to GPT-4. He defines AGI by a meaningful rise in the rate of scientific discovery ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/sam-altman-openai-gpt-5-sora-board-saga-elon-musk-ilya-power-agi/)).
- Altman sees compute demand behaving like energy, with energy the hardest constraint. LeCun contrasts a GPU's half to one kilowatt with about 25 watts for the brain ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/sam-altman-openai-gpt-5-sora-board-saga-elon-musk-ilya-power-agi/), [Yann LeCun](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/yann-lecun-meta-ai-open-source-limits-of-llms-agi-the-future-of-ai/)).

### Power, governance and openness in AI

- Altman calls the November 2023 board crisis the most painful professional experience of his life. He says no single person, himself included, should control AGI ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/sam-altman-openai-gpt-5-sora-board-saga-elon-musk-ilya-power-agi/)).
- LeCun argues open-source foundation models keep AI assistants diverse, the way a free press does, and that an unbiased system is impossible ([Yann LeCun](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/yann-lecun-meta-ai-open-source-limits-of-llms-agi-the-future-of-ai/)).
- Mark Cuban calls open-sourcing models a smart business choice that should not be forced, and expects millions of specialised, licensed models ([Mark Cuban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/mark-cuban-shark-tank-dei-wokeism-debate-elon-musk-politics-drugs/)).

### War, history and catastrophe

- Serhii Plokhy calls the Russo-Ukrainian war a war of Soviet succession and says NATO is a pretext. He notes Finland's accession doubled Russia's NATO border without any matching troop shift ([Serhii Plokhy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/serhii-plokhy-history-of-ukraine-russia-soviet-union-kgb-nazis-war/)).
- In a hostile debate, Norman Finkelstein and Mouin Rabbani say displacement was built into Zionism, while Benny Morris and Steven Bonnell blame Arab rejection of offers. None of the four expects peace soon ([Israel-Palestine Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/israel-palestine-debate-finkelstein-destiny-m-rabbani-benny-morris/)).
- Annie Jacobsen says launch on warning leaves the US president about six minutes to order a counterstrike, with no one's permission needed. She sees communication between adversaries as the only way out ([Annie Jacobsen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/annie-jacobsen-nuclear-war-cia-kgb-aliens-area-51-roswell-secrecy/)).

### Builders and promoters

- Kimbal Musk describes Zip2, a founding seat on Tesla's board and SpaceX launches, and argues shared meals create connection people lack ([Kimbal Musk](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/kimbal-musk-the-art-of-cooking-tesla-spacex-zip2-and-family/)).
- Dana White says the UFC grew by fixing what he disliked in boxing: commentators who talked fighters down and promoters who avoided risky matchups ([Dana White](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/dana-white-ufc-fighting-khabib-conor-tyson-ali-rogan-elon-zuck/)).
- Cuban argues transparency can disrupt opaque industries. Cost Plus Drugs publishes its cost, a 15% markup and fees, and he accuses the big pharmacy benefit managers of chasing rebates. He also defends DEI against Fridman's pushback ([Mark Cuban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/03/mark-cuban-shark-tank-dei-wokeism-debate-elon-musk-politics-drugs/)).
