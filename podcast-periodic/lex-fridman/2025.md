## Overview

The Lex Fridman Podcast spent 2025 moving between war and politics, technology and deep history. The first half leaned on world leaders and debates about Ukraine, Gaza and Iran. AI came through big-company leaders and working programmers rather than as a weekly topic. The year closed with long conversations on minds, ancient writing and the foundations of mathematics.

## Main themes

### War, peace and world leaders

- Volodymyr Zelenskyy argues a ceasefire without hard security guarantees would only let Putin rearm ([Zelenskyy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/01/volodymyr-zelenskyy-ukraine-war-peace-putin-trump-nato-and-freedom/)); Narendra Modi presents a nation-first but peace-seeking foreign policy ([Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)).
- Douglas Murray says democracies in Ukraine and Gaza face enemies that want something other than peace, while Lex pushes for compromise ([Douglas Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/douglas-murray-putin-zelenskyy-trump-israel-netanyahu-hamas-gaza/)).
- Dave Smith and Scott Horton argue US wars create the blowback they claim to fight ([Dave Smith](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/dave-smith-israel-ukraine-epstein-mossad-conspiracies-antisemitism/), [Scott Horton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/scott-horton-the-case-against-war-and-the-military-industrial-complex/)). Horton and Mark Dubowitz debate the US strikes on Iran ([Iran War Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/06/iran-war-debate-nuclear-weapons-trump-peace-power-the-middle-east/)).

### AI, compute and the craft of programming

- Dylan Patel and Nathan Lambert say DeepSeek's cheap models came from real engineering, and the contest with China turns on chips and power ([DeepSeek](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/02/deepseek-dylan-patel-nathan-lambert-deepseek-china-openai-nvidia-xai-t/)).
- Sundar Pichai expects dramatic progress by 2030 but AGI slightly later ([Sundar Pichai](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/06/sundar-pichai-ceo-of-google-and-alphabet/)); Demis Hassabis calls AGI by 2030 a coin flip ([Demis Hassabis](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/07/demis-hassabis-future-of-ai-simulating-reality-physics-and-video-games/)).
- ThePrimeagen and DHH defend hard-won skill and hand-typed code over AI autopilot ([ThePrimeagen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/theprimeagen-programming-ai-adhd-productivity-addiction-and-god/), [DHH](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/07/dhh-david-heinemeier-hansson-dhh-future-of-programming-ai-ruby-on-rail/)); Dan Houser doubts LLMs will produce magic in games ([Dan Houser](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/10/dan-houser-gta-red-dead-redemption-rockstar-absurd-future-of-gaming/)).

### China, economics and American politics

- Jeffrey Wasserstrom describes Xi Jinping's selective use of Mao and Confucius ([Wasserstrom](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/jeffrey-wasserstrom-china-xi-jinping-trade-war-taiwan-hong-kong-mao/)); Keyu Jin says China's economy is decentralised and that tariffs backfired on the US ([Keyu Jin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/keyu-jin-china-s-economy-tariffs-trade-trump-communism-capitalism/)).
- Marc Andreessen predicts a growth boom as regulation eases ([Marc Andreessen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/01/marc-andreessen-trump-power-tech-ai-immigration-future-of-america/)); Ezra Klein and Derek Thompson want Democrats to trade process for outcomes ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).

### History, from clay tablets to World War II

- Irving Finkel says the Genesis flood borrows from the Mesopotamian story: “It wasn’t two stories about the same thing. It was literary dependence.” ([Irving Finkel](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/12/irving-finkel-deciphering-secrets-of-ancient-civilizations-flood-myths/)).
- James Holland argues World War II was decided by logistics and production ([James Holland](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/05/james-holland-world-war-ii-hitler-churchill-stalin-biggest-battles/)); Norman Ohler adds drugs as a missing layer ([Norman Ohler](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/09/norman-ohler-hitler-nazis-drugs-ww2-blitzkrieg-lsd-mkultra-cia/)).
- Jack Weatherford credits Genghis Khan with merit, trade and religious freedom more than conquest ([Genghis Khan](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/jack-weatherford-genghis-khan-and-the-mongol-empire/)).

### Mathematics, minds and physics

- Joel David Hamkins says “almost every major result in mathematical logic is using in an abstract way the idea of diagonalization” and argues for a set-theoretic multiverse ([Hamkins](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/12/joel-david-hamkins-infinity-paradoxes-that-broke-mathematics-g-del-inc/)).
- Michael Levin treats intelligence as a continuum found by experiment: “I don’t believe in any such line.” ([Michael Levin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/michael-levin-hidden-reality-of-alien-intelligence-biological-life/)).
- Terence Tao expects Lean-verified, AI-assisted proofs to change mathematics ([Terence Tao](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/06/terence-tao-hardest-problems-in-mathematics-physics-the-future-of-ai/)); David Kirtley says Helion aims to reach the grid by 2028 ([David Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
