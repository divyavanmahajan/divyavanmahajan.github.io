## Overview

Lex Fridman's 2025 opened with Trump's return to power, which ran through eight episodes as a central subject. The first half of the year was dominated by war and politics: Ukraine, Gaza, Iran, DOGE and China. AI came in through DeepSeek, Google's leaders and a run of programmers asking what AI means for their craft. The second half turned more to history, science and mathematics.

## Main themes

### Trump's second term and American politics

- Marc Andreessen expects a growth boom as censorship and regulation ease, and backs DOGE ([Marc Andreessen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/01/marc-andreessen-trump-power-tech-ai-immigration-future-of-america/)).
- Ezra Klein and Derek Thompson see DOGE as about control, not efficiency ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).
- Narendra Modi presents a nation-first but peace-seeking foreign policy ([Narendra Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)), and Keyu Jin argues US tariffs and export controls have backfired ([Keyu Jin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/keyu-jin-china-s-economy-tariffs-trade-trump-communism-capitalism/)).

### Wars in Ukraine and the Middle East

- Zelenskyy wants hard security guarantees before any ceasefire: “If he manages to make a ceasefire deal, and three months later, Putin launches a new wave of attacks, what will Trump look like?” ([Zelenskyy](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/01/volodymyr-zelenskyy-ukraine-war-peace-putin-trump-nato-and-freedom/)).
- Douglas Murray says in Ukraine and Gaza a democracy faces an enemy that does not want peace ([Douglas Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/douglas-murray-putin-zelenskyy-trump-israel-netanyahu-hamas-gaza/)).
- Dave Smith and Scott Horton argue US interventions create blowback ([Dave Smith](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/dave-smith-israel-ukraine-epstein-mossad-conspiracies-antisemitism/), [Scott Horton](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/08/scott-horton-the-case-against-war-and-the-military-industrial-complex/)).
- Mark Dubowitz and Horton debate the US strikes on Iran and whether war invites proliferation ([Iran War Debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/06/iran-war-debate-nuclear-weapons-trump-peace-power-the-middle-east/)).

### The AI race and AGI timelines

- Dylan Patel and Nathan Lambert credit DeepSeek's cheap models to real engineering efficiency, with compute, chips and power deciding the race with China ([DeepSeek](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/02/deepseek-dylan-patel-nathan-lambert-deepseek-china-openai-nvidia-xai-t/)).
- Sundar Pichai expects dramatic progress by 2030 but says of AGI, “I don’t think we’ll quite get there by 2030, so my sense is it’s slightly after that” ([Sundar Pichai](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/06/sundar-pichai-ceo-of-google-and-alphabet/)).
- Demis Hassabis calls AGI by 2030 a coin flip ([Demis Hassabis](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/07/demis-hassabis-future-of-ai-simulating-reality-physics-and-video-games/)); Terence Tao sees Lean-verified, AI-assisted proofs changing mathematics ([Terence Tao](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/06/terence-tao-hardest-problems-in-mathematics-physics-the-future-of-ai/)).

### Programmers, platforms and AI

- ThePrimeagen argues hard-won skill still matters, noting “there’s been no computer improvement up to this date that did not result in more jobs” ([ThePrimeagen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/theprimeagen-programming-ai-adhd-productivity-addiction-and-god/)).
- DHH defends small teams and hand-typed code ([DHH](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/07/dhh-david-heinemeier-hansson-dhh-future-of-programming-ai-ruby-on-rail/)).
- Tim Sweeney attacks Apple's 30% app store fee ([Tim Sweeney](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/tim-sweeney-fortnite-unreal-engine-and-the-future-of-gaming/)), and Pavel Durov explains how a 40-person team keeps Telegram independent ([Pavel Durov](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/10/pavel-durov-telegram-freedom-censorship-money-power-human-nature/)).

### History, physics and mathematics

- James Holland says logistics and production decided World War II ([James Holland](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/05/james-holland-world-war-ii-hitler-churchill-stalin-biggest-battles/)); Norman Ohler adds drugs as a missing layer of the war ([Norman Ohler](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/09/norman-ohler-hitler-nazis-drugs-ww2-blitzkrieg-lsd-mkultra-cia/)).
- Helion's David Kirtley argues manufacturing-led iteration will bring fusion to the grid by 2028 ([David Kirtley](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/11/david-kirtley-nuclear-fusion-plasma-physics-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- Joel David Hamkins links Cantor, Gödel and Turing through diagonalisation ([Joel David Hamkins](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/12/joel-david-hamkins-infinity-paradoxes-that-broke-mathematics-g-del-inc/)).
