## Overview

May 2021 mixed hard science with questions about the mind. Two physicists and a planetary scientist covered quarks, moons and alien life, while Sam Harris and Bryan Johnson argued about consciousness and how to measure the brain. Chainlink's Sergey Nazarov, mathematician Po-Shen Loh and coach John Danaher rounded out the month with trust, invention and mastery.

## Main themes

### Trust, contracts and technology

- Sergey Nazarov argues that hybrid smart contracts, which pair on-chain code with decentralized oracle networks, can replace the brand-based guarantees of banks and insurers with agreements that settle on pre-agreed facts ([Chainlink](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/sergey-nazarov-chainlink-smart-contracts-and-oracle-networks/)).
- He compares oracle networks to the Google Maps, Twilio and Stripe APIs that Uber relied on, and points to parametric crop insurance for farmers in emerging markets ([Chainlink](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/sergey-nazarov-chainlink-smart-contracts-and-oracle-networks/)).
- Po-Shen Loh describes NOVID, a contact-tracing app that tells users how many hops away a reported case is, so its incentives work with self-interest rather than against it ([Po-Shen Loh](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/po-shen-loh-mathematics-math-olympiad-combinatorics-contact-tracing/)).

### Consciousness, free will and the brain

- Sam Harris says consciousness is the one thing that cannot be an illusion, while the self and free will can be seen through. Fridman pushes back on his view of AI risk, expecting a slower path through human society ([Sam Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/sam-harris-consciousness-free-will-psychedelics-ai-ufos-and-meaning/)).
- Bryan Johnson frames Kernel's non-invasive headsets as a measurement system for the one major input to society that is still unmeasured, and says only one of 228 investors said yes to his first round ([Kernel](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/bryan-johnson-kernel-brain-computer-interfaces/)).
- Johnson argues brain data needs individual control and the right to delete. Frank Wilczek prefers studying self-awareness through feedback and recurrent neural nets over debating qualia ([Kernel](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/bryan-johnson-kernel-brain-computer-interfaces/), [Frank Wilczek](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/frank-wilczek-physics-of-quarks-dark-matter-complexity-life-aliens/)).

### Learning, invention and mastery

- Loh says the most valuable thing mathematics teaches is how to invent. His CMU exams are open notes and never repeat past problems ([Po-Shen Loh](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/po-shen-loh-mathematics-math-olympiad-combinatorics-contact-tracing/)).
- John Danaher teaches beginners escapes first, calls drilling for numbers a waste of time, and holds that a well-designed program can make a world-class athlete in five years ([John Danaher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/john-danaher-the-path-to-mastery-in-jiu-jitsu-grappling-judo-and-mma/)).
- Asked about an AI that could beat Gordon Ryan, Danaher traces computer chess to AlphaZero and concludes humans should look for heuristic rules ([John Danaher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/john-danaher-the-path-to-mastery-in-jiu-jitsu-grappling-judo-and-mma/)).

### Physics, planets and life elsewhere

- Wilczek says the world has a compact description built on symmetry, nearly complete for technology, while life and minds need complementary concepts ([Frank Wilczek](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/frank-wilczek-physics-of-quarks-dark-matter-complexity-life-aliens/)).
- Katherine de Kleer treats Jupiter's volcanic moon Io as a laboratory for how planets work and ranks Europa, Enceladus and Titan as the likeliest habitats ([Katherine de Kleer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/katherine-de-kleer-planets-moons-and-asteroids-in-our-solar-system/)).
- Both guests are cautious on alien life: de Kleer doubts the Venus phosphine claim, and Wilczek suspects simple life is common but intelligence rare ([Katherine de Kleer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/katherine-de-kleer-planets-moons-and-asteroids-in-our-solar-system/), [Frank Wilczek](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/frank-wilczek-physics-of-quarks-dark-matter-complexity-life-aliens/)).
