## Overview

May 2021 kept returning to the meaning of life, which came up in four of seven episodes, with a grappling coach and a philosopher giving the fullest answers. Sam Harris brought free will and AI risk to the front, while physicists and a mathematician filled the rest of the month with quarks, moons and contact tracing. Two founders, Sergey Nazarov and Bryan Johnson, talked about trust and measurement as products.

## Main themes

### Meaning once survival is solved

- John Danaher argues that “the minute mankind's existence became more or less guaranteed the problems shift from survival to meaning” ([John Danaher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/john-danaher-the-path-to-mastery-in-jiu-jitsu-grappling-judo-and-mma/)).
- Danaher, a materialist, says death gives our days value through scarcity, and that meaning comes from living for something larger than yourself ([John Danaher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/john-danaher-the-path-to-mastery-in-jiu-jitsu-grappling-judo-and-mma/)).
- Sam Harris places meaning in attention to the present moment. Nazarov ties it to choosing to live in society and to the body of work you pursue ([Sam Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/sam-harris-consciousness-free-will-psychedelics-ai-ufos-and-meaning/), [Sergey Nazarov](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/sergey-nazarov-chainlink-smart-contracts-and-oracle-networks/)).

### Free will, consciousness and AI alignment

- Harris says consciousness is the one thing that cannot be an illusion, while the self and free will can. Neither determinism nor randomness gives the authorship people feel they have ([Sam Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/sam-harris-consciousness-free-will-psychedelics-ai-ufos-and-meaning/)).
- Frank Wilczek uses complementarity to let free will and determinism both be valid descriptions ([Frank Wilczek](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/frank-wilczek-physics-of-quarks-dark-matter-complexity-life-aliens/)).
- On superhuman AI, Harris says “there are probably more ways to do it badly than to do it perfectly”. Fridman expects AI to pass through human society, which will force alignment over decades ([Sam Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/sam-harris-consciousness-free-will-psychedelics-ai-ufos-and-meaning/)).
- Danaher walks through computer chess from Deep Blue to AlphaZero after Fridman asks about a robot that could beat Gordon Ryan. Fridman expects a cyborg Gordon Ryan first ([John Danaher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/john-danaher-the-path-to-mastery-in-jiu-jitsu-grappling-judo-and-mma/)).

### Founders building trust and measurement

- Nazarov argues hybrid smart contracts, pairing on-chain code with decentralised oracle networks, can replace brand-based trust. He calls Bitcoin the first true non-governmental fiat money ([Sergey Nazarov](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/sergey-nazarov-chainlink-smart-contracts-and-oracle-networks/)).
- Bryan Johnson frames Kernel's headsets as a measurement system rather than a control device, and says brain data needs individual control and the right to delete ([Bryan Johnson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/bryan-johnson-kernel-brain-computer-interfaces/)).
- Johnson recalls building Braintree around software engineers would love, with customers such as Uber, Airbnb and GitHub, before selling to PayPal ([Bryan Johnson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/bryan-johnson-kernel-brain-computer-interfaces/)).

### Physics from quarks to moons

- Wilczek explains asymptotic freedom, where quarks interact weakly at short distances, and proposes axions as both a fix for the strong CP problem and the dark matter ([Frank Wilczek](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/frank-wilczek-physics-of-quarks-dark-matter-complexity-life-aliens/)).
- Katherine de Kleer treats Io's tidal volcanism as a laboratory for how planets work, and ranks Europa, Enceladus and Titan as the most plausible habitats ([Katherine de Kleer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/katherine-de-kleer-planets-moons-and-asteroids-in-our-solar-system/)).
- De Kleer says asteroid science is mostly statistical, and she stays sceptical of headline claims about phosphine on Venus and an alien 'Oumuamua ([Katherine de Kleer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/katherine-de-kleer-planets-moons-and-asteroids-in-our-solar-system/)).

### Mastery and invention

- Danaher teaches beginners escapes first and calls drilling for numbers a waste of time ([John Danaher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/john-danaher-the-path-to-mastery-in-jiu-jitsu-grappling-judo-and-mma/)).
- Po-Shen Loh says mathematics should teach invention, and shows it in NOVID, an anonymous app that tells users how many hops away a reported case is ([Po-Shen Loh](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2021/05/po-shen-loh-mathematics-math-olympiad-combinatorics-contact-tracing/)).
