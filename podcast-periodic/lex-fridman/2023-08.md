## Overview

August had only two episodes, both about minds. Joscha Bach looked at artificial minds and what conscious AI would take, while Andrew Huberman talked about the human subconscious, loyalty and relationships.

## Main themes

### Machine minds and the AI debate

- Bach calls transformer models ugly and brutalist, like Claude Shannon's brute-force chess strategy. He thinks their gaps are solvable with larger context windows, a database and nightly retraining, but finds them wasteful ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/)).
- He says language models are golems with nobody home, and proposes building small, controlled conscious systems from self-organisation principles instead ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/)).
- Bach takes Eliezer Yudkowsky's warnings seriously but expects a singleton AI, so thinks effective accelerationism is probably wrong. He opposes FDA-like licensing for training models and is not worried by Meta open-sourcing Llama ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/)).
- He maps AI fears onto stages of self-awareness adapted from Robert Kegan, arguing our worries reflect the stage of development we are at ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/)).

### Human minds, peace and relationships

- Citing psychiatrist Paul Conti, Huberman says the subconscious drives most decisions, and calls his mental health series with Conti the most important work of his career ([Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/andrew-huberman-relationships-drama-betrayal-sex-and-love/)).
- He separates overt agreements from covert contracts, hidden resentful taking such as jabs or lateness, and says covert contracts always end badly ([Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/andrew-huberman-relationships-drama-betrayal-sex-and-love/)).
- Huberman says friends in long, happy relationships built the friendship first, and that peace at home and focus at work feed each other ([Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/andrew-huberman-relationships-drama-betrayal-sex-and-love/)).
- Both guests close with life advice. Bach tells young people to create rather than consume, drawing on East Germany after reunification; Huberman passes on the advice that a relationship that does not feel fully right inside will not work ([Bach](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/joscha-bach-life-intelligence-consciousness-ai-the-future-of-humans/), [Huberman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/08/andrew-huberman-relationships-drama-betrayal-sex-and-love/)).
