## Overview

So far in 2026 Lex Fridman has moved away from the politics that filled 2025. AI dominated February and March, with a state-of-AI panel, OpenClaw's creator and Jensen Huang, and returned in August and October through DHH and three mathematicians. The rest of the year has been history, craft and physics, ending with a return to the war in Ukraine.

## Main themes

### Labs, scaling and open weights

- Nathan Lambert and Sebastian Raschka say progress now comes from post-training, inference-time scaling and compute, with no lab holding a lasting lead ([State of AI in 2026](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/02/ai-sota-2026-state-of-ai-in-2026-llms-coding-scaling-laws-china-agents/)).
- Lambert is bullish on scaling: “they’re all really still working, but the low-hanging fruit has mostly been taken” ([State of AI in 2026](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/02/ai-sota-2026-state-of-ai-in-2026-llms-coding-scaling-laws-china-agents/)).
- He expects more open model builders in 2026, many in China, while saying US models are currently better; Raschka credits Chinese models' friendlier licences ([State of AI in 2026](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/02/ai-sota-2026-state-of-ai-in-2026-llms-coding-scaling-laws-china-agents/)).

### Personal agents and agentic engineering

- Peter Steinberger explains how OpenClaw went viral and argues personal agents will replace most apps ([OpenClaw](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/02/peter-steinberger-openclaw-the-viral-ai-agent-that-broke-the-internet/)).
- He calls prompt injection unsolved and says many early security reports came from users exposing the debug interface to the internet ([OpenClaw](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/02/peter-steinberger-openclaw-the-viral-ai-agent-that-broke-the-internet/)).
- On jobs, Steinberger says hand programming will survive, but “it’s gonna be like knitting” ([OpenClaw](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/02/peter-steinberger-openclaw-the-viral-ai-agent-that-broke-the-internet/)).
- DHH, once a sceptic, now has agents write all his shipping code and says taste and product judgement matter more than syntax ([DHH](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/08/dhh-future-of-programming-ai-agentic-engineering-vibe-coding-linux/)).

### NVIDIA and compute

- Jensen Huang argues intelligence now scales with compute, that CUDA's install base is NVIDIA's moat, and that computers have become token factories ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).
- Raschka also sees the CUDA ecosystem, not the chip, as the moat ([State of AI in 2026](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/02/ai-sota-2026-state-of-ai-in-2026-llms-coding-scaling-laws-china-agents/)).
- Huang runs 60 direct reports without one-on-ones ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/jensen-huang-nvidia-the-4-trillion-company-the-ai-revolution/)).

### AI reaches mathematics and craft

- Three mathematicians react to AI proofs on Navier-Stokes blow-up and the Unique Games Conjecture, arguing understanding and good questions matter more once solving is cheap ([AI Crisis in Math](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/10/math-ai-crisis-ai-crisis-in-math-navier-stokes-riemann-hypothesis-comp/)).
- Rick Beato calls AI-generated music a boring idea generator ([Rick Beato](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/03/rick-beato-greatest-guitarists-of-all-time-history-future-of-music/)); FFmpeg and VLC maintainers say a few volunteers carry the world's video infrastructure ([FFmpeg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/05/ffmpeg-the-incredible-technology-behind-video-on-the-internet/)).

### History and war

- Lars Brownworth traces the Vikings from raiders to state builders ([Vikings](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/04/lars-brownworth-vikings-ragnar-berserkers-valhalla-the-warriors-of-the/)); Anthony Kaldellis treats Byzantium as the Roman state continuing ([Anthony Kaldellis](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/06/anthony-kaldellis-roman-empire-byzantine-empire-rise-fall-of-empires/)).
- Gary Gallagher argues slavery caused the Civil War and that Union victory was never inevitable ([Gary Gallagher](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/07/gary-gallagher-american-civil-war-slavery-lincoln-grant-lee/)).
- Michael Kofman and Preston Stewart call 2026 Ukraine's best year on the battlefield, though Putin's aims still exceed his military's reach ([Russia-Ukraine War](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2026/11/ukraine-war-preston-stewart-michael-kofman-russia-ukraine-war-putin-ze/)).
