## Overview

December leaned towards physics and AI risk. Lisa Randall and Lee Cronin defended speculative but testable scientific ideas, and Guillaume Verdon made the e/acc case against safety-led regulation. Jeff Bezos talked about Blue Origin and Amazon's management habits, and Teddy Atlas closed the month with boxing, loyalty and fear.

## Main themes

### Testing speculative science

- Lisa Randall says eight or nine independent methods agree on how much dark matter exists, and defends testing a speculative thin dark disk that might raise the odds of impacts like the one that ended the dinosaurs ([Randall](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/lisa-randall-dark-matter-theoretical-physics-and-extinction-events/)).
- Randall calls the LHC both a victory and a warning: the Higgs confirmed a 50-year-old prediction, but many had expected supersymmetry ([Randall](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/lisa-randall-dark-matter-theoretical-physics-and-extinction-events/)).
- Lee Cronin defends assembly theory from his Nature paper with Sara Walker: a complex object found in many copies signals selection. He says the paper drew anger from evolutionary biologists ([Cronin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/lee-cronin-controversial-nature-paper-on-evolution-of-life-and-univers/)).
- Cronin also argues time is fundamental, so the universe is deterministic looking back but undetermined going forward. Lex Fridman finds the leap to free will too large ([Cronin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/lee-cronin-controversial-nature-paper-on-evolution-of-life-and-univers/)).

### AI risk, regulation and acceleration

- Guillaume Verdon argues thermodynamics favours systems that grow, so AI should stay open and competitive. He treats concentrated power, not AI itself, as the main risk and warns of a government-backed AI cartel ([Verdon](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/guillaume-verdon-beff-jezos-e-acc-movement-physics-computation-agi/)).
- Verdon rejects p(doom) estimates, cites the OpenAI board crisis as proof that centralised control is fragile, and is building a thermodynamic computer for generative AI at Extropic ([Verdon](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/guillaume-verdon-beff-jezos-e-acc-movement-physics-computation-agi/)).
- Cronin puts the chance of AGI doom at zero but sees fake data and fake users as real risks now, while Randall says she has become more concerned about AI losing control ([Cronin](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/lee-cronin-controversial-nature-paper-on-evolution-of-life-and-univers/), [Randall](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/lisa-randall-dark-matter-theoretical-physics-and-extinction-events/)).
- Randall sees large language models as generalisations of existing knowledge, and Bezos calls them discoveries rather than inventions ([Randall](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/lisa-randall-dark-matter-theoretical-physics-and-extinction-events/), [Bezos](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/jeff-bezos-amazon-and-blue-origin/)).

### Bezos on rockets and decisions

- Bezos says reaching orbit is solved and the real problem is cost. For Blue Origin that means building New Glenn at rate, with 24 launches a year implying an upper stage every two weeks ([Bezos](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/jeff-bezos-amazon-and-blue-origin/)).
- He wants to spend his Amazon winnings on heavy space infrastructure, such as Blue Ring and lunar landers, so others can build on it ([Bezos](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/jeff-bezos-amazon-and-blue-origin/)).
- He explains one-way and two-way doors, scepticism of proxy metrics, and six-page memos read in silence instead of slides ([Bezos](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/jeff-bezos-amazon-and-blue-origin/)).

### Loyalty and fear

- Teddy Atlas argues that loyalty keeps people human and fear makes bravery possible. He tells the story of his break with Cus D'Amato over Mike Tyson ([Atlas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/teddy-atlas-mike-tyson-cus-d-amato-boxing-loyalty-fear-greatness/)).
- Atlas calls Tyson sensational but not great, because he did not overcome his few real tests ([Atlas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/teddy-atlas-mike-tyson-cus-d-amato-boxing-loyalty-fear-greatness/)).
