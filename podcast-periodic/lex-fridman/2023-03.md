## Overview

March closed with the show's first big run on AI after GPT-4's launch: Sam Altman defended shipping early, and Eliezer Yudkowsky argued humanity is on course to fail at alignment. Earlier in the month, Sam Harris and Chris Voss argued opposite sides of empathy versus reason, Ginni Rometty talked about leading change at IBM, and Shannon Curry and the B-Team covered relationships and grappling.

## Main themes

### GPT-4 and the alignment debate

- Sam Altman calls GPT-4 early, slow and buggy but still a milestone, and says shipping imperfect models lets the world find flaws while stakes are low ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/sam-altman-openai-ceo-on-gpt-4-chatgpt-and-the-future-of-ai/)).
- Altman credits ChatGPT's leap to RLHF and the chat interface more than the base model. He admits no technique yet aligns a superpowerful system ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/sam-altman-openai-ceo-on-gpt-4-chatgpt-and-the-future-of-ai/)).
- Eliezer Yudkowsky argues alignment must work on the first critical try, that capabilities are far outpacing it, and that large GPU clusters should be shut down. Fridman pushes back that incremental research could close the gap ([Eliezer Yudkowsky](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/eliezer-yudkowsky-dangers-of-ai-and-the-end-of-human-civilization/)).
- Yudkowsky says GPT-4 went further than he expected and opposes open-sourcing such models. Sam Harris says his concern about superintelligence is unchanged since 2016 ([Eliezer Yudkowsky](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/eliezer-yudkowsky-dangers-of-ai-and-the-end-of-human-civilization/), [Sam Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/sam-harris-trump-pandemic-twitter-elon-bret-idw-kanye-ai-ufos/)).

### Empathy, reason and public debate

- Sam Harris argues reason, not emotional empathy, should guide big decisions, and that society needs trustworthy experts. Fridman makes the case for visible empathy and talking to opponents ([Sam Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/sam-harris-trump-pandemic-twitter-elon-bret-idw-kanye-ai-ufos/)).
- Harris says Twitter was making him a worse person and that Fridman should not have aired his Kanye West interview; Fridman defends it ([Sam Harris](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/sam-harris-trump-pandemic-twitter-elon-bret-idw-kanye-ai-ufos/)).
- Chris Voss replies to Harris that people decide on what they care about. His tactical empathy means understanding the other side without agreeing with it ([Chris Voss](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/chris-voss-fbi-hostage-negotiator/)).

### Leading change and negotiating

- Ginni Rometty argues power is needed to do good, and that leaders should be judged on how they get results. She describes IBM's reinvention, including the $34 billion Red Hat purchase ([Ginni Rometty](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/ginni-rometty-ibm-ceo-on-leadership-power-and-adversity/)).
- Rometty says Watson's obstacles were people, trust and processes rather than technology, and favours regulating AI uses rather than the technology ([Ginni Rometty](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/ginni-rometty-ibm-ceo-on-leadership-power-and-adversity/)).
- Voss recommends labelling negative emotions, walking away when the other side is not negotiating, and treating the word fair as a manipulation weapon ([Chris Voss](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/chris-voss-fbi-hostage-negotiator/)).

### Relationships and rivalry

- Shannon Curry argues lasting relationships rest on learnable skills, citing the Gottmans' five-to-one ratio and contempt as the strongest predictor of divorce ([Shannon Curry](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/shannon-curry-johnny-depp-amber-heard-trial-marriage-dating-love/)).
- She explains her court-ordered role as an expert witness in the Depp v. Heard trial and how forensic tests fit into an evaluation ([Shannon Curry](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/shannon-curry-johnny-depp-amber-heard-trial-marriage-dating-love/)).
- The B-Team founders discuss their split from John Danaher's team, the Gordon Ryan rivalry and steroids in untested grappling, and favour teaching beginners through problems before techniques ([B-Team](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/b-team-jiu-jitsu-craig-jones-nicky-rod-and-nicky-ryan/)).
