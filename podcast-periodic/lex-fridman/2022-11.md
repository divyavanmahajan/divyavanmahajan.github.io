## Overview

November leaned heavily on geopolitics and US politics, with conversations on Iran's protests, Putin's war in Ukraine and two guests from opposite sides of American debate. Climate and energy economics came up with Bjørn Lomborg, Andrew Revkin and Chamath Palihapitiya. The month closed on technology craft: cybercrime investigation, Python's evolution and how Bethesda builds open worlds.

## Main themes

### Iran, Russia and the war in Ukraine

- Abbas Amanat reads the protests after Mahsa Amini's death as a leaderless, youth-led break with 43 years of Islamic Republic social engineering, and hopes for gradual change rather than another 1979 ([Amanat](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/abbas-amanat-iran-protests-mahsa-amini-history-cia-nuclear-weapons/)).
- Fiona Hill argues that US domestic dysfunction told the Kremlin Washington did not take Ukraine seriously, and urges strategic empathy for how Putin thinks. Fridman pushes back that the war's course was not obvious in advance ([Hill](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/fiona-hill-vladimir-putin-and-donald-trump/)).
- Guests split on how the war ends. Ben Shapiro wants Biden to push an off-ramp, Destiny praises Biden's limits on US involvement, and Palihapitiya says US interest payments make a negotiated settlement more likely ([Shapiro](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/ben-shapiro-politics-kanye-trump-biden-hitler-extremism-and-war/), [Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/destiny-politics-free-speech-controversy-sex-war-and-relationships/), [Palihapitiya](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/chamath-palihapitiya-money-success-startups-energy-poker-happiness/)).

### Free speech and polarised politics

- Shapiro welcomes Elon Musk's purchase of Twitter and says Trump and Alex Jones should be restored, while Fridman raises worries about viral misinformation ([Shapiro](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/ben-shapiro-politics-kanye-trump-biden-hitler-extremism-and-war/)).
- Destiny, a progressive who is pro-free speech and pro-institutions, says the Trump ban was understandable but permanent bans are wrong. Fridman presses him on his use of slurs ([Destiny](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/destiny-politics-free-speech-controversy-sex-war-and-relationships/)).
- Palihapitiya argues for and against the Trump ban, then says he would not have removed him, and wants networks to attach a social cost through real identity or economics ([Palihapitiya](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/chamath-palihapitiya-money-success-startups-energy-poker-happiness/)).

### Climate, energy and cost-benefit

- Lomborg and Revkin both accept human-caused warming but say alarmism skews policy. Revkin argues cutting vulnerability through zoning, insurance and warning systems works faster than emission cuts ([Climate debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/climate-change-debate-bj-rn-lomborg-and-andrew-revkin/)).
- Lomborg puts the EU's 2030 target at about a trillion dollars a year for roughly 30 cents back per dollar, and says innovation such as cheaper nuclear is the realistic fix ([Climate debate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/climate-change-debate-bj-rn-lomborg-and-andrew-revkin/)).
- Palihapitiya bets the marginal cost of energy and compute is heading to zero, with rooftop solar and batteries competing with utilities ([Palihapitiya](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/chamath-palihapitiya-money-success-startups-energy-poker-happiness/)).

### Building and securing software

- Chris Tarbell says Silk Road's Ross Ulbricht and LulzSec's Sabu were caught through basic mistakes, and that most cyber risk comes from skipped hygiene such as 2FA and patching ([Tarbell](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/chris-tarbell-fbi-agent-who-took-down-silk-road/)).
- Guido van Rossum explains that Python 3.11 got faster through an adaptive specializing interpreter, keeps type hints optional, and is wary of removing the GIL without years of warning ([Van Rossum](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/guido-van-rossum-python-and-the-future-of-programming/)).
- Both van Rossum and Palihapitiya point to AI in daily work: van Rossum uses GitHub Copilot to save typing, and Palihapitiya says Facebook's real opportunity was an AI reasoning layer ([Van Rossum](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/guido-van-rossum-python-and-the-future-of-programming/), [Palihapitiya](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/chamath-palihapitiya-money-success-startups-energy-poker-happiness/)).
- Todd Howard describes Bethesda games as simulations that keep running out of sight, and says The Elder Scrolls VI must be architected to be played for two decades ([Howard](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/11/todd-howard-skyrim-elder-scrolls-6-fallout-and-starfield/)).
