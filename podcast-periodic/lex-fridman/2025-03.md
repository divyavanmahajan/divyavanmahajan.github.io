## Overview

March was a politics month. Donald Trump was central in three of the four episodes, viewed by India's prime minister, by two writers on the American left and by a British commentator on Ukraine and Gaza. The one exception was a long conversation with ThePrimeagen about programming skill, addiction and what AI tools do to developers.

## Main themes

### Trump's second term from three angles

- Narendra Modi calls Trump a friend and links America First to his own India first. He says that this time Trump “seems far more prepared than before”, with a clear roadmap and a strong team ([Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)).
- Ezra Klein and Derek Thompson see Trump's power as built on attention. Klein argues that agenda control means accepting negative attention, and Thompson says Trump excels at performing authenticity ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).
- Douglas Murray watched the Trump-Zelenskyy Oval Office blow-up from a Ukrainian dugout. He calls the meeting premature and expects any ceasefire Trump negotiates to be only a pause ([Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/douglas-murray-putin-zelenskyy-trump-israel-netanyahu-hamas-gaza/)).

### Abundance, DOGE and the state

- Klein and Thompson argue in their book Abundance that America must build and invent more of what it needs. Thompson says liberalism has become “very good at the politics of blocking rather than the politics of building” ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).
- Their examples include Chicago spending $11 billion on 10,000 affordable units, a $1.7 million public toilet in San Francisco, and a 14-stage rural broadband process ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).
- On DOGE, Thompson offers a steelman for a government efficiency department, while Klein says it was about deletion and control, run by only about 50 people ([Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/)).
- Modi links DOGE to his own reforms, claiming 100 million fake or duplicate welfare names removed through direct benefit transfer ([Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)).

### Ukraine, Gaza and antisemitism

- Murray calls Putin a bloody, repressive dictator whose motive is rebuilding the Soviet Union. He expects Ukraine to cede territory in the east and rearm ([Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/douglas-murray-putin-zelenskyy-trump-israel-netanyahu-hamas-gaza/)).
- Modi presents India as a peace-seeker that can tell both Putin and Zelensky the war will not be settled on the battlefield. He also defends his record on the 2002 Gujarat riots, noting the Supreme Court found no involvement ([Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)).
- Murray argues Hamas spent 18 years building tunnels rather than Gaza, and rejects the idea that proportionality means equal casualties. He sees antisemitism as the easiest explanation that blames a small group, while Fridman pushes for empathy and negotiation ([Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/douglas-murray-putin-zelenskyy-trump-israel-netanyahu-hamas-gaza/)).

### Programming skill and AI

- ThePrimeagen dropped GitHub Copilot after noticing he was autopiloting and adding bugs. He finds AI good at predictable work such as TypeScript and weak on less-documented languages like Zig ([ThePrimeagen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/theprimeagen-programming-ai-adhd-productivity-addiction-and-god/)).
- On jobs he notes “there’s been no computer improvement up to this date that did not result in more jobs”, though he concedes programming may one day become unnecessary ([ThePrimeagen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/theprimeagen-programming-ai-adhd-productivity-addiction-and-god/)).
- Fridman finds coding with AI more fun and less lonely, but observes that stronger programmers want to use it less. Klein worries AI will automate the hardest parts of thinking, and Modi argues AI will be incomplete without India's engineers ([ThePrimeagen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/theprimeagen-programming-ai-adhd-productivity-addiction-and-god/), [Klein and Thompson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/ezra-klein-and-derek-thompson-politics-trump-aoc-elon-doge/), [Modi](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/03/narendra-modi-prime-minister-of-india-power-democracy-war-peace/)).
