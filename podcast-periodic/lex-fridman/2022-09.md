## Overview

September 2022 kept returning to what separates minds from machines: whether AI can feel, whether it can make art or comedy, and how emotion can be sensed by software. Ray Kurzweil restated his singularity timeline, while John Vervaeke and Nick Lane approached meaning and consciousness from cognitive science and biology. Chess, poker and a history of World War I rounded out the month.

## Main themes

### Can AI feel or be conscious

- Nick Lane says AI can match human information processing, but “I don’t think that AI necessarily will become conscious because I think it’s the property of life.” ([Nick Lane](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/nick-lane-origin-of-life-evolution-aliens-biology-and-consciousness/)).
- Fridman argues emotional intelligence may emerge as systems scale, and that a deep connection needs an AI that can be unhappy and leave ([Nick Lane](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/nick-lane-origin-of-life-evolution-aliens-biology-and-consciousness/)).
- Kurzweil says he would consider a machine that validly passes a rigorous Turing test conscious, while calling consciousness a philosophical question ([Ray Kurzweil](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/ray-kurzweil-singularity-superintelligence-and-immortality/)).

### Kurzweil's singularity and merging with AI

- Kurzweil holds to a valid Turing test pass in 2029 and a singularity by 2045, driven by exponential growth in computing price-performance ([Ray Kurzweil](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/ray-kurzweil-singularity-superintelligence-and-immortality/)).
- He predicts that “in the 2030s when we can actually connect our neocortex which is where we do our thinking to computers”, and says Neuralink lacks the bandwidth ([Ray Kurzweil](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/ray-kurzweil-singularity-superintelligence-and-immortality/)).
- He frames the future as merging with AI rather than replacement, and points to falling extreme poverty and rising income as signs of progress ([Ray Kurzweil](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/ray-kurzweil-singularity-superintelligence-and-immortality/)).

### Emotion AI, comedy and art

- Rana el Kaliouby says Affectiva quantifies facial expressions in aggregate, favours multimodal signals, and avoided security and surveillance work as a core value ([Rana el Kaliouby](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/rana-el-kaliouby-emotion-ai-social-robots-and-self-driving-cars/)).
- She says Smart Eye's in-cabin sensing has been selected by 14 carmakers, and that semi-autonomous cars need driver monitoring for safe handovers ([Rana el Kaliouby](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/rana-el-kaliouby-emotion-ai-social-robots-and-self-driving-cars/)).
- Will Sasso argues art made without a human is worth less. Fridman says AI can explain jokes but cannot yet generate them, and expects stand-up to be the hardest form ([Will Sasso](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/will-sasso-comedy-madtv-ai-friendship-madness-and-pro-wrestling/)).

### Meaning, wisdom and the origins of life

- Vervaeke describes meaning as felt connectedness to reality and argues the meaning crisis comes from losing practices that cultivated wisdom ([John Vervaeke](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/john-vervaeke-meaning-crisis-atheism-religion-the-search-for-wisdom/)).
- Lane argues simple life is close to inevitable on a wet, rocky planet but complex cells arose only once, so bacteria may be common and intelligence rare ([Nick Lane](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/nick-lane-origin-of-life-evolution-aliens-biology-and-consciousness/)).

### Games, solvers and history

- Daniel Negreanu says AI solvers made poker something that can be studied, but he prefers exploiting real opponents over pure game theory optimal play ([Daniel Negreanu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/daniel-negreanu-poker/)).
- The Botez sisters describe how streaming changed their relationship with chess, and the burnout that came with fame ([Botez Sisters](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/botez-sisters-chess-streaming-and-fame/)).
- Christopher Capozzola argues World War I redefined American citizenship and built the surveillance state ([Christopher Capozzola](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/09/christopher-capozzola-world-war-i-ideology-propaganda-and-politics/)).
