## Overview

In 2023 the Lex Fridman Podcast ran from GPT-4's launch to the e/acc movement, with AI risk and openness the most argued topic of the year. From July the show turned to Israel and Palestine and to great power conflict, talking to leaders and critics on several sides. Free speech, tribal politics and relationships filled much of the rest, alongside physicists, rocket builders and CEOs.

## Main themes

### GPT-4, doom and the pause debate

- Days after GPT-4 shipped, Sam Altman defended iterative public deployment while admitting superhuman alignment is unsolved ([Sam Altman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/sam-altman-openai-ceo-on-gpt-4-chatgpt-and-the-future-of-ai/)).
- Eliezer Yudkowsky argued capabilities are outpacing alignment and a misaligned superintelligence gives no second try ([Eliezer Yudkowsky](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/03/eliezer-yudkowsky-dangers-of-ai-and-the-end-of-human-civilization/)).
- Max Tegmark made the case for a six-month pause on training beyond GPT-4 to end a race to the bottom ([Max Tegmark](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/04/max-tegmark-the-case-for-halting-ai-development/)).
- Yuval Noah Harari warned that AI threatens people by taking over decisions and stories ([Yuval Noah Harari](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/yuval-noah-harari-human-nature-intelligence-power-and-conspiracies/)).

### Open AI against centralised control

- Mark Zuckerberg called open-sourcing today's models net positive and expects many specialised AIs rather than one ([Mark Zuckerberg](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/06/mark-zuckerberg-future-of-ai-at-meta-facebook-instagram-and-whatsapp/)).
- Marc Andreessen warned that fear-driven regulation would entrench incumbents ([Marc Andreessen](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/06/marc-andreessen-future-of-the-internet-technology-and-ai/)); George Hotz sees centralised power as the real risk ([George Hotz](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/06/george-hotz-tiny-corp-twitter-ai-safety-self-driving-gpt-agi-god/)).
- Guillaume Verdon closed the year arguing AI progress should be decentralised and market-driven ([Guillaume Verdon](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/guillaume-verdon-beff-jezos-e-acc-movement-physics-computation-agi/)).
- On the technical side, Stephen Wolfram and Chris Lattner described what language models need underneath ([Stephen Wolfram](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/05/stephen-wolfram-chatgpt-and-the-nature-of-truth-reality-computation/), [Chris Lattner](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/06/chris-lattner-future-of-programming-and-ai/)).

### Israel, Palestine and great powers

- Benjamin Netanyahu defended his judicial reform and a security-first view of Palestinian self-rule ([Netanyahu](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/benjamin-netanyahu-israel-palestine-power-corruption-hate-and-peace/)), while Mohammed el-Kurd framed the conflict as land and dispossession ([Mohammed El-Kurd](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/07/mohammed-el-kurd-palestine/)).
- After October 7, Jared Kushner tied the attack to easing pressure on Iran ([Jared Kushner](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/10/jared-kushner-israel-palestine-hamas-gaza-iran-and-the-middle-east/)).
- John Mearsheimer applied offensive realism to Ukraine, Gaza and China ([John Mearsheimer](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/11/john-mearsheimer-israel-palestine-russia-ukraine-china-nato-and-ww3/)).

### Free speech and tribal politics

- Tim Urban argued that how people think matters more than left or right ([Tim Urban](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/tim-urban-tribalism-marxism-liberalism-social-justice-and-politics/)).
- Harvey Silverglate and Greg Lukianoff attacked campus censorship and cancel culture ([Harvey Silverglate](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/05/harvey-silverglate-freedom-of-speech/), [Greg Lukianoff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/09/greg-lukianoff-cancel-culture-deplatforming-censorship-free-speech/)).

### Builders, physics and space

- Dennis Whyte sees fusion reaching the grid in the 2030s ([Dennis Whyte](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/01/dennis-whyte-nuclear-fusion-and-the-future-of-energy/)).
- Robert Playter described legged robots becoming industrial products ([Robert Playter](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/04/robert-playter-boston-dynamics-ceo-on-humanoid-and-legged-robotics/)); Jeff Bezos tied cheap space access to rate manufacturing ([Jeff Bezos](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/jeff-bezos-amazon-and-blue-origin/)).
- Andrew Strominger and Lisa Randall worked at the edges of physics, from quantum gravity to dark matter ([Andrew Strominger](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/02/andrew-strominger-black-holes-quantum-gravity-and-theoretical-physics/), [Lisa Randall](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2023/12/lisa-randall-dark-matter-theoretical-physics-and-extinction-events/)).
