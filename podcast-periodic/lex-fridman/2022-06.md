## Overview

June 2022 paired opposing views on Karl Marx in back-to-back episodes with Richard Wolff and Douglas Murray. Robin Hanson and Donald Hoffman took on aliens, the simulation hypothesis and the nature of reality. The rest covered social media's harm to teenagers, how Tony Fadell builds products, and medicine's limits.

## Main themes

### Marx, argued from both sides

- Richard Wolff says Marx was mainly a critic of capitalism and never wrote a book about communism. He sums up the tradition as “Human Society can do better than capitalism and it ought to try” ([Wolff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/richard-wolff-marxism-and-communism/)).
- Wolff argues Soviet-style regimes missed the point: Stalin declared that holding the state was itself socialism, so exploitation continued. Fridman, whose family history runs through the Soviet Union, pushes back on human nature, risk and competition ([Wolff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/richard-wolff-marxism-and-communism/)).
- Wolff cites a federal minimum wage unchanged at $7.25 since 2009 and CEO pay about 300 times the average worker's ([Wolff](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/richard-wolff-marxism-and-communism/)).
- Douglas Murray says Marxism has failed wherever tried and calls critical race theory an activist discipline. He accepts that the squeezed middle is a real problem but wants to repair markets ([Murray](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/douglas-murray-racism-marxism-and-the-war-on-the-west/)).

### Aliens, competition and the nature of reality

- Robin Hanson argues that humanity's early arrival implies fast-expanding grabby aliens, and that expansion means accepting competition, which “just happens if you can't coordinate to stop it” ([Hanson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/robin-hanson-alien-civilizations-ufos-and-the-future-of-humanity/)).
- Hanson is a cryonics customer betting on brain emulation, and expects human-level AI at least a decade away. Fridman says concentrated power makes him more nervous than war ([Hanson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/robin-hanson-alien-civilizations-ufos-and-the-future-of-humanity/)).
- Donald Hoffman argues evolution tunes perception for fitness, not truth, and that consciousness is fundamental: “Brains are something that consciousness makes up.” ([Hoffman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/donald-hoffman-reality-is-an-illusion-how-evolution-hid-the-truth/)).
- Nick Bostrom and the simulation hypothesis came up in both episodes ([Hanson](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/robin-hanson-alien-civilizations-ufos-and-the-future-of-humanity/), [Hoffman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/donald-hoffman-reality-is-an-illusion-how-evolution-hid-the-truth/)).

### The case against social media

- Jonathan Haidt blames the ad-driven, viral business model, not smartphones as such, for the teen mental health crisis, with pre-teen girls hit hardest ([Haidt](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/jonathan-haidt-the-case-against-social-media/)).
- He proposes keeping children off these platforms until 16, user verification for posting and regulation. Fridman argues platforms could be built for long-term well-being ([Haidt](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/jonathan-haidt-the-case-against-social-media/)).

### Building products

- Tony Fadell says products succeed when they kill a real pain, and that teams should write the press release first so the why is clear ([Fadell](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/tony-fadell-iphone-ipod-and-nest/)).
- He argues version-one decisions rest on owned opinions, not data, and that companies stall when they turn those into data-driven decisions to protect jobs ([Fadell](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/tony-fadell-iphone-ipod-and-nest/)).
- Fadell recalls Apple in 2001 as cautious and barely break-even, and says the hard part of Nest was go-to-market rather than the device ([Fadell](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/tony-fadell-iphone-ipod-and-nest/)).

### Medicine knows less than it thinks

- Emergency physician Jonathan Reisman and Fridman agree that medicine is data-poor and that plausible treatments often fail controlled trials. Fridman wants AI to flag issues for doctors ([Reisman](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2022/06/jonathan-reisman-the-human-body-from-sex-sperm-to-hands-heart/)).
