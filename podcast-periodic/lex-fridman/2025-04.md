## Overview

April moved away from Washington. Two episodes stayed with geopolitics (Dave Smith's anti-war case on Israel and Ukraine, and historian Jeffrey Wasserstrom on Xi Jinping's China), while the other two were about making things: Robert Rodriguez on low-budget filmmaking and Tim Sweeney on Epic, Unreal Engine and the app stores. Trump was the only thread across episodes.

## Main themes

### War, blowback and Israel

- Dave Smith argues that US-backed wars create the hatred they claim to fight. He cites General McChrystal's insurgent math, that “10 minus two equals 20”, and the Yemen drone campaign ([Dave Smith](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/dave-smith-israel-ukraine-epstein-mossad-conspiracies-antisemitism/)).
- Smith calls October 7th indefensible terrorism, but says Netanyahu propped up Hamas for years. Fridman argues that a stated aim to spare civilians is an ethical difference ([Dave Smith](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/dave-smith-israel-ukraine-epstein-mossad-conspiracies-antisemitism/)).
- Smith, who is Jewish, says he sees more Jew hatred on X than ever. He argues that calling legitimate questions antisemitic makes the word meaningless ([Dave Smith](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/dave-smith-israel-ukraine-epstein-mossad-conspiracies-antisemitism/)).

### Trump, Ukraine and the trade war

- Smith says NATO expansion provoked the invasion of Ukraine while agreeing Putin bears responsibility for launching it. He backs Trump's push for a deal and says Trump is highly incentivised to get it done quickly ([Dave Smith](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/dave-smith-israel-ukraine-epstein-mossad-conspiracies-antisemitism/)).
- Fridman sets out the tariffs between the US and China (145% and 125%). Wasserstrom argues the relationship hinges on third parties, and thinks a move on Taiwan is more likely if Xi feels desperate than confident ([Wasserstrom](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/jeffrey-wasserstrom-china-xi-jinping-trade-war-taiwan-hong-kong-mao/)).

### How Xi's China controls its story

- Wasserstrom says Xi shares Mao's personality cult but not his love of chaos, and plays to the century of humiliation narrative that both Nationalists and Communists used for legitimacy ([Wasserstrom](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/jeffrey-wasserstrom-china-xi-jinping-trade-war-taiwan-hong-kong-mao/)).
- He explains censorship as fear, friction and flooding. VPNs work, he says, but they are effort, and “censorship is most restrictive when it’s things that are actually about China” ([Wasserstrom](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/jeffrey-wasserstrom-china-xi-jinping-trade-war-taiwan-hong-kong-mao/)).
- He describes a post-1989 bargain of consumer choice without political choice, and Fridman frames China as turning two knobs, Brave New World and 1984 ([Wasserstrom](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/jeffrey-wasserstrom-china-xi-jinping-trade-war-taiwan-hong-kong-mao/)).

### Platforms, fees and the economics of making things

- Tim Sweeney calls Apple's 30% a junk fee on commerce done on a phone the user already owns. He says Epic's data puts Google Play's running cost at about 6% of revenue, a mark-up he argues only a monopoly could charge ([Sweeney](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/tim-sweeney-fortnite-unreal-engine-and-the-future-of-gaming/)).
- Sweeney sees big social games like Fortnite as the real metaverse. He says Verse and Unreal Engine 6 aim to scale game logic beyond the current cap of about 100 players per match ([Sweeney](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/tim-sweeney-fortnite-unreal-engine-and-the-future-of-gaming/)).
- Robert Rodriguez argues that tight limits and doing every job yourself beat big crews. His new Brass Knuckle Films will make action films for $10 to 30 million with audience members as real investors, not crowdfunders ([Rodriguez](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/robert-rodriguez-sin-city-desperado-el-mariachi-alita-and-filmmaking/)).
- Rodriguez says Alita was a training ground for Weta that refined the water in the second Avatar film, and that a sequel is in the works ([Rodriguez](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2025/04/robert-rodriguez-sin-city-desperado-el-mariachi-alita-and-filmmaking/)).
