## Overview

July was a quiet two-episode month with no AI guests. Ivanka Trump talked through her career in building, fashion and policy, and Alone Season 6 winner Jordan Jonas explained how he survived 77 days in the wild. Both conversations touched on how divided the United States really is.

## Main themes

### Building, brands and policy

- Ivanka Trump calls architecture her first passion and says “I really believe that the form has to follow the function” ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/)).
- She describes redeveloping Washington's Old Post Office, an 1890s building losing $6 million a year, with almost 300 rooms in almost 300 layouts. She credits her father's habit of questioning workers on site for an overhaul of its heating and cooling design ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/)).
- Her fashion brand began with fine jewellery for women buying for themselves and grew to hundreds of millions of dollars in sales ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/)).
- In the White House she credits the doubled child tax credit with giving 40 million families an average of $2,200 a year, and explains why she is staying out of the 2024 campaign ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/)).

### Surviving Alone

- Jonas explains the format: ten contestants, ten items each, and no end date. He says not knowing when it would end was the hardest part, and that Season 7's 100-day cap changed the psychology ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).
- After missing his first moose he built a fence to funnel game, then killed a moose with a bow on day 20. He says “I think bow hunting is probably the most pure form of it” ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).
- His approach combined fishing, trapping and hunting, a simple A-frame shelter and a focus on calories. Rabbits alone were not enough, because they carry almost no fat ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).

### Siberia, faith and hardship

- Jonas went to Russia to help build an orphanage, then trapped furs and joined Evenki reindeer herders building 30-kilometre log fences by axe ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).
- He borrowed Evenki techniques on Alone, including an elevated food cache to protect his moose meat ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).
- He describes his faith as a mooring and an outside check on himself, and credits The Gulag Archipelago with teaching him to pursue duty rather than happiness ([Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).

### How divided is America

- Ivanka Trump says campaigning showed her that “we are a whole lot less divided than the media and others would have us believe”, and that a loud minority does most of the shouting ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/)).
- Fridman argues surface bickering is a feature of democracy. After the Jonas interview he responds to the attempted assassination of Donald Trump, naming extreme division as the real enemy and asking for transparency on the Secret Service failure ([Ivanka Trump](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/ivanka-trump-politics-family-real-estate-fashion-music-and-life/), [Jordan Jonas](/podcasts/lex-fridman/2024/07/jordan-jonas-survival-hunting-siberia-god-and-winning-alone-season-6/)).
