## Overview

April on The Pragmatic Engineer was about how engineering work is organised: code review and tooling, software design, company culture and the relationship with product. Uber came up in four of the five episodes, often through Gergely Orosz's own experience there. AI coding tools appeared as a reason to care more about review and design, not less.

## Main themes

### AI coding tools raise the stakes for review

- Tomas Reimers says companies adopting AI coding tools see more pull requests, bigger ones and more bugs or CI failures. He calls AI “a lasting change on the software development industry” ([Stacked diffs](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/stacked-diffs-and-tooling-at-meta-with-tomas-reimers/)).
- Reimers cites research that reviewers stop reading long pull requests and just approve them. He argues that if the author of vibe-coded work did not read the code, someone else should ([Stacked diffs](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/stacked-diffs-and-tooling-at-meta-with-tomas-reimers/)).
- He describes Meta's tightly integrated tooling and stacked diffs, which keep pull requests small, and says most Graphite customers are in or moving to a monorepo ([Stacked diffs](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/stacked-diffs-and-tooling-at-meta-with-tomas-reimers/)).

### Software design becomes the main job

- John Ousterhout expects AI tools to churn out ever better low-level code, but sees nothing yet suggesting they will take over higher-level design. He says “software design is going to become more and more important” ([Software design](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/the-philosophy-of-software-design-with-john-ousterhout/)).
- He defines a deep module as one that gives the most functionality behind the simplest interface, and says combining closely related units often makes things deeper ([Software design](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/the-philosophy-of-software-design-with-john-ousterhout/)).
- His design-it-twice exercise costs 1% or 2% of project time, and he says “The second idea is always better.” Orosz compares it to alternatives-considered sections in Uber design docs ([Software design](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/the-philosophy-of-software-design-with-john-ousterhout/)).
- Ousterhout disagrees with Clean Code on very short methods and TDD, and Orosz likens AI coding tools to prolific, sloppy tactical tornadoes ([Software design](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/the-philosophy-of-software-design-with-john-ousterhout/)).

### Engineering culture at Amazon and Uber

- Dave Anderson says Amazon is rigid at corporate level but gives dev teams near-total control over language, process and tooling, along with ownership of oncall ([Amazon](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/working-at-amazon-as-a-software-engineer-with-dave-anderson/)).
- Promotions need a multi-page written narrative and get roughly exponentially harder at each level. Every hire needs a yes from both the hiring manager and the bar raiser ([Amazon](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/working-at-amazon-as-a-software-engineer-with-dave-anderson/)).
- Uber recurs as a reference point: Reimers' favourite metric comes from Uber, and Orosz recalls an ex-Amazon director joining a routine Uber outage call ([Stacked diffs](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/stacked-diffs-and-tooling-at-meta-with-tomas-reimers/), [Amazon](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/working-at-amazon-as-a-software-engineer-with-dave-anderson/)).

### Rebuilding Reddit's mobile apps

- Lauren Darcey says mobile headcount grew from 40-50 to around 200 in two years, and leadership asked for a prescription that became Core Stack: a monorepo per platform, GraphQL, MVVM and a new design system ([Reddit](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/building-reddits-ios-and-android-app/)).
- Reddit moved from near-zero unit test coverage and an overseas manual QA vendor to coverage ratchets. Eric Kuck says the push produced flaky tests but still worked ([Reddit](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/building-reddits-ios-and-android-app/)).
- Kuck says server-driven UI for the feed caused double fetching. Darcey reports higher crash-free rates, and P90 Android startup fell from 13 seconds to under three to four ([Reddit](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/building-reddits-ios-and-android-app/)).

### Engineers working with product

- Ebi Atawodi and Orosz describe turning a rocky PM-EM relationship at Uber into a productive one. Orosz notes that at Uber product gave engineering its headcount ([Product](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/how-to-work-better-with-product-as-an-engineer-with-ebi-atawodi/)).
- Atawodi shared a scorecard of P0 business metrics with engineers. Two of them then built web payments without approved headcount and came back with results ([Product](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/04/how-to-work-better-with-product-as-an-engineer-with-ebi-atawodi/)).
