## Overview

Gergely Orosz launched The Pragmatic Engineer podcast in September 2024 as a companion to his newsletter, promising deep dives on how software is built at Big Tech and startups ([Trailer](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/09/the-pragmatic-engineer-podcast-trailer/)). The first season mixed AI tools with company case studies: Sourcegraph, Bending Spoons, Linear and Notion. Engineering culture was the steadiest thread, from promotions at Google to remote teams and how projects really ship. Grady Booch closed the year with a long view of software architecture.

## Main themes

### AI tools without the hype

- Simon Willison argues LLMs are hard-to-learn power-user tools that reward experienced engineers, and that he can still evaluate code in languages he does not know well ([Simon Willison](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/09/ai-tools-for-software-engineers-but-without-the-hype-with-simon-willis/)).
- Willison calls fine-tuning mostly a waste of time and says Claude 3.5 Sonnet is “the first time somebody who is not Open AI has had the clearly best model” ([Simon Willison](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/09/ai-tools-for-software-engineers-but-without-the-hype-with-simon-willis/)).
- Quinn Slack argues for incremental automation, likening fully new agent UIs to concept cars with no steering wheel, and describes the shift in priorities needed to build Cody ([Sourcegraph](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/10/efficient-scaleups-in-2024-vs-2021-sourcegraph-with-ceo-co-founder-qui/)).
- Grady Booch argues that LLMs alone are the wrong architecture for AGI ([Grady Booch](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/12/software-architecture-with-grady-booch/)).

### Efficient companies and unusual operating models

- Slack says many 2021 hires joined Sourcegraph and other companies as rocket ships, and managers ran growth with a peacetime mentality he now sees as wrong ([Sourcegraph](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/10/efficient-scaleups-in-2024-vs-2021-sourcegraph-with-ceo-co-founder-qui/)).
- Luca Ferrari says Bending Spoons buys companies with its own money to run them for good; the team describes rebuilding Evernote, which ran on 750 manually provisioned virtual machines ([Bending Spoons](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/10/twisting-the-rules-of-building-software-bending-spoons-the-team-behind/)).
- Bending Spoons runs on radical simplicity, with no on-call, bonuses or titles ([Bending Spoons](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/10/twisting-the-rules-of-building-software-bending-spoons-the-team-behind/)).

### Promotions and engineering culture

- Irina Stanescu contrasts Google's design-doc and production-readiness culture with early Uber, where ex-Googlers wrote a document translating internal tools ([Irina Stanescu](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/promotions-and-tooling-at-google-with-irina-stanescu-ex-google/)).
- She explains Google's promo committees and says a promotion meant learning “to show you're operating at the next level” ([Irina Stanescu](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/promotions-and-tooling-at-google-with-irina-stanescu-ex-google/)).
- Sabin Roman describes Linear preferring principles over process, a zero-bug policy, a coding exercise cut from real Linear code and a one-week work trial ([Linear](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/linear-move-fast-with-little-process-with-first-engineering-manager-sa/)).

### Remote teams and shipping at Big Tech

- Roman says remote work at Linear brings focus and autonomy, while belonging takes weekly demos and offsites ([Linear](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/11/linear-move-fast-with-little-process-with-first-engineering-manager-sa/)).
- Sean Goedecke argues projects ship because one engineer holds the whole technical context, and credits GitHub's async-first culture for working across time zones ([Sean Goedecke](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/12/shipping-projects-at-big-tech-with-sean-goedecke/)).

### Architecture and native mobile

- Booch traces architecture from defence systems to cloud building blocks and says his method looked at systems through objects and classes, though it overemphasised inheritance ([Grady Booch](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/12/software-architecture-with-grady-booch/)).
- Notion moved its apps from a Cordova webview to native code one tab at a time, driven by launch times of 1.5 to 2 seconds and tracked by an initial home render metric ([Notion](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2024/12/notion-going-native-on-ios-and-android/)).
