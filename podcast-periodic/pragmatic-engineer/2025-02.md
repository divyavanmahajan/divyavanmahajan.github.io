## Overview

February brought AI into The Pragmatic Engineer's conversations through two guests: Chip Huyen on building products on foundation models, and Nicole Forsgren on how AI tools change developer work and its measurement. Engineering culture, and Google's in particular, linked Forsgren's episode with Manu Cornet's comics. Ashutosh Agrawal added a detailed case study of streaming at record scale.

## Main themes

### AI engineering as product work

- Chip Huyen says AI engineering shifts the focus from machine learning to engineering and product. Teams start with a demo over an API and need neither their own data nor an AI degree ([AI Engineering](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/ai-engineering-with-chip-huyen/)).
- She recommends a progression: define good and bad responses, then prompting, examples and RAG, with keyword retrieval before vector databases. Fine-tuning is a last resort ([AI Engineering](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/ai-engineering-with-chip-huyen/)).
- Huyen says evaluation gets harder as models get smarter, and wrote two chapters on it. She also suggests daily human review of about 50 real interactions ([AI Engineering](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/ai-engineering-with-chip-huyen/)).
- On jobs, she argues coding is not the whole of software engineering: “AI can have you automate coding, but I don't think it's going to fully automate problem solving” ([AI Engineering](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/ai-engineering-with-chip-huyen/)).

### Developer productivity and AI tools

- Nicole Forsgren says PR counts are useful for spotting friction but harmful as individual metrics. She argues for a constellation of measures plus qualitative feedback ([Developer productivity](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/developer-productivity-with-dr-nicole-forsgren-creator-of-dora-co-crea/)).
- She defines developer experience as the developer's lived experience and jokes that “It's real hard to build the AI things when your pipeline's trash” ([Developer productivity](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/developer-productivity-with-dr-nicole-forsgren-creator-of-dora-co-crea/)).
- Forsgren says coding with AI is becoming more of a review exercise. She advises developers to try tools such as Copilot or Cursor and keep what fits, and sees release engineering as an LLM opportunity ([Developer productivity](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/developer-productivity-with-dr-nicole-forsgren-creator-of-dora-co-crea/)).

### Google's culture and its side effects

- Manu Cornet calls Google's naming confusion the bad consequence of a good, bottom-up culture, and says Google treated employees very well but offered customers little service ([Big Tech comics](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/the-man-behind-the-big-tech-comics-with-manu-cornet/)).
- Cornet says Gmail and probably Google News began as 20% projects, which became less encouraged over time. Orosz wonders whether this links to Google retiring so many products ([Big Tech comics](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/the-man-behind-the-big-tech-comics-with-manu-cornet/)).
- Cornet sees Google becoming more siloed, like Apple. Forsgren, by contrast, calls Google's developer experience the best she has seen, and argues tech culture cannot change without the wider company culture ([Big Tech comics](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/the-man-behind-the-big-tech-comics-with-manu-cornet/), [Developer productivity](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/developer-productivity-with-dr-nicole-forsgren-creator-of-dora-co-crea/)).

### Live streaming at record scale

- Ashutosh Agrawal explains how JioCinema served 32 million concurrent streams at the 2023 IPL final, using four-to-six-second HLS segments and CDN TTLs tuned between cache misses and stale data ([Live streaming](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/live-streaming-at-world-record-scale-with-ashutosh-agrawal/)).
- Capacity planning starts at the end of the previous year with pessimistic forecasts, and vCPUs are usually the limit ([Live streaming](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/live-streaming-at-world-record-scale-with-ashutosh-agrawal/)).
- Agrawal says “the auto-scaling has never been able to respond to that in a very speedy manner”, so every service scaled against a single concurrency metric ([Live streaming](/podcasts/pragmatic-engineer/2025/02/live-streaming-at-world-record-scale-with-ashutosh-agrawal/)).
