## Overview

May on The Moonshot Podcast covered three episodes and five X projects. Two succeeded by making large volumes of hidden data visible, two were killed when the evidence or the business case did not hold, and one spin-out is scaling geothermal heating by rethinking installation.

## Main themes

### Visibility as the product

- Mike Wiacek traces Chronicle, code-named Lantern, to a 2015 FBI briefing where six-month-old bad domains met Google systems that already showed what attackers were using that morning ([The Digital Trail](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/the-digital-trail/)).
- The team argues defenders lose because they delete logs after 90 days or cannot search them quickly. One of them says “The hackers aren't hacking in. They're logging in because they have exploited credentials of your employees” ([The Digital Trail](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/the-digital-trail/)).
- Google Cloud acquired Chronicle, and Shapor Naghibzadeh says it is now the core of Google's security operations platform alongside Mandiant and Wiz ([The Digital Trail](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/the-digital-trail/)).
- Chorus applies the same idea to physical goods with printable Bluetooth labels read by customers' existing Wi-Fi access points and scanners. One customer's 28-day transit fell below seven days after an idle trailer was found ([The Digital Trail](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/the-digital-trail/)).

### Brains, computing and the honest kill

- Sarah Laszlo pitched Valkyrie as brain-inspired computing, noting that “The fruit fly brain is the size of a sesame seed, and it draws less power than a AA battery” ([Neurons and Networks](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/neurons-and-networks/)).
- Using reservoir computing, the team matched Wing's drone image segmentation accuracy and trained a classifier for the agriculture moonshot in six steps ([Neurons and Networks](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/neurons-and-networks/)).
- Interns then found random networks did just as well given the right sparsity, so Laszlo asked to shut the project down. She still believes AI should become more neuromorphic ([Neurons and Networks](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/neurons-and-networks/)).
- Obi Felten's Amber built a low-cost EEG cap for measuring depression and anxiety. Of its three kill criteria, the business one failed: Felten says “The biggest problem in mental health is that we don't have enough providers” and spending follows care ([Neurons and Networks](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/neurons-and-networks/)).

### Geothermal and the cost of installation

- Kathy Hannun says buildings account for 40% of US energy use, and geothermal cuts demand most on the hottest days that size the grid ([Underground Potential](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/underground-potential/)).
- Ground-source heat pumps are the cheapest to run but historically the most expensive to install. X's percussive mole and miniaturised drill both failed ([Underground Potential](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/underground-potential/)).
- Hannun's lesson is that less drilling and ease of hiring, training and repair matter more than drilling speed. Dandelion now sells to homeowners in the Northeast and to top-10 US homebuilders ([Underground Potential](/podcasts/moonshot/2026/05/underground-potential/)).
