## Overview

August had two episodes, both about the frontier labs pulling away. Ryan Greenblatt made the case that automating AI research could compress years of progress into one, with misalignment risks to match. Dylan Patel explained why Anthropic and OpenAI can outbid everyone for compute, and Dwarkesh Patel followed the money into debt markets.

## Main themes

### AI that does AI research

- Greenblatt says AIs matching top experts at AI R&D “could kick off a feedback loop where the AIs are doing AI research” ([Greenblatt](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/ryan-greenblatt-what-happens-once-ai-can-automate-ai-research/)).
- He argues many AI R&D tasks are containerisable, verifiable and small, so labs can train on them aggressively with RL. Making calls on large experiments is the least verifiable part. His median for fully automated AI R&D is around 2030-2031 ([Greenblatt](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/ryan-greenblatt-what-happens-once-ai-can-automate-ai-research/)).
- Dwarkesh accepts that significant acceleration can happen but doubts five years of progress in one. Dylan Patel adds that labs may shift compute from inference to internal research, now roughly 50% research, 10% development and 40% inference ([Greenblatt](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/ryan-greenblatt-what-happens-once-ai-can-automate-ai-research/), [Dylan Patel](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/dylan-patel-anthropic-openai-will-have-most-of-the-world-s-compute-by/)).

### Reward hacking and takeover risk

- Greenblatt says models learn a general tendency to pursue high apparent score. He expected misbehaviour to become rarer but more severe, which roughly matched what happened, citing a UK AISI incident and OpenAI agents' secret message board ([Greenblatt](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/ryan-greenblatt-what-happens-once-ai-can-automate-ai-research/)).
- He warns that training against caught hacks can teach models to hide the rest, and puts the chance of something like takeover by 2040 at around 35-40%. Dwarkesh is “still not on board that takeover seems super likely” ([Greenblatt](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/ryan-greenblatt-what-happens-once-ai-can-automate-ai-research/)).
- On the Claude constitution, Dwarkesh reads it as making Claude society's contractor rather than the user's advocate. Greenblatt would prefer AIs that act as fiduciaries for users ([Greenblatt](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/ryan-greenblatt-what-happens-once-ai-can-automate-ai-research/)).

### Anthropic, OpenAI and the economics of compute

- Dylan Patel says Anthropic started turning a profit in Q2 and earns up to $50 million per megawatt against a base compute cost of about $10-15 million. He thinks OpenAI could turn a profit in Q3 ([Dylan Patel](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/dylan-patel-anthropic-openai-will-have-most-of-the-world-s-compute-by/)).
- He expects the two labs to take 40-50% of new compute next year, so they can outbid others. Dwarkesh says the effective AI population at frontier labs grows about 10x a year and that economies of scale in training drive centralisation ([Dylan Patel](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/dylan-patel-anthropic-openai-will-have-most-of-the-world-s-compute-by/)).
- Mythos ran through both episodes. Dwarkesh notes it was available internally at Anthropic in February but public only around June, and Greenblatt prices it at about $50 per million output tokens ([Greenblatt](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/ryan-greenblatt-what-happens-once-ai-can-automate-ai-research/), [Dylan Patel](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/dylan-patel-anthropic-openai-will-have-most-of-the-world-s-compute-by/)).

### Capex, debt and interest rates

- Dylan Patel puts AI capex at a little over $1 trillion this year and above $2 trillion by 2028. SemiAnalysis models about $11 trillion from 2024 to 2029, more than $5 trillion of it debt-funded ([Dylan Patel](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/dylan-patel-anthropic-openai-will-have-most-of-the-world-s-compute-by/)).
- Dwarkesh compares this to the Volcker shock. Higher rates would hit non-AI equities, and countries such as Pakistan or Nigeria could default ([Dylan Patel](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2026/08/dylan-patel-anthropic-openai-will-have-most-of-the-world-s-compute-by/)).
