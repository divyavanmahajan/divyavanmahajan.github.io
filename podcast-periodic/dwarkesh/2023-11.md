## Overview

November was a history and governance month with no AI lab guests. Dominic Cummings described how the British state works from inside Number 10, Andrew Roberts explained why leadership and ideology decide wars, and Jung Chang recounted Mao's rule from her own family's experience. AI came up only at the edges, through Cummings' reading of Bismarck and Roberts' chapter on future war.

## Main themes

### Why Western government cannot act

- Cummings argues nobody in the British state holds both responsibility and authority. Only the Prime Minister can force decisions, as with sending grounded airliners to China for PPE in March 2020 ([Cummings](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/dominic-cummings-covid-brexit-fixing-western-governance/)).
- He says Number 10 has been a press entertainment service since Thatcher, and that Cabinet meetings are scripted, with talking points supplied by officials ([Cummings](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/dominic-cummings-covid-brexit-fixing-western-governance/)).
- Cummings describes process overwhelming outcomes: a data project with eight to twelve weeks of work and two and a half years of emails about legal permissions ([Cummings](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/dominic-cummings-covid-brexit-fixing-western-governance/)).

### Rebuilding the state

- Cummings says: “The single biggest mistake Thatcher made was that she never actually got to grips with the permanent state” ([Cummings](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/dominic-cummings-covid-brexit-fixing-western-governance/)).
- After COVID, about half a dozen permanent secretaries were removed in summer 2020, but Cummings says Boris Johnson dismantled the 2020 task forces in 2021 and the vaccine task force and rapid testing were wound down ([Cummings](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/dominic-cummings-covid-brexit-fixing-western-governance/)).
- His remedy is to build parallel systems and close the old ones, as he did when cutting the Department for Education communications team from over 250 to under 50. He also reads Bismarck's career as a live AI alignment problem ([Cummings](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/dominic-cummings-covid-brexit-fixing-western-governance/)).

### Leadership and ideology in war

- Roberts, co-author with David Petraeus of a book on wars since 1945, says the winner is often not the side with the cities, the most men or the best weapons, but the one whose leaders get the big idea right ([Roberts](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/andrew-roberts-why-hitler-lost-wwii-churchill-as-applied-historian-nap/)).
- He argues: “Again and again, Hitler put his Nazi ideology before the strategic best interests of the German Reich”, including declaring war on the US ([Roberts](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/andrew-roberts-why-hitler-lost-wwii-churchill-as-applied-historian-nap/)).
- Roberts credits the bomb for keeping about 140 post-1945 wars limited and holds that deterrence still works on Xi Jinping over Taiwan. Cummings, by contrast, calls a war over Taiwan an insane idea ([Roberts](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/andrew-roberts-why-hitler-lost-wwii-churchill-as-applied-historian-nap/), [Cummings](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/dominic-cummings-covid-brexit-fixing-western-governance/)).
- Roberts accepts Dwarkesh Patel's comparison of Napoleon to a startup founder and notes that 13 of his 26 marshals came from the working classes or below ([Roberts](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/andrew-roberts-why-hitler-lost-wwii-churchill-as-applied-historian-nap/)).

### Mao's China from the inside

- Jung Chang argues the Great Leap famine, with nearly 40 million deaths, came from Mao seizing grain to export for Soviet and Eastern European weapons and industry ([Jung Chang](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/jung-chang-wild-swans-author-living-through-history-s-largest-man-made/)).
- She says Mao launched the Cultural Revolution in 1966 mainly as revenge on Liu Shaoqi, who had halted the food-export policy. Her father was purged and her mother went through over a hundred denunciation meetings ([Jung Chang](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/jung-chang-wild-swans-author-living-through-history-s-largest-man-made/)).
- Chang describes forced self-criticism as “a psychological warfare against his own population, which is quite effective” and says controls on banned books have tightened under Xi ([Jung Chang](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2023/11/jung-chang-wild-swans-author-living-through-history-s-largest-man-made/)).
