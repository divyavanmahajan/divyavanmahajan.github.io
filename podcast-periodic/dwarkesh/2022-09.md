## Overview

September paired an engineer, a historian and an economist. Austin Vernon made the case for energy superabundance and simpler designs, Charles C. Mann revisited the meeting of the Americas and Eurasia, and Tyler Cowen talked about his book Talent. Nuclear power and civilisational collapse were the two items that came up in more than one episode.

## Main themes

### Energy superabundance and nuclear economics

- Vernon says products such as ethylene or methanol from CO2 electrolysis become competitive at roughly $10-20 per megawatt hour, against about $25 for solar today. Global electrolyser output is still measured in kilograms per day ([Vernon](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/austin-vernon-energy-superabundance-starship-missiles-finding-alpha/)).
- In the paper he wrote with Eli Dourado, rich countries might double their energy use while Africa's could rise about 10x. He argues the ultimate limit is human capital, which cheap energy frees up ([Vernon](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/austin-vernon-energy-superabundance-starship-missiles-finding-alpha/)).
- On nuclear, Vernon says US plants stalled “because the market conditions changed” rather than mainly through regulation, and that reactor capacity factors rose from about 60% to over 90%. He prefers micro-reactors to large grid plants ([Vernon](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/austin-vernon-energy-superabundance-starship-missiles-finding-alpha/)).
- Mann expects geoengineering trials within 10 years and wants research effort moved towards crops such as cassava and tree crops ([Mann](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/charles-c-mann-americas-before-columbus-scientific-wizardry/)).

### Complexity, Starship and military cost curves

- Vernon argues software has not produced runaway productivity growth because digitising the world means capturing all of its complexity and corner cases ([Vernon](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/austin-vernon-energy-superabundance-starship-missiles-finding-alpha/)).
- He says Starship makes spaceflight about 100 times cheaper for cargo and 1000 times for people, cheap enough to overwhelm ship-based interceptors with kinetic projectiles. A tungsten rod, he says, is “a big bomb, but it's not a super weapon” ([Vernon](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/austin-vernon-energy-superabundance-starship-missiles-finding-alpha/)).

### Collapse and contingency

- Mann argues contact and epidemics were close to inevitable but how they played out depended on chance and agency. Cortés and Pizarro exploited internal divisions, since empires have weak points in leadership conflict ([Mann](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/charles-c-mann-americas-before-columbus-scientific-wizardry/)).
- Mann rejects collapse narratives such as Jared Diamond's, noting that 30 million Maya still live in the region ([Mann](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/charles-c-mann-americas-before-columbus-scientific-wizardry/)).
- Cowen is gloomier about the long run: nuclear war probably would not kill everyone, but the risk adds up and could set humanity back permanently. He jokes that sex, which exists to escape parasites, is the real pessimist ([Cowen](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/tyler-cowen-talent-collapse-pessimism-of-sex/)).
- He says existential risk matters more than most people think, but that effective altruists overvalue specific hypotheses and that the useful work looks like growth and science ([Cowen](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/tyler-cowen-talent-collapse-pessimism-of-sex/)).

### Finding talent

- Cowen argues talent spotting will always be an art, pointing to venture capitalists' modest hit rates, and says he is suspicious of very humble candidates ([Cowen](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/tyler-cowen-talent-collapse-pessimism-of-sex/)).
- He agrees with Bryan Caplan that students forget classroom facts, but says higher education gives them context, which is what is scarce ([Cowen](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/tyler-cowen-talent-collapse-pessimism-of-sex/)).
- On Emergent Ventures he says “a lot of foundations give out too many large grants and not enough small grants” ([Cowen](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2022/09/tyler-cowen-talent-collapse-pessimism-of-sex/)).
