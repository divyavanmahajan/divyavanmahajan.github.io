## Overview

June was mostly an economics month. Ken Rogoff and Arthur Kroeber gave two different readings of China's economy, and Rogoff added a warning about US debt and the dollar. George Church closed the month with a broad case that biology is improving as fast as computing.

## Main themes

### China: crisis or controlled slowdown

- Rogoff says China is overbuilt in housing and infrastructure, with consumption around 45% of the economy against close to 70% in the US. He traces the problem to the 2010 stimulus ([Ken Rogoff](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/china-is-digging-out-of-a-crisis-and-america-s-luck-is-wearing-thin-ke/)).
- He expects China to gain only about a percent a year on the US, and questions official growth figures ([Ken Rogoff](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/china-is-digging-out-of-a-crisis-and-america-s-luck-is-wearing-thin-ke/)).
- Kroeber argues China has too much supply-side strategy and not enough demand-side strategy, with falling returns on investment and more zombie companies ([Arthur Kroeber](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/why-china-s-manufacturing-economy-is-dominating-arthur-kroeber/)).
- Kroeber puts total debt at about 300% of GDP, high for a middle-income economy. He says China avoids a Japan-style crisis because industrial companies may not own banks ([Arthur Kroeber](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/why-china-s-manufacturing-economy-is-dominating-arthur-kroeber/)).

### How China built its manufacturing lead

- Kroeber compares the Chinese state to a giant VC fund that accepts losses, with EV subsidies estimated at $200-300 billion. He contrasts this with the US reaction to the failed Solyndra loan ([Arthur Kroeber](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/why-china-s-manufacturing-economy-is-dominating-arthur-kroeber/)).
- The early auto joint venture strategy did not create national champions: “Basically this failed. It failed massively.” Tesla's Shanghai Gigafactory later helped BYD and rivals catch up by around 2022 ([Arthur Kroeber](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/why-china-s-manufacturing-economy-is-dominating-arthur-kroeber/)).
- Kroeber defends wasteful growth as the price of 20 years at 10% a year, arguing effectiveness mattered more than efficiency ([Arthur Kroeber](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/why-china-s-manufacturing-economy-is-dominating-arthur-kroeber/)).
- On power he says “China has generating capacity that is more than double that of the United States”, which he sees as an advantage for AI data centres ([Arthur Kroeber](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/why-china-s-manufacturing-economy-is-dominating-arthur-kroeber/)).

### US debt, the Fed and the dollar

- Rogoff expects a US debt shock to bring 10-20% inflation rather than default, and says markets have too much faith in Federal Reserve independence ([Ken Rogoff](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/china-is-digging-out-of-a-crisis-and-america-s-luck-is-wearing-thin-ke/)).
- He values the dollar's privilege at half a percent to a percent lower interest on $37 trillion of debt, and expects it to decline gradually as China builds its own payment rails ([Ken Rogoff](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/china-is-digging-out-of-a-crisis-and-america-s-luck-is-wearing-thin-ke/)).
- Rogoff sees AI pushing interest rates up through energy and capital needs. He adds that fast AGI would dwarf today's populism and that “Nobody ever defaulted or had high inflation because of arithmetic” ([Ken Rogoff](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/china-is-digging-out-of-a-crisis-and-america-s-luck-is-wearing-thin-ke/)).

### US-China relations and biology

- Kroeber notes Trump cut about 100 working-level US-China dialogues to one, and argues an anti-China trading bloc is unworkable ([Arthur Kroeber](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/why-china-s-manufacturing-economy-is-dominating-arthur-kroeber/)).
- George Church argues biotech is on a curve like Moore's law, favours narrow scientific AI over a race to AGI, and wants stronger oversight of dual-use biology rather than voluntary moratoria ([George Church](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/06/a-billion-years-of-evolution-in-a-single-afternoon-george-church/)).
