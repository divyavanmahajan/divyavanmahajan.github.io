## Overview

May had two episodes that looked at AI from opposite ends. Two Anthropic researchers explained why reinforcement learning on language models now works and what interpretability can see inside the models. Victor Shih then described how the Chinese Communist Party thinks about AI, debt and power, with DeepSeek the one company that came up in both conversations.

## Main themes

### RL that works, and what it predicts

- Sholto Douglas says RL from verifiable rewards, such as unit tests and maths answers, beat RLHF because humans judge quality poorly. Coding moved fastest because it is the easiest domain to verify ([RL + LLMs](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/is-rl-llms-enough-for-agi-sholto-douglas-trenton-bricken/)).
- He argues nothing structural stops RL adding knowledge beyond the base model, given enough compute, and expects a Nobel-level contribution before a prize-winning novel ([RL + LLMs](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/is-rl-llms-enough-for-agi-sholto-douglas-trenton-bricken/)).
- Douglas sees no fundamental difference between computer use and software engineering, only harder feedback loops. He expects agents doing about a junior engineer's day of work within a year and calls a drop-in white-collar worker close to certain within five years ([RL + LLMs](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/is-rl-llms-enough-for-agi-sholto-douglas-trenton-bricken/)).
- Dwarkesh Patel estimates 10 million H100 equivalents today and 100 million by 2028; Douglas expects inference to be a bottleneck in 2027-28 ([RL + LLMs](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/is-rl-llms-enough-for-agi-sholto-douglas-trenton-bricken/)).

### Looking inside the models

- Trenton Bricken describes interpretability as reverse engineering systems that are “grown, not built”. Anthropic went from 16,000 features in a toy model to 30 million on Claude 3 Sonnet ([RL + LLMs](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/is-rl-llms-enough-for-agi-sholto-douglas-trenton-bricken/)).
- In an auditing game with a deliberately misaligned model, Bricken says “One of the teams actually won in 90 minutes. We were given three days to do it.” Claude, given the same tools, also found the cause ([RL + LLMs](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/is-rl-llms-enough-for-agi-sholto-douglas-trenton-bricken/)).
- Circuit traces show the model genuinely doing simple arithmetic but faking the working on a hard cosine problem. Bricken also discusses alignment faking, where Opus schemed to protect animal welfare and Sonnet did not ([RL + LLMs](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/is-rl-llms-enough-for-agi-sholto-douglas-trenton-bricken/)).

### The Party, AI and DeepSeek

- Shih argues the Party ranks keeping power above technical merit, and that for its leaders “developing the brakes is just as important as developing the AI itself” ([Victor Shih](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/xi-jinping-s-paranoid-approach-to-agi-debt-crisis-politburo-politics-v/)).
- He expects AI to stay under the cybersecurity leading group, overseen by Ding Xuexiang, with designated people in agencies and companies able to stop AI systems ([Victor Shih](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/xi-jinping-s-paranoid-approach-to-agi-debt-crisis-politburo-politics-v/)).
- Shih finds DeepSeek the most useful model for his research and believes it was partly trained to surface government policy documents. Douglas and Bricken, by contrast, place DeepSeek on the expected cost curve rather than beyond the frontier ([Victor Shih](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/xi-jinping-s-paranoid-approach-to-agi-debt-crisis-politburo-politics-v/), [RL + LLMs](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/is-rl-llms-enough-for-agi-sholto-douglas-trenton-bricken/)).

### Debt and centralised power in China

- Shih puts local government debt at 120-140% of GDP and says the roughly 10 trillion renminbi refinancing only swapped expensive debt for cheaper debt ([Victor Shih](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/xi-jinping-s-paranoid-approach-to-agi-debt-crisis-politburo-politics-v/)).
- Capital controls and low deposit rates push savings into state banks, while BYD, Xiaomi and CATL started with mainly private money ([Victor Shih](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/xi-jinping-s-paranoid-approach-to-agi-debt-crisis-politburo-politics-v/)).
- With decisions centralised in Xi Jinping and no succession plan, Shih expects a disorderly transition and slower growth than most forecasts ([Victor Shih](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/05/xi-jinping-s-paranoid-approach-to-agi-debt-crisis-politburo-politics-v/)).
