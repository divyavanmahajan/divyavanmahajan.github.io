## Overview

August had three guests who each argued that a hard problem has a practical path. Lewis Bollard covered factory farming, Casey Handmer the energy supply for AI data centres, and Jacob Kimmel the economics of medicines for ageing. This summary is based on episode summaries only.

## Main themes

### Factory farming will not end by default

- Lewis Bollard of Open Philanthropy argues that neither AGI nor cultivated meat will end factory farming on its own. Chickens turn grain into meat so cheaply that alternatives must beat roughly twice the price of grain ([Artificial meat](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/artificial-meat-is-harder-than-artificial-intelligence-lewis-bollard/)).
- He estimates under $300 million a year goes to all work on factory farming globally, and under $10 million a year of venture capital to humane technology such as in-ovo sexing ([Artificial meat](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/artificial-meat-is-harder-than-artificial-intelligence-lewis-bollard/)).
- Corporate campaigns have secured over 3,000 pledges, and the US went from under 10% to 47% cage-free. Bollard also describes the pork industry pushing farm bill language to override state sales standards ([Artificial meat](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/artificial-meat-is-harder-than-artificial-intelligence-lewis-bollard/)).

### Powering AI with solar and batteries

- Casey Handmer of Terraform Industries argues hyperscalers care about power availability, not cost. Around 10 cents of electricity produces $1,000 of value for a user, in his estimate ([Casey Handmer](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/china-is-killing-the-us-on-energy-does-that-mean-they-ll-win-agi-casey/)).
- Gas turbines are sold out until about 2030, so he predicts that by 2027 most new data centres breaking ground will run mostly on solar ([Casey Handmer](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/china-is-killing-the-us-on-energy-does-that-mean-they-ll-win-agi-casey/)).
- He estimates 5 gigawatts needs about 50,000 acres of solar, with land around 0.1% of the GPU bill, and calls for a NEPA exemption for solar ([Casey Handmer](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/china-is-killing-the-us-on-energy-does-that-mean-they-ll-win-agi-casey/)).

### China, energy and the AI race

- Handmer says China's solar manufacturing lead does not decide the race, because chips are the real bottleneck. He puts US manufacturing about five years behind and able to scale in two years or less ([Casey Handmer](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/china-is-killing-the-us-on-energy-does-that-mean-they-ll-win-agi-casey/)).
- Dwarkesh Patel pushes back on timing and permitting, and suggests energy use may track AI's value better than GDP ([Casey Handmer](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/china-is-killing-the-us-on-energy-does-that-mean-they-ll-win-agi-casey/)).

### The economics of longevity medicine

- Jacob Kimmel of NewLimit argues evolution put little effort into keeping us healthy for long, so ageing should be tractable. He expects medicines that add healthy years rather than a single cure ([Jacob Kimmel](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/evolution-designed-us-to-die-fast-we-can-change-that-jacob-kimmel/)).
- He sees AI models trained on proprietary lab data as essential, and compares Eroom's Law, the long fall in new drugs per billion dollars, with the need for each discovery to feed the next ([Jacob Kimmel](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/evolution-designed-us-to-die-fast-we-can-change-that-jacob-kimmel/)).
- Because US patients switch insurers every few years, Kimmel proposes pay-for-performance and direct-to-consumer models. He describes big pharma acting like venture capital, with small biotechs originating around 70% of approved molecules ([Jacob Kimmel](/podcasts/dwarkesh/2025/08/evolution-designed-us-to-die-fast-we-can-change-that-jacob-kimmel/)).
