## Overview

No Priors spent its first year mapping the post-ChatGPT landscape with founders, lab leaders and incumbent CEOs. The central thread was agents that take actions, followed by how slowly enterprises put LLMs into production. Open models and the GPU shortage ran through the year, and hosts Sarah Guo and Elad Gil argued against early regulation.

## Main themes

### From answering to acting: AI agents

- Alex Graveley argues the next few years of AI are about taking action, and founded Minion AI to build such agents ([Beyond GitHub Copilot](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-s-beyond-github-copilot-with-copilot-s-chief-architect-and-founde/)).
- Shyam Sankar says LLMs become useful when grounded in Palantir's ontology and built to change application state rather than chat ([Palantir](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/07/how-palantir-s-ai-bet-is-revolutionizing-defense-and-beyond-with-cto-s/)); the hosts say agents should start narrow ([Q&A: GPU Crunch](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/08/listener-q-a-2024-tech-market-predictions-long-term-implications-of-to/)).
- Amjad Masad expects coding agents within months ([Replit](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/how-replit-s-ai-tools-are-changing-software-development-with-co-founde/)); Imbue treats agents as a reliability problem ([Imbue](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/ai-agents-that-reason-and-code-with-imbue-co-founders-kanjun-qiu-and-j/)).

### Enterprise adoption lags the hype

- Lukas Biewald says “there's probably more companies that have raised money as like llm tools than companies that have LMS in production” ([Weights & Biases](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/08/eradicating-machine-learning-pain-points-with-weights-biases-ceo-lukas/)).
- Kawal Gandhi says success depends on organisational maturity, trust and data security more than the technology ([Google Cloud](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/what-google-cloud-can-teach-enterprises-developing-rolling-out-ai-tool/)).
- Clara Shih puts enterprise generative AI in its second or third inning ([Salesforce AI](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/12/ai-is-the-new-enterprise-ui-with-clara-shih-ceo-salesforce-ai/)).

### Open, small and customised models

- Emad Mostaque says “my take is that Foundation models will all be open source for the Deep learning phase” ([Stability AI](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/how-can-we-make-sure-that-everyone-has-access-to-ai-can-small-models-o/)); Clem Delangue expects many specialised models to win ([Hugging Face](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/02/why-the-future-of-machine-learning-is-open-source-with-huggingface-s-c/)).
- Matei Zaharia sees LLMs commoditising, with value in data and retrieval ([Databricks](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/04/the-future-is-small-models-with-matei-zaharia-cto-of-databricks/)).
- Arthur Mensch argues the field closed up too early after 2020 ([Mistral 7B](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/11/mistral-7b-and-the-open-source-revolution-with-arthur-mensch-ceo-mistr/)); the hosts compare Meta's Llama sponsorship to Facebook's work on MySQL ([Meta and open source](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/10/model-quality-fine-tuning-meta-sponsoring-open-source-ecosystem/)).

### Compute and the GPU crunch

- Jensen Huang traces NVIDIA's 30-year bet on accelerated computing ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/04/the-computing-platform-underlying-ai-with-jensen-huang-founder-ceo-of/)).
- Elad Gil says near-term capacity depends on how fast Nvidia and perhaps AMD can scale manufacturing ([Q&A: GPU Crunch](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/08/listener-q-a-2024-tech-market-predictions-long-term-implications-of-to/)); Andrew Feldman pitches wafer-scale chips against an inflexible supply chain ([Cerebras](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/the-world-s-largest-ai-processor-with-cerebras-ceo-andrew-feldman/)).

### Search, regulation and jobs

- Sridhar Ramaswamy and Perplexity's founders expect cited answers to replace links ([Neeva](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-is-the-future-of-search-with-neeva-s-sridhar-ramaswamy/), [Perplexity](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/how-do-we-go-from-search-engines-to-answer-engines-with-perplexity-ai/)).
- Elad Gil argues regulation locks in incumbents ([Q&A: Regulation](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/04/listener-q-a-ai-investment-hype-foundation-models-regulation-opportuni/)); Reid Hoffman warns against regulating before innovating ([AI Optimism](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/12/the-case-for-ai-optimism/)).
- Kevin Scott says “We've got a cognitive exponential” but no robotics one, so physical jobs gain demand ([Kevin Scott](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/05/going-full-send-on-ai-and-the-positive-impact-of-ai-on-jobs-with-kevin/)).
