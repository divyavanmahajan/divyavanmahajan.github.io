## Overview

September on No Priors covered AI compute and AI agents. Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman made the case against Nvidia's dominance, while Illia Polosukhin and Amjad Masad described agents that pay people and write software. The month closed with Ginkgo Bioworks on applying AI to biology as a data business.

## Main themes

### Chips, Nvidia and the supply crunch

- Feldman says Nvidia is “extorting customers they're extremely expensive they're unable to ship”, which opens the door for alternatives. He adds that Nvidia itself missed its sales forecast ([Cerebras](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/the-world-s-largest-ai-processor-with-cerebras-ceo-andrew-feldman/)).
- He blames the shortage on a profoundly inflexible chip supply chain and expects demand to keep being underestimated for two to three years ([Cerebras](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/the-world-s-largest-ai-processor-with-cerebras-ceo-andrew-feldman/)).
- Feldman says Cerebras laid down its wafer-scale architecture before Transformers existed and targets general sparse linear algebra rather than Transformer-specific circuits. He cites a customer whose GPU run had not converged after 60 days but converged on Cerebras in three and a half ([Cerebras](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/the-world-s-largest-ai-processor-with-cerebras-ceo-andrew-feldman/)).
- Polosukhin doubts alternative chipmakers can match Nvidia's economies of scale. He also says decentralised training cannot compete with fast links between GPUs ([NEAR](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/the-intersection-of-ai-and-blockchain-with-transformers-author-and-nea/)).

### Agents as economic actors

- Polosukhin argues that blockchain accounts turn AI agents into economic agents that can pay people and other AIs. He sees marketplaces for compute, models and data as the most obvious overlap between the two fields ([NEAR](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/the-intersection-of-ai-and-blockchain-with-transformers-author-and-nea/)).
- He describes organisations “run completely by AI right where quote unquote CEO role is taken by AI agent”, overseen by a community or board, with DAOs adopting this first ([NEAR](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/the-intersection-of-ai-and-blockchain-with-transformers-author-and-nea/)).
- For misinformation, he proposes cameras that sign images and an SSL-style green lock for signed content ([NEAR](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/the-intersection-of-ai-and-blockchain-with-transformers-author-and-nea/)).
- Masad also wants money as a platform primitive. Replit bounties pay programmers abroad, and he imagines automated bounty-hunter agents ([Replit](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/how-replit-s-ai-tools-are-changing-software-development-with-co-founde/)).

### AI coding at Replit

- Masad estimates from Replit's usage research that AI gives 30 to 50 percent more productivity on coding itself. Beginners benefit most, including a user who built a quarter-million ARR startup in months ([Replit](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/how-replit-s-ai-tools-are-changing-software-development-with-co-founde/)).
- He expects basic agentic coding within 6 to 18 months and says the field is in the demo era of GPT models. Replit is building a service that lets the AI install packages and edit files inside the container ([Replit](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/how-replit-s-ai-tools-are-changing-software-development-with-co-founde/)).
- Replit trained its own 3 billion parameter autocomplete model because commercial APIs were slow and expensive. Masad argues models know code syntax but not semantics, and could learn from execution results ([Replit](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/how-replit-s-ai-tools-are-changing-software-development-with-co-founde/)).

### Biology as a data business

- Jason Kelly describes Ginkgo's model: customers agree a spec, Ginkgo engineers and licenses the organism, and earns payments plus a royalty ([Ginkgo](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/if-dna-is-code-can-ai-help-write-it-scaling-cell-programming-and-synth/)).
- He says data is the main constraint on AI in this field. Ginkgo produces it in a 300,000 square foot robotic lab and announced a model partnership with Google ([Ginkgo](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/if-dna-is-code-can-ai-help-write-it-scaling-cell-programming-and-synth/)).
- Kelly frames biosecurity as similar to cybersecurity, and gives employees super-voting shares so staff govern the platform ([Ginkgo](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/09/if-dna-is-code-can-ai-help-write-it-scaling-cell-programming-and-synth/)).
