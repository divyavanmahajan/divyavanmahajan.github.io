## Overview

June on No Priors kept returning to one question: are AI systems starting to help build their own successors? Anthropic's Ben Mann and DeepMind's AlphaEvolve team both addressed it from different angles. The other half of the month was about AI in the physical world, from vehicle operating systems to 3D world models.

## Main themes

### Recursive self-improvement and timelines

- Elad Gil sketches a liftoff in which each Claude version builds the next one faster. Ben Mann says models are already useful for writing RL environments: “Models are pretty good at writing environments” ([Ben Mann](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/will-we-have-superintelligence-by-2028-with-anthropic-s-ben-mann/)).
- Mann calls a 2028 arrival of superintelligence quite possible but hard to bound with confidence. His own test for transformative AI is an agent hired over a human after a month-long trial, across roles covering half of economically valuable tasks ([Ben Mann](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/will-we-have-superintelligence-by-2028-with-anthropic-s-ben-mann/)).
- Sarah Guo cites AlphaEvolve's 23% speed-up in part of Gemini's training infrastructure. Matej Balog calls it self-improvement with a feedback loop of months, and whether gains compound is still open ([AlphaEvolve](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/meet-alphaevolve-the-autonomous-agent-that-discovers-algorithms-better/)).

### Anthropic's models, Claude Code and MCP

- Mann says Claude 4 mostly stops the reward hacking seen in Sonnet 3.7, and some customers run it unattended for hours on large refactors ([Ben Mann](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/will-we-have-superintelligence-by-2028-with-anthropic-s-ben-mann/)).
- Claude Code exists because a direct relationship with coding users teaches Anthropic faster than learning through partners such as Cursor and GitHub. Inside the tool, Opus delegates to Sonnet sub-agents to save cost, latency and context ([Ben Mann](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/will-we-have-superintelligence-by-2028-with-anthropic-s-ben-mann/)).
- Mann positions Anthropic like Adyen rather than Stripe: less mind share, a strong enterprise API business. He says OpenAI, Google and Microsoft now back MCP, including hosted remote servers ([Ben Mann](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/will-we-have-superintelligence-by-2028-with-anthropic-s-ben-mann/)).
- On safety, Opus 4 is classified ASL-3 under Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy, and a consumer computer-use agent was held back over irreversible actions and prompt injection ([Ben Mann](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/will-we-have-superintelligence-by-2028-with-anthropic-s-ben-mann/)).

### AlphaEvolve and the evaluator bottleneck

- Pushmeet Kohli traces AlphaEvolve from AlphaGo through AlphaTensor and FunSearch. It pairs Gemini with evolutionary search over a diverse pool of candidate programs ([AlphaEvolve](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/meet-alphaevolve-the-autonomous-agent-that-discovers-algorithms-better/)).
- Balog sums up the division of labour: “you define the what by providing the evaluation function”, and the system fills in the how ([AlphaEvolve](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/meet-alphaevolve-the-autonomous-agent-that-discovers-algorithms-better/)).
- Kohli says a strict evaluator turns hallucinations into something useful. Both guests think the need for precise evaluators can be relaxed with LLM judges and imperfect simulators ([AlphaEvolve](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/meet-alphaevolve-the-autonomous-agent-that-discovers-algorithms-better/)).

### AI in the physical world

- Qasar Younis describes Applied Intuition as a profitable company valued at $15 billion that follows Microsoft's path of tools, then OS, then applications: “We're doing the same except the hardware is not PC manufacturers. It's cars and trucks.” ([Applied Intuition](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/the-operating-system-for-self-driving-cars-and-tanks-and-trucks-with-q/)).
- Younis expects FSD-like features to be common in the US within five years and prices to fall towards free by 2030-2035. He calls Europe asleep at the wheel compared with Chinese manufacturers ([Applied Intuition](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/the-operating-system-for-self-driving-cars-and-tanks-and-trucks-with-q/)).
- Fei-Fei Li argues AI stays incomplete without spatial intelligence. World Labs is building a 3D generation foundation model, and data is the main bottleneck because 3D lacks abundant internet data ([Fei-Fei Li](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/teaching-ai-to-understand-the-physical-world-with-dr-fei-fei-li-of-wor/)).
