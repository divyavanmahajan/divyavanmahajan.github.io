## Overview

June on No Priors brought four big-name guests with little overlap: Satya Nadella, the Biohub founders, Intel's Lip-Bu Tan and OpenAI's Noam Brown. The common thread was who controls the next layer of AI, from chips and fabs to evaluations and open models.

## Main themes

### Test-time compute changes how models are judged

- Noam Brown argues the standard benchmark grid misleads because models keep improving with more thinking time. He wants results plotted against tokens, cost or time ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/really-big-test-time-compute-in-ai-changes-benchmarks-safety-and-resea/)).
- He says GPT-5.5 looked underwhelming at launch, but “once you control for the amount of thinking time, actually you can see that 5.5 is a substantial jump over 5.4.” ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/really-big-test-time-compute-in-ai-changes-benchmarks-safety-and-resea/)).
- On safety, Brown says preparedness frameworks and responsible scaling policies do not specify a compute budget for testing dangerous capabilities. Full evaluation would mean delaying releases, and new models arrive every two or three months ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/really-big-test-time-compute-in-ai-changes-benchmarks-safety-and-resea/)).
- He expects a gradual takeoff rather than an overnight intelligence explosion, because time is a bottleneck and models still lack research taste ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/really-big-test-time-compute-in-ai-changes-benchmarks-safety-and-resea/)).

### Intel, CPUs and the chip supply chain

- Lip-Bu Tan describes his first 14 months at Intel as fixing the culture and the balance sheet, with the US government, Nvidia and SoftBank as backers ([Intel](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/re-engineering-the-semiconductor-supply-chain-with-intel-ceo-lip-bu-ta/)).
- He says agentic AI makes CPUs matter again: the CPU-to-GPU ratio, “1 to 8 in the training CPU to GPU, now I can see 1 to 4, maybe 1 to 1.” ([Intel](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/re-engineering-the-semiconductor-supply-chain-with-intel-ceo-lip-bu-ta/)).
- Tan argues domestic chipmaking is critical for resilience. Intel is moving 14A into production and helping Elon Musk's Terafab reach production faster ([Intel](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/re-engineering-the-semiconductor-supply-chain-with-intel-ceo-lip-bu-ta/)).
- As an investor he looks for the bottleneck being solved, such as interconnect, optics and power, and wants a hyperscaler as the first customer ([Intel](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/re-engineering-the-semiconductor-supply-chain-with-intel-ceo-lip-bu-ta/)).

### An ecosystem built on private evals

- Satya Nadella, in a live crossover with Latent Space at Microsoft Build, argues every company should operate at the frontier with its own intelligence, built from its data, traces and private evals on an open harness ([Nadella](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/we-need-an-ecosystem-in-ai-and-every-company-can-win-a-place-in-it/)).
- He calls private evals possibly a company's biggest IP, since public evals can all be maxed. His test of control is whether you can switch from model A to model B and keep improving ([Nadella](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/we-need-an-ecosystem-in-ai-and-every-company-can-win-a-place-in-it/)).
- Nadella says SaaS is unbundled rather than dead, with pricing moving to a mix of per-user and consumption. Data centres, he adds, must show visible community benefits within 12-18 months ([Nadella](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/we-need-an-ecosystem-in-ai-and-every-company-can-win-a-place-in-it/)).

### Open models for science

- Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan describe Biohub as their main philanthropy, pairing an AI lab with in-house data generation and releasing models as open source so the whole field moves faster ([Biohub](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/biohub-the-future-of-biology-is-open-source-with-co-founders-mark-zuck/)).
- They chose a nonprofit because of a 10-15 year horizon, heavy capital needs and the pull a neutral party has on the field. Zuckerberg links open source to empowering individuals while noting biosafety trade-offs ([Biohub](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/biohub-the-future-of-biology-is-open-source-with-co-founders-mark-zuck/)).
