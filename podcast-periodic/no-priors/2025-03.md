## Overview

March on No Priors was four single-guest interviews. Dan Hendrycks framed AI risk as national security strategy, Thomas Dohmke described GitHub Copilot's move to agents, Chelsea Finn explained Physical Intelligence's approach to robot foundation models, and Shiv Rao made the business case for Abridge in healthcare.

## Main themes

### AI risk as geopolitics

- Dan Hendrycks says AI is “not actually that relevant for National Security currently this could well change within a year's time” ([Dan Hendrycks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/national-security-strategy-and-ai-evals-on-the-eve-of-superintelligenc/)).
- He treats alignment as a subset of safety: obedient AIs on both the US and Chinese sides would still feed a risky strategic race. He rejects both voluntary pauses and an all-out race to superintelligence ([Dan Hendrycks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/national-security-strategy-and-ai-evals-on-the-eve-of-superintelligenc/)).
- His proposal, written with Eric Schmidt and Alexandr Wang, is deterrence through mutually assured AI malfunction, plus tracking AI chips like fissile material. He says DeepSeek shows you can restrict a superpower's intent but not its capabilities ([Dan Hendrycks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/national-security-strategy-and-ai-evals-on-the-eve-of-superintelligenc/)).
- On evals, he describes Humanity's Last Exam as the end of the road for exam-style benchmarks; agent tasks are still near the floor ([Dan Hendrycks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/national-security-strategy-and-ai-evals-on-the-eve-of-superintelligenc/)).

### Coding agents and software pricing

- Thomas Dohmke describes agent mode in VS Code and a preview where Copilot takes an issue and opens a draft pull request. Early Copilot wrote about 25% of code, later around 50% depending on language ([GitHub Copilot](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/copilot-agent-mode-and-the-new-world-of-dev-tools-with-github-s-ceo-th/)).
- He says agents need to be predictable, steerable, verifiable and tolerable. Today one still gets stuck and “just keeps rewriting the same file”, so autonomous backlog clearing is not close ([GitHub Copilot](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/copilot-agent-mode-and-the-new-world-of-dev-tools-with-github-s-ceo-th/)).
- Dohmke expects compute-based agent pricing, like a dishwasher rather than a salary, with deflation in some software and higher prices for high-value products ([GitHub Copilot](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/copilot-agent-mode-and-the-new-world-of-dev-tools-with-github-s-ceo-th/)).

### Robot foundation models

- Chelsea Finn says Physical Intelligence is building one network to control any robot doing any task, trained on data pooled across different robot bodies ([Chelsea Finn](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/the-robotics-revolution-with-physical-intelligence-s-cofounder-chelsea/)).
- She sees data diversity, not hardware or sensors, as the main bottleneck, and thinks humanoids are a little overrated because they are harder to teleoperate. The company has open-sourced weights to help the field mature ([Chelsea Finn](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/the-robotics-revolution-with-physical-intelligence-s-cofounder-chelsea/)).

### Healthcare AI as a business

- Shiv Rao says Abridge targeted large health systems first, the hardest end of the market, and usually wins head-to-head trials against Microsoft ([Abridge](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/conversations-are-the-source-of-truth-in-healthcare-with-abridge-ceo-s/)).
- Founded in 2018, Abridge struggled to sell until post-pandemic burnout and ChatGPT arrived together. Rao says “the only currency that ends up mattering in healthcare is trust” ([Abridge](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/conversations-are-the-source-of-truth-in-healthcare-with-abridge-ceo-s/)).
- Abridge has raised more than $500 million, including a $250 million Series D, with about 80% meant for R&D ([Abridge](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/conversations-are-the-source-of-truth-in-healthcare-with-abridge-ceo-s/)).
