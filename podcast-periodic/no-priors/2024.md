## Overview

In 2024 No Priors moved from what models can do to how companies ship them. Enterprise adoption and evaluation were the most central threads, with agents narrowing to coding and customer service. Compute ran all year, from Nvidia's moat to energy and cooling limits. Sora and humanoid robots brought video and the physical world into the conversation.

## Main themes

### Enterprise adoption has barely started

- Elad Gil says “the big wave of Enterprise adoption hasn't even happened yet”, with most spending so far on vendors ([New Markets](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/07/how-ai-is-opening-up-new-markets-and-impacting-the-startup-status-quo/)).
- Howie Liu blames imagination rather than models, and wants AI-built apps to come out as inspectable no-code ([Airtable](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/07/low-code-in-the-age-of-ai-and-going-enterprise-with-howie-liu-from-air/)).
- Matt Garman sees inference as another cloud building block ([AWS](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/cloud-strategy-in-the-ai-era-with-matt-garman-ceo-of-aws/)); Aidan Gomez says enterprises already have years of integration work with today's models ([Cohere](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/11/model-plateaus-and-enterprise-ai-adoption-with-cohere-s-aidan-gomez/)).

### Evals and data decide quality

- Suhail Doshi says “most evals in the industry are relatively flawed” and picks releases by inspecting thousands of images ([Playground AI](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/the-future-of-ai-artistry-with-suhail-doshi-from-playground-ai/)).
- Alexandr Wang argues easy internet data is exhausted, so progress needs expert data and held-out evals ([Scale](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/05/the-data-foundry-for-ai-with-alexandr-wang-from-scale/)).
- Ankur Goyal explains why evals stay essential for non-deterministic products ([Braintrust](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/10/launching-ai-products-with-braintrust-s-ceo-ankur-goyal/)).

### Agents, coding and retrieval

- Harrison Chase says LLM planning is the biggest gap, so working agents are controlled state machines ([LangChain](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/03/open-sourcing-ai-app-development-with-harrison-chase-from-langchain/)); Bret Taylor pitches branded company agents sold on outcomes ([Sierra](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/09/transforming-customer-service-through-company-agents-with-sierra-s-bre/)).
- Beyang Liu aims for issue-to-pull-request automation built on retrieved code context ([Sourcegraph](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/coding-in-collaboration-with-ai-with-sourcegraph-cto-beyang-liu/)); Scott Wu argues Devin will raise demand for engineers ([Cognition](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/05/cognition-s-scott-wu-on-how-devin-the-ai-software-engineer-will-work-f/)).
- Tengyu Ma expects RAG to stay cheaper than long context ([Voyage AI](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/06/the-evolution-and-promise-of-rag-architecture-with-tengyu-ma-from-voya/)), while Eric Steinberger bets on a long-context coding model ([Magic.dev](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/building-toward-a-bright-post-agi-future-with-eric-steinberger-from-ma/)).

### Compute, chips and energy

- The hosts call Nvidia's moat a mix of chip performance, CUDA and interconnect ([Big Tech Earnings](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/03/big-tech-earnings-and-the-current-ai-debates-with-sarah-guo-and-elad-g/)); Mark Papermaster pitches AMD as the alternative ([AMD](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/competition-makes-for-better-chip-design-with-amd-cto-mark-papermaster/)).
- Sarah Guo notes nobody has yet built a gigawatt data center ([Energy Consumption](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/05/context-windows-computer-constraints-and-energy-consumption-with-sarah/)); Akash Systems treats heat as the limit ([Diamond Cooling](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/how-diamond-cooling-could-power-the-future-of-ai-with-akash-systems/)).
- Jensen Huang hopes to double or triple performance at data-center scale every year ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/11/nvidia-s-jensen-huang-on-ai-chip-design-scaling-data-centers-and-his-1/)).

### Video, robots and world models

- Sora's leads say scaling video models will yield world simulators, calling Sora the GPT-1 of video ([Sora](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/openai-s-sora-team-thinks-we-ve-only-seen-the-gpt-1-of-video-models/)).
- Brett Adcock argues for humanoids because “the whole world was built for humans” ([Figure](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/the-argument-for-humanoid-ai-robots-with-brett-adcock-from-figure/)); Peter Chen bets on real warehouse data ([Covariant](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/01/building-the-factories-of-the-future-with-covariant-ceo-peter-chen/)).
- Dmitri Dolgov says Waymo has cracked the long tail and is scaling ([Waymo](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/10/waymo-s-journey-to-full-autonomy-ai-breakthroughs-safety-and-scaling/)).
