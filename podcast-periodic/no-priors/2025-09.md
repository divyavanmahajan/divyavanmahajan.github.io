## Overview

September on No Priors was about AI reaching institutions: large enterprises, governments, schools and medical practice. The shared thread was how adoption actually happens, with Jared Kushner and Decagon's Jesse Zhang both describing it as driven from the top. Alpha School's Joe Liemandt made the most radical case, for rebuilding the school day around AI tutors.

## Main themes

### How enterprises adopt AI

- Zhang says enterprise buying has changed: “at the enterprise it's becoming a lot more of a top down motion.” Boards and C-suites push adoption, and customer service is the easiest place to start ([Decagon](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/ai-agents-talking-to-ai-agents-reinventing-commerce-with-decagon-ceo-j/)).
- Kushner describes BrainCo, co-founded with Elad Gil in January 2024, as an AI implementation company. Gil frames it as a common platform plus reusable apps for the world's largest institutions ([Kushner](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/jared-kushner-brainco-affinity-partners-and-the-geopolitics-of-ai/)).
- Kushner looks for a hard problem, the right data and leadership able to push through resistance: “do they have the right command and control structure in the company where people are willing to buy into it” ([Kushner](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/jared-kushner-brainco-affinity-partners-and-the-geopolitics-of-ai/)).
- In a government permitting case, BrainCo reached over 90% accuracy on 35 steps of a process that took humans 3 to 4 months, and the client plans to cut more than 10 steps ([Kushner](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/jared-kushner-brainco-affinity-partners-and-the-geopolitics-of-ai/)).

### Customer service agents and agent commerce

- Zhang sees agents as a substitute for mundane labour inside the existing CRM and telephony stack. Case studies show contact-centre costs cut by 60-70% ([Decagon](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/ai-agents-talking-to-ai-agents-reinventing-commerce-with-decagon-ceo-j/)).
- He prices per conversation rather than per seat; Gil notes this targets the salaries of people doing the work rather than software seats ([Decagon](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/ai-agents-talking-to-ai-agents-reinventing-commerce-with-decagon-ceo-j/)).
- Zhang expects consumer agents to talk to company agents soon, still in natural language because both must stay compatible with humans ([Decagon](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/ai-agents-talking-to-ai-agents-reinventing-commerce-with-decagon-ceo-j/)).

### AI in schools

- Liemandt warns: “There's a bigger wave coming, which is the current school system does not know what's about to hit it.” ([Alpha School](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/reinventing-k-12-education-using-ai-with-alpha-school-principal-joe-li/)).
- Alpha students spend two hours a day with an AI tutor that keeps them at 80-85% accuracy, then do afternoon workshops on life skills. Liemandt says a grade level of one subject takes 20-30 hours to master ([Alpha School](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/reinventing-k-12-education-using-ai-with-alpha-school-principal-joe-li/)).
- Guides earn at least $100,000 and focus on motivation. Liemandt bans chat in the academic block, calling ChatGPT a cheating tool for most students ([Alpha School](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/reinventing-k-12-education-using-ai-with-alpha-school-principal-joe-li/)).

### Selling to professionals and countries

- Daniel Nadler credits OpenEvidence's spread to 40% of US doctors to selling directly to physicians as consumers through a free app, instead of through health systems. Citations were a first-class feature from the start ([OpenEvidence](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/a-new-operating-system-for-physicians-with-openevidence-founder-daniel/)).
- Nadler expects doctors to stay in the loop for our lifetimes, because people personify trust ([OpenEvidence](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/a-new-operating-system-for-physicians-with-openevidence-founder-daniel/)).
- Kushner treats countries as mispriced assets, citing Affinity's investments in Albania and Israel's Phoenix Holdings. He backs building energy fast, noting a large backlog of capacity awaiting permits ([Kushner](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/jared-kushner-brainco-affinity-partners-and-the-geopolitics-of-ai/)).
