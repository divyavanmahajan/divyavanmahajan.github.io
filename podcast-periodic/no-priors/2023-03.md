## Overview

March on No Priors was dominated by search. Neeva and Perplexity both argued that cited answers would replace lists of links, with Google as the incumbent to beat. The rest of the month covered GitHub Copilot and the next step to agents, Stanford's view of academia's role in foundation models, and machine learning in drug discovery.

## Main themes

### From search engines to answer engines

- Sridhar Ramaswamy says users always wanted answers over links. Google's featured snippets never covered more than about 5-7% of queries, while LLM summaries let Neeva answer 50-70% ([Neeva](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-is-the-future-of-search-with-neeva-s-sridhar-ramaswamy/)).
- Ramaswamy calls launching answers “the single biggest positive thing for the team” at Neeva. He sees distribution, serving costs and publisher economics as the hard problems ([Neeva](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-is-the-future-of-search-with-neeva-s-sridhar-ramaswamy/)).
- Aravind Srinivas and Denis Yarats expect answer engines to become their own market segment. Perplexity says only what it can cite and shows just three to four links ([Perplexity](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/how-do-we-go-from-search-engines-to-answer-engines-with-perplexity-ai/)).

### Search economics and the incumbent

- Ramaswamy says ads carry elements of self-destruction and that answer products are harder to monetise with ads. He expects large sites such as Reddit and Quora to restrict LLM use of their content ([Neeva](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-is-the-future-of-search-with-neeva-s-sridhar-ramaswamy/)).
- Yarats argues Google is slow to ship answers because ad clicks fund its business. Srinivas expects fewer but more valuable clicks and lists an API, a prosumer tier and later ads as options ([Perplexity](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/how-do-we-go-from-search-engines-to-answer-engines-with-perplexity-ai/)).

### Copilot, latency and agents that act

- Alex Graveley says Copilot began as a skunkworks project whose pass rate on real repository tests rose from under 10% to over 60% ([Copilot](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-s-beyond-github-copilot-with-copilot-s-chief-architect-and-founde/)).
- He says every 10 milliseconds of latency cost about 1% of completions, and weekly-cohort retention stayed above 50% months after launch ([Copilot](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-s-beyond-github-copilot-with-copilot-s-chief-architect-and-founde/)).
- Graveley's Minion AI applies the Copilot idea to everyday tasks such as flights, schedules and taxes. Elad Gil says agents may now be able to do these things “for us for real” ([Copilot](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-s-beyond-github-copilot-with-copilot-s-chief-architect-and-founde/)).

### Academia, evaluation and transparency

- Percy Liang says CRFM's mission is transparency and accessibility of foundation models, as open culture retreats behind APIs ([Percy Liang](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-is-the-role-of-academia-in-modern-ai-research-with-stanford-profe/)).
- HELM compared 30 models across 42 scenarios and seven metrics, including robustness, calibration and fairness, and is refreshed every two weeks ([Percy Liang](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-is-the-role-of-academia-in-modern-ai-research-with-stanford-profe/)).
- Liang says in-context learning surprised him, and hopes the transformer is replaced within a decade ([Percy Liang](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/what-is-the-role-of-academia-in-modern-ai-research-with-stanford-profe/)).

### Machine learning in drug discovery

- Daphne Koller argues drug development is expensive mainly because about 95% of programmes fail, so Insitro focuses on the early target-discovery step ([Insitro](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/how-ai-can-make-drug-discovery-fail-less-with-daphne-koller-from-insit/)).
- She describes Insitro as a discovery engine that can partner on or license out programmes, and notes that slow regulatory and disease timelines limit how far ML can speed things up ([Insitro](/podcasts/no-priors/2023/03/how-ai-can-make-drug-discovery-fail-less-with-daphne-koller-from-insit/)).
