## Overview

No Priors spent 2025 tracking AI agents as they moved from customer support and research tools into enterprise software, coding and commerce. Reasoning models and reinforcement learning were the technical thread, with evals named again and again as the limit. In the second half the show turned to chips, power and US industrial policy. Founder interviews from Harvey, Glean, Snowflake and Decagon showed which vertical AI businesses were working.

## Main themes

### Agents find their first real markets

- Jesse Zhang picked customer support because agents can roll out gradually with easy-to-measure ROI, and expects many other agent uses to move “a lot slower than people think” ([Decagon](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/01/how-ai-agents-are-transforming-customer-support-with-decagon-s-jesse-z/)). By September he described agents negotiating with consumers' own agents ([Agents Talking to Agents](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/09/ai-agents-talking-to-ai-agents-reinventing-commerce-with-decagon-ceo-j/)).
- Isa Fulford explained how RL on expert browsing tasks produced Deep Research, and what still separates it from a general agent ([Inside Deep Research](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/04/inside-deep-research-with-isa-fulford-building-the-future-of-ai-agents/)).
- Arvind Jain and Sridhar Ramaswamy built permission-aware search and an opinionated data agent for employees ([Glean](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/ai-is-making-enterprise-search-relevant-with-arvind-jain-of-glean/), [Snowflake Intelligence](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/11/meet-snowflake-intelligence-a-personalized-enterprise-intelligence-age/)).
- Nikesh Arora argued agents threaten business models more than generative AI does ([Palo Alto Networks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/the-impact-of-ai-from-business-models-to-cybersecurity-with-palo-alto/)).

### Reasoning, reinforcement learning and evals

- OpenAI's Eric Mitchell and Brandon McKinzie said RL plus tool use makes o3's longer thinking pay off, with evals and simulation now the limit ([o3](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/05/o3-and-the-next-leap-in-reasoning-with-openai-s-eric-mitchell-and-bran/)).
- Dan Hendrycks said models are still weak agents and that “the intelligence Frontier is just so Jagged” ([Dan Hendrycks](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/national-security-strategy-and-ai-evals-on-the-eve-of-superintelligenc/)). Brendan Foody expects building evals to become a major job ([Brendan Foody](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/04/from-job-displacement-to-ai-trainers-brendan-foody-on-work-in-the-ai-a/)).
- Misha Laskin and Edwin Chen tied RL progress to rewards, customer-driven evals and high-quality human data ([ReflectionAI](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/07/asimov-building-an-omniscient-rl-oracle-with-reflectionai-s-misha-lask/), [SurgeAI](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/07/the-power-of-quality-human-data-with-surgeai-founder-and-ceo-edwin-che/)).
- Ben Mann saw recursive self-improvement within reach, and AlphaEvolve paired Gemini with strict evaluators to find better algorithms ([Ben Mann](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/will-we-have-superintelligence-by-2028-with-anthropic-s-ben-mann/), [AlphaEvolve](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/meet-alphaevolve-the-autonomous-agent-that-discovers-algorithms-better/)).

### Chips, data centers and power

- Dylan Patel listed bottlenecks moving from CoWoS and HBM to transformers and power, and said “Meta is literally building these like temporary like tent structures to put GPUs in” ([SemiAnalysis](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/chips-neoclouds-and-the-quest-for-ai-dominance-with-semianalysis-found/)).
- Sriram Krishnan and Jacob Helberg framed infrastructure, supply chains and nuclear power as a race with China ([Sriram Krishnan](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/07/america-s-plan-to-dominate-the-full-ai-stack-with-sriram-krishnan/), [Jacob Helberg](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/reshaping-america-s-economy-for-the-superintelligence-century-with-jac/)).
- Zach Dell argued home batteries can unlock idle grid capacity ([Base Power](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/unlocking-the-road-to-energy-abundance-with-base-power-ceo-and-co-foun/)).

### Jobs, coding and vertical winners

- Brendan Foody said displacement is already showing in support and recruiting, while Parker Conrad expects AI to raise the bar for software rather than shrink teams ([Brendan Foody](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/04/from-job-displacement-to-ai-trainers-brendan-foody-on-work-in-the-ai-a/), [Rippling](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/07/why-platforms-win-and-point-solutions-fail-with-rippling-ceo-parker-co/)).
- Thomas Dohmke, Andrew Ng and Zach Lloyd saw coding agents shifting the bottleneck to systems thinking and product decisions ([GitHub](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/copilot-agent-mode-and-the-new-world-of-dev-tools-with-github-s-ceo-th/), [Andrew Ng](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/08/how-agentic-ai-is-transforming-the-startup-landscape-with-andrew-ng/), [Warp](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/10/reinventing-the-developer-terminal-with-warp-co-founder-and-ceo-zach-l/)).
- Harvey went from lawyer tools to whole-firm profitability ([Winston Weinberg](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/how-harvey-ai-is-changing-the-legal-industry-with-winston-weinberg/), [Gabe Pereyra](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/12/scaling-legal-ai-and-building-next-generation-law-firms-with-harvey-co/)).

### Robots and world models

- Chelsea Finn said robot foundation models need diverse real-robot data, and Sunday Robotics collects it with a sensor glove ([Physical Intelligence](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/03/the-robotics-revolution-with-physical-intelligence-s-cofounder-chelsea/), [Sunday Robotics](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/11/sunday-robotics-scaling-the-home-robot-revolution-with-co-founders-ton/)).
- Fei-Fei Li argued AI needs spatial intelligence and 3D world models ([World Labs](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/06/teaching-ai-to-understand-the-physical-world-with-dr-fei-fei-li-of-wor/)).
