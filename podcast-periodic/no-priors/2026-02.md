## Overview

February on No Priors had four separate subjects with no shared thread: cars, capital, software markets and games. RJ Scaringe explained Rivian's bet on neural-net autonomy, Neil Tiwari of Magnetar explained how GPU clusters are financed, and the hosts argued that the SaaS panic is overdone. Dave Baszucki rounded out the month with Roblox's plans for AI-driven NPCs.

## Main themes

### Autonomy and the software-defined car

- Scaringe says Rivian reset its autonomy platform at the turn of 2022, and the Gen 2 stack shared no code or perception and compute hardware with Gen 1. He argues that rules-based systems have “a truly a 0% chance of progressing to be competitive with a neural net based approach” ([Rivian](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/rivian-s-roadmap-to-ai-architecture-and-autonomy-with-founder-and-ceo/)).
- He says the line between Level 2, 3 and 4 is fading and now comes down to rare corner cases. Rivian built its own inference chip because onboard compute, not sensors, is the expensive part ([Rivian](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/rivian-s-roadmap-to-ai-architecture-and-autonomy-with-founder-and-ceo/)).
- Most cars run 100 to 150 supplier ECUs; Rivian's zonal design uses one to three computers and ships an over-the-air update about once a month. The approach underpins a $5.8 billion licensing deal with Volkswagen Group ([Rivian](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/rivian-s-roadmap-to-ai-architecture-and-autonomy-with-founder-and-ceo/)).
- R2 starts at $45,000 and adds lidar, turning the fleet into a training data platform. Scaringe frames it as a real alternative rather than another Model Y ([Rivian](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/rivian-s-roadmap-to-ai-architecture-and-autonomy-with-founder-and-ceo/)).

### Financing the AI buildout

- Tiwari says GPU debt is secured mainly by five-year take-or-pay contracts with investment-grade buyers, with the GPUs as secondary collateral ([Magnetar](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/how-capital-is-powering-the-ai-infrastructure-buildout-with-magnetar-c/)).
- The debt fully amortises over four to five years against a two-to-three-year payback, so there is “no balloon payment”. Newer SPVs now mix in AI labs and startups as counterparties ([Magnetar](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/how-capital-is-powering-the-ai-infrastructure-buildout-with-magnetar-c/)).
- He traces Magnetar's role to meeting CoreWeave in 2021 as it moved from Ethereum mining to high-performance compute, and credits CoreWeave's early win to scale and reliability ([Magnetar](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/how-capital-is-powering-the-ai-infrastructure-buildout-with-magnetar-c/)).

### Power, steel and people

- Tiwari says the 2023-24 bottleneck was chips; in 2026 it is turning chips into revenue-generating assets through people, power and infrastructure ([Magnetar](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/how-capital-is-powering-the-ai-infrastructure-buildout-with-magnetar-c/)).
- He argues there is stranded power on the grid held for peak demand, so storage and distribution matter more than new generation in the short term. Structural steel, electricians, transformers and chillers are the 6-12 month constraint ([Magnetar](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/how-capital-is-powering-the-ai-infrastructure-buildout-with-magnetar-c/)).
- He expects Fortune 500 and AI companies to want dedicated AI factories for workloads they control ([Magnetar](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/how-capital-is-powering-the-ai-infrastructure-buildout-with-magnetar-c/)).

### The SaaS sell-off

- Elad Gil calls the sell-off a market correction around an idea with long-run truth, overstated in the short term because it projects small startups' habits onto the largest enterprises ([SaaS to AI-First](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/from-saas-to-ai-first-how-companies-are-reshaping-innovation/)).
- Gil sees a real shift in customer support, where Decagon and Sierra move from per-seat to usage pricing. He argues bundles are the best defence; Sarah Guo lists platforms, networks and hardware as control points ([SaaS to AI-First](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/from-saas-to-ai-first-how-companies-are-reshaping-innovation/)).
- Tiwari also calls the SaaS sell-off overdone, since free-cash-flow margins rose while multiples fell ([Magnetar](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/how-capital-is-powering-the-ai-infrastructure-buildout-with-magnetar-c/)).

### Roblox and NPCs

- Baszucki says Roblox wants to store about 13 billion hours of monthly play as vector data, replayable from any angle, and use it to train NPCs that go beyond an LLM ([Roblox](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/introducing-4d-creation-open-beta-npcs-4d-worlds-and-the-future-of-gam/)).
- He sets an interim goal of 10% of global gaming content, about 300 million daily users ([Roblox](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/introducing-4d-creation-open-beta-npcs-4d-worlds-and-the-future-of-gam/)).
