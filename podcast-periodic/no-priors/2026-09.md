## Overview

September on No Priors was four CEO interviews, each about a different layer of the AI economy. Arm's Rene Haas covered chips and compute supply, Inception's Stefano Ermon made the case for diffusion models at inference time, and Coinbase's Brian Armstrong described finance for agents. Sequence Holdings' Michael Lee closed the month with an ownership model for transforming incumbents.

## Main themes

### Chips and compute supply

- Haas describes Arm's path from licensing CPU IP to compute subsystems and then its first chip, the Arm AGI CPU. He says Meta “wanted a general purpose agentic CPU” that nobody else offered ([Arm](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/redefining-chip-architecture-with-arm-ceo-rene-haas/)).
- He says customers such as Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft and Google pushed back less than expected, though the move forced Arm to build supply-chain and physical-design teams ([Arm](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/redefining-chip-architecture-with-arm-ceo-rene-haas/)).
- Chip design takes 24 to 36 months, mostly verification, validation and debug, where Haas says AI is strong. RTL generation and physical design lag because the data is proprietary ([Arm](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/redefining-chip-architecture-with-arm-ceo-rene-haas/)).
- Haas expects compute to stay constrained for three to five years, with data centre construction the next bottleneck, and sees no bubble ([Arm](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/redefining-chip-architecture-with-arm-ceo-rene-haas/)).

### Diffusion models for inference

- Ermon argues inference economics will decide AI. Autoregressive models leave GPUs memory-bound, while diffusion refines many tokens in parallel ([Inception](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/why-diffusion-will-win-ai-inference-with-inception-co-founder-and-ceo/)).
- He cites the bitter lesson: “the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win” ([Inception](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/why-diffusion-will-win-ai-inference-with-inception-co-founder-and-ceo/)).
- Inception is about two years old with around 50 people. It built its own serving engine because vLLM and SGLang cannot run its models ([Inception](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/why-diffusion-will-win-ai-inference-with-inception-co-founder-and-ceo/)).
- Ermon says diffusion may be easier to control and more data efficient. Voice-agent company OpenCall moved from Cerebras to Mercury on Nvidia GPUs with similar speed ([Inception](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/why-diffusion-will-win-ai-inference-with-inception-co-founder-and-ceo/)).

### Finance for agents

- Armstrong says about 76% of the agentic commerce transactions Coinbase sees are under 30 cents, below the fixed card fee. Coinbase wants to bank the AIs with self-custodial agent wallets ([Coinbase](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/coinbase-s-everything-exchange-agentic-finance-stablecoins-and-tokeniz/)).
- He predicts more agents than humans and an agentic economy larger than the human one ([Coinbase](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/coinbase-s-everything-exchange-agentic-finance-stablecoins-and-tokeniz/)).
- 88% of Coinbase revenue now comes from beyond Bitcoin trading. Its tokenized stocks are real securities held one-to-one, and prediction markets reached a $100 million revenue run rate within months ([Coinbase](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/coinbase-s-everything-exchange-agentic-finance-stablecoins-and-tokeniz/)).
- Inside Coinbase, each team keeps a markdown brain that agents learn from. Armstrong argues AI removes tasks rather than people. He also outlined his longevity company New Limit at a business level ([Coinbase](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/coinbase-s-everything-exchange-agentic-finance-stablecoins-and-tokeniz/)).

### Re-founding incumbents

- Lee argues a permanent holding company is the only structure that matches capital duration to the long investment AI transformation needs. Sequence is taking Baldwin private for $7.7 billion with the Dell family office ([Sequence](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/re-founding-incumbents-for-the-ai-era-with-sequence-holdings-co-founde/)).
- He says software vendors sell to workflows as they exist and services firms drift into incrementalism ([Sequence](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/re-founding-incumbents-for-the-ai-era-with-sequence-holdings-co-founde/)).
- At BankSouth, Lee says consumer underwriting time fell 94% and the average loan went from 30 days to 11, while loan volume doubled ([Sequence](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/re-founding-incumbents-for-the-ai-era-with-sequence-holdings-co-founde/)).
- He calls the human side of change management harder than the engineering ([Sequence](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/re-founding-incumbents-for-the-ai-era-with-sequence-holdings-co-founde/)).
