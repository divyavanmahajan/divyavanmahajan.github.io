## Overview

December on No Priors moved between the bottom and the top of the stack. Akash Systems argued that heat is the real constraint on AI data centres, while Bolt and Agency showed founders using models to let small teams and non-developers do far more. The year closed with a compilation of 2024 clips on compute, small models, company agents and the long tail of reliability.

## Main themes

### Coding models open software to non-developers

- Eric Simons says Bolt.new worked because Claude Sonnet 3.5 crossed a threshold for production-grade code; Sarah Guo describes it going from zero to about $20 million ARR in two months ([Bolt](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/bolt-s-eric-simons-on-enabling-everyone-to-generate-websites-with-ai/)).
- Simons credits four years of work on web containers that run dev environments in the browser, and says open-sourcing the system prompt is safe because the end-to-end product is the moat ([Bolt](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/bolt-s-eric-simons-on-enabling-everyone-to-generate-websites-with-ai/)).
- He reports almost 200,000 weekly builders, mostly non-technical, and expects code generation to keep improving quickly because code correctness is verifiable ([Bolt](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/bolt-s-eric-simons-on-enabling-everyone-to-generate-websites-with-ai/)).

### Heat as the limit on AI compute

- Felix Ejeckam argues that chip supply, power and reliability problems all trace back to temperature: “everything you mentioned is heat” ([Akash Systems](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/how-diamond-cooling-could-power-the-future-of-ai-with-akash-systems/)).
- Akash bonds lab-grown diamond to chips, a technique proven in satellites. It ships complete Nvidia servers that are both liquid and diamond cooled, with about 10 degrees of reduction now and 20 to 40 targeted ([Akash Systems](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/how-diamond-cooling-could-power-the-future-of-ai-with-akash-systems/)).
- Ty Mitchell points to Nvidia's heat-related delays, and the year-end episode recalls Nvidia tripling its stock price in 2024 ([Akash Systems](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/how-diamond-cooling-could-power-the-future-of-ai-with-akash-systems/), [Best of 2024](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/the-best-of-2024-with-sarah-guo-and-elad-gil/)).

### AI-native companies and customer relationships

- Elias Torres says Agency applies the lessons of his four earlier companies to looking after B2B customers personally at scale. His target: “it's a billion in Revenue with 100 people” ([Agency](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/revolutionizing-customer-success-with-agency-s-elias-torres/)).
- Torres argues that doing things that don't scale no longer applies with AI, and that change management and trust are harder than the LLM work ([Agency](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/revolutionizing-customer-success-with-agency-s-elias-torres/)).
- In a replayed clip, Bret Taylor says a branded AI agent will be what a website was in 1995, and that company agents are ready with current technology ([Best of 2024](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/the-best-of-2024-with-sarah-guo-and-elad-gil/)).

### Lessons from the year's guests

- Andrej Karpathy questions who controls a person's AI exocortex and argues that “the cognitive core can be extremely small”, perhaps around a billion parameters ([Best of 2024](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/the-best-of-2024-with-sarah-guo-and-elad-gil/)).
- The Sora team says video prediction taught the model 3D structure and everyday physics, and Dmitri Dolgov repeats that removing the driver depends on the long tail of reliability ([Best of 2024](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/the-best-of-2024-with-sarah-guo-and-elad-gil/)).
- Alexandr Wang compares the path to AGI to many separate problems, each with its own data flywheel and little transfer between modalities ([Best of 2024](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/12/the-best-of-2024-with-sarah-guo-and-elad-gil/)).
