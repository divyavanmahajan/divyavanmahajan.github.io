## Overview

November so far on No Priors has three founder conversations about AI outside software. Vlad Tenev covers ownership and agentic trading at Robinhood, Max Jaderberg makes the case for AI-first drug design at Isomorphic Labs, and Bob McGrew explains why Arda is automating factories.

## Main themes

### Ownership and agents in finance

- Vlad Tenev argues a society without broad ownership is “inherently very unstable. It's a fragile society.” He worries that most AI gains go to venture and institutional investors ([Robinhood](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/tokenization-agentic-trading-harmonic-robinhood-s-mission-to-expand-ow/)).
- Tenev says more than 150,000 customers opened agentic trading accounts within a few months, with tool calls at about 30 million a day. He describes Robinhood Agents as a Claude Code or Codex-like harness optimised for trading ([Robinhood](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/tokenization-agentic-trading-harmonic-robinhood-s-mission-to-expand-ow/)).
- Stock tokens on Robinhood Chain are his default route to US equities for people abroad. Robinhood Ventures funds give retail investors exposure to private companies ([Robinhood](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/tokenization-agentic-trading-harmonic-robinhood-s-mission-to-expand-ow/)).
- Tenev co-founded Harmonic to build mathematical superintelligence and objects that AI maths is too centralised in labs ([Robinhood](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/tokenization-agentic-trading-harmonic-robinhood-s-mission-to-expand-ow/)).

### AI-first drug discovery as a business

- Elad Gil notes drug costs rose to about $1.5 billion and timelines to 15 years while approvals stayed flat. Jaderberg says each drug is still a bespoke project with little learning carried over ([Isomorphic](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/powering-ai-driven-drug-discovery-with-president-of-isomorphic-labs-ma/)).
- Isomorphic's bet is general models accurate enough to replace many experiments, so that learnings compound across programmes. Jaderberg hopes to “get compounding improvements the more medicines you go after” ([Isomorphic](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/powering-ai-driven-drug-discovery-with-president-of-isomorphic-labs-ma/)).
- The company does not license its models. It uses them for an internal pipeline and pharma partnerships, and Jaderberg gives no date for a first clinical trial ([Isomorphic](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/powering-ai-driven-drug-discovery-with-president-of-isomorphic-labs-ma/)).

### Superintelligence meets the factory floor

- Bob McGrew says OpenAI was limited by GPUs in 2024, not researchers. Automating research would be a one-time boost, not a runaway loop ([Arda](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/superintelligence-needs-the-physical-world-with-ceo-and-co-founder-of/)).
- He argues today's automation is bespoke, so fast-changing products such as iPhones are assembled by hand. Arda aims to redesign and reconfigure an automated line in seven days ([Arda](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/superintelligence-needs-the-physical-world-with-ceo-and-co-founder-of/)).
- Arda is deployed across all of Anduril's product divisions. One engineer cut a virtual build from a few days to 90 minutes ([Arda](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/superintelligence-needs-the-physical-world-with-ceo-and-co-founder-of/)).
- McGrew says “The frontier model is not adding all the value”; models orchestrate motion-planning and CAD tools ([Arda](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/superintelligence-needs-the-physical-world-with-ceo-and-co-founder-of/)).

### Forward deployed engineering

- McGrew credits Palantir's Shyam Sankar for the model and calls it a product strategy, not consulting: if FDEs keep filling the gap, there is no leverage ([Arda](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/superintelligence-needs-the-physical-world-with-ceo-and-co-founder-of/)).
- He adds that enterprise sales need both a visionary sponsor and a power user ([Arda](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/superintelligence-needs-the-physical-world-with-ceo-and-co-founder-of/)).
