## Overview

April on No Priors moved beyond text. Figure argued for general-purpose humanoid robots, OpenAI's Sora team explained their video model, and Playground AI made the case that image generation still has a long way to go. OpenAI ran through three of the four episodes, and the hosts' own episode looked at how hyperscalers fund and profit from the model race.

## Main themes

### Humanoid robots enter the factory

- Brett Adcock argues one general-purpose humanoid beats thousands of special-purpose machines, because “the whole world was built for humans” ([Figure](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/the-argument-for-humanoid-ai-robots-with-brett-adcock-from-figure/)).
- Figure is 21 months old and has raised $675 million from Microsoft, OpenAI, Nvidia, Intel and Jeff Bezos. BMW is its first commercial customer, starting at Spartanburg with five use cases chosen ([Figure](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/the-argument-for-humanoid-ai-robots-with-brett-adcock-from-figure/)).
- OpenAI supplies the vision-language model for high-level reasoning while Figure's own neural nets drive low-level control. Adcock plans to earn trust in factories before moving into homes ([Figure](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/the-argument-for-humanoid-ai-robots-with-brett-adcock-from-figure/)).

### Video and image generation

- Sora's co-leads Aditya Ramesh, Tim Brooks and Bill Peebles combine diffusion from the DALL-E models with the scalable transformer from GPT, and say results improve steadily with compute ([Sora](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/openai-s-sora-team-thinks-we-ve-only-seen-the-gpt-1-of-video-models/)).
- Peebles explains spacetime patches, which let Sora handle any resolution, duration or aspect ratio. The team says Sora learned 3D structure without being trained on it explicitly, and names election misinformation as a central safety concern ([Sora](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/openai-s-sora-team-thinks-we-ve-only-seen-the-gpt-1-of-video-models/)).
- Suhail Doshi says today's image models produce “text to Art” and that generation feels like a loot box. He wants Playground to focus on editing real images and, later, a large vision model ([Playground](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/the-future-of-ai-artistry-with-suhail-doshi-from-playground-ai/)).
- Doshi agrees Transformers are the right direction but doubts DiT is the right architecture. He calls most industry evals flawed and credits curated data most ([Playground](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/the-future-of-ai-artistry-with-suhail-doshi-from-playground-ai/), [Sora](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/openai-s-sora-team-thinks-we-ve-only-seen-the-gpt-1-of-video-models/)).

### Hyperscalers and the model market

- Sarah Guo and Elad Gil expect several GPT-4-level models by the end of 2024, some open source, citing Mistral and Databricks' DBRX ([Hyperscaler strategy](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/hyperscaler-strategy-in-ai-the-application-landscape-heats-up-and-what/)).
- Gil says most foundation model funding comes from hyperscalers and big tech, and that much of the value may flow to the clouds hosting the models. He cites roughly 5% Azure growth from AI ([Hyperscaler strategy](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/hyperscaler-strategy-in-ai-the-application-landscape-heats-up-and-what/)).
- Guo reads the Microsoft and Inflection deal as Microsoft wanting its own AI leadership and partly as a hedge on OpenAI ([Hyperscaler strategy](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/hyperscaler-strategy-in-ai-the-application-landscape-heats-up-and-what/)).

### Agents, white space and prosumer apps

- Gil likens today's agents to a junior intern, eager but needing direction, and sees Devin's interface as a new UI where humans watch and redirect ([Hyperscaler strategy](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/hyperscaler-strategy-in-ai-the-application-landscape-heats-up-and-what/)).
- He says startups pile into the same ideas while obvious areas sit empty. Guo names time series, finance data and robotics as under-served, and argues the first application wave is prosumer ([Hyperscaler strategy](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/04/hyperscaler-strategy-in-ai-the-application-landscape-heats-up-and-what/)).
