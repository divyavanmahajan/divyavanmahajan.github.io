## Overview

No Priors spent 2026 talking mostly to CEOs about what AI does to their businesses: chips, inference clouds, enterprise software, travel, delivery, finance and security. Three research-heavy conversations (Andrej Karpathy, Noam Brown, Bob McGrew) framed how fast AI itself will improve. Talk of self-improving AI and agentic commerce grew in the second half, while jobs and the SaaS sell-off faded.

## Main themes

### Open models and how fast AI improves

- Jensen Huang defends open source, saying “Without open source today, all of that AI work would be suffocated.” He also says no country contributes more to open source than China ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/01/nvidia-s-jensen-huang-on-reasoning-models-robotics-and-refuting-the-ai/)).
- Misha Laskin expects open-weight models to take most token demand as enterprises move from renting to owning intelligence. George Kurtz notes attackers use the same models ([Beam](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/10/beam-the-great-american-open-model-with-reflectionai-co-founder-and-ce/), [CrowdStrike](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/10/the-new-apex-predator-crowdstrike-ceo-george-kurtz-on-adversaries-arme/)).
- Noam Brown doubts an overnight intelligence explosion because models lean on test-time compute; for now AI is “more about transforming what researchers do rather than fully replacing the researchers” ([Noam Brown](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/06/really-big-test-time-compute-in-ai-changes-benchmarks-safety-and-resea/)).
- Bob McGrew calls automated AI research a one-time boost limited by compute, labs and factories ([Arda](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/superintelligence-needs-the-physical-world-with-ceo-and-co-founder-of/)).

### Enterprises, SaaS and jobs

- Elad Gil calls the SaaS sell-off “a massive market correction around something that in the long run has a lot of truth to it” ([SaaS to AI-First](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/from-saas-to-ai-first-how-companies-are-reshaping-innovation/)).
- Bill McDermott expects agents to cut new hiring sharply; CJ Desai says large companies still move slowly ([ServiceNow](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/04/scaling-global-organizations-in-the-age-of-ai-with-servicenow-chairman/), [MongoDB](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/01/no-priors-live-building-durable-software-in-the-ai-age-with-mongodb-pr/)).
- Alexander Taubman and Michael Lee make the case for owning services firms and incumbents and rebuilding them with AI ([Amex GBT](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/05/amex-global-business-travel-the-world-s-first-ai-take-private-with-lon/), [Sequence](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/re-founding-incumbents-for-the-ai-era-with-sequence-holdings-co-founde/)).
- Simon Last and Karpathy describe engineers turning into managers of coding agents ([Notion](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/03/from-coder-to-manager-navigating-the-shift-to-agentic-engineering-with/), [Karpathy](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/03/andrej-karpathy-on-code-agents-autoresearch-and-the-loopy-era-of-ai/)).

### Chips, inference and power

- Huang points to a global compute shortage and says Nvidia's AV business nears $10 billion ([Jensen Huang](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/01/nvidia-s-jensen-huang-on-reasoning-models-robotics-and-refuting-the-ai/)).
- Neil Tiwari says the bottleneck moved from chips to people, power and infrastructure; Isaiah Taylor pitches cheap nuclear ([Magnetar](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/02/how-capital-is-powering-the-ai-infrastructure-buildout-with-magnetar-c/), [Valar Atomics](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/07/how-nuclear-will-unlock-energy-abundance-with-valar-atomics-founder-is/)).
- Inference got its own run: Baseten, Cerebras, Inception's diffusion models and Fractile's memory-bandwidth chips ([Baseten](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/05/baseten-ceo-tuhin-srivastava-on-the-ai-inference-crunch-custom-models/), [Cerebras](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/05/the-story-behind-cerebras-63-billion-ipo-with-founder-and-ceo-andrew-f/), [Inception](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/why-diffusion-will-win-ai-inference-with-inception-co-founder-and-ceo/), [Fractile](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/10/frontier-chips-for-frontier-ai-labs-with-walter-goodwin-founder-ceo-of/)).

### Security and the agentic web

- Maxim Bar Kogan argues human-in-the-loop oversight will not scale as agent actions multiply ([Onyx](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/05/building-an-ai-guardian-for-enterprise-with-onyx-security-ceo-maxim-ba/)).
- Greg Brockman, Kurtz and Dane Knecht all describe cheap AI vulnerability discovery and a short window for defenders ([Brockman](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/10/the-changing-physics-of-cybersecurity-and-drug-development-with-openai/), [CrowdStrike](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/10/the-new-apex-predator-crowdstrike-ceo-george-kurtz-on-adversaries-arme/), [Cloudflare](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/10/democratizing-security-for-the-agentic-web-with-cloudflare-cto-dane-kn/)).
- Andy Fang says every company must design an agent-first experience; Glenn Fogel thinks travel is harder to disrupt than outsiders assume ([DoorDash](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/07/building-an-autonomous-delivery-experience-with-doordash-co-founders-a/), [Booking](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/07/travel-through-the-lens-of-ai-with-with-booking-com-ceo-glenn-fogel/)).

### Money for agents

- Jeremy Allaire and Brian Armstrong want agents to transact on stablecoin and crypto rails ([Circle](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/04/the-agentic-economy-how-ai-agents-will-transform-the-financial-system/), [Coinbase](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/09/coinbase-s-everything-exchange-agentic-finance-stablecoins-and-tokeniz/)).
- Vlad Tenev expects stock tokens to become the default route to US equities in weaker capital markets ([Robinhood](/podcasts/no-priors/2026/11/tokenization-agentic-trading-harmonic-robinhood-s-mission-to-expand-ow/)).
