## Overview

August on No Priors split into three pairs. AWS's Matt Garman and Foundry's Jared Quincy Davis covered the cloud and compute underneath AI, Brex and Ramp showed how finance companies put AI into their products, and Google DeepMind's Oriol Vinyals and Magic's Eric Steinberger talked about reasoning, long context and AGI.

## Main themes

### Cloud and compute economics

- Matt Garman says about 85% of workloads still run on premises, so cloud migration is early. AWS won by offering familiar building blocks and spent a decade working through banks' compliance lists ([AWS](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/cloud-strategy-in-the-ai-era-with-matt-garman-ceo-of-aws/)).
- Garman bet on many models rather than one, delivered through Bedrock, where “you never interact with the GPU”. He expects models to become a smaller part of the picture next to knowledge bases, guardrails and agents ([AWS](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/cloud-strategy-in-the-ai-era-with-matt-garman-ceo-of-aws/)).
- Jared Quincy Davis argues AI cloud today is closer to colocation, with customers pushed into reservations of often three years. He puts H100 utilisation in many cases at 20-25% or lower ([Foundry](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/the-marketplace-for-ai-compute-with-jared-quincy-davis-from-foundry/)).
- Davis expects compound AI systems, many model calls plus verifiers, to reduce dependence on giant clusters. Both he and Garman expect compute to stay constrained ([Foundry](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/the-marketplace-for-ai-compute-with-jared-quincy-davis-from-foundry/), [AWS](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/cloud-strategy-in-the-ai-era-with-matt-garman-ceo-of-aws/)).

### AI in fintech products

- Pedro Franceschi says Brex Assistant serves about 30,000 customers and completes over a third of their expenses automatically. Brex shows suggestions according to model confidence, and in-workflow accounting suggestions reached an 80% acceptance rate ([Brex](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/innovating-spend-management-through-ai-with-pedro-franceschi-from-brex/)).
- Franceschi argues LLMs make account-based marketing cheap enough for any account. He says writing the email is easy and aggregating the data is the hard part ([Brex](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/innovating-spend-management-through-ai-with-pedro-franceschi-from-brex/)).
- Ramp's Karim Atiyeh says AI in finance is low-risk when tasks are tightly constrained. Ramp dropped fine-tuning for evals and moved about 90% of tasks to GPT-4o mini within a day ([Ramp](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/how-ai-can-help-build-smarter-systems-for-every-team-with-eric-glyman/)).
- Eric Glyman frames AI's best current use as productivity, and Atiyeh wants AI review of outgoing marketing copy, like a test suite. He says good taste stays human ([Ramp](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/how-ai-can-help-build-smarter-systems-for-every-team-with-eric-glyman/)).

### Reasoning, long context and AGI

- Oriol Vinyals says general models exist but reliable reasoning does not yet. Pre-training takes over 90% of compute and could fall towards 50%, with more going to RL once good reward models exist ([DeepMind](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/google-deepmind-s-vision-for-ai-search-and-gemini-with-oriol-vinyals-f/)).
- Vinyals expects context windows to grow about 10x in one to two years, with retrieval staying in a hybrid setup. Eric Steinberger goes further, arguing that a model seeing all the data beats retrieval ([DeepMind](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/google-deepmind-s-vision-for-ai-search-and-gemini-with-oriol-vinyals-f/), [Magic](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/building-toward-a-bright-post-agi-future-with-eric-steinberger-from-ma/)).
- Steinberger sees a coding model as the shortest path to AGI. He says people will use such a system “for 90% of your tasks are zero”, and predicts wrapper companies get swallowed by model companies ([Magic](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/building-toward-a-bright-post-agi-future-with-eric-steinberger-from-ma/)).

### AI and jobs

- Glyman cites a fall of about a million bookkeepers alongside a rise in accountants and advisers as evidence that jobs shift rather than vanish ([Ramp](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/how-ai-can-help-build-smarter-systems-for-every-team-with-eric-glyman/)).
- Steinberger expects all computer work to be automated after AGI, with handmade goods and games growing, and a bimodal outcome overall ([Magic](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/08/building-toward-a-bright-post-agi-future-with-eric-steinberger-from-ma/)).
