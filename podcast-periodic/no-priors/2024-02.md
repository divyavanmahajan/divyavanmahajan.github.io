## Overview

February on No Priors was split between the application layer and the hardware underneath it. Two episodes, with Notion and Pinecone, made the case for retrieval-augmented generation as the way to give models useful knowledge. Stripe explained how a company that is not primarily an AI company adopts LLMs, and AMD's CTO argued that the AI chip market needs real competition.

## Main themes

### Retrieval as the knowledge layer

- Ivan Zhao calls retrieval over embeddings an underrated category. He argues RAG helps adoption because it keeps “the existing Behavior but better output” ([Notion](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/rag-is-the-key-for-smarter-productivity-tools-with-notion-ceo-ivan-zha/)).
- Edo Liberty says Pinecone augmented GPT-3.5, GPT-4, Llama, Mixtral and Anthropic models with Common Crawl. Hallucinations fell by up to 50% and the models converged to similar accuracy ([Pinecone](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/improving-search-with-rag-architecture-with-pinecone-ceo-edo-liberty/)).
- Liberty argues that scale and cost decide whether retrieval works, which is why Pinecone rebuilt itself as a serverless service tested at tens of billions of vectors. He sees RAG as cheaper and easier to govern than long-context stuffing or fine-tuning ([Pinecone](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/improving-search-with-rag-architecture-with-pinecone-ceo-edo-liberty/)).
- Notion Q&A runs on Pinecone, linking the two episodes ([Notion](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/rag-is-the-key-for-smarter-productivity-tools-with-notion-ceo-ivan-zha/), [Pinecone](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/improving-search-with-rag-architecture-with-pinecone-ceo-edo-liberty/)).

### Notion, organisation and bundling

- Zhao says five-plus years of building primitives such as a text editor and a relational database let Notion ship AI features quickly, with AI acting like a new engine ([Notion](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/rag-is-the-key-for-smarter-productivity-tools-with-notion-ceo-ivan-zha/)).
- He suggests semantic retrieval may remove the need to organise knowledge, so the organising sidebar could become unnecessary: “I think we might be moving past the need for organization” ([Notion](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/rag-is-the-key-for-smarter-productivity-tools-with-notion-ceo-ivan-zha/)).
- Zhao argues language models want data in one place, which favours bundled tools over fragmented SaaS and saves enterprise customers money ([Notion](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/rag-is-the-key-for-smarter-productivity-tools-with-notion-ceo-ivan-zha/)).

### AI inside a non-AI company

- Emily Glassberg Sands says three engineers built Stripe's internal LLM Explorer in three weeks, and a third of roughly 7,000 employees were using it within days ([Stripe](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/build-ai-products-at-on-ai-companies-with-emily-glassberg-sands-from-s/)).
- Small funded accelerator teams turn the best ideas into products such as Radar Assistant and Sigma Assistant, which turn plain English into fraud rules and SQL ([Stripe](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/build-ai-products-at-on-ai-companies-with-emily-glassberg-sands-from-s/)).
- She says AI startups differ from earlier waves: high compute costs from the start, global demand, subscription models and lean teams. Over half of Forbes' top 50 AI companies use Stripe ([Stripe](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/build-ai-products-at-on-ai-companies-with-emily-glassberg-sands-from-s/)).

### Chip competition and holistic design

- Mark Papermaster argues a lack of competition hurt the chip market, and that AMD can now compete through chiplets, open software such as ROCm, and the MI300 launched in December 2023 with commitments from Meta and Microsoft ([AMD](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/competition-makes-for-better-chip-design-with-amd-cto-mark-papermaster/)).
- He claims the MI300 is competitive on training and leads in inference. With Moore's Law slowing, he says gains now come from matching engines to workloads, packaging and software ([AMD](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/competition-makes-for-better-chip-design-with-amd-cto-mark-papermaster/)).
- Papermaster names advanced packaging, not only wafer fabs, as the current supply bottleneck and power as the lasting one. He expects inference to spread to the edge and AI PCs ([AMD](/podcasts/no-priors/2024/02/competition-makes-for-better-chip-design-with-amd-cto-mark-papermaster/)).
