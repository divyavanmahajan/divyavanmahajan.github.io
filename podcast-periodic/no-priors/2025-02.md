## Overview

February opened with Sarah Guo and Elad Gil's 100th episode, which put DeepSeek's cheap model in context and weighed OpenAI's Deep Research. The rest of the month covered applied AI businesses: Harvey in legal services, Kyle Vogt's move from robotaxis to home robots, and two conversations about AI in scientific discovery.

## Main themes

### DeepSeek, reasoning models and falling costs

- The hosts say DeepSeek drew attention as a state-of-the-art open Chinese model with a claimed cheap training run. Gil notes that everybody thinks “they were spending hundreds of millions of dollars on compute” beforehand ([DeepSeek and Deep Research](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/deepseek-deep-research-and-2025-predictions-with-sarah-and-elad/)).
- Guo points out that the V3 base model arrived in December without moving Nvidia's stock; the R1 reasoning model was what broke the narrative. Gil calls the roughly 20% Nvidia drop unwarranted ([DeepSeek and Deep Research](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/deepseek-deep-research-and-2025-predictions-with-sarah-and-elad/)).
- They cite a 180x fall in cost per token for GPT-4-level models over 18 months and see frontier models converging, while big clusters such as Stargate still matter ([DeepSeek and Deep Research](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/deepseek-deep-research-and-2025-predictions-with-sarah-and-elad/)).

### Agents for knowledge work

- Guo says Deep Research “immediately raises the bar for a number of different types of knowledge work”, but in fields she knows well she distrusts its sense of which sources are authoritative ([DeepSeek and Deep Research](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/deepseek-deep-research-and-2025-predictions-with-sarah-and-elad/)).
- For 2025 Gil expects vertical apps and early agents; Guo expects agents in security, support and SRE, plus capable small local models ([DeepSeek and Deep Research](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/deepseek-deep-research-and-2025-predictions-with-sarah-and-elad/)).

### Vertical AI in legal services

- Winston Weinberg calls Harvey the AI platform for legal and professional services. Guo cites more than 250 clients and over $50M in ARR ([Harvey](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/how-harvey-ai-is-changing-the-legal-industry-with-winston-weinberg/)).
- His strategy is to expand and collapse: build 30 to 50 reusable AI patterns and specialised workflows, then fold them into a simple interface, because the UI for professional services “is just email” ([Harvey](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/how-harvey-ai-is-changing-the-legal-industry-with-winston-weinberg/)).
- Weinberg finds public benchmarks useless for Harvey and relies on senior lawyers for evaluation. He expects automated tasks to move to fixed fees while senior advice stays on the billable hour ([Harvey](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/how-harvey-ai-is-changing-the-legal-industry-with-winston-weinberg/)).

### From robotaxis to home robots

- Kyle Vogt recalls Cruise starting as a retrofit kit for regular cars, then moving to robotaxis once Uber and Lyft's driver costs created demand. GM bought it for about a billion dollars and later abandoned it ([Kyle Vogt](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/building-hard-tech-in-hard-markets-kyle-vogt-on-cruise-twitch-and-the/)).
- He argues learned models now make messy homes tractable, and that a home robot, unlike a robotaxi, can ship before it is perfect. He will keep The Bot Company small and never sell a company again ([Kyle Vogt](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/building-hard-tech-in-hard-markets-kyle-vogt-on-cruise-twitch-and-the/)).

### Platforms and data in AI for science

- Noubar Afeyan argues breakthroughs are emergent, not designed, and that Flagship's job is to underwrite uncertainty others avoid. Every Flagship company is a platform; he expects a shake-out of single-asset biotechs ([Noubar Afeyan](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/building-the-platform-for-scientific-breakthrough-with-noubar-afeyan-o/)).
- Vevo Therapeutics and the Arc Institute argue that models of whole cells are held back by data. They present the open Tahoe-100M dataset and Arc's Virtual Cell Atlas as a shared starting point, and favour small teams and open sourcing ([Virtual Cell Models](/podcasts/no-priors/2025/02/virtual-cell-models-tahoe-100-and-data-for-ai-in-bio-with-vevo-therape/)).
