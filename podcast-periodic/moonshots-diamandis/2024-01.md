## Overview

January opened with a New Year X Spaces conversation between Peter Diamandis and Elon Musk on abundance, the media and AI. Longevity carried over from December through that talk and an episode with Mark Hyman, while the second half of the month turned to company building: hiring at Tesla and Archer's plan for electric air taxis.

## Main themes

### Abundance against a negative news cycle

- Musk agrees that “the most likely outcome is one of abundance of goods and services”, but warns against complacency and entitlement ([Musk on Abundance](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/elon-musk-on-abundance-agi-and-the-media-in-2024-ep-79-x-spaces/)).
- Both say people evolved to react more to danger than to reward, and that news media exploit this. Musk says he reads no newspapers, and Diamandis cites extreme poverty falling from 90% in the 1800s to under 10% ([Musk on Abundance](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/elon-musk-on-abundance-agi-and-the-media-in-2024-ep-79-x-spaces/)).
- Musk says Tesla's analysis shows solar plus batteries could power all of Earth's industry, heating and transport, and he names a falling birth rate as a bigger danger than overpopulation ([Musk on Abundance](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/elon-musk-on-abundance-agi-and-the-media-in-2024-ep-79-x-spaces/)).

### AI scale and safety

- Musk estimates the compute dedicated to AI is growing about tenfold every six months ([Musk on Abundance](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/elon-musk-on-abundance-agi-and-the-media-in-2024-ep-79-x-spaces/)).
- He argues AI should be maximally truth-seeking and curious, and warns that programming an explicit morality risks the Waluigi problem. He adds that “Earth is much more interesting to an advanced AI with humans on it than without humans” ([Musk on Abundance](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/elon-musk-on-abundance-agi-and-the-media-in-2024-ep-79-x-spaces/)).

### How far, and how fast, longevity can go

- Diamandis wants a 120-150 year healthspan. Musk worries that very long lives could ossify society, though he thinks ageing may not be that hard to solve ([Musk on Abundance](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/elon-musk-on-abundance-agi-and-the-media-in-2024-ep-79-x-spaces/)).
- Mark Hyman thinks existing lifestyle measures could take most people into their 90s, while longer gains depend on regenerative therapies that are not yet proven in humans ([Mark Hyman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/the-proven-longevity-practices-extending-lifespans-w-dr-mark-hyman-ep/)).
- Hyman says private capital, cheaper tools and AI are speeding up the field. Diamandis says his fund Bold Capital is now about two-thirds biotech and health tech, and Hyman describes his diagnostics company Function Health ([Mark Hyman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/the-proven-longevity-practices-extending-lifespans-w-dr-mark-hyman-ep/)).
- Hyman blames ultra-processed food for much of the rise in chronic disease, links it to 1970s farm subsidies, and calls for more clinical-trial funding ([Mark Hyman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/the-proven-longevity-practices-extending-lifespans-w-dr-mark-hyman-ep/)).

### Building companies: people and hardware

- Gaby Toledano, former Tesla Chief People Officer, says founders hire a senior head of people far too late. She says culture starts with founder storytelling and lore, such as Musk sleeping on the factory floor ([Tesla Hiring](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/unlocking-teslas-hiring-secrets-interview-with-ex-chief-people-officer/)).
- At 40,000 to 50,000 staff she met Musk weekly about senior candidates. Her advice: behavioural questions, 30-minute interviews, and offers made by the CEO, as she puts it, “you don't let recruiting make the offer” ([Tesla Hiring](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/unlocking-teslas-hiring-secrets-interview-with-ex-chief-people-officer/)).
- Adam Goldstein argues the hard part of hardware is building a business around it. Archer designed its four-passenger Midnight aircraft around a business model and partnered with Stellantis for manufacturing ([Archer](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/tech-ceo-on-the-future-of-travel-technology-w-adam-goldstein-ep-82/)).
- Goldstein expects launch prices near Uber Black, falling below car ownership within five to seven years, and targets operations in 2025, starting with New York ([Archer](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/01/tech-ceo-on-the-future-of-travel-technology-w-adam-goldstein-ep-82/)).
