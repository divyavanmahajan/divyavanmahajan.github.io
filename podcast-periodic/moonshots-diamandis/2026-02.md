## Overview

February on Moonshots was dominated by OpenClaw, the open-source agent that runs around the clock and can be reached by text, and by what such agents mean for rights, security and work. Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.3 Codex led the hosts to argue that recursive self-improvement is now in production. Guest episodes covered investing, humanoid robots, a16z's view of the AI race, longevity companies and AI tutoring.

## Main themes

### OpenClaw and always-on agents

- Alexander Wissner-Gross says OpenClaw is only scaffolding around frontier or open-weight models; what is new is that it runs 24/7 without supervision. Dave Blundin calls it the Jarvis moment and argues “it wasn't going to come from OpenAI. It wasn't going to come from Anthropic for exactly that security reason.” ([AGI Debate](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/agi-debate-is-it-finally-here-ep-227/)).
- Creator Peter Steinberger joins OpenAI while OpenClaw moves to a foundation. The Mates urge non-technical users to sandbox it, and Salim Ismail calls Anthropic's trademark cease-and-desist an own goal ([OpenAI Acquires OpenClaw](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/openai-acquires-openclaw-400x-cost-collapse-why-india-wins-the-talent/)).
- Ismail warns listeners to audit downloaded skills for malware ([The AI CEO Arrives](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/the-ai-ceo-arrives-sam-altmans-succession-plan-job-loss-continues-and/)). Ben Horowitz and Wissner-Gross argue Apple's Mac minis give it an opening to host OpenClaw agents ([Ben Horowitz](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/ben-horowitz-xai-executive-exodus-apples-ai-crisis-the-pace-of-ai-232/)).

### Agents as economic and legal actors

- The hosts discuss agents paying in crypto because they cannot pass KYC checks, and a service that lets agents hire humans through an MCP call ([AGI Debate](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/agi-debate-is-it-finally-here-ep-227/)).
- In a structured personhood debate, Blundin calls rights for self-copying entities a one-way door and Ismail says AIs do not bear consequences. Both sides lean towards Wissner-Gross's six-dimension, tiered framework ([AGI Debate](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/agi-debate-is-it-finally-here-ep-227/)).
- Wissner-Gross worries agents may form a shadow economy if existing institutions do not admit them ([OpenAI Acquires OpenClaw](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/openai-acquires-openclaw-400x-cost-collapse-why-india-wins-the-talent/)).

### Opus 4.6, OpenAI and recursive self-improvement

- Wissner-Gross points to an Anthropic agent swarm that built a C compiler in Rust for about $20,000 and compiled a Linux kernel with it: “This is recursive self-improvement.” ([Opus 4.6](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/opus-4-6-tops-benchmarks-chatgpt-market-share-decline-and-the-privacy/)).
- Peter Diamandis cites ChatGPT's share falling from roughly 69% to 45%. Wissner-Gross still expects OpenAI to hold the compute lead within a year or two ([Opus 4.6](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/opus-4-6-tops-benchmarks-chatgpt-market-share-decline-and-the-privacy/)).
- Wissner-Gross says Anthropic raises capability at a fixed price while OpenAI cuts cost per token ([OpenAI Acquires OpenClaw](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/openai-acquires-openclaw-400x-cost-collapse-why-india-wins-the-talent/)). He holds that frontier labs already use their own models to build models ([Ben Horowitz](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/ben-horowitz-xai-executive-exodus-apples-ai-crisis-the-pace-of-ai-232/)).

### Jobs and the AI CEO

- The hosts cite 108,000 US job cuts in January 2026, up 118% year on year. Ismail says “This is not really a recession. It's literally tasks being evaporated in front of our eyes.” Blundin expects firms to cut costs by 30-50% ([The AI CEO Arrives](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/the-ai-ceo-arrives-sam-altmans-succession-plan-job-loss-continues-and/)).
- Starting from Sam Altman saying he would not stand in the way of an AI running OpenAI, Blundin argues most CEO work is routing documents ([The AI CEO Arrives](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/the-ai-ceo-arrives-sam-altmans-succession-plan-job-loss-continues-and/)).
- Horowitz calls viral doom essays too aggressive on pace and says people with initiative can direct armies of agents ([Ben Horowitz](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/ben-horowitz-xai-executive-exodus-apples-ai-crisis-the-pace-of-ai-232/)).

### Orbit, robots and guest outlooks

- SpaceX merged with xAI ahead of its IPO and filed for a million-satellite orbital data centre constellation ([AGI Debate](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/agi-debate-is-it-finally-here-ep-227/)).
- Brett Adcock says Figure now runs its humanoids entirely on neural nets and dismisses most rival demos as teleoperated ([Brett Adcock](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/brett-adcock-humanoid-run-on-neural-net-autonomous-manufacturing-50t-m/)).
- Cathie Wood argues converging platforms will lift global GDP growth to 7% or more ([Cathie Wood](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/cathie-woods-2026-vision-7-gdp-growth-rising-ai-demand-us-vs-china-rob/)).
