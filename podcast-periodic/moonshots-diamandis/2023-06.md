## Overview

June on Moonshots was mostly about what AI does to people: their jobs, their sense of purpose and the values machines learn from them. Mo Gawdat, Rana el Kaliouby, Brian Keating and Emad Mostaque each took a different angle on risk and benefit. Palmer Luckey and Salim Ismail covered hardware, defense and how organizations should experiment.

## Main themes

### AI, jobs and purpose

- Mo Gawdat predicts “a disaster to jobs” and to the meaning and pay that come with them, with serious disruption to truth and democracy within two to three years ([Mo Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/ai-experts-urgent-wake-up-call-unveiling-the-silent-threat-w-mo-gawdat/)).
- Gawdat calls for FDA-like testing of public AI products and for governments to prepare for universal basic income. Peter Diamandis cites Andrew Yang and UBI trials in support ([Mo Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/ai-experts-urgent-wake-up-call-unveiling-the-silent-threat-w-mo-gawdat/)).
- Emad Mostaque claims 41% of GitHub code is AI-generated and predicts no programmers in five years. He describes a Big Four firm spending $300 million to ask whether it needs as many auditors ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/why-ai-matters-w-emad-mostaque-ep-52/)).
- Diamandis ranks job loss first among AI risks, ahead of misuse by bad actors and an immature early AGI ([Brian Keating](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/the-realistic-future-of-ai-w-brian-keating/)).

### Teaching AI human values

- Gawdat argues AI's character comes from its training data, not its code, so human behaviour online is the real alignment lever. His moonshot is to tilt the singularity “in favor of having humans best interest in mind” ([Mo Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/ai-experts-urgent-wake-up-call-unveiling-the-silent-threat-w-mo-gawdat/)).
- Rana el Kaliouby says AI is obsessed with IQ and lacks emotional intelligence. Affectiva's models moved from three facial expressions to more than 50 emotional, cognitive and behavioural states ([Rana el Kaliouby](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/ais-moral-dilemma-are-we-building-our-own-nightmare-w-dr-rana-el-kalio/)).
- El Kaliouby splits AI ethics into development (bias in training data) and deployment (consent). She tied executive bonuses to ethics work and turned down surveillance-linked funding in 2011 ([Rana el Kaliouby](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/ais-moral-dilemma-are-we-building-our-own-nightmare-w-dr-rana-el-kalio/)).
- Brian Keating wants AI risk estimated with error bars. Diamandis calls a Terminator scenario highly improbable but expects voice-cloning deepfakes in the 2024 election ([Brian Keating](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/the-realistic-future-of-ai-w-brian-keating/)).

### Open models and AI tutors

- Mostaque argues for small, open, owned models across sectors and nations, since firms will not send internal data to ChatGPT. He expects ChatGPT-class models on phones without internet ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/why-ai-matters-w-emad-mostaque-ep-52/)).
- He sees one-to-one AI tuition as the biggest lever in education and plans an AI for every child in Malawi through a World Bank-backed outcome bond ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/why-ai-matters-w-emad-mostaque-ep-52/)).
- Keating proposes a free round-the-clock university as his XPRIZE; both he and Diamandis see education as a big disruption this decade ([Brian Keating](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/the-realistic-future-of-ai-w-brian-keating/)).

### Headsets, defense and experimentation

- Palmer Luckey says Apple got Vision Pro right by aiming high first. He argues cost-plus contracts reward failure, with 80% of procurement going to five companies ([Palmer Luckey](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/oculus-founder-on-apple-vision-pro-w-palmer-luckey-ep-50/)).
- Luckey describes Anduril as a software company wrapped in hardware that funds products itself and sells them finished ([Palmer Luckey](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/oculus-founder-on-apple-vision-pro-w-palmer-luckey-ep-50/)).
- Salim Ismail says the most ExO-like Fortune 100 companies delivered 40 times better shareholder returns. He and Diamandis push cheap, constant experiments over gut feel ([Salim Ismail](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/06/the-successful-entrepreneur-formula-w-salim-ismail-ep-47/)).
