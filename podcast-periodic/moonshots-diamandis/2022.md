## Overview

Moonshots launched in October 2022 with five episodes on one day, opening with Elon Musk and the carbon removal XPRIZE. The first three months mixed exponential technology with founder advice. Longevity and its regulation was the most central thread, alongside AI timelines, space and Bitcoin. The year closed with Emad Mostaque on generative AI and two listener AMAs with Peter Diamandis.

## Main themes

### AI timelines and the generative AI opening

- Eric Schmidt says “I predict right now April 2042 is the arrival of AGI”, and expects only five to ten such systems, run by nation states ([Eric Schmidt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/10/ep-7-eric-schmidt-ex-google-ceo-how-to-run-a-tech-giant/)).
- In his AMA, Schmidt argues decision speed decides tech outcomes, citing Google buying YouTube after it blew past Google Video ([Schmidt AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/10/ep-8-eric-schmidts-ama-ai-national-security-and-cyborgs/)).
- Emad Mostaque calls open-source generative AI public infrastructure and tells 20-year-olds to “drop everything. You should focus entirely on this.” ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-16-ai-is-creating-massive-entrepreneurial-opportunity-w-emad-mostaq/)).
- Peter Diamandis predicts every profession will get an AI co-pilot ([Ask Peter Anything Part 1](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-19-ask-peter-anything-part-1-our-future-in-space-successful-mindset/)).

### Longevity, regulation and the FDA

- Balaji Srinivasan argues the FDA and the US regulatory state hold back biomedical innovation, and that network states are a way around them ([Balaji Srinivasan](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/10/ep-6-abuses-of-the-american-regulatory-state-w-balaji-srinivasan/)).
- David Sinclair and Diamandis say drug-development cost and regulation are the main brakes on longevity therapies, and preview a planned $101 million age-reversal XPRIZE ([Sinclair AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-17-david-sinclairs-ama-age-reversal-breakthroughs-fda-approval-and/), [Why Aging is a Disease](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-18-why-aging-is-a-disease-w-david-sinclair/)).
- Tony Robbins argues people care more about quality of life than its length ([Tony Robbins](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/10/ep-4-how-to-create-a-successful-life-w-tony-robbins/)); Tim Urban and Steve Aoki discuss cryonics ([Tim Urban](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/10/ep-3-are-humans-headed-toward-a-utopian-or-dystopian-future-w-tim-urba/), [Steve Aoki AMA](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-14-steve-aokis-ama-a-djs-crazy-schedule-cryonics-to-live-forever-an/)).

### Space and XPRIZE moonshots

- Musk and Diamandis launch the $100 million carbon removal XPRIZE, asking for affordable methods proven at real scale ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/10/ep-1-how-to-save-the-world-w-elon-musk/)).
- Diamandis tells how he flew Stephen Hawking into weightlessness and says “The real element of opening up space is inspiring kids to go into math and science.” ([Stephen Hawking](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/10/ep-2-i-almost-killed-stephen-hawking-true-story-of-flying-stephen-hawk/)).
- Sian Proctor traces her path from a NASA rejection to piloting Inspiration4 ([Sian Proctor](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/10/ep-5-becoming-the-first-black-female-space-pilot-w-dr-sian-proctor/)).

### Bitcoin and institutions under pressure

- Michael Saylor frames Bitcoin as digital energy and property, now beyond any single regulator's reach ([Michael Saylor](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/10/ep-9-the-future-is-bitcoin-w-michael-saylor/)).
- Diamandis and Salim Ismail argue Twitter and national democracies move too slowly for exponential technology ([Salim Ismail](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/11/ep-11-the-future-of-twitter-american-democracy-and-ai-w-salim-ismail/)).

### Purpose and mindset

- Marc Benioff describes a company run on vision, ranked values and service to all stakeholders ([Marc Benioff](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/11/ep-10-the-multi-billion-dollar-purpose-behind-salesforce-w-marc-beniof/)); Naveen Jain sets out his moonshot test ([Naveen Jain](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/11/ep-13-how-to-think-like-a-billionaire-w-naveen-jain/)).
- Jane McGonigal argues games build curiosity and urgent optimism ([Jane McGonigal](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/11/ep-12-gaming-will-save-the-world-w-jane-mcgonigal/)); Diamandis tells listeners to share their massive transformative purpose constantly ([Ask Peter Anything Part 2](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2022/12/ep-20-ask-peter-anything-part-2-how-to-be-a-great-entrepreneur-leader/)).
