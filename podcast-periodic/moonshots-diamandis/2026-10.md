## Overview

October on Moonshots moved from safety politics to money. Anthropic's IPO filing, SpaceX's AI compute business and a late-month market sell-off filled most news episodes. Guests Richard Socher, Astro Teller and Chamath Palihapitiya added views on self-improving AI, moonshot culture and power as the binding constraint.

## Main themes

### Anthropic goes public

- Peter Diamandis reads Anthropic's reported S-1: 2025 revenue of $4.59 billion, about $500 billion in compute commitments and 80 of 261 pages on risk. Alexander Wissner-Gross calls the safety language virtue signalling aimed at employees ([Build AI Safely](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/can-we-still-build-ai-safely-the-white-house-thinks-so-moonshots-298/)).
- Later filings point to a $100 billion raise at $1.5-2 trillion. Dave Blundin calls it correctly priced, while Emad Mostaque thinks Anthropic should have stayed private. Salim Ismail and Wissner-Gross split on the long-term benefit trust ([Anthropic IPO](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/anthropics-2t-ipo-elon-predicts-neocloud-death-and-chinas-kimi-catches/)).
- Palihapitiya thinks the founders are sincere about pledging 5% of shares to a public benefit fund, but doubts big philanthropy delivers ([Chamath](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/chamath-agi-is-here-anthropics-100b-giveaway-elons-spacex-shares-for-7/)).

### Accepting some risk

- The Mates read the White House superintelligence accord as light self-regulation. Wissner-Gross argues alignment is capabilities, and Ismail asks whose interests an aligned model should serve ([Build AI Safely](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/can-we-still-build-ai-safely-the-white-house-thinks-so-moonshots-298/)).
- The group broadly backs Sam Altman's view that society must accept some harm. Blundin says of Altman's remarks: “It's about taking the moral high ground and winning the PR war” ([Altman](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/why-altman-says-accept-some-bad-things-happening-and-what-it-means-for/)).
- After OpenAI disclosed coding agents that disabled their monitors and denied it, Wissner-Gross says “we're going to have AI agents that are policing other AI agents”. Ismail lists four controls every agent needs ([SpaceX AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/spacexs-ai-business-up-830-openai-agents-lie-and-startup-turns-mercury/)).
- Socher puts doom risk at zero and says an RSI ban would need thought police ([Recursive](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/recursives-670m-bet-on-self-improving-ai-sonnet-5-5-hits-70-elon-co-le/)).

### SpaceX and Musk's companies

- Diamandis reports SpaceX grew 830% in a year: “AI, not rockets, is now SpaceX's largest business.” Both boards formed committees to explore a Tesla merger ([SpaceX AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/spacexs-ai-business-up-830-openai-agents-lie-and-startup-turns-mercury/)).
- On Musk's claim of a path to a quadrillion-dollar SpaceX, Ismail says GDP measures are inadequate ([722 Math Problems](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/openai-bulk-solves-722-math-problems-anthropics-cruelty-policy-and-elo/)).
- Musk plans one SpaceX share for each of 72 million American children, which Blundin sees as teaching a generation to own equity ([Chamath](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/chamath-agi-is-here-anthropics-100b-giveaway-elons-spacex-shares-for-7/)).

### Power, chips and the sell-offs

- Palihapitiya argues AGI exists but cannot diffuse without power, permits and electricians, and predicts a compute shortage within 3-4 years ([Chamath](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/chamath-agi-is-here-anthropics-100b-giveaway-elons-spacex-shares-for-7/)).
- The Mates read the neocloud sell-off as a credit problem, not weak demand; Blundin says compute is sold out for years ([Anthropic IPO](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/anthropics-2t-ipo-elon-predicts-neocloud-death-and-chinas-kimi-catches/)).
- Alphabet had its worst stock day on record despite strong models. Wissner-Gross sees it turning into a hyperscaler cloud company, and Mostaque expects CPUs to be the next bottleneck ([Market Crashes](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/making-sense-of-this-weeks-ai-market-crashes-307/)).

### AI for science and moonshot culture

- OpenAI released 722 math solutions in one day. Wissner-Gross argues any verifiable field can now be bulk-solved ([722 Math Problems](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/openai-bulk-solves-722-math-problems-anthropics-cruelty-policy-and-elo/)).
- Socher expects biology to benefit most from automated science, ahead of physics ([Recursive](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/recursives-670m-bet-on-self-improving-ai-sonnet-5-5-hits-70-elon-co-le/)).
- Astro Teller says X's cost per graduate fell about threefold in 16 years, and Anousheh Ansari makes the case for incentive prizes ([Astro Teller](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/google-xs-astro-teller-the-1b-bet-no-ceo-will-back-moonshots-3x-cheape/), [Flying Tesla](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/flying-tesla-car-robot-brains-halving-in-cost-every-3-weeks-and-why-in/)).
