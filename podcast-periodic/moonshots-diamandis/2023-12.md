## Overview

December on Moonshots stepped away from AI and into personal longevity. Two episodes started a series with Helen Messier of Fountain Life, based on the book Longevity Practical Playbook, and a third had Valter Longo of the USC Longevity Institute on fasting. Peter Diamandis framed all three as general discussion, not medical advice, and kept returning to the companies, prizes and investments forming around healthspan.

## Main themes

### Lifestyle as the cheapest healthspan lever

- Helen Messier and Peter Diamandis present diet as one of the most accessible levers for healthspan, the years lived in good health rather than total lifespan. Messier argues lifestyle matters far more than genes ([Eat to Live Longer](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/how-to-eat-to-live-longer-in-2024-w-dr-helen-messier-ep-76/)).
- Messier stresses that “there is no one right diet for everybody”, and both recommend personal measurement with consumer devices such as glucose monitors ([Eat to Live Longer](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/how-to-eat-to-live-longer-in-2024-w-dr-helen-messier-ep-76/)).
- In the second episode Messier says that if people could pick only one lifestyle pillar, it should be exercise, and both see its evidence as less contested than any diet's ([Exercise Routine](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/the-simple-exercise-routine-that-will-extend-your-life-w-helen-messier/)).
- Diamandis says that exercise, if it could be bottled, “would be you know a trillion dollar Blockbuster drug” ([Exercise Routine](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/the-simple-exercise-routine-that-will-extend-your-life-w-helen-messier/)).

### From fasting research to a product

- Valter Longo argues that periodic fasting could become mainstream only if it is safe and easy enough for most people. He says most patients and oncologists in an early USC trial would not accept water-only fasting ([Water Fasting](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/the-groundbreaking-science-of-water-fasting-w-valter-longo-ep-78/)).
- That led his lab to a packaged fasting-mimicking diet, licensed commercially as ProLon by L-Nutra. Diamandis says he uses it, and ProLon also sponsored the diet episode ([Water Fasting](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/the-groundbreaking-science-of-water-fasting-w-valter-longo-ep-78/), [Eat to Live Longer](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/how-to-eat-to-live-longer-in-2024-w-dr-helen-messier-ep-76/)).
- Diamandis cites about 21 completed ProLon clinical studies, and Longo says roughly 30 more are running or about to start ([Water Fasting](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/the-groundbreaking-science-of-water-fasting-w-valter-longo-ep-78/)).
- Longo also cautions that radical lifespan extension within a decade is wishful thinking, and puts 20-30 extra healthy years about 30 years out ([Water Fasting](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/the-groundbreaking-science-of-water-fasting-w-valter-longo-ep-78/)).

### The business around longevity

- Diamandis says Fountain Life, the diagnostics and longevity centre company he co-founded, had four centres and planned to grow toward 30 or 40 worldwide ([Eat to Live Longer](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/how-to-eat-to-live-longer-in-2024-w-dr-helen-messier-ep-76/)).
- He describes XPRIZE Healthspan, the episode sponsor, as a seven-year, $101 million competition for therapeutics that target ageing ([Eat to Live Longer](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/how-to-eat-to-live-longer-in-2024-w-dr-helen-messier-ep-76/)).
- Diamandis says he has invested in startups working on age-related muscle loss. Messier counters that such therapies still need people to use their muscles ([Exercise Routine](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/12/the-simple-exercise-routine-that-will-extend-your-life-w-helen-messier/)).
