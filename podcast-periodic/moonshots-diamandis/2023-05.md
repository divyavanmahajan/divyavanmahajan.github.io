## Overview

May 2023 was dominated by a three-part series with Salim Ismail built around their book Exponential Organizations 2.0, covering scaling, purpose and AI. Peter Diamandis also closed Abundance360 with a look from 1923 to 2033. Two guest episodes, with Axon's Rick Smith and XPRIZE CEO Anousheh Ansari, showed moonshot thinking applied to policing and spaceflight.

## Main themes

### Exponential organizations and abundance

- Salim Ismail defines an exponential organization (ExO) as one that scales as fast as its technology. He says ExOs like Airbnb and Uber figured out “how do you drop the cost of Supply to near zero” ([EP #44](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/10x-your-business-why-transitioning-from-linear-to-exponential-is-cruc/)).
- Diamandis argues that only exponential organizations will survive this decade, retelling how Kodak ignored its own digital camera while 13-person Instagram sold for a billion dollars ([EP #44](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/10x-your-business-why-transitioning-from-linear-to-exponential-is-cruc/)).
- Ismail expects the move from scarcity to subscription models that hit music to reach energy, healthcare and education, and advises reinventing the organization every year or two ([EP #44](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/10x-your-business-why-transitioning-from-linear-to-exponential-is-cruc/)).
- In his Abundance360 closing, Diamandis reads ten faculty predictions for 2033, from free AI tutoring to commercial fusion, as the basis for trillion-dollar industries ([Meditation](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/a-meditation-about-the-future-bonus-episode/)).

### Purpose and 10x moonshots

- Ismail says the one trait shared by 200 of the fastest-growing unicorns was a massive transformative purpose (MTP). It lets junior managers decide on their own and helps hiring ([EP #45](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/success-guaranteed-what-all-ultra-successful-founders-or-companies-hav/)).
- Diamandis calls the MTP “the emotional energy that drives you” and treats moonshots as ten-year, 10x goals on that canvas, citing Elon Musk dropping a working Falcon 1 for Falcon 9 ([EP #45](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/success-guaranteed-what-all-ultra-successful-founders-or-companies-hav/)).
- Ismail cites BlackBerry as lacking a purpose and Kodak as forgetting George Eastman's, and says Unilever's most profitable brands were the ones that adopted a purpose most ([EP #45](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/success-guaranteed-what-all-ultra-successful-founders-or-companies-hav/)).

### AI as a required business capability

- Diamandis says that by the end of the decade there will be companies fully using AI and companies out of business. He urges every firm to appoint a chief AI officer ([EP #46](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/how-ai-will-change-business-forever-w-salim-ismail-ep-46/)).
- On jobs, Ismail notes the first robots-take-all-jobs article appeared in 1964 and points to ATMs, after which teller numbers held steady. Diamandis agrees that someone using AI, not AI itself, will take your job ([EP #46](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/how-ai-will-change-business-forever-w-salim-ismail-ep-46/)).
- Both expect tiny companies: Diamandis believes a three-person billion-dollar company will appear within about a year, and argues unique data is the moat ([EP #44](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/10x-your-business-why-transitioning-from-linear-to-exponential-is-cruc/), [EP #46](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/how-ai-will-change-business-forever-w-salim-ismail-ep-46/)).
- The authors say they rewrote large parts of the book after ChatGPT and added a chatbot that answers questions from its text ([EP #46](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/how-ai-will-change-business-forever-w-salim-ismail-ep-46/)).

### Moonshots in policing and spaceflight

- Rick Smith says Axon's moonshot is to cut gun deaths between police and the public by 50% by 2032, using non-lethal tools that outperform the gun. He credits Abundance360 with shaping it ([EP #43](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/stopping-police-shootings-with-revolutionary-tech-w-rick-smith-ep-43/)).
- Smith cites a survey in which 70% of Americans supported drones or robots to stop mass shooters, but insists the decision to use force “should not be made by an algorithm” ([EP #43](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/stopping-police-shootings-with-revolutionary-tech-w-rick-smith-ep-43/)).
- Anousheh Ansari recalls funding the $10 million Ansari XPRIZE after about 150 others declined, and argues prizes are high-leverage because only the winning team gets paid ([EP #42](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/ep-42-what-its-really-like-to-live-in-space-w-anousheh-ansari/)).
- Ansari blames billionaires' caution on a scarcity mindset; Diamandis adds that the Forbes list gamifies keeping wealth ([EP #42](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/05/ep-42-what-its-really-like-to-live-in-space-w-anousheh-ansari/)).
