## Overview

November on Moonshots mixed weekly news roundtables with two Eric Schmidt conversations. The Moonshot Mates tracked a fast-moving lab race (OpenAI's restructuring, Anthropic's enterprise gains, Gemini 3, Claude Opus 4.5) and kept returning to power, chips and the US-China contest. A second thread asked whether falling AI costs bring abundance for everyone or a wider wealth gap first.

## Main themes

### The frontier lab race

- OpenAI's restructuring leaves Microsoft with 27% and the foundation with 26%. Alex Wissner-Gross thinks OpenAI could reach $100 billion in revenue by 2027 by running agents around the clock ([OpenAI Going Public](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/openai-going-public-the-china-us-ai-race-and-how-ai-is-reshaping-the-s/)).
- The hosts discuss reports that Anthropic has overtaken OpenAI in enterprise API share. Salim Ismail says the banks and enterprises he talks to are “all using anthropic because they trust it with sensitive data” ([AI Wealth Gap](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/the-ai-wealth-gap-why-40x-deflation-changes-everything-w-dave-blundin/)).
- Peter Diamandis opens the Gemini 3 roundtable saying Google is winning. Dave Blundin argues Google only moves under pressure from OpenAI ([Gemini 3](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/ai-roundtable-what-everyone-missed-about-gemini-3-w-salim-ismail-dave/)).
- Anthropic is to spend $30 billion on Azure while Microsoft and NVIDIA invest up to $15 billion in it ([AI This Week](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/ai-this-week-nvidias-record-revenue-elons-data-centers-in-space-gemini/)).

### Eric Schmidt on superintelligence and China

- Schmidt says “we probably need another algorithmic breakthrough” for real superintelligence, because systems are trained against fixed objectives ([Superintelligence](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/how-life-changes-when-we-reach-artificial-superintelligence-w-dr-fei-f/)).
- He warns that network effects may concentrate AI's gains in early adopters and well-run countries. Fei-Fei Li adds that shared prosperity depends on policy ([Superintelligence](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/how-life-changes-when-we-reach-artificial-superintelligence-w-dr-fei-f/)).
- With Blundin, Schmidt says the US leads on frontier AI, while China focuses on wide adoption, cheap robots and abundant power. He worries more about open-source proliferation than big labs ([US vs China](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/ex-google-ceo-breaks-down-the-us-vs-china-ai-race-how-we-avoid-a-globa/)).
- Diamandis cites China's share of EVs, solar panels and AI patents ([OpenAI Going Public](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/openai-going-public-the-china-us-ai-race-and-how-ai-is-reshaping-the-s/)).

### Power and compute set the pace

- Schmidt's testimony put US data center demand at 92 gigawatts by 2030, and he warns that without more energy the US cannot fully exploit its AI lead ([US vs China](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/ex-google-ceo-breaks-down-the-us-vs-china-ai-race-how-we-avoid-a-globa/)).
- NVIDIA reported $57 billion in quarterly revenue. Elon Musk talks of solar-powered data centres in orbit, and Blundin expects a power shortfall between 2030 and 2035 ([AI This Week](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/ai-this-week-nvidias-record-revenue-elons-data-centers-in-space-gemini/)).
- Wissner-Gross reads NVIDIA's $5 trillion valuation as a signal that compute is scarce ([OpenAI Going Public](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/openai-going-public-the-china-us-ai-race-and-how-ai-is-reshaping-the-s/)). The $80 billion Westinghouse reactor deal and Amazon's $50 billion government AI push also feature ([AI Wealth Gap](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/the-ai-wealth-gap-why-40x-deflation-changes-everything-w-dave-blundin/), [Opus 4.5](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/claude-opus-4-5-white-house-genesis-mission-amazons-50b-ai-push-w-emad/)).

### Abundance, jobs and the wealth gap

- Diamandis shows job openings falling while the S&P 500 climbed. Blundin sees a break; Wissner-Gross blames interest rates ([OpenAI Going Public](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/openai-going-public-the-china-us-ai-race-and-how-ai-is-reshaping-the-s/)).
- An FII survey ranks cost of living and unemployment as top worries. Ismail argues for UBI-style structures, Diamandis for universal basic services ([AI Wealth Gap](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/the-ai-wealth-gap-why-40x-deflation-changes-everything-w-dave-blundin/)).
- Wissner-Gross argues the cost of intelligence, falling about 40x a year, is upstream of abundance ([Gemini 3](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/ai-roundtable-what-everyone-missed-about-gemini-3-w-salim-ismail-dave/)).

### Recursive self-improvement and agents

- With Claude Opus 4.5, Wissner-Gross says labs are “nearing, if not already, at the point of recursive self-improvement”. Emad Mostaque says Opus scores higher when orchestrating sub-agents ([Opus 4.5](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/claude-opus-4-5-white-house-genesis-mission-amazons-50b-ai-push-w-emad/)).
- Gemini 3's Vending-Bench result leads Wissner-Gross to talk of zero-human startups. Blundin puts the non-human economy at about a trillion dollars ([Gemini 3](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/ai-roundtable-what-everyone-missed-about-gemini-3-w-salim-ismail-dave/)).
- The White House Genesis Mission, likened to the Manhattan Project, aims to connect US supercomputers and lab data for AI-driven science ([Opus 4.5](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/11/claude-opus-4-5-white-house-genesis-mission-amazons-50b-ai-push-w-emad/)).
