## Overview

February on Moonshots kept returning to AI and jobs, from Ray Dalio's macro view to Mo Gawdat's warning of a decade of disruption. Richard Socher and an FII Miami panel looked at where AI value will sit and why most enterprise projects stall. Two health episodes and a conversation with Steven Kotler on flow and creativity rounded out the month.

## Main themes

### AI, jobs and the economy

- Ray Dalio calls AI “a super plus for productivity” but a divider in who benefits, which makes distribution of the gains a social question ([Dalio](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/ray-dalio-on-ai-job-loss-the-future-of-the-economy-ep-148/)).
- Diamandis asks whether cheap borrowing will lead companies to buy robots and agents instead of hiring, citing Benioff's claim that Salesforce is hiring no new engineers ([Dalio](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/ray-dalio-on-ai-job-loss-the-future-of-the-economy-ep-148/)).
- Dalio places AI inside his big forces: an 80-year debt cycle, internal disorder, a shift to a unilateral world order, nature and demographics. His indicators look like 1998, with perhaps a year and a half of good times left ([Dalio](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/ray-dalio-on-ai-job-loss-the-future-of-the-economy-ep-148/)).
- Salim Ismail points to high robot use and low unemployment in Sweden, South Korea and Germany. Gawdat expects real suffering and argues for social systems rather than new jobs ([Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/agi-is-here-you-just-dont-realize-it-yet-w-mo-gawdat-salim-ismail-ep-1/)).

### Defining AGI and the frontier race

- Socher proposes a financial definition of AGI: automating about 80% of digitized work. He says superintelligence could be purely digital and calls the Turing test broken ([Socher](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/the-man-who-invented-prompt-engineering-on-ai-agi-the-future-of-humano/)).
- Diamandis notes xAI built its Grok 3 cluster in 122 days. Ismail thinks claims that Grok 3 beats every model are more hype than reality ([Socher](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/the-man-who-invented-prompt-engineering-on-ai-agi-the-future-of-humano/), [Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/agi-is-here-you-just-dont-realize-it-yet-w-mo-gawdat-salim-ismail-ep-1/)).
- Socher expects foundation-model makers to look like telcos, with value in trust layers and agents on top. Ismail thinks data centres are overbuilt; Socher cites Jevons paradox ([Socher](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/the-man-who-invented-prompt-engineering-on-ai-agi-the-future-of-humano/)).

### Enterprise adoption and specialised AI

- McKinsey's Alexander Sukharevsky says only about 11% of AI use cases outside tech reached production in five years, because companies bolt technology onto old processes ([FII Miami](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/ai-leaders-reveal-the-next-wave-of-ai-breakthroughs-at-fii-miami-2025/)).
- The panel describes narrow models for film, small on-device models, quantitative models for chemistry and cheaper open hardware ([FII Miami](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/ai-leaders-reveal-the-next-wave-of-ai-breakthroughs-at-fii-miami-2025/)).

### Ethics, power and a dystopia before abundance

- Gawdat argues AI is already smarter than him and expects a dystopia from 2027 lasting about ten years, until competition forces decisions onto AI ([Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/agi-is-here-you-just-dont-realize-it-yet-w-mo-gawdat-salim-ismail-ep-1/)).
- He says that “if we manage to teach them ethics we will reduce the intensity and the duration of the dystopia”, and predicts trillionaires and more surveillance ([Gawdat](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/agi-is-here-you-just-dont-realize-it-yet-w-mo-gawdat-salim-ismail-ep-1/)).

### Creativity, flow and longevity

- Kotler argues LLMs drift to the average while humans excel at lateral thinking, so humans in flow working with AI will lead for 10 to 15 years ([Kotler](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/achieve-peak-creativity-merging-flow-states-with-ai-technology-w-steve/)).
- Robert Hariri argues the US approval model is too slow and costly for cell therapy and calls for provisional approvals backed by real-world data. Diamandis discloses he is Celularity's vice chairman ([Hariri](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/leading-stem-cell-expert-stem-cells-101-the-future-of-medicine-w-rober/)).
- Gary Brecka and Diamandis tour consumer health devices and expect AI to pull wearable data into a personal health assistant ([Brecka](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/02/ai-biohacking-breakthroughs-transform-your-health-with-gary-breckas-to/)).
