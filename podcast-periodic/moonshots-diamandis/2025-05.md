## Overview

May on Moonshots was dominated by roundtables with Salim Ismail and Dave Blundin, which kept returning to one idea: AI is moving faster than schools, governments and incumbent businesses. Bitcoin came up in five of eight episodes, and the OpenAI and Google news cycle framed a fight over the consumer interface. Guest episodes covered defence with Palmer Luckey, brain-computer interfaces with Max Hodak, sleep, and founder advice.

## Main themes

### AI outruns institutions

- The hosts welcomed a US executive order bringing AI into classrooms, and Ismail cited estimates that people learn 2x to 4x faster with AI ([Trillion Dollar AI Reset](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/the-trillion-dollar-ai-reset-advertising-gaming-is-next-w-salim-ismail/)).
- On the UAE's plan to draft laws with AI, Blundin argued that “nothing can keep up with AI other than AI”, while Ismail described a sequence from navigating regulation to rewriting it from first principles ([Trillion Dollar AI Reset](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/the-trillion-dollar-ai-reset-advertising-gaming-is-next-w-salim-ismail/)).
- At MIT, Diamandis and Blundin argued that education is falling far behind, and an audience poll favoured Thiel Fellowship and Y Combinator over Harvard and MIT degrees ([After Interviewing AI Founders](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/after-interviewing-ai-founders-heres-what-you-really-need-to-know-ep-1/)).

### The fight for the consumer AI interface

- Diamandis predicted agents that buy on users' behalf will undercut advertising. Blundin cited Richard Socher's estimate that revenue per AI session is one-tenth to one-hundredth of a Google search ([Trillion Dollar AI Reset](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/the-trillion-dollar-ai-reset-advertising-gaming-is-next-w-salim-ismail/)).
- Anish Acharya of a16z said Google's AI Mode felt watered down by 20 years of ad commitments, and called OpenAI's Operator underrated ([The AI Reset Is Here](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/the-ai-reset-is-here-search-jobs-and-everything-else-w-anish-acharya-d/)).
- Blundin called OpenAI's $6.5 billion Jony Ive deal a bid for a direct-to-consumer device, and read Google I/O as a claim to the top spot in every category, backed by control from algorithm to TPU chip design ([OpenAI and Google I/O](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/why-openai-paid-6-5-billion-to-make-the-new-iphone-how-google-just-end/)).
- On Veo 3, Diamandis asked how studios survive two or three years; Ismail expects professional filmmakers, not consumers, to gain most ([OpenAI and Google I/O](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/why-openai-paid-6-5-billion-to-make-the-new-iphone-how-google-just-end/)).

### Bitcoin and concentrating capital

- Ismail argued treasury secretaries should hold Bitcoin as a reserve. Blundin said he gave two CEOs a green light to buy it and neither did ([Trillion Dollar AI Reset](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/the-trillion-dollar-ai-reset-advertising-gaming-is-next-w-salim-ismail/)).
- The hosts discussed Michael Saylor's 42/42 plan and corporate purchases outpacing new supply by 3.3x, alongside Gulf AI deals they read as Saudi Arabia aligning with the US ([Global Power Shift](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/tech-leaders-on-bitcoin-ai-and-the-new-global-power-shift-w-salim-isma/)).
- Blundin sees concentration winning and the AI race settled within a few years; Ismail expects democratization to level it ([Global Power Shift](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/tech-leaders-on-bitcoin-ai-and-the-new-global-power-shift-w-salim-isma/)).

### Jobs, abundance and founders

- Citing reports that engineers were about 40% of Microsoft's layoffs, Blundin said “almost any white collar coding or other job, it's not going to exist in 2030” and urged CEOs to plan humanely ([Global Power Shift](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/tech-leaders-on-bitcoin-ai-and-the-new-global-power-shift-w-salim-isma/)).
- Acharya called AI the substrate for a more abundant life, while Diamandis worried about growing too dependent on it ([The AI Reset Is Here](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/the-ai-reset-is-here-search-jobs-and-everything-else-w-anish-acharya-d/)).
- Diamandis told founders that mindset (purpose, exponential, abundance, longevity) matters more than business plans, drawing on the X Prize story ([After Interviewing AI Founders](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/after-interviewing-ai-founders-heres-what-you-really-need-to-know-ep-1/)).

### Defence and hard technology

- Palmer Luckey said Anduril was built assuming AI and autonomy would work, and argued China, not rogue actors, is the threat to plan for, with production designed to surge in wartime ([Palmer Luckey](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/aliens-ai-weapons-china-global-conflict-palmer-luckey-sounds-the-alarm/)).
- Max Hodak argued brain-computer interfaces need a revenue-generating product to avoid a funding winter, and presented Science Corp's retinal implant as that business ([Max Hodak](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/05/ex-neuralink-founder-ai-enhanced-bodies-are-nearly-here-w-max-hodak-ep/)).
