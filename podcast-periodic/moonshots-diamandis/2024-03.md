## Overview

March leaned on the 2024 Abundance360 Summit, with Elon Musk and Michael Saylor recorded there. The month's two threads were how soon AI surpasses humans and whether Bitcoin belongs on corporate balance sheets. Emad Mostaque's exit from Stability AI and Will Marshall's satellite business rounded it out.

## Main themes

### Superintelligence timelines and safety

- Giving 50th-percentile estimates, Musk says it is “probably end of next year before AI can do better than any individual human could do”, and expects digital intelligence to exceed all humans combined around 2029-2030 ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/elon-musk-on-agi-safety-superintelligence-and-neuralink-2024-ep-91/)).
- Musk puts the chance that superintelligence ends humanity at roughly 10-20%, agreeing with Geoffrey Hinton. His prescription is to raise AI to be maximally truthful and never forced to lie, citing HAL in 2001: A Space Odyssey ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/elon-musk-on-agi-safety-superintelligence-and-neuralink-2024-ep-91/)).
- Earlier in the month Diamandis says the Turing test passed with little notice, and Ismail recalls Kurzweil's view that the singularity is a process rather than a single event. Diamandis feels it will arrive around 2033 ([Bitcoin Surge and AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/bitcoin-surge-ai-job-loss-2024-agi-predictions-w-salim-ismail-ep-89/)).
- Ismail says AGI has no clear definition and proposes artificial common sense as a better milestone ([Bitcoin Surge and AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/bitcoin-surge-ai-job-loss-2024-agi-predictions-w-salim-ismail-ep-89/)).

### Abundance, compute and jobs

- Musk calls abundance the most likely outcome. With Full Self-Driving cars and Optimus robots, he says the only scarcity left will be the kind “we just decide to create artificially” ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/elon-musk-on-agi-safety-superintelligence-and-neuralink-2024-ep-91/)).
- Musk says AI compute is growing tenfold every six months, with the bottleneck moving from chips to transformers and then electrical power ([Elon Musk](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/elon-musk-on-agi-safety-superintelligence-and-neuralink-2024-ep-91/)).
- The hosts discuss Klarna's AI assistant, which reportedly did the work of 700 agents. Ismail notes it already handles two-thirds of chats and says customer service routing is ripe for AI ([Bitcoin Surge and AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/bitcoin-surge-ai-job-loss-2024-agi-predictions-w-salim-ismail-ep-89/)).

### Bitcoin as a treasury asset

- With Bitcoin at about $67,000, Ismail joins the million-dollar camp and argues that 1% of the Fortune 100 putting 1% of treasury into Bitcoin would get it there ([Bitcoin Surge and AGI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/bitcoin-surge-ai-job-loss-2024-agi-predictions-w-salim-ismail-ep-89/)).
- Saylor recounts MicroStrategy's 2020 move from $500 million in cash earning 0% to a first $250 million Bitcoin purchase, and says enterprise value has since reached $30 billion ([Michael Saylor](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/the-future-of-bitcoin-w-michael-saylor-2024-ep-92/)).
- Saylor calls Bitcoin digital property, not a currency, and argues companies cannot outwork inflation. He names indefinite intangible accounting as the main barrier to corporate adoption ([Michael Saylor](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/the-future-of-bitcoin-w-michael-saylor-2024-ep-92/)).
- He dates mainstream adoption to the January 2024 spot ETF approvals and argues AI agents will need Bitcoin to transact ([Michael Saylor](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/the-future-of-bitcoin-w-michael-saylor-2024-ep-92/)).

### Decentralised AI and democracy

- Five days after stepping down as Stability AI CEO, Mostaque says the urgent job is decentralising AI, with a national model owned by the people of each country ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/why-im-leaving-my-company-immediately-stability-ai-w-emad-mostaque-ep/)).
- He argues democracy will either improve or end under AI, and warns of a 1984-style path driven by personalised persuasion ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/why-im-leaving-my-company-immediately-stability-ai-w-emad-mostaque-ep/)).
- He also wants open models for health and AI tutors for every child, and advises leaders to treat curated data sets as infrastructure ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/03/why-im-leaving-my-company-immediately-stability-ai-w-emad-mostaque-ep/)).
