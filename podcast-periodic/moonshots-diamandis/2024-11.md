## Overview

November on Moonshots drew heavily on stage sessions from the FII8 conference in Riyadh, where Elon Musk, Eric Schmidt, Ben Horowitz and Kai-Fu Lee gave their views on how fast AI is moving and where value will land. The other half of the month reacted to the 2024 US election: Salim Ismail and Anthony Scaramucci joined Peter Diamandis to discuss deregulation, DOGE and Bitcoin.

## Main themes

### How fast AI is moving

- Musk says AI is getting about 10x better per year and could do anything a single human can do within a year or two ([Elon's Predictions](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/elons-predictions-for-the-future-ai-free-energy-and-more-ep-129/)).
- Schmidt predicts specialized savants within five years and a single system at 80-90% of expert ability in every field around 2030-2032. Such a system, he says, would be “effectively smarter than any human” ([FII AI Panel](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/the-future-of-ai-leaders-from-tiktok-google-more-weigh-in-fii-panel-ep/)).
- Richard Socher argues AI becomes superhuman fastest where a domain can be simulated, such as Go and programming, and that individual contributors will have to learn to manage AI agents ([After GPT-4o](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/top-minds-in-ai-explain-whats-coming-after-gpt-4o-ep-130/)).

### Safety, cost and where value accrues

- Musk calls AI the biggest near-term existential risk and puts the chance of a bad outcome at 10-20%. He argues AI must be maximally truth-seeking, his stated reason for founding xAI ([Elon's Predictions](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/elons-predictions-for-the-future-ai-free-energy-and-more-ep-129/)).
- Horowitz compares AI infrastructure to the bandwidth glut after 1999 and warns that debt-financed data centers “could get upside down very fast” ([FII AI Panel](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/the-future-of-ai-leaders-from-tiktok-google-more-weigh-in-fii-panel-ep/)).
- Kai-Fu Lee says 01.AI trained a top-ranked model for $3 million with about 2,000 GPUs and serves it at 10 cents per million tokens. He credits limited GPU access, and Socher frames it as Jevons paradox ([After GPT-4o](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/top-minds-in-ai-explain-whats-coming-after-gpt-4o-ep-130/)).

### The election, DOGE and deregulation

- Diamandis and Ismail expect a deregulation-minded White House to help crypto, drones, robotics and biotech. Ismail sees no mechanism to regulate AI and expects private auditing firms to fill the gap ([Election Results](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/what-the-election-results-mean-for-emerging-tech-w-salim-ismail-ep-128/)).
- Scaramucci argues DOGE might find $300-500 billion in cuts but not $2 trillion, and notes it is not a real department unless Congress creates one. He proposes pay-as-you-go budget rules instead ([Scaramucci](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/anthony-scaramucci-elon-doge-americas-fate-under-new-leadership-w-sali/)).
- Musk names overregulation as his biggest obstacle and says he is more optimistic under a Trump administration ([Elon's Predictions](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/elons-predictions-for-the-future-ai-free-energy-and-more-ep-129/)).

### Bitcoin as a reserve asset

- Ismail sees the main tension in crypto's challenge to US dollar hegemony, and wants everyday crypto payments freed from property-transaction tax ([Election Results](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/what-the-election-results-mean-for-emerging-tech-w-salim-ismail-ep-128/)).
- Scaramucci says 55% of his net worth is in Bitcoin and argues it can reach gold's $18 trillion market cap, citing state reserve moves in Pennsylvania and Wisconsin ([Scaramucci](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/anthony-scaramucci-elon-doge-americas-fate-under-new-leadership-w-sali/)).

### Abundance and AI in healthcare delivery

- Diamandis presents long-run data on falling poverty and rising literacy, arguing technology turns scarcity into abundance. Musk agrees society may become post-capitalist ([Stats](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/the-stats-traditional-media-will-never-show-you-ep-131/), [Elon's Predictions](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/elons-predictions-for-the-future-ai-free-energy-and-more-ep-129/)).
- An FII health panel sees adoption, not capability, as the bottleneck: incentives, regulation and clinician workflows. Panellists expect countries with less legacy infrastructure to move faster than the US ([Future of Medicine](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/11/the-future-of-medicine-how-ai-can-transform-healthcare-for-billions-fi/)).
