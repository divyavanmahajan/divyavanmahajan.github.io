## Overview

June on Moonshots was a month of debate. Mo Gawdat appeared twice as the cautious voice, first against the Moonshot Mates on jobs and warfare, then alongside Steven Kotler, who called AI overhyped. The month closed with a roundtable that framed the race to superintelligence as winner-take-all.

## Main themes

### Jobs: how many, how fast

- Gawdat predicted “10 20 30 40% uh unemployment in some sectors” within two to three years, and argued that when jobs go they will go all at once ([Job Loss, Privacy and Warfare](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/ai-experts-debate-ai-job-loss-the-end-of-privacy-beginning-of-ai-warfa/)).
- Diamandis estimated from BLS data that around 40% of jobs could be automated in three to five years. Ismail countered that people adapt faster than expected and called for government experiments with UBI and four-day weeks ([Job Loss, Privacy and Warfare](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/ai-experts-debate-ai-job-loss-the-end-of-privacy-beginning-of-ai-warfa/)).
- Reacting to Dario Amodei's warnings about entry-level work, Blundin said far more people in power are in denial than overreacting. Diamandis argued entrepreneur is the job that survives, and Blundin described a singularity sprint to build before AI erodes human leverage ([Job Loss, Privacy and Warfare](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/ai-experts-debate-ai-job-loss-the-end-of-privacy-beginning-of-ai-warfa/)).

### Hype, misuse and cooperation

- Kotler compared AI hype to Bitcoin and the metaverse, noting that “Everybody I hear see on stage talking about this stuff is making a living off of it” ([Overhyped or Underhyped](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/ai-experts-debate-overhyped-or-underhyped-opposite-opinions-mo-gawdat/)).
- Gawdat replied that AI is underhyped given where it is heading, but sees near-certain misuse by bad actors and predicts a drastic event within two to three years ([Overhyped or Underhyped](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/ai-experts-debate-overhyped-or-underhyped-opposite-opinions-mo-gawdat/)).
- Gawdat and Diamandis hope a smarter AI turns benevolent; Kotler rejected the idea of a code god and called for a Manhattan-style project and an XPRIZE for global cooperation ([Overhyped or Underhyped](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/ai-experts-debate-overhyped-or-underhyped-opposite-opinions-mo-gawdat/)).

### Warfare, privacy and safety

- The hosts discussed Meta and Palmer Luckey's company teaming up on a $100 million US Army VR contract. Gawdat called the autonomous-weapons race a mistake, while Blundin argued constant surveillance shifts future warfare towards leaders rather than populations ([Job Loss, Privacy and Warfare](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/ai-experts-debate-ai-job-loss-the-end-of-privacy-beginning-of-ai-warfa/)).
- On Palantir compiling data on Americans, Ismail said US privacy protections have eroded and Blundin said Google holds far more data than government ([Job Loss, Privacy and Warfare](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/ai-experts-debate-ai-job-loss-the-end-of-privacy-beginning-of-ai-warfa/)).
- Gawdat named AlphaEvolve and self-evolving AI the top topic to watch; Blundin would draw the line at models changing their own weights ([Job Loss, Privacy and Warfare](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/ai-experts-debate-ai-job-loss-the-end-of-privacy-beginning-of-ai-warfa/)).

### The race to superintelligence

- The hosts discussed Elon Musk's claim that digital superintelligence may arrive this year or next. Blundin said “the natural dynamic is winner takes all because the AI becomes self-improving very very soon” ([Race for Super Intelligence](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/the-race-for-super-intelligence-will-decide-our-future-as-a-species-w/)).
- Ismail expects governments to nationalize an emerging superintelligence; Blundin expects the US to use private contractors instead ([Race for Super Intelligence](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/the-race-for-super-intelligence-will-decide-our-future-as-a-species-w/)).
- Diamandis read Meta's $100 million sign-on offers and its $14.8 billion Scale AI stake as rational for a company worth $1.8 trillion that fears falling behind after Llama 4 ([Race for Super Intelligence](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/the-race-for-super-intelligence-will-decide-our-future-as-a-species-w/)).
- The hosts named energy as the main US weakness against China, contrasting China's nuclear and solar build-out with US licensing times of 10 to 12 years ([Race for Super Intelligence](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/06/the-race-for-super-intelligence-will-decide-our-future-as-a-species-w/)).
