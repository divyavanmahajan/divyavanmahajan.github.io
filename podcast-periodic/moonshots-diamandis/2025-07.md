## Overview

July on Moonshots treated the AI race as an industrial contest: Grok 4 at the top of the rankings, data centres measured in gigawatts and huge pay for researchers. Eric Schmidt named electricity as the real limit, and the US AI Action Plan closed the month. Guests also debated Musk's exit from government, Bitcoin and new economic models.

## Main themes

### Grok 4 and the end of easy benchmarks

- Peter Diamandis noted that xAI reached the top model spot 28 months after its founding. Elon Musk claimed Grok 4 beats PhD level in every academic subject ([Grok 4](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ai-experts-react-elons-grok-4-is-now-1-in-ai-this-changes-everything-w/)).
- Emad Mostaque pointed to a 100% AIME score but judged that the model is “reasoning but it's not planning as yet”. He expects agents, interfaces and chip access to separate the labs next ([Grok 4](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ai-experts-react-elons-grok-4-is-now-1-in-ai-this-changes-everything-w/)).
- Alexander Wissner-Gross warned that “we're running out of evals that test frontier capabilities” and asked for benchmarks built on open problems. He expects 20% of hard mathematician-level problems to be solvable by year end ([AI Action Plan](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ai-experts-look-inside-americas-plan-to-win-the-global-ai-race-w-alexa/)).

### Electricity and data centres as the limit

- Eric Schmidt argued AI is underhyped and limited by electricity rather than chips. He cited a need for 92 more gigawatts, with small modular reactors not starting until 2030 ([Eric Schmidt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ex-google-ceo-what-artificial-superintelligence-will-actually-look-lik/)).
- Schmidt also said “the economics of these things are unproven”, given $50 billion of capital that depreciates over three to four years ([Eric Schmidt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ex-google-ceo-what-artificial-superintelligence-will-actually-look-lik/)).
- Diamandis described Meta's Prometheus and Hyperion sites, partly built in tents, and Sam Altman's claim of over a million GPUs by year end. Dave Blundin expects the data-centre buildout to reach a trillion dollars a year from 2029 ([AI Action Plan](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ai-experts-look-inside-americas-plan-to-win-the-global-ai-race-w-alexa/)).

### Startup speed and the talent war

- Blundin called 36 AI unicorns in the first half of 2025 the biggest peak of his lifetime. Diamandis said the median time to $1M of annual revenue fell from 16 months to 5, and Salim Ismail argued incumbents can only keep up at the edge of the business ([Millionaires](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ai-is-making-more-millionaires-than-anything-in-history-w-salim-ismail/)).
- Meta's superintelligence team reportedly earns $10-100 million a year. Blundin said defections are the only way Link Ventures deals have failed ([AI Action Plan](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ai-experts-look-inside-americas-plan-to-win-the-global-ai-race-w-alexa/)).

### Policy, Musk and Bitcoin

- Anthony Scaramucci argued Musk was hurt by being too visible around the president, while David Sacks works quietly. He also disagreed with the DOGE cuts to USAID on soft-power grounds ([Scaramucci](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/elons-departure-bitcoins-takeover-how-america-wins-in-the-ai-era-w-ant/)).
- Scaramucci, with about 70% of his net worth in Bitcoin, predicted $200,000 by year end and $1 million in ten years ([Scaramucci](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/elons-departure-bitcoins-takeover-how-america-wins-in-the-ai-era-w-ant/)).
- Wissner-Gross read the US AI Action Plan as the broadest industrial strategy since Eisenhower. Blundin named Taiwan's chip supply as the real weakness ([AI Action Plan](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ai-experts-look-inside-americas-plan-to-win-the-global-ai-race-w-alexa/)).

### New economics for an AI economy

- Mostaque argued labour is no longer the key driver of productivity. He proposed a Bitcoin-like coin funding open AI and research supercomputers, plus citizen culture coins in place of UBI ([Emad Mostaque](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/emad-mostaque-the-plan-to-save-humanity-from-ai-ep-184/)).
- Schmidt expects automation to raise wages and employment over five to ten years. Ismail rejected Vinod Khosla's forecast of 80% of jobs replaced by 2030 ([Eric Schmidt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ex-google-ceo-what-artificial-superintelligence-will-actually-look-lik/), [Millionaires](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2025/07/ai-is-making-more-millionaires-than-anything-in-history-w-salim-ismail/)).
