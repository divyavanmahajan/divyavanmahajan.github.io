## Overview

December on Moonshots split into two halves. Three guest interviews covered China's frugal approach to frontier models, open-source AI and a new way to organise work. Peter Diamandis and Salim Ismail then closed the year with three conversations on 2025 predictions, the Musk-OpenAI dispute and listener questions.

## Main themes

### Predictions for 2025: Bitcoin and space

- With Bitcoin at $107,000, Diamandis says it is his largest holding and predicts $300K by the end of 2025. Ismail frames it as a hedge against a weakening dollar ([What's Coming in 2025](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/whats-coming-in-2025-bitcoin-robots-space-exploration-the-ai-revolutio/)).
- In the year-end AMA Diamandis repeats the $300K call and prefers Bitcoin to index funds. Ismail argues that the most adaptable companies win, citing an ExO study of the Fortune 100 ([Bitcoin vs. Stocks](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/bitcoin-vs-stocks-whats-the-smarter-investment-ama-w-salim-ismail-ep-1/)).
- Diamandis expects both Starship stages to be caught in the first half of 2025. Because fuel is about half of 1% of launch cost, he says full reuse could cut costs a hundredfold ([What's Coming in 2025](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/whats-coming-in-2025-bitcoin-robots-space-exploration-the-ai-revolutio/)).
- Diamandis predicts a White House challenge to put boots on Mars by 2030, likely an Optimus robot. Ismail says Mars is “for the next hundred years or so definitely a one-way ticket” and would go to the Moon first ([Bitcoin vs. Stocks](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/bitcoin-vs-stocks-whats-the-smarter-investment-ama-w-salim-ismail-ep-1/)).

### Musk, OpenAI and the capital race for AGI

- Diamandis summarises OpenAI's published letter as saying Musk wanted a for-profit model and wanted to run it. Ismail calls it “just a defensive move” against a lawsuit now including Microsoft ([Elon vs. OpenAI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/elon-vs-openai-the-battle-over-for-profit-ai-w-salim-ismail-ep-138/)).
- The hosts treat the fight as one front in a race with hundreds of billions committed, citing SoftBank's $100 billion pledge and Oman's stake in xAI. Diamandis recounts xAI building a 100,000-H100 cluster in 122 days ([Elon vs. OpenAI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/elon-vs-openai-the-battle-over-for-profit-ai-w-salim-ismail-ep-138/)).
- Diamandis argues the race can be guided but not slowed. He says Musk's odds of AI going well moved from 80/20 to 90/10, and Ismail calls himself 99.9% optimistic ([Elon vs. OpenAI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/elon-vs-openai-the-battle-over-for-profit-ai-w-salim-ismail-ep-138/)).
- Both hosts expect AI to restructure healthcare, mainly by cutting administration and supporting diagnosis, and Ismail blames regulatory capture for slow change ([Elon vs. OpenAI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/elon-vs-openai-the-battle-over-for-profit-ai-w-salim-ismail-ep-138/), [Bitcoin vs. Stocks](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/bitcoin-vs-stocks-whats-the-smarter-investment-ama-w-salim-ismail-ep-1/)).

### Open source and US-China competition

- Kai-Fu Lee says he founded 01.AI partly because OpenAI was not available in China. He says 01.AI trained on only 2,000 GPUs and argues that falling inference cost, not bigger models, will unlock applications ([Kai-Fu Lee](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/ex-google-china-president-on-how-china-is-shaping-the-future-of-ai-w-k/)).
- Lee sees B2B tech becoming a parallel universe because of export controls, while open source and academic work remain areas where countries still collaborate ([Kai-Fu Lee](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/ex-google-china-president-on-how-china-is-shaping-the-future-of-ai-w-k/)).
- Mark Surman of Mozilla argues the core innovations of transformers and LLMs will become commoditized and should end up as shared public infrastructure. Diamandis plays the sceptic, citing SpaceX's speed ([Open Source](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/should-ai-be-open-sourced-the-debate-that-will-shape-everything-w-mark/)).
- Surman agrees on moving fast against China, but towards a set of players that supports democracy and pluralism rather than one Western owner ([Open Source](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/should-ai-be-open-sourced-the-debate-that-will-shape-everything-w-mark/)).

### Humanoid robots, jobs and work

- Diamandis counts “a good solid 100 well-funded humanoid robot companies” and puts a leased $30,000 robot at about $10 a day. Ismail expects liability and social friction to slow adoption at home ([What's Coming in 2025](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/whats-coming-in-2025-bitcoin-robots-space-exploration-the-ai-revolutio/)).
- Lee expects white-collar work to be disrupted within five years and robots to arrive more slowly; Diamandis, an investor in Figure, disagrees on the timeline ([Kai-Fu Lee](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/ex-google-china-president-on-how-china-is-shaping-the-future-of-ai-w-k/)).
- Jack Hidary and Nadia Harhen of SandboxAQ describe a third way between office and remote work: no permanent offices, outcome-based goals, frequent offsites and teams embedded with customers. Hidary predicts AI will create millions of net new jobs ([Returning to the Office](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2024/12/returning-to-the-office-wont-work-in-the-age-of-ai-jack-hidary-nadia-h/)).
