## Overview

October was largely an Abundance360 month, with conversations recorded at the March summit. Tony Robbins appeared twice, on the psychology of big goals and on generational cycles, and Roger Hamilton joined Peter Diamandis on mindset. will.i.am brought AI and creative ownership into the mix, and Jack Hidary made the case for quantum technologies that are useful now.

## Main themes

### Moonshots start with what and why

- Tony Robbins argues that a moonshot has to become a must rather than a should, and that people should start with what and why, not how. He sums up his approach as “Execution is power. Knowledge is potential power.” ([Tony Robbins](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/this-will-change-how-you-do-everything-w-tony-robbins-ep-69/)).
- Robbins describes a success cycle of potential, action, results and belief, where certainty drives massive action. He says he now runs 112 companies doing $7 billion in business ([Tony Robbins](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/this-will-change-how-you-do-everything-w-tony-robbins-ep-69/)).
- In the live Q&A he tells an aerospace engineer not to choose a moonshot while uncertain. His advice is to get into the right environments, set 10-year goals and break them into one-year chunks ([Live Q&A](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/tony-robbins-unveils-his-blueprint-for-a-brighter-future-live-q-a/)).

### Hunger and philanthropy at scale

- Robbins traces his food work to a stranger's Thanksgiving delivery when he was 11. With the UN's David Beasley he launched a 100 billion meal challenge, and says enough for 60 billion meals was raised and delivered in six months ([Tony Robbins](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/this-will-change-how-you-do-everything-w-tony-robbins-ep-69/)).
- He blames COVID policies, the Ukraine war and fertiliser shortages for 350 million people facing food insecurity, against a normal 80 million ([Tony Robbins](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/this-will-change-how-you-do-everything-w-tony-robbins-ep-69/)).
- An audience member proposed freeze-drying food to cut waste, and Robbins mentioned a Feeding America tracking programme he says could save about a billion meals ([Live Q&A](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/tony-robbins-unveils-his-blueprint-for-a-brighter-future-live-q-a/)).

### Mindset and generational seasons

- Diamandis and Roger Hamilton walk through seven mindsets: gratitude, curiosity, moonshot, abundance, exponential, purpose-driven and longevity. Diamandis says we are “constantly training our neuronet”, so the people and media around us shape how we think ([Roger Hamilton](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/how-to-reprogram-your-mind-to-become-successful-w-roger-hamilton-ep-67/)).
- Hamilton says Genius Group is near 5 million students and aims for 100 million by 2030, with GPT-4 linked to each student and credits replacing student debt ([Roger Hamilton](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/how-to-reprogram-your-mind-to-become-successful-w-roger-hamilton-ep-67/)).
- Robbins draws on Strauss and Howe to argue society is about halfway through a hard winter with perhaps eight years to go, and that hardship builds stronger people ([Live Q&A](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/tony-robbins-unveils-his-blueprint-for-a-brighter-future-live-q-a/)).

### AI, jobs and who owns your identity

- will.i.am argues people should own their facial data and vocal timbre before AI imitates them, and says there is no regulation yet protecting identity ([will.i.am](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/how-ai-will-change-the-music-industry-forever-w-will-i-am-ep-70/)).
- He presents FYI, a creative workspace running on GPT-4, and predicts drivers, legal assistants and finance managers will lose jobs within about ten years. Robbins also warns that self-driving vehicles could affect about five million US drivers ([will.i.am](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/how-ai-will-change-the-music-industry-forever-w-will-i-am-ep-70/), [Tony Robbins](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/this-will-change-how-you-do-everything-w-tony-robbins-ep-69/)).
- will.i.am names the digital divide and algorithmic bias as his short-term worries. His foundation backs FIRST robotics, which he says grew from three teams to 400 LA schools ([will.i.am](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/how-ai-will-change-the-music-industry-forever-w-will-i-am-ep-70/)).

### Quantum technologies now

- Jack Hidary of SandboxAQ argues quantum sensing, simulation and post-quantum security deliver value today, without waiting for fault-tolerant quantum computers ([Jack Hidary](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/the-technology-bigger-than-ai-w-jack-hidary-ep-68/)).
- He describes magnetic navigation as an alternative to jammed GPS and warns about store-now, decrypt-later attacks on legacy encryption ([Jack Hidary](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2023/10/the-technology-bigger-than-ai-w-jack-hidary-ep-68/)).
