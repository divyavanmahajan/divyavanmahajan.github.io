## Overview

June on Moonshots was Anthropic's month: it featured in all eight episodes, with new models, an IPO filing and a paper on AI building itself. The bigger story was the US government stepping into model releases, from a voluntary preview order to export-control suspensions and capped access lists. The Mates also argued over who should own the labs, AI personhood and compute in orbit.

## Main themes

### The government enters the release loop

- An executive order asks labs to share new models with the government 30 days before release. Dave Blundin calls it right for now but not a long-term fix ([EP #262](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/anthropic-files-965b-ipo-trump-signs-ai-executive-order-and-chatgpt-cr/)).
- An export-control directive led Anthropic to disable Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for everyone. Blundin says government control over releases is now permanent ([EP #265](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/spacex-ipos-at-2-89t-market-cap-us-govt-suspends-fable-mythos-5-altman/)).
- By month end GPT-5.6 was limited to 20 companies and Mythos 5 to 100. Blundin says “the real issue is the query, can you build yourself?” rather than hacking, and Emad Mostaque expects KYC and licence-style access ([#267](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/why-the-us-government-is-blocking-model-releases-gpt-5-6-267/)).

### Anthropic's month

- Peter Diamandis frames a two-lab monthly release race; Alexander Wissner-Gross calls Opus 4.8 an incremental update ([EP #260](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/opus-4-8-beats-gpt-5-5-the-220b-openai-foundation-and-hassabiss-2029-a/)).
- Anthropic's paper When AI Builds Itself claims Claude writes more than 80% of merged code. Wissner-Gross expects a soft takeoff; Salim Ismail doubts any lab will pause unilaterally ([EP #263](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/emerging-situation-anthropics-global-pause-recursive-self-improvement/)).
- Wissner-Gross says Anthropic is back in the lead with Fable 5, while Blundin notes the price doubled, so commodity intelligence “is not happening” ([EP #264](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/brian-armstrong-on-bitcoin-anthropic-drops-fable-5-mythos-5-newlimits/)). Polymarket gives a 60% chance its IPO passes $1.8 trillion on day one ([EP #262](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/anthropic-files-965b-ipo-trump-signs-ai-executive-order-and-chatgpt-cr/)).

### Who owns the labs

- Blundin dismisses a 50% stock levy as unworkable because the market lacks liquidity. Mostaque would rather put lab shares into children's investment accounts ([EP #262](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/anthropic-files-965b-ipo-trump-signs-ai-executive-order-and-chatgpt-cr/)).
- Diamandis expects a push towards 10% government ownership. Blundin calls it a dangerous precedent, and Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong says government should leave capital allocation to the private sector ([EP #264](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/brian-armstrong-on-bitcoin-anthropic-drops-fable-5-mythos-5-newlimits/)).
- The Mates reject taxing AI directly and favour a lower cost of living or a universal basic dividend ([EP #265](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/spacex-ipos-at-2-89t-market-cap-us-govt-suspends-fable-mythos-5-altman/)).

### AI personhood and AGI timelines

- Javier Milei's op-ed proposes no AI regulation and a nonhuman corporation category. Ismail predicts a jurisdictional race; Wissner-Gross calls it a large step for AI personhood ([EP #263](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/emerging-situation-anthropics-global-pause-recursive-self-improvement/)).
- Will Marshall calls for more safety and governance work on AI-run corporations ([EP #266](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/the-10b-satellite-empire-putting-ai-in-orbit-why-chips-beat-rockets-ch/)).
- Ray Kurzweil holds to AGI by 2029 but says AI still lacks an understanding of physics and robots must get cheap ([EP #261](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/why-agi-is-close-but-not-here-yet-ray-kurzweil-ep-261/)). Wissner-Gross argues some form of AGI has existed since about 2020 ([EP #260](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/opus-4-8-beats-gpt-5-5-the-220b-openai-foundation-and-hassabiss-2029-a/)).

### Orbital compute and open weights

- SpaceX opened at about $2.89 trillion in market cap, framed as launch, Starlink and orbital AI compute ([EP #265](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/spacex-ipos-at-2-89t-market-cap-us-govt-suspends-fable-mythos-5-altman/)). Google reportedly pays SpaceX $11 billion a year for GPU access ([EP #264](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/brian-armstrong-on-bitcoin-anthropic-drops-fable-5-mythos-5-newlimits/)).
- Planet CEO Will Marshall argues chip efficiency, not launch cost, will decide orbital data centres. Ismail says frontier intelligence can no longer be monopolised after Zhipu's GLM 5.2 ([EP #266](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/the-10b-satellite-empire-putting-ai-in-orbit-why-chips-beat-rockets-ch/)).
- Mostaque says a harness puts GLM 5.2 at the top of Frontier SWE, and Blundin concludes the government may be too late to gate access ([#267](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/why-the-us-government-is-blocking-model-releases-gpt-5-6-267/)).
