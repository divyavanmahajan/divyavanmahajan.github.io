## Overview

Moonshots spent 2026 tracking a frontier race in which Anthropic moved from challenger to the centre of the story, ending the year with a public listing. Peter Diamandis and the Moonshot Mates kept returning to AI and jobs, to compute and power as the real limits, and to SpaceX as an AI infrastructure company. From June onwards Washington, safety pauses and Chinese open-weight models took up more airtime. Throughout, the hosts argued that recursive self-improvement had already begun.

## Main themes

### Anthropic overtakes OpenAI

- The Mates credit enterprise demand, not consumer chat, for Anthropic's growth ([Anthropic vs. The Pentagon](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/anthropic-vs-the-pentagon-claude-outpaces-chatgpt-and-consulting-gets/)).
- Anthropic withheld Mythos in April ([SpaceX Goes Public](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/04/spacex-goes-public-claudes-mythos-release-and-the-us-data-center-delay/)) and took cash and chips from Google and Amazon for equity ([Google Invests $40B](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/google-invests-40b-into-anthropic-gpt-5-5-drops-and-google-cloud-domin/)).
- By November the hosts read the IPO as turning AI into a liquid asset class that will flood startups with capital ([Anthropic's $1.4T IPO](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/11/anthropics-1-4t-ipo-the-biggest-wealth-event-in-human-history/)).

### AI and white-collar jobs

- Elon Musk says AI can already do half or more of jobs that only involve bits ([Elon Musk on AGI Timeline](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/elon-musk-on-agi-timeline-us-vs-china-job-markets-clean-energy-humanoi/)).
- Tony Robbins treats job loss as a psychological shock ([Tony Robbins](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/tony-robbins-on-overcoming-job-loss-purposelessness-the-coming-ai-disr/)); Andrew Yang wants universal basic income before any universal high income ([Andrew Yang](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/andrew-yang-ubi-before-uhi-solving-job-loss-and-the-future-of-work-236/)).
- Later in the year the Mates argued layoffs mask a hiring freeze and a startup boom ([Pope Leo vs. AI](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/pope-leo-vs-ai-gpt-5-5-beats-claude-and-sam-altman-walks-back-job-apoc/)).

### Compute, power and SpaceX

- Anjney Midha calls energy the fundamental constraint ([AI Investor Panel](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/ai-investor-panel-how-will-we-fund-the-global-ai-revolution-ep-219/)); Ramez Naam narrows it to grid connection ([200GW Hiding in Grid](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/200gw-hiding-in-grid-sodium-batteries-10x-cheaper-wave-powered-datacen/)).
- Compute started trading like a financial asset ([Bernie Demands the Labs Stop](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/08/bernie-demands-the-labs-stop-wall-street-turns-gpus-into-bonds-grok-4/)).
- The SpaceX IPO filing read as a bid to become an AI infrastructure giant ([SpaceX' $75B+ IPO](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/05/spacex-75b-historic-ipo-gpt-5-5-outperforms-polymarket-and-ai-solves-8/)), and its October earnings call as the debut of a hyperscaler ([SpaceX's AI Business Up 830%](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/10/spacexs-ai-business-up-830-openai-agents-lie-and-startup-turns-mercury/)).

### Recursive self-improvement and AGI timelines

- Musk predicted AGI in 2026 and says of slowing AI: “I can either be a spectator or a participant but I can't stop it” ([Elon Musk on AGI Timeline](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/elon-musk-on-agi-timeline-us-vs-china-job-markets-clean-energy-humanoi/)).
- Alexander Wissner-Gross read Opus 4.6 as recursive self-improvement in production ([Opus 4.6 Tops Benchmarks](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/02/opus-4-6-tops-benchmarks-chatgpt-market-share-decline-and-the-privacy/)); Eric Schmidt said it was not here yet ([Eric Schmidt](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/03/eric-schmidt-singularitys-arrival-the-92-gigawatt-problem-and-recursiv/)).
- Ray Kurzweil held to AGI by 2029 ([Ray Kurzweil](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/why-agi-is-close-but-not-here-yet-ray-kurzweil-ep-261/)); by November Claude writing most of Anthropic's code was cited as proof ([Big Tech's $3T AI Bet](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/11/big-techs-3t-ai-bet-claude-writes-90-of-anthropics-code-ais-editing-ai/)).

### Washington, safety and open weights

- Wissner-Gross argues “almost every alignment or safety effort is actually a capabilities effort in a trench coat” ([The 2026 Timeline](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/01/the-2026-timeline-agi-arrival-safety-concerns-robotaxi-fleets-hypersca/)).
- A reported White House hold on releases ([Blocking Model Releases](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/why-the-us-government-is-blocking-model-releases-gpt-5-6-267/)) and Fable 5's return under safeguards ([Fable 5 Is Back](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/07/fable-5-is-back-govt-leashed-altman-offers-5-of-openai-ai-grows-consci/)) drew criticism as possible regulatory capture.
- Salim Ismail says “frontier intelligence cannot be monopolized anymore” ([The $10B Satellite Empire](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/06/the-10b-satellite-empire-putting-ai-in-orbit-why-chips-beat-rockets-ch/)), a view Kimi K3 reinforced ([AI Sputnik Moment](/podcasts/moonshots-diamandis/2026/07/urgent-update-ai-sputnik-moment-kimi-k3-released-w-emad-mostaque-ep-27/)).
