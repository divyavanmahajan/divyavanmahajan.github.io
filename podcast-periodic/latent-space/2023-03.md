## Overview

March 2023 on Latent Space moved between the economics of serving models and the practice of building products on them. Varun Mohan explained how cheap inference let Codeium go free, an emergency episode reacted to ChatGPT plugins on launch day, and two guests looked at data: Sarah Nagy on trustworthy natural-language queries and Shreya Shankar on why LLM pipelines repeat old MLOps problems.

## Main themes

### Inference efficiency and AI coding

- Mohan says a GPU usually serves one container and sits idle. Exafunction virtualises GPUs and adds compiler work such as operator fusion; the hosts cite a customer that went from thirty clients to one GPU and cut costs by 97% ([Codeium](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/97-cheaper-faster-better-correct-ai-with-varun-mohan-of-codeium/)).
- Codeium was not planned as free. Mohan says the team realised heavy optimisation made it affordable against Copilot's $10 a month, and reports over 10,000 users and 4-5% daily growth, all word of mouth ([Codeium](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/97-cheaper-faster-better-correct-ai-with-varun-mohan-of-codeium/)).
- He frames LLM products around “latency, quality, and correctability.” Multi-file PR generation is not shippable yet because the output is hard to correct, and user trust erodes easily ([Codeium](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/97-cheaper-faster-better-correct-ai-with-varun-mohan-of-codeium/)).
- Mohan also argues that compute-optimal training ignores serving, so code models are over-trained to stay small, and that HumanEval scores are inflated by contamination ([Codeium](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/97-cheaper-faster-better-correct-ai-with-varun-mohan-of-codeium/)).

### ChatGPT plugins as a platform

- swyx calls plugins “the App Store moment for AI” and notes a plugin is roughly 10 lines of JSON plus an OpenAPI spec ([Emergency Pod](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/emergency-pod-chatgpt-s-app-store-moment-w-openai-s-logan-kilpatrick-l/)).
- He reads Greg Brockman's FFmpeg demo as proof that ChatGPT is becoming a general compute platform, not just a chat app ([Emergency Pod](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/emergency-pod-chatgpt-s-app-store-moment-w-openai-s-logan-kilpatrick-l/)).
- Participants debate launch partners such as Expedia, Kayak and Zapier as presumptive winners, thin moats for standalone apps, and missing reviews, ratings and monetisation ([Emergency Pod](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/emergency-pod-chatgpt-s-app-store-moment-w-openai-s-logan-kilpatrick-l/)).
- Logan Kilpatrick says rollout is deliberately slow and that plugin descriptions sit in the context window, so more plugins leave less room for conversation ([Emergency Pod](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/emergency-pod-chatgpt-s-app-store-moment-w-openai-s-logan-kilpatrick-l/)).

### From MLOps to LLMOps

- Shankar's interview study found velocity, validation and versioning as recurring themes. She says “people are not doing data validation” and cannot explain performance drops ([Grounded Research](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/grounded-research-from-google-brain-to-mlops-to-llmops-with-shreya-sha/)).
- She describes a gap between development and production at every layer, from hardware to feature freshness to notebooks versus scheduled jobs ([Grounded Research](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/grounded-research-from-google-brain-to-mlops-to-llmops-with-shreya-sha/)).
- LLM pipelines she has seen combine changing prompts, database lookups and many output filters. She thinks relying on an API is often better than hosting a model ([Grounded Research](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/grounded-research-from-google-brain-to-mlops-to-llmops-with-shreya-sha/)).

### Accuracy-first data products

- Nagy built Seek.ai so business users can query company data in natural language, removing the queue of simple data requests ([Seek.ai](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/from-astrophysics-to-ai-building-the-future-ai-data-stack-with-sarah-n/)).
- She compares LLMs to an improv actor playing a doctor, believable but not always factual, so Seek adds guardrails and a review workflow with the data team ([Seek.ai](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/from-astrophysics-to-ai-building-the-future-ai-data-stack-with-sarah-n/)).
- Both Nagy and Shankar mention EleutherAI and expect enterprises to struggle with data when moving prototypes to production ([Seek.ai](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/from-astrophysics-to-ai-building-the-future-ai-data-stack-with-sarah-n/), [Grounded Research](/podcasts/latent-space/2023/03/grounded-research-from-google-brain-to-mlops-to-llmops-with-shreya-sha/)).
