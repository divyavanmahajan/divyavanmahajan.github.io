## Overview

March 2024 on Latent Space asked where the next gains in language models would come from: synthetic data, longer inference and cheaper serving. Two guests from big labs, Soumith Chintala of Meta and David Luan of Adept, gave their views on open models and enterprise agents. The month closed with Suno's approach to music generation and Microsoft's Sam Schillace on building reliable AI apps.

## Main themes

### Synthetic data and open models

- Soumith Chintala argues open models spread opportunity and stop power concentrating in two or three providers. He says open source loses on coordination, especially of human feedback, and proposes a central store for thumbs-up/down data ([Open Source AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/open-source-ai-is-ai-we-can-trust-with-soumith-chintala-of-meta-ai/)).
- Chintala says synthetic data “is not a magic wand” and works only where humans already hold good symbolic models, such as mathematics or physics. Distilling GPT-4, in his view, is closer to copying ([Open Source AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/open-source-ai-is-ai-we-can-trust-with-soumith-chintala-of-meta-ai/)).
- swyx ranks synthetic data second among five research directions, pointing to Apple's rephrasing of C4 and Meta's self-rewarding models, but questions how high the ceiling is ([Top 5 Research Trends](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/top-5-research-trends-openai-sora-google-gemini-groq-math-jan-feb-2024/)).

### Inference time and serving costs

- swyx puts long inference first: training runs can grow from months to years but not decades, so inference time is the remaining easy gain. Alessio Fanelli adds that this shifts cost to the customer ([Top 5 Research Trends](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/top-5-research-trends-openai-sora-google-gemini-groq-math-jan-feb-2024/)).
- The hosts estimate Llama 2 on Groq needs 576 cards costing about $2.3 million, against $300,000 for an eight-card H100 system. Alessio says that at 50% utilisation “you would be much better off running on Nvidia” ([Top 5 Research Trends](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/top-5-research-trends-openai-sora-google-gemini-groq-math-jan-feb-2024/)).
- Chintala expects inference margins to fall to the minimum as kernel tricks spread within a month or two. He cites Meta's target of 600K H100 equivalents and says time between model generations, not GPUs, is the constraint ([Open Source AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/open-source-ai-is-ai-we-can-trust-with-soumith-chintala-of-meta-ai/)).
- Gemini 1.5 Pro's 1 million token multimodal context raised doubts about the need for sub-quadratic architectures such as RWKV and Mamba ([Top 5 Research Trends](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/top-5-research-trends-openai-sora-google-gemini-groq-math-jan-feb-2024/)).

### Agents for the enterprise

- David Luan argues Google missed GPT-3 because it spread compute thinly; a large job meant persuading 19 or 20 colleagues to give up their chips ([Adept](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/why-google-failed-to-make-gpt-3-why-multimodal-agents-are-the-path-to/)).
- Luan says many agent demos work about 60% of the time while enterprises need several nines. Adept sells no APIs or open models, and was sold out of onboarding capacity for Q1 ([Adept](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/why-google-failed-to-make-gpt-3-why-multimodal-agents-are-the-path-to/)).
- He expects agent interfaces to sit above the GUI, as the GUI came to sit above the command line, and wants multimodal models trained on charts, invoices and UIs ([Adept](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/why-google-failed-to-make-gpt-3-why-multimodal-agents-are-the-path-to/)).
- Sam Schillace's advice is to let the model do the fuzzy thinking and keep the plan in code. His Textbook Factory, about 200 lines of Python and six prompts, builds a full-year textbook ([World's Fair](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/presenting-the-ai-engineer-world-s-fair-with-sam-schillace-deputy-cto/)).

### Generating music

- Mikey Shulman says audio is roughly one to two years behind images and text. Suno predicts the next audio token like a language model, with as little built-in music knowledge as possible ([Suno](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/making-transformers-sing-with-mikey-shulman-of-suno/)).
- He keeps models small so music streams as fast as people listen, and wants music to become active and social, noting gaming is about 50 times larger than the music industry ([Suno](/podcasts/latent-space/2024/03/making-transformers-sing-with-mikey-shulman-of-suno/)).
