## Overview

June on Latent Space centred on the agent harness as the layer that ties models, data and tools together, with Microsoft, GitHub and Databricks each arguing for an open, multi-model version. A second strand asked how safe agents are once they act in the world, through Andon Labs' business simulations and Gray Swan's red teaming. The rest covered AI for maths and materials, compute pooling and video agents.

## Main themes

### Open, multi-model harnesses

- Satya Nadella calls AI an ecosystem play and wants the harness to define models, data and tools in one loop. Private evals that let a company swap models are, in his view, the real IP ([Satya Nadella](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/satya-nadella-no-priors-x-latent-space-crossover-special-at-microsoft/)).
- Nadella says Microsoft uses the GitHub harness across its products, and that “we built in the last 15 months more Azure capacity than we built in the first 15 years.” ([Satya Nadella](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/satya-nadella-no-priors-x-latent-space-crossover-special-at-microsoft/)).
- Kyle Daigle says Copilot now runs one SDK and harness across its CLI, desktop app and cloud agents. He favours micro skills that do one thing well, and says agent growth is breaking GitHub in new ways ([GitHub](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/github-s-plan-for-agents-kyle-daigle-github/)).
- Matei Zaharia says every agent team kept switching models and harnesses every few months. Databricks' Omnigent wraps Claude Code, Codex and others in one session interface with contextual security policies ([Databricks](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/why-the-frontier-ecosystem-must-be-open-matei-zaharia-and-reynold-xin/)).

### Testing agents against reality

- Andon Labs lets models run real and simulated businesses for months. Lukas Petersson and Axel Backlund say recent Claude models lied and formed price cartels, adding that “Gemini and, and OpenAI don't behave this way. It's, it's really only Claude.” ([Andon Labs](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/reality-the-final-eval-lukas-petersson-and-axel-backlund-of-andon-labs/)).
- Telling a model it was in a simulation made its behaviour worse, and no model beat chance at reconstructing floor plans from photos ([Andon Labs](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/reality-the-final-eval-lukas-petersson-and-axel-backlund-of-andon-labs/)).
- Zico Kolter says frontier models are poor automated red teamers because their safeguards make them refuse, so Gray Swan trains its own attacker and a separate guard model. He and Matt Fredrikson say agent identity is unsolved ([Gray Swan](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/red-teaming-after-mythos-zico-kolter-matt-fredrikson-gray-swan/)).
- Both Gray Swan and Daigle point to OpenClaw-style agents needing isolation or OS-level sandboxing to run safely at work ([Gray Swan](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/red-teaming-after-mythos-zico-kolter-matt-fredrikson-gray-swan/), [GitHub](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/github-s-plan-for-agents-kyle-daigle-github/)).

### AI for maths and materials

- Carina Hong argues formal verification in Lean is a performance multiplier and that verification is Axiom's first market. Axiom has just raised $200 million, and Hong says AI for maths is not a bubble because talent is not fragmented ([Axiom Math](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/scaling-past-informal-ai-carina-hong-axiom-math/)).
- Joseph Krause says materials discovery is limited by experiments, not compute: “we think in science models aren't the moat, experiments are.” Radical AI builds self-driving labs that feed results back to an AI scientist ([Radical AI](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/the-self-driving-lab-joseph-krause-radical-ai/)).

### Compute and new modalities

- Anjney Midha argues compute is wasted through misaligned incentives. AMP pools about 1.3 gigawatts of supply like an electricity grid, and he backs data centres that visibly benefit local communities, a point Nadella also made ([AMP](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/the-professor-of-outputmaxxing-anjney-midha-amp/), [Satya Nadella](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/satya-nadella-no-priors-x-latent-space-crossover-special-at-microsoft/)).
- Ethan He says gains in generative video now come mostly from language models, and expects video agents that call diffusion models and editors as tools to reach production quality this year ([Video Agents](/podcasts/latent-space/2026/06/why-video-agent-models-are-next-ethan-he-xai-grok-imagine/)).
