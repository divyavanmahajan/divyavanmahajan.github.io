## Overview

May on Latent Space looked at agents that run for longer and do more of the work on their own. Factory's founders argued for delegating whole coding tasks, and Will Brown explained how multi-turn reinforcement learning could train agents for multi-hour tasks. CloudChef brought the same idea into a commercial kitchen, and Jo Bergum made the case that vector databases are fading as a separate category.

## Main themes

### From collaboration to delegation in coding

- Matan Grinberg and Eno Reyes argue the IDE was built for humans writing every line, and that agents need an interface built around delegation. They say the product experience of delegation is still immature, but enterprises see it as the holy grail ([AI Coding Factory](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/the-ai-coding-factory/)).
- They expect humans to keep the outer loop of planning, review and testing, while agents take over writing code. In their view, “The promise of test-driven development is gonna finally be delivered” in this setting ([AI Coding Factory](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/the-ai-coding-factory/)).
- Factory's droids cover technical writing, everyday coding and incident response. They draw context from the tools a new engineer would use, such as Slack, Linear, Sentry and PagerDuty ([AI Coding Factory](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/the-ai-coding-factory/)).
- The founders target codebases 30 or more years old. One large public company cut a migration from four months to about three and a half days ([AI Coding Factory](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/the-ai-coding-factory/)).

### Training agents for long tasks

- Will Brown, now leading reasoning research at Prime Intellect, sees Claude 4 as steady, linear progress with an emphasis on coding and longer runs. He welcomes Anthropic's reported drop in over-eager behaviour from about 45% to 15% ([Multi-Turn RL](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/aiewf-preview-multi-turn-rl-for-multi-hour-agents-with-will-brown-prim/)).
- His paper found that small models avoid tools or make dummy searches. Treating each turn as an action and crediting turns separately fixed this ([Multi-Turn RL](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/aiewf-preview-multi-turn-rl-for-multi-hour-agents-with-will-brown-prim/)).
- Evaluation came up in both agent episodes. Brown prefers LLM-judged rewards over brittle parsers, while Factory no longer competes on SWE-bench and notes that Sonnet 3.7's trained tool preferences can fight better custom tools ([Multi-Turn RL](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/aiewf-preview-multi-turn-rl-for-multi-hour-agents-with-will-brown-prim/), [AI Coding Factory](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/the-ai-coding-factory/)).

### Robots in the kitchen

- Nikhil of CloudChef says his robots now do line cooking for Michelin-star chefs, fresh fast-food restaurants and airline caterers. He calls the skill of judging browning and heat “culinary intelligence”, possible only since multimodal models improved ([CloudChef](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/aiewf-preview-cloudchef-your-robot-chef-michellin-star-food-at-12-hr-w/)).
- CloudChef buys general-purpose robots and only solves problems that can be modelled as software. Learning from a single demonstration works because the pipeline is several subsystems, not one end-to-end model ([CloudChef](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/aiewf-preview-cloudchef-your-robot-chef-michellin-star-food-at-12-hr-w/)).
- Restaurants rent the robot at $12 an hour, which Nikhil puts at about 40% of a loaded human worker. High staff turnover and little spare capex make rental fit the industry ([CloudChef](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/aiewf-preview-cloudchef-your-robot-chef-michellin-star-food-at-12-hr-w/)).

### Search over vector databases

- Jo Bergum argues vector search is now a feature of most databases and search engines, so a separate vector database category no longer makes sense. He says this is not a claim that RAG is dying ([Vector DB](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/the-rise-and-fall-of-the-vector-db-category/)).
- He recommends starting with clean data and a BM25 baseline, then hybrid search, then re-ranking if latency and cost allow. Long context does not remove retrieval for large collections ([Vector DB](/podcasts/latent-space/2025/05/the-rise-and-fall-of-the-vector-db-category/)).
