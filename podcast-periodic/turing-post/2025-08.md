## Overview

August on Turing Post brought four conversations with builders, all released on the same day. They covered the layers around the model: websites that serve agents, retrieval and hallucination checks, protocols that let agents act, and the cost of inference. The shared view was that models are capable enough and the surrounding systems decide what works.

## Main themes

### Building for agents as users

- Linda Tong says bots are a growing share of web traffic and expects them to be the majority by the end of the year. She advises against blocking good bots, since LLMs must index a site to reference it ([Webflow](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/ai-changed-the-web-here-s-how-to-build-for-it-a-conversation-with-lind/)).
- She sees answer-engine optimisation (AEO) as extra work on top of SEO, weighting freshness, authenticity and readable structure, with each model weighting these differently ([Webflow](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/ai-changed-the-web-here-s-how-to-build-for-it-a-conversation-with-lind/)).
- Tong treats today's MCP servers as a midterm fix and suggests sites publish a manifest of allowed actions and guardrails. She expects humans to stay in the loop through hybrid interfaces ([Webflow](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/ai-changed-the-web-here-s-how-to-build-for-it-a-conversation-with-lind/)).

### Context, protocols and MCP

- Alex Hancock argues models are already very smart and the hard problem is giving them the right context. MCP, in his telling, gives a brain in a jar arms and legs ([Block Goose](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/how-to-make-ai-actually-do-things-alex-hancock-block-goose-mcp-steerin/)).
- He lists the MCP work streams: SDKs keeping up with the spec, a global server registry, an agents working group and long-running async tool calls. Checking whether a server is trustworthy is still unsolved ([Block Goose](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/how-to-make-ai-actually-do-things-alex-hancock-block-goose-mcp-steerin/)).
- Hancock sees MCP and A2A as mostly complementary, and calls agentic coding the biggest productivity jump of his 15-year career ([Block Goose](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/how-to-make-ai-actually-do-things-alex-hancock-block-goose-mcp-steerin/)).

### Retrieval and hallucination control

- Amr Awadallah argues RAG is far from dead. LLMs struggle to find several facts in long prompts, context cost grows with the square of input length, and retrieval scales logarithmically ([Vectara](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/beyond-the-hype-what-silicon-valley-gets-wrong-about-rag-amr-awadallah/)).
- He treats access control before retrieval as a security measure, since data filtered out never reaches the model, while fine-tuned or in-context data can leak ([Vectara](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/beyond-the-hype-what-silicon-valley-gets-wrong-about-rag-amr-awadallah/)).
- Grounded hallucination fell from about 10-20% to around 1% for top models, which he still calls dangerous for diagnosis or anti-money-laundering work. Vectara builds guardian agents that detect and correct errors ([Vectara](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/beyond-the-hype-what-silicon-valley-gets-wrong-about-rag-amr-awadallah/)).

### The cost of inference

- Lin Qiao says GenAI startups can find product-market fit and still go broke, with waiting lists of millions held back by GPU costs. She expects far more apps if costs fall tenfold ([Fireworks AI](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/when-will-inference-feel-like-electricity-lin-qiao-co-founder-ceo-of-f/)).
- She blames the gap between labs' training data and each product's own data for shortfalls in accuracy, latency and cost. Fireworks' 3D optimizer tunes quality, speed and cost together ([Fireworks AI](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/when-will-inference-feel-like-electricity-lin-qiao-co-founder-ceo-of-f/)).
- Qiao sees open text models converging, with Chinese labs such as DeepSeek and Qwen innovating on fewer GPUs, and expects 100 times more efficient infrastructure over time ([Fireworks AI](/podcasts/turing-post/2025/08/when-will-inference-feel-like-electricity-lin-qiao-co-founder-ceo-of-f/)).
