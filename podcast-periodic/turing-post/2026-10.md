## Overview

October on Turing Post was about what agents need to finish real work: fast small decisions, business context and a well-built environment around the model. Ksenia Se tested TypeSafe's Jev and OpenAI's Dots herself, interviewed Salesforce's Rohan Kumar and Peter Norvig, and used her weekly digest to track world models and robots.

## Main themes

### Small, calibrated decisions for agents

- Se tested Jev, TypeSafe AI's first System One model, which returns yes/no probabilities, choices or scores instead of free text ([Is Jev What AI Has Been Missing?](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/is-jev-what-ai-has-been-missing-i-tested-it/)).
- Against Codex, Jev answered in about 0.2 seconds versus almost five and used 616 input tokens against nearly 16,000. Roughly 70,000 tokens cost her less than a cent ([Is Jev What AI Has Been Missing?](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/is-jev-what-ai-has-been-missing-i-tested-it/)).
- She frames TypeSafe's bet plainly: “A lot of useful AI should barely have anything to say.” Calibration lets software set thresholds for escalating to a person ([Is Jev What AI Has Been Missing?](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/is-jev-what-ai-has-been-missing-i-tested-it/)).
- The weekly digest noted Jev's reported 70-500 millisecond decisions alongside the robotics news ([AI That Acts](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/ai-that-acts-odyssey-3-helix-2-5-jev-deepmind-s-new-institute/)).

### Enterprise agents need business context

- Rohan Kumar says Salesforce's enterprise AI harness goes well beyond a runtime and golden-set evals: “There has to be a continuous scanning that happens because you need an enterprise-wide registry” ([Rohan Kumar](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/salesforce-s-rohan-kumar-on-the-unsolved-problem-holding-ai-agents-bac/)).
- He calls business context the unsolved part, since companies rarely record why decisions are made. He favours a curated context layer and price-performance model routing ([Rohan Kumar](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/salesforce-s-rohan-kumar-on-the-unsolved-problem-holding-ai-agents-bac/)).
- At Dreamforce, Silvio Savarese described self-evolving models that update their weights while running, trained together with a harness ([AI That Acts](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/ai-that-acts-odyssey-3-helix-2-5-jev-deepmind-s-new-institute/)).

### The always-on assistant

- Se set up an OpenAI dot running on GPT-6 Astra with its own cloud computer. Within three hours it helped her reply to 15 waiting emails and archive more than 2,000 ([OpenAI Dots](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/i-tried-openai-dots-how-much-can-i-hand-over/)).
- She argues the value lies in the environment, permissions and connections around the model, which is also what could lock users in ([OpenAI Dots](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/i-tried-openai-dots-how-much-can-i-hand-over/)).

### World models, from robots to theory

- Figure took Helix 2.5 into 30 unfamiliar homes zero-shot, and Odyssey-3 showed recovery behaviours not present in its demonstrations ([AI That Acts](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/ai-that-acts-odyssey-3-helix-2-5-jev-deepmind-s-new-institute/)).
- Peter Norvig agrees with Yann LeCun on what models should improve but sees language models as world models already, needing small tweaks rather than a rebuild ([Peter Norvig](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/peter-norvig-disagrees-with-yann-lecun-here-s-why/)).
- Se welcomed the new DeepMind Institute, led by Demis Hassabis, Shane Legg and James Manyika, which expects its contributors to disagree ([AI That Acts](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/ai-that-acts-odyssey-3-helix-2-5-jev-deepmind-s-new-institute/)).

### Education after AI

- Norvig says of programming courses, “if you look at the assignments that were given out, like, three years ago, AI could do every one of them perfectly” ([Peter Norvig](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/peter-norvig-disagrees-with-yann-lecun-here-s-why/)).
- He urges CS departments to optimise for minors rather than majors and suggests problem-setting and oral exams. Kumar tells juniors that analytical thinking and judgment still matter ([Peter Norvig](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/peter-norvig-disagrees-with-yann-lecun-here-s-why/), [Rohan Kumar](/podcasts/turing-post/2026/10/salesforce-s-rohan-kumar-on-the-unsolved-problem-holding-ai-agents-bac/)).
