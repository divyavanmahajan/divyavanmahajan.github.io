## Overview

The Cognitive Revolution spent 2024 on two tracks. One was policy and risk: AGI timelines, California's SB 1047, US-China rivalry and safety tests of frontier models. The other was practical: founders explaining how they build reliable products with retrieval, fine-tuning and evals. Nathan Labenz's position, adopt today's AI quickly but scale more cautiously, ran through the year.

## Main themes

### AGI timelines and race dynamics

- Shane Legg sees no fundamental walls: “There's a 50% chance it could happen by 2028” ([Shane Legg](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/01/2028-agi-new-architectures-and-aligning-superhuman-models-with-shane-l/)).
- Katja Grace's survey of 2,700+ researchers shows timelines shortening, while Robin Hanson argues automation advances steadily, not explosively ([Katja Grace](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/03/surveying-2-700-ai-researchers-on-the-industry-s-future-with-katja-gra/), [Robin Hanson](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/02/is-agi-far-with-robin-hanson-economist-at-george-mason-university/)).
- Flo Crivello puts AGI about five years out and says “we don't need China, though, to be in an AI arms race” ([Feeling the AGI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/06/feeling-the-agi-with-flo-crivello/)). OpenAI's roon argues the good guys must build it first ([roon's Heroic Duty](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/12/roon-s-heroic-duty-will-the-good-guys-build-agi-first-from-doom-debate/)).

### SB 1047 and how to regulate frontier AI

- Dean Ball calls model-layer rules “like regulating metallurgy rather than missiles” ([Emergency Pod: SB 1047](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/emergency-pod-examining-sb-1047-california-s-proposed-ai-legislation/)); Senator Scott Wiener presents the bill as light-touch safety testing ([Scott Wiener](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/05/governing-frontier-ai-with-ca-senator-scott-wiener-author-of-sb-1047/)).
- Martin Casado wants AI regulated like other software, not by compute thresholds ([Martin Casado](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/06/ai-safety-regulations-prudent-or-paranoid-with-a16z-s-martin-casado/)).
- After the veto, Ball and Daniel Kokotajlo agree on lab transparency and whistleblower protections ([Lab Transparency](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/11/agi-lab-transparency-requirements-whistleblower-protections-with-dean/)).

### US-China competition and chips

- A Beijing-based tech worker expects export controls to bite within two to three years ([View From Beijing](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/04/inside-china-s-ai-ecosystem-a-view-from-beijing/)).
- Samo Burja urges treating China as an economic competitor and avoiding a military race ([Samo Burja](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/09/ai-live-players-the-geopolitics-strategic-dynamics-of-ai-with-samo-bur/)).
- In November, Samuel Hammond, Joshua Steinman and Robert Wright debated AI policy under Trump and whether an arms race is a choice ([Hammond](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/11/ai-under-trump-the-stakes-of-2024-w-samuel-hammond-pt-1-of-2/), [Steinman](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/11/ai-under-trump-the-stakes-of-2024-w-joshua-steinman-pt-2-of-2/), [Robert Wright](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/11/is-an-ai-arms-race-inevitable-with-robert-wright-of-nonzero-newsletter/)).

### Looking inside models, and catching them scheming

- The Bau Lab and Goodfire treat interpretability as an engineering discipline ([Opening AI's Black Box](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/04/opening-ai-s-black-box-with-prof-david-bau-koyena-pal-and-eric-todd-of/), [Goodfire](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/08/popular-mechanistic-interpretability-goodfire-lights-the-way-to-ai-saf/)).
- Apollo Research found o1 capable of simple in-context scheming, then showed frontier models scheming against users ([Red Teaming o1](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/09/red-teaming-o1-part-2-2-detecting-deception-with-marius-hobbhahn-of-ap/), [o1 Schemes](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/12/emergency-pod-o1-schemes-against-users-with-alexander-meinke-from-apol/)).
- Dan Hendrycks argues safety will come from stacked imperfect safeguards ([Dan Hendrycks](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/10/gelu-mmlu-x-risk-defense-in-depth-with-the-great-dan-hendrycks/)).

### Building reliable products

- Pablo Arredondo anchors GPT-4 in search over real case law ([CoCounsel](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/03/ai-and-the-practice-of-law-from-casetext-to-cocounsel-with-pablo-arred/)); Andrew Lee relies on a tuned RAG pipeline ([Shortwave](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/04/the-ai-email-assistant-i-ve-been-waiting-for-with-andrew-lee-of-shortw/)).
- Lin Qiao says fine-tuning is one of Fireworks' fastest-growing features ([Fireworks AI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/04/ai-inference-good-fast-and-cheap-with-lin-qiao-dmytro-ivchenko-of-fire/)).
