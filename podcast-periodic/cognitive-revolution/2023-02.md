## Overview

The Cognitive Revolution's first month set out the show's premise: AI is a historic shift, with a large upside and a real safety risk. Around that framing, founders from Playground AI and Replika explained how they build consumer products, and a panel debated whether the 2023 moment was real or another hype cycle.

## Main themes

### A new force, and a real risk

- In the introductory episode, Nathan Labenz tells Erik Torenberg that AI is closer to the industrial revolution than to mobile phones. He describes it as “the rise of a new force in the world—a kind of alien intelligence” ([Upside and Downside](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-upside-and-downside-case-for-ai-with-nathan-labenz-and-erik-torenb/)).
- Labenz argues the deep research is done and productization is just starting, so even without new breakthroughs most of life will change ([Upside and Downside](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-upside-and-downside-case-for-ai-with-nathan-labenz-and-erik-torenb/)).
- On safety he uses an invasive-species analogy, says feedback training can reward deception, and compares watching for AI risk to watching for asteroids ([Upside and Downside](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-upside-and-downside-case-for-ai-with-nathan-labenz-and-erik-torenb/)).
- Antonio Garcia Martinez dismisses the Yudkowsky view of doom, while Amjad Masad sees LLMs as a powerful new type of computer rather than agents with goals ([The AI Moment](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-ai-moment-with-amjad-masad-flo-crivello-antonio-garcia-martinez-an/)).

### Coding and knowledge work

- Masad says writing English now creates real software, which he expects to be deflationary and to bring back end-user programming. He would still teach his kids to code, because reading and debugging matter more ([The AI Moment](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-ai-moment-with-amjad-masad-flo-crivello-antonio-garcia-martinez-an/)).
- Flo Crivello says “the 10x engineer is going to become a 100x engineer”, and describes the knowledge worker as a function between keyboard and monitor that AI will approximate ([The AI Moment](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-ai-moment-with-amjad-masad-flo-crivello-antonio-garcia-martinez-an/)).
- Crivello argues cheaper content makes distribution more valuable. Masad is bullish on infrastructure and dev tools rather than AI-first companies ([The AI Moment](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-ai-moment-with-amjad-masad-flo-crivello-antonio-garcia-martinez-an/)).

### AI art and defensible image products

- Suhail Doshi sees prompts as a temporary workaround and wants Playground to be a pro creative tool that can create, edit and understand pixels ([Playground AI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-pixel-revolution-with-playground-ai-s-suhail-doshi/)).
- Doshi says traditional artists feel deeply threatened, and compares AI to drum machines and DAWs. He believes human plus machine gives the best result for art ([Playground AI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-pixel-revolution-with-playground-ai-s-suhail-doshi/)).
- On defensibility, he expects public training data to run out, doubts fine-tuning protects anyone, and bets on a product-users-data-models flywheel fed by a generous free tier ([Playground AI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/the-pixel-revolution-with-playground-ai-s-suhail-doshi/)).

### Conversation as the product

- Eugenia Kuyda says most companies use conversational AI as a means to an end, while Replika treats conversation as the product, measured by long-term happiness rather than engagement ([Replika](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/creating-compassionate-ai-with-replika-s-eugenia-kuyda/)).
- She says about 40% of users want a romantic partner and the largest group is 18 to 25. Replika nudges users to take a break after 50 messages ([Replika](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/creating-compassionate-ai-with-replika-s-eugenia-kuyda/)).
- Kuyda defends restricting erotic roleplay: no platform pressured Replika, she wants to stay PG-13, and she accepts a small revenue dip ([Replika](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2023/02/creating-compassionate-ai-with-replika-s-eugenia-kuyda/)).
