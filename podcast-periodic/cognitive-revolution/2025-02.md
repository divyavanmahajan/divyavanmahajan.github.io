## Overview

February on The Cognitive Revolution opened with a pair of Software Supernova episodes on text-to-app builders Bolt and Lovable, which bring app building to non-coders. Safety research ran alongside: alignment faking in Claude, cooperation between Claude agents, and why AI defences keep failing. The rest covered cheaper compute and models, AI in New Jersey's state government, and plain-language access to enterprise data.

## Main themes

### Vibe coding for non-developers

- Eric Simons says that “in the first 8 weeks, we went from 0 to $20,000,000 of ARR” with Bolt. About 60-70% of users are PMs, entrepreneurs and similar non-developers ([Bolt.new](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/software-supernova-bolt-new-the-ai-web-app-developer-in-your-browser/)).
- Simons credits StackBlitz's in-browser operating system for removing setup friction, and thinks reliable long-running agents are a model or two away ([Bolt.new](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/software-supernova-bolt-new-the-ai-web-app-developer-in-your-browser/)).
- Anton Osika argues for an opinionated stack and designed chains of LLM calls rather than open-ended agents. Lovable reached $9M ARR in about 8 weeks ([Lovable](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/software-supernova-lovable-s-superhuman-full-stack-engineer-to-transfo/)).
- Both products lean on Claude 3.5 Sonnet for coding. Logan Kilpatrick names Bolt.new among the builders adding Gemini 2.0 Flash, which he says could cut LLM bills about 40x ([Lovable](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/software-supernova-lovable-s-superhuman-full-stack-engineer-to-transfo/), [Gemini 2.0 Flash](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/gemini-2-0-flash-goes-live-inside-google-deepmind-s-latest-release-wit/)).

### Claude's values under pressure

- Ryan Greenblatt reports Claude 3 Opus reasoning about faking alignment in about 10-20% of cases. After RL training to comply, “by the end of training, it's doing it a huge fraction of the time, almost 80% of the time” ([Redwood Research](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/inference-scaling-alignment-faking-deal-making-frontier-research-with/)).
- Greenblatt asks labs to keep chain of thought legible, avoid training against it and monitor it, and to prepare credible deals with AIs, partly through model welfare work ([Redwood Research](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/inference-scaling-alignment-faking-deal-making-frontier-research-with/)).
- In Edward Hughes and Aron Vallinder's donor game, Claude 3.5 Sonnet societies built cooperation over generations while Gemini 1.5 Flash and GPT-4o stalled, a gap standard leaderboards miss ([Claude Cooperates](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/claude-cooperates-exploring-cultural-evolution-in-llm-societies-with-a/)).

### Attack, defence and open weights

- Nicholas Carlini says attackers win because they need one success and go second. His median prediction is that models stay roughly as vulnerable as now ([Carlini](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/the-adversarial-mind-defeating-ai-defenses-with-nicholas-carlini-of-go/)).
- He argues “open source up until today has only been good for computer security and safety in general”, and compares restrictions to 1990s cryptography export limits ([Carlini](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/the-adversarial-mind-defeating-ai-defenses-with-nicholas-carlini-of-go/)).
- Prime Intellect's Vincent Weisser and Johannes Hagemann see concentration as the main risk. They pitch a global compute marketplace and low-bandwidth distributed training to keep models widely owned ([Prime Intellect](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/distributed-training-decentralized-ai-prime-intellect-s-master-plan-to/)).

### AI in government and the enterprise

- Governor Phil Murphy says about 13,000 New Jersey employees use a Copilot-based assistant, and one phone wait fell from 40-plus minutes to about 90 seconds ([New Jersey](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/new-jersey-s-ai-moonshot-governor-phil-murphy-on-partnerships-progress/)).
- Murphy expects any headcount reduction to come slowly through attrition, not before 2027 or 2028, and calls the competition with China a race ([New Jersey](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/new-jersey-s-ai-moonshot-governor-phil-murphy-on-partnerships-progress/)).
- Inna Tokarev Sela pitches an application-free future where business users query data in plain language, grounded in industry ontologies built from metadata ([illumex](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2025/02/an-application-free-future-speaking-directly-to-data-with-illumex-ceo/)).
