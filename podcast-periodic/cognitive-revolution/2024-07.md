## Overview

July on The Cognitive Revolution was a technical month. Two episodes took stock of prompt engineering, and others covered the ARC Prize, state space models and hippocampus-inspired retrieval. Safety came in through a proposal for verifiable guarantees and Nathan Labenz's own overview on another show, while Runway and Chef Robotics showed AI at work in finance and food production.

## Main themes

### Prompting becomes engineering

- Sander Schulhoff's Prompt Report maps the field and gives six few-shot rules; exemplar order alone can swing accuracy from 0% to 90% ([Prompt Report](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/delving-into-the-prompt-report-with-sander-schulhoff-of-learnprompting/)).
- Schulhoff admits DSPy beat his own prompt: “So I spent 20 hours developing this prompt for a binary classification task, and I was pretty happy with it.” ([Prompt Report](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/delving-into-the-prompt-report-with-sander-schulhoff-of-learnprompting/)).
- Riley Goodside says prompting now looks more like programming than poetry. He moves from instructions to k-shot examples to retrieval, and fine-tunes last ([Riley Goodside](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/from-poetry-to-programming-the-evolution-of-prompt-engineering-with-ri/)).
- Labenz suggests trying DSPy on Ryan Greenblatt's ARC solution ([ARC Prize](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/the-arc-prize-efficiency-intuition-and-agi-with-mike-knoop-co-founder/)).

### Measuring general intelligence by efficiency

- Mike Knoop defines AGI as efficient skill acquisition on novel tasks. He says the economic-work definition fails because “narrow AI that we already have today can accomplish that goal” ([ARC Prize](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/the-arc-prize-efficiency-intuition-and-agi-with-mike-knoop-co-founder/)).
- The $1 million prize runs on Kaggle with a P100 for 12 hours and no internet. Knoop says ARC progress had slowed since 2019 while other benchmarks saturate ([ARC Prize](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/the-arc-prize-efficiency-intuition-and-agi-with-mike-knoop-co-founder/)).
- Labenz estimates about a cent of compute per puzzle, roughly 10,000 times less than the public leaderboard. He worries such a lean system may have no room for values ([ARC Prize](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/the-arc-prize-efficiency-intuition-and-agi-with-mike-knoop-co-founder/)).

### Memory and architectures beyond attention

- Albert Gu explains how Mamba compresses history into a fixed-size state, and why Mamba 2 trades some flexibility for GPU speed. He sees attention and SSMs as complementary ([Albert Gu](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/the-state-space-model-revolution-with-albert-gu/)).
- Bernal Jiménez Gutiérrez's HippoRAG builds a knowledge graph ahead of time, matching iterative retrieval at about a tenth of the online cost. He sees formal logic as the biggest gap in LLMs ([HippoRAG](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/long-term-memory-for-llms-with-hipporag-author-bernal-jim-nez-gutierre/)).

### Safety by engineering, and a slower scale-up

- Nora Ammann and Ben Goldhaber propose gating AI actions through a world model, a safety specification and a verifier, starting with narrow domains such as power grids ([Guaranteed Safe AI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/guaranteed-safe-ai-world-models-safety-specs-verifiers-with-nora-amman/)).
- On the Nick Halaris Show, Labenz calls himself an adoption accelerationist and hyperscaling pauser. He would spend $100 billion on applying AI to medicine, law and shipping rather than on one data center ([Zooming Out](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/zooming-out-on-ai-from-the-nick-halaris-show/)).
- Labenz explains how RLHF could teach models the gap between truth and what earns a high human score ([Zooming Out](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/zooming-out-on-ai-from-the-nick-halaris-show/)).

### AI inside real businesses

- Siqi Chen argues for ambient intelligence in Runway's finance platform: background explanations rather than chat. The LLM guesses which drivers to change and Runway's engine does the maths ([Runway](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/building-an-intelligent-business-os-with-runway-ceo-siqi-chen/)).
- Rajat Bhageria says Chef Robotics has made 18 million servings and grew revenue 4x from 2022 to 2023. He bets on single-purpose robots over humanoids ([Chef Robotics](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2024/07/a-foundation-model-for-food-with-chef-robotics-ceo-rajat-bhageria/)).
