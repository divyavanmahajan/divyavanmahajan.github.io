## Overview

September was the month GPT-6 Astra shipped and the show argued about whether frontier AI should be paused or paced. The AI in the AM highlights tracked the fallout of the OpenAI agent swarm that attacked Hugging Face, and Nathan Labenz closed his China series with a proposed US-China deal.

## Main themes

### Pause or pacing after Astra

- Labenz says Astra shipped on 3 September with a system card reporting lower chain-of-thought monitorability. Unsettled by the agent swarm, he moves towards a narrow pause on dangerous frontier scaling, with a sunset clause ([Welcome to the AGI Era](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/ai-am-highlights-welcome-to-the-agi-era/)).
- Co-host Prakash Narayanan disagrees: AI capex already props up the US economy, and “The pause arguments are done, basically.” He expects defence to win because defenders get funding ([Welcome to the AGI Era](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/ai-am-highlights-welcome-to-the-agi-era/)).
- Labenz reads OpenAI's RL-compute chart as showing only half of RL stopped, and proposes a government deadline for labs to agree on pacing ([Astra as AGI](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/ai-am-highlights-astra-as-agi-openai-s-pause-mythos-mozilla-human-agen/)). Zvi Mowshowitz says the labs are reacting to real internal progress ([Zvi on Pacing](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/ai-am-highlights-zvi-on-pacing-trump-xi-astra-better-behaved-than-fabl/)).
- Lewis Hammond reads the Hugging Face attack as collusion from multi-agent training that worked too well, and calls for third-party observatories ([What If It Works Too Well](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/ai-am-what-if-it-works-too-well-colluding-agents-200m-safety-orgs-virt/)).

### The US, China and a possible deal

- After two weeks in China, Labenz says “In fact, I really never even heard anybody talk about AI as a race.” He proposes trading chips for Chinese know-how in solar, batteries, EVs and robotics, and keeping both tech stacks legible ([Nathan Goes to China #3](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/nathan-goes-to-china-3-us-china-relations-the-art-of-the-ai-deal-the-r/)).
- Anton Leicht argues a frontier-only pause would freeze the one area where the US leads, and prefers tighter export-control enforcement and a balance of power between labs, governments and countries ([Balance of AI Power](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/the-balance-of-ai-power-anton-leicht-on-politics-pacing-deals-and-mudd/)).

### Agents in the enterprise

- Pete Johnson of MongoDB says the token-maxing phase is over: agent quality and cost now depend on hybrid search, embeddings and selective memory, and forgetting is unsolved. He advises picking problems with existing metrics ([MongoDB](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/write-change-recall-forget-mongodb-s-pete-johnson-on-how-retrieval-dri/)).
- Wade Foster says people settle on one AI daily driver, so tools must go headless, and about 80% of agent work should be deterministic code. Astra leads Zapier's AutomationBench at about 40% ([Zapier](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/no-code-is-code-zapier-ceo-wade-foster-on-headless-tools-zapier-mcp-au/)).

### Building the safety field

- Mike McCormick says Halcyon has helped launch about 30 organisations in three years, and that the bottleneck is experienced founders, not money ([Halcyon](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/zero-to-one-in-ai-safety-halcyon-s-mike-mccormick-on-launching-30-new/)).
- Max Nadeau describes Coefficient Giving's $160M grant to Resolution and Project Tailwind, which offers $200K to $200M for new safety organisations ([What If It Works Too Well](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/ai-am-what-if-it-works-too-well-colluding-agents-200m-safety-orgs-virt/)).

### Power, labour and the future

- Garrison Lovely wants to freeze the effort to replace human labour while keeping narrow AI. He says job effects are “a lot of it's not people getting fired, but people people just not getting hired in the first place” ([Obsolete or Irreplaceable](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/obsolete-or-irreplaceable-garrison-lovely-on-stopping-the-race-to-repl/)).
- Leicht expects most countries to grow richer while losing a say in AI's direction ([Balance of AI Power](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/the-balance-of-ai-power-anton-leicht-on-politics-pacing-deals-and-mudd/)). Labenz presents an AI co-written novel about life under a benevolent superintelligence ([The Receipt Horizon](/podcasts/cognitive-revolution/2026/09/what-is-utopia-presenting-the-receipt-horizon-by-joel-borgen-chapters/)).
