## Thesis

Valter Longo, director of the USC Longevity Institute, argues that periodic fasting cycles could become a mainstream health intervention if they are made safe and easy enough for most people to follow. That is why his lab developed the fasting-mimicking diet (FMD), which is licensed commercially as ProLon by L-Nutra. Peter Diamandis, who says he uses the product, presents it as an accessible longevity practice. Longo also cautions that radical lifespan extension within the next decade is wishful thinking.

## Key points

- **Why a product rather than water fasting.** Longo says a USC cancer trial that started around 2010 found that most patients and oncologists would not accept water-only fasting. He estimates that only about 1% of people could do it. That led to a five-day, plant-based, low-protein plan of 1,100 calories on day one and 800 on days two to five.
- **Evidence base.** Diamandis cites about 21 completed ProLon clinical studies, and Longo says roughly 30 more are running or about to start. Longo reports diabetes trials (Heidelberg, Leiden) with monthly cycles, and he says 15-20% of patients became diabetes-free within a year. He also describes a 500-patient trial in southern Italy that tests a cycle every three months.
- **Comparison with water fasting.** Longo says that in mouse studies of inflammatory bowel disease, water-only fasting achieved about half the effect of the FMD. He attributes the difference partly to the prebiotic ingredients in the FMD.
- **Regeneration claims.** Longo describes mouse work in which FMD cycles were followed by insulin production in a damaged pancreas, and he links this to developmental gene programmes and Yamanaka factors. He says half his group now works on stem-cell activation.
- **Timelines and frequency.** Longo thinks adding 20-30 healthy years is achievable within about 30 years, citing slow progress on ALS since the 1990s as a caution. For healthy people he recommends three or four FMD cycles a year, and he says about 60% of measured changes are still present after 90 days.
- **Everyday guidance.** Longo favours a 12-13 hour daily fast over skipping breakfast, and suggests far less protein than Diamandis's one gram per pound.
