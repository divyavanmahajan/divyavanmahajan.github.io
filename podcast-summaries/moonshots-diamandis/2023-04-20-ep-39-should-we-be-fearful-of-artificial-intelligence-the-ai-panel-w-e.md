## Thesis

In an Abundance360 Q&A, Peter Diamandis puts audience questions to Emad Mostaque (Stability AI), Alexandr Wang (Scale AI) and Andrew Ng (AI Fund, Coursera). The panel agrees that AI is moving faster than governments and schools can absorb. They argue that companies should start now, that data is the main moat, and that labs building powerful models owe the public transparency about how they are governed.

## Key points

- **Data is the moat.** Wang tells two physicians that being first to market lets you collect more data than anyone else and stay ahead. His advice is to catalogue your data, upload it into one tool, label it and train a custom model.
- **What AI won't disrupt.** Mostaque says nothing is safe in the long run. Wang argues that physical work such as construction and mining is far from being disrupted. Ng jokes that a robotics professor told him his own haircut could be done by a robot.
- **Chief AI officer.** Ng says he coined the term in a Harvard Business Review piece. In his view the role has to be technical enough to make buy-versus-build calls and business-minded enough to find use cases.
- **Ethics and OpenAI.** Wang praises OpenAI's deployment as responsible. Ng argues that Stable Diffusion created far more value than harm. Mostaque wants OpenAI to be more transparent about governance, given its own statement that the technology could be existential.
- **Healthcare models.** For a hospital chatbot, Mostaque points to open models such as GPT-NeoX and Flan-T5, with StableLM coming. Wang argues that health data with PHI needs a model you control.
- **Schools and money.** Ng says openly disclosed ChatGPT use is not cheating, but he worries it could become a crutch. Mostaque argues school should not be a contest. He compares AI's investment wave to a bubble and predicts it will create wealth faster than anything before.
