## Thesis

Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin and Alex Wissner-Gross review a week of AI news and argue that the frontier labs are racing to own both content creation and commerce. Wissner-Gross reads benchmarks like GDPval as a sign that most knowledge work could be done at superhuman level within 6 to 12 months. The group sees compute, energy and supply chains as the real limits.

## Key points

- **Generated feeds.** Meta's Vibes (built with Midjourney and Black Forest Labs) and OpenAI's Sora 2 lead Wissner-Gross to describe a shift from algorithmic content selection to generation. Diamandis stresses that both are free, and Suno's latest model prompts claims that a musical Turing test has been passed.
- **Anthropic's coding focus.** Wissner-Gross says Claude Sonnet 4.5 reportedly working for 30-plus hours points to hyper-exponential growth in autonomy. "Imagine with Claude", which writes code on each user click, suggests to him that eventually every pixel will be generated.
- **Ads and commerce.** Blundin expects Google's roughly $300 billion ad business to move to AI conversations, and warns that data-centre costs push labs toward aggressive ads. He and Diamandis debate whether personal AIs will pick products on merit or keep margins flowing to brands.
- **Benchmarks as policy tools.** The hosts discuss GDPval (44 jobs, nine industries) and Mercor's APEX. Blundin says good benchmarks make AI's urgency easier to explain to government, and that every startup should create one.
- **Compute and finance.** They discuss Sam Altman's call for a gigawatt of new AI capacity per week, Stargate's five new sites and a reported $100 billion Nvidia chip-leasing deal. Blundin notes that Altman has far less cash than Meta or Google.
- **Energy, robots, longevity.** Ismail shows forecasts that kept missing solar's exponential growth. Diamandis points to Chinese robot exports to Poland and Mexico. Blundin says the US lacks China's component supply chain. Diamandis closes with longevity companies such as Retro Biosciences.
