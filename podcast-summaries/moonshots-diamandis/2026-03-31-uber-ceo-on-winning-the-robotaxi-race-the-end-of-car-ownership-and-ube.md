## Thesis

Recorded on stage at the 2026 Abundance360 Summit, Dara Khosrowshahi tells Peter Diamandis and Salim Ismail that Uber will win the robotaxi race by partnering rather than building. He expects a long hybrid period in which human and robot drivers share city fleets, with Uber supplying demand, data and fleet services, and calls autonomy another trillion-dollar marketplace.

## Key points

- **Focus as turnaround.** Diamandis notes Uber went from losing about $4.5 billion a year to earning over $10 billion. Khosrowshahi credits a friend's advice that strategy is as much about what you choose not to do, which is why he sold Uber Elevate to Joby.
- **Partners, not a vertical stack.** Uber has over 20 autonomy partners, including Waymo in Austin and Atlanta, Nvidia, WeRide, Pony and Wayve, plus Lucid and Nuro for vehicles. Khosrowshahi aims for 15 cities by year end and the most robotaxi rides in the world by 2029, and says he would welcome Teslas once a camera-only approach proves safe.
- **Asset-light fleets.** He compares the future model to Marriott: Uber operates, cleans and repairs fleets while financial players such as Blackstone own the cars for yield.
- **Slow fleet turnover.** He expects every new car to be autonomous-ready within 10 years, but with US cars lasting over 10 years and many developing markets, the shift to 50% autonomous will take much longer. He sees the Middle East as the most open region, with WeRide already running in Abu Dhabi and Joby air taxi trips planned there.
- **Delivery and drivers.** Uber is pairing drones for suburbs with sidewalk robots such as Coco for short urban trips. Khosrowshahi says about 20% of drivers leave each year, so Uber slows recruitment where robotaxis launch, and Austin and Atlanta are growing faster than average.
- **Work as a platform.** Answering Ismail, he describes Uber AI Solutions (data labelling, model evaluation) and plans to let drivers become fleet owners.
