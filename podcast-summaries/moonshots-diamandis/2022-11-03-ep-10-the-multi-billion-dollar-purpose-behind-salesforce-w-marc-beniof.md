## Thesis

In a conversation recorded at Abundance360 in January 2021, Salesforce co-founder Marc Benioff tells Peter Diamandis that a company should manage for all stakeholders, not just shareholders. He describes the planning method he uses each year to turn a vision and a ranked set of values into budgets, obstacles and measures. He argues that the same method applies to Salesforce, to his philanthropy and to personal life.

## Key points

- **Three founding bets.** Benioff says Salesforce started with a new technology model (the cloud), a new business model (subscription instead of licensing) and the 1-1-1 model: 1% of equity, profit and employee time going to Salesforce.org. He says that work has produced 5 million volunteer hours and serves 50,000 nonprofits for free.
- **The V2MOM method.** Benioff's steps are: start with a beginner's mind and decide what you want, rank your values, define methods and their costs, list obstacles, and set measures. Salesforce's values, in order, are trust, customer success, innovation and equality.
- **Homelessness and climate.** Benioff describes backing San Francisco's Proposition C, which he says raises $300 million a year for homelessness services, plus a $35 million gift to UCSF. On climate he cites net zero commitments, the 1 trillion trees effort and river machines that catch ocean plastic.
- **Cloud and remote work.** Benioff says Time magazine produced its first issue from home in 100 years, and Salesforce's 54,000 staff work fully remotely. He calls this "cloud three" and presents the Slack acquisition as a way to make Salesforce products work-from-home first.
- **Mental health and reskilling.** Benioff says a third of employees reported mental health problems during the pandemic. Salesforce responded with Trailhead for reskilling and the Be Well Together series.
- **Investing themes.** Asked by an audience member where to invest, Benioff names digital health, space (SpaceX, Swarm, Planet Labs, Astra) and biodiversity, with Windward's illegal-fishing tracking as one example.
