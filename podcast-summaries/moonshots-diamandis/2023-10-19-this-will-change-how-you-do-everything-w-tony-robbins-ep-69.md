## Thesis

Recorded at Peter Diamandis's Abundance360 summit in March 2023, this conversation has Tony Robbins arguing that big goals fail when people start by asking "how". He says a strong enough "what" and "why" creates certainty, certainty drives massive action, and early results build momentum. Diamandis uses Robbins's own philanthropic moonshots as worked examples.

## Key points

- **From two families to 100 billion meals.** Robbins traces his food work back to a stranger's Thanksgiving delivery when he was 11. He says he fed two families at 17, later finished a billion-meal challenge, and then launched a 100 billion meal challenge with the UN's Governor Beasley. He claims 5 billion meals were pledged in an hour at a Forbes philanthropy dinner and enough for 60 billion delivered in six months.
- **The success cycle.** Robbins describes potential, action, results and belief as a loop. In his telling, uncertainty leads to "trying" and a downward spiral, while certainty leads to massive action and an upward one. His advice is to build certainty by rehearsing the result vividly in your mind.
- **Matching funds and identity.** Robbins says he asks the organisations he backs for matching funds. He argues that lasting change is a change in identity, from needing help to giving it. He cites water projects in India and 71 million of a planned 100 million trees with Trees for the Future.
- **Jobs and the transition.** Robbins says self-driving vehicles alone could affect about five million US drivers. He expects more jobs in the long run but warns that the speed of change could cause short-term disruption. He argues any UBI should be tied to some form of work.
- **Health business model.** The two discuss Fountain Life, which they co-founded, and its health insurance idea: pay for diagnostics up front instead of paying for disease later. Diamandis frames this as flipping the insurance model.
- **Energy bet.** Robbins describes a technology he is validating that strips carbon from hydrocarbons before burning and yields hydrogen and graphite. He claims power plants could be converted in under 12 months.
