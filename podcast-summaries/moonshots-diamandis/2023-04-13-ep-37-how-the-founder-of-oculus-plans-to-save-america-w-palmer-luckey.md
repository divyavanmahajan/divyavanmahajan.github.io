## Thesis

Recorded on stage at Abundance360, Palmer Luckey argues that the US tech sector had wrongly assumed large-scale conflict was over. He founded Anduril to build defense products with its own money and sell them to the government only once they work. He describes the company as primarily an AI and autonomy business. He remains a VR believer, and he thinks the remaining gains will come from engineering and marketing, not new inventions.

## Key points

- **Selling Oculus.** Luckey says he turned down Mark Zuckerberg's first $1 billion offer at age 20. The final deal was $2.3 billion plus $700 million in performance bonuses, and he says Facebook's commitment to spend billions a year on VR mattered more than the price.
- **Why defense.** Luckey says big tech firms were dependent on China and refused Pentagon work, incumbent primes were weak in AI and sensor fusion, and startups could not raise money to sell to the government. He criticises cost-plus contracting for rewarding slow, expensive work.
- **Anduril's products.** He says two-thirds of staff work on software, built around Lattice, an AI sensor-fusion platform. Hardware includes robotic submarines that dive below 6,000 m, counter-drone and targeting drones, and autonomous underground vehicles. He also pitches a humanoid-robot interface for operating legacy weapon systems.
- **VR roadmap.** Luckey predicts that VR will replace the sense of vision within 10 years. He points to pancake optics in the Bigscreen headset, which he says weighs about a fifth as much as the Quest Pro, and expects Apple to make VR fashionable through marketing.
- **Neural interfaces.** He expects future AR to work through the peripheral nervous system rather than vision, citing BrainPort and median-nerve cuff electrodes. He calls his VR headset that kills the player on in-game death a performance-art piece.
- **Other bets.** He says quantum computing is too far off to matter for now, but quantum communication interests him. He also sees practical uses for de-extinction, which he has discussed with Colossal.
