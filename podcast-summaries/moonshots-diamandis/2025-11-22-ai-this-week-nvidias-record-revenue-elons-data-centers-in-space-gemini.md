## Thesis

Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin and Alexander Wissner-Gross read NVIDIA's record quarter, new compute alliances and Elon Musk's plan for solar-powered data centres in orbit as signs that power is now the main limit on AI. Wissner-Gross argues the spending holds up only if AI revenue keeps growing, and the hosts expect a mix of vertically integrated players rather than one dominant chip supplier.

## Key points

- **NVIDIA's quarter.** Diamandis cites $57 billion in revenue, up 62% year on year, and guidance of $65 billion. Blundin says NVIDIA is only halfway through redesigning chips for AI. Wissner-Gross expects TPUs, AMD and inference ASICs to break up CUDA's lead.
- **New alliances.** Anthropic is to spend $30 billion on Azure, while Microsoft and NVIDIA invest up to $15 billion in it. One of the Mates predicts an Anthropic-Microsoft merger. Wissner-Gross sees Anthropic becoming one of a few vertically integrated frontier labs.
- **Sovereign compute.** xAI will be the first customer of an NVIDIA-backed data centre in Saudi Arabia. Wissner-Gross calls it a sovereign stack that will later take in humanoid robots. Blundin says Saudi is more an investment vehicle and test bed, and that the big data centres will go to Texas and then space.
- **Data centres in orbit.** Musk talks of launching 100 gigawatts of solar-powered AI satellites a year. Wissner-Gross argues radiating heat towards the cold sky makes cooling workable. Blundin notes the first H100 in orbit is cooled with aluminium only.
- **Energy gap.** A chart shows China far ahead of the US in new electricity generation. Blundin expects a power shortfall between 2030 and 2035 before fusion arrives. Diamandis highlights X-energy's TRISO pebble fuel plant and Google's $40 billion Texas investment.
- **Robots and drones.** Zipline plans to build 20,000 drones a year. Sunday Robotics trains robots using data from gloves worn by humans. Wissner-Gross expects Gemini 3 Pro's lead over radiology residents to mean radiology is solved within a year.
