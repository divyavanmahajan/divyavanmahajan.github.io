## Thesis

In a New Year X Spaces conversation, Peter Diamandis and Elon Musk argue that the world is getting better, even though negative news drowns this out. Musk calls abundance of goods and services the most likely outcome, but warns against complacency. He names two risks: a falling birth rate, and AI that absorbs an anti-human, extinctionist view. His proposed safeguard is AI that is maximally truth-seeking and curious.

## Key points

- **Negativity bias.** Both say evolution wired people to react more to danger than to reward, and that news media exploit this. Musk says he does not watch network news or read newspapers. Diamandis cites global extreme poverty falling from 90% in the 1800s to under 10% today.
- **Energy and batteries.** Musk says Tesla's analysis shows that solar plus lithium-ion batteries could power all of Earth's industry, heating and transport without running short of materials. He also says battery buffering could raise grid output two to three times, because grids are sized for peak demand.
- **Connectivity and Neuralink.** Musk says the direct-to-cell Starlink satellites had just launched and still had to prove they work. He expected the first human Neuralink implant within a month or two, with "Blindsight" as a later product.
- **Longevity and population.** Diamandis pushes for a 120-150 year healthspan. Musk worries about societal ossification, but argues that aging is synchronised across cells and so may not be that hard to solve. He says the danger is population collapse, not overpopulation.
- **Regulation.** Musk describes laws accumulating until everything is illegal. His example is a Department of Justice suit against SpaceX over hiring practices that he says ITAR forced on the company.
- **AI scale and safety.** Musk estimates AI compute is growing about tenfold every six months. He warns that programming an explicit morality risks the "Waluigi problem" and says xAI aims to build AI that is truth-seeking and curious. Diamandis gives an update on the carbon removal XPRIZE that Musk funded.
