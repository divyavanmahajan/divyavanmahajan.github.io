## Thesis

Peter Diamandis and Salim Ismail set out their predictions for 2025, arguing that technology is in a phase of "hyper exponential" growth. Diamandis is the more aggressive forecaster on Bitcoin, model IQ and robots. Ismail adds caveats: AGI will arrive unnoticed, and in-home robots will take longer than people hope.

## Key points

- **Bitcoin.** With Bitcoin at $107,000, Diamandis says it is his largest holding and predicts $300K by the end of 2025. He told 200 Fortune 1000 CFOs that a small share of corporate treasuries moving in would push it to $1 million a coin. Ismail frames it as a hedge against a dollar he says loses about 14% a year.
- **Space.** Diamandis expects both Starship stages to be caught in the first half of 2025. He says fuel is about half of 1% of launch cost, so full reuse could cut costs a hundredfold. He again predicts a White House challenge to reach Mars this decade, likely with an Optimus robot.
- **Model intelligence.** Diamandis cites IQ scores of 101 for Claude 3 and 120 for OpenAI's o1. He predicts that Grok 3 reaches about 140, and expects GPT-5 and a Claude 4 in 2025. Ismail predicts that within 18 months an AI will be self-aware or simulate it convincingly, citing Hod Lipson's approach, and expects HeyGen-style avatars to attend Zoom calls.
- **Humanoid robots.** Diamandis counts about 100 well-funded humanoid companies. He says a $30,000 robot works out to about $10 a day, and that Musk and Brett Adcock both forecast 10 billion humanoids by 2040. Ismail expects liability and social friction to slow adoption at home, and questions copying the human form at all.
- **Autonomous cars.** Ismail says Waymo has crossed 100,000 rides a week, and argues that driving costs should fall from about a dollar a mile to a few cents.
- **Brains.** Diamandis points to the fruit-fly connectome (154,000 neurons) being simulated in silico, and to Max Hodak's Science as a BCI company to watch.
