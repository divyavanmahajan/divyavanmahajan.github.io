## Thesis

Peter Diamandis and the Mates frame the week as a fight over open versus closed AI. Alexander Wissner-Gross and Salim Ismail argue that open weights strengthen the wider ecosystem and that regulation should target what AI does, not how capable it is. Dave Blundin is more sympathetic to Dario Amodei's biosecurity argument, but most of the panel reads the joint OpenAI-Anthropic lobbying as at least partly a bid for a regulatory moat.

## Key points

- **Jensen vs Dario.** Diamandis summarises Jensen Huang's letter, which he says 77 companies signed, launching an "open secure AI alliance". He sets it against Dario's three proposals: chip export controls, a crackdown on distillation and safety testing for all powerful models. Blundin believes Dario speaks without an agenda. Wissner-Gross sees Nvidia trying to commoditise the model layer and rejects biosafety as a reason to restrict open models.
- **Lobbying in DC.** Diamandis reports that OpenAI and Anthropic are pushing for a federal review process and a voluntary 30-day government look before releasing high-risk models. Ismail calls it regulatory capture. Blundin disagrees, saying both CEOs are trying to make the world safe.
- **Kimi K3 and Opus 5.** Blundin calls the Kimi K3 open-weights release a turning point and says he had it running on Modal within an hour. Wissner-Gross points to its lack of position embeddings. He calls Claude Opus 5 strong on ARC-AGI-3 (30.2%) but suspects some benchmark-maxing, and still prefers Fable 5.
- **AI as statecraft.** Citing a Financial Times report on China exporting AI to the Global South, Ismail warns that a restrictive US open-model policy would hand that ecosystem to China. Wissner-Gross argues countries should aim to train models domestically.
- **Starship, BCIs and money.** The hosts cover Starship 13's soft splashdown, Science Corporation's Prima retinal implant (a Diamandis portfolio company) and Neuralink's mind-controlled wheelchair. They also discuss Elon Musk's claim that money will not matter by 2036. Wissner-Gross guesses that full demonetisation is about 30 years away.
