## Thesis

In an ask-me-anything session with listeners, Peter Diamandis argues that mindset is the deciding asset for entrepreneurs and that exponential technologies, especially AI, will reshape every profession, organisation and school. He frames humanity's challenges as interrelated and urges each person to attack the one they are most passionate about.

## Key points

- **AI co-pilots.** Asked about GPT-3, Diamandis predicts that every profession will have an AI co-pilot, a phrase he credits to Reid Hoffman. He gives the example of a designer chaining GPT-3 and Stable Diffusion into photorealistic room designs. He suggests diagnosing without AI in the loop will become malpractice.
- **Space.** He says spaceflight on Virgin Galactic, Blue Origin and SpaceX is in a "barnstorming" phase, as aviation was before Lindbergh in 1927. He calls Starship the first vehicle designed to land on the Moon and Mars. He expects people to live in lava tubes on the Moon and in domed cities on Mars, with robots preparing habitats first.
- **Metaverse.** Diamandis sees the metaverse as a convergence of better headsets, real-time generative AI (citing Emad Mostaque's Stability AI) and crypto for exchanging value. His sons, he notes, already value Robux over dollars.
- **Threats.** His concerns include worse pandemics than COVID-19, asteroid impacts without adequate early warning, and extremism and isolation, which he links to a negative news cycle.
- **Education and organisations.** He argues schools should teach how to learn. He wants his kids to find their passion, ask great questions and build grit. He describes exponential organisations as flatter, driven by an MTP and data-led rather than opinion-led.
- **Health tech.** He predicts AI will recommend diets from blood chemistry. For depression, beyond antidepressants, he points to VR therapy, brain-computer interfaces and AI-discovered drugs.
