## Thesis

Emad Mostaque, former CEO of Stability AI and founder of Intelligent Internet, argues that AI breaks the link between labour and capital, so money and infrastructure must be redesigned. His plan pairs a Bitcoin-like coin, whose sales fund open AI and research supercomputers, with small transparent models for regulated sectors. Diamandis, Blundin and Ismail broadly endorse the vision and press him on rollout, trust and timelines.

## Key points

- **Compute becomes capital.** Mostaque says value will come from "wrapped compute", not labour. He predicts AI agents will transact with each other faster than current systems can follow, and that a Fed rate cut will soon lead firms to hire GPUs and robots instead of people.
- **Foundation coin and proof of benefit.** He describes a coin built on about 99% Bitcoin code with a 21 million supply. National champions mine it by providing free "universal basic AI", and 100% of sales go to supercomputers for cancer, autism, multiple sclerosis and longevity.
- **Small open models.** Mostaque says his 8-billion-parameter II Medical model runs on a Raspberry Pi and scores 47% on OpenAI's HealthBench, against 15% for human doctors. He wants regulated-sector AI trained on fully open data. Blundin argues such models ease a shortage: about 20 million GPUs shared among 8 billion people.
- **Culture coins instead of UBI.** Mostaque argues UBI cannot work as AI erodes the tax base. Instead, citizens would mint national "culture coins" pegged to the foundation coin, earning more for socially positive AI use.
- **Jobs and GDP.** He expects any keyboard-video-mouse job outside the public sector to be replaceable within a few years. Ismail adds that automating driving and similar work would shrink GDP and force a new economic model.
- **Resilience.** Mostaque warns of a "Stuxnet" for frontier models causing mode collapse across identical systems, and argues a diverse mix of AIs is safer.
