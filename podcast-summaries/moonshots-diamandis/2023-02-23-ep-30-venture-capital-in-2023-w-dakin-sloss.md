## Thesis

Dakin Sloss, founder and general partner of Prime Movers Lab (PML), argues that founders should pursue breakthrough science that can affect billions of lives, but must pitch it as a business rather than a science project. Peter Diamandis draws out his advice for first-time founders: lead with the customer, build skills on simpler companies first or partner with a seasoned entrepreneur, and survive long enough for timing to turn in your favour.

## Key points

- **The fund.** Sloss says PML manages about $1.2 billion, has backed roughly 40 companies out of 6,000-8,000 seen, and concentrates most capital in about 15. It only leads deals and says other firms' interest has no sway on its decisions.
- **Four lenses.** Sloss assesses team, technology, business and deal. A 10x fund target means each bet must plausibly reach $10 billion-plus. PML prefers to enter at the shift from science risk to engineering risk, and does the deep technology diligence last because it takes the most time.
- **Pitching advice.** Sloss tells founders to treat the investor as a customer, ask questions first, show demos and credible order books, and keep pitches conversational. He says PML has done zero deals from cold emails, and never invests without meeting in person.
- **Valuations in 2023.** Sloss cites seed rounds at roughly $10-20 million and Series A at $25-60 million. He says Series B/C valuations are down 70-80%, Series A 40% and seed 20%, and predicts many new funds will shut down. Diamandis advises founders to raise enough cash to survive two or three years.
- **Portfolio math.** From PML's 2018 fund of 15 seed deals, Sloss says about half are zeros, and three of the five follow-on companies are breaking out, a ratio he calls unusually good.
- **Portfolio examples.** Sloss describes Bill Gross companies (Heliogen, Energy Vault, a direct-air-capture firm boosted by the Inflation Reduction Act), Greg Wyler's ventures, Paradromics, Gilgamesh, Axiom Space, Focused Energy, Commonwealth Fusion, Venus and Boom Supersonic. For his XPRIZE, he would choose raising consciousness.
