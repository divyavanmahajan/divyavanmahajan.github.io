## Thesis

In a weekend "emergency" episode, Peter Diamandis, Dave Blundin, Alexander Wissner-Gross and guest Emad Mostaque discuss reports that the US executive branch is now gating who gets the newest models from OpenAI and Anthropic. They broadly expect gating, licensing and KYC to become normal, but question whether it can work when harnesses around older models and Chinese open-weight models are close behind.

## Key points

- **Government in the release loop.** Diamandis reports that GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, Luna) was limited to 20 companies and Anthropic's Mythos 5 to 100. Blundin calls control inevitable and says the real worry is a model helping someone rebuild itself, not hacking.
- **Harnesses and open weights.** Mostaque says Intelligent Internet's harness puts GLM 5.2 at the top of Frontier SWE. Blundin concludes the government may already be "too late". Wissner-Gross cites a chart projecting the Chinese open-weight lag reaching zero by Christmas.
- **Licensing scenarios.** Mostaque expects KYC, prompt retention and driver's-licence-style access. Wissner-Gross warns that a gap between internal lab capability and what users can access creates perverse incentives, and raises policy for "importing" foreign AI.
- **Defensive cyber and trust.** Diamandis cites GPT-5.5 Cyber (codenamed Daybreak) scoring 85.6 on CyberGym. Mostaque and Blundin argue nobody reviews AI-written code any more, so certified, trusted models will matter.
- **OpenAI IPO delay.** Blundin dismisses SpaceX's share volatility as the reason, pointing to OpenAI's $120 billion raise and the burden of public-company disclosure. Wissner-Gross says OpenAI focused on consumers too long and Codex now drives revenue.
- **Distillation, quantum, other news.** Anthropic accuses Alibaba of 28.8 million fraudulent exchanges across 25,000 accounts. Wissner-Gross is only mildly excited by the quantum executive orders, while Blundin bets on quantum photonics. The hosts also cover Neuralink telepathy plans, Seedance 2.5 video and Eli Lilly's $6.3 billion orexin deal.
