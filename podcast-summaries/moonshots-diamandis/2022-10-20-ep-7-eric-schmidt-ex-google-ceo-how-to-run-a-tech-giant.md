## Thesis

In a conversation recorded at Abundance360 in April 2022, Peter Diamandis and former Google CEO Eric Schmidt agree that digitization is accelerating across every industry. Schmidt's main worry is the "compression of time": AI-driven systems may need decisions faster than people, governments and treaties can make them. He argues that philosophers, technologists and economists need to work out the rules now, rather than leave them to engineers as happened with social media.

## Key points

- **AI for biology.** Schmidt says biology becomes a computational problem once it moves from "squishy" to digital. He notes that there is still no digital model of the cell and argues that AI can approximate mechanisms that scientists then test.
- **Quantum timelines.** Schmidt estimates useful quantum computers are 8 to 10 years away because error correction needs 100 to 1,000 physical qubits per accurate qubit. He chairs SandboxAQ, Jack Hidary's Google spin-out, which works on quantum-safe communications and quantum simulation for chemistry.
- **Language models.** Schmidt cites Google's PaLM, which can translate between programming languages without paired training data. Diamandis cites a figure of 10x growth in model size every 10 months.
- **AGI forecast.** Schmidt says the median expert prediction puts AGI about 20 years out, and he names April 2042. He expects five to ten such systems, controlled by nation states. He and James Manyika announced a $125 million research programme on these problems.
- **Regulating software.** Schmidt uses hypersonic missiles and nuclear doctrine to argue that defensive AI may have to act without humans in the loop. He argues that software, unlike plutonium, is easy to copy, steal and strip of safeguards, so nuclear-style arms control does not transfer cleanly.
- **US and China.** Schmidt estimates the US is one to two years ahead in AI and expects the two countries to be roughly equal within five years, competing with different values. He also describes his role at Google as the "helper" to brilliant founders.
