## Thesis

David Sinclair tells Peter Diamandis that aging is mainly a loss of information in the epigenome, the system that controls which genes are on or off, and that cells keep a backup copy that can be restored. He predicts that within about ten years a course of pills could reset the body, and he argues that AI is what makes the move from expensive gene therapy to cheap molecules possible.

## Key points

- **From gene therapy to pills.** Sinclair says Life Biosciences plans to take its reprogramming gene therapy into human trials for eye diseases (glaucoma and NAION), after monkey studies. Making the first batch cost over $10 million, and gene therapies often sell for hundreds of thousands of dollars. His lab says it has AI-found molecules costing about $100 or less for a month's course.
- **AI in the lab.** Sinclair describes an imaging algorithm that estimates cell age from microscope pictures, and virtual docking of huge molecule libraries against four enzyme "levers". He says a cocktail of six molecules is now down to three, and work that once took thousands of years now takes months.
- **Competition and capital.** Diamandis names Altos (Bezos, Milner), NewLimit (Brian Armstrong) and Retro (Sam Altman) as well-funded rivals. Sinclair expects room for hundreds of successful companies rather than one winner.
- **Economics of healthy years.** Sinclair cites economic work he co-authored. The transcript gives $38 trillion of global value for one extra year of life expectancy, after on-air confusion between figures. Diamandis argues that longer health is the best fix for national debt and ageing populations.
- **Funding cuts.** Sinclair says all of his lab's federal grants were cancelled as part of the government's dispute with Harvard. His lab runs on about $3 million a year, and Diamandis appeals to listeners for donations.
- **Global clusters.** Sinclair names Singapore, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar as nations investing in longevity. He says China is more than a decade behind the US, but US research cuts put that lead at risk.
