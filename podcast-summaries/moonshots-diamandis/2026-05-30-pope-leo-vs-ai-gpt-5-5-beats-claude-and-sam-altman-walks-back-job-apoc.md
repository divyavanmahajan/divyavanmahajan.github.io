## Thesis

Peter Diamandis and the Moonshot Mates read the week's news as evidence that AI development cannot be slowed. They point to Pope Leo XIV's encyclical and a White House executive order that was pulled at the last minute. On jobs, they argue the real effect so far is a freeze on hiring young people plus a boom in startups, not mass displacement.

## Key points

- **The encyclical.** Pope Leo XIV's 42,000-word encyclical asks for regulation, worker protections and a slower pace. Salim Ismail says it moves the debate from safety to human dignity but insists AI cannot be regulated. Alexander Wissner-Gross notes reports that Anthropic shaped parts of it, while the text itself rejects AI personhood. Dave Blundin welcomes its use of the word slavery for AI-enabled control.
- **Executive order pulled.** The hosts say a planned order for 90-day pre-release model reviews was dropped after pushback from David Sacks and others. Wissner-Gross argues that 90 days roughly matches estimates of the US lead over China.
- **DeepSWE benchmark.** On Data Curve's new benchmark, GPT-5.5 scored 70% and Claude Opus 4.7 54%, with other models below 32%. Wissner-Gross expects it to saturate within months. Blundin says Opus 4.7 uses about twice as many tokens for similar results.
- **Revenue and Jevons paradox.** Peter cites token prices falling about 75% while usage exploded, OpenAI earning $5.7 billion in a quarter, and a Joseph Jacks projection that Anthropic could pass Alphabet's revenue by 2028. Blundin urges enterprises to sign long-term compute contracts.
- **Jobs.** Peter reports Sam Altman walking back his job-apocalypse warnings, and he cites a Dallas Fed finding that the decline is concentrated in younger workers. Blundin says automation at Vesmark is turning into margin without layoffs. Salim cites 25% more startups year on year, and Peter cites a16z data showing AI solo founders doubling to 3,000 in a quarter.
- **Space.** Starship V3 flew for the first time. Peter puts the chance of a Tesla-SpaceX merger at 100%, against 50/50 on Kalshi. The hosts also discuss Chun Wang's private Mars flyby and Starlink's plans for lunar connectivity.
