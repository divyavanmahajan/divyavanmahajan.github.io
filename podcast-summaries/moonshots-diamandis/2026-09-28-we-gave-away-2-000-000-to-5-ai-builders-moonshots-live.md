## Thesis

Peter Diamandis argues that the old social contract of school, degree and job is over, and that people should instead find a problem they care about and use AI to build a business around it. The Build with Gemini XPRIZE, run with Google, was meant to prove this can be done: teams had 90 days from a clean sheet to build a revenue-generating company addressing a problem that affects 100,000 people.

## Key points

- **The judges.** Palmer Luckey, ARK Invest CEO Cathie Wood, Google DeepMind's Logan Kilpatrick (product lead for AI Studio and the Gemini API) and Zynga founder Mark Pincus judged the five finalists.
- **Starting from zero.** Asked what they would do with no wealth or network, Luckey proposed oxytocin-based marriage counselling to reduce divorce, citing his interest in the US birth rate. Pincus would rebuild online social experiences to reduce mindless scrolling, and Luckey partly agreed, defending niche online communities.
- **Wood on jobs and drug discovery.** Wood tells graduates facing a weak entry-level market to start an AI company with no employees while still interviewing. She claims AI could cut drug development cost from $2.4 billion to $600-700 million and time from 13 years to eight or fewer.
- **Kilpatrick on data.** Kilpatrick says the labs have de-risked the AI recipe and compute, leaving data as the bottleneck, and points to the high share of data startups in the current YC class.
- **The winners.** Polyfork (customisable AI-native 3D assets) won first place and $500,000; Tician Cierre (automated VAT credit recovery for Mexican businesses) took second and $200,000. MyFixam (a TaskRabbit-style marketplace for Nigerian artisans), LaunchBridge (idea to LLC, website and payments in under 72 hours) and DodoPrep (an AI tutor, $100,000 for fifth) completed the top five.
