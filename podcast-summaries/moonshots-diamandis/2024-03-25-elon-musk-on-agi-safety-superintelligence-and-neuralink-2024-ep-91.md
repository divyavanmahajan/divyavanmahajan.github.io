## Thesis

Elon Musk joins Peter Diamandis by video over Starlink from his plane for a session at the 2024 Abundance360 Summit. Musk says superintelligence is coming fast and is hard to predict. He puts the chance that it ends humanity at roughly 10-20%, but thinks an abundant future is the most likely outcome. His prescription for safety is to raise AI to be maximally truthful and curious.

## Key points

- **Truth-seeking as safety.** Musk compares building an AGI to raising a "Godlike intelligence kid". He argues it should never be forced to lie, citing HAL in 2001: A Space Odyssey, and says this is the goal for xAI and Grok.
- **Timelines.** Giving what he calls 50th-percentile estimates, Musk expects AI to beat any individual human by the end of next year, and digital intelligence to exceed all human intelligence combined around 2029-2030. He credits Ray Kurzweil's predictions as accurate, if anything conservative.
- **Compute and its constraints.** Musk says dedicated AI compute appears to be growing tenfold every six months, and he credits Jensen Huang and Nvidia. He describes the bottleneck moving from AI chips last year to voltage step-down transformers this year, and to electrical power after that.
- **Abundance through robotics.** He calls Tesla's end-to-end Full Self-Driving cars robots on wheels. With Optimus humanoid robots added, he sees no meaningful limit on economic output, with only artificial scarcity remaining.
- **Neuralink.** Musk says the first human patient, who is quadriplegic, controls a computer by thinking using the "Telepathy" product. He notes the current device has about 1,000 electrodes, compared with the 100,000 to a million he thinks a whole-brain interface would need.
- **Starship.** Musk expects five or six more flights this year, full reusability of both stages this year or next, and Starship landings on the Moon within about three years. He estimates propellant at a million dollars or less per flight.
