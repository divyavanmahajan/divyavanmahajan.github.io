## Thesis

Recorded the day after the 2022 US midterms, Salim Ismail and Peter Diamandis argue that legacy institutions cannot keep pace with technological change. Ismail expects smaller, city-level governance and AI-assisted policymaking to gain ground. Diamandis is open to AI eventually taking on governing decisions, and both expect AI and robotics to make universal basic income affordable.

## Key points

- **Twitter.** Ismail calls Twitter the "global public square" and says it should be a lightly governed commons. He argues the tension between free speech and advertisers is hard to resolve. He cites ex-Twitter engineers who value the company at $10-12 billion, against the $45 billion Musk paid.
- **FTX and crypto.** Ismail says contacts warned for about 18 months that FTX's high interest rates looked like a Ponzi scheme. He predicts a market bottom within months and a new bull market in January. He cites Jeff Booth's trio of security, decentralization and scalability, and Hernando de Soto's estimate that putting land titles on a blockchain could release about 10% of $280 trillion in real estate value.
- **Democracy.** Ismail calls the US Constitution the country's software and argues it was meant to be amended. Citing futurist Paul Saffo, he predicts that city-state micro-democracies will outperform large nation-states.
- **AI in government.** Diamandis cites Ray Kurzweil's 2029 forecast for human-level AI and says he would prefer an incorruptible AI decision-maker. Ismail points to Google's AI cutting building energy use by about 40%. He gives it about a year before a Jarvis-style assistant handles basic tasks, but disputes the idea of "human-level AI" as a single measure.
- **Robots.** Ismail thinks general household robots are further off than people expect, citing autonomous cars, laundry folding and robot vacuums. Diamandis expects a Tesla Optimus at about $20,000 to lease for roughly $400 a month.
- **UBI.** The hosts cite the Stockton pilot ($500 a month), the ATM and bank-teller story, and Reid Hoffman's view that the numbers still don't add up without large productivity gains. Ismail's X Prize idea is a 10x improvement in emotional intelligence.

## Quotes
