## Thesis

Brian Armstrong, CEO of Coinbase and co-founder of NewLimit, tells Peter Diamandis and the Moonshot Mates that Bitcoin is "digital gold" and that on-chain stablecoins will become the financial rails for AI agents. The roundtable then argues over whether the US government should own equity in frontier labs, how three trillion-dollar IPOs can be absorbed, and what Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 release says about the model race.

## Key points

- **Bitcoin's slump.** Armstrong attributes the recent fall to AI absorbing risk capital and stablecoins becoming "the new meta" after the GENIUS Act. He guesses the bottom was around $60k and calls $100-200k by year end plausible, against a Citi projection of up to $189,000. He says Coinbase's quantum advisory council favours the BIP 360 proposal; what to do with Satoshi's coins is the contentious part.
- **Agents as customers.** Armstrong says agent transactions on Coinbase's network are now around 100 million. His plan has three steps: connect LLMs to accounts via MCP, build Coinbase Advisor into the app, and give every agent its own self-custodial wallet without KYC. He floats an on-chain, PageRank-style reputation score to reduce fraud.
- **Government stakes.** Diamandis expects a push towards 10% government ownership of frontier labs. Blundin calls it a dangerous long-term precedent, Wissner-Gross sees some quasi-nationalisation as likely if the labs keep growing, and Armstrong says government should set policy and leave capital allocation to the private sector.
- **IPOs and compute.** The hosts discuss OpenAI's S-1 filing, Google reportedly paying SpaceX $11 billion a year for GPU access, the AI1 satellite and a Gigasat factory in Texas. Diamandis cautions that Morgan Stanley's SpaceX revenue forecast is also a fundraising tool.
- **NewLimit.** Armstrong describes AI-guided screens for epigenetic reprogramming that restore a cell's age without changing its type. He says the first candidates are expected in the clinic next year.
- **Fable 5 and Mythos 5.** Wissner-Gross says Anthropic is "back in the lead" over GPT 5.5. Blundin notes the price doubled, and the Mates point out that Fable's safeguards can fall back to Opus on biology or chemistry queries.
