## Thesis

Will Marshall, co-founder and CEO of Planet, argues that AI models need real-world data, and that Planet's daily imaging of the whole Earth lets it build "large earth models". He also argues that once launch costs fall far enough, the efficiency of the chips will decide who wins orbital data centres, not rocket capacity. The Moonshot Mates then debate AI talent moves, AI personhood and China's GLM 5.2.

## Key points

- **Indexing the Earth.** Marshall says Planet runs about 200 satellites, has roughly 3,000 images of every point on land over 10 years (150 petabytes), and gets about 60% of revenue from defence and intelligence. He says AI is lowering the barrier so farmers, journalists and NGOs can query the data in plain language.
- **Edge compute in orbit.** Marshall describes putting Nvidia GPUs on satellites. In April a satellite spotted aircraft at Alice Springs and sent back only their positions, within seconds.
- **Project Suncatcher.** Planet is building early TPU test satellites for Google. Marshall says a study with Google found orbital compute becomes cheaper than ground data centres at about $200-300 per kilogram to orbit. Diamandis and Blundin call his "Nvidia tax" framing (below) a key insight.
- **Talent and Google.** On Noam Shazeer moving to OpenAI and John Jumper to Anthropic, Wissner-Gross argues Google DeepMind has fallen behind the frontier. Marshall disagrees, pointing to Google's compute, data and people. Ismail says smaller labs give individuals more agency.
- **AI personhood.** On Javier Milei's push for AI-run corporations and Yuval Noah Harari's rebuttal, Wissner-Gross backs AI personhood, Marshall calls for much more safety and governance work, and Ismail lists "machine-native" sanctions such as revoking compute.
- **GLM 5.2 and the cost of compute.** Wissner-Gross says Zhipu's open-weight model uses about twice the tokens of top Western models at half the total price, which challenges the claim that Chinese models trail by 6-8 months. Blundin argues hyperscaler capex above cash flow is fine because it can be financed.
