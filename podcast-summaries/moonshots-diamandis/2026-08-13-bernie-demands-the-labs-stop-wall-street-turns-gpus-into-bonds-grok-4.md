## Thesis

Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin, Alexander Wissner-Gross and guest Emad Mostaque argue that AI is now moving fast enough that capital markets, Hollywood and regulators are all being forced to adapt. Their common view is that pausing or labelling AI will not hold, and that the better answer is financial and defensive infrastructure that scales as fast as the technology.

## Key points

- **GPUs as bonds.** Diamandis reports that Nvidia has partnered with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield and KKR to mobilise over $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure. Blundin calls it a structure that can be repeated cluster by cluster. Ismail warns that new architectures could strand securitised compute. Wissner-Gross argues that "compute-backed securities" can be hedged with futures and derivatives.
- **Grok's catch-up.** The hosts say Grok 4.6 matches the top score of 61 on the Artificial Analysis index at $2/$6 per million tokens. Wissner-Gross attributes this to post-training on Cursor reasoning traces. Mostaque says Elon Musk expects Grok 4.7 to pass Opus.
- **Hollywood economics.** Diamandis describes Higgsfield's 110-minute AI film, made for about $2 million with 28 people in four weeks. Mostaque expects compute costs for such a film to drop about tenfold by the new year.
- **Sanders' pause letter.** Senator Sanders asked Anthropic, Meta and OpenAI to pause after a Stanford team used the open Evo 2 model to design working bacteriophages. Mostaque and Ismail call a pause too late. Wissner-Gross argues it would worsen race dynamics. Diamandis wants controls on DNA synthesisers, and Blundin wants prompts monitored.
- **Watermarks and labels.** Anthropic's text watermarking and the EU's labelling icons get a sceptical reception. Wissner-Gross compares them to cookie banners and expects watermarks to be used as attacks.
- **Meta and eVTOLs.** Zuckerberg's "personal superintelligence" essay and community data-centre fund divide the panel. Diamandis then surveys Joby, Archer, EHang, Beta and Eve after Archer bought three Boeing units.
