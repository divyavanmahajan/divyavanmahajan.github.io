## Thesis

Peter Diamandis, Salim Ismail, Dave Blundin and guest Emad Mostaque, founder of Intelligent Internet, react to xAI's Grok 4 launch. Mostaque argues the model can reason but cannot yet plan. With benchmarks close to saturated, he says the differences between frontier labs now come from data quality and engineering, and next from agents, interfaces and access to chips.

## Key points

- **Benchmarks.** Diamandis cites Grok 4 Heavy at 44.4% on Humanity's Last Exam and notes that xAI reached the top spot 28 months after its founding. Mostaque points to a 100% score on the AIME math benchmark and says the field is "running out of benchmarks". He expects Humanity's Last Exam to be solved within two years.
- **Post-training.** Mostaque explains that post-training used to take about 1% of compute and about 10% with DeepSeek. With Grok 4 it matched pre-training, partly because labs use frontier models to generate structured training data.
- **Pricing.** Mostaque expects the cost of a given level of intelligence to fall 5-10x a year. Blundin predicts premium tiers well above $300 a month for coding and design work. Mostaque thinks the $300 tier is probably a loss leader used to sell into enterprises.
- **Entertainment.** Mostaque argues Hollywood studios gain from lower costs because attention and distribution stay scarce. In his view the change is good for companies and individual creators but "terrible" for people working in the industry. He cites video games at about $450 billion against film at about $70 billion.
- **Coding.** Recalling his earlier "no more coders in five years" prediction, Mostaque says context engineers will replace programmers. He points to Cursor reaching $500 million in revenue and Anthropic reaching $4 billion.
- **Compute.** The panel discusses xAI's 340,000 GPUs and plans for 100,000 GB200s to train a video model. Mostaque says video models are world models, and he names Meta as a dark horse. Ismail counters that a single human cell is still orders of magnitude beyond current models.
