## Thesis

Bernt Bornich, founder and CEO of 1X, tells Peter Diamandis and Dave Blundin why he chose the home over the factory. He gives two reasons: consumer hardware scales faster than anything else, and intelligence comes from diverse data. He presents 1X as a company aiming for AGI through embodiment. In his view, humanoids will remove the labour bottleneck that limits data centres, chips and energy.

## Key points

- **Why the home.** Bornich says 1X's earlier robot, Eve, plateaued after 20 to 40 hours of learning in guarding and logistics work. Homes show no cap on diversity yet. He estimates that 10,000 robots would collect more useful data each day than is uploaded to YouTube.
- **Neo Gamma specs.** Bornich cites 66 lb weight, about 150 lb lift, 50 lb carry, roughly four hours of battery and 22 degrees of freedom per hand. He says in-house tendon-driven motors deliver five and a half times the world record in torque without gears. That keeps the robot light, soft and cheap to build.
- **Scale and price.** Bornich targets a factory run rate above 20,000 a year by end of 2026. When a host floats $30k or $300 a month, he says 1X could do better. He calls the robot "closer to a refrigerator" than a car, with a few hundred parts versus about 50,000.
- **Teleoperation and privacy.** Bornich describes teleoperation as expert demonstration data. Owners approve named operators, and the robot's lighting changes when one is connected. Engineers can review data only with the owner's consent. A 24-hour delay lets owners delete clips before training. Cooking is excluded at launch for safety.
- **World models.** Bornich says 1X uses world models to evaluate new models and red-team them for safety before deployment. He plans to productize them eventually.
- **Bottlenecks.** Diamandis cites Elon Musk's and Brett Adcock's forecast of 10 billion humanoids by 2040, and Bornich calls it roughly correct. He and the hosts flag aluminium, magnets, chip fabs, permitting and China's hardware ecosystem as constraints. He suggests US free economic zones.
