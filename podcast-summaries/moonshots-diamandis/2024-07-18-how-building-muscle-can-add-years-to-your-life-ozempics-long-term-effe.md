## Thesis

Peter Diamandis and Krystal Zell, CEO of Tonal, argue that strength training is a high-value health habit that most people skip for lack of time or know-how. Zell positions Tonal's wall-mounted "digital weight" system, combined with a growing dataset and a conversational AI coach, as a way to remove both barriers at home. Diamandis frames it as an early step towards AI coaches that guide people throughout the day.

## Key points

- **Why people don't train.** Zell names habit formation, time and not knowing what to do. Diamandis adds a third reason: people don't realise how important it is. He calls muscle mass his top personal priority.
- **The product.** Zell describes a roughly 7-by-7 wall unit with motors, cables and a screen that sets and adjusts resistance automatically, pairs it with instructor-led programmes, and supports separate accounts per household. Professionals install it within about two weeks of ordering.
- **Data as an asset.** Zell says Tonal has more than 150,000 members, millions of workouts and billions of pounds lifted. She says the average member's strength score rises 80% in the first year, and the average session lasts 35 minutes.
- **Conversational AI coach.** A beta assistant called "Tony" combines language models with Tonal's data, so members can state a goal (Zell's example: learning tennis) and get a tailored programme. Zell, a long-time consumer executive, sees it as a direct way to learn what customers want.
- **Business model.** Zell describes a hardware purchase plus a $59.95 monthly subscription for content, trainers and AI, with Affirm financing. She pitches it as a replacement for personal training. She says Tonal's engineer founder started the company after finding gym training had no data or feedback loop.
- **Industry outlook.** Zell calls connected fitness young and collaborative, expects more consumer flexibility and a bigger role for intelligence, and says GLP-1 drugs are raising awareness of muscle loss. Diamandis cites longevity escape velocity forecasts (Ray Kurzweil's 2030) and his Healthspan XPRIZE.
