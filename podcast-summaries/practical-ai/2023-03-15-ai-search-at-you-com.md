## Thesis

Bryan McCann, co-founder and CTO of You.com, tells Daniel Whitenack and Chris Benson that search is shifting from lists of blue links to generated answers. He argues those answers must be grounded in search results and app data, with clear attribution. For practitioners, he urges treating language models as tools, measured as critically as any other engineering system.

## Key points

- **Origins.** McCann says he and CEO Richard Socher worked on transfer learning, contextualised word vectors and multitask NLP at Salesforce, and started You.com so that language understanding would serve users rather than just improve ads.
- **What is new.** He identifies two shifts: models that understand context far better, and generation of answers. YouChat sits on top of a traditional search engine and cites results to reduce hallucination.
- **Open platform.** McCann describes apps that partners can build in a couple of hours, where any monetisation inside the app belongs to the partner, which he sees as a response to answers replacing link clicks.
- **Multimodal answers.** For weather or stock queries, YouChat shows an app or chart instead of text, and McCann wants it to reason over that data too. Earlier tools included YouCode and YouWrite, the latter popular with students.
- **Do not anthropomorphise.** He warns that chat interfaces make models feel like people. Benson and McCann both describe using them as creative aids; McCann compares this to humans and chess engines working better together.
- **Practitioner advice.** Ground responses, anticipate hallucinations, give attribution, expect prompts to matter, and collect real numbers on outputs.
