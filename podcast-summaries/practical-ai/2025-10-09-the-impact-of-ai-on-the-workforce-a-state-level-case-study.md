## Thesis

Chelsea Linder, VP of Innovation and Entrepreneurship at TechPoint, argues that aligning workers with new AI skills is now employers' main talent problem in Indiana, ahead of recruiting and retention. Drawing on TechPoint's AI workforce report and its AI Innovation Network, she says AI is transforming roles rather than eliminating them, and that peer communities and approved access matter as much as the technology.

## Key points

- **A peer network built on sharing.** The SBA-funded AI Innovation Network launched in January and had 399 members at recording. It runs hybrid events so rural and factory-floor members can join, including quarterly case studies run like a PhD seminar; Linder credits an advisory council and openness among members.
- **Report priorities.** TechPoint's report calls for building AI into training (higher education and in-company upskilling) in industry-specific ways, extending adoption beyond early sectors into lagging ones such as agriculture, and sharing knowledge across sectors.
- **Demand numbers.** Job postings for generative AI engineers are up seven times year over year, and postings requiring generative AI skills in other IT roles are up 35%. Census Bureau survey data shows business AI use rising from under 4% in September 2023 to about 10% by July 2025.
- **Software engineers still in demand.** Whitenack notes software developer remains the most in-demand role. Linder calls this trailing data, and describes a company that replaced junior developers with AI, only for mid-level engineers to ask six months later to hire juniors again.
- **Shadow use needs policy.** Linder believes nearly everyone uses AI, often unknowingly; her daycare's staff used generative AI for status updates without the administrator knowing. She recommends an acceptable-use policy and approved access for everyone.
- **Spread of access.** TechPoint works with firms from Eli Lilly, recently named the most AI-ready company in the world, to small businesses with almost no technology, which now ask how to start.
