## Thesis

Liam Dunne and Ben Moore, co-founders of the organic search agency Discovered Labs, argue that SEO is not dead but now includes answer engine optimisation (AEO). Brands show up in LLM answers through four layers: model weights, reasoning, retrieval and the final response. Moore says most of the industry measures only citations and does so without statistical rigour.

## Key points

- **Same jobs, new priorities.** Dunne says SEO still means on-page, off-page and technical work, but tactics differ between ranking on Google and appearing in ChatGPT. Lost organic traffic reflects a change in buyer behaviour: people now read the answer inside the LLM instead of on the website.
- **Measure the noise.** Moore criticises tools like Profound and an industry without data science skills. He wants citation rate, mention rate and share of voice measured within about a 5% margin of error, and changes checked for statistical significance.
- **How an answer is built.** Moore describes query fan-out that pulls in up to about 60 candidate sources, followed by a consensus step similar to a DeepMind "agree"-style algorithm. In their research, ChatGPT gave roughly a fifth to a third of retrieval slots to Reddit, but most Reddit threads were used to ground the answer, not cited.
- **Relevancy, consensus, consistency.** Dunne's framework: clear messaging aimed at long-tail queries, third-party votes of confidence (Reddit, YouTube, digital PR, G2 reviews) and the same wording everywhere. Upvotes did not correlate with citations; relevant passages did, even in comments with one or two upvotes.
- **Weights and blank space.** Moore says weights encode factual accuracy, sentiment and co-occurrence, so even a brand name matters. Where competitors stay silent on a topic such as pricing, publishing directionally correct numbers gets cited easily.
- **Agents that act.** Both expect a move from read-only to read-write agents. Clients have reported an AI agent booking a demo, and some users now arrive through command-line agents that install tools for them.
