## Thesis

This is the show's trailer rather than a full episode. Daniel Whitenack and Chris Benson present Practical AI as a weekly podcast for two audiences: people deep in AI tools and trends, and people trying to understand what AI means for their lives. The trailer illustrates each audience with a short clip from a later episode and ends with an invitation to subscribe.

## Key points

- **The hosts.** Whitenack introduces himself as CEO of Prediction Guard and Benson as a principal AI and autonomy research engineer. These titles, and the clips, show that the audio was refreshed after the 2020 feed date.
- **The pitch.** The hosts describe the show as making AI practical, productive and accessible, noting that AI now affects the daily lives of billions of people.
- **Clip for practitioners.** In a clip the show notes attribute to episode #357, a guest argues that AI tools multiply individual leverage. The example: Hermes Agent was first built internally at Nous by someone called Technium, who the guest says is not a developer, and it became the top open source repository on GitHub.
- **Clip for the AI-curious.** In a clip from episode #348, a guest quotes Susan Sontag ("to resist metaphor is to endure the thing itself") and argues that AI should be understood on its own terms, not through analogies to nuclear technology or the internet, even though those analogies are familiar to parts of the U.S. government.
- **Call to action.** The hosts suggest starting with any recent episode and subscribing; the show notes add a link to upcoming webinars.
