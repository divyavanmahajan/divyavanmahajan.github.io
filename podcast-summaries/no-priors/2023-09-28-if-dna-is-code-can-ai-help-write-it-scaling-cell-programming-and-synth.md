## Thesis

Jason Kelly, co-founder and CEO of Ginkgo Bioworks, argues that DNA works like code but cells are physical, undesigned and hard to debug, much like neural networks. Because humans never learned to read or write that language, he expects AI models to beat human experts in biology sooner than in law or other English-language work. The main constraint, he says, is data, which Ginkgo produces at scale in its automated labs.

## Key points

- **Abstraction from computing.** Kelly describes splitting lab work from design from the start: a "Foundry" automates and scales experiments, and Ginkgo's DNA programmers order work from it. He says this was culturally hard because scientists do not want others running their experiments.
- **Business model.** Customers such as Merck, Biogen, Bayer, Syngenta and Corteva agree a spec and timeline. Ginkgo engineers the cell, licenses it, and earns payments plus a royalty, working much like a contract software shop.
- **Foundation model that speaks protein.** Ginkgo announced a deal with Google to build a protein foundation model that adds to its enzyme design loop of testing around 1,000 DNA designs, feeding the results back and repeating. Kelly says AI drug discovery is held back mainly by lack of data, and Ginkgo has a 300,000 square foot robotic lab.
- **Fragmented industry.** Kelly says pharma and agriculture companies are vertically integrated with no horizontal platforms. Their view is that work differs too much between products to share infrastructure; Ginkgo's business depends on that being wrong.
- **Biosecurity as cybersecurity.** With the CDC, Ginkgo sequences wastewater from inbound flights, and runs similar programmes at Doha airport and in Ukraine. Kelly calls lone-actor AI bioweapon risk low today because data on case fatality is hard to collect, but says society is far too exposed to natural pandemics.
- **Employee super-voting.** Ginkgo gives every employee B shares with 10x votes, which they lose if they leave. Kelly argues the people who work there, not distant capital, should govern a powerful platform.
