## Thesis

Eric Simons, co-founder and CEO of StackBlitz, discusses with Sarah Guo how Bolt.new went, in her words, from zero to $20 million of ARR in two months. He argues it worked because Claude Sonnet 3.5 crossed a tipping point for production-grade code. Unlike closed drag-and-drop site builders, he says, Bolt outputs standard code that professional developers can later take over, so it is not a dead end for non-technical users.

## Key points

- **Web containers as the moat.** Simons says StackBlitz spent about four years building an operating system in WebAssembly that runs dev environments in the browser, so Bolt can install packages such as Firebase or Supabase and deploy without servers. He contrasts this with thin "GPT wrapper" companies.
- **Open source and community.** StackBlitz open-sourced Bolt's system prompt and much of its code. Simons says power users know the product better than the team, and that AI apps without community education can see churn of 60-70%. Guo compares this with Midjourney's power users.
- **A practical benchmark.** The local open-source version of Bolt has become a way to test new coding models, such as a recent Nvidia release, since academic evals do not cover building real applications.
- **Cost collapse for builders.** Simons cites a product manager in Thailand who built Viral Hooks after a $5,000, two-to-three-month quote, using a $50 plan in two weeks, and a CRM quoted at $30,000 built on a $200 plan in a month. Most users are non-technical; the most successful have managed development teams.
- **Origins.** Simons and childhood friend and co-founder Albert learned to code at 13. Inspired by Figma's browser-first pitch, they built StackBlitz, which Simons says serves about three million developers a month. Bolt was shelved in February 2024 because models produced broken code, then revived with Sonnet.
- **Outlook.** Simons expects engineers to become "software composers" who instruct agents at a higher level, and expects coding models to keep improving because code success is verifiable.
