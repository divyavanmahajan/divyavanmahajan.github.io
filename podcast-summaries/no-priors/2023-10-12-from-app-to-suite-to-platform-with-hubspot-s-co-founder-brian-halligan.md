## Thesis

HubSpot co-founder and executive chairman Brian Halligan tells Sarah Guo that HubSpot grew by betting against the consensus, which he calls zigging when everyone else zags. HubSpot sold to SMBs, moved under Salesforce with an easy freemium CRM, and treated its IPO as a starting line. On AI, Halligan argues that this is an unusual disruption in which incumbents with years of customer data have the advantage over startups.

## Key points

- **Inbound as a category.** Halligan noticed that Dharmesh Shah's blog drew about two orders of magnitude more interest than the venture-backed startups using cold calls and bought lists. Outbound became the enemy that defined "inbound". The INBOUND conference started with 400 people in Kendall Square and now has 12,000 attendees in person. Halligan says "inbound sales" failed.
- **App to suite.** After Salesforce bought four marketing companies in one year, HubSpot pivoted to a freemium CRM. Halligan built the new unit in a separate building, copying Apple's Mac team. His first trio of leaders could not agree on a vision, and it took about two and a half years to get a decent product. The sales business then "acquired" the marketing business.
- **Public-company habits.** At the IPO, HubSpot had a roughly $200 million marketing business and a $1 million sales product. HubSpot treated every employee like an officer, with the same access to information and the same trading windows. Halligan later stepped down as CEO at $2 billion in revenue.
- **AI and CRM.** Sam Altman told him about two and a half years earlier not to hire PhDs and to wait for a step change. HubSpot's ChatSpot uses OpenAI and Anthropic models. Halligan expects chat answers to cut website visits and gated content to return.
- **Culture and climate.** Colin Angle of iRobot told him culture is how people make decisions when you're not in the room. HubSpot's Culture Code copied Netflix's format. After a snowmobile accident in which he broke 13 bones, Halligan started Propeller, a $120 million fund for ocean-climate startups that has made about 10 investments.
