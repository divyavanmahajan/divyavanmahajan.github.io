## Thesis

Mati Staniszewski, co-founder and CEO of ElevenLabs, tells Sarah Guo that voice is the most natural interface for computers, robots and media, and that ElevenLabs wins by pairing foundational audio research with a product and ecosystem layer. He treats research as a six-to-twelve-month head start, with lasting value coming from voices, integrations and distribution.

## Key points

- **Scale.** Founded in 2022, ElevenLabs has about 350 people and $300 million in ARR, split roughly evenly between self-serve creators (over 5 million monthly actives) and a few thousand enterprise customers on its agents platform.
- **Origin in Polish dubbing.** Staniszewski and his co-founder grew up with foreign films narrated by a single flat voice in Polish. That led to the goal of dubbing that keeps the original voice and emotion, and later to real-time translation, which he expects within about two years.
- **Labs per problem.** The company organises small "labs" of researchers, engineers and operators around one problem at a time: first a voice lab of about five people, then an agents lab, then music. Product teams build without waiting for research if it is more than about three months away.
- **Agents go proactive.** Customer support (Cisco, Twilio, Telefónica Digital) is moving from reactive to proactive help, as with Meesho in India and Square. Other examples include Darth Vader in Fortnite with Epic Games, chess.com tutoring and a Chris Voss negotiation practice on MasterClass. Ukraine's Ministry of Digital Transformation is building an "agentic government".
- **Beating the big labs.** Staniszewski says audio needs architectural breakthroughs more than scale, estimates 50 to 100 capable audio researchers worldwide and claims ElevenLabs employs about 10. Scribe v2 speech-to-text runs under 150 milliseconds with 93.5% accuracy across the top 30 languages.
- **Cascaded versus fused.** He expects base models to commoditise. For reliable enterprise use, cascaded speech-to-text, LLM and text-to-speech remains the choice for the next year, while fused speech-to-speech suits more expressive use cases.
