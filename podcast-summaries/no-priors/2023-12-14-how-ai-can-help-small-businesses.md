## Thesis

Alyssa Henry, former CEO of Square and earlier the first GM of AWS storage, tells Sarah Guo and Elad Gil that the main effect of generative AI on small businesses is to unlock work that never got done because it was too hard, slow or expensive. She sees the same pattern in cloud and chips: an early land grab, followed by consolidation around a few leaders and simpler, bundled services.

## Key points

- **Early AI at Square.** Henry says Square has long used machine learning for risk, fraud and cross-selling across millions of small merchants. Around 2018 it acquired a startup founded by two Stanford machine learning PhDs, introduced by Vinod Khosla, and used GPT-2 in Square Messages to help draft replies to customers.
- **Latent demand.** Most owners know they should do marketing but lack time and expertise, Henry argues. Square's Photo Studio began as a physical studio in Brooklyn with a 360 camera and became an iPhone app using AI to remove backgrounds; she expects generative AI to make such tasks "10x faster 10x cheaper". Guo adds that demand for generated content at a hundredth or thousandth of the cost surprised many tech people.
- **Integration and bundling.** Henry describes owners copying data between browser windows and tools, and predicts a shift from best-of-breed point products to bundles.
- **Cloud and foundation models.** She says AI adds new workloads rather than replacing old ones. As S3 started with a simple API and became a foundation for other AWS services, she expects model services to gain capabilities while staying simple, citing Amazon Bedrock as a bundle of models.
- **Chips.** In Henry's view each computing wave has a clear number one and two: "really hard to be number three and you're dead if you're number four". She thinks Nvidia will not hold a monopoly and expects chiplets and modular designs to speed innovation. Guo says she is less confident than a few months earlier that Transformers will stay the dominant architecture.
- **2024 outlook.** Gil sees the most open space in B2B and consumer applications.
