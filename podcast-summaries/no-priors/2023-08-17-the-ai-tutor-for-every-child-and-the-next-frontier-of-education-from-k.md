## Thesis

Sal Khan argues that generative AI can finally approximate the personal tutor, which Benjamin Bloom's "two sigma" research showed lifts an average student to the 96th percentile. He expects AI to be a useful add-on in the short term and transformative within three to five years. Basic skills such as reading, writing and maths will matter more, not less, because fluent learners get the most out of these tools.

## Key points

- **Origins in tutoring cousins.** Khan describes tutoring his cousin Nadia remotely in 2004, building practice software, then posting YouTube lessons. He says his cousins liked him better on YouTube than in person because the videos were on demand and free of judgement.
- **Mastery learning over batch schooling.** Khan says mass education moves fixed-pace groups of 30 forward regardless of gaps. He cites Khan Lab School students gaining 1.5 to 2 grade levels a year, against about 0.7 in traditional schools, and Khan World School students learning three to four times faster.
- **How Khanmigo began.** Greg Brockman and Sam Altman contacted Khan in late summer 2022, before GPT-4's first training run had finished. Bill Gates had challenged OpenAI to pass the AP Biology exam. Khanmigo launched with GPT-4 on 15 March 2023.
- **Guardrails.** Khan lists prompts that make the tutor ask leading questions instead of giving answers, conversation logs visible to parents and teachers for under-18s, a second AI that moderates chats and flags problems, and no use of student data for training.
- **Where it is heading.** Khan predicts nearly all teachers will use generative AI for lesson plans and grading within five years. He describes a tutor with memory of a student's interests, linked to Neal Stephenson's "The Diamond Age". A Canvas (Instructure) integration would report on how an assignment was done, not just the result.
- **Universities and skills.** Khan expects elite universities and nimble community colleges to do well and the expensive middle to face a reckoning over student debt. He compares AI to the camera freeing painters, with workers moving into editor and architect roles.
