## Thesis

Pedro Franceschi, co-founder and CEO of Brex, tells Sarah Guo and Elad Gil that AI in finance works when products show users how confident the model is and fit suggestions into existing workflows. He argues that AI will reshape the software layer, from expense assistants to account-based marketing at scale, while the hard work of moving money globally stays a durable advantage.

## Key points

- **From co-CEO to solo CEO.** Franceschi says the move from co-CEO with Henrique to a CEO-and-chairman structure mostly formalised how the company already ran, alongside fewer management layers and decision makers over seven months.
- **A weekend prototype becomes a product.** After ChatGPT launched, he built a Python prototype on GPT-3.5 that used calendar context to write memos, categorise expenses and find receipts. Brex Assistant is now used by roughly 30,000 customers and completes over a third of their expenses automatically.
- **Designing for ambiguity.** High-confidence suggestions are applied automatically, medium ones shown as options, low ones hidden. Accounting suggestions offered inside the workflow reached an 80% acceptance rate. Franceschi says data scientists became essential for scoring results.
- **Account-based marketing for every account.** He argues LLMs make the personalised marketing once reserved for large enterprise deals cheap enough for any account. The hard part is Brex's own customer data platform with proprietary signals; writing the email is easy.
- **Money movement as a moat.** Brex connects directly to Mastercard and local payment rails instead of vendors such as Stripe or Marqeta. That global, local-currency capability is why many of its 130 public-company customers sign up. AI also powers adverse media monitoring for compliance.
- **Continuous finance and focus.** Franceschi describes three horizons: cards, total spend and real-time "continuous finance" that could make the ERP unnecessary. He defends dropping 20,000 small-business customers, saying leadership bandwidth, not headcount, was the bottleneck.
