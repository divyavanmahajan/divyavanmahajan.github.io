## Thesis

Reid Hoffman, co-founder of LinkedIn and Inflection AI, tells Sarah Guo and Elad Gil that AI will amplify human ability the way the steam engine amplified physical power. He argues that doomers misread exponential curves and ignore the opportunity cost of slowing down. In his view, critics have to describe a better future rather than only warn of danger.

## Key points

- **Amplification, not just replacement.** Hoffman wants more focus on "people plus AI". He cites a line of sight to a medical assistant and a tutor on each of 5 billion smartphones, while far fewer than a billion people have access to doctors.
- **Why he did not sign the risk statement.** He declined the 22-word statement that put AI alongside pandemics and climate change. His reason is that AI, unlike those risks, also has a positive column and may help solve both. He calls the six-month pause letter a misunderstanding of how people behave.
- **Advice for white-collar workers.** He expects mid-level white-collar jobs to change first and fastest. His advice is to start using AI on tasks that matter to you. He argues the transition will take longer than feared: even if every manufacturer built only autonomous trucks from tomorrow, half the trucks on the road would not be autonomous for 10 years.
- **Inflection's Pi.** Hoffman describes Pi as a compassionate personal intelligence with a point of view. It pushes back on a white supremacist, and it steers lonely users towards human friends rather than keeping them for itself, unlike the film Her.
- **Values and regulation.** He says technology is not value-neutral and that developers should be open about the values they build in. He dismisses the UK AI Summit line about regulating before innovating and is critical of the EU AI Act.
- **Next two years.** Hoffman expects better language-model assistants and narrow tools in areas such as drug discovery. Gil predicts gains in reasoning, memory and RAG, and bespoke models. Guo points to smaller, efficient models such as Mistral and to video tools from HeyGen and Pika.
