## Thesis

Michael Lee, co-founder and CEO of Sequence Holdings and formerly head of private investing at Lone Pine, tells Sarah Guo that in some industries incumbents hold the lasting advantages. AI winners there will be incumbents that are "re-founded" around the technology. He argues that this works best through a permanent holding company that owns the business, celebrates engineers and plans over decades rather than a three-year exit.

## Key points

- **The Baldwin deal.** Sequence, about 20 months old, is taking insurance broker Baldwin private for $7.7 billion with the Dell family office, which co-controls it. It is billed as the largest AI take-private to date.
- **Why not consultants or software.** Lee says companies cannot recruit the engineers themselves, because their celebrated persona is not the engineer. Services firms are paid to grow their share of your wallet, which leads to incrementalism. Software is beta that everyone can buy, and it is built around today's "human assembly line".
- **BankSouth as proof.** A services engagement with a Georgia bank owned by the family of Avenir co-founder Jamie Reynolds became Sequence's first investment. In about six months, Lee says, consumer underwriting time fell 94% and the average loan went from 30 days to 11. Loan volume doubled from Q1 to Q2 with a smaller underwriting team.
- **What Sequence looks for.** Lee wants large markets where incumbents have the advantages, "dense" centralised operations and management already adopting technology. Insurance brokerage fits: more than $2 trillion of premiums a year, 90% gross retention, and brokers paid by carriers, which keeps startups out.
- **Atlas platform.** Lee says businesses are about 80% homogeneous and 20% vertical-specific. Atlas has four layers: a data ontology that makes the business "legible to models", an agent builder, the Lattice orchestration engine, and an artifacts application builder.
- **Versus private equity.** Fund structures reward deploying capital and selling within about three years. Sequence aims to do one deal a year. Lee calls the "human engineering" of employee anxiety harder than the technical work.

## Quotes

> "In a world where you believe that alpha comes from engineering and AI, you need to create a culture whereby the celebrated persona is the engineer." — Michael Lee
