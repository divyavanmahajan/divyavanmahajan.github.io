## Thesis

In a crossover with Latent Space recorded live at Microsoft Build, Satya Nadella tells Sarah Guo, Elad Gil and swyx that a platform is defined by how much value it creates beyond itself. He argues that every company, AI-native or traditional, should be able to operate at the frontier with its own intelligence, built from its data, traces and private evals on an open harness.

## Key points

- **Clean-lineage models.** Nadella describes Microsoft's MAI models as built from carefully ablated pre-training data. In a Land O'Lakes demo, traces from a frontier model were used to train a 5B reasoning model that scored higher.
- **Private evals as IP.** Public evals can all be maxed, he says. A company's private eval is its key asset, and the test of control is whether it can switch from model A to model B and keep improving. He points to GitHub's harness, used across Microsoft products and available in Foundry.
- **Tacit knowledge on the balance sheet.** Traces from agents and humans working together could train a "company veteran agent", capturing knowledge that human capital never could.
- **SaaS is unbundled, not dead.** Nadella says data models and business logic, such as Power BI semantic models, keep their value. Work IQ exposes Microsoft 365 data to agents. Pricing will mix per-user and consumption: GitHub Copilot needed a consumption meter once agents ran all day. Build-versus-buy should come down to the marginal cost of building and maintaining.
- **Making work meta.** Microsoft built more Azure capacity in the last 15 months than in the first 15 years. The networking team, which handles 500-plus fiber operators, built an agent called Miles and asked for tokens rather than head count. LinkedIn created a "full stack builder" role.
- **Earning permission.** Nadella says data centers must deliver visible community benefits, such as energy prices, water replenishment, jobs and tax base, within 12-18 months. He suggests the next big startup might be a new kind of university.
