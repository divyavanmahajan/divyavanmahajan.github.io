## Thesis

Astro Teller presents two X projects aimed at feeding a population heading for 10 billion. Mineral's Elliott Grant argues that AI could transform agriculture but lacked enough good field data, so the team built machines to capture it. Tidal's Evan Rapoport applied the same idea underwater to salmon farming. Both teams relied on cheap, fast prototypes and then spun their technology out to industry.

## Key points

- **Data was the bottleneck.** Grant says crop breeding in 2025 is still done much as Gregor Mendel did it. Mineral started with two bicycles, scaffolding and Pixel phones in Watsonville strawberry fields, where it could test every two weeks, and reached a production phenotyping rover by the seventh generation.
- **Deliberately crude prototypes.** Joe Sargent of X's design kitchen describes rigs built from wheelchair motors and a bok choy imaging robot, followed by the "trucky" rover, a golf cart on stilts.
- **Bean breeding in Africa.** David Guerena of the International Center for Tropical Agriculture says beans provide about 90% of human protein for much of East Africa. Rovers, and later a smartphone app, produced continuous flower and pod curves that breeders had never seen. He hopes to cut the 10-year breeding cycle to 2-3 years and says the app helps jobs rather than replacing them.
- **Where Mineral went.** The Africa work moved to Google Research, the yield tool was sold to Driscoll's, and the sensing package went to John Deere.
- **Tidal and the fish whisperers.** Rapoport points to salmon's roughly one-to-one feed conversion and found that feeding in Norway relied on staff watching monitors. Starting from a koi pond, puppeted toy fish and a "fish treadmill", the team estimated fish weight from stereo images to within 1-2%.
- **Results at sea.** CEO Rajesh Jadhav says Tidal runs in hundreds of pens across four countries, cuts parasite treatments by a quarter, and is exploring seagrass carbon measurement. Grant expects AI to compress a century of agricultural discovery into 5-10 years.
