## Thesis

Neeru Khosla, co-founder of the free learning platform CK-12 Foundation, argues that earlier technology gave students richer formats (simulations, flexible books) but never showed what was happening in their minds. AI changes that: by analysing the questions students ask, educators can trace exactly where understanding breaks down. She sees this as the lever to slowly change a school system built around standardised tests.

## Key points

- **Prompting is learning.** Khosla admits she was afraid of AI at first, but concluded that a student who keeps prompting, questioning answers and digging deeper is thinking about concepts, not cheating.
- **Flexi at scale.** CK-12 launched its AI tutor Flexi about two and a half years ago; she says more than 50 million students use it. The team tags questions: many are procedural, but some reveal misconceptions, such as a child asking how the sun burns without oxygen in space, which shows confusion between combustion and nuclear fusion.
- **Beyond right or wrong.** Standardised testing records only whether an answer is correct; deep knowledge tracing shows where and how a student went wrong.
- **System inertia.** Schools resisted CK-12 because adoption budgets came with paid textbooks and because "free" looked like junk. Khosla expects systemic change in perhaps five more years, and says India is keen on that change. She and her husband Vinod fund the foundation, and it remains free after almost 20 years.
- **The C in CK-12.** C stands for every child and for creativity, critical thinking, collaboration, communication and community, which she calls non-negotiables. She argues students must be generalists and that language skills now matter for prompting.
- **From lab to classroom.** A former Stanford molecular biologist, she spent about 10 years in her children's child-centred classrooms, returned to college at 50 and started CK-12 at 52. She says the field now needs "intention and judgment" more than attention. Her book picks include Viktor Frankl's *Man's Search for Meaning*.
