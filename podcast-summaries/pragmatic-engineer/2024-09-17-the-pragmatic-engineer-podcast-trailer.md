## Thesis

In a one-minute trailer, Gergely Orosz, author of The Pragmatic Engineer newsletter, blog and The Software Engineer's Guidebook, announces a new podcast. He frames it as a more interactive format than his writing, aimed at software engineers and the managers of engineers who want practical, first-hand lessons on building software at Big Tech companies and startups.

## Key points

- **A new format.** Orosz says the podcast is something "that is not writing": a conversational complement to the newsletter, the blog and his book.
- **Insider perspective.** He describes the show as covering software engineering at Big Tech and startups from the inside, through deep dives with experienced engineers and tech professionals.
- **What guests share.** Orosz says guests will bring hard-earned lessons, stories and advice: what worked for them, what did not, and why.
- **Two audiences.** His stated goal is that both software engineers and engineering managers come away from each episode with pragmatic and innovative approaches they can apply.
- **Topics and timing.** The show notes list scaling teams, engineering leadership and the changing tech landscape as themes. Orosz says the first episode arrives the following week and asks listeners to subscribe via podcast players, YouTube or pragmaticpodcast.com.
