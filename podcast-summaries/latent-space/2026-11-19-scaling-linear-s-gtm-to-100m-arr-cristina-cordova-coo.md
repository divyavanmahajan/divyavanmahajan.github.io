## Thesis

Cristina Cordova, Linear's COO for about three and a half years, argues that Linear grew by sequencing its go-to-market carefully and doing more with fewer people. She says CEO Karri Saarinen's "issue tracking is dead" post reflects the bet that agents should handle the manual work around product development, and that Linear must make agent workflows better inside its own tool rather than become a database behind an MCP server.

## Key points

- **Enterprise only when ready.** When Cordova joined in 2023, Linear had one salesperson. At an offsite three weeks in, she struck the enterprise goal and aimed at growth-stage companies switching from incumbent tools instead. Linear only set an enterprise goal in 2025. She says the business has grown at least 10x since she joined.
- **A deliberately lean team.** Linear has no SDRs, does not outsource support and uses solutions engineers instead of forward-deployed engineers. It has about 170 people. AI drafts support replies, but humans check and send them.
- **Agents create the work now.** Cordova says Linear launched its MCP server in 2025, and over 50% of issues are now created by agents, while issue creation in the app is also rising. Features such as Triage Intelligence and Loops do routing and agent assignment without customers building their own agents.
- **Pricing new AI features.** Linear has about 40,000 paying customers and was entirely seat-based until June. Seats remain; coding sessions are billed at the published model API rate plus a margin, and Loops uses AI credits. Beta features start free, and Cordova prefers launching at a higher price and cutting it later.
- **Unplanned 2025 roadmap.** The MCP server, an agents API and integrations with Codex, Cursor and GitHub Copilot displaced planned enterprise features. Cordova believes this put Linear on enterprise shortlists it would not otherwise have reached.
- **Lessons from Stripe.** Stripe kept at least 40% of staff in engineering, product, design and data; Cordova tracks a similar ratio at Linear.

## Quotes
