## Thesis

In this cross-post from Humanloop CEO Raza Habib's High Agency podcast, swyx revisits his "Rise of the AI Engineer" essay eleven months on. He argues that foundation models moved the API line between companies, creating a product-focused engineer who works on zero-to-one problems while ML engineers optimise existing systems. Habib largely agrees and adds that product managers and domain experts now write prompts directly.

## Key points

- **Role and ratio.** swyx describes a spectrum rather than a fixed skill set, and suggests mature teams might have about four AI engineers per ML engineer, partly because universities are not producing enough ML engineers.
- **Critics.** Jared at Vercel told him every software engineer is an AI engineer. swyx answers that roughly half of Hacker News still has not adopted Copilot, and expects the title to stay low status for a long time. He notes Mastercard now has a VP of AI engineering, while OpenAI's listing asks for five to seven years of ML experience.
- **Early is not enough.** AutoGPT gained more GitHub stars than PyTorch, Bitcoin, Kubernetes and Django combined but did not last. His advice is "fire, ready, aim": ship quickly, collect feedback and treat hallucination risk as manageable for companies that are not Google.
- **Vertical beats horizontal.** swyx says vertical startups such as Harvey, Midjourney (an estimated $200-300 million a year with about 50 people), Perplexity and Brightwave are growing fastest. For tooling, he advises buying evals and observability first and building only once needs are clear.
- **Trends to plan around.** He says the cost of 70 MMLU falls five to ten times a year, GPT-4-level pricing has dropped from about $20 to $2 per million tokens, and Groq is reportedly aiming for 5,000 tokens per second. He also names long contexts, multimodality and "temperature two" creative uses.
- **The conference.** The World's Fair went from one track to nine, including evals and ops, AI in the Fortune 500 and VPs of AI, and was targeting 2,000 attendees.
