## Thesis

Cristina Cordova, Linear's COO for about three and a half years, explains why CEO Karri Saarinen's "issue tracking is dead" post did not alarm customers: the issue remains, but tracking work by hand does not. She argues Linear must bring agent workflows into its own product instead of becoming a database behind an MCP server, and that its lean, step-by-step go-to-market made that pivot possible.

## Key points

- **Small enough to reinvent itself.** Cordova contrasts Linear with Google, which would struggle to disrupt search ads. Linear now pitches a product development system for agent orchestration rather than an issue tracker.
- **Agents create half the work.** Linear launched its MCP server in 2025; she says over 50% of issues are now created by agents, while issue creation in the app is also rising. Triage Intelligence and Loops label, route and assign agents to issues.
- **Enterprise only when ready.** When she joined in 2023, Linear had one salesperson, and at an offsite three weeks in she crossed out the enterprise goal in favour of growth-stage companies. Enterprise became a goal only in 2025, and the business has at least 10x'd since she joined.
- **Lean by design.** Linear has no SDRs, uses solutions engineers instead of forward-deployed engineers, does not outsource support, and has about 170 people. AI drafts support replies but humans check them; she says she has never had a support escalation.
- **Pricing AI features.** With about 40,000 paying customers and seat-only pricing until June, Linear keeps seats, charges coding sessions at the published API rate plus a small margin, uses AI credits for Loops, and starts beta features free. Cordova prefers launching high and lowering prices later.
- **Unplanned roadmap, Stripe habits.** The MCP server, agents API and Codex, Cursor and GitHub Copilot integrations displaced planned enterprise features in 2025, which she believes put Linear on enterprise shortlists. From Stripe she keeps watching that roughly 40% of staff are in engineering, product, design and data.
