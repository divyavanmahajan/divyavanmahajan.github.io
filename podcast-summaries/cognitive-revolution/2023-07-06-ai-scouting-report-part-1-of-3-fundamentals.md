## Thesis

This audio episode is a short announcement. The full report is a YouTube video. Nathan Labenz presents his "AI scouting report" for June 2023 as a way to step back from daily AI news and teach the core fundamentals of modern AI as plainly as possible. He argues that the material needs visual aids, so the series lives on YouTube while the podcast feed carries short audio announcements.

## Key points

- **Purpose.** Labenz says the report brings together the concepts and trends he has studied closely for two years, so listeners can engage with AI research, products and debate in more depth.
- **Part 1 scope.** According to Labenz, Part 1 opens with a summary of current general-purpose AI capabilities, then covers how modern AIs are designed and trained, the major capability unlocks, and what is known about how they work and what they understand.
- **Video outline.** The show notes list sections on "we now have human-level AI (that does not mean human)", whether memory is the next big shift, data, compute and algorithms, foundation models, scaling, grokking and emergence, and capabilities that remain hard to predict.
- **Part 2 preview.** Labenz says the next part will cover fine-tuning, agent design and memory systems, market dynamics, economic impact, AGI timelines, live players and the outlook for AI safety.
- **Disclosure.** Labenz discloses that he advises Athena, an executive-assistant company, and that a group of 200 Athena EAs was the first audience for the report after his wife. He describes how his own EA moved into AI operations work at Waymark, including fine-tuning and validating more than 100 custom models.
