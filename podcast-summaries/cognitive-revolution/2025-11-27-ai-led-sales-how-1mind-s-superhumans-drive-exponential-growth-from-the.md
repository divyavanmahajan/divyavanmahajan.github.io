## Thesis

In this cross-post from Zapier CEO Wade Foster's Agents of Scale show, Amanda Kahlow (founder of 6sense, now CEO of 1Mind) argues that roughly 80% of a sales deal is the transfer of information, which AI "superhumans" already do better than people. She expects marketing, sales and customer success to merge into one lifecycle team, and says leaders should be honest that many current jobs will disappear.

## Key points

- **What a superhuman is.** Kahlow describes a face, voice and "brain" that can pitch, share slides, run live demos and read the screen; she calls the face a gimmick to get people talking and says the value is in conversation quality and recall across thousands of slides.
- **Drinking their own champagne.** Kahlow says 76% of 1Mind's pipeline opportunities were sourced by its own superhuman, Mindy. In the HubSpot deal, about 20 stakeholders held some 50 conversations with Mindy, cutting a likely 90-day cycle to 30 days.
- **Customer results.** She cites HubSpot raising revenue 25% after adding a superhuman to its product-led growth motion, with 88% of page visitors talking to it; an unnamed large social platform cut sales cycles from 22 days to two and doubled average deal size.
- **Which roles are safe.** Kahlow sees account executives as safest because of soft skills and org navigation, while information-heavy roles are exposed. 1Mind promises a new role, and ideally an equity reward, to staff who automate their own job.
- **New jobs.** She is hiring someone to manage all AI agents across the company, a "chief of staff for agents"; Foster describes the work as reviewing agent output against rubrics and evaluations.
- **Hearing no.** Kahlow says she worries when prospects say yes too easily, because category-defining products should not fit existing playbooks.
