## Thesis

Derya Unutmaz, a human immunologist at the Jackson Laboratory working on aging and cancer immunotherapy, argues that models such as o1 Pro and Deep Research now contribute at every step of science, including original hypotheses. He says what separates people who get value from those who do not is mindset rather than prompt engineering: treat the model as a peer, expect trial and error, and look for what works instead of hunting for flaws.

## Key points

- **From survey tool to eureka moments.** Unutmaz used GPT-4 mainly to summarise literature, but says o1 Preview produced his first "wow moment" when it applied Battle Royale game mechanics (like PUBG) to engineered T cells competing in a tumour, suggesting ideas he had not considered.
- **Data interpretation.** Given gene expression data on naive T cells from young and old people, Deep Research proposed that the "young" cells in elderly subjects had themselves changed epigenetically, an insight he says summed up decades of his own work.
- **Simple prompting habits.** He writes down what he thinks in plain language, asks for ten ideas that each improve on the last, and sets up an enthusiastic and a skeptical scientist to debate for about ten rounds. He also uses Grok's Deep Search for finding and simplifying information.
- **Medicine and productivity.** Unutmaz says not using AI in medicine has become unethical, cites 12 million misdiagnoses a year in the US, and says Deep Research drafted a patent application in about half an hour that would have cost over $10,000 through lawyers.
- **Careers.** He sees AI as a democratiser for curious, high-agency people and says he might hire a motivated 19-year-old using AI over a 40-year-old with 20 years of experience. Labenz worries about how many people can meet that bar.
- **Safety by ecosystem.** Drawing on regulatory T cells, Unutmaz argues for "regulator AIs" and defender AIs that check other AIs, and says he worries more about humans misusing AI than about ASI itself. Labenz takes catastrophic risk more seriously.
