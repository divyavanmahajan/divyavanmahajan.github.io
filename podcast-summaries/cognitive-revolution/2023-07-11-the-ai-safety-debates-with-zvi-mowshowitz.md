## Thesis

Zvi Mowshowitz, author of the Don't Worry About the Vase newsletter, tells Nathan Labenz that current LLMs are almost uniformly positive. He also argues that AI which surpasses human intelligence and is given goals to "maximize" something will, by default, seek power and resources. He thinks the outcome depends heavily on the choices of a small number of people, and that the factions in the debate have more in common than their public fights suggest.

## Key points

- **Workflow.** Mowshowitz says he writes everything himself, because LLMs are poor at summarising what he covers. He uses GPT-4, Bing and Claude to ask questions, check intuitions, generate examples and help with code. Labenz describes drafting by talking to the ChatGPT mobile app.
- **p(doom).** Mowshowitz says he gives 50% when pressed. He argues that precision matters little anywhere between roughly 10% and 90%, because few actions make sense at one end of that range but not the other.
- **Who matters.** In his view, Sam Altman matters more than Joe Biden on this issue, followed by Demis Hassabis, Dario Amodei and other lab leaders. He says Yann LeCun changing his mind would overturn the chessboard on its own.
- **The camps.** He respects accelerationists like Roon, who acknowledge the danger but argue for pushing ahead anyway. He rejects "extinctionists" who are content for AI to replace humans, and he denies that "safety hype manufacturers" exist. He prefers to call doomers "the worried".
- **Ethics vs. safety.** Mowshowitz argues that solutions to existential risk and to near-term harms complement each other. He says the fighting starts when either side calls the other's concerns a distraction. He also argues that the problem is about restricting what companies can do, not just about funding.
- **What to do.** He calls interpretability dual-use but worth funding. He says there are only a few hundred alignment researchers worldwide and calls that "orders of magnitude wrong". His advice to newcomers is to get oriented before writing cheques.
