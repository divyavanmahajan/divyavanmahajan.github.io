## Thesis

Nathan Labenz and co-host Prakash Narayanan stitch together clips from a week of live AI in the AM shows around one question: what sets the pace of AI from here? Zvi Mowshowitz argues capabilities are accelerating faster than policy is responding, Evan Conrad argues the real bottleneck is credit rather than chips or power, and Lonis Hamaili of Cambrian argues the physical supply chain will capture more value than the labs.

## Key points

- **Recursive self-improvement.** Zvi accepts that analyses showing a 1.6x speedup petering out hold only "if nobody ever hits a home run". He says people he trusts at OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind and METR think RSI has, at a low level, already begun.
- **"Already clean" was oversold.** Zvi calls Nick Cammarata's Goodfire essay a good paper "dramatically oversold" by commentators: weak activations and attention remain unexplained, and the showcase Colorado neuron also fires on tornado systems. He says the real barrier was the bitter lesson, a lack of compute to go neuron by neuron.
- **Against the cognitive core.** Zvi rejects Karpathy's idea of a small reasoning core that looks facts up, arguing that memorised facts produce intuition, and that a pure core would be amoral because values like "don't steal" sit outside it.
- **Credit is the constraint.** Conrad says lenders cannot value a repossessed GPU cluster, so they underwrite the AI lab's credit, which forces big deposits, three-to-five-year contracts and huge pre-revenue rounds. He calls the hyperscaler price premium an illusion of on-demand versus reserved pricing and expects a year or two of lab consolidation.
- **Atoms over bits.** Hamaili (Labenz discloses he has committed to invest in Cambrian) says semis trade near 16 times earnings against 22 for the S&P, and that no expert he knows expects DRAM bits to grow more than 2.5x by the end of the decade.
- **The leaked bill.** Zvi says Anthropic's draft federal bill would nullify state laws immediately while its own rules take six months or more, and that once preemption is traded away it cannot be offered again.
