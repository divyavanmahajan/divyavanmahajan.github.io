## Thesis

In a six-month recap with Nathan Labenz, Zvi Mowshowitz argues that the biggest recent shifts are strategic and political rather than technical. He sees the veto of SB 1047 as a setback that leaves states copying worse, EU-style rules. He calls OpenAI's for-profit conversion a misappropriation from its nonprofit, and he describes a second Trump term as high variance for AI, with more downside and more upside than a Harris administration.

## Key points

- **Scaling and o1.** Zvi reads Ilya Sutskever's remark that the age of scaling is over as a sign that labs are searching for new methods, and he doubts SSI can make superintelligence safe. He finds o1 limited ("you're thinking really hard, but you're not that smart"). Using Claude Sonnet inside Cursor more than doubled his coding speed compared with the chat interface.
- **Computer use and live players.** Zvi accepts Anthropic's iterative-deployment case because computer use was coming within a year anyway. Labenz counts five to seven live players. Zvi argues that cooperation is much harder with seven players than with two, and that lock-in to diverging models eases racing pressure.
- **Timelines.** Labenz cites Metaculus's 2027 median for weak AGI. Zvi says the labs believe in 2026-27 but would bet against 2027, putting the odds under 50%.
- **Regulation.** Zvi calls the SB 1047 veto a disaster. States are now weighing use-case bills like Texas's, which carry all the costs and no benefit against existential risk. He gives double-digit odds to a NEPA-style compliance nightmare.
- **OpenAI conversion.** OpenAI raised $6 billion at a $150 billion valuation on condition that it converts to a for-profit. Zvi calls the conversion attempted theft from the nonprofit and argues the nonprofit could fairly claim about 49%.
- **Elections, UBI and Trump.** Zvi says deepfakes barely mattered because fake news is driven by demand. He calls the Altman-backed UBI study basically a failure. His good Trump scenario keeps the Biden executive order minus what Trump would call "woke" parts, and he gives conditional support to a unified AI project if a race is unavoidable.
