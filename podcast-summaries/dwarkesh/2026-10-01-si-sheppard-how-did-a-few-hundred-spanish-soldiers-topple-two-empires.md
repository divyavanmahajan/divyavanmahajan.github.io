## Thesis

Military historian Si Sheppard tells Dwarkesh Patel that Cortés and Pizarro won their battles with horses, steel and the shock of the unknown, but won their wars through diplomacy. Both empires were narrow elites ruling resentful subject peoples, and seizing the emperor gave the Spanish the whole structure. Patel frames the episode as a historical precedent for a technologically advanced outside force taking over a much larger established order.

## Key points

- **The numbers.** Cortés took the Aztec empire of about 6 million people with a few hundred men in two and a half years. At Cajamarca in 1532 Pizarro had 168 men against an army in the tens of thousands, and the only Spanish casualty was Pizarro's hand, cut by one of his own men.
- **What actually mattered.** Sheppard says guns lost their impact once natives understood slow reload times. Toledo steel against obsidian and, above all, the horse were decisive; the Aztecs' only domestic animals were the turkey and the Chihuahua.
- **Allies, not just soldiers.** The Tlaxcalans, surrounded by the Aztecs and cut off from salt, saw Cortés as their way out. In the final march on Tenochtitlan the Spanish were perhaps 0.5% of the force, and during the siege of Cuzco more local peoples joined the besieged Spaniards.
- **Natives did adapt.** The Aztecs fought in city streets, flooded Iztapalapa and used captured crossbows; the Inca used bolas against horses and Manco Inca learned to ride. The learning curve was too steep, and Aztec warfare aimed to capture victims for sacrifice rather than kill.
- **Silver as a trap.** Potosí silver bought Chinese goods via Manila and funded Spanish wars, but Sheppard argues it imported inflation and kept Spain from developing capital markets, unlike the Netherlands and England. The later loss of silver helped bring down the Ming.
- **Corporations and empire.** Patel suggests the East India Company's ability to replace leaders beat Indian monarchies prone to bad successors. Sheppard agrees and calls the conquistadors capitalist freebooters.
