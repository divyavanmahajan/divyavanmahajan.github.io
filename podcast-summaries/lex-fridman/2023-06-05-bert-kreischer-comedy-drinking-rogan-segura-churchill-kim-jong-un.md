## Thesis

Bert Kreischer tells Lex Fridman that his career rests on a need to have fun and bring a spark to other people, not on being the edgiest or coolest comic. He credits humility learned from his father, blind trust in friends such as Tom Segura and Joe Rogan, and a willingness to look foolish in promotion. Both men talk about drinking as a route to camaraderie.

## Key points

- **Drinking habits.** Kreischer marks the anniversary of Winston Churchill's death by copying Churchill's daily routine. He favours a Campari spritz or a double Jack on the rocks, but says he avoids alcohol when depressed and leans on it mainly for anxiety.
- **Humility from his father.** On his 26th birthday his father told him he had no humility and had to chase his dream as hard as he could. Kreischer says that lesson led him to become a paid regular at the Comedy Store late in his career. His father sobbed at the premiere of The Machine.
- **Trust and money.** When his team suggested Segura had leaked information, Kreischer chose to keep trusting him, and it was not Segura. The two run 2 Bears 1 Cave on a handshake deal and once split money evenly so that feelings came before profit. He calls Rogan the most selfless person he has met, someone who helped many friends build careers.
- **Promotion without fear.** His main advice is not to be afraid to suck and to turn the camera on. Hip-hop dance lessons ($1,200) during Sober October sold out a tour, and he recorded a Red Rocks promo while going under anaesthesia for arm surgery.
- **Joy and getting drunk.** Kreischer traces his persona to college and a trip to Russia, where mispronouncing a word earned him the nickname "the machine". He sees getting drunk around someone as a gift. Fridman says he dislikes the physical effect of alcohol but values the camaraderie.
- **Comedy greats and advice.** Kreischer names Dave Chappelle and Bill Burr as the best of his generation. Fridman lists Norm Macdonald, Mitch Hedberg and George Carlin. Kreischer tells young people to find the thing they love so that it never feels like work.
