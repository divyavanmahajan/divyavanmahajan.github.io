## Thesis

Mark Zuckerberg tells Lex Fridman that Meta's open approach to AI suits the current stage, because models like Llama are still far from superintelligence and openness makes them more secure. He expects many different AIs, for creators, businesses and individuals, rather than one central assistant, and he argues that the near-term risk is AI amplifying known harms such as fraud and coordinated inauthentic behaviour.

## Key points

- **Llama and open source.** Zuckerberg says the 65-billion-parameter Llama was released in February under a research licence and leaked beyond researchers. He says open models get more efficient through community work such as Llama CPP, and that GPT-4 and Google's PaLM are, to his understanding, about ten times bigger.
- **Many AIs, not one.** He describes AI agents for creators to talk to their fans, agents for small businesses handling commerce and support on WhatsApp, plus AI photo editing and generated ad creative, and contrasts this with OpenAI's and Google's single-assistant approach.
- **Moderation.** Zuckerberg says Meta tracks about 18 categories of harmful content and takes down coordinated inauthentic networks. He wants removal reserved for harms people broadly agree on, with labels or fact-check flags for disputed claims, and he says Meta draws a harder line on government requests for user data than on takedown requests.
- **Leaner Meta.** He links the layoffs to making Meta a stronger technology company. The average manager had about three reports, and he wants this closer to seven or eight. His hiring rule is to hire only people you would be happy to work for.
- **Quest 3 versus Vision Pro.** Quest 3 adds high-resolution mixed reality, twice the graphics processing and 40% sharper screens, priced at $499. He says Apple's $3,500 headset validates the category but serves different use cases.
- **Intelligence versus autonomy.** On existential risk, Zuckerberg separates intelligence from autonomy, uses the neocortex as an analogy, and argues that governance should focus on how much autonomy systems are given.
