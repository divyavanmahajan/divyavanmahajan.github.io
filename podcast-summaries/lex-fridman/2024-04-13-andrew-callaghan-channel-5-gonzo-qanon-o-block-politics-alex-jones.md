## Thesis

Andrew Callaghan, host of Channel 5 on YouTube, describes how he moved from comedic ambush interviews to serious street journalism whose first goal is to humanise people who usually appear only as news items. He argues that corporate media profits from keeping Americans divided, that conspiracy movements such as QAnon steer people away from real class grievances, and that listening is the only way to heal the divide. He also gives his first public account of the 2022 misconduct allegations against him.

## Key points

- **From confessions to the road.** Callaghan hitchhiked for 70 days at 19, then filmed Quarter Confessions on Bourbon Street. Doing Things Media hired him for All Gas No Brakes with an RV and 45K a year, but his 360 deal blocked outside work and the company fired him and two friends after he asked for a 50% profit split.
- **The turn to politics.** Covid protests and the George Floyd riots in Minneapolis, where he says he was hit by a National Guard rubber bullet, turned the party show into reporting. Jonah Hill, Tim and Eric and A24 backed the HBO film This Place Rules on the lead-up to January 6th.
- **QAnon and Alex Jones.** He says the Spencer family's life was consumed by Q lore within four months, and that Enrique Tarrio told him he did not think the election was stolen. Including Alex Jones led to a confrontation with Don Lemon on CNN, after which his press tour was cancelled.
- **Copyright fight.** Fox 5's owner Gray Media had his Vegas tunnels documentary removed over a 10-second clip; he says he spent about 70K on it and is going to court to protect smaller creators.
- **Allegations and recovery.** Callaghan says he treated Instagram like a dating app while famous, accepts a pattern of pushy behaviour, describes suicidal thoughts and recovery programmes, and urges men with any platform to recognise the power dynamic they carry.
- **Drill and O Block.** He traces drill rap from Chicago (Chief Keef, King Louie, Waka Flocka Flame) to the UK and New York, and says O Block is now attempting an image rehab.
