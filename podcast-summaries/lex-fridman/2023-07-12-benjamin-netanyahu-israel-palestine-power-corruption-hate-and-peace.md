## Thesis

Benjamin Netanyahu, speaking with Lex Fridman during mass protests over his government's judicial reform, presents himself as a "19th century democrat" restoring balance between Israel's branches of government. On peace, he argues for an "outside in" approach: normalise relations with Arab states first, then return to the Palestinian question with a model of Palestinian self-government under overall Israeli security control. Fridman, who notes the conversation was cut to one hour, pushes back on power, corruption and settlements.

## Key points

- **Judicial reform.** Netanyahu calls the claim that the reform makes him a dictator "ridiculous", says Israel's Supreme Court became the most activist on the planet, and states he dropped the sweeping 61-vote override clause months earlier. He wants elected officials to have more say in choosing judges.
- **Economy and tech.** He credits his free-market reforms with raising per capita income from $17,000 to $54,000 and cites Nvidia's supercomputer plans and Intel's $25 billion plant as signs of confidence.
- **AI and jobs.** After talks with Sam Altman and Elon Musk, Netanyahu says Israel must enter AI "with full force", that regulation is needed but global regulation is uncertain, and that AI will likely destroy more jobs than it creates. Fridman bets on the opposite.
- **Power and corruption.** Netanyahu denies being corrupted by power, says the media is largely against him, and describes the cases against him as collapsing, pointing instead to alleged spyware use and witness pressure by investigators.
- **Palestinians and settlements.** He argues the core obstacle is Palestinian refusal to recognise a Jewish state in any boundary, cites rockets fired from Gaza after Israel's withdrawal, and says about 90% of settlers live in urban blocks expected to stay in Israel.
- **Abraham Accords and history.** He traces the accords with the UAE, Bahrain, Morocco and Sudan to shared concern about Iran, hopes to add Saudi Arabia, and says his father taught him that a prime minister above all needs a broad education.
