## Thesis

Will Sasso, the MADtv alumnus and co-host of the Dudesy podcast, tells Lex Fridman that acting, not stand-up, is the craft he has always wanted to master, and that its value lies in human connection. On AI, Sasso is the sceptic: he thinks an AI can feed comedians absurd premises but can never duplicate a real person. Fridman takes the more optimistic view that AI will eventually capture a person's essence and generate comedy and art on its own.

## Key points

- **Why no stand-up.** Sasso says he respects stand-up but doesn't want audiences to know his opinions, and dislikes people famous for something else switching to it. He started acting professionally at 16 in Vancouver and prefers the illusion of playing characters.
- **Depression and medication.** Sasso describes a family history of depression, talk therapy and medication, and says the stigma around mental health should be removed completely. When he started medication, it felt like doors and windows opening in his brain.
- **Loneliness in Planes, Trains and Automobiles.** Sasso calls the John Hughes film his favourite movie and argues that John Candy's Del Griffith carries loneliness through every scene; he thinks Candy would have won an Academy Award if comedies had been respected then.
- **Dudesy and AI.** Sasso describes Dudesy as a podcast curated by an AI that reads his and Chad Kultgen's emails and search histories and generates segment premises. Fridman sees it as a human-machine collaboration in which the AI supplies absurdity. Sasso says it "is genuinely cracking me up".
- **Can AI copy a person?** Fridman predicts digital replicas of people will soon be possible but thinks uploading consciousness is extremely hard. Sasso doubts any AI could ever replicate him for his family, and argues art made without a human behind it is worth less.
- **Craft and wrestling.** Sasso says he still rehearses lines in the bathroom mirror and hates giving up on a take. He names Bret Hart as the greatest in-ring wrestler and Ric Flair as the greatest overall.
