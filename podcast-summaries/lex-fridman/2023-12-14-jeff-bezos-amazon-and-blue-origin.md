## Thesis

Jeff Bezos tells Lex Fridman that reaching orbit is a solved problem and the only interesting one is cutting its cost, which for Blue Origin means manufacturing rockets at rate and becoming "the world’s most decisive company". He presents his Amazon management habits, from one-way and two-way doors to six-page memos, as mechanisms for truth-seeking and decision speed.

## Key points

- **New Glenn and rate production.** Bezos describes New Glenn as carrying about 45 metric tons to LEO on seven BE-4 engines (LNG and LOX), with a reusable first stage and an expendable hydrogen upper stage. He expects a first launch in 2024, but says the hard part is rate: 24 launches a year means an upper stage every two weeks and a BE-3U engine every week.
- **Infrastructure for others.** Amazon could rely on credit cards, postal services and the phone network; Bezos wants to spend his "Amazon winnings" on heavy space infrastructure so that a valuable space company can one day start in a dorm room. Examples include Blue Ring, which offers payloads power and compute "a little bit like Amazon Web Services", and the Mark 1 and reusable Mark 2 lunar landers.
- **One-way and two-way doors.** He argues most decisions are reversible and should be made by individuals or small teams; irreversible ones go to senior executives, which made him Amazon's "chief slow down officer". He calls compromise and wars of attrition poor ways to settle disputes and prefers escalation or "disagree and commit".
- **Skepticism of proxies.** Metrics drift from what they stand for, Bezos says. When a weekly business review showed phone wait times under 60 seconds, he called the 1-800 number in the meeting and waited more than 10 minutes.
- **Memos over slides.** Meetings start with 30 minutes of silent reading of a six-page narrative memo, which he says can take two weeks to write. In his view PowerPoint is a sales tool that lets sloppy thinking hide behind bullet points.
- **AI as discovery.** Bezos calls large language models discoveries rather than inventions, and is optimistic they will help more than harm. He also mentions a smarter Alexa and AWS's Titan and Bedrock.
