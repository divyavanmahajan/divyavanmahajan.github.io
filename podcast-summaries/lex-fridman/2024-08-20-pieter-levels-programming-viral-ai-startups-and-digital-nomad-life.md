## Thesis

Pieter Levels argues that a solo developer without venture funding can outrun large companies by building small products quickly, validating them with real payments and then automating the running of them. He credits a plain PHP, jQuery and SQLite stack, deploying straight to production and avoiding hires for his speed. Lex Fridman largely agrees and pushes on when frameworks, teams and big-company bureaucracy help or hurt.

## Key points

- **Validate with money.** Levels launches ideas within about two weeks and only counts them as validated when people pay. He started with a "12 startups in 12 months" challenge while depressed and broke at 27, and Nomad List began as a public, crowdsourced Google spreadsheet.
- **Viral AI products.** Interior AI made 10-20K a month within a week and still makes 40-50K, he says. His AI avatar launch ran on a Stripe payment link and a Typeform, trained by hand at first, and made about 150K in a week before Lensa built a similar feature and, he believes, made around $30 million.
- **Making Photo AI better.** He runs models on Replicate and tested generation settings on about 10% of users, measuring favourites and downloads. He says he still uses Stable Diffusion 1.5 because later versions were worse, and that the training data matters more than training time.
- **Simple stack, fast deploys.** Levels says frameworks backed by large funding rounds push developers towards expensive hosting. He pushes to production without staging (about 37,000 commits in 12 months) and relies on cron jobs, health-check pages, Uptime Robot and Telegram alerts.
- **Automate, don't hire.** Nomad List meetups organise themselves, and GPT-4o moderates reviews and a 10,000-person chat. Remote OK peaked at 140K a month in 2021 from job-post upsells, then fell to about 10K before recovering to around 40K.
- **Exits and Europe.** With 90% margins and sale multiples of three to five times profit, he sees little reason to sell. He also argues that regulation and a lack of entrepreneurial culture hold Europe back.
