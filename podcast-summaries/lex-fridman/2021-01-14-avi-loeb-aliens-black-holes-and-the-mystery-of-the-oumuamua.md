## Thesis

Avi Loeb, author of Extraterrestrial, tells Lex Fridman that modesty should lead us to expect other civilizations, and that the 2017 interstellar object 'Oumuamua is best explained as an artificial light sail. His wider argument is that science should treat anomalies as gifts, test hypotheses against evidence, and stop letting ego, prizes and groupthink make the search for technological signatures taboo.

## Key points

- **The case for 'Oumuamua as technology.** Loeb lists its anomalies: a tenfold change in reflected light every 8 hours (best fit a flat, pancake shape), no cometary tail, rest in the local standard of rest (one in 500 stars), and a non-gravitational push. He argues only sunlight pressure on an object under a millimetre thick explains the push, and compares it to 2020 SO, a 1966 Surveyor rocket booster identified the same way.
- **Look for trash and pollution.** Loeb says closed-off civilizations still leave trash, and proposes searching exoplanet atmospheres for industrial pollutants such as CFCs rather than only oxygen, which Earth lacked for its first 2 billion years and which natural processes can produce.
- **Taboo and missed opportunities.** He cites Otto Struve's 1952 idea for finding close-in Jupiters, delayed four decades, and early ridicule of gravitational-wave work, as examples of how resistance costs discoveries. He contrasts funding for dark matter searches with neglect of extraterrestrial technology.
- **UFOs and Proxima.** Loeb is sceptical of UFO reports because images have not become sharper as cameras improved, but favours funding scientific instruments to check them. He thinks the Parkes radio signal from Proxima Centauri was likely human interference.
- **Starshot and space.** He describes Breakthrough Starshot, launched with Yuri Milner in 2015: a 100-gigawatt laser pushing gram-scale sails to a fifth of light speed. He calls cosmic rays the underplayed risk of living on Mars.
- **Black holes and theory.** Loeb explains quasars, LIGO, and the 2017 and 2020 Nobel Prizes, suggests Planet Nine could be a black hole testable with LSST flares, and calls string theory a monologue lacking evidence.
