## Thesis

Jimmy Wales tells Lex Fridman that Wikipedia works because its incentives are different from social media: there is no open "what's on your mind" box, no advertising and no engagement algorithm, only a shared goal of a neutral, well-sourced encyclopedia. He argues that the real divide among editors is not left versus right but kind and thoughtful people versus jerks, and that business models shape outcomes more than intentions do.

## Key points

- **From Nupedia to Wikipedia.** Wales says Nupedia's seven-stage academic review produced little in about two years, while the wiki launched as a side project got more done in two weeks. He cites this pivot when advising young founders to persist but be willing to change direction.
- **Biographies and undue weight.** Wales says biographies of living people need extra care, flags "controversies" sections as poor practice, and describes deprecating the MailOnline as a source. Fridman describes inaccuracies in his own page about MIT and his birthplace.
- **ChatGPT.** Wales says ChatGPT-4 invented three plausible athlete quotes for him, but he sees promise in tools that check an article against its sources. He describes a weekend demo by Wikimedia's head of machine learning that answered questions only from retrieved Wikipedia entries, and says grounding with citations should keep Wikipedia visible.
- **Bias and politics.** Wales rejects broad claims of left-leaning bias and Larry Sanger's criticism, arguing that bias persists more in obscure topics such as anime or video games and in gaps from a mostly male editor base. He explains the recession and Twitter Files disputes as routine editorial process.
- **Business models drive outcomes.** Wales says advertising pushes platforms towards engagement and outrage, suggests Facebook pause political advertising, and describes WT.Social's trust-based membership model. Wikipedia is funded mainly by small donors; its first fundraiser after a Christmas 2003 server crash raised nearly $30,000.
- **Governments and the future.** Wales says Wikipedia has received zero FBI requests to change content and refuses to bow to government pressure. He expects AI-assisted translation to grow smaller-language Wikipedias and notes an endowment that has passed 100 million.
