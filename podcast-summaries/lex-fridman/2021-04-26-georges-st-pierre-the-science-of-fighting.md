## Thesis

Georges St-Pierre tells Lex Fridman that he fought for the freedom, money and lifestyle the sport gave him, not because he enjoyed fighting, and that fear and uncertainty cost him more than any physical damage. What he loved was the science: studying opponents, building plan A, B and C, and turning anxiety into preparation. He argues that fighters should train smarter rather than harder and walk away while they are on top.

## Key points

- **Fasting and health.** St-Pierre says gaining to 195 pounds for the Michael Bisping fight left him inflamed and ill, and he was later diagnosed with ulcerative colitis. A fasting programme from Dr Jason Fung (16:8 eating plus a three-day water fast four times a year) cleared his symptoms; scans showed he kept his muscle mass.
- **Managing fear.** He hides severe pre-fight fear from his coaches so it does not spread, and before walking out uses a mirror ritual, listing every reason he will win until he believes it. Once the fight starts he runs on "cruise control".
- **Data as a secret weapon.** A friend in Montreal measured fight footage frame by frame and found BJ Penn had the fastest reaction time on the UFC roster but a slow reset. In the rematch St-Pierre used constant feints to overload Penn's nervous system and tire him.
- **Fighting not to lose.** He says opponents who see they are losing switch to survival mode, which is why dominant champions finish fewer fights later in their reigns. He would have fought Khabib Nurmagomedov with feints and timed takedowns in the centre of the octagon, but says the UFC did not want the fight.
- **Free will and AI.** St-Pierre calls free will an illusion because everything has a cause; Fridman counters that consciousness is too poorly understood to settle it, and worries more about "dumb" autonomous weapons than intelligent AI.
- **Father, bullying and acting.** He credits his father, a Kyokushin black belt who quit drinking, for his work ethic, says he escaped bullying by changing his own confidence, and now puts the same drive into acting, including The Falcon and the Winter Soldier.
